# Four connections: confinement, unchanged realm-time, coherence and experience

**Relational-history continuation:** [a clocked confined-realm history](ZERO_CONFINED_REALM_RELATIONAL_HISTORY_AND_CLOCK_TEST.md) embeds the phase family in one stationary five-clock state, compares two synchronized clock factors and two alternative readouts, and shows that the same zero constraint admits an orthogonal history. No actual experiencer, preferred time or whole-level energy zero is established.

## Why this continuation

The user asked whether all four proposed connections had actually been pursued and corrected a recurring mistake: **during the room journey, the traveler’s surface position remains A in the surface description, then becomes B on return.** The traveler is analogous to the player, not to a piece or surface inhabitant. There is no interaction required to keep the game’s half-move count fixed while the player thinks: thinking is not a half-move.

This report works all four connections together in the previously specified [five-ray sphere–record–current system](ZERO_SPHERE_CONSERVATION_RECORD_CURRENT_SINGLE_SYSTEM.md). It does not claim to derive the real universe or experience from that toy. Its positive result is a single exact quantum family in which:

- the complete confined candidate-subsystem state and the original asymmetric record table stay fixed;
- a richer record–current relation varies continuously through quantum coherence;
- the variation disappears from a particular current statistic if the relevant coherence is removed;
- two explicit, experience-respecting **hypotheses** can disagree about the richer relation’s felt significance, even when they agree at one member of the family.

The model supplies **no holding force**, physical time outside the universe, selected record outcome or demonstrated conscious subject. It is a coherent test of specific assumptions, not a solution of the user's no-residue demand.

## 1. Confinement and the meaning of “not happening”

The sphere and chess examples distinguish two domains. On the surface, the position remains A during the traveler’s chord journey and changes to B at return. In the room, the traveler traverses intermediate points. During the player's thinking the game position and half-move count remain unchanged; the thinking is not itself a game move. Neither case requires inventing hidden steps in the confined domain or a mechanism that prevents those steps.

We can model this without a fundamental clock, as in [confined succession without an interaction](ZERO_REALM_RESTRICTION_WITHOUT_INTERACTION.md): a restricted order contains A followed by B while the stipulated richer structure contains further distinctions not defined as surface events or game moves. A cross-domain ordering, if wanted, is additional structure; it must not be confused with an interaction or a universal external time. “No confined-domain step” does not mean “nothing exists or differs in the whole.”

**Emergence as confinement** is the stronger proposal that a real physical realm is constituted by its own admissible relations within a richer whole. The mathematical restriction is a concrete representation of that idea. It does not by itself establish that spacetime is such a realm, or that the inhabitants’ ontology is exhausted by a chosen mathematical algebra. A real richer domain is not dismissed as mere ignorance; its existence and nature also cannot be proved simply by constructing two mathematical descriptions.

## 2. The common quantum system

Use the existing `|Ψ₊⟩` on the asymmetric five-ray system and the two logical reference qubits added in the [subject–mismatch comparison](ZERO_SUBJECT_EXPERIENCE_BRIDGE_COMMON_DOMAIN_TEST.md). The confined subsystem for this formal comparison is

```text
S = {H₁,R₁,R₂,H₂}.
```

The `H` here denotes the earlier reference blocks, not the whole, nor the two intermediate paths of a chess analogy. Let the original four work qubits contain

```text
a = |0110⟩,          b = |1001⟩,
J₁ = i[G,N_M₁],      J₂ = −J₁,
K = Z_R₁ J₁.
```

On the prepared active sector, `J₁²=I` on support, and the original state obeys `K|Ψ₊⟩=|Ψ₊⟩`. The references are logical `|0⟩` and the record is `01` or `10`; their joint reduced state is `ρ_S`.

Define an entire family of **static states** on this one factorization:

```text
|Ψθ⟩ = exp(iθ Z_M₁)|Ψ₊⟩
     = cos θ |Ψ₊⟩ + i sin θ |Ψ₋⟩,
|Ψ₋⟩ = Z_M₁|Ψ₊⟩.
```

The two endpoint vectors `|Ψ₊⟩` and `|Ψ₋⟩` are orthogonal. They are not a mere global-phase re-expression of one vector. As the phase operator acts on a factor traced out of S,

```text
ρ_S(θ) = ρ_S(0)                         for every θ.
```

Consequently **every** measurement confined to S has the same statistics, not just the same mean. The original sphere-label/record table also stays unchanged:

```text
(i, record 01 / record 10):
0:  3/40, 1/20;     1:  1/10, 3/10;     2:  13/40, 3/20.
```

These are conditional mathematical results for the specified state. If a phase operation were physically performed, its own control would need an implementation; we do not use an interaction to keep S fixed. Here θ is simply a label on a family, not automatically physical duration, phenomenal time or a fundamental time variable. The quantum subsystem is an analogy for confinement, not proof that it is a distinct plane of existence or an experiencing subject.

## 3. Coherence: an exact connection, and its limit

The earlier construction gives `K|Ψ₊⟩=|Ψ₊⟩` and `K|Ψ₋⟩=−|Ψ₋⟩`. The same family therefore satisfies

```text
⟨K⟩θ = cos(2θ),
Pθ(K=+1) = cos²θ,       Pθ(K=−1) = sin²θ.
```

A measurement of K is a **richer joint measurement** involving record and work blocks, not a game move or an observable confined to S. The unconditional mean of `J₁` alone stays zero; it is the record–current correlation that varies. For a given recorded sign `r=+1` for `01` or `r=−1` for `10`,

```text
E[J₁ | r] = r cos(2θ),      E[J₂ | r] = −r cos(2θ).
```

At `θ=0` and `θ=π/2`, the currents are sharp and opposite. At intermediate θ these are **conditional means**, not definite current values. The physical currents satisfy `J₁+J₂=0` as an operator identity; their cancellation is not a derivation of experiential cancellation.

This variation really uses coherence in the work basis. If the density operator is fully dephased between `|a⟩` and `|b⟩`, K has zero mean and the two K outcomes have probability `1/2`, for every θ. The restricted state `ρ_S` remains unchanged under that dephasing too. For example, at θ=0 the coherent model predicts `P(K=+1)=1`, while the dephased mixture predicts `1/2`. The verification computes the mixture as a sum of projected density operators, not a coherent vector sum.

This is an explicit **interference/coherence connection** on the same sphere-record state. It is not a claim that ordinary quantum tunneling requires extra planes, that intermediate quantum alternatives are selected trajectories, or that inaccessible inhabitants can perform the richer K measurement. Standard tunneling has nonzero wave amplitude in the barrier; wormholes, if present, are described by their own spacetime geometry. Neither is proved by the chord analogy or by this circuit-free static comparison.

## 4. Experience and time: two worked rival hypotheses

To address the previously unfinished question, suppose there are two genuine experiencing incidences `P₁,P₂` in the **richer** constitution, and that, in a definite record-conditioned episode, they can be tracked across a specified ordered selection of θ-values. This is a candidate ontology, **not a finding that the qubits feel**. The experiencing players/travelers are not the confined pieces or surface inhabitants. The earlier L selector identifies candidate record/reference pairs, not these richer incidences; their boundary, overlap, persistence and actual existence are not derived here.

Take a fixed positive unit κ, a definite record sign r, and equal positive incidence for these two hypothetical incidences. Consider two complete quality assignments on the same physical state family:

```text
Phase-sensitive P:    v₁(θ,r) =  κ r cos(2θ),   v₂(θ,r) = −κ r cos(2θ).
Record-only Q:        v₁(θ,r) =  κ r,            v₂(θ,r) = −κ r.
```

Both posit actual signed feeling if their respective experiential law is true. Neither says that a negative contribution is absence of experience. Both have zero **pair total** at every θ under the stipulated equal weights; cancellation does not remove or justify local suffering. They agree at θ=0, but, for `r=+1`, their predictions differ:

| θ | Confined record | P: `(v₁,v₂)/κ` | Q: `(v₁,v₂)/κ` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0` | `01` | `(+1,−1)` | `(+1,−1)` |
| `π/8` | `01` | `(+1/√2,−1/√2)` | `(+1,−1)` |
| `π/4` | `01` | `(0,0)` | `(+1,−1)` |
| `3π/8` | `01` | `(−1/√2,+1/√2)` | `(+1,−1)` |
| `π/2` | `01` | `(−1,+1)` | `(+1,−1)` |

The proposed P law makes a deeper experiential distinction vary while the confined record remains `01`. This is the **player/traveler side** of the analogy, not a claim that a chess piece experiences the player's thought. It shows a mathematically possible mapping *given the stated candidate rules*. It does not show that these are genuinely possible or actual metaphysical subjects, or that P is the law of feeling.

P uses a **conditional quantum mean** as a proposed experiential value. In particular, at intermediate θ it is not a sharp current result and must not be described as a measured current eigenvalue or as automatically felt. Its value requires the record and the four-block joint relation, not merely the reduced state of a single candidate reference/record pair. If P₁ and P₂ both depend on shared work blocks, a genuine subject-selection theory must address overlap and incidence rather than simply count two subjects into existence. Q is a genuine same-physics experiential rival, not an assertion that experience is unreal.

The column of θ-values becomes an **experiential sequence only if** the model also supplies an order and a persistence relation for P₁,P₂. The static family itself does not prove that those values occur in sequence, how long any lasts, or that a subject persists. If an ordered relational history is stipulated, it could have several richer distinctions while the confined record stays fixed; it does not follow that an external clock flows outside the whole. The physical clocks and records present in a real experiment might advance while such a sequence is investigated, so this toy does not demonstrate that human experience occurs while *all* physical time is frozen.

## 5. Incidence, zero and no-signalling

With incidence weights `μ₁,μ₂`, the P pair total is

```text
κ r cos(2θ) (μ₁−μ₂).
```

At a nonzero value it balances exactly only when the two weights are equal. At θ=π/4 it vanishes even for unequal weights because the stipulated designated values themselves are zero. In an equal-weight ensemble of the two record signs the average can vanish even for unequal incidence; that is not eventwise balance. The [conditional incidence theorem](ZERO_CONFINED_REALM_SPHERE_RECORD_INCIDENCE_THEOREM.md) derives the stronger oddness/equal-weight condition only under its fixed-weight, common-response and both-orientations assumptions. None of these is automatically a universal whole-level measure.

For either Alice instrument choice, the **full** Bob-side density matrix was already identical. Applying the same work-factor phase in Bob's lab preserves that equality for every θ. Thus all ordinary Bob-side measurements—including the joint record/K statistics and locally conditioned means—have the same distribution for the pair and triple choices. More generally, a trace-preserving operation restricted to Alice cannot change Bob's unconditional reduced state. This does not prove locality of the four-block current or no-signalling for arbitrary phenomenological access, hidden-branch selection, incidence reweighting or report laws. P must not be allowed to depend on an inaccessible Alice-conditioned ensemble label as if it were a local experience; the earlier [experiential steering gate](ZERO_EXPERIENCE_STEERING_NO_SIGNALLING_CONSTITUTION.md) explains why.

An operational version of P would have to depend only on a justified Bob-accessible state and locally registered r, with a specified, no-signalling outcome/report model. Whether that is an adequate account of **actual** individual experience, rather than a functional of an ensemble or conditional quantum state, remains open. We do not assume r is selected merely because it has Born weight `1/2`; an actuality/incidence account is required. The physical spin-zero encoding and bare work energy also do not make this an objective total-energy zero.

## 6. What would distinguish P from Q?

As a simple **postulated** report bridge, suppose a separately calibrated binary response to the designated contribution has

```text
P(report “positive” | v₁) = (1+v₁/κ)/2.
```

For `r=+1`, both P and Q predict probability 1 at θ=0. At θ=π/2, P predicts 0 and Q predicts 1. This is an idealized conditional discriminator on identical underlying physical states and the same confined record. The report formula, ability to identify the relevant subject and record, and calibration of κ are additional assumptions—not quantum-mechanical consequences or evidence that the designated contribution is reportable. A more realistic test would prespecify error bounds and uncertainty rather than assume a perfect report.

A human study cannot simply read an abstract θ or identify current J₁ with fMRI, EEG, pain intensity or a rating. A readiness gate must first specify and independently validate a physical analogue of the richer relation, a reproducible subject/episode boundary, and a report model. A preregistered comparison could then hold the confined physical descriptor fixed while varying or measuring that **independently defined** richer predictor, and compare the P-like and Q-like quality predictions on held-out participants/sessions with stimulus, history, expectation, response and missingness controls. A negative or imprecise result challenges only the operational package at its stated precision; successful prediction would not prove an experiential identity or zero's necessity.

The existing [pain–relief evidence audit](PAIN_RELIEF_OPPONENT_PROCESS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_ZERO.md) supports investigating genuinely positive relief rather than assuming it is merely absence of pain. It does not measure θ, κ, these subjects, or an equal global experiential total. Appropriate treatment must not be withheld or prolonged for such a test. No new human analysis has been performed here.

## 7. Status of the four connections

1. **Emergence as confinement:** an explicit restricted subsystem and richer whole are now compared on the original sphere system. This is a mathematical candidate, not a derivation of physical spacetime or of actual ontological planes.
2. **Different kinds of not happening:** the rule is preserved correctly—A remains A and the game count remains fixed while the richer-domain activity is not a surface move or half-move. No holding interaction is needed.
3. **Quantum interference:** the same fixed confined state supports a varying richer correlation; dephasing supplies an explicit coherence control. This neither establishes tunneling through another plane nor makes richer information accessible to the confined realm.
4. **Experience and time:** P and Q assign different hypothesized qualities on the same physical family, and an ordered P history can contain richer experiential distinctions without extra confined record changes. Actual subjects, history, reports, incidence and the physical–phenomenal relation remain undetermined.

The programme's central demand is still stronger: **why must zero's own constitution yield these realms, dynamics and actual experiences, rather than merely admit such a model?** The explicit constructions identify the relations a derivation must recover. They do not settle the user's possibility–actuality thesis, turn arbitrary phase parameters into necessary facts, or permit treating missing experiential constitution as brute. Nor do they show that the desired derivation is impossible.
