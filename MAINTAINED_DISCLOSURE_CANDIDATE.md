# Maintained disclosure: frozen candidate and separating contrast

## 1. The claim being tested

> **H_M1:** within a declared, independently warranted domain of finite experience, the qualitative occurrence and local incidence associated with an active content-maintaining organization are constituted by its realized maintenance powers. Additional distribution to specified consumers is not necessary when that maintaining basis is genuinely preserved.

This is a restricted constitutive hypothesis within the [intrinsic-articulation account](NECESSARY_ARTICULATED_UNITY.md), not a new whole-account. It proposes how finite disclosure could be internally grounded without a simulator, separate owner, or process creating a second reality. It does not identify the absolute with a recurrent machine or say that every memory register is conscious.

**Frozen rival H_D1:** in the same declared domain, the specified additional multi-consumer distribution is necessary for that anchored qualitative/incidence organization, even if the nominated maintenance basis is preserved.

Neither claim represents every version of GWT, IIT, or another named theory. H_D1 is not a claim that every aspect of consciousness disappears whenever this distribution is absent. It denies preservation of the nominated organization under the proposed separating condition. The hypotheses are not exhaustive.

No neural domain, qualitative identity, or physical sufficiency claim has been validated by this report. Freezing these claims makes their conditions and possible failure precise; it does not freeze a conclusion about experience.

## 2. Independent premises and load-bearing additions

| ID | Premise | Status and why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | An actual qualitative contrast and its ownership/incidence are independently warranted in the domain. | Needed evidence target; not supplied by stimulus labels, model bits, or reports alone. No new human observations are supplied here. |
| P2 | The maintaining organization C and the relevant interval are physically specified without labeling them conscious. | Required boundary/temporal identification; the finite audit below stipulates a reduced C solely for a controlled comparison. |
| P3 | The intervention preserves C's constitutively relevant state, dynamics, localization, and counterfactual powers. | Physical preservation premise. Equal trajectories or a reduced update law need not satisfy it. |
| P4 | The intervention removes the specified additional distribution and does not replace it with an equivalent distributed organization. | Must be examined in the full actual implementation; a new feedback controller may add powers omitted from the reduced model. |
| P5-M | C is sufficient for the anchored local qualitative/incidence organization. | H_M1's substantive constitution premise, not independently established physics. |
| P5-D | The specified distribution is additionally necessary. | H_D1's incompatible premise under a valid separating contrast, not an empirical conclusion here. |
| P6 | The observation relation remains warranted under the intervention. | A report can discriminate only with independently justified reliability/sensitivity. Do not stipulate faithfulness for the favored rival and failure for the other. |

P1–P4 and P6 are shared obligations; P5-M and P5-D locate the disagreement. The absolute's unity, completeness, and self-knowledge do not independently establish either P5 premise.

### Stable scope of the hypotheses

Before empirical execution, the candidate must declare its physical domain, target contrast, evidence relation, relevant interventions, localization, and temporal regime. Changes to those commitments after a counterexample are revisions, not unnoticed reinterpretations of “maintenance.” Calling C “whatever preserves consciousness” is forbidden.

## 3. Worked finite case: what the existing implementation actually contains

Reuse [the implemented workspace](research/realized_workspace/model.py), not a new model labeled conscious. It has one workspace slot W, source feedback F, consumer memory, action, comparison, and a direct report. For this *physical audit*, declare

    C = (W, F).

A content is a source tag and one binary value. The two visual payloads 0 and 1 are **physical model distinctions only**, not established orientations, red/blue qualities, or experiencing subjects. The source names do not make the abstract model a visual or auditory brain system.

The model is one-slot: a visual and auditory proposal can compete for selection, but their presence as input proposals does not demonstrate concurrent phenomenal co-presentation. The report therefore does not claim to work through a two-quality unity case that the model cannot represent.

### Four conditions

1. **Reference:** distribution, maintenance, action, and feedback enabled.
2. **Ordinary lesion:** distribution disabled. Feedback may change, so this is not a clean removal of distribution alone.
3. **Fixed replay:** replace feedback with a previously recorded baseline source. It can match the baseline while failing a forced-content counterfactual.
4. **Content-sensitive replacement:** distribution disabled; a replacement route reads actual W and sets the same reduced feedback function as the reference.

For the fourth condition, the existing audit proves equality of the reduced controlled update under its declared inputs and interventions. That is a real physical-model result. It does not establish preservation of full localization, omitted pathway powers, or the hypothetical constitutive basis in P3.

### Local disclosure interpretation, conditional on a warranted law

If the reference supports an independently anchored finite episode and H_M1 is true with P3–P4, the replacement preserves that episode's nominated organization while changing downstream distribution. The whole's complete awareness includes both reference and replacement occurrences without transferring all content into either local organization. Distinct actual implementations remain distinct tokens; “preservation” means the justified target equivalence, not numerical identity of two episodes.

If H_D1 is true with the same premises, that target organization is not preserved. Neither interpretation follows merely from the physical model's bit values. Exact chi, actual episode boundaries, and the whole/local acquaintance relation still require warrant.

## 4. Frozen predictions

| Condition | Physical preservation status | H_M1 consequence | H_D1 consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary distribution lesion | C may change through feedback | No unconditional preservation prediction | Loss of the distribution-dependent target if the nominated distribution is necessary; other changes confound interpretation |
| Fixed baseline replay | Baseline match, counterfactual mismatch | No guarantee of preservation | Does not restore the nominated distribution |
| Genuinely C-preserving replacement with distribution absent | Required separating premise, not established in full physics by this model | Preserve the anchored qualitative/incidence organization | Do not preserve that organization |

H_M1 is rejected in its declared domain if the target demonstrably changes despite independently established preservation and absence. H_D1 is rejected if the target demonstrably persists despite independently established absence and appropriate control of other conditions. A failed preservation premise makes the contrast inconclusive, not automatically favorable to either rival.

If the new controller realizes the supposedly removed feature, P4 fails. If the omitted consumer structure is part of the actual maintaining basis, P3 fails for the proposed boundary. These failures must be addressed by independent physical characterization, not by defining every surviving case outside the hypothesis.

## 5. Observation audit: why the present example does not decide the rivals

In the model, direct report is computed from W, independently of the distribution gate. Therefore reference and replacement can have identical reports while memory/action/comparison differ. Report-disabled comparisons retain the reduced match but provide no affirmative phenomenal evidence.

The new [contrast-contract check](verification/maintained_disclosure_contrast.py) records the two input payloads, content perturbations, report-on/off conditions, lesion/replay witnesses, and the status of the missing premises. It reuses the existing transition model; it assigns no experience to any condition.

Run `python verification/maintained_disclosure_contrast.py`. The retained [diagnostics](verification/maintained_disclosure_contrast.json) contain 16 explicit comparison rows: two payloads, two report settings, and four workspace-intervention cases. Lesion and fixed-replay witnesses are also checked. These are supplemental physical-model checks, not additional tests in the 43-folder research suite.

Its result is not a neural observation or a new consciousness metric. It makes the evidence bottleneck explicit:

- An identical report is not independently established preserved experience.
- Different consumer outputs establish a distribution change in this model, not a qualitative or subject-incidence change.
- The hypotheses do not, by themselves, specify different likelihoods for these physical observations. They need a warranted observation relation to their disagreeing phenomenal target.
- If report reliability under the intervention were independently warranted, equal reliable reports could count against a rival predicting a reportable difference. If that reliability is assumed solely to obtain the result, the discrimination is circular.

Neither hypothesis is selected by the present checks. The old 64,800-comparison reduced-equivalence audit is not rerelabeled as phenomenal validation.

## 6. From a finite contrast to a genuine evidence design

A responsible empirical contrast sheet must specify all of the following before execution:

1. **Target:** one independently anchored content/quality or incidence contrast; content persistence and subject togetherness are different targets.
2. **Candidate basis:** the actual proposed maintaining organization, including the spatial/temporal bounds, state estimation uncertainty, and relevant intervention family.
3. **Distribution requirement:** which consumer uses H_D1 says are necessary; “all relevant distribution” cannot silently change meaning after the intervention.
4. **Preservation assessment:** content, maintenance dynamics, feedback, timing, and known omitted physical effects—not one matching neural signal or replay trajectory.
5. **Evidence relation:** justified measurements or judgments of the target and their known failure modes under the intervention; no single report channel is privileged by definition.
6. **Predeclared outcomes:** predictions of each rival, criteria for premise failure, and which result would genuinely reject a stated version.

The retained COGITATE protocol/code audits constrain content/task/report separation and timing estimates. They do not execute this intervention or supply a clean manipulation of phenomenal togetherness. No participant data, new primary-paper findings, or registered empirical experiment is claimed here.

## 7. Relation to particular articulation and privation

This candidate targets one potential ground of **bounded disclosure**, not the full character of redness or valence. Successful incidence/maintenance evidence would constrain Gamma without automatically fixing chi. A physical basis with several independently anchored qualitative assignments still leaves the no-independent-selector obligation unsatisfied.

Actual downstream distribution is also not **due practical integration** merely because both are called integration. Money-seeking with missing regard can involve effective physical organization while omitting something normatively appropriate. Nothing in H_M1 or H_D1 establishes that empathy, regard, or fulfillment is automatically supplied by recurrence or broadcast.

The same discipline applies at both levels: actual knowledge or representation of a relation is not its enactment. Missing due regard remains privation, not an extra positive constituent. No conclusion about the necessity or justification of all local omissions follows from the finite mechanism.

## 8. Status and next decision

**Delivered:** one restricted candidate, an explicit rival, an independent-premise ledger, a worked one-slot physical contrast, frozen conditional predictions, rejection conditions, and an executable observation/preservation audit.

**Open:** independently grounded domain and intervals; full constitutive preservation; genuine absence of the extra feature in a real implementation; warranted experiential evidence under intervention; qualitative identity. The model's stipulated reduced C is not a discovered subject.

The next useful decision is whether a real candidate basis can support P3–P4 and a warranted P6 **at the same time**. If not, the intervention must be redesigned or the comparison reported unresolved. More toy labels cannot close that gap. This advances W2–W4 and the delivery specification for Gate 2; it does not pass Gate 1 or Gate 2, select either rival, or complete the absolute's constitution.
