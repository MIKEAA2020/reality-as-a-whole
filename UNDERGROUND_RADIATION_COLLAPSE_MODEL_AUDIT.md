# Underground radiation tests, collapse operators, and the framework

## Decision

The two papers sharpen an empirical constraint on **specific physical collapse laws**, not on the proposed experiential foundation. [Donadi et al., “Underground test of gravity-related wave function collapse,” Nature Physics 17, 74–78 (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-020-1008-4) report a 95% posterior lower limit `R₀ > 5.4×10⁻¹¹ m` for their specified gravity-related, white-noise mass-density dynamics and radiation calculation. Their nuclear-wavefunction prescription `R₀ ≈ 5×10⁻¹² m` is incompatible with that limit **within the assumed completion**. This is not an exclusion of every gravity-related collapse theory, nor an experiment showing consciousness causes collapse.

[Carlesso et al., “Present status and future challenges of non-interferometric tests of collapse models,” Nature Physics 18, 243–250 (2022)](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.04231v1) is a review, not a second independent replication of Donadi: some authors overlap and the review cites the same experiment. Its important lesson is that collapse noise may be tested via heating, motion and radiation without making a macroscopic superposition. The correct consequences depend on the **operator, noise spectrum, coupling, measurement, and clock**. These papers complement the [2026 nanoparticle interference audit](NANOPARTICLE_INTERFERENCE_COLLAPSE_AUDIT.md) and materially constrain any future collapse extension, but neither maps a laboratory rate to the [cosmological energy-collapse model](COSMOLOGICAL_COLLAPSE_CLASSICALITY_GATE.md).

**Source access.** The [supplied release](https://github.com/MIKEAA2020/reality-as-a-whole/releases/tag/two_physics_papers) identifies `donadi2020.pdf` and `arXiv-2203.04231v1.tar.gz`. The PDF was read via the web PDF text interface and the review via its corresponding arXiv v1 HTML; the [Donadi supplement](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41567-020-1008-4/MediaObjects/41567_2020_1008_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) provides the count and inference formulas below. Binary release download failed, so the supplied binaries and release hashes were not independently verified and the tarball was not extracted. Some apparatus details are subject to confidentiality; the publisher links source data, but we have not independently analyzed the detector data, efficiencies, or Geant4 background simulation.

## 1. What Donadi tests

The proposed nonrelativistic, Markovian DP master equation uses a spatially smeared mass-density operator and a gravity-related spatial kernel. The accompanying microscopic stochastic dynamics causes momentum diffusion. For charged constituents, the authors calculate a spontaneous photon spectrum under their electromagnetic and approximation assumptions. The emission arises as a **side effect of that particular completed dynamics**, not from the lifetime estimate `τ ∼ ħ/ΔE_G` by itself. Their derivation assumes a Poissonian, translation-invariant, momentum-independent collapse structure and a particular multiparticle extension; changing these assumptions requires a new derivation, not automatic transfer of the bound. Distinguish the heuristic, its stochastic completion, and its electromagnetic embedding.

A low-background germanium detector at Gran Sasso recorded `576` counts in the 1–3.8 MeV region over about 62 days; the simulated standard background was `506` counts, roughly 88% of the observed count. The overburden is approximately 3,100 **metres water equivalent**, not physical depth. Nuclear proton contributions are used because electrons are relativistic in this range; the expected counts include energy-dependent efficiencies for detector components from Geant4. The difference between observed and simulated counts is not in itself evidence for collapse: background uncertainties, response and the full spectral model matter, and we have not performed a significance analysis.

Their [supplement, section 1](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41567-020-1008-4/MediaObjects/41567_2020_1008_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) reports `z_s=a/R₀³`, `a=1.756×10⁻²⁹ m³`, and uses `Λ_c(R₀)=a/R₀³+508`. The baseline 508 incorporates their two `+1` conventions, and is not simply the observed background 506. The authors form a posterior for the total Poisson mean with a prior flat in that mean, not flat in `R₀`; prior choice under parameter changes matters. From `n=576`, the gamma shape is 577. Their stated prior truncation `R₀>10⁻¹⁴ m` corresponds to a very large upper mean, numerically immaterial to the 95% quantile. Using their summary inputs gives:

| Quantity | Calculation |
|---|---:|
| 95% upper total mean | `617.071` counts |
| Corresponding `a/R₀³` | `109.071` counts |
| Lower `R₀` | `5.4401×10⁻¹¹ m` |
| `a/R₀³` at `R₀=5×10⁻¹² m` | `140,480` counts |

The authors round the bound to `0.54×10⁻¹⁰ m`, about 10.8 times the cited germanium wavefunction-width estimate. These are expected counts and a conditional Bayesian credible limit, not observed collapse photons or a model-free frequentist confidence bound. Our arithmetic accepts the published coefficient and fixed background; it does not derive the Geant4 coefficient or reproduce systematic uncertainties. The model's `R₀` is an effective mass-density smearing length, not automatically a literal measured nuclear radius.

A minor sensitivity check: the supplement integrates its flat-mean posterior from zero, even though its displayed map with fixed baseline implies `Λ_c≥508`. The gamma posterior puts about `0.143%` of its mass below 508. Truncating there while otherwise retaining that flat-mean prior gives `617.0885` and `R₀>5.4398×10⁻¹¹ m`. The numerical change is tiny here; this is a transparent alternative calculation, not the authors' reported procedure, nor a substitute for treating background uncertainty or examining other priors.

## 2. How the review updates the research strategy

- **Different observables probe different frequencies and couplings.** Mass-density CSL and DP predict excess motion/heating or radiation under particular noise spectra. The review reports, for a standard mass-proportional white CSL convention at `r_C=10⁻⁷ m`, an X-ray bound `λ<5.2×10⁻¹³ s⁻¹`; this 2022 value is not an operator-independent constant or a claim about today's strongest limit. Compare a precise model and convention before combining tests.
- **Classicality and non-interference side effects are joint constraints.** Increasing smearing can reduce radiation but also weaken position localization. [Figurato et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ad8c77) examine this tension and find that not all macroscopic systems collapse effectively in their DP analysis; their proposed upper bound depends on a relaxed, partly arbitrary classicality criterion. Passing a radiation limit alone is not enough to make a useful classicalizing theory.
- **Interference and radiation are complementary, not interchangeable.** The [nanoparticle experiment](NANOPARTICLE_INTERFERENCE_COLLAPSE_AUDIT.md) constrains a specified spatial-dephasing family using visibility versus grating power, and its supplement says it does not reach physically allowed DP smearing parameters. Donadi constrains a radiation-producing DP completion in another regime. Neither experiment supplies a universal bound on all collapse laws.
- **Later work matters.** The [Majorana Demonstrator analysis, revised 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.01343v5) uses another detector and energy region and reports a stronger model-dependent radiation constraint; its DP result was corrected in an [erratum](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.239902). Different DP normalization conventions and Bayesian versus frequentist constructions complicate direct numerical comparisons; this audit does not attempt a current strongest-limit survey.

Colored or dissipative alternatives may suppress some high-frequency effects, but this is not a free pass: they need a specified spectrum, complete dynamics and independent predictions for both radiation and macroscopic classicality. Nor does an observed side effect identify objective collapse uniquely if conventional environmental noise or another dynamical model produces the same observable statistics.

## 3. The energy-collapse transfer gate

The [JHEP cosmological model](COSMOLOGICAL_COLLAPSE_CLASSICALITY_GATE.md) uses a reduced Hamiltonian in a chosen internal time; its collapse operator is proportional to that Hamiltonian. It does not specify the laboratory many-body, electromagnetic, or detector extension. Thus `R₀` from DP and CSL's `λ,r_C` have **no supplied conversion** to its `ε`. Adding one by hand is an additional physical hypothesis, not a consequence of the whole being timeless. The [moving-window result](COLLAPSE_MOVING_WINDOW_HISTORY_GATE.md) concerns conditional one-time relative volume localization and is not laboratory position localization or an observed multi-time record.

For a time-independent Hermitian laboratory operator `A`, the Markovian ensemble law

`dρ/dt=−i[H,ρ]/ħ − (κ/2)[A,[A,ρ]]`, with `κ≥0`,

has `d⟨O⟩/dt|noise=−(κ/2)⟨[A,[A,O]]⟩`. This standard adjoint identity gives a direct operator-level feasibility test:

1. **Global energy choice `A=H`.** The noise contribution to `d⟨H⟩/dt` is zero. In an energy eigenbasis, coherence between levels `i,j` is attenuated as `exp[−κt(E_i−E_j)²/2]`; a degenerate energy subspace is unaffected. In particular, energy dephasing is not generically equivalent to spatial localization or momentum diffusion. As an ideal formal free-particle example, `H=p²/(2m)` commutes with `p²`, so this law does not increase `⟨p²⟩`; it also does not distinguish `+p` and `−p` energy-degenerate plane waves. Plane waves are generalized, nonnormalizable states; real wavepackets and interactions can behave differently.
2. **Position-like choice `A=x`.** For the ideal 1D canonical particle, `[x,[x,p²]]=−2ħ²`, hence `d⟨p²⟩/dt|noise=κħ²` (with `κ` in the corresponding units). This simple schematic illustrates the diffusion channel underlying radiation searches; it is **not** the quantitative CSL/DP master equation or an electromagnetic emission calculation. The identities assume states/domains permitting these manipulations.
3. **Energy conservation does not prove zero detector response.** For a two-level toy with `H=A=σ_x`, initially `|0_z⟩`, and bare excitation observable `N=(I−σ_z)/2`, unit `κ` gives `d⟨H⟩/dt|noise=0`, but `d⟨N⟩/dt|noise=1` initially and `⟨N⟩=(1−e⁻² cos 2)/2≈0.5282` at `t=1` under the full law (units `ħ=1`). Without the noise, the result would be `sin²(1)≈0.7081`, so the bare observable is genuinely affected. The initial state is not the interacting ground state. The actual ground state of `H` is invariant. This artificial model does not predict photon emission; it shows why conservation of *total* energy alone cannot establish that every subsystem or detector count remains unchanged.

The [companion verifier](verification/underground_collapse_scope.py) checks the finite matrix identities, plus a position-like finite-level control. Its `H` and `A` are dimensionless and unrelated to a physical detector. The prior nanoparticle audit independently gives the related degenerate-path contrast. These are new **applications and scope checks**, not claims to have invented energy-driven collapse or the double-commutator identity; see e.g. [Brody and Hughston](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9909026) and [the non-interferometric review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.04231).

A nontrivial experimental bound on cosmological energy collapse requires, in order:

1. Specify the complete lab system and environment, total versus local energy operator, coupling and units, noise spectrum, and how the clock used in the reduced cosmology maps to laboratory time. A constant rate need not survive a clock reparameterization unchanged.
2. Supply a consistent many-body/field and electromagnetic embedding including detector, switching, preparation and energy exchange, and establish admissible domains and positivity. Local energy operators need not commute with the full Hamiltonian and can produce heating; the global-energy result above does not cover them.
3. Compute both **interferometric** predictions for actual mass, velocity, grating and selection distributions and **non-interferometric** predictions for the apparatus, including any frequency dependence, normal backgrounds and efficiency. Assess whether one set of parameters gives sufficiently robust classical records as well as passing laboratory bounds.
4. Fit specified raw observations with a declared likelihood, nuisance/systematic uncertainties, priors or test construction and calibrated null/rival models. Freeze the comparison before using it to claim a bound; check noise-only and ordinary open-system rivals, not just unitary dynamics.

Unless such an extension and mapping exist, the honest conclusion is **unbounded here**, not proven viable or excluded. Ensemble dephasing alone also admits random-unitary descriptions; identical reduced statistics do not identify collapse trajectories, ontology or subjective experience. These constraints apply to emergent physical models even if spacetime is not fundamental; they do not establish how spacetime or local time emerges.

## 4. Reproduction and remaining foundational status

Install `verification/requirements-field-probe.txt` in a suitable environment, then run `python verification/underground_collapse_scope.py`. The saved [JSON](verification/underground_collapse_scope.json) reproduces the above summary arithmetic and finite-level controls. It does not replay raw data, establish a systematic-error budget, establish any current strongest limit, or validate a cosmological-to-laboratory embedding. The numerical checks are not an independent proof of the papers' derivations.

For the full research target, this is a **physics selection gate**, not a foundation: neither necessary existence, intrinsic experience, the individuation of genuine subjects, nor strongest possibility=actuality follows. Neither paper requires a literal simulation, an external creator, a mechanism converting possibility to actuality, or a universal external clock. An adequate whole-framework account still needs the independent B1–B2 experiential and subject-constitution bridges as well as a viable physical model; collapse remains an optional empirical candidate rather than a premise forced by the metaphysics.
