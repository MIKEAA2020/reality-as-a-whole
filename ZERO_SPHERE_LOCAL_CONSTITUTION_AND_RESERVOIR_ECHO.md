# Local subject candidates and a coherent echo inside the reservoir clock

## Result

I tested two explicit *candidate* physical–experiential constitutions on the existing sphere history, and joined the earlier memory echo to the actual finite-reservoir clock:

1. If a signed quality is determined solely by the state accessible at a proposed `j+M` boundary, the candidate `κ s z` can satisfy that locality condition, but `κ s b` cannot on the actual joint outcome support. For a proposed `B+M` boundary, the result reverses: `κ s b` can satisfy it, but `κ s z` cannot. The two boundaries, their persistence and the local-state principle remain hypotheses; neither construction establishes a subject or a felt quality.
2. A fresh neutral echo memory `E`, coupled by the **already constructed joint pulse** `T Q T`, records `b` exactly without accessing any source reference. Its output, the final j and M jointly recover *both* pre-echo physical parities. This does not contradict the previous no-reference theorem: the echo adds a new interaction with j and the joint old sphere/reference system, and it changes j.
3. The echo and its inverse can be inserted into the sphere-coupled reservoir program as a **20-tick** closed clock history while retaining both previously stipulated zero signed charges. It restores the prepared whole when reversed. This is a finite controlled construction, not evidence of irreversible global time, objective cosmic energy or experience.

The new progress is a precise constraint on what a **local-state-sufficient** experiential bridge could be, plus a physical way to change which signs are locally recoverable. It is not a derivation of local-state sufficiency, the eligible boundary, or the pulse from zero. The complete verifier is [here](verification/zero_sphere_local_constitution_echo_test.py).

## 1. Same physical history, two candidate constitutions

After the old sign gate W, let the conserved sphere sign be `b=±1`, the disturbed old record sign `z=±1`, and the registered sign pointer `s=±1`. The old Born table, valid separately at all 16 original clock histories, has

```text
P(b=+,z=+) = 1/2,
P(b=−,z=+) = 1/4,
P(b=−,z=−) = 1/4,
```

with s equally weighted at fixed b,z. The source interface and added reservoir apparatus are the actual ones from [the access test](ZERO_SPHERE_MEMORY_EXPERIENCE_ACCESS_AND_REPORT.md); neither source qubits nor source-reference qubits are included in the proposed local access below. Conditional on b,z,s, the added apparatus remains in the same state, because its prepared source labels are uniform and its dynamics depend on b only through those labels. This is the previous factorization theorem, not an added independence assumption.

Compare these two *conditional* candidate constitutions on that domain:

|---|---|---|
| `C_j` | j, M, and any of the added non-source apparatus | `v_j = κ s z` |

Each also needs a rule that identifies actual subject incidences and transports subject identity; neither is supplied by the factor list. ## 2. A local-state sufficiency test

Consider the following substantive **candidate principle**, not an established law:

> For the chosen carrier and a specified clock reading, its signed quality is a single-valued function of its accessible reduced physical state, with the same physical environment already included in the selected boundary.

A function may be arbitrarily complicated; it is not restricted to a linear observable. The principle may fail if relevant intrinsic facts depend on a larger relational context, process history, a different subsystem algebra, or other constitution not contained in the chosen instantaneous reduced state. Those alternatives are not ruled out by the argument.

For each of the 16 actual old histories, and at either fixed recorded s, consider the **jointly conditioned** b,z outcome states:

- `(b,z)=(+,+)` and `(−,+)` both have positive Born weight. On j+M they give the **same** reduced state, with the same added apparatus state. But `s b` has opposite values on them. No single-valued function of that selected local state can reproduce `s b` on both. In contrast `s z` is fixed by the accessible j and M signs.
- `(b,z)=(−,+)` and `(−,−)` both have positive weight. On B+M they give the **same** reduced state, with the same added apparatus state. But `s z` has opposite values. No single-valued function of that selected local state can reproduce `s z` on both. In contrast `s b` is fixed by B and M.

The test computes the actual normalized conditional quantum reductions on j and B, not just a classical table. The states refer to specified simultaneous commuting b,z,s conditioning; no unmeasured pre-existing trajectory is presumed. For the stated registered-M/no-reference access model, the best guess of the unavailable parity is `3/4`; it is not exactly determined by that local state. The model allows arbitrary measurements on the stated local register and apparatus, not merely one chosen readout.

This yields a useful but conditional discriminator:

```text
local-state sufficiency + selected j,M boundary  ⇒  reject v_B on this domain;
local-state sufficiency + selected B,M boundary  ⇒  reject v_j on this domain.
```

The reverse implications do **not** follow: being locally readable does not establish that either sign is felt, nor that the selected boundary is a subject. Both candidates can satisfy the same stated gauge and counterpart requirements. The actual dynamics distinguishes B and j—B is conserved and j is disturbed—so this is **not** a claim that swapping them is a symmetry of the full physical theory. It shows that locality cannot select a boundary until the boundary and the notion of intrinsic state are independently justified.

Under the further hypothesis that the displayed Born weights are actual subject-incidence weights, each parity has equal positive and negative weight and signed mean zero. The same equal arithmetic for both candidates does not select one, prove incidence weights, or imply that positive experience erases suffering.

## 3. A reference-free echo that changes the physical interface

Now add a separate neutral qubit `E`, initially in the pure ready state `r=+1`. This is a fresh stipulated preparation. Let `T` write j into E and let `Q=I−P+O` be the previously constructed unitary pulse. T and Q are involutions, are compatible with the stated old gauge and constraints, and commute with B. The previously verified operator identity is

```text
T Q T = Q C_B,
```

where `C_B` copies the conserved B sign into E on the valid sector. The pulse Q has support on the encoded old reference/work factor, B and j; it is **not** merely an operation available inside a j-only observer. Its physical implementation and energy cost are not given. T is a specified j–E coupling. Thus the new echo supplies an interface rather than assuming a confined inhabitant already has one.

On commuting observables, with E initially ready, the coherent unitary produces

```text
before echo:  (b, z, s, r=+) 
after T Q T:  (b, b z, s, r=b).
```

These are operator-transport relations; there need be no intermediate z measurement. In particular, after the echo,

```text
pre-echo h_B = s b = s r,
pre-echo h_j = s z = s r j_final.
```

Thus a reader supplied with **j, M and the echo memory E** can read both *pre-echo physical parities* without source-reference access. The pre-echo `h_j` is a transported historical observable. It is not automatically the present experienced sign: an instantaneous j-based candidate at the end is `s j_final = s b z`, which differs from pre-echo `s z` when b=−, with Born weight `1/2`. Persistence of a subject does not imply constancy of its quality under a genuine intervention.

The verifier checks the operator identities on the whole valid sector, and checks the coherent old state through TQT and its inverse for each b and s on **all 16 actual histories**. It does not insert a measurement between T and Q. The added E record can be used in the earlier conditional report comparison only if a reporting interface is independently supplied; a physical bit equal to b does not itself report felt experience.

### Why there is no contradiction with the no-reference theorem

The earlier theorem applies to the specified source-controlled reservoir circuit after the source and reference qubits are traced. There the added apparatus factorizes from the dephased old system and gives no extra old information. The echo introduces **new T and Q gates acting on old degrees of freedom and E**. T couples j to E; Q acts jointly on the old reference, B and j. The old record is changed and E becomes correlated with B. No hidden access to the discarded source reference is needed, but a new physical interaction and a fresh blank are required.

## 4. Embed it in the zero-charge reservoir clock

Start with the actual seven forward sphere/interface and reservoir steps F, including the already tested auxiliary memory coding. Insert the coherent echo and then its inverse, and finally undo F:

```text
F  ;  T Q T  ;  T Q T  ;  F⁻¹
7      3          3          7       = 20 clock steps.
```

Since T and Q are involutions, `TQT` is its own inverse. The original source interface and auxiliary gates use B only as a conserved control; both T and Q commute with B and act on different added apparatus factors. Q and T therefore commute with the old sphere/source-controlled steps in the stated tensor model. The added echo qubit is neutral under both constructed signed charges. Consequently every gate preserves

```text
Q_old/ε = (n−3) + A + Σ(Rᵢ−Pᵢ),
Q_aux/ε = (d−k) + Σ(Cⱼ−Eⱼ),
```

where `Eⱼ` in the second line denotes the **auxiliary purifiers**, not the distinct echo qubit E. Both charges vanish on every prepared component and remain zero during all 20 steps. Their negative purifier terms, offsets and initial correlations remain stipulations; Q's commutation with these *added charges* is not an account of the actual energetic cost of implementing Q in an independently defined physical system.

Let each clock step apply its corresponding unitary and advance a cyclic 20-level clock. The product of the 20 gates is identity on the full tensor space because the program is followed by its exact inverse, including unpaired battery endpoints. Thus the unitary clock shift D satisfies `D²⁰=I`. The normalized coherent history

```text
|H⟩ = (1/√20) Σ_{t=0}^{19} |t⟩ U_t |input⟩
```

obeys `D|H⟩=|H⟩` and `(2I−D−D†)|H⟩=0`; the two specified signed charges vanish on it. This follows algebraically for the actual unitary gates, not from interpreting the label ledger as the complete quantum pulse. The verifier checks the actual old coherent echo on all 16 histories, the full-space echo algebra, and the finite reservoir/auxiliary charge ledger through all 20 ticks for every prepared source, thermal-purifier and clean-bit branch. It does not explicitly build a dense 20-clock tensor state for every old history. Nothing here selects a preferred spacetime foliation or shows that the complete universe literally implements this schedule.

## 5. Consequences for the no-residue program

This round offers a concrete **candidate constitutive principle**—quality locally supervenes on a selected carrier state—and shows what it would exclude. It also shows why that principle cannot be used to select its own carrier: the j-boundary and B-boundary satisfy it for different physical parities. The new joint intervention can make both parities physically readable at a larger j+M+E interface, at which point local readability alone again does not distinguish the bridges.

A successful one-reality constitution must therefore do more than name a parity or choose a graph:

1. explain why its eligible boundary and lineage are intrinsic to the same whole, rather than selected for the desired answer;
2. say whether quality depends on current reduced state, relational context, process history, or a derived combination, and account for the effect of interventions like Q;
3. account for actual incidence, counterpart pairing and any signed experiential sum, including a genuine counterpart to negative felt valence if that sum is fundamental; and
4. connect the resulting intrinsic quality to physical records without defining a physical record to be the experience.

No part of this calculation rules out the user's stronger thesis that zero's own constitution necessitates those facts. It establishes that the particular locality-and-balance premises tested here do not yet do so. The echo is useful because it turns the access question into a real coupled intervention with explicit resource assumptions, rather than a verbal claim about confinement.

The subsequent [joint constitution, incidence and echo test](ZERO_JOINT_CONSTITUTION_INCIDENCE_AND_ECHO.md) admits both candidates conditionally in one history, tests their relative measures and follows their disagreements through this echo. It distinguishes full pure-physical-state effect consistency from a weaker commuting-record incidence algebra.
