# Experiential grounding: a positive candidate and its strongest rival

## Decision

The strongest economical case for an experiential foundation is not that logic forces experience, or that physical science disproves all alternatives. It is that **experience is directly given**, and an experiential intrinsic nature might account for both that datum and the structural roles described by physics without adding a separate non-experiential substance. That is a serious *candidate explanation*, with precedents in [Russellian monism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/) and [cosmopsychism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/panpsychism/).

But this argument has a missing step: direct awareness that an experience occurs does not, by itself, show that it is metaphysically fundamental or that all physical structure is constituted by the experiential. I construct a neutral-ground rival that **retains the very same experiences**, not a zombie or a denial of the datum. If the rival and experiential candidate make the same predictions for every admitted observation and intervention, even adding all their common phenomenal data cannot distinguish their *direction of grounding*. Parsimony can favor a candidate only once a defensible accounting of both theories' full primitives and bridges is supplied.

This is a contribution to the precise B1–B2 challenge in the [knowability gate](KNOWABILITY_AND_ACTUALITY_FOUNDATION_GATE.md), not a new general discovery about empirical underdetermination or grounding. It does **not** pass B1, prove rival accounts genuinely possible, or show that no future evidence or argument can ever distinguish them.

## 1. A positive experiential-constitution proposal

Take as our datum `E`: there is at least one actual concrete experience. It need not be a report, judgment or piece of information. The user's target further requires multiple genuine subjects, but their incidence, unity and boundaries are to be explained, not imported from that bare datum.

One provisional argument for an experiential base is:

1. **Actual phenomenal datum:** some experience occurs. Physical reports and the fact of experiencing must be distinguished; the latter is not inferred solely from a machine's output.
2. **Intrinsic-nature hypothesis:** physical structural and dynamical descriptions do not yet specify everything about reality's intrinsic constitution. This is a philosophical thesis about physics and ontology, not an experimental discovery; pure structural realists and some physicalists contest it.
3. **Unification proposal:** the relevant intrinsic nature is experiential; physical structures are aspects or relational organization of that same actuality, not a second substance produced by experience. If this works it avoids treating the phenomenal as an alien add-on and can respect the target's elegance and parsimony.
4. **Constitution demand:** specify a rule `F` mapping the actual experiential constitution to physical structure and a rule `S` determining actual subjects and qualities. They must recover the empirical interfaces without an independent hidden list of outcomes. At present neither is supplied as a justified complete rule. The formal example below is not such a recovery.
5. **Necessity demand:** show why that actual constitution exists necessarily, and why strongest genuine possibility is actuality. Neither follows merely from points 1–4. Adding the global schema gives a conditional result, not its justification.

This direction does not require space, matter or time to be fundamental. Local clocks and physical spacetime remain to be recovered. Nor does it require a temporal production or conversion process: `F` and `S` are intended as **constitutive** relations, not chronological mechanisms. An experiential whole is not automatically a single universal *subject* or an agent; if universal consciousness in that sense is claimed, its subjecthood and relation to finite subjects require an extra argument. Conversely, the framework's target of genuine plural subjects is not demonstrated by introducing plural labels.

**The attraction is explanatory integration, not deductive compulsion.** A categorical intrinsic nature may not be needed in the form premise 2 asserts. Even granting one is needed, experience's being known from within does not show that all such nature is experiential. A proposed `F` that merely puts already-known physical facts into the input would not derive them. For the literature's objections concerning physical causal roles, combination and constitution, see the same [Russellian monism review](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/) and [Pautz's critique of grounding bridges](https://philarchive.org/archive/PAUCRM).

## 2. The rival must preserve actual experience

Compare two **formal completions of the same stipulated empirical and experiential history**, including the same individuals' experiences and all modeled observable records:

- **A — experiential base:** an actual experiential constitution `X` is fundamental; its admitted constitution rule yields a physical relational history `P` and the specified local experiences `Eᵢ`. Subject incidence and qualitative interpretation are still obligations.
- **B — neutral base:** an actual intrinsic constitution `N`, neither stipulated to be an experience nor an external mind, grounds the same `P` **and the same real `Eᵢ`** by explicitly supplied constitution principles. It is not the claim that experiences are imaginary. These principles may prove obscure, inadequate or more costly; stipulating them is not a completed account of why experience occurs.

The arrows are proposed **explanatory grounding**, not time-directed production. A common history does not make the groundings true: A and B explicitly add distinct grounding claims. A would satisfy the experiential-nature part of the goal if its claims are defended; B does not satisfy that target merely by including experience. Either could separately postulate necessary existence, strong possibility–actuality, and subject plurality, but neither follows from its diagram. The neutral rival is used to challenge the inference from observed experience to the experiential *base*, not to replace the user's target or silently claim that a non-agentive neutral whole is universal consciousness.

Both completions can take exactly the same actual first-person evidence seriously. They differ in what makes it and the structural facts obtain. Rejecting B because its bridge is unexplained is a valid **comparative criticism only if** A's `F`, `S`, phenomenal base and necessity claims are assessed at the same standard. Labeling A's physical recovery “identity” while leaving its content unspecified would conceal, not solve, the parallel obligation. Conversely, treating every grounding relation as a separate arbitrary brute law is not established either: a principled derivation might reduce the costs of either candidate.

The models are formal rivals, not demonstrated metaphysically genuine alternatives. If the strong modal schema applies globally to their incompatible grounding claims, only the actually true one can be genuinely possible; this **does not identify which one is true**. Formal counterdescriptions do not contradict the user's “if not actual, impossible” principle, because formal consistency here is not a claim of genuine possibility.

## 3. What even very rich data can and cannot decide

Let `aₜ` be a permitted physical intervention chosen from prior data, and `zₜ` the observed record. Let an idealized phenomenal oracle additionally return the stipulated actual experience `eₜ`; this oracle is a **generous identifiability assumption**, not a claim that another person's experience is fully accessible or measurable. Suppose A and B assign the same joint conditional kernel for `(zₜ,eₜ)` after **every** intervention and prior history in a specified protocol class.

For any finite adaptive protocol, the chain rule then gives the same joint likelihood under A and B: each next-step conditional factor is equal, including after actions chosen in response to earlier results. If an observed history has nonzero likelihood under both, Bayes' rule leaves their odds at the prior odds. This is a standard probability consequence, not an empirical theorem that all real A/B completions must have equal kernels. It applies to the *specified* matching rivals and admitted protocols. If a correctly derived experiential constitution predicts a different record or experience, it can in principle be tested against B; if an additional justified principle makes grounding itself observable, our equality premise no longer covers that evidence.

The [finite verifier](verification/experiential_grounding_rival_gate.py) checks eight adaptive two-stage policies and all four binary outcome histories per policy, with exactly equal model likelihoods. It includes a control with different intervention-response probabilities, for which the specified two-stage distribution has total-variation distance `17/144 ≈ 0.1181`. This confirms that the test is not declaring all rivals indistinguishable. Its single binary label for `e` is **not** a model of the qualitative character of real experiences. The general adaptive statement follows from the chain rule; 32 finite comparisons do not establish an all-protocol empirical result.

Experience in this argument is not a simulation or a mere graph label: the comparison **assumes the same actual experiences in both metaphysical descriptions**. The code tests the logical consequence of equality of their stipulated data kernels; it does not implement or explain consciousness. Nor is there a cosmic lottery choosing which actuality exists: probabilities in the example are operational likelihoods/credences, and may be interpreted as descriptions of local outcomes within the actual whole.

### Why simply intervening on “the ground” is not a shortcut

One could write structural equations with an arrow `E → P` in A and `P → E` in a rival, then imagine independently setting either variable and observe that the equations give different answers. That would assume that a physical experimental operation corresponds to the relevant **metaphysical intervention**, and that the arrow represents a causal mechanism. Neither follows from naming a grounding relation. Some authors develop interventionist accounts of grounding; others dispute that grounding explanations generally have this counterfactual form. Compare [Wilson](https://alastairwilson.org/files/mc111114.pdf) and the [critical discussion of grounding and counterfactual explanation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11098-020-01518-8). This is a live research question, not a blanket ban on using counterfactuals.

The operational obligation is to state which manipulations are physically available, how they map to changes in the proposed constitution, what each theory predicts for actual experiences and records, and why those mappings are justified. Under the user's global possibility–actuality reading, ordinary metaphysical counterfactuals about unrealized total worlds also need careful semantics; a formal intervention is not automatically a genuinely possible alternative.

## 4. An honest parsimony comparison

The experiential proposal's strongest **positive** simplicity claim is that a known kind of actuality—experience—may do the work of the intrinsic base and avoid adding a separate neutral primitive. The strongest competing consideration is that a neutral constitution may generate both phenomenal and physical aspects under one unifying principle, without reducing either to the other. Neither advantage is established until the relevant rules are explicit and independently constrained.

A ledger for any comparison should count, without equating unlike items mechanically:

| Obligation | Experiential A | Neutral B |
|---|---|---|
| Intrinsic primitive | Explain why the experiential base is actual and what it contains. | Explain why the neutral base is actual and what it is. |
| Physical recovery | Give `F` and show it reproduces measured physics. | Give its physical constitution and the same recovery. |
| Phenomenal incidence and quality | Give `S`, subject unity and qualitative content; “experiential” alone does not fix a person's boundaries or redness. | Explain how genuinely phenomenal subjects and qualities are constituted, not just labeled. |
| Modality | Defend necessity and the intended actuality principle. | Defend its own necessity/modal account; the rival is not granted these for free. |
| Records and discrimination | Reproduce reports, interventions and local clocks; identify a divergence if one exists. | Meet the same evidence and divergence requirements. |

**One word versus two is not a complexity proof.** The verifier includes two prefix-free toy description codes for the same pair of hypotheses. If one code gives lengths 1 and 2 bits, normalized `2⁻length` weights are `2/3` versus `1/3`; swapping the code assignment reverses them. This demonstrates the dependence of this very small coding exercise on its chosen language; it is not a general theorem that all reasonable simplicity measures are arbitrary or that the full theories are equally complex. A real comparison needs a common, predeclared representation, detailed candidate laws, encoding of all free parameters and predictive performance. Elegant compression can be evidence within justified assumptions, but it is not an automatic deduction of necessary experiential existence.

## 5. Implications for the formal battery and next constructive test

- **B1, concrete/necessary actuality:** actual experience remains the starting datum. Neither A's experiential fundamentality nor its necessity has been derived. The new comparison narrows *what* must be defended: not experience's occurrence, but the direction and content of constitution.
- **B2, strongest modality:** assuming the global schema and actual experience conditionally yields necessary experience, as already shown. A/B matching evidence does not prove that schema, and the two formal rivals are not asserted to be genuine co-possibilities.
- **B4–B5, subjects and quality:** the shared-history stipulation is deliberately neutral about the correct incidence rule. Both models inherit the need for a rule that handles plural subjects, unity, persistence and qualitative differences, rather than presuming that one experience label equals one subject.
- **B6–B8 and B10:** the future `F` must meet the existing local-time, instrument, gravity and empirical constraints. It must explain the [negative EEG transfer result](ROY_S08_FIXED_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md) without turning report prediction into an experience detector, and meet applicable [collapse-model bounds](UNDERGROUND_RADIATION_COLLAPSE_MODEL_AUDIT.md) if it actually introduces those dynamics. The present toy is not a physical model.
- **B9/B11–B12:** no measure, likelihood for the actual whole, independently calibrated discriminating prediction or total-cost ranking is derived here. The finite codebook is a warning about selection, not a measure.

**Next concrete bridge:** choose one already-developed subject/record interface and define a *single* constitution rule capable of taking a physically specified preparation and interventions to (i) a physical record distribution and (ii) a separately specified phenomenal contrast. Freeze the rule and a neutral-base rival on the same domain. Give a held-out criterion that would discriminate their *predictions*, or prove their admitted predictions identical and identify what independent metaphysical premise would choose between them. The rule must handle symmetry, subject plurality and changing representation, not assume a subject count or an experience-to-report calibration. This tests whether the positive proposal can actually recover more with fewer independent assumptions; a successful fit would still not prove necessary existence.
