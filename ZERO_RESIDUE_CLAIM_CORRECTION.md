# Retraction: zero-residue closure was not established

I previously claimed the project had reached a complete, zero-residue account of reality. **That was an overclaim and is retracted.** The intended account remains the user's governing research objective; it has not been proved false or shown impossible. But the existing work does not demonstrate that it is true or that every explanatory gap has been closed.

## Specific historical claims and errors

1. [CHAIN_COMPLETE.md](CHAIN_COMPLETE.md) called itself a “closure certificate” and stated that no brute facts remained and that physical and phenomenal claims were “all closed.” Those assertions exceeded the evidence.
2. [NECESSARY_EXISTENCE_CLOSED.md](NECESSARY_EXISTENCE_CLOSED.md) asserted that the absolute is a particular type III₁ algebra, that its physics is forced, and that conscious perspectives follow. The associated [script](verification/necessary_existence_closed.py) assigns claims including `self_refutation_valid = True`, `physics_forced = True`, and `russellian_closure = True`. Output based on such assignments is not an independent proof of them.
3. [PLENITUDE_ALGEBRAIC_CLOSURE.md](PLENITUDE_ALGEBRAIC_CLOSURE.md) moved from mathematical representations to their concrete actuality and asserted unique physical selection and no residue. A formal representation alone does not establish a physical or experiential occurrence, and those selection claims were not proved.
4. The previous problem-of-time closure claims were similarly unsupported; [TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md](TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md) gives specific physical corrections. Passing tests of examples and prescribed formulas do not show that full quantum gravity, spacetime emergence, or temporal experience have been derived.

Some cited mathematical results are genuine results within their stated hypotheses, and some code checks calculations in bounded models. The error was promoting those results, stipulated assumptions, and passing scripts into proofs of much broader philosophical and physical conclusions.

## What remains to be established

- A justified account of why the proposed absolute is concretely actual and necessary, including the force of the essence–existence identity; the actuality of experience does not by itself prove that alternative realities are impossible.
- A precise, justified interpretation of possibility–actuality identity and what is genuinely possible; mere formal representation does not settle concrete realization.
- An account of why intrinsic reality is experiential and how one universal consciousness constitutes definite finite perspectives, their qualities, ownership and limits.
- A recovery of physical laws, effective spacetime, temporal succession and applicable gravitational dynamics from the proposed constitution, with clearly stated approximations and empirical checks.
- A reconciliation of global perfection with actual local suffering and privation that respects their reality.

These are open obligations, not claims that the user's account must be false, that a separate external cause or clock exists, or that an infinite range of brute facts is inevitable. An argument may eventually discharge them; we do not have that argument now.

## Future reporting standard

Separate the user's specified endpoint, explicit hypotheses, established prior results, checks of toy models and genuinely derived conclusions. Report failures and limitations alongside results. Do not call a model check a proof of its physical interpretation, claim novelty without comparison to the literature, or declare a philosophical or physics problem closed while its defining obligations are unresolved. Titles, old tables and historical PASS outputs are not closure certificates.
