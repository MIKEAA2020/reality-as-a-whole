# Realization, perspective, and justified self-location

## The strongest surviving thesis

> A whole-reality account must explain concrete occurrence, phenomenal constitution, and the individuation of perspectives without hiding independent assumptions inside a unified label. Bostrom is useful because his argument exposes those dependencies: probability requires a population, a population requires distinct realization occurrences, and occurrence identity cannot be recovered merely by minimizing a selected behavioral description. Conditional causal and computational constructions can identify organization relative to specified interventions, observations, interfaces, and temporal protocols. They do not thereby identify subjects or their epistemic weights. When those inputs leave multiple admissible answers, the proper result is explicit, model-relative non-identification—not an invented boundary, count, or probability. This preserves, rather than abandons, the search for complete intelligibility.

The central technical result is a distinction between **quotienting alternative states of a model** and **individuating actual realization occurrences**. The central methodological result is that suspension can be warranted at an intermediate stage without proving that ultimate explanation is impossible or nonunique.

## 1. The governing ambition and the discovery method

The settled aim is maximum intelligibility of reality as a whole: no exempted brute fact, unexplained constitutive link, or merely relocated explanatory burden. Fidelity to Bostrom is a discovery guide and correspondence test, not a veto on necessary revision.

The uniqueness-and-elegance conjecture is best formulated as a research hypothesis: completion may converge on one explanatory framework, up to genuine explanatory equivalence, whose economy lies in reducing independent assumptions. Neither verbal brevity nor a unique mathematical quotient establishes such convergence.

The search should therefore preserve useful distinctions until a specific explanatory failure requires their revision. In particular, it should not assume in advance that:

- every grounded process is simulated;
- all possible programs are actual;
- only one subject exists;
- reality is fundamentally computational;
- an algorithmic prior is the correct observer measure;
- or a unique numerical answer must exist for every self-location question.

A unique complete framework could explain why some local classifications or inference problems lack a uniquely warranted answer. Local model-relative nonuniqueness neither proves nor refutes the global uniqueness conjecture.

## 2. Realization is broader than simulation

An implemented observer, if genuinely conscious, has a real experience. Implementation need not make an occurrence less real, but it does not make all reality a simulation.

**Being grounded or constituted is not the same relation as being computationally simulated.** A framework that renames every occurrence “simulated” obtains a verbal probability of one by erasing the distinction the original question concerned.

The meaningful correspondence task is narrower: explain when genuine simulation relations and distinct conscious realizations exist, then recover the conditional demographic inference where its population and weighting assumptions are justified.

Any host belongs within total reality. That prevents an external simulator from serving as an explanation of everything while escaping scrutiny itself. It does not establish a self-explanatory absolute, necessary existence, or experiential ontology merely by including every real process in the whole.

## 3. Three connected tasks are not yet one explanation

The programme concerns:

1. **Actuality:** why determinate concrete occurrence obtains.
2. **Experience:** why a particular occurrence is phenomenal and what fixes its unity and character.
3. **Perspectival inference:** what a perspective’s information licenses it to believe about its situation.

There are earned connections. An actual pain is not first a merely possible experience that needs a separate event to make it actual. And restricted access can leave several situations compatible with one perspective’s evidence, even within a fully determinate reality.

But these observations do not establish that all actuality is experiential, that appearing exists necessarily, that computation constitutes feeling, or that compatible situations receive equal epistemic weights.

The proposed unification must explain those links. Collecting an ontology, a consciousness assignment, and a measure into one object called “the absolute” does not do so. Conversely, one explanatory framework need not make actuality, experience, and probability identical phenomena. The ambition is a justified relation among their explanatory roles.

## 4. Type, occurrence, identity, and continuation

A single abstract trajectory is compatible with one execution described twice or two separate executions with the same abstract history. Conditional on genuine duplication producing another realization, abstract type alone cannot determine numerical multiplicity.

This does not refute computational accounts. It shows that a computational type must be connected to an occurrence account rather than silently treated as its population.

A disciplined realization theory must distinguish:

- alternative descriptions of one occurrence;
- genuinely additional occurrences of one type;
- different roles supported by overlapping machinery;
- another implementation layer describing the same role;
- another process actually running within that layer;
- continuation, splitting, merging, and overlap.

An equivalence relation cannot encode unrestricted branching identity. If a predecessor is equivalent to both distinct successors, symmetry and transitivity identify the successors. Directed continuation should therefore not be collapsed into description-equivalence. This is a bookkeeping constraint, not a verdict about personal survival through fission.

The initial anchored normal-form construction is useful at this limited scope. Within a supplied concrete frame, matching event blocks, active-cut trajectory, stride, and decoding kernel can certify the same computational role-segment. Its dependence on that frame is explicit. It does not discover event occurrences, prove completeness as an occurrence-identity criterion, or identify subjects.

In particular, failure of its equivalence test does not entail two experiences. Its exact matching requirements can be stricter than the eventual constitutive relation should be; additional irrelevant background differences may remain in the supplied trajectory. The construction is a sufficient certificate within its declared domain, not the final ontology of realization.

## 5. Conditional causal structure: what can genuinely be derived

### Intervention structure can distinguish a factorization

Bare identity dynamics on four states admits three binary factorizations exchanged by its symmetries. A rule invariant under all those symmetries cannot select one of them without more structure.

A specified algebra of independent coordinate resets can distinguish one factorization up to factor and value renaming. The reset monoid does selection work even when its operations are not individually labeled.

This reduces dependence on notation. It does not derive the intervention algebra itself. Passive renaming must also be distinguished from choosing a different physical intervention structure.

### Minimal causal envelopes are target-relative

In a deterministic full-product network with coordinate interventions, define an influence edge when changing one input coordinate can change an output coordinate. The ancestor closure of a target is the unique smallest autonomous coordinate set containing it.

This is an exact theorem under its assumptions. Restricted or correlated state domains, different intervention families, stochastic dynamics, or different notions of autonomy require separate analysis.

The theorem determines what a target depends on, not which target is experiential or which dependencies are constitutive. A causal envelope is not automatically a subject boundary.

### Given ports, factorization can be canonical

For supplied external ports, weak components of the remaining internal dependency graph determine the finest conditional product factorization in the stated deterministic product setting.

But conditional autonomy is not constitutive identity. A controller can be structurally unique without the structure proving whether it belongs inside the realization or is enabling machinery outside it.

“Parameter-free” also does not mean “assumption-free.” Minimizing externalization and maximizing conditional factorization can be coefficient-free yet select different answers. Their philosophical authority must be justified independently.

## 6. The two decisive cases

### A. Processes move; storage sites need not define them

For

\[
F(x,y,z)=(y,z,x),
\]

one-step fixed-site dependence links all three registers, while \(F^3\) is identity. A fixed-location autonomy criterion therefore changes under temporal sampling.

The unrolled event graph instead exposes three independent lanes moving through the storage sites. On the restricted finite DAG class with indegree and outdegree at most one, maximal paths form unique thread components. Branches and merges fall outside that certificate: maximal paths can then overlap and cannot simply be counted as separate occurrences.

Coarsening can also invent apparent paths. Merging the intermediate nodes of two separate paths admits cross-combinations that lift to neither original execution. Preserving endpoint adjacency is therefore weaker than preserving provenance. Conversely, a coarse description can erase genuine multiplicity without inventing paths.

These examples constrain an occurrence theory. They do not establish one subject per thread, experiential invariance under every physical subdivision, or a universal temporal grain.

### B. Shared control does not decide constitutive status

For

\[
F(x,y,c)=(x\oplus c,y\oplus c,c),
\]

\(c\) is structurally distinctive. Yet the specified source-autonomy constraints permit four finest interface annotations:

| Ports | Internal blocks |
|---|---|
| none | \(\{x,y,c\}\) |
| \(\{c\}\) | \(\{x\},\{y\}\) |
| \(\{x,c\}\) | \(\{y\}\) |
| \(\{y,c\}\) | \(\{x\}\) |

The constraints therefore do not decide whether control is an internal constituent or an external enabling interface. These are alternative annotations, not four actual populations.

The point is not that a selector is mathematically impossible: several selectors are available. The missing result is a justified link from a distinctive causal role to its constitutive significance.

## 7. The central limit: predictive minimization does not count occurrences

For a finite deterministic machine with fixed inputs, outputs, state family, and step protocol, define two states as equivalent when every finite input word produces the same observation history.

The behavioral quotient is the greatest observation-respecting transition congruence. It yields a minimal predictive machine unique up to interface-preserving isomorphism for the selected behavior family. An initial-state restriction, if used, must be stated through its reachable family.

This is a genuine standard uniqueness result. It removes unnecessary predictive distinctions **after** the interface is fixed; it does not derive which interface matters.

### Hidden-dynamics lemma

Let \(A\) be a machine, and append nonempty hidden dynamics \(Z\) in an uncoupled product. Observe only \(A\). For corresponding states and every allowed input history, the product and the original have identical selected behavior. Under the stated product/surjectivity assumptions,

\[
\operatorname{Min}(A\times Z)\cong\operatorname{Min}(A).
\]

The hidden system may be static memory, irrelevant to that interface, or another complete realization of \(A\). Neither case is discriminated by the selected predictive quotient.

Therefore:

> Invisible to one complete interface is not equivalent to globally absent, nonphenomenal, or irrelevant to the total realization population.

This is a limit on the information preserved by that abstraction. It is not a proof that all possible causal evidence is insufficient, or that a richer full implementation model cannot distinguish copies.

### Exact counting incompatibility

Suppose a finite-valued nonnegative count \(N\) obeys both:

\[
N(A\times A)=N(A)
\]

from unrestricted invariance under the selected local abstraction, and

\[
N(A\times A)=2N(A)
\]

from genuine duplicate preservation. Then \(N(A)=0\). A nonzero finite count cannot obey both requirements.

The finite-value qualification matters: the same algebra does not exclude extended infinite values or infinite cardinalities satisfying \(2\kappa=\kappa\). More fundamentally, duplicate preservation applies only after a process has been independently established as observer-realizing and the product represents genuinely distinct occurrences. The toggle examples do not establish consciousness.

The result rules out the **particular unrestricted invariance-plus-duplication rule**, not every possible population measure.

## 8. The two maps that must not be conflated

The distinction can be expressed precisely:

\[
q:S_{\mathrm{implementation}}\longrightarrow S_{\mathrm{predictive\ quotient}},
\]

versus

\[
\tau:\Omega_{\mathrm{actual\ realizations}}
\longrightarrow\mathcal T_{\mathrm{organizational\ types}}.
\]

The first groups alternative **states** by their selected behavior. The second assigns a type to an actual **occurrence**. Many occurrences may instantiate one type, while many states may describe alternatives of one compound system.

Consequently, quotient-fiber sizes, numbers of machine states, and reachable-state counts are not observer counts. Enlarging hidden memory can enlarge fibers without adding a candidate execution. Synchronizing two executions can reduce reachable alternatives without making their occurrence supports numerically one.

The supplied formal work makes \(q\) precise relative to its inputs. It does not derive the actuality of \(\Omega\), its complete membership, phenomenal realization, or \(\tau\). Providing event labels in a model is not yet an explanation of why those modeled occurrences are actual.

## 9. Probability requires more than equal reality or equal evidence

Suppose one base observer and 99 simulated observers share relevant evidence. For every \(a\in[0,1]\),

\[
\mu(b)=a,\qquad \mu(s_i)=(1-a)/99
\]

is normalized and symmetric among the simulated observers. It yields simulation weight \(1-a\).

This proves insufficiency of those weak constraints, not rational permissibility of every assignment. Further epistemic principles may narrow the family.

The finite counting lemma is valid if distinct, intrinsically equivalent realizations have the same positive finite weight, those weights add, and conditional probabilities are obtained by normalization. A subset of \(k\) out of \(N\) then has probability \(k/N\).

But the conclusions must not outrun the assumptions:

- Equal reality does not establish equal rational weight.
- Absence of an ego-allocation mechanism does not establish indifference.
- Intrinsic equivalence is stronger than merely sharing evidence.
- A result for exact duplicates does not establish equal weighting for a heterogeneous human-type population.
- Subject, life, episode, observer-moment, execution, and duration are different contribution units.
- Infinite equal per-instance weights may not normalize.

Recovering Bostrom’s ratio therefore requires an established relevant population, an explicit contribution unit, and justified weighting. The simplified demographic formula additionally requires appropriate assumptions about observer yields; raw simulation counts alone need not suffice.

Explaining why no uniquely warranted global probability follows from a particular information state can be a legitimate result. Merely failing to find a measure is not, by itself, such an explanation.

## 10. What the presentation-family construction establishes

The final construction tests partitions stable under the supplied transformations. In the full binary-product setting, stability under every coordinate reset restricts these to equality on a coordinate subset. Adding dynamics further restricts which subsets support autonomous fixed quotients.

For the rotating-register model:

- Only constant and full-state fixed presentations survive at primitive stride.
- Time-dependent readouts can follow any of seven nonempty subsets of the three lanes.
- At stride three, all eight coordinate-subset partitions survive relative to that endpoint protocol.

The three threads and seven readout candidates count different mathematical objects. Neither is an established number of experiences. Nor is the seven-element family an enumeration of every imaginable phenomenal organization: it is the family generated by the specified construction.

For shared control, the stable supports are

\[
\varnothing,\{c\},\{x,c\},\{y,c\},\{x,y,c\}.
\]

The smallest nonconstant presentation is the controller, while the finest is the whole state. Neither mathematical extremum thereby becomes the experiential unit.

This is conditional progress: causal operations determine a structured family without a fitted numerical threshold. The operation family and its relevance to intrinsic experience remain unexplained.

## 11. Actual appearing: a starting datum, not an exemption

An attempted experiential denial presupposes an occurring experience. But

\[
E,\quad\Box(D\rightarrow E)
\]

does not entail \(\Box E\). A normal modal frame can satisfy the actual occurrence and the denial conditional while containing an accessible world with neither denial nor appearing.

That is a logical countermodel to one inference. It does not establish that the alternative is a metaphysically possible total reality, nor refute a richer argument for necessary appearing. Equally, an alternative model with necessary appearing shows that these weak premises do not establish nonnecessity either.

Actual history also need not determine counterfactual dynamics. Distinct machines can match a witnessed trace while diverging under untried inputs. Even complete behavior at one selected interface leaves uncoupled hidden extensions possible in a model class admitting them.

The surviving conclusion is limited and constructive: actual appearing secures the starting occurrence and can supply evidence, but the tested arguments have not derived its necessity, unique organization, or total realization population. Appearing remains within the explanatory demand rather than an exempt brute foundation.

## 12. Principled suspension is the intermediate result

For a declared class \(K\) of admissible models and a quantity \(Q\), define

\[
\operatorname{Id}_K(Q)=\{Q(k):k\in K\}.
\]

A singleton gives point identification in that class. Multiple values give non-point-identification. An empty set may indicate an empty or inconsistent chosen class, not necessarily inconsistency of reality.

An identification set is neither a population nor a probability distribution. Uniformly weighting candidate annotations would add a new assumption and could depend on arbitrary representation choices.

The final suspension result is supported at this scope:

- Some structural relations are identifiable.
- Some annotation and presentation families are enumerable.
- The tested constraints do not determine phenomenal selectors or epistemic weights.
- More justified constitutive principles, richer evidence, or global physical constraints might narrow the families.

The remaining substantive inputs are explicit:

**intervention structure; target/observation; ports; event grain and provenance; temporal protocol.**

Removing their labels or canonically processing them does not derive their constitutive status. Conversely, listing five inputs does not prove five irreducible metaphysical primitives. An eventual common principle might explain several together.

This is successful scope control, not completion of zero residue. The final report appropriately avoids the separate constitutive transcript’s unsupported claim that zero residue is impossible.

## 13. What would advance the work

The next result should add independently defended content, not another optimizer that forces a preferred output.

1. **State the constitutive bridge precisely.** Which aspect of actual occurrence is claimed to select experienced distinctions, rather than merely behaviorally useful ones?
2. **Specify what changes the model class.** Actual resource bounds, physically admissible couplings, global evidence, or a defended occurrence ontology may exclude hidden extensions. State their costs and scope.
3. **Separate interface selection from interface minimization.** Derive why an observation family is intrinsically relevant, or report its residual identification set.
4. **Test both decisive cases together.** A criterion must follow processes through moving storage while distinguishing shared enabling conditions from constituents without assuming the answer.
5. **Keep phenomenal and epistemic evidence distinct.** A calibrated experiential association can help identify a readout; it does not automatically explain that association or select rational weights.
6. **Retain suspension where justified.** Further research may explain the unresolved distinctions, or explain why they are not genuine determinate targets. Neither conclusion should be forced beforehand.

The long-term success condition remains that the account explaining concrete actuality also explains experiential organization, occurrence individuation, and the warranted scope of self-location—without borrowing those as independent unexplained inputs.

## Final formulation

> Pursue a maximally intelligible account through concrete realization rather than a universal simulation metaphor. Preserve genuine occurrences, distinguish them from their types and descriptions, and recover demographic self-location only where population and weights are justified. Causal envelopes, path provenance, behavioral quotients, and stable presentation families provide conditional structure; none alone establishes a phenomenal subject. A selected interface can conceal additional executions, so predictive equivalence cannot serve as unrestricted global count invariance. Return identified facts and explicit families where the current constraints stop, while continuing the constitutive and actuality investigation. Uniqueness and explanatory elegance remain goals to demonstrate, not premises that authorize a forced answer.

**Audit verdict:** The q/τ distinction, the restricted abstraction–duplication incompatibility, the conditional causal constructions, and model-relative suspension survive strongly. The work has not unified actuality, experience, and probability into one completed explanation. It also has not disproved that possibility.
