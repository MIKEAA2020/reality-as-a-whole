# Complete self-knowledge without a semantically total self-model

## 1. Result and role in the constitution

The governing absolute is completely self-knowing. That must not be replaced by a stronger and potentially inconsistent requirement: a classical self-model that assigns a truth value to every sentence about its own knowledge, with unrestricted self-reference.

A standard Knower-paradox argument makes the difference exact. Complete factive knowledge is incompatible with one particular negative self-referential fixed point. It does not follow that complete knowledge is incompatible with self-knowledge in general. A separate, general interpretation below permits arbitrarily iterated knowledge of knowledge without adding a second knower.

This develops the existing semantic qualification in [complete awareness and finite disclosure](COMPLETE_AWARENESS_FINITE_DISCLOSURE.md). It does not identify the concrete essence of awareness or derive an aware absolute. The aim is to justify the qualification rather than leave it as an unexplained exemption from the zero-residue demand. The argument is standard logical reasoning, not a novel theorem or a consciousness experiment.

## 2. Three claims that cannot all hold

Fix classical bivalent propositional reasoning. Let Kp mean that A knows p. Distinguish:

1. **Factivity:** Kp implies p.
2. **Truth-completeness:** p implies Kp, for every proposition p in the claimed domain.
3. **Negative epistemic self-reference:** there is a proposition L in that same domain satisfying L if and only if not KL.

These three claims are jointly inconsistent.

**Proof.** Suppose KL. Factivity gives L. The defining equivalence then gives not KL, a contradiction. Hence not KL. The other direction of the equivalence now gives L. Truth-completeness gives KL, again a contradiction.

No premise says the knower is a finite machine, effectively axiomatized, physically bounded, or unable to step outside a formal system. This is not an application of Goedel incompleteness. It concerns a proposed proposition that negates knowledge of itself.

Conversely, the contradiction does not follow merely from factivity and truth-completeness: the self-referential fixed point is an additional, load-bearing assumption. A theory cannot conclude from the inconsistency that every conception of an omniscient being is inconsistent without defending that assumption.

## 3. A constructive interpretation of iterated self-knowledge

Let P be any nonempty set of propositional atoms and v0 an assignment of true or false to them. Form the well-founded, finite expressions generated from P by Boolean connectives and a unary operator K. Their collection need not be finite; K may be iterated to arbitrary finite depth.

Define interpretation compositionally:

    v(p)       = v0(p) for atoms p,
    v(not phi) = not v(phi),
    v(phi and psi) = v(phi) and v(psi),
    v(K phi)   = v(phi).

Structural recursion on finite expression trees makes v well-defined. It follows for every expression phi that K phi and phi have the same truth value. Thus factivity and truth-completeness both hold in this presentation. Also:

    v(K K phi) = v(K phi) = v(phi).

There is no extra knower observing the first knower; the same K is applied again. True facts about false propositions being unknown are covered too: when v(phi) is false, v(not K phi) and v(K not K phi) are true.

The language supports arbitrarily iterated knowledge-about-knowledge. It does not thereby contain a proposition satisfying L iff not KL. Under this interpretation such a proposition would require v(L) = not v(L), which has no classical bivalent solution.

This is a general semantic interpretation, not an assertion that knowledge is literally a Boolean truth value or that truth by itself constitutes acquaintance. Extensional equivalence of K phi and phi does not prove an identity between the concrete natures of knowing and being. The interpretation supplies a consistency witness for the stated formal claims, not a physical or phenomenal realization.

**Scope restriction:** this construction is not a complete semantics for natural language, arithmetic with an unrestricted predicate about sentence codes, or all metaphysical facts. Adding quotation, semantic predicates, quantification, and diagonalization requires a new analysis. Its v0 assignment is supplied, not derived. Consequently this is not a proof that the intended absolute exists, that all actual truths have been captured, or that the constitution is explanatory rather than stipulated.

## 4. Why rejecting one fixed point need not hide an unknown fact

A sentence token, an attempted truth condition, and a coherently interpreted bivalent proposition are not interchangeable.

If an utterance attempts to specify L iff not KL under the first two conditions, the proof shows that the attempted specification has no bivalent solution. The result is not that there is a determinate true proposition L that A fails to know while retaining all three assumptions. It is that the three assumptions cannot be jointly satisfied.

There are several possible responses: limit the semantic closure, revise the knowledge principles, or investigate a nonclassical interpretation. The governing account preserves factivity and truth-completeness for genuine truths and does not assume that every self-referential sentence supplies such a proposition. This choice requires an adequate semantic theory; merely calling awkward sentences meaningless is not enough.

The choice has an independently demonstrated reason in this case: the proposed joint truth conditions are inconsistent. It is not justified by a desire to protect a favored cosmic subject. Nor does the argument prove that every kind of self-reference is illegitimate. Some positive or otherwise constrained self-referential conditions can be consistent.

An account may explain why a sentence fails to receive the attempted interpretation. That explanatory claim is about the sentence and its conditions; it is not an assignment of a truth value to the failed fixed point. If one tries to pack every such metalinguistic fact into a single self-applicable total semantic predicate, revenge-style paradoxes must be addressed rather than presumed solved. The simple interpretation above is not a general solution to semantic paradox.

Semantic distinctions and typing would be internal features of an adequate whole-account, not an external knower or an ontological reality outside the absolute. Their consistency, applicability, and adequacy remain part of what the account must explain. A local language and its interpretation must not be mistaken for a completed model of everything.

## 5. Consequence for self-presence and self-modelling

Complete self-knowledge cannot be required, without further qualification, to take the form of a semantically total classical self-model closed under the negative fixed point above. That architecture is excluded by the three-condition proof.

It does **not** follow that every representational theory is false, that reflective cognition is impossible, or that nonrepresentational awareness has been proved. A representational account may use a typed language, a restricted syntax, or non-linguistic structures. The constructive interpretation itself is representational. The result rules out an overstrong specification, not representation in general.

The positive whole-account commitment remains:

> Complete self-presence is complete acquaintance with actuality, not a promise that every string claiming to describe actuality denotes a coherent fact. Reflective self-models, where they occur, are internally constituted ways of describing that actuality; they are not automatically the ground of its qualitative presence.

The acquaintance claim remains a substantive constitutive conjecture. The logic prevents one inappropriate requirement from being imposed on it; it does not derive the claim from logical consistency.

## 6. A strict limit on dissolving explanatory questions

This distinction must not be abused to dismiss genuine explanatory debts. The following questions have not been shown to have inconsistent truth conditions:

- Why is a particular concrete process qualitative acquaintance?
- What constitutes its local ownership and qualitative articulation?
- Why do the actual laws, states, and finite scopes obtain?
- How is complete perfection compatible with actual local privation?

They remain live. Failure to answer them is not evidence that they are Knower-style semantic failures. A successful dissolution must exhibit the alleged incoherent assumptions and justify the alternative, rather than merely call the question a category error.

Likewise, knowing that a real privation occurs is not fulfillment of what is due. Nothing in the formal knowledge construction excuses cruelty or proves global goodness.

## 7. Position reached

The earlier warning about unrestricted truth predicates now has an explicit argument and a contrasting interpretation. Self-knowledge need not create an endless hierarchy of independently experiencing observers. Complete factive knowledge and unrestricted negative epistemic fixed points cannot simply be conjoined.

This is work on a load-bearing feature of the absolute conjecture, not a derivation of necessary existence, a concrete nature, physical law, or finite acquaintance. It does not repair the failed attempt to identify ownership through generic self-regulation. No software count, new synthetic experience label, or claim to the requested non-incremental metaphysical breakthrough is attached.
