# An unregulated internal-clock POVM in one FRW branch

## What is established

Within a specified, unregulated **one-branch flat FRW minisuperspace model**, the positive-momentum POVM used by van Neerven and Portal can be interpreted as a measurement of the geometric reading relative to the scalar clock. There is an explicit unitary change between the scalar-clock and geometric-clock reduced descriptions, with matching probabilities and the correct Jacobian. This is a concrete application of known constructions, not a claim to have discovered the clock POVM or solved the full problem of time; its novelty has not been established by a comprehensive literature review.

The matching also shows **what is not established**: the algebraic flow generated by an externally selected generalized weight has the correct covariance on these effects, but the weight is not a normalized normal physical state and its inverse-temperature scale β is not determined by the gravitational constraint. The published construction's open Page–Wootters/weight questions are not thereby solved.

## 1. Prior results used, not claimed as discoveries

- [Höhn, Smith and Lock, *Equivalence of approaches to relational quantum dynamics in relativistic settings*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00580) use clock POVMs and frequency-sector-wise relational reductions; their class includes flat massless-scalar FRW. The reduction and clock-change methods are established prior work, not invented here.
- [Van Neerven and Portal, *Thermal time as an unsharp observable*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13774), Theorem 6.1, construct a compressed-position POVM for the positive-frequency sector of |D|, and a covariant generalized modular time. Their paper distinguishes that construction from ordinary normalized-state Tomita–Takesaki theory and lists its embedding and Page–Wootters interpretation as open problems.
- The scalar-clock branch and its non-normalizable Gibbs operator are summarized in [FRW_MODULAR_CLOCK.md](FRW_MODULAR_CLOCK.md). Finite clock-window limitations appear in [FRW_CLOCK_WINDOW_MISMATCH.md](FRW_CLOCK_WINDOW_MISMATCH.md).

The relevant new *work in this repository* is an explicit mapping of those ingredients to this particular branch and wavepacket, with sign conventions, clock-frame Jacobian, and limitations written out. Whether this mapping or example is new in the research literature is unknown. The search has not ruled out prior equivalent results.

## 2. Constraint and physical sector

Use the branch and ordering of FRW_MODULAR_CLOCK.md: α=log(a), κ=1/√6 in units 8πG=ħ=1, p=p_α>0, and p_φ=-κp. The rescaled linear branch constraint is

    C = p_φ + κp_α = 0.

The chosen positive-p sector of the reduced physical Hilbert space is H_phys=L²(R₊,dp). For a dense class of smooth compactly supported momentum wavefunctions, group averaging the linear constraint gives ∫₀∞|ψ(p)|²dp, up to overall convention; extend by completion. If starting from the quadratic rescaled constraint instead, the delta-function measure includes a factor (2κp)^(-1), removable by the corresponding half-density map away from p=0. This is an explicit branch/order/inner-product choice, not a proof that all quantizations or the original unrescaled ADM constraint are equivalent.

With Fourier convention

    f(x) = (1/√(2π)) ∫₀∞ e^(ipx) ψ(p) dp,
    U(φ)ψ(p) = e^(-iκpφ)ψ(p),

f is understood as the L² Fourier transform of the zero extension to negative p. The scalar-frame wavefunction is f(x−κφ). The unreduced “history” on the real scalar line is generalized rather than a normalized kinematical vector; physical probabilities are normalized on each reduced slice, not by a uniform distribution over the whole clock line.

## 3. A gauge-invariant geometric effect

On the kinematical space define Z=α−κφ. The constraint generates simultaneous translations of φ and α at rates 1 and κ, so Z is invariant. More precisely its spectral effects commute strongly with the unitary group generated by C. Let P₊ project onto p_α>0; P₊ also commutes with C. Hence the bounded compressed effects

    P₊ 1_B(Z) P₊

are constraint-compatible, for Borel B⊂R. In the scalar-clock physical frame this descends to

    E(B) = P₊ 1_B(X) P₊ |_(H₊),
    E_φ(B) = U(φ)† E(B) U(φ) = E(B−κφ),

where X is multiplication by x on L²(R,dx), and H₊ is the positive-momentum subspace. E is a normalized POVM, not generally a projection-valued position measure or a self-adjoint canonical time operator on H₊; the cited paper supplies the POVM result. For normalized ψ, the geometric probability at scalar reading φ is

    P(x∈B | φ) = <ψ|E_φ(B)|ψ> = ∫_B |f(x−κφ)|² dx.

Here x denotes α, so a depends on the fiducial scale convention. The equation describes geometric conditional statistics within this idealized model; it does not demonstrate a measurable laboratory clock or resolve both frequency sectors.

## 4. Explicit reciprocal clock-frame map

Let q=-p_φ=κp>0. For each geometric reading x define

    (W_x ψ)(q) = κ^(-1/2) exp(i qx/κ) ψ(q/κ).

The change of variable q=κp proves W_x is unitary from L²(R₊,dp) to L²(R₊,dq). The reduced scalar wavefunction in the negative-p_φ sector is

    g_x(φ) = (1/√(2π)) ∫₀∞ exp(-iqφ)(W_xψ)(q)dq
           = √κ f(x−κφ),

in L², with pointwise formulas valid for sufficiently regular packets. The scalar coordinate measurement is likewise the compressed coordinate POVM in its negative-momentum sector. Therefore

    p_geom(x | φ) = |f(x−κφ)|²,
    p_scalar(φ | x) = κ |f(x−κφ)|².

Each density integrates to one in its own reading; the factor κ is the change-of-variable Jacobian. These are probabilities in two separately reduced frames, not a normalized joint density over the infinite gauge direction and not an assumption of a uniform physical clock prior. They are one-time readout statistics only: a sequential measurement instrument, backreaction, or a complete clock-change theorem for full gravity is not established by this equality.

## 5. Relation to the published generalized flow

The reduced Hamiltonian is h=κp, unitarily equivalent, up to the factor κ, to the positive-momentum part of the |D| Hamiltonian in van Neerven–Portal. For an imposed β>0, set T_β=exp(-βh). The paper's generalized flow has the corresponding algebraic action

    γ_s(A) = T_β^(is) A T_β^(-is)
           = U(βs) A U(βs)†
           = α_(-βs)(A),    α_t(A)=U(t)† A U(t).

With these conventions it acts on the geometric POVM by

    γ_s(E(B)) = E(B+κβs).

Thus the published generalized-flow covariance and this model's scalar-clock relational covariance are the **same operator transformation after rescaling**, on the specified effects. This is an application of their mathematical result, not a new independently constructed Tomita–Takesaki state.

Crucially, T_β is not trace-class on H_phys. It cannot be normalized as a density matrix, and β is supplied, not physically derived. The state-dependent modular flow of an actual normal physical preparation is not identified with γ. We have not proved that a physical internal-clock *instrument* produces the published weight, that its non-normal construction becomes an ordinary Tomita–Takesaki state, or that β is selected independently of relational dynamics. Those questions remain open. There is no contradiction with the finite-window mismatch: that mismatch concerns the modular flow of the selected finite-window **state**, a different reference object.

## 6. A normalizable wavepacket and testable formulas

For a width parameter a>0, take

    ψ(p)=2 a^(3/2) p exp(-ap),  p>0,
    f(x)= [2 a^(3/2)/√(2π)]/(a−ix)².

Both are normalized. The conditional densities above become

    p_geom(x | φ) = 2a³ / {π[a²+(x−κφ)²]²},
    p_scalar(φ | x) = κ p_geom(x | φ).

The geometric reading has mean κφ and variance a²; in the reciprocal frame the scalar reading has mean x/κ and variance a²/κ². For finite interval [l,r], the probability equals F(r−κφ)−F(l−κφ), where

    F(y)=1/2 + [arctan(y/a)+ay/(a²+y²)]/π.

These are calculated model distributions, not empirical predictions. The chosen packet has finite α variance but a power-law α tail; in particular the exponential volume moment ∫e^(3x)|f(x)|²dx diverges. It therefore does not establish finite expected physical volume or a semiclassical expanding universe. The checks independently integrate Fourier transforms and the probability densities, test the Jacobian, variance, interval shift and constraint equation for this packet.

## 7. Remaining research gate

The key unresolved step is an operational and algebraic one: specify an internal clock instrument and physically motivated state/weight on a suitable observable algebra, derive its conditional probabilities and modular action, and show under exactly what conditions they reproduce one another without imposing the desired flow. This must also handle the non-normal reference, multiple clock choices, other frequency sectors, and ultimately inhomogeneous constraints. The current model supplies a controlled test bed, not that derivation, a Shlaer comparison, or full quantum gravity.
