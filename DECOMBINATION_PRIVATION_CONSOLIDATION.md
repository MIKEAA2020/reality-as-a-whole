# Articulation without subject-summing: the proper role of privation

## 1. Surviving thesis

> **The perfect complete absolute must intrinsically ground every actual experiential difference. Finite experience can be real as appearance, mode, or bounded disclosure without being an independently existing substance. Its positive character and scope require a constitutive explanation. Privation describes an absent realization relative to that positive constitution; it is not another ingredient, a subject-producing power, or a demonstrated condition of the absolute's self-knowledge.**

This strengthens [necessary articulated unity](NECESSARY_ARTICULATED_UNITY.md) without claiming that subject incidence or qualitative identity has been derived. The preferred account is nonproductive: no prior cosmic subject literally breaks apart, becomes ignorant, or creates another independently actual reality. A finite mode's real limitation and the whole's complete self-knowledge are predicates of different scopes, not the same predicate both affirmed and denied.

The attachment supports this direction when read through its later corrections. Its strongest remaining proposal—using privation to explain the necessity of differentiation—needs qualification. **The dependence of lack on positive actuality does not entail the dependence of positive actuality or self-knowledge on actual lack.**

## 3. Combination and decombination: a conditional asymmetry, not a solved problem

Three questions must be distinguished:

- **Subject combination:** why do supposed smaller subjects constitute another unified subject?
- **Subject differentiation:** why does one experiential actuality include genuinely bounded local acquaintance or ownership?
- **Qualitative determination:** why do the resulting occurrences have these particular characters and relations?

A whole-first account can avoid *starting* with independent micro-subjects and then summing them. This is a genuine architectural advantage under the adopted unity requirement. It does not derive finite acquaintance, ownership, or privacy. The direction of explanation changes; the burden of explaining the actual differences remains.

The transcript's proper-parthood objection identifies a conditional symmetry: if both proposals require exactly the same unexplained subject-within-subject relation, reversing grounding direction does not by itself explain that relation. It does not prove that every whole-first account and every micro-first account face an identical problem. That requires checking their actual dependence and incidence claims.

Conversely, **ontological independence is not required for an individuation problem to arise**. Finite modes need not be independent substances for it still to be meaningful to ask why this content is locally present here and not there. The export repeatedly overstates the claim that decombination disappears once independent experiential atoms or literal fragments are rejected.

Preferred formulation:

> We reject literal subject-summing and subject-fission as the basic production story. We retain their legitimate explanandum: the intrinsic ground of actual qualitative differentiation, finite scope, and subject incidence.

This may remove an ill-posed composition mechanism without removing the need for a positive constitution. It is not a refusal to investigate finite subjects.

## 4. Appearance preserves a datum; it does not by itself explain it

An appearance is an actual appearing, even if an accompanying judgment about independent existence is mistaken. Therefore recognizing experience as appearance need not eliminate experience. Nor does the occurrence of a seemingly separate self automatically establish an independently substantial owner.

The distinction is:

1. **The occurrence and character of experience** must be accommodated.
2. **Its interpretation as a particular kind of enduring or separate subject** remains open to examination.
3. **Its occurrence as appearance or mode** still requires explanation within a zero-residue account.

We must therefore not infer either that finite subjects are independently ultimate or that no finite ownership is real. Denying an interpretation can be legitimate; denying or merely renaming the actual experiential differentiation is not an explanation.

The attachment also sometimes treats accepting the experiential datum as excluding epiphenomenalism. Those are distinct questions: causal efficacy is an additional commitment, defended conditionally in the [non-additivity argument](NONADDITIVE_CONSTITUTION_ARGUMENT.md), not a definition of experience's existence.

Calling a field continuous does not explain its actuality or intrinsic character; it also must not presuppose fundamental spacetime when spacetime is meant to be derivative. Rejecting enduring subjects does not settle unity at an instant or across an episode. Naming a collective bearer or an intrinsic quiddity is not a derivation of its experiential character.

Dissociation research may motivate investigations of access, memory, and organization. A clinical diagnosis does **not by itself** demonstrate an exact metaphysical count of subjects, the division of a previously unified phenomenal subject, or cosmic dissociation. The export's empirical analogy must not become constitutive evidence without further argument.

The structural-realism discussion also overgeneralizes. A view neutral about intrinsic nature is not thereby inconsistent with an experiential intrinsic-nature hypothesis, and plural representations do not logically require many independently existing substances. Structural realism has distinct formulations; the export does not establish a universal denial of all intrinsic character or its knowability through every route. No structural plurality alone solves subject incidence, but it need not make the problem harder: complementary, bridged descriptions may constrain it. Our [unified constitution map](UNIFIED_CONSTITUTION_MAP.md) requires exactly such justified correspondences, not framework names used as explanatory substitutes.

No numerical or confident qualitative likelihood ranking follows merely from which proposal renames or avoids an initial problem. The export's pessimism about full resolution is not a proof of impossibility, and the incomplete state of a proposal is not evidence by itself that it is likely false. None of these points requires weakening the complete-absolute endpoint.

## 6. Necessary articulation without a necessary defect

The strongest available articulation argument starts with actual experience, not a claimed universal law that all self-knowledge requires an external contrast:

1. Actual experience includes determinate differences; even an appearance of difference is not featureless nonoccurrence.
2. Complete actuality and complete self-knowledge cannot omit or confuse those actual differences.
3. Under the sole-absolute account, their ultimate ground cannot come from outside the whole.
4. Therefore the whole's concrete nature must ground their determinate articulation.

This establishes an obligation of the adopted account. It does not independently prove that the absolute exists, or deduce all actual qualities and subjects from the word completeness. **Necessary adequacy to actual differentiation is not yet a derivation of this particular finite architecture.**

The alternative chain in the export is too fast:

    self-knowledge -> subject/object polarity
                   -> many finite perspectives
                   -> local privation
                   -> necessary actual deficiency.

Every arrow needs justification. Reflexive knowledge need not posit two independent knowers or a separate object-substance. Even a justified distinction between knowing and known does not entail multiple finite owners. Distinct contents can be positive determinations rather than failures of due fulfillment. And an intelligible contrast can be represented without its deficient alternative being enacted.

The absolute need not instantiate blindness in order to know sight, nor cruelty in order to know care. This denies a proposed inference to the necessity of those deficiencies; it does not prove that their actual occurrence has already been explained or that a particular alternative world is metaphysically possible.

Simplicity retains its fixed meaning: no independent ultimate constituents or selectors, not no genuine intrinsic differences. Internal articulation is not automatically dualism. Nor may a merely renamed perspective hide an independently ungrounded boundary.

## 7. Privation's asymmetric role

Distinguish:

- **Positive determinacy:** a content or organization is what it is, and differs from another.
- **Local limitation:** a mode does not disclose all of the whole's content or possess every capacity.
- **Privation:** an identified positive form, capacity-realization, or appropriate fulfillment is absent in a specified respect. Evaluative badness additionally requires that the absent fulfillment be due in the relevant circumstances.

A true negation such as A not being B need not describe a deficiency or a positive negative constituent. It is not meaningless merely because it names no additional entity.

Darkness is absence of illumination, not another substance. An agent's lack of due regard is not a positive evil constituent added to the agent's actual intelligence, effort, or pursuit of money/power. Truthfully describing an absence does not convert it into a present thing. At the same time, describing a real limitation does not establish that it is wrong or a defect: not every form or capacity is due to every mode.

**A privation presupposes its positive context.** A bearer, organization, relevant respect and positive form must be identified to distinguish this lack from another. Consequently:

1. Absence alone does not supply the positive bearer or its organization.
2. Merely calling two modes privative does not individuate their actual scopes.
3. The priority of positive fulfillment to its lack does not imply that positive fulfillment requires the actual lack.
4. Awareness of a missing relation is not its enactment.

The strongest role for privation is therefore **explanatory asymmetry**, not productive negativity: finite non-disclosure or missing regard can be understood relative to positive actuality without a second principle of evil. The positive constitution must still explain why this particular local non-disclosure or missing relation obtains.

If 'privation' is used more broadly for a finite mode's limited disclosure relative to absolute fullness, that comparative use must not silently become evaluative deficiency. Neither use makes the mode itself an absence. Its actual character is positive; its not including everything else is a true limitation of its scope.

## 8. A local lack is not automatically an absolute lack

The export says that a whole containing a real moment of lack is thereby incomplete. This fails without a same-respect argument. The whole's complete knowledge of p and a mode's lack of local knowledge of p are not contradictory. A positive local capacity and its absent realization also need not be a missing capacity of the absolute itself.

But the converse inference is also invalid: global awareness or positivity does not demonstrate that every local omission is justified or necessary. Knowing a missing act of regard does not enact that act where it is due. The significance of prevention, care, and repair remains actual and explanatory, not cancelled by the whole's comprehension.

Thus neither conclusion is earned automatically:

- 'Local lack exists, so the absolute is imperfect.'
- 'The absolute is perfect, so this local harm must be necessary for its self-knowledge.'

The remaining obligation is the constitution of the particular finite limitation and the justification of any due-fulfillment claims. Privation can prevent a false two-substance problem; it cannot by itself settle that obligation.

## 9. Consolidated verdict and next positive requirement

**Retain:** one complete experiential actuality; internally grounded distinction without independent ultimate substances; appearances as genuine experiential occurrences; a possible advantage of whole-first constitution over subject-summing; and privation as dependent absence rather than a rival positive principle.

**Do not adopt:** that modes or continuity alone dissolve individuation; that all schools named in the export have been correctly characterized; that self-knowledge necessarily requires multiple finite owners; that actual evil is needed as contrast; that local lack proves global deficiency; or that failure to supply a solution establishes a necessary residue.

The preferred explanation runs in this direction:

    positive concrete constitution
        -> determinate qualitative occurrence and grounded local scope
        -> true absences relative to that scope and its appropriate fulfillments.

It must not run from a freestanding absence to the positive being of an experiencer. These arrows state the candidate dependence and its obligations; they are not newly discovered physical or phenomenal laws. Nor does knowing the shape of an explanation yet explain why its constitution is actual or necessary.

The [common-constitution closure requirement](COMMON_CONSTITUTION_CLOSURE.md) supplies the completion test: a unique map after the basis and targets are supplied is not enough. The intrinsic nature must fix the actual qualities, scopes, and their relations without an extra selector, and its determining premises must themselves be explained. This is the precise remaining task within the same perfect complete absolute—not a reason to retreat to brute differentiation, creation-by-fiat, or an allegedly necessary failure of intelligibility.

No new empirical result, clinical inference, executable discovery, consciousness assignment, or increase in the historical research-test count is claimed.
