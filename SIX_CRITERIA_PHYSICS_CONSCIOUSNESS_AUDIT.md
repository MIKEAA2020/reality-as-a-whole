# Six criteria: physical and consciousness-theoretic adequacy

## Governing update

The updated discussion adds two user requirements to the existing four:

- **C5:** most plausible trends in modern/future physics.
- **C6:** best, most plausible theories of consciousness.

The [absolute-constitution framework](ABSOLUTE_CONSTITUTION_FRAMEWORK.md) remains the governing account and implementation queue. C1–C4 specify the complete-absolute endpoint; C5–C6 require that its constructive articulation recover established science and engage the strongest warranted physical and consciousness candidates. They are not optional checks after metaphysical completion.

**Positive synthesis:** develop one complete self-knowing experiential actuality whose physical structure, local perspectives, access mechanisms, and qualitative-relational fulfillment are internally constituted. Quantum/algebraic, geometric, thermodynamic, cognitive, and phenomenal descriptions must constrain that same constitution. Preserve whatever survives from each theory in its proper domain; do not concatenate incompatible sufficiency claims.

**Critical distinction:** the user asks for plausible science. The source assistant's declaration that a named bundle achieves “100% satisfaction” is not additional evidence, a user stipulation, or a completed verification. Strongest interpretation means strongest warranted content, not maximum confidence in every fashionable extrapolation.

A deeper theory may revise a formalism while recovering its successful content; fidelity does not require freezing every existing equation as ultimate.

Recover successful mechanisms without defining consciousness into a preferred proxy. The proposal must account for quality, togetherness, duration, access, self-modeling, agency, and valence without assuming these always coincide.

A hybrid is warranted only if its parts have distinct compatible jobs or a justified common derivation. If two theories assign different consciousness to the same physical case, that disagreement is a discrimination target, not a seam that disappears by naming both theories.

## 4. Three mathematical distinctions that prevent false closure

### A. Statistical boundary is not a subject selector

A classical blanket B for internal X and external Y specifies, in the relevant probabilistic model,

    p(x,y | b) = p(x | b) p(y | b).

This is a conditional-independence relation. Sensory/active interpretation and dynamically maintained boundaries require further structure. The equality alone entails neither experience nor a unique boundary. It also does not specify a quantum subsystem factorization or a quantum conditional-independence notion.

Therefore a proposed rule “every blanket is a finite mind” requires independent physical and phenomenal warrant, including how alternative or nested blankets are handled. The absence of that warrant does not refute all blanket-based models; it prevents calling individuation solved.

### B. Variational free energy is not pain or thermodynamic free energy by identity

For a specified generative model p(o,z) and variational distribution q(z), with suitable support and normalization,

    F[q;o] = E_q[log q(z) - log p(o,z)]
           = KL(q(z) || p(z|o)) - log p(o).

Thus F bounds negative log evidence for that model. Minimizing over q for fixed o and fixed model tightens the bound; it does not by itself change the observation's evidence, establish a physical descent dynamics, or determine valence. Policy selection/expected free energy introduces additional definitions and assumptions.

Variational free energy, thermodynamic free energy, action, prediction error, and suffering are different quantities or targets. Relations between them need a stated model, scale, units where applicable, dynamics, and independent empirical or constitutive warrant. No identity of these quantities follows from the shared word “free” or from an optimization analogy.

### C. An action principle presupposes physical content

    delta S = 0

is a stationarity condition, not a universal minimum or a value ranking. A schematic quantum amplitude involving exp(i S/hbar) requires a specified action and the relevant domain, boundary conditions, measure/regularization, and interpretation. Quantum amplitudes are not nonnegative observer probabilities. Stationary-phase reasoning does not select the action itself or prove that every represented path is an actual experienced history.

These are clarification arguments, not new physical discoveries or new evidence that the preferred metaphysics is true.

## 5. Integration and discrimination protocol

Use the existing W1–W6 queue; no new competing programme is created.

1. **W1–W2:** retain the complete-absolute endpoint, but require an actual local-perspective constitution. Compare blanket-based selection with intrinsic recurrent/togetherness proposals. Neither gets phenomenal validity from its name.
2. **W3:** add a C5 assumption sheet to each proposed bridge: tested content recovered, mathematical domain, frontier hypothesis used, serious alternative, and failure condition. Retain AQFT localization and type-III qualifications; a neural blanket cannot silently replace them.
3. **W4:** give each consciousness candidate a separate predicted-output row for occurrence, quality, togetherness, temporal extent, access/report, and self-modeling. An unspecified entry remains unspecified. Test content/task/report contrasts and preservation premises; do not infer results from a protocol alone.
4. **W5:** separate valence, model evidence, thermodynamic cost, geometric symmetry, welfare, standing, and specific claims. Specify which equality, correlation, or explanatory relation is proposed. Use the payment/satisfaction and exploitation cases to prevent reducing all goodness to current pleasure.
5. **W6:** audit cross-theory consistency before claiming a hybrid. Flag differences of substrate sufficiency, subject exclusion, temporal grain, and phenomenality versus access. Record unsupported links rather than count the number of named theories as progress.

**Discriminating cases to design, not claimed empirical results:** (a) same declared blanket but changed internal recurrence; (b) same reduced maintenance with distribution changed under the audit's preservation assumptions; (c) altered report or self-model with basic qualitative warrant independently assessed; (d) similar nominated symmetry/free-energy statistic but independently different valence; (e) equivalent full presentation versus a physically distinct matching implementation. These cases require feasible interventions and evidence before execution or interpretation.

A candidate is not refuted merely because its identity is unfinished. It is also not validated merely because its terms can be translated into the preferred ontology. Future empirical evidence may change the ranking of mechanisms without lowering the complete-account endpoint.

## 6. Consolidated six-criterion commitment

> **Under C1–C4, reality is the perfect complete absolute. Develop its self-knowing experiential constitution under C5–C6 by recovering warranted physics and consciousness mechanisms, establishing their actual bridges, and preserving finite subjects, quality, temporal organization, causal powers, and due regard. Variational, informational, geometric, and cognitive formulations are valuable where justified; none alone supplies actuality, subjecthood, valence, or perfection.**

The updated exchange broadens scientific adequacy and supplies concrete candidate connections. It does not yet discharge the essence–instance gap, the Gamma–chi law, local individuation, the actual physical action/state, or the perfection–harm problem. Unitarity cannot redeem suffering by itself, and no claim of “100% closure” is retained.
