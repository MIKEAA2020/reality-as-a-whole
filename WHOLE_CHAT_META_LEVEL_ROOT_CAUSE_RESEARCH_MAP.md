## Where to work first

The highest-leverage investigation is **one joint problem**, not three unrelated patches:

> Can one constitution determine (1) what is a real subject and its intrinsic quality, (2) which occurrences are genuinely distinct and their incidence weights, and (3) the signed quantity and its zero, while its physical description is the extrinsic aspect of those same occurrences?

This connects candidates 1–5 below to the existing clock, pair and relational sphere. Candidate 6 supplies the physical bridge; 7–10 constrain geometry, time and genuine possibility. Candidates 11–12 prevent an apparently closed model from gaining its success through redescription or an unexamined criterion of explanation. The priorities are judgments about potential leverage, **not** declarations that these are the only problems or that any framework has already failed.

## 1. Zero: constraint, constitution and self-subsistence

**Source result.** The [gauge-constrained pair](ZERO_GAUGE_CONSTRAINED_ANGULAR_PAIR.md) has opposite local values on every state in its specified physical sector without selecting a ready pair. The [self-cancelling performance audit](ZERO_TOTALITY_PERFORMANCE_AND_RESIDUE_AUDIT.md) separately shows that a zero net sum can coexist with nontrivial internally ordered configurations. Neither result shows that an arbitrary vanishing scalar is identical to literal nonexistence, or that the physical sector follows from zero.

**Root-cause hypothesis to test.** The problem may be treating zero as an output number and then asking it to determine its own inputs. The user's stronger proposal instead concerns the *constitution of the whole*. Investigate whether cancellation, distinguishability, admissibility and the explanatory relation can be aspects of one self-subsistent structure, rather than four separately stipulated facts. A formal representation may use auxiliary mathematics without thereby positing an external physical generator; equally, a representation does not establish concrete actuality merely by being written down.

**Discriminating construction.** On the existing constrained clock/record carrier, distinguish: the whole, its internal contributions, the map to a signed total, the constraint, and the rule of actual incidence. Try to derive each from one explicitly stated constitutive principle. Compare a nontrivial internally differentiated zero history with the zero vector and with a differently structured zero history. Record whether each comparison is a difference of physical ontology, gauge, or mere presentation. A successful result would show why the same principle supplies the distinctions and relations, not just that its equations are consistent.

**Guardrail.** The user asserts that if zero cannot meet the no-residue demand no framework can, and that the demand is satisfiable. These are substantive guiding claims, neither established nor refuted by the finite models. Do not treat disclosed assumptions as an acceptable residue or infer impossibility from an incomplete model.

## 2. What makes a physical relation an intrinsic quality?

**Source result.** On one echo history, the [joint constitution test](ZERO_JOINT_CONSTITUTION_INCIDENCE_AND_ECHO.md) compares `h_B=s b` and `h_j=s j`; they disagree on physical weight 1/4. The [echo-quality theorem](ZERO_ECHO_INTRINSIC_QUALITY_AND_INCIDENCE.md) conditionally selects `s j r` over alternatives given a ready calibration, fixed boundary and full-echo neutrality. The [angular reconstruction](ZERO_INTRINSIC_QUALITY_ANGULAR_RECONSTRUCTION.md) gives exact nonzero best-estimation errors for two candidate magnitude laws on a restricted old carrier. These are different but connected tests, not a single proof of felt valence.

**Root-cause hypothesis to test.** We may be treating an intrinsic aspect as if it were an added readout that must be independently attached to one of several extrinsic observables. Conversely, calling two things “aspects” cannot make their identity true merely by definition. Determine the correct identity criterion: which transformations preserve the physical–experiential occurrence, which change its felt character, and which merely change an extrinsic description? A richer experiential organization could include both physical relations, but that possibility requires its own unity and composition law.

**Construction and control.** Use the same M/j/R boundary, the actual clock history and the two sign rules. Explicitly define the domain of each proposed occurrence and transport its quality through the real gates. Test a calibrated report only as an independently justified interface, with inverted-decoder and inaccessible-record controls. Ask whether the distinction survives the physically admissible gauge and subject-individuation rules. Success would explain why a particular qualitative identity is the intrinsic aspect of the specified occurrence, while allowing first-person acquaintance to contribute experiential intelligibility without pretending a third-person formula reproduces what suffering feels like.

## 3. Incidence, Born weight and the identity of an occurrence

**Source result.** The [refinement test](ZERO_INCIDENCE_REFINEMENT_AND_BORN_MEASURE.md) has the same coarse physical state but 10, 16 or 34 formal slots; naive unit counting gives `1/5`, `1/2` or `1/17` for one event. The [two-type construction](ZERO_JOINT_CONSTITUTION_INCIDENCE_AND_ECHO.md) allows `σ_α=αρ⊕(1−α)ρ` for any `0<α<1` while physical effects `E⊕E` retain the same predictions. All-effects additivity and certainty of a pure state give a conditional Born result, not the extra type weight or actual subject identity.

**Root-cause hypothesis to test.** A label, a physical effect, a persistent record, a subject and an experienced occurrence are different kinds of object. The apparent measure ambiguity may partly come from using one of them as a proxy for another. Conversely, declaring every inconvenient refinement an alias would merely hide the choice.

**Construction and control.** Define a typed physical–experiential incidence domain, an equivalence relation that identifies *the same* occurrence across mere redescription, and a refinement operation that can also represent genuinely distinct recorded occurrences. Derive or test additivity and effect consistency on that domain. Apply it to all three slot resolutions and to the α family, then to self-location within the one whole—not a literal simulator. Examine whether a type-exchange symmetry acts on actual incidences and preserves their measure before inferring α=1/2. The result should distinguish a necessarily fixed weight from an actually fixed outcome.

## 4. Opposites: quantity, sign and weighted balance

**Root-cause hypothesis to test.** Several different “opposites” have been at risk of being merged: reversal of a mathematical sign, physical charge conjugation or exchange, opposite values in distinct local records, and positive versus negative *felt valence*. Their relationship may be constitutive, but is not automatic. Nor does whole-level zero imply every selected pair cancels separately.

**Construction and control.** On the same physical pair and proposed actual-incidence domain, define the candidate signed quantity, its transformation under the actual counterpart, and the relevant measure. Test whether involution, oddness and measure preservation follow together. Include overlapping subject boundaries, nonuniform multiplicity, and a whole that balances through more than one pair. If experience is the relevant additive whole-level quantity, identify the corresponding positive and negative actual experiences without treating pleasure as erasing suffering. If it is physical energy instead, explain the identity or relation separately.

**Root-cause hypothesis to test.** Several things called “the sphere” may have been conflated: a topological sphere, a round observer-frame sphere, a projective null-ray sphere, a quantum state space, and an intrinsic space of distinctions. Some may be structures of one object rather than separate entities, but the identification has to supply the relevant transformations and observables.

**Construction and control.** Begin with the point–sphere's proposed relational algebra; derive or explicitly identify the complex/projective structure and its transformation class, then build the cone and compare relational observables for different observers. Check which measure is invariant under which action and distinguish conformal structure from metric scale. Include the zero-mode/weighted-potential control and the actual pair/clock record. Success requires a pathway toward physical gravity and empirical constraints, not only a mathematically valid cone.

**Root-cause hypothesis to test.** A discontinuous quotient reflection, a finite coarse graining, and a physical locality relation may be getting treated as the same structure. A non-neighbor transition in the angle graph is not yet spacetime nonlocality; neither is it automatically harmless. **Construction and control.** Analyze the continuum quadratic-form domain and its boundary matching under the old reflection; compare refining bin discretizations. **Construction and control.** Build a dependency graph whose nodes include physical structure, subject/quality, incidence, admissible transformations and actuality. Label each arrow as definition, theorem, interpretive identity, empirical input or hypothesized constitutive grounding. ## Suggested sequence of passes

1. **Joint constitutive pass:** attack 1–5 together on the existing coherent echo, with the strongest internally unified subject–quality–incidence hypothesis and explicit noncircularity/equivalence controls. Do not first choose a subject, a sign rule and a probability independently and call their conjunction a derivation.
2. **Physical invariant pass:** connect that candidate to 6–8: whole-level physical quantity, relational geometry, local observables and two-clock transport. Keep actual-energy and experience identifications distinct until a joint argument is supplied.
3. **Modal and locality pass:** use 9–11 to determine how genuine occurrence, angle/refinement, accessible records and compatibility with the full history constrain one another. Do not reopen the settled case against infinity or call necessitated outcomes irreducibly random.
4. **Closure and audit pass:** apply 12 to the resulting *same* construction, not a disconnected toy. Preserve provenance and frozen-packet rules; missing historical evidence remains missing until actually recovered.

For each pass, report (a) the exact original result and scope, (b) the hidden distinction or assumption suspected to cause the anomaly, (c) a strongest positive resolution, (d) a counterexample or operational control that could discriminate it, and (e) what the result actually establishes. The aim is genuine constitutive progress, not a perpetual list of caveats or a false claim that the whole is already complete.
