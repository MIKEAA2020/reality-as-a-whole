# Common constitution: from a correspondence to an intelligible determination

## 1. The positive claim to pursue

The leading account is not a nonexperiential physical world plus a second assignment of experiences. It is **one concrete nature whose internally grounded differentiations have public physical powers and, where episodes occur, intrinsic qualitative presence**. The particular occurrence's physical organization and experienced differentiation are to be explained together, not paired by a further selector.

For the governing perfect complete absolute, this is internal articulation, not an act of producing another reality. Simplicity remains noncomposition: public and experiential presentations are not independent ultimate ingredients. Genuine differences cannot be eliminated merely to make the word unity easier to satisfy.

This is a strong working conjecture, supported by explanatory integration and the [conditional non-additivity argument](NONADDITIVE_CONSTITUTION_ARGUMENT.md). It is not yet a specified intrinsic nature or a warranted identity law. The purpose here is to sharpen its positive content: **what must a constitutive determination accomplish that a correspondence does not?**

There are three distinct obligations:

1. **Sufficiency:** the specified basis contains enough information to determine the target in the declared domain.
2. **Constitutive warrant:** the determination follows from what that concrete occurrence is, rather than an independently added dictionary or merely predictive fit.
3. **Ultimate closure:** the nature, determining principles, domain, and actuality themselves have no unexplained residue.

A successful result at one level does not automatically supply the others. Conversely, incomplete ultimate closure does not make a well-supported local identity false.

## 2. An exact sufficiency criterion

Fix a domain D of compared concrete occurrences/configurations, a proposed physical description

\[
p:D\to B,
\]

and a target description

\[
e:D\to Y.
\]

These maps are formal descriptions of a candidate investigation, not two substances or a construction of actuality from sets. D need not be a population of possible universes; it may be a domain of actual finite occurrences. Its boundaries and comparisons must be justified separately.

**Target discipline:** Y must be specified before testing the basis. A qualitative-organization target, a report target, and a token-incidence target are different. If experiential organizations are compared up to a warranted equivalence, use those equivalence classes explicitly. If distinct episode tokens matter, neither p nor e may silently erase their distinction through type coding. This criterion supplies no phenomenal equivalence, token boundary, or subject count by itself.

There exists a function F on the **realized image** p(D) such that

\[
e=F\circ p
\]

if and only if

\[
p(d_1)=p(d_2)\;\Longrightarrow\; e(d_1)=e(d_2)
\quad\text{for all }d_1,d_2\in D.
\]

**Proof.** If F exists, equal p-values have equal F-values. Conversely, if e is constant on every p-fiber, define F(b) to be e(d) for any d with p(d)=b. Fiber constancy makes this well-defined. It is the only such F on p(D). Its values outside p(D), if B is larger, are not fixed by these observations. No choice of a preferred representative is needed because the target value is already unique.

This elementary factorization criterion makes an important distinction precise:

> A basis can determine a target only if it does not identify occurrences that differ in that target. But determination on a domain does not explain why that target is intrinsic to the basis.

Noninjective F is allowed: physically distinguishable configurations may share a qualitative organization. There is no requirement that every physical difference be a qualitative difference. Also, an injective p automatically supports a factorization for **any** target on D. Therefore making descriptions arbitrarily detailed can make sufficiency vacuously easy while supplying no constitutive explanation.

The unresolved intervals remain excluded from this binary onset-target comparison, not reassigned to one content.

Thus actual data illustrate the precise middle task: identify a basis that determines an independently fixed target **without obtaining sufficiency merely by copying its labels or uniquely indexing the observations**.

## 4. Why unique determination still leaves residue

The factorization proof is about a fixed triple (D,p,e). It does not select that triple, justify the causal relevance of p, or establish what e is. Its uniqueness conclusion is therefore not the no-independent-selector result we ultimately need.

Three notions of uniqueness must remain separate:

- **Given-description uniqueness:** once p and e are fixed and fiber constancy holds, F is unique on their realized support.
- **Empirical identification:** independently justified observations, interventions, and candidate restrictions discriminate the proposed correspondence from rivals.
- **Constitutive necessity:** the experiential differentiation follows from the concrete nature, not an additional primitive pairing.

The first can be trivial even when the second and third are absent. The second can support an excellent local psychophysical law without completing the third. Ultimate necessity of the entire nature is a further question beyond all three.

A mere state renaming transports p, e, and their dictionaries; it changes no occurrence. A reassignment of already anchored contents while holding the independently specified physical basis and reference fixed is not such a renaming. To exclude it constitutively, however, we need more than another notation for F. The proposed nature must explain why the alternative would not be that same concrete constitution.

## 5. The strongest identity argument—and its presently open premise

The existing non-additivity argument supports the following direction, conditionally:

1. Some experiential distinctions genuinely contribute to discrimination, thought, action, or knowledge of those experiences.
2. The relevant effects have causally complete physical explanations in the declared domain, without systematic independent duplication.
3. Therefore their experiential contribution is not an independent addition beside the realized process.

This favors **common constitution** over an extra agent. It does not choose between token identity, grounded realization, or a further intrinsic specification of the same occurrence.

The additional premise needed for a local identity argument is:

> **Constitutive determination:** an independently specified concrete nature N fixes the anchored qualitative differentiation and its public powers through its own intelligible character, without a further assignment that can vary while N is held fixed.

If that premise is defended, the contrast is not unexplained extra meaning attached to a neural symbol. In the identity version, this experiencing occurrence is the nominated concrete occurrence considered in its intrinsic character. But **the premise is not established by writing it**. It is where a substantial discovery or argument must occur. Replacing N with “whatever constitutes this experience,” or with an ordered pair of the already supplied physical and experiential facts, would simply hide the assignment.

An actual candidate must independently specify N's realized state/history/powers and show why their intrinsic character warrants the particular qualitative contrast. It must also explain why the proposed whole, rather than its monitor, a larger aggregate, or a convenient temporal window, is the experiencing occurrence. Neither matching reports nor formal factorization supplies that boundary.

This locates the explanatory task without assuming that all causal organization is experiential, that every part of the absolute is a subject, or that the absolute's completeness alone determines these particular finite qualities.

## 6. What would count as a positive constitutive explanation

A serious local explanation should supply a connected package, not one further consciousness score:

1. **Actual reference.** Defeasibly anchored qualitative contrasts, including uncertainty and reporting limitations. This need not be an infallible phenomenal oracle.
2. **Independent basis.** Realized physical organization selected by state, dynamics, material/temporal constraints, and relevant interventions—not by reading the target labels back into the basis.
3. **Sufficiency and losses.** A justified domain in which relevant basis equivalence preserves the target; explicit failures under information-losing summaries. The criterion above constrains this step.
4. **Causal placement.** Evidence separating content maintenance, input encoding, and downstream report/monitoring, with reference reliability assessed under those interventions.
5. **Intrinsic determination.** A defensible explanation of why this organization has this qualitative character and incidence. Merely declaring them identical is not enough.
6. **Foundational dependence.** An account of why the constitutive nature and determining relation belong necessarily or otherwise non-brutely to the complete actuality.

The first four would be major scientific progress even without the last two. They should not be discounted for being local, or renamed zero-residue completion. If two developed proposals agree on all physically and experientially warranted observations, those observations alone do not decide between identity and a primitive pairing. An additional constitutive argument is then needed; a larger dataset with the same interpretive content cannot replace it.

## 7. A clarification about identity and complete intelligibility

**Zero residue requires no unexplained determination, not the assertion that every predicate denotes the same property.** One concrete occurrence can have different, internally grounded predicates without being assembled from independent ultimate substances. A fully explained realization or dependence relation could in principle discharge the explanatory obligation without strict identity of every physical and experiential property. Whether such a proposal genuinely explains the dependence, rather than makes it primitive, is the substantive test.

Token identity, property identity, and explanatory unification must therefore not be used interchangeably. Our leading identity-oriented constitution remains a strong candidate, but the completeness objective cannot be defended by simply defining every nonidentity explanation as residue. Equally, calling an unexplained pairing “realization” earns no exemption.

This clarification preserves the endpoint and the fixed meaning of simplicity. Internal distinction is necessary to completeness; an independent selector is not. A sufficient explanation of internal differentiation need not erase the differentiation it explains.

## 8. Connection to the mathematical–physical unity

The [finite workspace/algebra/channel bridge](WORKSPACE_ALGEBRA_CHANNEL_BRIDGE.md) proves exact physical factorization through a declared reduced core, with an observable embedding, dual state correspondence, and a channel extension that loses quantum coherence. It does **not** prove that the experiential map factors through that same core. That would require precisely the additional target-fiber preservation established above as necessary and sufficient mathematically, with experiential premises independently warranted.

Likewise, matching AQFT observables under a limited state restriction, choosing a region, or finding entanglement does not by itself identify an experiential episode. Faithful mathematical re-presentations must preserve the same constitutive facts; lossy descriptions must be tested for which targets they preserve. A realistic unified account must justify its physical scale transitions and the applicability of its phenomenal determination together.

The single perfect complete absolute remains the governing whole-account. This result neither derives its actuality nor downgrades it because its implementation remains incomplete. It sharpens what finishing the account entails: **not only a unique map once the facts are given, but an intelligible constitution that explains why those facts and that determination belong to the same actuality.**
