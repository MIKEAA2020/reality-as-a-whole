## Starting point

The user has supplied `ptito2009.pdf` and `shelat-and-giesbrecht-2026.pdf` in response to our statement that the full results of Ptito et al. 2009 and Giesbrecht and Kingstone 2004 were inaccessible. Before inspecting the uploads, the project had a publisher abstract and partial thesis methods for Ptito and a publisher excerpt including conditional T2 analysis for Giesbrecht and Kingstone, not either complete results section. The file names alone do not establish contents or editions. The 2026 paper may be a separate work rather than the missing 2004 original.

## Procedure

1. Identify each supplied file, authors, publication, pages, completeness and provenance; extract and read its complete text and visually inspect relevant tables/figures as feasible. Clearly distinguish the actual 2004/2009 primary study from later papers, reviews or secondary descriptions and independent new experiments. Record uncertainty about missing pages or unreadable content.
2. If the original Ptito 2009 paper is present, reconstruct its sample, four-stream comparisons, exact timing, responses, exclusion, operational lag-dependent blink, results and statistics. Distinguish population claims from the single patient, null significance from equivalence, and the derived measurement from identification or direct experience. Compare with earlier partial-source claims and fix any substantive errors.
3. Identify whether the supplied 2026 paper actually contains the full 2004 study, only cites it, or offers new evidence. Search for the 2004 original through its prior institutional link and accessible alternatives, read it if obtainable, and accurately report access. Give the user a usable title and link if any necessary source remains inaccessible; never imply a secondary summary is the full original. Do not upload copyrighted source files to the repository without an explicit need and permission.
4. Compare the designs and evidence without conflating across participants, tasks, time windows, phenomenal occurrence, ownership or the four-spin selector. Preserve first-person testimony and P's distinct private local/global tokens; keep q and open substantive gates separate.
5. Update existing audits and status links to distinguish historic access from the new reading, refresh inventories, check `git diff --check` and run relevant existing regressions. No new patient-level raw data, clinical research, replication, or subject census is claimed.
