# One global plan: from experiential actuality to recoverable physics

> **Current reassessment:** The user has challenged the universal-agent endpoint because our universe is suboptimal and now asks what can be relaxed while retaining structural and experiential intelligibility. The [subject-pluralist experiential synthesis](SUBJECT_PLURALIST_EXPERIENTIAL_SYNTHESIS.md) treats a single universal subject and universal agency as optional, compares live rivals, and leaves the experiential constitution bridge open. Read references to universal consciousness below as the earlier target, not a settled requirement or conclusion.

> **Cosmological-collapse gate:** The [JHEP 2024 paper audit](COSMOLOGICAL_COLLAPSE_CLASSICALITY_GATE.md) distinguishes narrowing its λ spectrum from a demonstrated classical volume history and supplies a Dirichlet-model localization bound. It does not solve clock choice, gravity, experience, or the cosmological constant problem. Its next conditional test is a relational moving-window history with a physical clock and records; it is a rival, not a required mechanism.

## 1. The question and the honest answer

The target is one self-sufficient, timeless absolute, without fundamental mass or spacetime, whose intrinsic nature is experience; finite perspectives and internal temporal relations should be intelligible within it. The intended account should explain possibility and actuality, essence and existence, universal consciousness, and Bostrom's simulation question without positing a simulator outside the whole. These are **desired conclusions and constraints on a candidate**, not propositions already proved. The user's premise that our universe is non-optimal is a governing constraint: the earlier perfection requirement is withdrawn. The user also adopts the implication that a perfect whole would entail a best possible universe; under these premises the whole is not perfect in that sense. The [revision](NONOPTIMAL_WORLD_REVISED_FRAMEWORK.md) and [whole-implications analysis](NONOPTIMAL_WORLD_WHOLE_IMPLICATIONS.md) distinguish that conditional conclusion from the further claim that a better possible totality exists.

Can more turns guarantee a solution? No. These questions include open problems for which neither the required results nor even a fully adequate method of testing every proposed metaphysical claim is currently established. More turns can support a sustained, cumulative research programme: developing candidates, identifying contradictions, connecting results and testing predictions. They cannot guarantee that any particular proposed endpoint is true, provable, empirically decidable, or discoverable by this agent. If evidence or reasoning rules out a candidate, the responsible outcome is revision or failure, not indefinite manufacture of supporting models.

The previous [dependency register](DEPENDENCY_DRIVEN_RESEARCH_PLAN.md) lists local obligations. This document supplies the global architecture and a decision rule for which obligations are worth doing. The [seven-criteria synthesis](SEVEN_CRITERIA_EXPERIENTIAL_SYNTHESIS.md) compares the preferred experiential whole-first direction with live rivals, without claiming that every criterion is already satisfied.

## 2. One candidate, typed interfaces, and no circular bridge

Seek a *single* candidate constitution `C`. Do not assume that a label, a mathematical structure, or a physical theory by itself constitutes concrete experience. A viable proposed account must supply all of these interfaces:

    C: stated intrinsic constitution and status of actual existence
       ├─ P: finite perspective, its phenomenal quality/unity and limits
       ├─ R: realized physical process and intervention/record structure
       ├─ T: internal relational succession and clock comparisons
       ├─ G: emergent geometry and gravitational dynamics
       └─ S: internal computation/simulation and conditional observer measure

This diagram describes simultaneous explanatory relations, **not temporal creation**. In particular, a timeless C is not assumed to evolve in an external time. Candidate maps must say what their source and target are, when they exist, what structures and empirical predictions they preserve, and what input was assumed rather than derived. The links below are requirements, not existing maps:

| Interface | Needed bridge | Discriminating challenge / failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| `C -> R` | Explain which physically organized processes are concrete, and recover their states, probabilities and causal interventions. | A purely formal description with an unexplained actualization rule, or unconstrained multiple incompatible physical laws, leaves the bridge open. |
| `R -> T,G` | Recover relational clocks, local causal structure, metric calibration, dynamics and applicable gravity from the **same physical realization**. | A supplied clock, metric, coordinate ordering, or independent toy model cannot count as an emergent derivation. A fixed-background benchmark is admissible only as a limit to be recovered. |
| `C,R -> P` | Explain incidence, qualitative contrasts, finite unity/ownership and experienced succession, linked to a physically characterized process. | Mere correlations, records or a postulated label 'experience' are insufficient. Specify rivals and conditions under which evidence or argument distinguishes them. |
| `C -> actuality/necessity` | Justify whether and why actuality, possibility, essence and existence have the intended relation without assuming that very relation. | Exhibit countermodels: consistent descriptions that are not shown concrete; actual existence that is not shown necessary. Identify the non-circular premise that rules each out, or keep the claim open. |
| `C,R,P -> S` | Distinguish internal physically realized simulation from mathematical description; if a probabilistic claim is made, specify observer class, implementation and measure. | Merely writing a program, counting infinite descriptions, or denying an external base does not produce implementation, experience or Bostrom probabilities. |

These interfaces must agree on *which realized process and which records* they refer to. For example, a local instrument can show how a physical record is generated and transported; it cannot by itself show that the record has a subject or that its content is experienced. Conversely, a phenomenological premise cannot make an inconsistent gravitational constraint algebra valid.

## 3. A shared audit object, not another free-standing toy universe

Use a common **process-and-evidence dossier** for each serious candidate. It must name:

1. the candidate C and the extra assumptions left open;
2. a realized physical process `R`, state and preparation, applicable scales, and a map from any effective model to R;
3. a finite region, interventions and **full instruments** (outcomes and conditional post-states), including reference, clock, memory and relevant resource costs;
4. how the same relational event and record are identified in alternative descriptions, including gauge and cutoff changes;
5. the specifically nominated experiential contrast and how it is related to R, including competing constitution hypotheses and an independently warranted observation procedure;
6. predictions, uncertainties, alternatives, explicit failure criteria, and the residues carried forward.

The dossier is a discipline for connecting tracks; listing its fields does not establish a candidate. An effective model can be reused only with an explicit embedding or controlled approximation. Distinct testbeds must not be quietly equated.

### Two linked but distinct near-term anchors

- **Physical control:** the [1D source/readout test](CROSS_FRAMEWORK_CAUSALITY_RECORD_TEST.md) gives a regulated local-causality and record interface. The new [complete-instrument order bound](CUTOFF_INSTRUMENT_UNIFORMITY.md) tests whether that interface is stable under allowed inputs. It is a necessary quality check for the chosen gravity/record route, not evidence for C, an experiential law, or emergent spacetime. If that route cannot be embedded in a local probe and gravity, it should be dropped or redesigned.
- **Empirical time anchor:** [Barontini's cold-atom benchmark](COLD_ATOM_CLOCK_COMPARISON.md) constrains internal clock proposals in its experimental domain. The processed-data test does not supply a gravitational physical state, intrinsic experience or an absolute clock. Its common interface with the physical control is **relational event identification and record probabilities**, not an assertion that the two are the same apparatus. A future bridge must specify an actual model and recovery map.

For phenomenal evidence, the [maintained-disclosure comparison](DISCLOSURE_OBSERVATION_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) supplies a rival and an assay-calibration requirement, but its finite process is not yet embedded in either physical anchor. Constructing that embedding or showing it is untenable is a real cross-track task; calling them all 'records' does not make it exist.

## 4. Global milestones and dependencies

The phases overlap, but each later milestone needs named outputs from earlier ones. If a milestone fails, diagnose which candidate, map, approximation or assumption failed before proceeding.

**M0 — Freeze candidate and rivals.** Define a minimal provisional ontology and at least one genuinely distinct rival; specify actualization, identity, and testable versus purely philosophical consequences without axiomatizing the conclusion. Produce explicit countermodels for modal identity and experiential attribution. *Pass:* differences and assumptions are precise enough to assess. *Current:* [truth-grounding versus no-concrete-ground rivals](NOTHINGNESS_TRUTH_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_NECESSITY.md) and the necessary-experience/no-necessary-individual distinction sharpen the modal target; no unique C is established.

**M1 — Common operational interface.** For one physical control, make state, intervention, full outcome/post-state instrument, costs, and equivalent event descriptions precise. Include independent reference ancillas and regulator limitations. *Pass:* named norm/domain, same physical operation under comparison, error bound plus adversarial controls. *Current:* finite-N ordered instruments now have an energy-constrained bound; continuum instrument convergence and physical probe remain open.

**M2 — Real clocks and local physics.** Define compact local probe interactions; derive controlled continuum and 3+1 limits; map to one measured clock experiment without treating a coordinate as a physical clock. *Pass:* a reproducible, independently calibrated prediction and controlled errors. *Current:* two distinct restricted anchors, no recovery map.

**M3 — Gravitational consistency and emergent geometry.** Include source, probe, clocks, rods, controls and stress-energy in one model; establish admissible states, constraints, same-event relational comparisons, and a warranted low-energy recovery of spacetime/gravity. *Pass:* a concrete bound or theorem covering the intended observable; agreement with relevant tested physics. *Current:* classical initial-data and fixed-background results are partial; neither quantum constraint closure nor emergence from C is proved.

**M4 — Physical-to-phenomenal bridge.** Select a physically realizable process and a precise qualitative contrast; state rival constitutive laws and their preservation/disruption predictions; justify observation calibration under intervention. *Pass:* a non-circular, discriminating account that survives relevant evidence, plus explicit limitations concerning first-person character and subject unity. *Current:* [one-whole finite-access test and no-report experiment audit](ONE_WHOLE_FINITE_ACCESS.md) give a causal scope control. The [intervention/calibration follow-up](INTERVENTION_CONSCIOUSNESS_CALIBRATION.md) identifies an actual paired human gaze/report study, a separate prefrontal TMS study, and explicit error/dependence gates; none of the inspected studies simultaneously establishes P3/P4/P6. The [specific extrastriate–frontal pathway test](SPECIFIC_VISUAL_PATHWAY_GATE_TEST.md) evaluates an actual cTBS/fMRI candidate and gives fixed-decoder preservation and residual-information criteria; it is not a three-gate pass. The [consumer-coverage analysis](CONSUMER_COVERAGE_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) shows why a null measured channel cannot upper-bound the full consumer without an independently justified coverage bound. The [boundary-cut and record audit](DISTRIBUTION_CUT_AND_RECORD_PERSISTENCE.md) supplies a conditional complete-bottleneck certificate and shows in the existing workspace that prior memory can persist after the cut. No validated experiential bridge or proof of universal subjecthood.

**M5 — Necessity, simulation and full synthesis.** Combine the *same* C with the established maps, demonstrate the intended modal/existential implications or isolate irreducible premises, and derive conditional simulation claims with a justified measure where needed. *Pass:* no unexplained selector or bridge under the declared standard; the result withstands countermodels and independent review. *Current:* open. The [experience/non-optimality attachment audit](EXPERIENCE_NONOPTIMALITY_ATTACHMENT_AUDIT.md) identifies an unresolved choice between total-world modal collapse and a better possible total world, and distinguishes a neutral-ground rival from the universal-consciousness target. A viable physical theory alone does not complete M4 or M5.

## 5. What gets done next—and why

1. **Use the new bound, do not multiply toy models.** Complete L3 by putting cutoff and continuum instruments on a common domain and bounding the same four selective outputs; the finite-N order result is not that proof. This serves M1 and a possible M2/M3 route, and remains dispensable if that route fails.
2. **In parallel freeze a candidate-and-rival sheet (M0/M4).** State one concrete constitution hypothesis and a distinct rival using an existing physical process, list identical physical predictions versus distinctive phenomenal claims, and identify exactly what independent evidence could separate them. If they are empirically indistinguishable under the stated evidence, record that instead of pretending the microphysics selects one.
3. **Make the connection explicit before investing in a gravity model.** Specify which measured cold-atom quantities and uncertainties a local-probe model must reproduce, and what mapping would be needed from its records to the intervention dossier. Until a valid map exists, retain the tests as separate anchors. Compare gravity options and their prior art in parallel; do not interpret the 1D control as experimental gravity.
4. **Review after each bridge, not after an arbitrary number of checks.** Ask whether it narrowed a global ambiguity, excluded a serious rival, constrained an empirically relevant prediction, or reduced a named dependency. If not, stop or redirect it. Reassess whether an alternative approach to C or emergence would better satisfy the global constraints.

A plan cannot promise to finish an unsolved problem. It can make every step accountable to the whole, expose missing bridges early, and preserve negative results instead of treating them as failures to be hidden.
