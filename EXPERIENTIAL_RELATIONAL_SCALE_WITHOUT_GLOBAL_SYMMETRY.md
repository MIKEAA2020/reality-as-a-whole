# An intrinsic experiential scale without assuming global symmetry

## Aim and result

**Concrete continuation:** [normalized relational response as an experiential-invariant candidate](EXPERIENTIAL_SCORE_INVARIANT_CANDIDATE.md) supplies a dimensionless signed score with zero weighted mean, tests its reparameterization and coarse-graining properties, and identifies the independent experiential and physical assumptions required.

Can positive and negative experience have a common magnitude **without first assuming that the whole is symmetric or already balances**? One candidate is that their relation is fixed by a network of intrinsic comparisons within the experiential constitution. This extends the [polarity proposal](EXPERIENTIAL_POLARITY_INTRINSIC_CALIBRATION.md), but does not require every positive experience to have an actual equal-and-opposite counterpart.

The precise conditional result is: if a finite network of **independently fixed, consistent cardinal ratio relations** connects all represented positive and negative components, those relations determine their relative magnitudes uniquely up to one common positive unit. With independently fixed occurrence weights and signs, whether the whole's additive total is zero is then a substantive question, not something that can be changed by rescaling one side alone.

This is a constructive candidate for how a common scale *could* be internal to one whole. It does not demonstrate that the required experiential relations exist, derive them from zero, or prove that their total is zero. The central challenge moves from a single polarity map to the content and grounding of these comparisons; it is not eliminated by calling them intrinsic.

## 1. A relational calibration network

Let the finite nodes `i` denote **distinguished positive or negative experiential components**, which could be aspects of mixed experiences. A node is not necessarily a whole subject, and no mere node or number creates a feeling. Let `m_i>0` be its hypothesized magnitude and `s_i=+1` for a positive component or `-1` for a negative one. Zero or neutral components are outside this positive-magnitude graph; their reality must be represented separately.

Suppose an edge from i to j expresses an independently specified *cardinal* relation

```text
m_j = r_ij m_i,     r_ij > 0.
```

The reverse edge expresses `r_ji=1/r_ij`. The ratios are supposed to be facts of the proposed constitution, not numbers selected to ensure the final result. They might relate components within a subject, across subjects, or across relational histories. Each kind requires substantive justification: the graph is not itself evidence of cross-subject comparability.

If the graph is connected, the ratio of any node to a chosen reference node follows by multiplying edge ratios along a path. If all closed-path products equal 1, this assignment is path-independent. Conversely, a positive assignment satisfying every edge necessarily has product 1 around every closed path. For a finite connected graph, it suffices to check a spanning tree and each non-tree edge.

Therefore, **if** such a positive consistent assignment exists on a connected graph, any other assignment satisfying all the same ratios is `m'_i=c m_i` for one `c>0`. This is a common unit freedom, not a freedom to make the negative components independently larger. This is a familiar elementary consistency result, applied here as a proposed *experiential* relation rather than claimed as a physical or phenomenal discovery.

## 2. Calibration does not itself produce balance

For a provisional additive quantity, let independently fixed nonnegative weights `w_i` specify incidence, duration/measure or other justified contributions, without double-counting. Define

```text
Q = sum_i w_i s_i m_i.
```

A connected, consistent ratio network fixes whether Q is zero: a common change of unit multiplies Q by c and leaves its zero/nonzero status unchanged. It does **not** force Q to vanish. Nor is Q necessarily well-defined for actual experience simply because this expression can be written down.

For instance, let three stipulated components be positive, negative and negative, with ratios `m_2/m_1=1/2` and `m_3/m_2=1`, and unit weights. Choosing the common unit `m_1=2` gives `(m_1,m_2,m_3)=(2,1,1)` and

```text
Q = +2 - 1 - 1 = 0.
```

There is no one-to-one equal-magnitude sign-reversing permutation of these three contributions. Thus, conditional on the network and weights, zero can hold without the actual-whole polarity symmetry used in the preceding report. But changing the last independently given ratio to `m_3/m_2=2` makes the same calculation `Q=2-1-2=-1`. Connectivity supplies a scale; an additional intrinsic principle is needed to establish the actual balance.

This example is not a derivation of suffering from zero or evidence of the asserted ratios. Cancellation in a signed total does not erase any subject's negative experience, or prove that suffering is morally compensated.

## 3. Disconnected networks: a more precise conclusion

If comparisons connect nodes only within separate components `C_k`, each component retains its own positive overall scale `c_k`. After selecting arbitrary internal reference units and fixing the incidence weights, write its signed sum as `S_k`. Then

```text
Q = sum_k c_k S_k,     c_k > 0.
```

A single connected scale is **sufficient**, but not necessary, for an objective zero verdict. In particular, the total is zero for every allowed independent choice of component scales exactly when **every** `S_k=0`. Each component could balance intrinsically without a cross-component scale. Conversely, if any `S_k` is nonzero, some permissible rescaling changes the total away from zero. If the nonzero `S_k` include both signs, some scales can be chosen to make Q zero and others to make it nonzero: apparent balance would then depend on unfixed relative calibration. If all nonzero sums have the same sign, Q is necessarily nonzero for positive scales even though its magnitude remains underdetermined.

These statements hold for the finite additive model with fixed weights and unconstrained independent component units. Other physical relations could tie the component scales together; non-additive global quantities or a different notion of zero could require a different analysis.

## 4. Why cycles are informative, but not yet foundational

Three hypothesized comparisons `m_2=2m_1`, `m_3=3m_2`, `m_3=6m_1` are mutually consistent. Replacing the last ratio by 5 yields a contradiction for positive magnitudes. This is a genuine compatibility condition: the third relation cannot be freely selected independently of the first two if all are exact cardinal comparisons of one quantity.

The check can expose inconsistency or redundancy, not establish the comparisons. An empirical study would also have noise: exact loop equality is not expected of raw reports, and a pre-specified model would need measurement error, uncertainty and out-of-sample checks. A failure of a particular proxy model would not refute every experiential-scale proposal. A successful fit would not establish a unique intrinsic phenomenal scale if distinct latent models make the same predictions.

Ordinary descriptions such as “this hurts more than that,” even if perfectly reliable, generally specify **order**, not numerical ratios. For example, magnitudes `(1,2,3)` and `(1,4,9)` give the same strict ordering but different ratios. A comparison of two subjects' reports adds the separate problem of interpreting their response conventions and whether they refer to comparable phenomenal magnitudes. A physical correlate, neural similarity, or a willingness-to-trade report is not automatically a direct cardinal measurement of felt quality.

## 5. What could ground the relations without an external calibrator?

A serious constitutional proposal would have to specify a candidate relation *before* using it to calculate Q:

1. **Experience and subjects:** explain what the related components actually are, why each is experienced, and how they belong to particular subjects. A relation between third-person physical descriptions alone cannot simply be relabelled an experienced relation.
2. **The comparator:** identify what in the combined physical–experiential organization fixes a ratio rather than merely an ordering or functional similarity. It must explain comparisons across subject boundaries if those are used, without positing a universal subject.
3. **Mixed and neutral character:** specify how co-occurring positive and negative aspects are distinguished and how neutral experience remains real even if it contributes zero to the signed scalar.
4. **Weights and domain:** independently specify which occurrences or aspects are counted, their weights and how decompositions avoid double counting. A common intensity scale alone does not determine duration, measure, subject incidence or the contribution of non-experiencing aspects of the whole.
5. **Independent consequences:** spell out a discriminating implication of the comparator that is not just the desired `Q=0`, for instance relations among separately established local physical and reported contrasts. Define plausible proxy and measurement assumptions in advance and compare rival mappings; neither reports nor finite data directly access the complete whole.
6. **Relational time and infinite domains:** express any duration or history weights through physical clock relations, not an assumed external clock or preferred foliation. Infinite sums need an intrinsically justified measure and convergence; separately infinite positive and negative totals cannot simply be subtracted.

One possible research direction is a **common constitutional invariant**: an independently specified feature of physical–experiential organization that varies with felt intensity and has a fixed relation across positive and negative cases. Its positive/negative mapping and subject incidence would have to be derived or supported, not chosen by requiring global cancellation. This is an outline of what a theory would need, not a claim that a known invariant has been found. The physical energy of a region is not already such an invariant, and equality of separate global physical and experiential zeros does not itself establish their identity.

## 6. Foundational consequence

A connected network shows that a shared scale could be relational and internal: no outside observer is mathematically needed to set its relative magnitudes. The whole's own constitution might provide both the comparison network and its balance principle as aspects of one self-subsistent reality. That is a genuine candidate direction consistent with the user's demand, **not** a completed derivation.

At present, the experiential character, comparator, scale relations, occurrence weights, balance principle and their unity are stipulated or open. Proving the arithmetic consistency of the network is not showing that these facts follow from zero's nature. Nor does this conditional limitation prove that complete intelligibility is impossible, that only a structural account is required, or that a different non-additive zero-totality cannot work. The next task is to construct a more definite candidate for the common constitutional invariant and test whether it constrains comparisons without using the desired answer as input.
