## Course correction and next calculation

The recent operational sequence was useful but has concentrated on WP1-like instruments while leaving WP0—the plan's explicit first priority—unpaid. The next research pass should **not** automatically add another variation of the cursor toy. Instead:

1. Independently check the rolling-matter model's action, compact/zero-mode conventions, combined constraint, allowed positive-momentum branch and clock POVM against the actual JT source; correct or stop if a premise fails. Do not conflate the closed-universe model with the different subregion model.
2. If that gate survives, select a normalizable wavepacket away from zero momentum and a test space; derive a regulated group-averaged physical inner product and its positivity and support in the declared model. Establish the operator domains and track the scalar's backreaction. A formal plane wave is not the required physical state.
3. Only after admissibility, identify a *single* state, physical region and matched relational task; construct both clock-dressed algebras, trace normalizations and common observables, or report exactly where one is unavailable. Compare entropy differences separately from same-task outcome probabilities. Failure to obtain an admissible comparator is an informative stop, **not** proof of clock equivalence, inequivalence or universal uniqueness.
4. Revisit the measure-first conjecture with separately typed trace, state, geometric measure and calibrated flow, and maintain independent gates for arrow, Lorentzian recovery and intrinsic experience. Use the existing operational bounds as controls, not as substitutes for this gravitational calculation.

This is a revised research priority, not a claim that the original plan or the problem of time is solved. Alternative physical routes remain open if the JT route fails or its assumptions are inappropriate.
