# The axiarchic bridge: granting a unique Good still does not derive actuality

## Direct result

Even a complete value ordering with a unique best candidate does not, by itself, determine which candidate is concretely actual. An explicit countermodel establishes this limited logical result.

This is not a counterexample to every possible axiarchic theory. A theory whose independently justified constitution connects value and actuality adds precisely what the countermodel leaves unconstrained. The result identifies the needed premise; it does not show that no such premise can be true.

It also changes the research priority: settling every dispute about due regard or distributive priority would not alone explain existence. The normative and actualization projects can be pursued in parallel.

This applies the earlier [unconstrained-actuality diagnostic](research/rigorous_closures/REPORT.md) specifically to a uniquely optimal value structure. It is a sharpening of that known limitation, not a newly discovered general theorem.

## 1. Grant the strongest ethical premise for the test

Suppose all prior normative debts have been paid. There is:

- a specified domain of candidate descriptions;
- a fully justified value ordering;
- a uniquely best candidate;
- no uncertainty about its moral or axiological ranking.

This is a concession for a conditional argument, not an assertion that the inquiry has achieved those results.

Does the unique best candidate's actuality follow?

## 2. An exact countermodel

Use a language describing two candidate descriptions a and b. Let their stipulated ordinal value levels be:

    V(a) = 2
    V(b) = 1.

Thus a is uniquely best. The numbers encode only the stipulated ordering; they are not measurements of consciousness or a derived welfare formula.

Let R(x) mean that candidate description x is concretely realized. Add the condition that exactly one candidate is realized.

Two expansions of the same candidate-and-value structure satisfy these premises:

    M_good:   R(a) is true;  R(b) is false.
    M_other:  R(a) is false; R(b) is true.

Both preserve the candidate domain, the full ordering, the unique optimum, and exactly-one realization. Therefore those premises do not entail R(a).

If the exactly-one condition is removed, the same unconstrained language also permits neither candidate to be realized. This is a formal expansion, not a claim that a reality with no concrete occurrence is metaphysically possible. Likewise, the mathematical domain of descriptions is not being confused with the concrete realization it represents.

This is elementary model-theoretic independence relative to the stated premises. It does not prove that value and actuality are independent in reality, that M_other is metaphysically possible, or that every necessary constitutive identity is reducible to the thin language used here.

## 3. What additional principle would close this inference?

An axiarchic bridge could say:

    A candidate is concretely realized iff it is the uniquely best candidate.

In the stipulated structure, this selects a. It also makes a much stronger commitment than the goodness of being or denial of an evil substance.

A weaker principle requiring every realized candidate to be best would exclude realization of b but would not, without an existence premise, establish that any candidate is realized. This separates **selection conditional on existence** from **actualization itself**.

The stronger biconditional supplies both in this finite unique-optimum example. Its logical effectiveness does not establish its truth or explanation. Under complete intelligibility, it needs justification rather than installation as a brute instruction.

A directive that the best candidate *ought* to be realized likewise does not establish that it *is* realized without a further principle. A normative requirement and its fulfillment are not identical simply because the requirement is justified.

## 4. Why the privation argument does not supply the bridge

The user's proposal concerns the constitution of good and bad: positive powers and their fulfilled or deficient organization. Even if completely established, it has not yet stated why the uniquely best description must be concretely realized.

“No separate evil substance exists” does not entail “only the best actuality exists.” A deficient arrangement can contain positive powers and privations without a second evil substance.

“Being is Good” also does not close the inference. If every concrete arrangement has goodness insofar as it exists, the predicate does not distinguish the uniquely realized arrangement. If Good instead includes full due fulfillment, its actualization is the additional claim at issue.

These are missing implications, not a refutation of the privation thesis.

## 5. Two possible completions

### Completion S: a substantive selection/actualization principle

Value explains realization through an independently justified bridge such as the one above. This is strict selection-style axiarchism.

It must explain why that bridge holds, how the value ordering is determined, what happens if there are ties or no best member, and why a concrete world follows rather than only a formal selection. Defining the realized world as best after inspecting it would not supply the explanation.

### Completion C: an intrinsically concrete constitution

There is no independent menu plus selector at the fundamental level. One necessary concrete nature explains why possibility, actuality, qualitative life, and value have their relations. Its actual occurrence is not an unexplained extra attached to an abstract specification.

This fits the leading common-constitution programme more naturally. But it is a target, not a completed solution. Naming the nature “necessarily actual” or building R into its definition does not establish that anything instantiates it.

A successful C would have to justify its necessary concreteness independently and disclose how its nature explains value. If value and existence have a common explanation, strict axiarchism additionally needs to show that value is indispensable to that explanation, not merely a consequence of it.

Neither completion is established by this audit. C is the preferred research direction under the non-additive whole-account, not a proven result or a calibrated probability ranking.

## 6. Starting from occurrence without making it brute

The inquiry already has actual occurrence as a datum: the experiences and physical processes under investigation are not merely optional entries in a proposed menu. An immanent explanation can begin there and investigate what its nature must be, rather than pretend that an abstract description has been shown to produce existence.

Beginning from an actual datum is not permission to leave it unexplained. Under the user's objective, the account must eventually explain why this actuality obtains and why its constitutive nature is not contingent residue. The datum provides an epistemic starting point, not a metaphysical exemption.

Likewise, the fact that asking a question involves occurrence establishes occurrence in that inquiry. It does not alone establish that no reality could lack occurrence, that this occurrence is uniquely necessary, or that its foundation is normatively Good.

## 7. What this changes in the research programme

Previous work on due regard and actual fulfillment remains useful for explaining value's constitution. But a complete moral theory would not automatically discharge the existence debt. We should not indefinitely postpone the actualization question until every ethical dispute is settled.

The direct next questions are:

1. What concrete constitution is being proposed, beyond a list of desired explanatory properties?
2. Why must it be concrete rather than merely describable?
3. Does it independently determine physical and qualitative structure?
4. Does its value play an indispensable explanatory role, or merely characterize its consequences?
5. How does it explain finite deprivation without defining all observed harms as necessary?

These questions constrain an honest completion. No supplied numerical welfare weights or simulation can replace them.

## 8. Conclusion

A unique optimum is insufficient for actuality in the absence of a bridge. A conditional selector is insufficient for existence in the absence of an actualization premise. These are distinct gaps.

The strongest current positive direction remains a self-explanatory concrete constitution with a possible intrinsic axiological aspect. The privation proposal can contribute to the value side of that account. It has not yet derived the actualization side.

This is a rigorous limitation on the stated inference, not a verdict that the broader conjecture is likely false. No new empirical finding, executable model, or research test count is claimed.
