# Can the relation between positive and negative experience be intrinsic?

## The proposed principle

The [previous candidate](EXPERIENTIAL_ZERO_CANDIDATE_AND_ENERGY_BRIDGE.md) represented an experiential contribution as positive intensity minus negative intensity. Its weakness was clear: if the relative scale is freely chosen *after* the total is known, we can always make the total zero.

A promising alternative is to ground the relative scale in an **intrinsic polarity transformation**: a physically and experientially meaningful relation that exchanges positive and negative components. If that relation is fixed independently, and if the desired quantity is linear and changes sign under it, the relative coefficient is determined rather than fitted to the total.

This yields a definite conditional result. It does **not** yet show that pain and pleasure possess such a symmetry, that the actual whole is symmetric, or that experience is generated by zero. The intended goal remains one self-subsistent experiential whole with no unexplained residue, not a new external operator that manufactures subjects.

## 1. A conditional uniqueness result

Let `(p,n,r)` represent, provisionally, positive, negative and neutral/other experiential components, with `p,n,r >= 0`. Assume the relevant space supports the stated linear operations. The numbers do not by themselves constitute felt experience or establish a unit of phenomenal intensity.

Consider an involutive polarity map, for a fixed `alpha>0`,

```text
J_alpha(p,n,r) = (alpha n, p/alpha, r),
J_alpha(J_alpha(p,n,r)) = (p,n,r).
```

It exchanges the positive and negative axes and leaves this neutral component unchanged. `alpha` converts between their presently chosen numerical units. Now ask for a real linear contribution

```text
q(p,n,r) = a p + b n + c r
```

that is **odd** under the same map: `q(J_alpha x)=-q(x)` for every allowed x. Comparing coefficients gives

```text
b = -alpha a,       c = 0,
q(p,n,r) = a (p - alpha n).
```

Thus, for a **specified** polarity map, the linear odd quantity is unique up to one overall scale. Choosing the positive direction and setting `a=1` in an appropriate unit gives `q=p-alpha n`. In matched coordinates with `alpha=1`, it reduces to `p-n`. Neutral experience has zero contribution to this *odd scalar*, not zero existence or zero qualitative content.

This calculation gives a principled route to the relative coefficient: it follows from the transformation rather than a separate choice of weights. It does **not** by itself force the global sum to vanish. Additivity and linearity are substantive restrictions; nonlinear odd quantities, such as `(p-alpha n)^3`, are not ruled out merely by oddness. Whether physical experiential intensity has the assumed vector-space structure needs an argument.

## 2. Where the apparent gain can become circular

The polarity map itself carries the calibration. In particular,

```text
J_alpha(0,1,0) = (alpha,0,0).
```

It says exactly which positive magnitude corresponds to one unit of the negative coordinate. There is a family of equally well-defined formal involutions `J_alpha`, one for each `alpha>0`; mathematics alone does not select one. If the only reason to choose a particular J is that its coefficient makes an already-known total zero, the new formulation merely relocates the fitting problem.

The useful, **non-circular** possibility is stronger: first establish the polarity map from the actual intrinsic constitution or independent consequences, and only then apply its uniquely associated odd quantity to the whole. An identity can do explanatory work if warranted by its content and relations; it does not have to be an external cause. But saying “this map is zero's nature” is not on its own that warrant.

The numerical value of alpha is also not itself independent of units. Under a change of coordinates `p'=u p`, `n'=v n` with `u,v>0`, the *same* polarity relation is represented by

```text
alpha' = u alpha/v,
p' - alpha' n' = u (p - alpha n).
```

Thus a change of units alone cannot alter whether the defined odd quantity totals zero. What needs independent justification is the underlying **matching relation**, not a particular displayed number such as `alpha=1`. Changing alpha while holding the coordinate conventions and all other physical identifications fixed changes that proposed relation; it is not just a unit conversion.

## 3. From intrinsic polarity to a zero whole

A second, separate premise can turn the calibrated quantity into the user's proposed global zero. Suppose the actual finite whole has a domain X of occurrences or aspects, with weights `w_i>=0` and a bijection T such that

```text
x_(T i) = J_alpha(x_i),     w_(T i) = w_i.
```

Then, for the odd q above,

```text
Q_X = sum_i w_i q(x_i)
    = sum_i w_(T i) q(x_(T i))
    = -Q_X,
so Q_X = 0.
```

No physical process need pair the occurrences in external time. T describes a structural relation within one whole, if it exists. It may exchange occurrences in one subject or between subjects; the formula alone establishes neither. With a well-defined T-invariant measure and an integrable q, the same change-of-variables argument works for an infinite domain. An infinite positive part minus an infinite negative part is not automatically defined.

The distinction between the two premises is important:

1. **Polarity:** J fixes which linear signed quantity to consider.
2. **Whole symmetry:** the actual domain, full relations and weights are preserved under T, producing a zero total.

The first does not entail the second. For example, with `alpha=1`, two stipulated occurrences `(p,n)=(2,0)` and `(0,1)` have `Q_X=1`. The type-level map exists formally, but the actual collection is not preserved by swapping its components. Conversely, a zero total need not have a pairwise polarity symmetry: `(+2,-1,-1)` sums to zero without a one-to-one sign-reversing permutation. Other intrinsically grounded, possibly non-additive, routes to zero remain open.

If T is a gauge redescription rather than an active physical transformation, the sign-odd q requires an orientation or relational interpretation; it is not automatically an invariant signed observable. A zero value can be invariant even when individual signed descriptions depend on a frame. The account must specify the transformation and its physical meaning rather than assume that a relabelling enforces a physical symmetry.

## 4. What would independently justify an experiential polarity?

The principle has to connect more than two numerical axes. A defensible proposal would specify:

- **Actual qualitative reference:** why its positive and negative directions correspond to positive feeling and suffering, including mixed and neutral cases, without making pain unreal or morally cancelled.
- **A genuine intrinsic relation:** what physical/experiential organization realizes J and why applying it is a legitimate transformation of the relevant full structure. Familiar opposites or the ability to write `(p,n)->(n,p)` do not establish such a relation in nature.
- **Magnitude matching:** what fixes the relation between positive and negative intensities, including across subjects if a global sum compares them. Ordinary reports can constrain accounts but do not by themselves provide direct access to another subject's feeling or establish a common cardinal unit.
- **Full relations and incidence:** whether and how the transformation acts on subject boundaries, memories, physical couplings and relational time. A symmetry of a selected score is not automatically a symmetry of the entire experiential whole.
- **Independent consequences:** what independently calibrated local observations, reports or physical relations would differ if a competing matching relation were used, and how experimental confounds would be handled. Finite observations cannot directly measure the entire whole; a fit would not prove metaphysical necessity.

For example, one could seek an independently specified physical transformation of an experiential organization, plus separately anchored evidence that it exchanges the relevant positive and negative experiential roles. The relation must be defined *before* checking global balance. This is a research strategy, not a demonstration that such a transformation exists or a prescription to induce suffering.

A strong symmetry of pain and pleasure is not obvious. They can have different causes, functions, contexts and qualitative characters; a reversal of one variable need not preserve the full organization. Nor does the negative gravitational term in an energy account establish a symmetry exchanging pleasant and unpleasant feelings. The user's experiential proposal remains open if this **particular** symmetry route fails: cancellation may be grounded by another constitution or composition rule.

## 5. What the result says about self-subsistence

The candidate offers a coherent route for the positive part of the argument: **one intrinsic relational principle could determine both an experiential polarity and the relative scale**, without a separate external calibration agent. If the actual whole's organization also realizes the corresponding symmetry, zero follows while local suffering remains genuine.

But the explanation is unfinished until it shows why the polarity relation, its actual realization, the relevant weights and the experiential constitution are themselves intelligible without an independent unexplained ground. The map may ultimately be one aspect of the self-subsistent whole rather than an extra thing; that identity remains to be established. Direct acquaintance provides genuine access to a local experience but does not automatically prove the universal map or the whole's symmetry. Nothing here establishes or refutes the user's further claim that zero is uniquely capable of complete intelligibility, or that structural and experiential intelligibility must be jointly satisfiable.

This is not an attempt to make suffering acceptable by cancellation: a subject still experiences harm locally. Nor does the argument presume a universal subject, a perfect world, or an external time. The next direct question is whether the **matching relation itself** can be specified from the whole's experiential and physical constitution independently of its desired total, and whether any such relation survives the full qualitative and subject-boundary demands.
