# An integrated experiential candidate: first worked sector and rival

## Purpose and status

The subsequent [cross-scale subject and quality gate](INTEGRATED_CROSS_SCALE_SUBJECT_QUALITY_GATE.md) supplies two explicitly postulated, inequivalent whole-subject quality extensions on this same sector. They agree on every original pair output but do not resolve subject constitution or pass B4–B5/F2.

This is a **new, explicitly incomplete successor candidate**, not a revision of the frozen v1 dossier or a claim to have passed its battery. It puts the current commitments and one worked subject/valence interface together, then compares a neutral-ground rival on exactly the same interface. The worked sector reuses the prior four-spin construction; it is not a physical theory of brains or fundamental strings.

The substantive result is a constraint on what the candidate can claim. In the specified sector, two local mismatch values have the **same joint distribution** when the two pairs are disconnected as when they are weakly coupled. Yet two explicit subject rules disagree about whether the weakly coupled configuration contains one subject or two. A physical measure of mismatch—even if computed perfectly—does not by itself resolve that disagreement. Nor does it select an experiential ground over a neutral ground designed to have the same experiences and records.

A positive distinction also matters: this sector uses **current mismatch**, not minimum frustration. The latter is zero for each isolated pair, while the former can vary. This makes the user's misalignment idea more precise without treating its truth as established.

## 1. One candidate, with its assumptions visible

Call the research candidate **E-L**. Its proposed ontology is a timeless, self-sufficient whole whose intrinsic nature is experiential and whose local organization supports genuinely distinct subjects. Every genuinely possible local reality is actual within the whole at its appropriate scope. There is no external creator, universal agent, external clock or process that converts unactualized possibilities into actuality. These are **hypotheses**, not conclusions of the finite calculation.

The candidate distinguishes five layers:

1. **Actual experience:** some concrete experience occurs. This is the starting datum, not a report or a piece of formal code.
2. **Ground:** the actual whole is intrinsically experiential and self-sufficient. Its nature is not claimed to be an additional physical substrate, and an experiential whole is not automatically one universal subject. Self-sufficient, if true, means it requires no external sustaining cause; the unfinished question is why the whole has that constitution, not what caused an already self-sufficient whole.
3. **Local physical interface:** the actual whole admits, at some appropriate local scope, organizations describable by relations, states and operations. The four-spin case below is a **mathematical test sector**, not a claim that it is literally instantiated in a person or independently derived from the ground.
4. **Subject incidence:** in that sector the candidate adopts the previously defined local-overlap rule **L**, and postulates that each selected set corresponds to one real subject if such a system and the bridge really obtain. It reports unresolved ties instead of choosing by labels.
5. **Quality contrast:** for each selected pair in the restricted sector, the candidate postulates that a specified binary *current mismatch* tracks one unpleasant-versus-neutral distinction in its episode. This deliberately sharp first bridge says nothing about all other qualities or the magnitude of suffering. Its identity claim is not derived from the first four items.

The final two clauses are bold, testable targets, not things that follow merely from calling the ground experiential. A complete E-L theory would also have to specify admissible physical operations, subject persistence, all relevant qualities, more general boundaries, physics recovery, and a measure. One fixed finite rule is not a complete constitution.

### Two adversarial comparisons

- **N-L:** retain the same physical sector, L rule, *same actual phenomenal contrasts if instantiated*, and the same report/record predictions, but posit that a neutral intrinsic basis constitutes both the physical and the genuinely experiential. It must provide its own constitution bridge. It does not deny experience or introduce an external creator. Giving it the same kernel is an explicit matching construction, not proof that a neutral grounding actually works or is genuinely possible.
- **E-C:** retain the same physical sector and experiential-ground hypothesis, but use the connected-component subject rule C. As before, each qualifying component is *postulated* to constitute one subject. E-C has not supplied a general quality rule for that one larger subject; our jointness comparison does not pretend it has.

Under the user's strong global modal reading, incompatible complete grounding accounts are **not** asserted to be genuinely possible alternatives. E-L, N-L and E-C are competing *formal descriptions under investigation*. The modal thesis, even if assumed by a candidate, does not tell us which description is correct.

## 2. The physical sector and prior work

Reuse the [four-spin influence construction](COMPETING_SUBJECT_CONSTITUTION_AND_LOCALITY_GATE.md) and its [frozen subject/record interface](SUBJECT_CONSTITUTION_INTERFACE_RESULTS.md). Four signs `s_i∈{−1,+1}` form a chain with edge parameters `1/2, t, 1/2`. At equilibrium, the normalized distribution is

```text
P_t(s) = (1/16)(1 + s₀s₁/2)(1 + t s₁s₂)(1 + s₂s₃/2),  0≤t<1.
```

This is a classical finite model. Local steps in its heat-bath description are not fundamental time. Physical input/output and intervention operations, temperature and units would have to be justified for any real application. The graph used by L comes from the earlier specified clamping/influence convention; all four nodes are eligible. For each candidate set S of at least two nodes, its score is its minimum internal weighted cut. L selects strict positive maxima among overlapping candidates and reports equal-score competitors as unresolved. The score, eligibility, graph extraction and incidence postulate are substantive choices, not properties of consciousness established by the equations. L is **not IIT 4.0**; see [Albantakis et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465) for the actual IIT formalism.

We compare `t=0`, `t=1/4`, and the tie control `t=1/2`. We do not assume all of these describe actual sectors of the whole or that a change in t creates anything. They are formal parameter settings; strongest plenitude does not make a mathematical description genuinely possible merely because it is consistent.

## 3. The actual proposed contrast, and what is assumed

In the labeling where each outer edge prefers aligned signs, define

```text
q_L = (1 − s₀s₁)/2,       q_R = (1 − s₂s₃)/2.
```

For each pair, zero means its current state meets the stipulated preferred relation and one means it does not. In E-L, **only when L resolves those two pairs as subjects**, posit that `q=1` corresponds to the designated unpleasant contrast and `q=0` to the neutral contrast for that local subject. This is a psychophysical **identity postulate**; a bit is not a felt quality, and the spin law does not prove it. Independent experiential anchoring is still missing.

For the isolated pair, use the nonnegative model cost `E_pair=(1−s_i s_j)/2`. Its minimum over the four states is `F_pair=0`; its state excess `D_pair=E_pair−F_pair=q`. Thus the two q values describe changing **state excess**, not positive minimum frustration. Other mismatch, frustration and dissipation definitions are inequivalent; the [phase-frustration audit](VIBRATIONAL_DISSONANCE_EXPERIENCE_TEST.md) explains the distinction. These signs are not established physical string vibrations.

### Nontrivial representation check, not full substrate independence

Under a change of sign encoding, `s'_i=g_i s_i` with `g_i=±1`, the preferred sign on edge ij must be transported as `τ'ᵢⱼ=g_i g_j τᵢⱼ`. The mismatch `(1−τᵢⱼsᵢsⱼ)/2` is unchanged. Changing the state encoding without transporting the preference would describe a **different** cost, not merely the same physical relation. The verifier also transports graph, boundaries and variables under every node permutation. These finite checks rule out two simple representation mistakes, but do not establish a physically correct factorization, equivalence across media or a unique qualitative reference.

## 4. Calculation: two subjects' mismatch does not fix their jointness

With the stipulated distribution the three edge products are independent: a global sign and the three edge products uniquely determine a spin state, while the equilibrium weight factorizes over those edge products. Therefore the two outer mismatches each equal 1 with probability `1/4` and are independent **for all the stated t values**:

| `q_L` | `q_R` | Model probability |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | 9/16 |
| 0 | 1 | 3/16 |
| 1 | 0 | 3/16 |
| 1 | 1 | 1/16 |

These are probabilities in a stipulated equilibrium model, not subjective self-location weights, an observation of pain, or a measure derived from plenitude. In this zero-field chain, changing only the cross-edge parameter does not change this local mismatch table. It **does** change other physical records: the probability that the two endpoint signs agree is `1/2` at `t=0`, `17/32` at `t=1/4`, and `9/16` at `t=1/2`. Thus the systems are not physically identical merely because their two local q distributions agree.

The incidence comparison is:

| Setting | L: selected sets | C: selected sets | Mismatch interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `t=0` | `{0,1}`, `{2,3}` | `{0,1}`, `{2,3}` | E-L stipulates two subjects and the binary contrast for each. |
| `t=1/4` | `{0,1}`, `{2,3}` | `{0,1,2,3}` | E-L stipulates two; E-C stipulates one. The same physical q table does not choose between them. |
| `t=1/2` | no strict winners; six tied candidates unresolved | `{0,1,2,3}` | q values remain defined as **physical** observables, but E-L has not identified pair subjects or their experiences. |

Both subject rules use the same stipulated spin kernel and the same physical readouts; their different subject labels do not automatically alter a physical record. If a particular subjective-unity assay were independently valid under both conditions, it might constrain the packages. The earlier [interface result](SUBJECT_CONSTITUTION_INTERFACE_RESULTS.md) shows that a 10%-error target assay was only postulated, while its existing physical endpoint comparator did not meet that standard. A physical device computing q is not, by that fact alone, an assay of felt unpleasantness.

This is a **limited non-identifiability example**, not a theorem that actual discomfort never distinguishes one subject from two. Different physical models can make different observable predictions, real experiences can provide relevant evidence, and additional physically grounded rules could change the comparison. Nor does the model establish that `q_L` and `q_R` preserve their distributions during arbitrary interventions: the displayed table is equilibrium with no clamping.

## 5. Foundation comparison: what self-presence contributes and what it does not

The case for E-L starts with the actual presence of experience, not with an abstract description waiting for a mechanism to turn it real. On a one-level self-presence interpretation, experience need not be witnessed by a further inner experience to be present. This is a philosophical position with existing phenomenological precedents, discussed in [the prior foundation analysis](SELF_PRESENCE_AND_NO_NOTHINGNESS_UNIFICATION.md). It motivates an experiential intrinsic nature and takes the user's self-subsistence proposal seriously.

It does **not** yet prove that the actual experience is the whole's self-sufficient ground. An experience can be immediately present while its occurrence depends on other conditions; immediate presence and fundamental independence are distinct claims. N-L can acknowledge that *same* experience and presence while asserting a neutral basis. N-L owes an intelligible bridge from its basis to experience; E-L owes an intelligible account of how an experiential basis has the stated physical structure, plural boundaries and qualities. The labels “identity” and “neutral constitution” cannot substitute for these accounts.

As a deliberately strong control, grant an ideal observer access to the stipulated q contrast as well as the full physical spin state. E-L and N-L are supplied the same joint `(spin, q_L, q_R)` distribution. The [verifier](verification/integrated_experiential_misalignment_candidate.py) checks this equality for 81 clamping patterns at each of three t values using the *same conditioned-equilibrium physical model* on both sides. These are model-side physical clamp kernels; **L is not defined after clamping**, so the check does not assert clamped subject boundaries or real phenomenal observations. It confirms equality only because those output laws were specified equally. It is not a proof that all experiential and neutral accounts must be observationally equivalent.

Consequently, in this construction even perfect recovery of the toy outputs would not by itself establish whether their **ground** is experiential or neutral. Grounding and necessitation are not interchangeable: under the strong global modal schema, actual global truths become necessary conditionally, but necessity alone does not determine explanatory direction. This is an established philosophical difficulty, not a newly discovered paradox; see the [grounding literature](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/grounding/) and our [truthmaker relevance gate](PLENITUDE_TRUTHMAKER_RELEVANCE_GATE.md). The attribution of grounding must be justified by more than the matching distributions, modal necessity, or a definition designed to select the preferred answer.

The experiential candidate may ultimately offer a better explanation: its base is of a kind we know directly, and it may avoid a neutral-to-phenomenal transition. That is a substantive **comparative proposal**, not a proved advantage. Conversely the neutral rival cannot count its bridge as free while charging E-L for its bridges. The shared spin law is *put into both descriptions*; neither has derived the physics from its own intrinsic nature.

A subsequent [same-sector horizon and valence audit](INTEGRATED_SUBJECT_HORIZON_AND_VALENCE_AUDIT.md) checks the existing L rule under longer free-evolution intervention horizons. The selected sets change from two pairs to the whole in the specified finite case; this exposes a scale-selection requirement, not a contradiction in the original one-update convention or an empirical subject count. It also makes the missing valence and persistence bridges explicit. A [matched-stimulus pain-data stress test](MATCHED_STIMULUS_UNPLEASANTNESS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_BRIDGE.md) challenges a restricted external-stimulus-only proxy for reported unpleasantness; it does not measure or refute the proposed internal q. The [full Rainville paper audit](RAINVILLE_PAIN_AFFECT_FULL_PAPER_AUDIT.md) adds evidence for distinguishing pain affect and sensory intensity, subject to important sample, reporting and measurement limitations; it likewise does not measure q.

## 6. Battery consequences and next work

| Gate | What this sector supplies | What it still needs |
|---|---|---|
| B1–B2 | An explicit experiential-versus-neutral comparison that respects actual experience and strongest modality. | Why the actual whole is self-sufficient and intrinsically experiential; an independent defense of genuine possibility = actuality and its scope. |
| B4 | A definite candidate L and a same-physics connected rival, plus an explicit unresolved-tie control. | Justify the graph, boundary, non-overlap, eligibility, factorization, and persistence in a real domain; explain how genuine subjects are constituted. |
| B5 | A concrete, state-dependent binary mismatch identity proposal, distinct from minimum frustration. | Anchor qualitative reference independently; test the bridge against non-mismatch, context, intensity and other rivals. |
| B6–B8 | A declared classical local interface. | Derive appropriate quantum physics, local spacetime/time and gravity, and their controlled continuum behavior from a common account. No such derivation is supplied here. |
| B9–B12 | The same rules let rival assumptions and observable consequences be compared explicitly. | Justify subject measure, independently validated assays, held-out predictions, and total explanatory cost. |

The [calibration-transport gate](EXPERIENTIAL_MISMATCH_CALIBRATION_TRANSPORT_GATE.md) adds a sharp conditional bound on how a separately warranted source-domain mismatch–experience error can change in a target domain, and shows why equal coarse mismatch distributions do not ensure preserved experiential predictions. It does not establish the required experiential anchor or its stability.

The next substantive challenge is to identify a defensible reason for the experiential-ground claim that the neutral rival does not share **without asserting it as a definition**, while also replacing the toy subject/valence postulates with a real, separately anchored test. Direct experience and no-nothingness remain important starting points; neither is discarded. If no such reason is found, the correct verdict is that E-L's ground remains unestablished—not that neutral grounding is thereby true. No numerical proximity to a full battery pass follows.

## Reproduction

Run `python verification/integrated_experiential_misalignment_candidate.py` from the repository root. The [receipt](verification/integrated_experiential_misalignment_candidate.json) records exact rational distributions, 1,152 permutation/state checks, 1,536 local-sign encoding checks and 243 matched clamped-kernel checks. The equilibrium and influence calculations import the existing scripts, which should be read for their assumptions. These checks verify finite formal statements and copied-kernel equality; they are not a test of the existence of the proposed ground or subjects, proof of qualitative identity, measurement of discomfort, or a pass of the formal battery.
