# Retrospective EEG baseline-deviation test: fixed analysis plan

## Provenance and hypothesis

This plan is fixed before inspecting numerical EEG/valence relationships or running the models below; it is not a prospective preregistration. We have read the public secondary repository's README and extraction code, its published subject-held-out classifier results and the feature-table schema. It claims to contain DREAMER-derived features from Katsigiannis and Ramzan's [DREAMER dataset](https://doi.org/10.1109/JBHI.2017.2688239), but we have not obtained the original data, validated this table against it, verified preprocessing, or checked consent and data-specific redistribution permissions. Conclusions are therefore conditional on third-party provenance. The repository at commit `4f7da1a235e9f144928d648a4d7a6c541de6bf2b` advertises no license. Keep the table under ignored `.arena/` and output only aggregate results.

Define a fixed **exploratory neural-signal deviation proxy**, not an inferred subject-internal interaction frustration: the root-mean-square across 14 scalp channels of the supplied alpha-band feature values. The supplied extraction code specifies each as `log1p(alpha_Welch_power_stimulus) − log1p(alpha_Welch_power_baseline)`; baseline is the trial-specific preceding comparison recording, not a proved neural optimum or neutral phenomenal ground. This yields a nonnegative magnitude for deviation in measured scalp spectral power. It depends on units/reference/preprocessing, cannot recover phase couplings or preferred offsets, and is not F, D or P in our oscillator model. As a comparator use the signed mean across those same 14 feature values. Both are defined without looking at the target labels. No label is generated from either proxy.

Outcome is supplied self-reported valence 1–5 treated as numeric, not direct continuous experience. The basic baseline is the training mean; where film identity is available at prediction time, a stronger baseline is the film's training-subject mean. Compare with each baseline plus one linear proxy effect, fitted on training data only. For the film-ID model, estimate a common slope after centering proxy and outcome within each film in the training fold, then predict `mean_y_film + slope*(q − mean_q_film)` in the test subject. Do not fit or standardize using held-out people or outcomes. Report ordinary out-of-fold RMSE and MAE, along with paired mean squared-error differences and their fixed-prediction participant-bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples, seed 1823; positive means candidate worsens MSE). The intervals condition on the fitted cross-validation predictions and selected films and omit model-refit and film-sampling uncertainty. No causal or population effect-size claim follows merely from an interval. Give all proxy variants, not only the winner.

## Two distinct generalization tests

1. **Leave one person out**, all clips available in training subjects: compare grand mean, grand mean + deviation, grand mean + signed mean, film mean, film mean + deviation, and film mean + signed mean. Film means are calculated from training people only. This tests a new person on **familiar film identities**; the film baseline does not use that person's labels.
2. **Double-disjoint blocked test:** assign sorted subjects to five groups by index modulo 5, and sorted film IDs to three groups by index modulo 3. For every one of the 15 crossed cells, train on rows whose subject and film are both outside the held-out groups and test only rows whose subject and film are both in them. Every observation gets one prediction. Compare only grand mean, grand mean + deviation, and grand mean + signed mean: film-specific coefficients cannot be evaluated for unseen films. Test rows are separate from train rows on both axes. There is no acoustic or content predictor for unseen films here; the grand mean is a weak baseline, not a state-of-the-art stimulus model. Use the same subject bootstrap but emphasize that 18 fixed films and overlapping training folds limit inference. Do not interpret it as an independent replication.

Validate complete subject×clip design, 14 finite alpha features per row, unique subject/clip keys, numeric 1–5 outcomes and zero overlap in every split. Output aggregate counts and metrics only, not participant features or individual labels. Record hash of the source CSV. Linear fits use no input-dependent target information; if proxy variance is zero predict the training mean rather than dividing by zero. Signed versus magnitude is not an optimization over candidate neurofeatures.

A result where deviation improves held-out error relative to both the film baseline and signed comparison would be exploratory predictive evidence only. A result that fails to improve does not rule out richer, independently identified internal mismatch. These measures may reflect eye or muscle artifacts, references, preprocessing, stimulus properties, arousal, subject identity and other variables. No within-person randomization of q or intervention is performed. This analysis cannot establish internal optimality, identify an experienced subject, link the candidate to string theory, or explain why valence feels as it does. If independent provenance remains unavailable, even a positive result is provisional.
