# Fixed plan: a concrete synaptic preference calibration and transport gate

## Prior access and scope

The prior physical `q` gates and published Jin, Li and Zhang reports are known. Before this plan, search results and method descriptions on paired neural recordings and synaptic reversal were read; no new participant outcomes, raw EEG, behavioral files or paired-cell data were inspected. This is a prospective design and an exact, invented-probability control, not a preregistration of the already-published studies or an operational clinical protocol.

## Chosen physical target

Specify a directed, local pair A→B of identified, directly connected neurons in an ex vivo, consented neurosurgical-tissue preparation. A is presynaptic; B is postsynaptic. Encode `a=+1` for a presynaptic spike in a fixed pre-window and `a=−1` for no spike; encode `b=+1` for a postsynaptic spike in a fixed subsequent window and `b=−1` otherwise. Fix bins, state preparation and context before any calibration or outcome comparison. The candidate target is the contribution of the designated A→B synaptic pathway to the difference in conditional B-spike log odds between experimentally set A states, not a scalp electrode pair, a correlation or a presumed microscopic Hamiltonian coefficient. Define `K` as that pathway's interaction contrast on the log-odds scale in an explicit additive decomposition; `τ=sign(K)` only if the separate binary pair-potential interpretation is defensible, and remains unresolved at zero or if the interpretation fails. This directed lagged construction is a proposed extension, not automatically the earlier equal-time E-L interaction.

Describe a randomized within-preparation on/off and A-state design, independent checks for state assignment, attenuation and residual drift, state and calibration transport, and a detailed refusal rule. Neither ex vivo tissue nor a group average supplies a first-person report or the same live subject and episode. No physical intervention in humans is recommended.

## Fixed synthetic control

Four hypothetical arms use Bernoulli outcomes for B with `P(b=+1|a=−1,on)=1/5`, `P(b=+1|a=+1,on)=3/5`, `P(b=+1|a=−1,off)=3/10`, and `P(b=+1|a=+1,off)=1/2`. Set `D_g=logit(p_g,+)−logit(p_g,−)`, `Δ=D_on−D_off=λK+δ`, `|δ|≤b` and `1/2≤λ≤1`. Use `n=10000` independent hypothetical observations in each arm, with observed proportions equal to these four probabilities. Obtain simultaneous intervals for the four probabilities by Hoeffding and a union bound with total nominal failure at most 0.05; then propagate endpoints monotonically through logits, Δ and the interval for K. Use hypothetical systematic bound `b=0.3`, and then `b=1.2`; report certificate or unresolved, not both as facts about equipment. Derive exact `D_on`, `D_off`, `Δ` and provide opposite-sign target models with exactly identical on/off conditional distributions when `b=1.2`, by choosing `λ=1`, `K=1` and `K=−1/4`. Check residuals, full probability support, interval containment and correct state-relabeling with tests. Rational exact odds where possible; logarithms evaluated numerically. These values are illustrative inputs, not a pilot dataset or sample-size recommendation.

## Decision and eventual comparison

Separate the causal effect of intervening on A, removal of a particular synapse/pathway, the conditional binary interaction potential and a first-person quality bridge. The latter two do not follow from a spike response. Give falsifiable physical and measurement prerequisites, including sham/placebo gate controls, channel specificity, residual bounds and uncertainty, nonstationarity, classification errors and common inputs. For a future genuine study fix inclusion, analysis, uncertainty, baseline comparators and a single primary held-out endpoint before opening target outcomes. Stop if a linked same-episode physical measurement is not independently justified; a calibrated slice from the same donor is not the same interaction in the living person. Keep PAINgen unresolved and distinguish all pain constructs.
