# One physical constitution: zero, relational distinction and intrinsic experience

## Purpose and result

The user's clarified task is not to treat brute fact or unresolved regress as live options to be developed. On the user's full-intelligibility principle, those fail the requirement: the aim is to find the whole's **own self-sufficient, noncausal constitution**. The user adds that experience is an **intrinsic identity**, in a proper form: no second substance or extra effect is generated by physical processes; nor does every physical distinction automatically constitute a subject.

This pass constructs a single physical toy model in which a zero gravitational constraint, local energy, relational time and the physical distinctions used in a candidate experiential contrast belong to **the same state and the same equations**. We do not paste the earlier spin circuit into a separate cosmology. A relational field observable is invariant under changes of the model's time parameter; its present value and the fact of a recent threshold crossing are demonstrably distinct even when the constraint vanishes.

This is progress on a physical recovery map, not the final constitution. The toy does **not** derive a subject, consciousness, a physical–experiential identity, or the actual universe's total energy. Its two scalar fields are homogeneous and the model contains no localized organism or report apparatus. Nevertheless, it puts the outstanding question in one place: **what intrinsic organization, accessible both structurally and experientially, makes a particular physical occurrence identical to a particular lived occurrence?** The [tests](verification/zero_intrinsic_identity_relational_gravity_test.py) check the mathematical relations under the stated premises.

## 1. The guiding identity, without an extra mental object

Let `S` be a candidate subject and `O` an occurrence constituted by some physical–relational organization. The identity thesis does **not** say that an equation creates feeling, or that a physical description causes a separate nonphysical object to appear. It says that, in the appropriate organized case,

```text
one occurrence O
  ├─ describable in physical–relational terms
  └─ directly present as a particular lived character to S.
```

Those arrows are not two substances or a temporal production process. They indicate different **access to and descriptions of one occurrence**. The physical side must be a physically meaningful description, not an arbitrary symbol; the experiential side must respect what is actually lived, not explain it away as a report. Direct acquaintance can supply information not replaceable by a list of symbols without implying an ontological gap.

We should not infer from this thesis that every field, interaction or subsystem is a subject, that a mathematical sign is pleasant or unpleasant, or that different people can automatically know one another's experience. The right subject and grain of organization remain to be identified. “Intrinsic” here means that experience belongs to the occurrence itself, not that it must be a property of a pointlike particle or independent of all internal relations. The subject may itself be relationally constituted.

An identity can be necessary without being obvious from either vocabulary alone. But asserting the identity is not the same as deriving it. To advance, we need a specific common candidate, its recovery map, and independently anchored experiential–physical correspondences, rather than silently defining a mathematical bit as a feeling. Such anchoring need not be an external creator or an extra psychophysical force: first-person acquaintance, reports and physical evidence are themselves internal to the world.

## 2. The shared physical state

Take the compact, spatially flat classical FLRW model used in the [previous GR recovery pass](ZERO_ENERGY_CONSTRAINT_REFERENCE_AND_PHYSICAL_RECOVERY.md), now with **two free homogeneous massless scalar fields** `φ,χ`, unit fiducial three-torus volume, `a>0`, lapse `N`, cosmological constant zero, and `κ=8πG>0`. In units `c=1`, the Lagrangian is

```text
L = −3a ȧ²/(κN) + a³(φ̇²+χ̇²)/(2N).
```

The canonical momenta and lapse constraint are

```text
pₐ = −6a ȧ/(κN),    pφ = a³φ̇/N,    pχ = a³χ̇/N,
H = NC,
C = −κpₐ²/(12a) + (pφ²+pχ²)/(2a³) = 0.
```

The same canonical state `(a,pₐ,φ,pφ,χ,pχ)` supplies both the gravitational balance and the relational distinctions below. Here `φ` is a clock **in the sector `pφ≠0`**; `χ` is another real, homogeneous physical field. The simplicity is deliberate, and it is not a claim that actual experience is a homogeneous scalar or that this action is derived from full intelligibility.

The local scalar energy density and Hubble parameter on the constraint surface satisfy

```text
ρ = (pφ²+pχ²)/(2a⁶) > 0       when (pφ,pχ) ≠ (0,0),
Hubble² = κρ/3.
```

The negative canonical geometric term balances the positive scalar kinetic term in C. This is an exact consequence of the assumed action and lapse variation, not a force applied after the fact. It is also **not yet** an established, invariantly defined total energy for the actual universe; the compact Hamiltonian constraint is a gauge generator, and separate gravitational contributions have representation-dependent interpretations. Nonzero vacuum terms, inhomogeneity, quantum theory, physical boundary charges and actual cosmology are not included.

## 3. A real internal distinction, not a coordinate-time artifact

Because `pφ,pχ` are constant and `pφ≠0`, the equations of motion give

```text
dχ/dφ = pχ/pφ.
```

The value of χ **when φ reads τ** is therefore

```text
χ[τ] = χ + (pχ/pφ)(τ−φ).
```

This is a complete relational observable in this restricted sector. Using the canonical Poisson brackets,

```text
{χ[τ], C} = (−pχ/pφ)(pφ/a³) + pχ/a³ = 0.
```

This equality is strong here: it holds even off the constraint surface within the domain `pφ≠0`. Equivalently, changing the evolution parameter moves both φ and χ along the same history without changing the value χ takes when φ equals τ. We are not introducing an outside observer or universal time.

Choose a threshold `χref` and define an explicitly **physical candidate bit**

```text
qτ = 1 if χ[τ] ≥ χref, otherwise 0.
```

At the threshold itself the chosen `≥` convention matters; no smoothness claim is made for the bit there. Away from it, the clock-relative bit is invariant along the gauge flow because χ[τ] is. `χref` is a stipulated comparison reference, not a derived subject, record or detector. If χ and `χref` are both re-expressed after the same constant field-origin shift, qτ is unchanged. That simultaneous change is a **redescription of the comparison**; shifting χ while keeping a physically fixed reference unchanged can change qτ. We do not declare every scalar shift to be a gauge symmetry, and we have not built a physical reference system.

This observable is a feature of the **same gravity–matter model** that gives C=0. It is not an independent toy system attached to a zero by name. But it is still merely a physical relational distinction. Its presence does not make it experienced.

## 4. Change, balance and history in one exact example

Set `κ=6` and select the expanding branch. At the clock reading `φ=1`, consider the two on-constraint states

```text
a=1, pₐ=−5, pφ=3, pχ=4, χref=0,
A: χ=1/2;      B: χ=2.
```

In both states,

```text
C = −25/2 + 25/2 = 0,
ρ = 25/2 > 0,
Hubble = 5,
da/dφ = 5/3.
```

The geometric and kinetic terms are computed at the stated clock reading, not globally conserved separate local energies. On the expanding solution through either state,

```text
a(τ) = exp[(5/3)(τ−1)],
χ[τ] = χ(φ=1) + (4/3)(τ−1).
```

Compare the clock readings `τ=0` and `τ=1`:

| State | `χ[0]` | `χ[1]` | `q₀` | `q₁` | Onset `q₁(1−q₀)` |
|---|---:|---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| A | `−5/6` | `1/2` | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| B | `2/3` | `2` | 1 | 1 | 0 |

Both have the same zero constraint, positive density, the same present level bit and different onset bits. The full scalar readings differ, so these are **not** two histories ending in the identical complete physical state. Indeed, given the complete state and the declared reversible equations, the earlier reading is recoverable without a separate memory. What loses the distinction is the coarse-graining to `q₁` alone. A real subject's memory or report interface would need its own physical specification.

The example makes three things compatible in one model: an exact zero constraint, nonzero local physical energy and nontrivial internal change. The threshold bit does not change or add energy to the state. It is not assumed to have a value in joules, nor does its 0/1 value represent signed physical energy or a sum of pleasures and pains.

For the scale factor one may define `α=−κ a pₐ/(6pφ)`. Direct calculation gives `{α,C}=−κC/(2pφ)`, so α is constant **on shell**, not generally off shell. Consequently `a[τ]=a exp[α(τ−φ)]` is invariant on the constraint surface. This supports the relational scale-factor formula; it is not an exact off-shell conservation claim. The [tests](verification/zero_intrinsic_identity_relational_gravity_test.py) check both the on-shell and off-shell statements.

## 5. A proper identity candidate—and the selection problem

A minimal candidate compatible with the user's position is conditional:

> Suppose a physically specified, sufficiently organized occurrence `O` belongs to a real subject `S`, and suppose its physical state includes a relational distinction that is correctly tracked by an observable such as `qτ`. Then a particular directly experienced contrast of `S` might be **identical with an aspect of O**, with `qτ` a partial physical descriptor of that contrast, rather than an external cause of a second thing.

This is intentionally **not** the assertion that our two-field homogeneous universe has a subject or that qτ is itself unpleasantness. A binary number can at best describe a chosen contrast, not the entire qualitative character of a lived episode. The model has no demonstrated local organization, memory/access mechanism, subject boundary or physically grounded threshold. It does not even fix why these two field types, this clock or these values occur. The literal identity candidate remains a program to be specified in an enriched physical model.

An important consistency control: the same physical equations admit `qτ`, `1−qτ`, and the onset bit as distinct invariant descriptors. One can use any of them to organize an abstract calculation; the equations alone do not say that one tracks a subject's particular feeling. If the experiential endpoints are fixed by independent first-person anchoring, inverting their assignment is a substantively different proposal, not just changing the names of symbols. If all labels **and their interpretations** are changed consistently, it is merely a redescription. A real anchor can distinguish such proposals, but it need not stand outside reality.

This does **not** require that the full texture of experience be exhaustible in language before an identity can be meaningful. It asks that the structural description connect to direct acquaintance in a principled way, and that the identification preserve the distinctions it claims to explain. A first-person acquaintance anchors a lived distinction; physical measurements and carefully specified reports can constrain which physical occurrence corresponds. None automatically supplies an infallible universal decoder or proves metaphysical necessity.

In the strongest realization, the identifying principle would make the two descriptions **necessarily co-refer** because of what the occurrence is, analogous to the way the geometrical constitution fixes the SR length relation without a force squeezing the rod. But the present constraint and threshold formulas have not supplied that principle. The SR analogy specifies the kind of explanation sought; it does not by itself establish the identity.

## 6. How the two forms of intelligibility fit

The user's distinction can be made precise without turning it into a metaphysical split:

- **Structural understanding:** equations, gauge-invariant relations, recovery maps, boundaries, dynamical dependencies and subject organization, to the extent correctly derived.
- **Experiential acquaintance:** what the actual occurrence is like for the subject, directly available in living it, and not replaced merely by having its symbolic description.
- **Unified intelligibility:** both are access to or understanding of one internally constituted reality. Neither a separate mental ingredient nor an external observer is required. Inability to communicate all of a quality in words is not the same as an unexplained brute quality.

The word “sum” is useful epistemically but must not suggest that structural description and experience are ontologically disjoint pieces whose union causes reality. Nor should it suggest that structural equations plus arbitrary private sensations automatically establish a particular correspondence. The actual common constitution must explain **which** occurrence, subject and quality belong together and why, including how internal evidence bears on them.

The two-field model demonstrates a coherent *physical format* for part of that program: a shared constraint and recoverable internal distinctions. It does not establish the unique identity, fully intelligible actuality, or the dominance claim that this is the only viable constitution. Those are affirmative next tasks, not an invitation to replace the user's demand with brute stopping points.
