# Correction: necessary plenitude as self-existent actuality, not literal simulation

## The correction

For the next conditional necessity and symmetry results, and the linked dependency-driven gap plan, see [Necessary experiential plenitude: narrowing the foundational gaps](PLENITUDE_FOUNDATION_GAPS_AND_SYMMETRY.md).

The user clarified that Bostrom's simulation hypothesis is a **starting point for the question of actuality**, not a request to posit aliens, computer simulations, simulated people or a creator within the framework. The previous [necessary-plenitude construction](NECESSARY_PLENITUDE_PLURAL_SUBJECTS_SIMULATION.md) got this wrong by adopting an actual internal simulation as a candidate premise. **Its N6 and its simulated/nonsimulated population are withdrawn as components of the user's framework.** Its arithmetic remains a conditional illustration of a different, literal scenario; it is not the intended reconstruction.

The relevant question is instead: **Why is there actual reality at all, rather than only something describable as possible—or nothing?** A creator outside the universe would move that question to the creator and whatever supports the creator. Taken as an explanation of reality *as a whole*, an external creator does not by itself eliminate the ultimate question.

The desired alternative is a **self-sufficient actuality**, not an externally produced world. In the user's stronger proposal, genuine possibility is actuality: what is genuinely possible is actual, and what is not actual was not genuinely possible. There is no intermediate warehouse of merely possible worlds and no separate operation that actualizes one of them.

## 1. The corrected candidate

> **Reality is one self-sufficient, necessarily actual whole whose intrinsic nature is experiential; it is internally differentiated into multiple genuinely distinct subjects and their experiences. Genuine possibility and actuality are identical, without a selector or a process turning possibility into actuality.**

This is the intended target, **not an achieved proof of it**. “One whole” means one encompassing actuality, not automatically one universal experiencer. The candidate adopts subject plurality; it does not say the non-agentive whole is the user's earlier intended universal consciousness. Whether its intrinsic unity and plurality can be made fully intelligible remains open.

Under the user's adopted premises that a perfect whole implies the best possible universe and that our universe is not the best, the whole is **not perfect**. Calling it necessary or self-sufficient does not make it perfect, and the suboptimality of actual experiences remains real.

### Self-actualization and time

“Self-actualization” can express the absence of an external creator, but it risks suggesting that the whole first does not exist, then acts on itself and comes into existence. On the user's earlier timeless-absolute constraint, that cannot be assumed as a process in an external time. The target is rather **actuality whose existence is intrinsic to what it is**, with any processes of coming to be located *within* reality if local time permits them. Whether such an intrinsic explanation is possible—and how local becoming and experienced time arise—still requires an argument.

This is not a verbal trick that proves necessary existence. An assertion such as “it exists because it exists” is merely circular; nor does naming a necessary essence establish why it is concrete, experiential, or the way it is. The project is to find an intelligible constitution that meets those demands, not to count the label as a solution.

## 2. Possibility, actuality and differentiation

Let `P(d)` mean that a description `d` is genuinely possible in the user's strongest sense; let `A(d)` mean that it is actually realized at the specified scope. The adopted thesis is

```
P(d) ↔ A(d).
```

No event of actualization is inserted between the two. But this schema by itself does not identify which descriptions are genuinely possible, prove that every logically consistent string is concretely real, derive experience, or establish the necessary existence of the whole. If an appropriate **unrestricted global modal schema** is additionally assumed with classical modal logic, it entails modal collapse of global truths; see [the modal analysis](NOTHINGNESS_TRUTH_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_NECESSITY.md) and the historical construction's global-scope discussion. That conditional result is not a proof that the schema correctly describes reality.

Genuine local variety need not be contradictory. For example, distinct subjects can have different experiences: `subject A experiences X` and `subject B experiences Y` concern different subjects. One cannot infer that `X` and `not-X` both hold of the same subject, at the same point and in the same respect. Nor does “different perspectives” alone prove distinct subjects: their individuation remains a real explanatory obligation.

The hardest unsolved step is **not** “what selects one possible world?” under this thesis. It is: why and how does the actual whole have its particular intrinsically experiential, differentiated constitution, such that the necessary identity of possibility and actuality is intelligible rather than merely asserted? The account must not smuggle in an unexplained criterion of admissibility, an external law or a concealed creator.

## 3. What survives from Bostrom's probability structure

The original Bostrom argument concerns a conditional comparison between simulated and nonsimulated observers. That factual distinction, its posthuman civilization counts, and the formula involving the numbers of ancestor simulations are **not premises of this corrected framework**. Calling all actuality “simulated” would not preserve the probability structure; it would only change a word.

The potentially useful **abstract form** is narrower:

1. There can be several actual, evidence-compatible subject-locations in one reality.
2. A finite subject can be uncertain which location or kind of situation is theirs.
3. A justified measure on those locations can turn that uncertainty into a conditional probability.

For a candidate measure `μ` and evidence `E`, the formal expression is

```
Cr(Q | E) = μ(Q ∩ E) / μ(E),
```

when the measure and conditioning are justified and the denominator is finite and positive. `Q` may be a local feature of experience or physical history; it is **not** a label for a simulated world. This is self-location within the actual whole, not a cosmic draw that makes one possibility actual. One must justify the relevant subject-locations, evidence, measure and interpretation of credence. If those cannot be justified, retaining the bare ratio is not a faithful or successful derivation of a probability.

The earlier claim that subject plurality is straightforwardly “more faithful to Bostrom's probabilistic structure” was too broad for the user's intended reconstruction. Plurality supplies candidate distinct locations naturally, but it does not establish the measure, nor show how the comparison between literal simulations and base observers translates into a probability about self-existent actuality. We must assess the retained structure **after** the metaphysical transformation, with full intelligibility taking priority over literal fidelity.

## 4. Finite sanity check, not the solution

[`verification/self_existing_plenitude_centers.py`](verification/self_existing_plenitude_centers.py) considers one fixed whole with four stipulated subject labels, three of which have a local property `Q`. Both local property values are stipulated possible and occur in the toy catalogue. If all four locations are evidence-compatible and assigned equal weight, `Cr(Q|E)=3/4`. With another normalized group-symmetric weighting it is `1/2`.

The point is limited: a **fixed actual whole** can be represented alongside uncertainty over one's location, and the choice of weighting matters. The check contains **no simulator or creation event**. Its labels do not establish actual subjects, possible=actual, experiential qualities, a physically warranted measure, or the existence or necessity of the whole. The alternative weighting is a logical model comparison, not a claim that nature selects between possibilities.

## 5. What would constitute real progress toward the user's goal?

A complete account would need to answer, without moving the unexplained fact elsewhere:

1. **Necessary actuality:** why absolute nonexistence is not genuinely possible, and why the actuality is self-sufficient rather than merely postulated.
2. **Possibility = actuality:** why the proposed identity holds, including what makes a description genuinely possible and how global incompatibility is handled.
3. **Intrinsic experience and plurality:** why the whole's nature is experiential, why there are distinct subjects, how their identities persist and why their particular qualities feel as they do.
4. **Physical and temporal order:** how quantum phenomena, local spacetime and lived succession follow or emerge without an external universal clock.
5. **Local probability:** when a subject-locating measure is justified, how it is fixed and whether it yields empirical predictions without a fundamental selector.
6. **Value:** how a non-perfect whole and our genuinely suboptimal experiences fit the account without dismissing suffering.

Existing finite quantum, history, subject and measure gates constrain candidate answers but do not resolve these obligations. The correction is to **direct the work at them**, not to add a literal simulator as another unexplained component.
