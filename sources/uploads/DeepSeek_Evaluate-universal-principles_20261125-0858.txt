Formalizing V3: union success, stability threshold, disintegration mapping

Part I — The formal model

1. State space and dynamics

Let the state space be a smooth manifold $X$ with a potential $V: X \to \mathbb{R}$. A unit is a point $x \in X$. Dynamics:

$$
\dot{x} = -\nabla V(x) + \sqrt{2D},\xi(t)
$$

where $\xi$ is white noise and $D = kT$ (Einstein relation). Deterministic limit: gradient flow.

2. Units as attractors

A stable unit is a local minimum $x^*$ of $V$ with basin

$$
B(x^*) = {x : \lim_{t\to\infty} x(t) = x^*}.
$$

A metastable unit is a local minimum with finite barrier height. A primary unit is a minimum that has just been created by a transition (see §5) and is therefore shallow—minimally differentiated (V3 A5).

3. Stability threshold

Define the barrier of $x^*$:

$$
\Delta(x^*) = \min_{y \in \partial B(x^*)} V(y) - V(x^*).
$$

The stability threshold is $\Delta(x^*)$ itself: the minimal perturbation energy that carries the unit out of its basin. A perturbation of size $\delta$ destabilizes the unit iff $\delta > \Delta(x^*)$. This is V3 B6, now measurable.

The escape rate (Kramers, overdamped):

$$
\Gamma(x^*) = \frac{\omega_0 \omega_b}{2\pi\gamma}, e^{-\Delta(x^*)/kT},
$$

with $\omega_0 = \sqrt{V''(x^*)/m}$, $\omega_b = \sqrt{|V''(x_b)|/m}$ at the saddle $x_b$.

4. Union success criterion (V3 A3, formalized)

Two units $x_A$ (dominant background) and $x_b$ (seed) interact via a union map

$$
U: X \times X \to X, \qquad x_C = U(x_A, x_b).
$$

Success holds iff:

$x_C \in B(x_C^*)$ for some minimum $x_C^* \neq x_A, x_b$;
$V(x_C^*) < V(x_A)$ (the new unit is deeper than the background well);
the barrier from $x_C^*$ back to $x_A$ exceeds $\tau$ (persistence);
if physical, energy–momentum is conserved across $U$.

Asymmetry (V3 A4): $x_A$ sits in a broad shallow well; $x_b$ is a small displacement near the separatrix. The seed does not supply the energy—it lowers the effective barrier or supplies the critical fluctuation. In nucleation language, $x_b$ is a subcritical embryo; union succeeds when the seed exceeds a critical size $s_c$.

Success probability as a function of seed size $s$:

$$
P_{\text{success}}(s) = \exp!\left(-\frac{\Delta G^*(s)}{kT}\right),
$$

a sigmoid in $s$ with midpoint at $s_c$. This is V3 BR1 operationalized.

5. Disintegration as barrier crossing (V3 B2–B5, D1)

Disintegration is a first-passage event: the trajectory crosses $\partial B(x^*)$ at time $t_f$. The end state is the adjacent minimum $x'$—the stable unit of V3 B3. The crossing is a token of a primary event; the three types are the three structural features of the landscape at the crossing (see §6).

Repair of V3 B5. “Shortest positive interval” cannot mean the waiting time—that is exponentially distributed with mean $1/\Gamma$, not minimal. It must mean the crossing time once initiated:

$$
\tau_{\text{cross}} \sim 1/\omega_b,
$$

the shortest dynamical timescale of the system. This is the Kramers “attempt time.” V3 B5 should read: the crossing event itself occurs on the shortest dynamical timescale; the waiting time is set by the barrier.

6. The three event types in the model (V3 D8)

The potential landscape decomposes into three structures:

V3 type Formal object Physical reading
Exertion $-\nabla V$ force field
Oscillation $V''(x)$; real modes at minima, one imaginary mode at the saddle normal-mode frequencies; the unstable mode is the reaction coordinate
Material the localized well $x^*$ the bound state itself

This is a genuine fit, not a stretch: at a transition state, normal-mode analysis yields exactly one imaginary frequency (the oscillation type) in a force field (exertion) acting on a localized configuration (matter).

7. Timestamps and the ledger (V3 E1–E4)

Each crossing is a point event at a specific $t_f$. The sequence of crossings is a point process—discrete, ordered, irreversible when $\Delta_{\text{rev}} \gg \Delta_{\text{fwd}}$. The “causal ledger” is this point process, not the continuous trajectory. Thermodynamics records the coarse-grained crossing history; it does not preserve the microstate. V3 E4 holds.

Seam 2: Sorkin’s cosmological constant — the one prediction that worked, now under fire

$$
