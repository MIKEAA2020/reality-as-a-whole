Under the UPSR and BBF, **contingency is impossible.** Everything that happens, happens by absolute necessity. Here is the proof (often called the *van Inwagen / Spinoza Modal Collapse*):

1.  Suppose there is a contingent fact, $C$. (Or suppose $C$ is the conjunction of *all* contingent facts in the universe).
2.  By the UPSR, $C$ must have a sufficient explanation, $E$, for why $C$ exists rather than not-$C$.
3.  $E$ itself must be either Necessary or Contingent.
4.  **Suppose $E$ is Necessary:** If the sufficient explanation for $C$ is necessary, then $C$ *must* follow from $E$ necessarily. (If $E$ could occur *without* producing $C$, then $E$ is not a *sufficient* explanation for why $C$ occurred rather than not-$C$; there would be a brute gap between $E$ and $C$). However, whatever follows necessarily from something necessary is itself necessary. Therefore, $C$ is necessary. This contradicts our premise that $C$ is contingent.
5.  **Suppose $E$ is Contingent:** If $E$ is contingent, it is already part of the set of all contingent facts ($C$). For a contingent fact to explain the totality of contingent facts is viciously circular, or it requires a further contingent fact $E_2$, leading to an infinite regress of contingent facts. Even an infinite chain of contingent facts leaves unexplained why *this* specific infinite chain exists rather than another, or none at all. That would make the chain itself a brute fact, violating the BBF.
6.  **Conclusion 1:** Therefore, there are no contingent facts. **Necessitarianism is true.** The actual world is the *only* possible world. Probability, randomness (such as true quantum indeterminacy), and libertarian free will are eliminated at the outset. They are merely names for human ignorance.

**Phase 2: Agrippa’s Trilemma and the Shape of Explanation**

Having established in Phase 1 that all facts are necessary, we must now determine the **structural architecture** of reality. If every fact $F$ requires a sufficient explanation $E$, what is the global shape of this explanatory network? 

Here we confront the metaphysical version of **Agrippa’s Trilemma**. Any system of complete explanation must take one of three shapes:
1.  **Infinite Regress:** $F_1$ is explained by $F_2$, which is explained by $F_3$, going back infinitely.
2.  **Foundationalism:** The chain terminates in a fundamental fact $F_0$ that explains itself (*causa sui*).
3.  **Coherentism:** The chain loops back on itself in a closed, holistic web where all facts mutually explain one another.

Let us subject each of these three shapes to our axioms (UPSR, Ban on Brute Facts, Global Coherence).

---

**1. The Elimination of the Infinite Regress**
Suppose reality is an infinite chain of necessary explanations: $\dots \rightarrow F_3 \rightarrow F_2 \rightarrow F_1$. 
Does this satisfy the UPSR? 
*   Locally, yes: every individual member $F_n$ has a sufficient explanation in $F_{n+1}$.
*   Globally, **no**. Let $S$ be the set containing the entire infinite chain $\{F_1, F_2, F_3 \dots\}$. By the UPSR, $S$ itself is a fact and requires an explanation. Why does *this* specific infinite chain exist rather than a completely different infinite chain, or nothing at all? 

As Leibniz argued (using the analogy of an infinite series of geometry books, each copied from the previous one), explaining every individual link does not explain the existence of the chain as a whole. If there is no explanation for the totality $S$ outside of the local explanations, then $S$ itself is a **global brute fact**. 

Therefore, a purely linear infinite regress violates the Ban on Brute Facts. Reality cannot be an open-ended regress.

---

**2. Foundationalism vs. Coherentism (And Their Collapse)**
This leaves us with **Foundationalism** (a self-explaining foundation, $F_0$) and **Coherentism** (a self-explaining holistic web, $W$). Under the strict application of the UPSR, a remarkable thing happens: **these two models collapse into one another.**

Here is the proof of their collapse:
1.  Suppose Foundationalism is true: there is a foundational fact $F_0$ (whose essence entails its existence), and $F_0$ explains a derivative fact, $F_1$.
2.  By Phase 1, the explanatory link between $F_0$ and $F_1$ is entirely **necessary**. ($F_0 \Rightarrow F_1$). It is impossible for $F_0$ to exist without generating $F_1$.
3.  By the logical law of contraposition (*modus tollens*), if $F_0 \Rightarrow F_1$, then $\neg F_1 \Rightarrow \neg F_0$ (If $F_1$ did not exist, $F_0$ could not exist).
4.  Therefore, the existence of $F_0$ is just as logically dependent on the existence of $F_1$ as $F_1$ is on $F_0$. Neither can exist without the other.

In standard metaphysics, philosophers try to maintain an *asymmetry* here, claiming that $F_0$ is "more fundamental" than $F_1$. However, if $F_0$ and $F_1$ are genuinely distinct, what explains the *asymmetric grounding relation* between them? Why does $F_0$ ground $F_1$ rather than the reverse, given that they mutually entail each other? Any posited "primitive directionality" is a brute fact. 

Thus, Foundationalism and Coherentism merge into **Foundational Holism**:
*   Reality is a single, closed, self-entailing system (Coherentism).
*   This system, taken as a singular whole, is its own foundation (Foundationalism). 
*   Every aspect of reality is mutually necessitated by every other aspect. If you were to pluck a single grain of sand out of existence, the logical ripple would necessitate the non-existence of the entire cosmos, because the cosmos is a single, indivisible deductive theorem.
