# Fixed plan: perturbation, unpleasantness and mediator identification

## Scope and prior access

Screen the 2026 Jin et al. study ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2026.e01107)) because it has randomized real/sham DLPFC rTMS, EEG, and first-person unpleasantness ratings. Before this plan, the paper's main text and published results were read through its discussion; source data, supplemental DOCX, raw recordings and individual outcomes were not inspected. The published effects are already known: this is not a preregistered replication. Search for accessible data but do not infer that article access implies data access.

## Fixed finite control

Use an exact binary structural-causal example to test whether knowing the joint distribution of randomized treatment `T`, mediator `M`, and outcome `Y`, including every treatment arm, suffices to identify mediation. This is **not a model of the actual participants** or a proof that their mediation analysis is wrong. Give two explicitly defined structural models with identical `P(M,Y | do(T))` for every T and full support in every `(T,M,Y)` cell, but different natural indirect and direct effects. Let U be uniform on {0,1,2,3}, E independent with P(E=1)=1/4, and randomized T independent of U and E. Define mediator potential states `M0 = 1[U=0]`, `M1 = 1[U!=3]`; `M=M_T`. Model A sets `Y(t,m)=m XOR E`; model B sets `Y(t,m)=M_t XOR E` (hence ignores intervention on m). All potential variables use the same U,E across counterfactual worlds. In both models the observed Y is `M XOR E`. Compute exact cell probabilities, ATE, total natural indirect effect `E[Y(1,M1)−Y(1,M0)]`, and pure natural direct effect `E[Y(1,M0)−Y(0,M0)]`; check equality and their sum. Verify full-support positivity and exhaustive equality, not only the mean. Independently test by hand-derived fractions and intervention edge cases. This demonstrates only that treatment randomization and full observed positivity are insufficient **without additional mediator-outcome assumptions**, not that no alternative experiment could identify a mediator effect or that actual mediator effects equal either toy.

## Decision rules

Keep unpleasantness separate from pain intensity and from the retrospective expected-pain/anxiety ratings. Distinguish a randomized total treatment contrast from a causal mediator effect; EEG is measured after stimulation while rating expectancy was collected retrospectively after the task. Test the project's physical `q` only if a designated interaction and its report-independent preferred relation are actually identified; TMS at F3 is not selective channel removal, and ERP/phase/correlation is not automatically `q`. If calibration or raw data are unavailable, no new held-out comparison; record conditions to revisit and avoid presenting publication results as our analysis. Report whether source data availability is stated, unverified, or on request, and distinguish known article results from this demonstration.
