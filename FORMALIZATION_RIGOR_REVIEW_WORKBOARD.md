# Formalization review: scope, receipts and remaining work

## User instruction and operating rule

The user requests that we **evaluate, verify, strengthen and complete every formalization as far as possible**, taking multiple turns if necessary. This is a continuing research task, not a claim that finite tests or an inventory complete it. For each substantive argument or model, identify its actual definitions and source, check proof steps and domains, independently test representative and boundary cases, correct errors in place while preserving provenance, and strengthen or construct a follow-through where possible. Record precisely what was and was not checked. Mathematical completion of a toy is not completion of a physical or experiential theory.

The current guiding framework treats space and time as **non-fundamental constructs**, proposes a self-sufficient whole with objectively zero energy and intrinsic experience, and allows genuinely plural finite subjects. Do not reimpose the superseded universal-consciousness framework, equate physical signs with felt values, or demand an external causal psychophysical bridge. Prior assumptions, models and source claims must be individually reassessed; not every older result or concern is automatically discarded.

## Coverage accounting

[Machine-readable review progress](verification/formalization_review_progress.json) conservatively inventories tracked and non-ignored `.md`, `.py`, `.m`, `.txt`, and `.pdf` files. It is an **overinclusive file queue**, not a count of distinct mathematical formalizations: it includes sources, tests and ordinary prose, and multiple files may address one proof. Each reviewed entry identifies exactly the selected claims and evidence in [manual receipts](verification/formalization_review_receipts.json); hashes of both the reviewed file and evidence are checked, and changes mark the receipt stale. The tracker does not infer semantic correctness from the existence of a test.

`not_rechecked_in_this_sweep` means just that; it does not erase previous audits or assert that a file has never been checked. A `selected_claims_reviewed` entry does not mean the file is completely reviewed. JSON, data, notebooks or other file types potentially containing claims must be reviewed through the [full repository manifest](verification/framework_inventory_manifest.json) and [source repass tracker](verification/source_repass_progress.json), and added explicitly when relevant; ignored, absent or nonpersisted material is outside current coverage. Test suite results are evidence about executed tests only, not evidence that every argument is rigorous or a theory is true.

## Continuing queue

1. **Gravity and time:** audit constraint domains, normalizability, lapse/clock changes, finite-resolution instruments, continuum limits, boundary charges, and recovery of geometry and experiments. Compare claims with the actual model, not an imported background disguised as emergent.
2. **Zero, symmetry and energy:** trace definitions of energy and signed balance across the paired/echo, gauge and continuum models; check which constraints, spectra, conservation and symmetries actually hold, including counterexamples and operational access. Do not identify an accounting zero or constraint with whole-level physical energy without justification.
3. **Experience, subjects, incidence and measure:** recheck identities, subject plurality and persistence, locality/overlap, normalization, physical access and first-person anchors. Treat rival models as logical tests of stated assumptions, not proof of genuinely possible alternative wholes or of experience arising as an extra effect.
4. **Statistical and experimental gates:** recheck distributions, confidence assumptions, calibration, data provenance, multiple comparisons, preregistration and transfer to the candidate; simulation or proof of a bound is not an empirical result.
5. **Source-derived mathematics:** recheck primary theorem statements, model/category correspondence, constants, signs and domains in modular theory, RG, fractals, arithmetic and categorical no-go work, distinguishing source claims from the current framework.
6. **Cross-model integration:** for every claimed connection identify the same state/carrier, maps, physical operations, assumptions and units; repair incompatible or merely analogous models rather than adding disconnected toy examples. Return to explanatory why-chains and the no-residue objective rather than stopping at a catalogue of mathematical interfaces.

For each batch, add a receipt with the **specific reviewed scope** and evidence hashes, run relevant checks, document failure/repair, and update this workboard. The task is not complete while the majority of the queue is unrechecked or while central explanatory and empirical targets remain open.
