# Can the actual whole force its own zero total?

## The proposed route

The [last investigation](ZERO_TOTALITY_SYMMETRY_SELECTION_AND_ZERO_LEVEL.md) obtained zero from sign symmetry plus a unique admissible constraint level. That leaves an awkward question: why should there be only one level? A whole-first proposal can bypass that particular assumption:

> If sign reversal is an intrinsic symmetry **of the actual whole itself**, and a well-defined global quantity changes sign under it, then the whole's value of that quantity is zero.

This conclusion does not require a choice between possible worlds or an external actualizer. It also need not make each local contribution zero: the symmetry may exchange internal locations while reversing their signs. The potentially important improvement is that **the cancellation comes from a symmetry of one actual, internally differentiated whole**, not from assuming that only one formal sector is admissible.

It remains a conditional argument. Neither the whole's self-subsistence nor the user's possibility–actuality thesis by itself establishes the required symmetry, the global quantity, or its experiential interpretation. The result does not yet deliver the requested explanation without residue, and it does not demonstrate that such an explanation is impossible.

## 1. The whole-first deduction

Let `W` be an actual whole and `T` a transformation of its full physical description. Suppose:

1. **Actual-whole symmetry:** `T(W)=W`, meaning the transformed whole is the same physical state or structure, not merely another state allowed by the laws.
2. **An intrinsic global value:** `Q(W)` is a single well-defined real value, unchanged when the same physical whole is described again.
3. **Oddness:** `Q(T(W))=-Q(W)` under this very transformation, with the physical zero and sign convention specified.

Then

```text
Q(W) = Q(T(W)) = -Q(W),    therefore Q(W) = 0.
```

No uniqueness of an admissible constraint *level* has been assumed. The uniqueness of the value assigned to a particular W is a well-definedness condition, not a premise that W lacks internal diversity or that every formal alternative is actual. If T is a change of description rather than an active symmetry, the odd quantity must be handled as a covariant sign-dependent quantity; for a description-independent scalar with both properties, zero is its only possible value. Section 4 explains the distinction.

The derivation is stronger than saying a symmetric *law* permits a zero state: it assumes the **actual whole** has that symmetry. It is also stronger than zero average in an ensemble: `Q(W)` is assumed to be an actual classical scalar, not an expectation over states. The quantum case needs its own analysis, as the [previous report](ZERO_TOTALITY_SYMMETRY_SELECTION_AND_ZERO_LEVEL.md) shows.

## 2. Nonzero local differences in the same symmetric whole

For a transparent instance, let the actual whole have two internal locations with signed contributions

```text
q_1 = a,     q_2 = -a,     a != 0.
```

Let T exchange the locations **and** reverse the sign of each contribution. In the original location labels, its action is

```text
(q_1,q_2) -> (-q_2,-q_1) = (q_1,q_2).
```

The configuration is fixed, even though each local contribution is nonzero. Under T the global sum changes sign, but because the configuration is unchanged its value is the same; therefore its value is zero. This is an illustration of the assumptions, not independent evidence that nature has this transformation or these contributions. It does not mean the two locations are different subjects, nor that their contributions are two opposite feelings.

The same reasoning generalizes. For a finite set of locations X, let T be a bijection of X and suppose `q(Tx)=-q(x)` for every location. Then

```text
Q = sum over x in X of q(x)
  = sum over x in X of q(Tx)
  = -Q,
```

so `Q=0`. The bijection is an internal symmetry of the stipulated full structure only if its transformation of **all** relevant relations and properties is specified and preserves that structure; a sign-reversing permutation of an isolated list alone does not establish a symmetry of the actual whole.

For an infinite domain, an analogous integral argument requires a specified T-invariant measure and an integrable q: then `∫q dμ = ∫q∘T dμ = -∫q dμ`. The measure here accounts for the candidate global quantity; it is **not automatically** a subject-locating probability or a Born measure. If positive and negative parts are both infinite, or a series is conditionally summed without a justified prescription, cancellation may be undefined or order-dependent. A formal pairing is not enough to establish an objective global zero.

## 3. Can plenitude supply the symmetry?

The user's possibility–actuality thesis suggests a promising line of thought. If a local configuration and its transformed counterpart are both genuinely possible and the whole realizes all genuine possibilities at their proper scopes, there is no additional event selecting just one for existence. In that sense, a full, internally balanced whole could be more natural than a selected unbalanced state.

But three further steps are needed before the deduction applies:

- **Closure:** explain why the transformed local configuration is genuinely possible. The existence of a formal sign reversal does not establish physical or experiential possibility.
- **Actual whole-level symmetry:** establish a structure-preserving bijection of the actual occurrences and their *full* relations, not merely the occurrence of both signs somewhere. Realizing two kinds of local configuration does not show they occur with matching incidence, connections or global organization.
- **Quantity and aggregation:** explain why the contributions reverse and why their weights or measure are preserved, with a defined total. Plenitude of support alone does not determine multiplicity or weight; see [relational plenitude and measure](RELATIONAL_PLENITUDE_HISTORY_AND_MEASURE.md).

This is a more targeted burden than an external selection problem. A sufficiently articulated intrinsic nature could perhaps establish all three together; they would not then be independent grounds. But possibility equals actuality does not, on its own, provide their content, and a list of all formal descriptions cannot be treated as a list of actual experiences.

### Why merely realizing both signs is weaker

Consider one actual collection of three local contributions `(2,-1,-1)`. Both signs occur and the total is zero, but no permutation maps each value to its negative: there is no `-2` contribution or `+1` contribution. Thus zero total does not entail a pointwise sign-reversing internal symmetry. Conversely, a collection `(2,-1)` contains both signs but has total `1`, not zero. The proposed symmetry is therefore a **stronger and potentially discriminating** principle, not just another name for a zero total or for the presence of both signs. These numerical illustrations do not identify actual subjects or physical quantities.

## 4. An active symmetry is not the same as a gauge relabelling

The phrase “same whole” can hide two distinct claims:

- **Active physical symmetry:** T transforms the concrete configuration, and the actual configuration is invariant under it. The conditions above can then force its sign-odd global scalar to zero. It is a substantive claim about the whole, stronger than symmetry of the equations.
- **Gauge redescription:** T changes only representational convention. A sign-odd Q is not, at nonzero values, an ordinary gauge-invariant scalar unless its sign is understood relative to additional structure. A gauge orbit containing both signs does not by itself prove that the physical magnitude is zero: `|Q|` or `Q²` can be nonzero on that orbit. If Q is required both to be scalar-invariant and sign-odd, zero follows, but the requirement and its physical meaning must be defended rather than smuggled in.

In both cases a reference orientation may be internal and relational rather than external. The theory must say which transformations are physically active, which are redundancies, and which quantities can be measured or compared. A mere change of units is not a physical argument that all dimensional quantities vanish; a sign reversal about an arbitrary reference level likewise cannot by itself privilege numerical zero.

## 5. Does this constitute experience?

The most ambitious identity remains: the same self-subsistent whole whose intrinsic structure makes the relevant total vanish is actually articulated as local, directly present experiences. The symmetry argument gives a possible **structural** part of that claim; it does not establish the experiential identity.

Actual qualitative contrasts are evidence of experiential articulation, but the existence of contrast does not show that feelings, awareness, energy or valence carry the sign-reversing q used above. Nor does an arithmetic balance of q imply that a pain is removed or morally offset. If T is said to be a symmetry of the *entire* experiential whole, the account must explain its action on qualitative character, subject incidence and relations, not just on charge labels. It must respect actual local suffering and need not posit a universal subject.

An internal symmetry can preserve plural experience without assigning a negative amount of awareness: the sign-reversing quantity might concern some other aspect of an experiential constitution. But saying this is possible is not a derivation of that constitution. Nor is an experienced episode established by labelling a mathematical location “subject.” Direct acquaintance and structural description have different explanatory roles, and neither automatically supplies the other.

## 6. Comparison and status

The three approaches now have distinct burdens:

| Route | Additional condition that yields zero | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Relational accounting | Every local contribution is an internal-interface divergence | Why that accounting law and its physical interpretation apply |
| Unique level | Sign symmetry of the full theory plus one admissible constraint level | Why the level is unique or the actual structure is experiential |
| Actual-whole symmetry | The actual whole is fixed by a sign-odd transformation of a well-defined global scalar | Why that full symmetry holds, or why its odd scalar is the required global quantity |

The third route makes **one actual whole** central and avoids assuming a uniquely admissible formal sector. It does not derive its own premises from self-subsistence. Conversely, a nonzero level or several levels have not been shown to satisfy the user's demand for no unexplained residue simply because they can be written down. A fair comparison must explain the grounds of each full candidate, including its actual experiential organization.

The crucial next question is whether **a reason for the whole to be invariant under an actual sign-reversing symmetry** can be derived from its supposed self-subsistence and the nature of its genuine possibilities, with a physically defined, objective Q and a preserved aggregation. If so, this route could connect the user's modal thesis to a nontrivial zero-totality without a separate selector. If not, the symmetry remains a conditional tool, not a demonstrated impossibility or permission to accept brute residue.
