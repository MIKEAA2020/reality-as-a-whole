# Necessary occurrence: the essence–instance gap and an actuality-first candidate

## Direct assessment

The most promising direction is to investigate the nature of **known actual occurrence**, rather than infer concrete existence from an abstract definition of maximal Good. The candidate is that the same nature constitutes occurrence, causal powers, qualitative perspectives, and possibly intrinsic value, with its necessary character explaining why actuality is not an added brute fact.

This is an explicit research preference under complete intelligibility. The necessary character has not been established. No actual underlying mathematical constitution is being claimed as discovered.

## 1. An essence can require concreteness without being instantiated

Let C(x) say that x instantiates the proposed ultimate nature, and K(x) that x is concrete. Suppose:

    necessarily, for every x: C(x) -> K(x).

This says there can be no abstract instance of C. It does not say any instance of C exists. The C predicate can be empty while this essential condition holds.

Adding more essential predicates—intelligible, qualitative, valuable, self-supporting—does not remove that logical possibility unless an existence premise or a genuinely existence-entailing constitutive argument is supplied.

This is the **essence–instance gap**. It is not proof that every real essence lacks existence-entailing character. It identifies what has not followed from the stated universal predicates.

## 2. The modal shortcut and its real burden

In standard S5 modal semantics:

    possibly necessarily E -> necessarily E.

Here E means that the proposed concrete nature is instantiated. The inference is valid in S5: possibilities within an equivalence class share the same accessible alternatives. If E holds at every alternative accessible from one possible world, it holds throughout that class.

But:

    possibly E -> necessarily E

is not valid. Two mutually accessible worlds, one with E and one without E, provide the elementary countermodel. E is possible but not necessary.

Therefore showing that a proposed nature seems coherent or could exist is not the same as showing that its necessary existence is possible. A modal actualization argument needs the stronger premise, and S5 itself is a substantive choice when interpreted metaphysically. This audit grants it for the conditional test; it does not establish that framework as the ultimate ontology.

A definition of “maximal Good” that includes necessary existence does not establish the stronger possibility premise. Nor does a consistency claim for a thin formal description establish the metaphysical possibility of its concrete instantiation.

The result is a standard modal distinction, not a new discovery or a refutation of all modal existence arguments.

## 3. An actuality-first alternative

There is already actual occurrence in the inquiry: experiences, actions, and the physical processes under investigation. Their existence is the datum to explain, not an optional label to be selected from an uninstantiated menu.

Let E mean that concrete occurrence exists. The actuality-first route has the form:

1. E.
2. The independently disclosed nature of occurrence entails that it cannot exist contingently.
3. Therefore necessarily E.

As a logical conditional, one could represent the needed second premise by E -> necessarily E. Its philosophical content must be established through the nature of occurrence, not simply asserted. The first premise does not prove the second.

This route has an epistemic advantage: it does not need to manufacture an instance from a definition. It begins with an instance and asks what its nature explains. The advantage is not a completed metaphysical proof.

Also, the occurrence of this inquiry does not establish that no possible concrete reality could be empty. A representation of an empty condition is not the condition represented. That purported shortcut has already failed in the [self-explanation audit](SELF_EXPLANATORY_ACTUALITY_AUDIT.md).

## 4. The positive candidate, stated as a hypothesis

> Concrete actuality is an intrinsically articulated occurrence whose causal and qualitative character are not independent ingredients. Its total constitution determines its organization, including the finite perspectives it realizes. The same constitution may ground the significance of those perspectives and the conditions of their fulfillment. If its nature is necessarily concrete and self-determining, there is no further unexplained selection of a description for realization.

The conditional sentence is load-bearing. No concrete nature meeting it has yet been independently identified. The description is a candidate account form, not a constitutive equation or a proof of necessary existence.

### What the candidate excludes as final explanations

- an otherwise complete abstract structure plus an unexplained actuality switch;
- independently complete physical and experiential realms plus an unexplained observer attachment;
- value bestowed by an arbitrary external evaluator;
- material labels or simulation labels treated as sufficient constitutive tests;
- “the whole must be Good” used to justify every local harm after the fact.

Excluding these as final stopping points under the objective is not evidence that every more sophisticated rival theory is false.

### What it leaves open

- whether the ultimate presentation is fundamentally temporal;
- the correct physics and the relation among its mathematical presentations;
- the constitutive law of subject incidence and qualitative differences;
- cross-material realization conditions;
- whether intrinsic normative value is real and privative badness universal;
- whether the whole is a subject;
- whether value explains actuality or shares its ground.

These questions are not optional if the account is to become complete. They are explicit unfilled parts, rather than results hidden in the name of the candidate.

## 5. Where Good would enter

The initial datum is not “a morally perfect being exists.” It is that concrete occurrence exists. The proposed axiological extension must disclose value as a feature of its nature rather than assume a perfect evaluator at the outset.

The strongest current normative candidate is realized qualitative-relational fulfillment: actual welfare, standing, agency, and legitimate claims, not merely pleasant reports. Wrongness may be missing due integration of those relations.

If the same necessary constitution explains occurrence and those values, value has an intrinsic and non-arbitrary place. Strict axiarchism additionally requires that value be indispensable to the explanation of actuality. Common grounding alone does not establish that asymmetric dependence.

The user's privation proposal remains a serious candidate for the relation between positive actuality and deficient fulfillment. It does not itself establish the modal premise needed for necessary occurrence.

## 6. Why this is a direction rather than a solution

The strongest explanatory ambitions do not identify the required essence by themselves. A successful continuation must provide an independently motivated constitutive characterization and show how its necessity, concreteness, phenomenal organization, and axiological relations follow.

A bare declaration of E -> necessarily E would be an unexplained rule. An unexplained natural essence would also fail the no-brute-facts objective. A completed explanation must reveal why its character is intelligible, not merely classify its existence as necessary.

This is compatible with promoting the account to a working conjecture. Missing an argument does not make its conclusion likely false. But repeated restatements of the desired conclusion are not additional evidence or successive constitutive discoveries.

## 7. Current verdict

- The essence–instance gap is a genuine limitation of the stated universal conditions.
- Possibility differs from possible necessity; S5 does not erase the difference.
- Known occurrence is a better epistemic starting point for the present inquiry than an uninstantiated definition of maximal Good.
- A necessarily concrete common constitution is the preferred strong completion under the contrastive objective, not an established finding.
- Intrinsic value and privative badness remain serious extensions; Good as the actualizer remains a further claim.

No empirical result, executable model, additional test count, or numerical probability is claimed.
