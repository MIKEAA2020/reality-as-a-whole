# Private local and global fields: one explicit completion

## Result

The [indirect-communication and nesting audit](SUBJECT_INCIDENCE_INDIRECT_COMMUNICATION_AND_NESTING_AUDIT.md) tests an independently motivated thinker-individuation argument against the local/global pair. It identifies additional mental-interaction, occurrence and identity premises; neither physical overlap nor a lateral relay determines the outcome.

The [split-brain private-subject identity evidence audit](SPLIT_BRAIN_PRIVATE_SUBJECT_IDENTITY_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md) follows up the P/C-layered incidence gap using a simultaneous-task confidence report and a sequential-task control. It does not establish either package or a clinical subject count.

The [Bigaj field-quanta audit](BIGAJ_FIELD_QUANTA_AND_PROJECT_ONTOLOGY_AUDIT.md) further distinguishes these postulated first-person fields from quantum field operators, modes and local algebras; no subject mapping follows from the shared terminology.

Follow-up: the [IIT 4.0 exclusion audit](IIT4_EXCLUSION_AND_PRIVATE_NESTED_FIELDS_AUDIT.md) compares P with a separately motivated non-overlap principle without treating the project selector as IIT.

A candidate can postulate **two local subjects and a distinct encompassing subject** while denying that any one experience token belongs to more than one subject. The encompassing subject can experience a coarse left–right conjunction; the two local subjects can separately experience detailed aspects. This avoids Basile's *shared-token* premise and does not require one subject to have Lopez's nontransitive unity structure. But it pays for that move with three assumed fields, a correspondence between their different experiences, and an unexplained rule making overlapping physical sets into distinct subjects. It is not a derivation of consciousness.

The important comparison is not merely a new diagram. Once the targets are made **content-type based**, the private nested candidate and a one-subject layered candidate agree on coarse global conjunction and lack of detailed local conjunction. They differ on whether local and global occurrences belong to distinct subjects. Consequently, a well-supported global conjunction alone could challenge a specified exclusive-L package but **cannot choose between those two alternatives**. No validated method for the additional subject-identity judgment is assumed here.

This is a retrospective conceptual construction following the [fixed plan](PRIVATE_NESTED_FIELDS_COMPARISON_FIXED_PLAN.md), with the physical sector, earlier completions and Basile's criticism already known. It is neither an experiment nor a claim that each interpretation is a genuinely possible global world.

## 1. The common physical and experiential interface

Use the existing four-spin kernel, `t=1/4`, and the original one-update query: L selects pairs `Pₗ={0,1}`, `Pᵣ={2,3}`; C selects `W={0,1,2,3}`. The spins are a mathematical sector, not a human brain or an image of a patient's anatomy. In the original encoding the pair bits are `qₗ=(1−s₀s₁)/2` and `qᵣ=(1−s₂s₃)/2`. A bit, a physical support, and a phenomenal subject are different things. The mapping from a support to a subject is an **added incidence postulate**.

To make the content comparison intelligible, suppose an externally specified, common episode contains two simultaneous visual features: a **red item on the left** and a **blue item on the right**. The detailed aspects are the red and blue features; the coarse aspects are that there is an occupied location on each side. "Coarse" here describes phenomenology, not a computed average, retinal centrality or mere knowledge. There is also a designated unpleasant-versus-neutral *affective contrast*, separate from color, location, pain intensity, fear or expectancy. The imagined episode, how its content relates to spins, and any reports are **not supplied by the original spin dynamics**. They are common supplemental stipulations for comparing the completed descriptions. Nothing here establishes that this episode occurs or that it involves the specified physical sector.

The standard E-L pair postulate identifies `q=1` with the designated unpleasant contrast and `q=0` with the neutral contrast for that pair subject. These are coarse categories, not complete accounts of how the whole episode feels. A subject with the neutral value still has the stipulated visual experience; it is not an absence of experience.

## 2. The private nested proposal, P

**P is a new, intentionally limited completion, not a claim made by Basile, Lopez, or the original L rule.** For the chosen window it adds all of the following:

1. **Incidence.** Two distinct local first-person fields `Fₗ` and `Fᵣ` are associated with the L-selected pairs and a third distinct field `F𝗴` with W. A thin subject is identified with each field-occurrence for this local comparison. Physical overlap is allowed, but does not itself establish phenomenal overlap or generate any field. P asserts no further designated subjects in this deliberately closed comparison; that is not a claim about the actual world. The person's continuing identity, agency and reporting abilities are separate questions.
2. **Local experience.** `Fₗ` experiences the left red detail and left coarse occupied-location aspect together; `Fᵣ` experiences the right blue detail and right coarse occupied-location aspect together. Neither experiences the other side's specified detailed or coarse aspect. Each also has the postulated pair affective contrast determined by its own `q`.
3. **Global experience.** `F𝗴` experiences both left and right coarse occupied-location aspects **together** in the same window. In this restricted example it does not experience the red or blue detailed aspects. Whether other unmodeled details occur is outside the target. On the declared target, no designated field experiences the red detail and blue detail together.
4. **Private occurrence, shared content type.** The global left-coarse occurrence and local left-coarse occurrence are **numerically distinct** experiential aspects, as are their right counterparts. Their attributed left-location content is the same *type* under the stipulated correspondence; they are not one token shared by two fields. Visual similarity does not guarantee identity of exact full qualitative character. Nor does this correspondence itself establish that the local experience is phenomenally unified with the global experience across subjects.
5. **Whole-field affect, a risky extra postulate.** For comparison only, assign the global field an independent coarse affect value `Q𝗴=qₗ OR qᵣ`: if either pair bit is one, its designated global contrast is unpleasant; otherwise it is neutral. The global unpleasant occurrence is distinct from either pair's occurrence; P does not say that local pains literally add up or are shared by the global subject. This very simple OR rule is **not entailed** by privacy, C, L, Basile or the physical kernel. It could be false, and it does not purport to specify exact pain intensity or the global field's full character.

P assumes that field occurrences are intrinsically first-personal, not mere strings of labels. It does not derive first-personal givenness from a physical support, or prove that any particular visual or affective character occurs. Its definition makes its predicted subject count clear **inside the designated population**; it does not justify that count or exclude additional inaccessible subjects in reality. At L's tie `t=1/2`, the pair selection is unresolved, so P as defined here makes no count prediction there.

### Context and qualitative reference

For the local fields P retains E-L's context-invariant **binary** pair-contrast rule within the stated sector. It does not say that two `q=1` experiences are the same token, indistinguishable in their full felt character, or unaffected by the rest of their respective fields. It allows holistic differences in their fuller character while keeping both within the coarse unpleasant category. The global field's separately postulated `Q𝗴` may also have a different total character. If warranted evidence showed that a pair's *designated binary contrast itself* varied at fixed q within the law's stated domain, the pair law would be challenged; a change in some other qualitative aspect alone would not refute this restricted law.

This is not yet a specified mechanism by which one felt quality relates to another. It is an explicit qualitative **assignment** and type correspondence, not a demonstration that q is physically measurable, that the labels are correct, or that global unpleasantness is constituted by the local unpleasantness. Each such obligation survives the privacy move.

## 3. Comparable predictions, with a necessary change of target

The [earlier layered comparison](LAYERED_UNITY_AND_SUBJECT_QUALITY_COMPARISON.md) used **specified aspect occurrences** as targets and did not settle their cross-package identity. Here we deliberately define new targets by their **specified content types** in the same imagined episode, precisely so that a private copy can be compared to a shared or single-field occurrence without pretending numerical identity:

- `D=1` iff at least one designated subject experiences the red-left and blue-right **detailed content-types together** in the window.
- `G=1` iff at least one designated subject experiences the **two coarse occupied-location content-types together** in that window.
- `N=1` iff, in that window, there are at least two **numerically distinct** designated subjects such that one has one of the specified local detailed experiences and another has the specified coarse left–right conjoint experience. Distinct output channels, physical supports, or names do not by themselves establish `N=1`.

These are existential claims about the **declared designated population**. An observation about a particular reporter is not automatically a census; `0` requires the full coverage implied by the stipulation or separately warranted evidence. Unknown remains unknown, not zero. A type-based `G` can hold through different occurrence tokens in different packages; it is not a statement that their global tokens are identical. This reframing makes the comparison meaningful but not identical to the earlier token-specific `Jlocal/Jglobal` test.

| Completed package, for the same physical sector and content specification | `D` | `G` | `N` | Subject and quality commitments |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **L-exclusive** | 0 | 0 | 0 | Two pair fields; no designated field has both sides. Pair q contrasts are stipulated. |
| **C-full** | 1 | 1 | 0 | One designated whole field has all detailed and coarse contents conjointly. |
| **C-layered** | 0 | 1 | 0 | One designated whole field owns local and global aspects, but detailed left–right aspects are not conjoint; this is the previously stipulated, contested Lopez-inspired partial-unity relation. |
| **P: private nested** | 0 | 1 | 1 | Two local fields plus a third global field; corresponding coarse content occurs in distinct private tokens. |

The C rows additionally stipulate exactly one designated field for this comparison; C's physical selector **alone** does not prove that no other field exists. To compare whole-field affect, extend both C rows with the **same added** `Q𝗴=qₗ OR qᵣ` quality postulate used by P. Earlier C had no whole-quality law; we do not retrospectively attribute this one to C or Lopez. L-exclusive retains only the original pair law, with no designated encompassing field. The P/C use of an OR over pair bits is a deliberately restrictive comparison hypothesis, not a general law of unpleasantness.

The table yields the intended conditional differences. A target-specific `G=1`, if warranted in the declared population, challenges L-exclusive, but is consistent with C-full, C-layered **and** P. A warranted `D=1` challenges P's stipulated lack of an encompassing detailed conjunction and C-layered's stipulated local disunity, while fitting C-full. `D=0, G=1` leaves P and C-layered tied. A warranted `N=1` challenges the **closed, one-field** C completions and is consistent with P; it does not validate P's physical selector or rule out a different multi-subject model. The table can also fail all four packages, or the evidence can be indeterminate. The contents' actual presence, window, field population and physical mapping are indispensable to any inference.

## 4. Basile and Lopez: what the construction does and does not answer

**Against Basile's particular contradiction:** P denies that the pair and whole feelers share the same experience token. The equality premises used in Basile's derivation therefore do not apply to those distinct occurrences. This does not prove privacy necessary, disprove shared-token alternatives, establish that P is metaphysically possible, or explain how its related occurrences arise. If one instead makes them the *same token* and accepts Basile's strong context-dependent holism and identity premises, his conditional contradiction returns. If the content relation is non-identical but constitutive, P owes a real account of that constitution; a written correspondence is not an explanation of experienced unity or quality.

**Against an overclaim about Lopez:** In P, the global coarse pair is conjoint *within* `F𝗴`, while each local–coarse same-side pair is conjoint *within its local field*. The local-side coarse occurrence is not the global-side coarse occurrence. The correspondence between them is not itself a phenomenal-unity edge. P therefore does **not** reproduce Lopez's same-subject chain by relabeling duplicate tokens, and it does not establish her experiential-centrality premise. It is an alternative nested-subject completion with the same coarse type-level `D,G` pattern as the specified C-layered package. Lopez's own account allows and discusses duplicate representations; she does not assert this four-spin construction or P's three-subject census. Her proposed experienced centrality is a real philosophical premise, not reducible to shared processing or our type correspondence.

**Compared with simple addition:** the global field is explicitly postulated to be an additional, intrinsically first-personal occurrence with a conjoint coarse character. It is not obtained by adding two unexperienced codes or assuming two separate local experiences must compose one subject. That avoids a logical shortcut, but also leaves unanswered why this global field exists and why these distinct experiences correspond. P may preserve the phenomenon **by assumption** without explaining it.

## 5. Common observation model and the actual evidence gate

For an idealized comparison only, use one common, fixed observation model: when a designated subject is available and the relevant content is present, a perfect experiential query for `D` or `G` reports that target truth for that subject and window. An existential positive may be recorded if a subject with the conjunction is genuinely accessed; an existential negative would require querying or otherwise adequately covering **all** designated subjects. A question about `N` additionally requires reliable evidence that two experiences belong to numerically distinct subjects. This stipulation is a *transparent limiting case*, not a claim that such an oracle or assay exists. P and C-layered produce the same target-level `D,G` outputs under it; identifying different inaccessible fields is extra information, not supplied by the same answers.

For any real use, replace the idealized channel with a **common, independently justified error/access model**, including misreport, uncertainty, nonresponse, fatigue, leakage, memory and the impact of the probe. A report from one hand and another from speech are not automatically reports from distinct subjects; shared action or one person is not automatically proof of one subject either. Positive first-person descriptions are meaningful evidence of that reporting perspective; failure to obtain a second report is not proof that no second perspective exists. A mere behavioral classifier cannot be assumed to validate its own phenomenal labels.

A concrete prospective gate would require, before outcomes: (i) a justified mapping from the same physical episode and window to the candidate supports and independent q measurements; (ii) separately developed questions about coarse and detailed simultaneous experience, including demonstrable comprehension and access limits; (iii) an independently defensible way to distinguish distinct subject-occurrences from two contents or reports of one subject; and (iv) a calibrated way to assess each designated affective contrast without substituting intensity or expectation. Repeated samples and subject-specific uncertainty would be necessary, and an ethically approved noninvasive design should not impose new clinical surgery. If identity or coverage cannot be defended, `N` is **unknown**, even if `G` is credibly positive. If q is unmeasured, do not test its assignment by relabeling a stimulus or report as q.

## 6. Assessment and next substantive work

P's positive contribution is a fully specified **private-token alternative** to a single partially unified subject on this restricted interface. It makes one explanatory price visible: two packages can match the same coarse and detailed content-type conjunctions while disagreeing about the subject incidence needed to account for them. The global qualitative rule is explicitly separable and can be wrong without invalidating every other clause.

Its decisive debts are (1) a noncircular reason why exactly these overlapping physical organizations constitute three first-personal fields; (2) an account of what fixes the visual and affective quality of each field rather than just assigns labels; (3) a reason for the cross-field correspondence and OR law; (4) defensible field identity, persistence and reporter access; and (5) an explanation of how these facts belong to a self-sufficient whole without assuming a universal subject. A priority for future work is to articulate a substantive, independently motivated **incidence principle** that selects P rather than merely adding the C and L outputs. Until then, P and its rivals are hypotheses with different costs, not a solved subject-constitution theory.

C3 remains **full structural and experiential intelligibility**; B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 remain open. This note provides no clinical outcome, measured q, established world possibility, subject census or substantive battery pass.
