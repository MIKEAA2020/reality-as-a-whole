# Subject-pluralist experiential synthesis: a provisional reassessment

> **Current reference:** This reassessment remains useful background, but later scope corrections and research are consolidated in the [current battery dossier](CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_BATTERY_DOSSIER.md), which governs where they conflict.

## Question and verdict

Reassess the seven criteria after the user's objection that a suboptimal universe undermines an agentive universal consciousness. The most promising **working research direction**, not an established or uniquely most probable theory, is **experiential monism with plural subjects**: one reality, genuine irreducible experiences associated with multiple finite subjects, and physical structures and experiential occurrences treated as aspects or constituents of the same reality. No universal subject, universal agent, or best-world selection is assumed.

The [self-existent plenitude correction](SELF_EXISTENT_PLENITUDE_PLURALITY_CORRECTION.md) states the user's clarified aim: Bostrom guides the question of actuality, not a premise of literal simulations or creators. It supersedes the literal-simulation premise in the earlier [necessary-plenitude construction](NECESSARY_PLENITUDE_PLURAL_SUBJECTS_SIMULATION.md); its toy numerical examples are only optional comparisons.

This is a family resemblance to, not a novel replacement for, Russellian and neutral monism. See the [Stanford Encyclopedia account of Russellian monism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/). In contrast with neutral monism in general, this candidate explicitly retains real phenomenal experience; it does not assume that every fundamental entity has a subject, or that a pile of little subjects automatically forms a bigger one.

The meaningful ontological claim is not merely the tautology that everything is part of reality: a shared account must actually connect physical structure, experienced qualities, and subject boundaries. That account is missing. A monistic label is not a bridge.

## Seven criteria

1. **Experience:** preserve its reality, intrinsic character, and genuine subjectivity; do not identify experience simply with verbal report or information processing. Hypothesis: conscious episodes and their physical organization are not two causally independent events. An identity or constitution map remains to be justified.
2. **Suboptimal universe:** retain it as the user's premise. The totality need not be a perfect agent optimizing the universe. Under the user's adopted `perfect whole → best universe`, the whole is non-perfect; a non-perfect whole is not by itself a plurality-of-subjects proof. Suffering remains real and morally serious.
3. **Structural and experiential intelligibility:** seek (a) an account of physical structure, lawful regularities, and subject boundaries, and (b) a warranted account of why a given organized process has its particular felt character. First-person acquaintance supplies evidence of felt character but does not explain it; structural isomorphism or a stipulated identity does not derive it. Neither kind of intelligibility is achieved completely here.
4. **Generative and predictive power:** demand operationally specified maps from measured brain and experimental conditions to predictions, including failure conditions and controls, not claims that the general metaphysics itself predicts neural results. The present framework supplies no unique testable prediction.
5. **Physics:** remain compatible with quantum theory and general relativity in tested regimes; investigate nonfundamental spacetime and relational time, rather than assert them. See [TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md](TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md).
6. **Consciousness science:** test local candidates against interventions, maintained access, representational content and phenomenal reports with appropriate assay limitations. [COGITATE](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1) challenged specific predictions of both IIT and GNWT and did not establish this metaphysics. See [DISTRIBUTION_CUT_AND_RECORD_PERSISTENCE.md](DISTRIBUTION_CUT_AND_RECORD_PERSISTENCE.md).
7. **Elegance and parsimony:** one reality and no universal mind may reduce some assumptions, but an undefined consciousness-selection and qualitative-mapping rule can reintroduce complexity. Judge total assumptions and explanatory success against physicalist and neutral-ground rivals, not by counting slogans.

## What to relax, retain, or leave conditional

- **Relax universal subject and universal agency.** Local subjectivity and agency remain real. Do not treat a non-agentive whole as satisfying the user's earlier agentive universal-subject target.
- **Relax perfection and best-world selection.** Whole imperfection follows conditional on the user's adopted bridge and suboptimality premise. It does not establish that a better possible totality exists.
- **Retain possibility = actuality as the user's strong modal thesis, provisionally.** No external actualization process is implied. It might account for the presence of imperfect sectors without selecting this universe as best, but does not select one subject over many. A precise indexed account is required: contradictory propositions about the same sector cannot both hold, and comparisons between possible universes must not silently become contradictions within one universe. If possibility is defined as actuality, the identity alone does not independently demonstrate that a specific described sector is possible/actual.
- **Treat essence = existence and necessary, self-sufficient actuality as goals or theses, not derivations.** They must explain the existence and nature of the whole, not merely rename them; individual finite subjects need not each be necessary. Do not assume either a brute existence claim or its successful elimination.
- **Treat nonfundamental spacetime as a research hypothesis.** The timelessness of the whole and emergence of local time remain to be demonstrated. Timeless should not be confused with a physical system literally frozen in an external time.
- **Do not relax either kind of intelligibility as a goal.** Relax only the claim that either has already been secured. If complete intelligibility requires a derivation of qualitative character from nonexperiential structure alone, no such derivation is given here; whether it is possible is disputed. If it permits warranted phenomenal primitives known through experience, the theory must still explain their subject-incidence and lawlike connections without hiding the remaining work.
- **Simulation:** an internal simulator and implementation remain separate questions; metaphysical unity or plenitude does not itself establish Bostrom's statistical trilemma or supply observer measures.

## Concrete research bottleneck

The central outstanding bridge is not merely a mathematical set of correlations. A candidate must specify: (i) the physical/relational domain and empirical access, (ii) a rule for subject boundaries and the persistence of episodes, (iii) a principled mapping from such organization to experienced qualitative content, and (iv) what observation would discriminate candidate rules, accounting for reports, hidden consumers and confounders. A fixed decoder or null result cannot prove the absence of experience. Existing local causal-boundary and assay audits supply constraints, not the desired map.

A useful next empirical step is a preregistered comparison of the existing maintained-disclosure and distribution-dependent hypotheses using the same access-sensitive tasks, perturbation, independently calibrated behavioral and no-report measures, and explicitly measured alternative pathways; it must state which results favor each hypothesis and which remain non-identifying. The [robust experiential contrast](ROBUST_EXPERIENTIAL_CONTRAST.md) gives sharp partial-identification bounds for imperfect physical intervention conditions and assays, including explicit overlap cases and a finite-sample illustration. The bounds are mathematical consequences of assumptions, not established calibration for any actual experiment. The [orientation-experience test](ORIENTATION_EXPERIENCE_CONSTITUTION_TEST.md) applies the contrast to real TMS studies and shows why coarse unawareness of orientation is distinct from reporting no visual stimulus; the examined experiment does not meet the shared physical or assay gates. The [V4–FEF gate audit](V4_FEF_ORIENTATION_GATE.md) proposes a concrete visual source and narrow frontal consumer, then identifies a macaque study in which FEF inactivation changes V4 orientation discriminability despite no significant change in average tonic visual response. The examined studies do not validate a source-preserving cut, complete FEF content removal, or an intervention-calibrated phenomenal assay. Separately, the [friction/unpleasantness audit](FRICTION_AND_UNPLEASANTNESS_AUDIT.md) formalizes one candidate for the user's proposed identity of dissonance and discomfort. The [vibrational dissonance follow-up](VIBRATIONAL_DISSONANCE_EXPERIENCE_TEST.md) tests a physically coupled-phase interpretation, distinguishing fixed network frustration from state-dependent mismatch and dynamical dissipation. The [string-duality selector audit](STRING_DUALITY_SUBJECT_SELECTION.md) gives a concrete T-dual counterexample to a naive frame-dependent winding-based subject count, while showing that equivariance is not a subject selector. Published pain/EEG findings do not establish the proposed phase quantities, a literal string substrate, or a physical-to-felt-character rule. The [thoughtful Local Friendliness audit](THOUGHTFUL_LOCAL_FRIENDLINESS_SUBJECT_AUDIT.md) applies the user's supplied Wiseman–Cavalcanti–Rieffel paper to the subject-first proposal: plurality and real experience do not alone determine a single absolute friend-event through quantum reversal, and subject identity under duplication is an open issue. A subsequent [subject-identity gate](SUBJECT_IDENTITY_LOCAL_FRIENDLINESS_TEST.md) gives an exact, conditional quantum-circuit example; its probability extension is not a physical subject model. A [record-complementarity audit](REVERSIBLE_FRIEND_RECORD_COMPLEMENTARITY.md) tests a stronger untouched-external-record requirement in a specific four-qubit circuit, without assuming experience requires such a record. A [temporary-record history gate](TEMPORARY_SUBJECT_RECORD_HISTORY_GATE.md) tests an independently fixed memory projector and shows that its sequential-Born weights are not additive in the coherent reversal context; it does not rule out alternative event laws. A [collapse-identifiability gate](CONSCIOUSNESS_COLLAPSE_IDENTIFIABILITY_GATE.md) finds that one simple hypothesized experience-triggered dephasing channel is confounded with ordinary dephasing and event prevalence unless independently calibrated; collapse is not implied by subject plurality. The proposed human-level experiment has not been demonstrated. These are local consciousness questions, **not** direct tests of the one-reality metaphysics or a completed experiential explanation.

The [Observer Patch Holography repository audit](OPH_REPOSITORY_AND_CUTOFF_BRIDGE.md) examines a substantial related operational observer program and reproduces two finite-observer receipts. Its record-and-feedback observers are not thereby phenomenal subjects; its finite instrument calibration also exposes a concrete limitation of our kinetic-energy-only continuum program, not a completed solution. The [book audit](OPH_BOOK_EXPERIENCE_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT.md) adds that OPH explicitly proposes a self-reading/experience identity as a further commitment and connects its alignment score to the user's dissonance question without identifying either with proven phenomenal pain.

## Comparison and uncertainty

- **Agentive universal consciousness:** fits the previous endpoint, but suboptimality and necessary plenitude raise an unaddressed agency problem; subject incidence also remains unresolved.
- **Non-eliminative physicalism:** strong continuity with productive physical/neural science, but must defend an account of experiential identity and why it is intelligible; not refuted by calling experience real.
- **Neutral-ground monism:** permits plurality and avoids an unexplained universal subject; must explain how its allegedly neutral ground constitutes experienced quality.
- **Subject-pluralist experiential monism:** takes phenomenal reality as foundational and avoids the universal-agent objection, but owes a subject/quality map, a defense of experiential foundations, and a full reckoning of its modal and ontological costs.

These are research judgments under the user's criteria, not calibrated probabilities or a proof that the experiential candidate is most likely true. No currently supplied candidate satisfies all seven at the requested level or achieves zero residue.
