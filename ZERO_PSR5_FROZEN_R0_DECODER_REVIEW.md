# PSR-5 battery: a frozen R0 decoder review

## Disposition

I examined the linked [`psr-5 battery` directory on main](https://github.com/MIKEAA2020/reality-as-a-whole/tree/1f4cc1ce917d06284c98f9b552961e724cccf5ac/psr-5%20battery), captured at main commit `1f4cc1ce917d06284c98f9b552961e724cccf5ac`. The exact six files are preserved unchanged in [the branch's source folder](psr-5%20battery/). The frozen [PSR-5 entry packet](psr-5%20battery/Plural_Structural_Realism_Entry_Packet_v5.md) has SHA-256 `b0d2a0fe6c72f19437aa1804230d0c2ba41c26a878a26deffbf50bd93d901321`; its separate [entry review](psr-5%20battery/Point-Sphere_Plural_Structural_Realism_PSR-5_Entry_Review_v1.md) admits it to the battery but explicitly does not execute R0–R3.

**My independent R0 result is a non-pass for the packet as a self-contained frozen specification.** It declares an explicit, determinate global decoder, but the record assigned to the macro `010` is not uniquely specified even up to the packet's own structural isomorphism. That ambiguity propagates into its infinite alias branch and into the alleged finite slice. The [verifier](verification/zero_psr5_global_decoder_r0_test.py) checks two concrete nonisomorphic completions and the exact affected binary codes.

This challenges the entry review's specific *Decode-Entry-Pass* finding; it does **not** retroactively change what that review said. It also does not establish that the proposed plural family is contradictory, that every possible completion fails, or that point–sphere has succeeded. A fuller independently frozen specification might resolve the ambiguity. As the packet forbids changing its core in place, adding that missing specification now would be a fresh candidate, not a silent PSR-5 repair.

**Execution boundary:** R0 is the only battery stage examined here. No R1, R2, R3.a, R3.b, R3.c or public truth/error bridge is executed or passed. The separate [S2 preregistration evaluation](psr-5%20battery/Point-Sphere_PSR-5_S2_Public_Truth_Error_Bridge_Preregistration_Evaluation_v1.md) records a draft bridge as entry-incomplete and likewise reports no bridge execution.

## 1. What this battery is for

The independent rival family proposes a plurality of actual structural classes, with role individuals defined by automorphism orbits. Its candidate explicitly leaves the actuality/instantiation relation and the connection to actual experience as primitives. The rival is therefore useful as a comparative control **without pretending its residue has been explained**. The packet itself does not claim a nonbrute, no-selector, or zero-residue pass.

The candidate's seven macro records, infinite generic codes, isomorphism quotient, actual extension and `N*=3` finite slice are intended to produce an executable global-to-local test. R0's frozen pass condition requires an explicit infinite model, actual plurality, orbit/pattern transport, finite slice and locality. The present check concerns the declared **global decoder**. It is separate from whether a structure is actual, whether its label denotes a genuinely felt experience, and whether the physical Ring5 example recovers empirical physics.

The [family preregistration](psr-5%20battery/Plural_Structural_Realism_Independent_Rival_Family_Preregistration_v1.md) and [family validation](psr-5%20battery/Point-Sphere_Plural_Structural_Realism_Rival_Family_Preregistration_Validation_v1.md) concern registration, not a completed battery. The PSR-5 entry review is a later candidate-level review; the family validation's earlier “no candidate” statements must be read at that earlier stage, not as a claim that PSR-5 has never been submitted.

## 2. The frozen code and the missing record

The packet states that the accepted-code decoder is total and determinate and gives

`010 → S_with`.

Its generic alias branch is

`1110 N(i) N(r) → π_r(S_i)`,

where `i=2` denotes `S_with`, `N(n)=1^ell(n) 0 bin_ell(n)`, and the permissible permutation index obeys `r < |U_i|!`. The structure record includes the typed carrier, relations, contents, temporal indices, successors, **finite role values ν**, and optional field Φ. Its isomorphisms must preserve every one of these fields, including ν.

The only specification of `S_with` in the frozen fixture section is that it is a nonisomorphic `StructCand` **with one Ag-role and no physical field**, and that it is not instantiated. The packet does not state its full carrier, relation table, role-value map, or a general rule that all its unlisted fields are empty. The sentence saying “All unlisted fields are empty” immediately precedes this and concerns `S_Φ`; it does not state a general default for all seven macros. There is no further `S_with` definition in the packet.

Even granting the strongest natural reading—**the entire carrier has exactly that one Ag role**—two different finite records meet the stated description:

| Record | Carrier and type | `ν(Ag).instrumentalized` | All other fields |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `A` | one `Ag` role | 0 | empty |
| `B` | one `Ag` role | 1 | empty |

Both are finite, typed, have no field or relations and satisfy the packet's stated `StructCand` conditions: there are no Pr, Ph or Ac roles requiring hosts, and no edges violating the order rules. Each is nonisomorphic to the specified 16-role semantic fixture and five-role physical fixture, so either can be outside the declared actual extension. The only carrier bijection maps the Ag role to the Ag role, but it cannot preserve the distinct ν values. Thus `A ≇_str B`.

This is an **underspecification**, not a claim that either A or B is the intended `S_with` or an actually instantiated object. A rule that only moral successors may contain the value `instrumentalized=1` would exclude B, but the packet does not state such a `StructCand` rule. Similarly, an established, pre-existing general default for unlisted fields could change the analysis; no such general default is supplied by this linked frozen packet.

## 3. The same accepted alias has two nonisomorphic possible outputs

Under both one-role completions, the identity permutation has index `r=0`, and `0<1!`. The frozen natural-number code gives

`N(2)=11010`, `N(0)=100`,

so the exact accepted alias is

`1110 · 11010 · 100 = 111011010100`.

For completion A this decodes to A; for completion B it decodes to B. They are not related by the declared isomorphism. The length-three macro `010` is already ambiguous; the alias demonstrates that this also affects the **global generic decoder**, not only an isolated finite label.

There is a second, independently conditional ambiguity. If “one Ag-role” permits other role types, an extra unconnected Si role also satisfies the stated fixture description. Then `|U_with|` may be 1 or 2, so the specific code

`1110 · N(2) · N(1) = 111011010101`

is rejected when `|U_with|=1` but accepted when `|U_with|=2`. This second argument depends on the permissive reading of the sentence; the **primary ν counterexample does not**. In either case, the missing information cannot be recovered from a label or a declaration that a map is total.

The issue also affects the purported `N*=3` finite slice: its carriers and the restriction of ν depend on which `S_with` is decoded by `010`. The *number* of macro strings is finite, and the claim that complete generic codes are longer than three is not being disputed. The non-pass concerns whether the frozen text uniquely supplies the stated slice and global decoded structure, and therefore the R0 locality comparison as an operation on that specified structure. It does not prove that no chosen completion could satisfy a locality theorem.

## 4. Precise limits of the result

1. **Entry review versus R0.** The existing entry review records a restricted-formal admission and explicitly withholds all battery results. This audit accepts that as the record of its decision, but supplies a counterexample to its specific assertion that the frozen decoder is determinate. R0 is non-passing on the standalone packet; it is not a finding that the entry reviewer executed R0.
2. **Family versus candidate.** PSR-5's underdetermination is not a refutation of plural structural realism as a family. Other explicitly specified structures or different formulations may work; they require their own entry and battery.
3. **Formal countermodels versus actuality.** A and B show what the supplied language fails to distinguish. They are not claimed to be two genuinely possible actual wholes or self-sufficient realities. The user's proposed necessity/plenitude cannot be settled by naming mathematical descriptions possible, nor by excluding one merely to get the desired conclusion.
4. **Public truth and experience.** The packet already discloses `Instantiates`, `ActualExperience`, `Qualia` and `ExpRealizes` as primitives. A correct code or a structurally integrated graph would not alone establish an actual subject or felt quality. Likewise, the S2 evaluation's missing public claim/reference channel cannot be filled in by this R0 check.
5. **Physical and normative scope.** The Ring5 table and lexicographic action interface are frozen restricted formal fixtures, not claimed QM/GR recovery or objective normativity. Their battery stages are **not executed** here.

## 5. A constructive fresh-candidate repair—not a PSR-5 amendment

The smallest useful next candidate would make **every macro a fully explicit finite record** and freeze the exact carrier order used by the permutation decoder. For example, one possible fresh specification for `S_with` is:

`U={0}; τ(0)=Ag; ν(0).instrumentalized=0; ρ=κ=η=α=Φ=∅`.

That is an illustrative new choice, **not** an assertion that PSR-5 already made it and not evidence of actuality or self-sufficiency. A successor must also give `S_noncand` exactly, state how its intentionally missing type is represented in the raw-code sort, specify default behavior for every field on all seven macros, fix the canonical carrier ordering, and verify accepted/rejected and isomorphic-alias transport against those exact records. It must then be separately frozen, receive its own entry review and, if admitted, run R0 before later stages. The packet itself calls a changed successor **PSR-6**.

That repair would improve a comparative formal control, not discharge the declared instantiation or experience residual. Nor should we weaken the user's no-residue requirement to call a declared primitive an explanation. Conversely, an R0 specification gap is not a reason to declare the rival metaphysically impossible. It simply prevents this frozen document from supplying the uniquely determinate model that its own battery requires.

## 6. Relation to the ongoing zero and sphere work

Our [echo quality calculation](ZERO_ECHO_INTRINSIC_QUALITY_AND_INCIDENCE.md) derived a unique `s j r` rule **conditional on** a chosen local algebra, ready-state calibration and composite echo neutrality; it did not derive an actual subject, felt sign or incidence measure. PSR-5's explicit `ExpRealizes` residual exposes the same kind of substantive bridge from a different direction. The proper comparison is symmetric: neither a graph predicate in PSR nor a chosen quantum parity in point–sphere acquires genuine phenomenality by its name.

Likewise, plural actual structural classes do not by themselves establish multiple ultimate wholes: a theory could regard many structural descriptions or domains as internal to one whole, but PSR-5 does not derive such an identification. Our one-whole proposal and the PSR family should be compared at the level of actual constitution, subject incidence, physical dynamics and empirical consequences, not by equating their terminology. Neither this R0 result nor an admission review resolves the zero-level constitution or rules out the user's stronger necessity thesis.
