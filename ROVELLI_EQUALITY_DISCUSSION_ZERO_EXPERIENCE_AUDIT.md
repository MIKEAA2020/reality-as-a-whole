# Rovelli, “equality of all things,” and our zero–experience investigation

## What was supplied, and what this analysis establishes

The supplied [50-page PDF](sources/uploads/on_the_equality_of_all_things.pdf) is a **secondary question-and-answer discussion about Carlo Rovelli's book**, with an explicitly hypothetical “Munger cut” and a further cross-disciplinary investing synthesis. It is **not the text of the book**. I read the text extracted from all 50 PDF pages; the author metadata identifies “Capital Ideas Online,” but the PDF does not establish who wrote the responses. The source is preserved at the link above (SHA-256 `92106c7ef947e4b0dd1bf3f8ef97628da0f3fcb22a833be9ce78d58f07cd9d2d`). Page references below are to the supplied PDF, not to the book.

I also inspected the cited [Science Friday book excerpt](https://www.sciencefriday.com/articles/on-the-equality-of-all-things-book-excerpt/) and [AnOther's interview with Rovelli](https://www.anothermag.com/design-living/17469/carlo-rovelli-cosmic-equality-metaphysics-penguin). These corroborate several central claims but are not the whole book. The [publisher's page](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/468031/on-the-equality-of-all-things-by-rovelli-carlo/9781837313167) is a description, not independent proof of every interpretation. The PDF's Munger passages are speculative reconstructions, not historical statements by Munger about this book.

**Main finding:** the attachment usefully challenges us to make *levels, observables and information loss explicit*. Its strongest challenge to the user's project is deeper: Rovelli does not merely say human knowledge is finite; in the excerpt he calls the questions of an ultimate foundation and ultimate reality meaningless. The user's self-sufficient whole, by contrast, takes ultimate intelligibility as a real demand. These positions overlap in their rejection of an external observer and a separate mental substance, but they genuinely disagree over whether an ultimate, zero-residue account is meaningful or required.

Neither side has been established by the attachment. A worked test below shows exactly how one physical coarse-graining can preserve one candidate distinction but destroy another, using our existing spin–record model. It does **not** establish what is experienced, or prove a cosmic energy balance.

## 2. Direct source check: where the PDF is fair, and where it compresses

**The equality claim is not “all theories are equally good.”** The excerpt explicitly says that a bottle, its atoms and the market that brought it have legitimate roles in partial effective descriptions, using appropriate, somewhat different criteria of reality. It says fundamental physics alone does not supply the concepts or worked-out solutions for every question, and describes high-level knowledge as autonomous. The PDF's equal-legitimacy versus unequal-usefulness distinction (pp. 14–15, 48) is a reasonable interpretation of that passage. This does not show that every alleged entity or economic explanation is real, or that different explanatory levels are separate substances.

**Rovelli's objection is stronger than limited human capacity.** In the excerpt he calls ultimate-foundation and ultimate-reality questions meaningless, not merely presently unanswerable. In the interview he says knowledge is partial and attacks the idea of an external, complete view. The PDF sometimes phrases this cautiously as “may be no ultimate foundation” (p. 2); that is more cautious than the excerpt's stated attitude to ultimate-foundation questions. Neither the excerpt nor this interview alone permits a complete reconstruction of every nuance in the book.

**Relational quantum mechanics is an interpretation, not an uncontested inference to a universal ontology.** In the interview Rovelli explicitly proposes that properties are relative to interacting physical systems and need not be relative to humans. The attachment (pp. 1–2, 5, 37) captures this. It is nonetheless an interpretive and philosophical extension to say *all* reality is fundamentally relational; the empirical success of quantum theory alone does not select that interpretation over every competitor. A physical observer in this sense is not thereby a conscious subject.

**“Not fundamental” does not mean “unreal.”** This is particularly relevant to experience. A description of neuronal or relational organization need not eliminate the reality or significance of an experience. But granting the legitimacy of a higher-level description does not by itself establish which physical organization is a subject or what its experience is. The PDF's examples support methodological pluralism; they do not solve consciousness.

**Causal loops are not automatically self-explanatory.** The PDF's trust–repeat purchasing–scale–service loop (pp. 43–44) can be a real dynamical feedback mechanism. Its existence does not alone explain its laws, initial and boundary conditions, stability, or why this loop rather than another exists. Equally, the absence of a single first event is not a defect if the issue is a non-temporal constitution. A mutually supporting *epistemic* network, a physical feedback cycle, and an ontologically self-sufficient whole must not be conflated.

**Multidisciplinary consensus is not a symmetry theorem.** The PDF's cross-model convergence idea (pp. 42–43) can provide corroboration when sources genuinely add information. If they use the same data, incentives or assumptions, agreement may be highly correlated and should not be counted as independent confirmations. Unlike a Lorentz transformation or gauge equivalence, switching from employee to supplier viewpoints has no generally specified invertible map or invariant metric. The analogy is useful, but its rigor must not be imported from relativity by name.

## 3. The real philosophical encounter

Let us distinguish three claims rather than slide between them:

1. **Embedded and finite knowing:** any actual investigator knows from within reality and has limited resources. This is compatible with the user's claim that reality is intrinsically intelligible; intelligibility need not mean that humans possess a complete external God's-eye report.
2. **Autonomous effective understanding:** an organizational explanation may be real, reliable and indispensable even when its constituents obey physical laws. This is also compatible with a self-sufficient whole and a noncausal identity: the right organizational description may be what makes an identity intelligible.
3. **No meaningful ultimate foundation:** the excerpt presents this as Rovelli's own more radical view. It conflicts with taking the demand for a fully self-sufficient explanation of the actual whole as a necessary and meaningful question.

One cannot infer (3) from (1) alone: an embedded investigator's finite knowledge does not demonstrate that reality itself is not fully intelligible. Nor does it follow from the user's ontological intelligibility demand that a finite observer has complete knowledge, or that every practical question must be answered at the lowest microscopic level. But conversely, insisting that the whole must be fully intelligible does not **by itself** refute Rovelli's contention that “ultimate foundation” is a malformed question. Their disagreement concerns the status of the demand itself and whether mutual dependence leaves a genuine unexplained remainder.

Steelman both. Rovelli's challenge: a demand for an ultimate, perspective-free inventory may misuse concepts whose application always depends on partial practices and relations; perhaps no further stopping point is needed. The user's challenge: a network's internal intelligibility should not be confused with an explanation of why that whole is actual and why its relations have their particular constitution; if the reality of the whole is left brute, the demand is unmet. The user does **not** require a temporal producer outside the network, and zero need not mean an empty world. The dispute is whether those final “why” questions have well-defined answers and whether a non-brute self-constituting whole can supply them.

For the strong conditional argument, full intelligibility plus the actual-whole dominance premises entails zero and identity, as set out in [the common-constitution note](ZERO_FULL_INTELLIGIBILITY_COMMON_CONSTITUTION.md). The present attachment neither proves nor disproves those premises. Equal status across levels does not entail zero total energy, and a picture of relations does not entail experiential–physical identity. Conversely, a meaningful ultimate constitution need not make organizational explanations redundant.

## 4. A quantitative test: when a useful compression loses the relevant distinction

The attachment's coarse-graining question (pp. 34–35)—did we discard what matters?—can be made exact in our already specified [four-spin subject–access circuit](CONSTITUTIVE_SUBJECT_QUALITY_ACCESS_CIRCUIT.md). This model is classical and hypothetical; its “quality” interpretation and subject boundary are proposals, not observations. The spin kernel and extended memory registers, not a real brain, fix the probability law.

Let `q⁰,q¹∈{0,1}` be the before/after mismatch bits. Two candidate targets are

```text
level   L = q¹,
onset   O = 1{q⁰=0 and q¹=1}.
```

With the existing equilibrium and one-step kernel, the exact weights are

```text
q⁰q¹:       00       01       10       11
weight:   295/448   41/448   41/448   71/448
L:             0        1        0        1
O:             0        1        0        0
```

Suppose an observer retains only `L`. That is a perfect compression for predicting the **level target**, because it is the target. But when `L=1`, the discarded history distinguishes both `O=1` (weight `41/448`) and `O=0` (weight `71/448`). Thus no deterministic function of L can recover O on every supported episode. For this specified distribution the best possible binary predictor of O from L always predicts 0 and has error

```text
P(error) = 41/448 ≈ 9.15% overall;
P(O=1 | L=1) = 41/112 ≈ 36.61%.
```

This low overall error is partly a base-rate effect; it should not be mistaken for a good onset detector on the selected positive-level cases. The same result holds for the optimal onset predictor given **all four final spin values**, not just L, in this particular kernel. At least one identical final spin state occurs with both onset values. This does not say current physical states can never contain a history: the existing circuit's memory registers explicitly do.

Retain `(q⁰,q¹)`, or retain just the already computed onset bit O, and O is recoverable with zero model error. These representations are equivalent only for this particular target: the two-bit history contains more information. Conversely O alone cannot recover L on persistent-mismatch histories; the optimal error for predicting L from O is `71/448`. Therefore **there is no context-free “right amount” of compression**: a representation can be exact for one question and lossy for another. This is a rigorous, narrow instance of the attachment's methodological point, not a claim that these particular bits are experiences.

The exact calculations and supported-state collision checks are in [the new test](verification/rovelli_equality_coarse_graining_test.py). The optimal error is the sum, over each representation cell, of the smaller of its target-0 and target-1 weights. It is conditional on the declared probability law, target, equal error costs and access to the stated representation; it is not a universal limit on inference or a real-world error rate.

## 5. Consequences for zero and experience

### The physical zero

The most recent [GR recovery model](ZERO_ENERGY_CONSTRAINT_REFERENCE_AND_PHYSICAL_RECOVERY.md) supplies a related but different example. Its whole-level Hamiltonian constraint is zero in each of two model solutions, while their effective local densities, local expansion relative to a scalar clock, and sphere Misner–Sharp energies differ. The zero alone therefore does not reconstruct those distinctions. The constraint and the quasi-local energy are physically **different defined quantities**, not competing values of one identical measured observable. Neither the PDF nor coarse-graining establishes that this constraint is the actual universe's total energy.

This is exactly where the attachment strengthens, rather than automatically defeats, the user's proposal: a self-sufficient zero would need to contain and make intelligible the real organization, not merely output a zero after discarding it. The SR analogy remains noncausal: a consequence of a constitution need not be produced by external coercion. But in each case we must specify the actual constitution and the recovery relation, not assume that conceptual usefulness establishes the desired identity.

### The experiential identity

The two-bit calculation provides a useful criterion for any proposed subject or quality description: **state what question it must answer and show what distinctions its map preserves**. If a proposed experiential target depends on temporal onset, retaining only a present mismatch is insufficient; if the target is level, the same compression suffices in the toy. First-person reports, history, physical measurements and access conditions must be related without treating any single named register as intrinsically trustworthy.

Yet whether actual unpleasantness is level, onset, another pattern, or not captured by this model remains unestablished. A coarse-graining can reproduce stipulated labels without producing a subject, nor does a software analogy decide whether substrate-independent consciousness is real. A supported physical–experiential identity may be noncausal and may describe one occurrence in different terms; it still needs an independently motivated account of **subject, quality, incidence and access**. A many-level description is not automatically a dualism, and it is not automatically the identity proof either.

## 6. Concrete next research move

A promising next task is a **target-preserving recovery map** for a genuinely common physical candidate: specify (i) the physical state and gauge redundancies, (ii) the whole-level zero and local energy/clock observables, (iii) the subject boundary and a candidate qualitative contrast, and (iv) what physical/first-person distinctions survive each proposed coarse-graining. Give an explicit counterexample when two distinct supported physical histories become indistinguishable after compression. Then test any purported mapping of our toy circuit to the gravitational model rather than assuming it.

The map would be a criterion for an adequate proposal, not by itself a derivation of the actual universe or its experience. Its most important question remains the user's: can the same internally necessary constitution select its zero, its differentiated physical content, and the lived character of its occurrences without leaving an independent unexplained remainder?
