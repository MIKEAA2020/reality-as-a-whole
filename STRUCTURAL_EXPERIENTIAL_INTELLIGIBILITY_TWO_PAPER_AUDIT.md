# Full structural and experiential intelligibility: two supplied papers

## The governing requirement

The third of the user's seven jointly required criteria is **full structural and experiential intelligibility**. It demands an intelligible account of the organization and existence of reality **and** of experience in its own experiential mode. Structural, physical, modal and existential explanations do not substitute for the experiential side; a real experiential datum does not by itself explain physical structure, necessity or the nature of the whole. Both sides must work together, without brute explanatory residue. This is the target, not a claim that the current framework has achieved it.

The two supplied papers are useful in very different ways. Penney proposes organization as a universal foundation but does not provide a complete predictive field theory or the required experiential constitution. Fischer and colleagues offer a specific, experimentally benchmarked quantum simulation, not an ontology of experience or a foundation of reality. Neither paper establishes the user's complete target. Both were assessed on their content, not their titles or publication venue.

## 1. Penney: organization is not yet a universal field theory

Darryl Penney, [*A Complete Universal Field Theory for Our Universe Built from Nothing Using Only Organization*](https://doi.org/10.63721/25JPQN0146), supplied [PDF](sources/uploads/WMJ-JPQN-25-146-A-Complete-Universal-Field-Theory-for-Our-Universe-Built-from-Nothing-Using-Only-Organization.pdf), SHA-256 `2be132fffc498a62d99b5a438ea9cfabc6751404134383ce906f2392d1258325`. I read extracted text from all **nine** pages, including the author's disclaimer, conclusion and references. The PDF describes itself as an opinion article.

The paper's main proposal is a “creation equation” described as `concept + context = nothing`, with the universe-specific expression `energy + organization = nothing`. It treats organization as a universal contextual field, invokes a parabolic gravitational effect originating in a previous universe, suggests fractal relations between atoms and planetary systems, and connects mind to the organization of concepts and context (pp. 2–5, 8). A useful motivation is to ask whether organization is indispensable to an intelligible account of reality. That motivation is compatible with the user's interest in relations, but it is not itself a model of their intrinsic experiential nature.

### Specific barriers to the claimed completeness

- **The basic expression is not a defined field theory.** The paper does not specify in the supplied text a field's domain, mathematical type, units, dynamics, coupling to matter, observables and boundary conditions sufficient to calculate and check its claims. Calling organization a field or two concepts “orthogonal” does not supply a metric or inner product, or by itself define physical orthogonality. Here “not specified in this paper” is not a claim that no such model could ever be constructed.
- **Zero is not non-existence.** If `E + O = 0` were a meaningful equation between elements of a specified vector space, a nonzero `E` and `O = −E` would satisfy it. Their sum's vanishing does not make the elements or the vector space absent. This elementary conditional example is **not** a reading of the author's undefined terms as actual physical vectors. It shows why an algebraic cancellation, by itself, cannot derive concrete actuality from absolute nothing. A timed creation from prior universes also does not supply the user's desired timeless self-existence of the whole.
- **The proposed route to mass–energy equivalence is dimensionally incomplete.** The paper proposes replacing acceleration in `F = ma` with `c²`, saying that the square is an acceleration (p. 6). In ordinary units, `[c²] = L²/T²` while `[a] = L/T²`; squaring a speed does not make it an acceleration. The dimensions of `mc²` are energy, not force. This is not a criticism of `E = mc²` itself: the issue is that the proposed replacement is not a valid derivation. The expression involving `c²/(c²−1)` additionally needs an explicit nondimensionalization and a meaning for the subtraction; in units where the dimensionless numerical `c` is set to 1, its written denominator vanishes. A different carefully defined normalization could avoid that specific issue, but is not supplied here.
- **The asserted nonlocal infinite-speed gravity is not a recovery of observed gravity.** The paper distinguishes local gravitational behavior from a separate proposed nonlocal parabolic effect (pp. 2–4), so it would be too quick to say gravitational-wave timing alone refutes the entire proposal. But it gives no worked dynamical coupling and quantitative predictions that allow one to check how that additional effect coexists with lensing, gravity tests and observed gravitational waves. Least-action formulations are variational boundary-value descriptions and do not by themselves imply an operational signal propagating instantaneously.
- **Experiential intelligibility is not delivered by naming organization.** The paper mentions consciousness and brain organization but does not specify an independently justified constitution of particular qualitative experiences, distinct subjects and their boundaries. Nor does it derive the probabilities and measurements of quantum theory. These are substantial obligations for the joint criteria, not grounds for denying the reality of experience.

The paper also advocates claims about selective breeding, human hierarchy and social engineering (pp. 6–9). These are separate assertions, not consequences established by a field equation or evidence for human worth or social policy. They are not adopted by this framework.

## 2. Fischer and colleagues: a bounded, benchmarked structural result

Laurin E. Fischer and colleagues, [*Dynamical simulations of many-body quantum chaos on a quantum computer*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-025-03144-9), supplied [PDF](sources/uploads/Dynamical-simulations-of-many-body-quantum-chaos-on-a-quantum-computer.pdf), SHA-256 `d567e1df1c157d6a56d8daca1ac75af2c4db7aea708c6b9e1354884c2437d3b0`. I read text extracted from all **eight** supplied PDF pages, including methods and references, and visually inspected main Figures 1–3. I have not audited underlying shot data or reproduced the authors' simulations.

The authors study discrete-time kicked Ising circuits, including dual-unitary gates. On the specified dual-unitary points `|J| = |b| = π/4`, they use a prepared state consisting of a `|+⟩` qubit and Bell pairs. For their selected observable, the paper reports the known exact infinite-temperature correlation result

```text
C_n(t) = [cos(2h)]^t   when n = t,
         0             otherwise,
```

within their stated odd-chain and time range `t ≤ (N−1)/2`. This is **their reported result** and builds on prior dual-unitary work, including Bertini, Kos and Prosen's [*Exact Correlation Functions for Dual-Unitary Lattice Models in 1+1 Dimensions*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.123.210601); it is not a new derivation here. The temporal units and the particular gate-layer convention must be taken from the paper; the circuit time is an internal simulation index, not a claim about a fundamental universal clock.

The experiment used up to **91 superconducting qubits** and **4,095 two-qubit gates**. Error-mitigated results for the specified observables are compared with the exact benchmark at several `h` values. The measurements use 262,144 shots per data point; the plotted error bars denote one standard error and do not automatically include all systematic errors. The paper itself notes residual systematic deviations at larger circuit volumes.

An important verification distinction: the **Clifford point `h=0` is used to calibrate the noise model**, so its agreement is not an independent validation of that calibration. The non-Clifford dual-unitary circuits provide a separate analytically checkable benchmark, although they still depend on the same learned noise model and assumptions. Tensor-network error mitigation is classical postprocessing of noisy output; it is not fault-tolerant error correction and does not change the dynamics that ran on the processor.

Away from the dual-unitary point, the same measured local expectation **is no longer the same infinite-temperature autocorrelation function**, as the authors explicitly warn. There the paper compares with approximate tensor-network calculations in both pictures; the two simulations disagree and their convergence criteria are heuristic. Good agreement with one approximate comparison is useful evidence, not exact independent verification or proof of unrestricted quantum advantage. The authors suggest possible future access to two-body correlators; those are not results established by the present single-observable benchmark.

### What this contributes to the seven criteria

The work illustrates C4–C5: a specified physical model, observable, independently calculable benchmark and documented errors. A zero in this **particular** correlator is not proof that no information, influence, other correlation or experience exists there. Scrambling, circuit duality and entanglement do not themselves constitute a subject, determine phenomenal qualities, or establish a timeless experiential ground. A structurally elegant circuit may help check a proposed physical realization only if a separately justified constitution supplies the link to actual experience and genuine plural subjects. Its mathematical duality between circuit directions does not establish that physical space and time are interchangeable ontologically.

## 3. Joint assessment and next constructive step

| Requirement | Penney | Fischer et al. | Needed for the project |
|---|---|---|---|
| Experience as valid datum and full experiential intelligibility | Mentions consciousness but gives no detailed justified constitution of felt quality or distinct subjects. | Does not purport to study or explain experience. | Preserve actual experience and derive or independently justify the subject/quality constitution. |
| Full structural intelligibility and self-sufficiency | Names organization and creation, but lacks an adequately specified field and recovery of accepted laws in this paper. | Rigorously specifies an effective circuit and selected observable, while assuming its physical apparatus and dynamics. | Explain why the underlying relational constitution exists and recover specified physical structures without external time. |
| Predictive power and physics alignment | Several physical assertions lack the quantitative definitions needed for a comparison; the mass–energy derivation has the dimensional issue above. | Exact benchmark on a restricted circuit family, controlled mitigation, approximate off-point comparisons. | A candidate must recover benchmarked results in an appropriate domain and give a genuinely discriminating, error-controlled prediction. |
| Elegance/parsimony | A short verbal expression is not an account of total assumptions and mappings. | Solvability is a valuable economy within a stipulated model, not an ultimate ontology. | Compare the complete physical **and** experiential bridges, not the number of symbols or labels. |

**Constructive path:** use a known exactly solvable circuit correlation as a structural acceptance test for any future relational constitution that claims to recover the corresponding effective model. State the initial state, observable, gate convention and error model, then compare to the published prediction and unmitigated/mitigated data. In parallel specify what, if anything, the proposed constitution says about *real* experiential subjects and qualitative character in that physical setting. Do not infer that a processor is conscious or that a vanishing correlator marks an absent subject; if there is no justified experiential bridge, that side of the criterion remains open. This is a proposed test programme, not a new experiment or solution of the hard problem.

- Fischer et al.: full supplied article and methods text plus main figures were read/inspected. The [supplementary information](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41567-025-03144-9/MediaObjects/41567_2025_3144_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), including 19 supplementary figures and a table, could not be retrieved by the page-fetch tool; it is not claimed globally unavailable. The article's [data and code record](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29069759) also failed to load through the page-fetch tool; datasets and code were not inspected. Its earlier [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00765) was consulted only at chunk 0 of 22; the supplied published version, not that partial preprint, is the basis of the assessment.
- Bertini, Kos and Prosen, [*Exact Correlation Functions for Dual-Unitary Lattice Models in 1+1 Dimensions*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.123.210601): citation and abstract/search excerpts only; full source not read. It is cited as the experiment's prior work, not independently audited.
- LIGO, [*LIGO Detection of Colliding Neutron Stars Spawns Global Effort to Study the Rare Event*](https://www.ligo.caltech.edu/news/ligo20171016): only chunk 0 of 4 read, not used to infer that Penney's distinct nonlocal effect is ruled out.
