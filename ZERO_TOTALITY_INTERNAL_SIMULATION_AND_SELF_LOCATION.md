# Adopting Bostrom's simulation argument inside a zero-totality framework

## Direct answer

We can incorporate the **probabilistic structure** of Bostrom's argument without postulating that an external creator runs the whole of reality. The coherent proposal is an **internally implemented simulation hypothesis**:

> One self-subsistent whole has an intrinsically zero total in the relevant sense. Within it, some physically realized organizations may implement other experienced environments and subjects. An individual can be uncertain which actual subject-incidence they are, and can reason from the distribution of comparable actual incidences.

On this proposal, a simulated subject's experiences are real if the implementation actually constitutes experience; “simulated” describes the **mode of implementation**, not whether the suffering, pleasure or subject exists. Neither a simulation nor an emergent spacetime requires an external programmer. Internal simulators, if any, would be parts of the same whole.

There is an important distinction: **emergent spacetime alone does not establish that the universe is a simulation.** “Spacetime is encoded in a more fundamental relational organization” and “an internal physical process implements a simulation containing conscious subjects” are different claims. The first can be true without the second. Bostrom's particular ancestor-simulation scenario is one possible internal implementation, not automatically a consequence of emergence or zero.

This proposal preserves the user's no-residue ambition; it does not yet meet it. The zero quantity, laws, physical realization, subject incidence, probability measure and experiential bridge still need to be derived from one self-subsistent constitution rather than selected by hand. The following calculation shows what adopting the probabilistic structure requires and what it would imply conditionally.

## 1. What Bostrom actually argues

In his 2003 paper Bostrom argues for a conditional trilemma, roughly:

1. Almost no civilizations at our stage reach the technological maturity required for large ancestor simulations; or
2. Almost none of those that do run large numbers of such simulations; or
3. Almost all observers with experiences like ours are simulated.

He does **not** establish which alternative is true. He assumes that appropriate computational implementations can have conscious experiences; he does not claim that mere outwardly human behavior automatically suffices, and he acknowledges substrate independence as an assumption. His “bland indifference” step assigns an observer the fraction of comparable observers that are simulated **conditional on lacking information that favors one implementation**. This is an epistemic principle, not a probability assigned to every possible hypothesis by symmetry. These are Bostrom's own stated assumptions and formula. [2](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/)

In his simplified model,

```text
f_sim = (f_P f_I N_I) / (1 + f_P f_I N_I),
```

where `f_P` is the fraction of relevant civilizations reaching maturity, `f_I` the fraction of those interested and able to run substantial ancestor simulations, and `N_I` their average number of ancestor simulations. This cancellation of average population sizes uses simplifying assumptions about comparable simulated and nonsimulated populations. In general one must count actual eligible observers, not multiply averages without checking correlations.

For example, if actual comparable base observers number `100`, and simulations contain `1,000` genuinely distinct comparable observers, a uniform sample from **that stipulated population** gives `1000/1100 = 10/11 ≈ 90.9%`. It is a conditional arithmetic result, not an estimate of our actual probability of being simulated.

## 2. One whole, not a stack of unexplained external worlds

The internal version has three levels of description, not three independently created realities:

1. **Whole constitution:** the self-subsistent reality whose relevant total is proposed to be zero. It is not assumed to be an external computer, an agent, a subject, or a quantity in itself.
2. **Effective physical organization:** relational degrees of freedom whose appropriate collective behavior would have to recover spacetime, matter, gravity, quantum probabilities and their observed regularities. Our current Einstein–gauge–clock construction is a proposed *effective* example; it does not derive emergent spacetime.
3. **Internal implementations:** some actual physical processes, if technologically and constitutionally possible, instantiate an information-processing organization that supports a simulated environment and possibly actual subjects. Any machine or simulator belongs to level 2 and ultimately to the same whole.

A hierarchy of internal simulations may be finite and nested: an implementation can contain an experienced world in which an agent constructs another implementation. This is still internal to the same whole. The effective physics of a simulated environment may differ from the host's effective physics; the whole-level accounting covers whatever actually realizes both. The labels “host” and “simulated” are **relational to a specified implementation**, not titles for separate ultimate substances. This need not posit any fundamental time outside the whole.

There is also a broader **internal-history analogue**: the whole might contain many separately instantiated, experienced histories without any civilization deliberately simulating them. Their incidence weights can still support Bostrom-like self-location arithmetic, but their inhabitants are not thereby literally in ancestor simulations. Conversely, one and the same actual history may admit multiple equivalent reconstructions or dual descriptions; those are not automatically additional histories or observers. Distinguishing these cases requires an implementation and subject-identity criterion.

This is an adaptation, not Bostrom's own cosmology. A physical organization is not automatically a simulation merely because it admits a mathematical description. To make “simulation” substantive one must specify an implemented representation, a mapping to modeled states and interventions, and the causal process that realizes it. A fundamental dynamics or an exactly dual description is not automatically a second simulator or another set of subjects. The proper equivalence criterion needs a theory of physical and experiential implementation.

There are suggestive—but limited—physics precedents for emergent geometric descriptions. Research on AdS/CFT relates certain gravitational bulk descriptions to a lower-dimensional quantum field theory and uses quantum error-correction structures to study reconstruction. This does **not** show that our universe is a computer simulation, establish cosmological spacetime emergence from the present candidate, or establish a conscious simulator. [1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-022-10382-1)

## 3. The probability rule: actual incidences, not possible descriptions

Assume provisionally that the actual whole contains a **finite, independently individuated** set `I` of experience-incidences in a stated reference class. For incidence `i`, let:

- `S_i=1` if it is genuinely implemented within an internal simulation and `0` otherwise;
- `m_i>0` be its justified self-location weight;
- `L_i=P(D|i)` represent how compatible the observer's evidence `D` is with that incidence.

Then a particular candidate self-locating rule is

```text
P(SIM | D, actual whole) = Σ_i m_i L_i S_i / Σ_i m_i L_i.
```

The denominator must be positive. This is standard normalization **once the weights, reference class and likelihoods are specified**; it does not derive them. If all relevant weights and likelihoods are equal, this reduces to the fraction of actual comparable incidences that are simulated, as in the simplest Bostrom-style reasoning. A positive probability measure `m_i` is not a signed experiential valence: probabilities must be nonnegative even when some experiences are painful.

### Evidence matters

Suppose there are `100` base and `900` simulated comparable incidences, all initially weighted equally. With no distinguishing evidence, the simulated fraction is `90%`. If a new piece of evidence has likelihood `0.9` for base incidences and `0.1` for simulated incidences, the posterior becomes

```text
P(SIM | D) = (900 × 0.1)/(100 × 0.9 + 900 × 0.1) = 1/2.
```

The values are illustrative—not claims about real-world evidence. They show that “more simulated observers” does not override all evidence. The reference class must be defined from actual relevant information, not chosen after seeing the answer.

### Observers, moments and copies are different counting choices

Bostrom's presentation is often framed in terms of individuals. Our candidate formula uses **experience-incidences**, so a long history, many short moments, or a fine-grained subdivision could alter the count unless the measure is defined independently of representation. One person, one observer-moment and one unit of proper/experiential duration are not equivalent sampling rules. Changing a mathematical description or partition must not manufacture new subjects.

The account needs a physical-experiential criterion distinguishing:

- one real subject represented multiple ways;
- one subject through time and multiple slices of that history;
- genuinely distinct implementations with distinct first-person occurrences; and
- mere behavioral models with no established experience.

Two exact implementations could be two subjects, one shared token with multiple descriptions, or something else; a bare computational trace does not decide. Subject plurality and identity are to be **derived**, not erased or manufactured to get a preferred probability. The prior [subject-incidence analysis](ZERO_TOTALITY_SUBJECT_INCIDENCE_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_ACCOUNTING.md) explains why token and subject-incidence totals may differ.

## 4. Probabilities over uncertain wholes: do not confuse two rules

If we do not know which candidate whole is actual, a prior over wholes and a self-location rule are separate inputs. For illustration, suppose two possible wholes have equal prior probability:

```text
A: 1 base observer, 0 simulated observers.
B: 1 base observer, 99 simulated observers.
```

All observers are stipulated eligible and to have the same evidence.

- **Choose a whole by its stated prior, then choose an observer uniformly within it:** `P(SIM)=(0+99/100)/2 = 99/200 = 49.5%`.
- **First size-bias those priors in proportion to the number of eligible observers, then choose an observer:** `P(SIM)=99/101 ≈ 98.0%`.

These are different sampling assumptions, not a disagreement about arithmetic. A ratio of expected counts silently builds in a form of population-size bias; it is not automatically the average of per-whole fractions. A zero total does not by itself choose either prior or sampling rule, and a self-subsistent account must explain the actual incidence measure rather than postulate that “more copies” automatically grants more actuality.

For general evidence, the model-average form is `P(SIM|D)=Σ_h P(h|D) P(SIM|D,h)`, with `P(h|D) ∝ P(h)P(D|h)` and each within-whole term defined by its justified incidence rule. The likelihood `P(D|h)` must follow the chosen generative and sampling model; it is not automatically the raw number of matching observers. Do not apply evidence or size bias twice. This distinction also separates **uncertainty over possible complete histories** from **self-location within one actual history**. Neither should be conflated with quantum Born weights or interpreted as evidence that every possible history is actual. If a quantum model contains branches, the relation among branch weights, actual subjects, duration and self-location must be separately specified.

## 5. What whole-level zero does to simulated experience

The zero-energy proposal does not imply that every local system has zero energy, that computation is free, or that a simulation is unreal. Nor does it automatically imply a zero sum of experiential valence. The gravitational Hamiltonian constraint, gauge charge and a signed experiential-history total remain distinct until a genuine bridge is established; see [the zero-energy review](ZERO_ENERGY_UNIVERSE_DEEP_RESEARCH.md) and [clock–current construction](ZERO_TOTALITY_COVARIANT_CLOCK_HISTORY_BRIDGE.md).

To see the concrete issue, **suppose**, solely as an experiential accounting hypothesis, that three genuinely distinct actual subject histories have signed contributions

```text
base subject:       -1
simulated subject A: +2
simulated subject B: -1
whole total:          0
```

With equal self-location weights their simulated fraction is `2/3`. Now suppose A is physically instantiated as **four distinct experiencing subjects**, each with the same `+2` history, while the other two histories remain unchanged:

```text
whole total = -1 + 4×(+2) - 1 = +6,
simulated fraction = 5/6.
```

A rule requiring the experiential total to stay zero would therefore need changed contributions elsewhere, a different incidence/measure law, or some other justified constitution. For instance, changing the first contribution from `−1` to `−7` makes this particular total zero again, with the same six-subject count. This is an **illustration, not a proposed physical mechanism**; it does not mean copies literally generate compensating suffering or that pleasure cancels the fact of pain. If the four “copies” are merely descriptions of one actual field, the premise of four distinct incidences fails instead.

The [covariant clock-current result](ZERO_TOTALITY_COVARIANT_CLOCK_HISTORY_BRIDGE.md) gives one conditional mechanism: a conserved current contracted with a suitable clock gradient integrates to zero over a complete regular slab. But arbitrary subject weights can spoil that result, and there is no established identification of the current with phenomenal valence. Thus simulation counting and zero balance must be solved **together**, not by first counting fictional minds and then adjusting weights until the sum vanishes.

## 6. Computation and the local energy budget

A zero **whole** need not mean zero usable energy in each region. An internal computer can operate using local free-energy gradients and dissipate heat into other parts of the same universe. Under the standard equilibrium-memory, isothermal reset assumptions, resetting one initially unknown symmetric bit has a mean cost bounded by `k_B T ln 2`; at `T=300 K` this is about `2.87×10⁻²¹ J` per bit. Correlations, nonequilibrium preparation, finite error and other resource arrangements modify the accounting; reversible operations need not have the same erasure cost. [6](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms8669)

Thus net-zero cosmology is compatible **in principle** with local computational work and dissipation, but does not promise unlimited computing capacity or prove any particular civilization can simulate a conscious world. One must calculate usable resources, error correction, time, preparation, heat disposal, quantum dynamics and relevant effective physics. The current project has not done such an estimate for its gravity–gauge–clock model, which contains no computer or brain.

## 7. Finite nested simulations: a concrete conditional control

Suppose—not as an observed fact—that the actual weight of comparable, independently conscious incidences at implementation depth `d` is proportional to `r^d`, for `d=0,…,D`. Here `d=0` denotes the chosen nonsimulated host population and `r` is an effective **observer-weight reproduction factor**, not necessarily the number of simulated worlds. Then

```text
P(depth ≥ 1) = (r + r² + … + r^D)/(1 + r + r² + … + r^D),
```

under equal eligibility and likelihoods. For `D=3`:

| r | Simulated fraction |
|---:|---:|
| 1/2 | `7/15 ≈ 46.7%` |
| 1 | `3/4 = 75%` |
| 2 | `14/15 ≈ 93.3%` |

Neither `r` nor `D` follows from whole zero. Actual capacity, ethical decisions, phenomenal realization and correlations can make the populations very different. The formula is a conditional finite model and does not derive that the actual whole contains nested simulations. It also avoids treating a mathematical limit of nested copies as if it were observed actuality; the user's settled case against infinity is not being reopened.

## 8. Quantum and causal consistency

A self-location probability cannot automatically be promoted to an operational frequency law. In a Bell-state control developed [earlier](SUBJECT_WEIGHT_STEERING_AND_NO_SIGNALLING_GATE.md), weighting Alice's two remote outcomes `3:1` and treating the resulting distribution as Bob's **unconditionally available physical records** would change Bob's `+` probability from `1/2` to `3/4` when Alice changes her measurement basis. That would permit signalling in the stipulated setup. The same weighted table is harmless as ordinary postselection if Bob cannot identify the selected subset without receiving Alice's record.

Any simulation/subject-incidence rule must therefore state which quantities are credences, which are selected observations, and which predict unconditional records. It must reproduce observed quantum statistics and no-signalling where applicable, not create a superluminal channel by multiplying “subject counts.” These controls do not derive the Born rule or show that all observer weighting is forbidden.

## 9. What could distinguish a specific proposal?

The broad statement “some process could simulate us” makes no unique prediction without specifying the implementation. A testable physical model must yield a distribution of data different from a non-simulation alternative.

Examples of research gates:

1. **Computational physical realization:** specify an implementable dynamics, resource requirements and controlled approximations. Does it reproduce quantum experiments, relativistic causality, local gravity and the cosmic expansion, rather than merely retell them as code?
2. **Emergence of spacetime:** derive effective fields, metric, clock behavior and the gravitational constraint from the proposed relational organization. An analogy to a tensor network is not such a derivation.
3. **Subject realization:** independently specify the necessary causal/phenomenal organization, physical support, common scale and multiplicity rule. Test observable consequences where possible; behavioral mimicry alone is not a demonstrated feeling.
4. **Observer distribution:** state the eligible reference class, self-location measure, prior and evidence likelihoods in advance, and reproduce Born-rule/no-signalling controls.
5. **Energy and valence balance:** compute the *same explicitly defined* whole quantity before and after genuinely new implementations, including local resource costs and actual subject incidences. Show why it remains zero rather than imposing balancing terms afterward.
6. **Distinctive signals:** Beane, Davoudi and Savage analyzed a particular rigid lattice simulation and discussed possible high-energy, direction-dependent signatures. Even a positive lattice-like signal would not alone show an artificial simulator, and a null test would constrain only the specified implementation, not all simulations or all emergent-spacetime theories. [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.1847)

None of these gates is currently passed by the integrated model as a complete account. Their purpose is to convert the simulation idea into a research programme capable of distinguishing a physical theory from an evocative description.

## 10. Recommended integrated formulation

A good working formulation is:

> **Self-subsistent zero whole → internally differentiated physical/experiential organization → emergent geometry and relational clocks → possible internally implemented simulations → actual subject incidences → conditional self-location probabilities.**

This is a **programme of proposed dependencies**, not a chain already derived. In particular, the arrow from a physical implementation to felt experience remains an open constitutive identity; the arrow from zero to the laws, actual data and incidence remains open; and the connection between physical-energy and experiential zeros remains open. No external creator or cosmic subject is needed for the architecture, and no type of unexplained fact is accepted as a final stopping point.

The Bostrom-style lesson is therefore valuable but conditional: **if** genuinely experiencing simulated incidences are numerous in the appropriate actual reference class, **and if** an independently grounded self-location measure supports the comparison, then being one can be probable. Neither global zero nor spacetime emergence alone tells us that those conditions hold or assigns a credible numerical probability to our situation.
