# Can possibility-as-actuality make the whole symmetric?

## The constructive result

The previous [whole-symmetry argument](ZERO_TOTALITY_SYMMETRY_OF_ACTUAL_WHOLE.md) needed the actual whole to be fixed by a sign-reversing transformation. The user's possibility–actuality thesis suggests a direct way to get there, **if it applies to complete global descriptions**:

> If a sign-reversed version of the entire actual whole is genuinely possible, and every genuinely possible global description is actual of the very same whole, then the sign-reversed whole is actual. If its global quantity has the opposite value to the original and that quantity has one actual value, that value is zero.

This is a real conditional bridge between the modal thesis and zero; it does not posit an external chooser, a temporal process of actualization, or a unique constraint level. It does require that sign reversal preserve **genuine global possibility**, not just that one can write down a negative number or find a local counterpart. It also uses a particularly strong global interpretation of possibility–actuality. The user's locally scoped plenitude thesis has different consequences, and should not be silently replaced by this stronger reading.

The result remains a piece of the proposed account, not a derivation of its self-subsistence or experiential constitution. The precise gain is that, under the stated premises, zero follows without separately assuming that the actual global state is already fixed by the reversal.

## 1. Direct proof for a global quantity

Let `Q` be an objectively defined, single-valued real quantity for the actual whole, and let its actual value be `q`. Interpret `◇` as *genuine global possibility*. Assume:

1. **Actual value:** `Q=q`.
2. **Genuine reversal:** `◇(Q=-q)`; for example, an independently justified transformation of the complete whole reverses Q and preserves genuine possibility.
3. **Global actuality principle, for this very proposition:** `◇(Q=-q) -> (Q=-q)`, where the conclusion concerns Q of the same actual whole and same scope.
4. **Single-valuedness:** Q cannot simultaneously have different real values for that whole in that respect.

From (2) and (3), actual `Q=-q`. By (1) and (4), `q=-q`, hence `q=0`.

Only one instance of the global principle is needed for this calculation. The full general schema is a stronger thesis. Here `q` denotes the actual value held fixed in the argument; no claim is made that anyone already knows its value, or that the expression is a local statement about another region. No additive decomposition, experience-cancelling inverse, or external observer is assumed.

**What is supplied, rather than derived:** an applicable Q, its objective zero and single value, the genuine possibility of reversing it, and the global scope of possibility–actuality. Whether Q is a generalized energy-like quantity or something else is open; fundamental reality itself need not be a quantity. If Q is represented by a quantum operator without a definite global value, this scalar proof does not apply as stated: a zero expectation is not generally a zero-eigenvalue constraint.

## 2. From a transformed complete whole to actual-whole symmetry

There is also a stronger, structural version. Let `D_W` be a **complete physical specification** of the actual whole, understood up to the theory's legitimate physical equivalence. Let T transform that specification, and suppose:

- `T(D_W)` is a genuinely possible **complete global** specification;
- the global actuality principle covers the proposition that the whole satisfies `T(D_W)`;
- there is one actual whole, and its complete specification determines its physical state up to that equivalence.

The transformed complete specification is actual, so both `D_W` and `T(D_W)` describe the same actual whole. Completeness then makes `T(D_W)` physically equivalent to `D_W`. In the appropriate sense, the actual whole is T-invariant. If Q is well-defined on those physical equivalence classes and T reverses Q, the previous [whole-symmetry theorem](ZERO_TOTALITY_SYMMETRY_OF_ACTUAL_WHOLE.md) gives `Q=0`.

This is not an argument that every partial model of W is complete, or that two distinct local structures are the same merely because each occurs within reality. The transformed description must refer to the **whole**, not to a part nested inside a larger whole. Nor is an assumed physical equivalence allowed to identify sign-opposite configurations while treating sign-dependent Q as an invariant scalar at nonzero values. If T is gauge rather than an active physical symmetry, its action and the invariant meaning of Q require the distinction already developed in the prior report.

Completeness is a strong requirement. A sign-reversed model of some physical variables may fail to transform the actual experiential, temporal or relational facts of the whole. It is therefore not enough to show that the equations of an isolated subsystem have a sign symmetry.

## 3. The load-bearing question: why is the reversal genuinely possible?

An attractive proposal is: T is an intrinsic symmetry of the full laws, so applying T to the actual whole gives another lawful global specification. But the argument also needs a **possibility-preservation principle**:

```text
Actual complete W + applicable T
    -> T(W) is genuinely possible as a complete whole.
```

Lawful description, mathematical consistency and genuine metaphysical possibility are not automatically identical. The relation between them is part of the desired account. A complete law could conceivably have further global constraints; a formal transformation could fail to preserve experiential facts or a physical boundary condition. On the other hand, these caveats do not show that the user's symmetry proposal is false. They identify the specific principle that would make it work.

The modal premise and reversal premise interact strongly. If actual `Q=q` is single-valued, the global actuality principle applies to `Q=-q`, and actual truths are genuinely possible, then

```text
◇(Q=-q)    if and only if    q=0.
```

The forward direction is the proof in section 1. For the reverse, if `q=0`, the proposition `Q=-q` is already actual and hence genuinely possible under the stated actual-implies-possible assumption. So, **within these assumptions**, establishing genuine reversal carries the full burden of showing zero in modal extension. This does not automatically make the premise viciously circular: a symmetry might have an independently intelligible constitutive justification. But simply declaring the reversed whole possible is not independent evidence that the burden has been met.

There is a useful consistency check on a proposed theory. If it says two distinct *complete global states* with different Q values are both genuinely possible, the unrestricted global `◇p -> p` schema makes both values actual of the same whole and conflicts with single-valued Q. Such a theory must revise its global possibility criterion, the schema's scope, or the interpretation of the descriptions. It cannot retain all three unchanged. This conditional tension does not establish which premise is false, nor that all local differences are impossible.

## 4. Why local plenitude is not the same proof

A different formulation of the user's thesis says that every genuinely possible **local** description occurs in some actual sector of one whole. Local descriptions can be incompatible at different locations without contradicting each other. In that reading, the proposition “a reversed local contribution occurs somewhere” is not the proposition “the whole's global Q equals the negative of its actual value.”

For example, a stipulated whole with local signed contributions `(2,-1,-2,1,1)` contains both signs and sign-counterparts for each *kind* of contribution, but its total is `1`. Finding a local `-2` for a local `2` does not remove the extra `+1` or prove a structure-preserving, measure-preserving symmetry of the entire whole. The example is a formal scope control, not a proposed physical universe or a claim that these numbers correspond to experience.

To obtain whole symmetry from local plenitude, one needs additional reasons for **matched occurrence or weighting**, transformation of all relations, and a well-defined global composition. Those reasons could belong intrinsically to the proposed whole; they need not be external interventions. They are not furnished by the existential statement that each kind occurs somewhere. Conversely, unrestricted global modality can coexist with abundant local variety: it excludes incompatible complete global alternatives, not differences among actual locations.

## 5. What the route achieves—and what it must not be used to claim

**Achieved conditionally:** genuine sign-reversal possibility at the global scope plus the global actuality principle selects zero without a separate selection act; under the stronger complete-description assumptions it yields actual-whole symmetry itself. It also clarifies why the actual nontrivial whole, rather than a prior empty state, is the starting point.

**Still required:**

- An independent account of why the whole's sign reversal is a genuine possibility, including its qualitative and relational content, not just a symmetry of a formal submodel.
- A well-defined global Q, an invariantly significant zero, and a justified relation to energy or another proposed universal quantity. Literal absence, zero net, and a richer non-additive zero-totality remain distinct notions.
- A constitutive account of why the actual whole and its principles are self-subsistent, not merely necessary by a modal schema. Necessary co-occurrence does not by itself determine explanatory priority; see [the grounding relevance analysis](PLENITUDE_TRUTHMAKER_RELEVANCE_GATE.md).
- A warranted connection to actual locally owned experience and plural subjects, preserving genuine suffering without assuming that subjective qualities cancel or that the whole is a universal agent.

The user's stronger claims—that zero uniquely can meet complete intelligibility, and that structural and experiential intelligibility are jointly satisfiable without residue—remain substantive questions. This proof neither establishes them nor shows them impossible.

## 6. Next direct inquiry

The next task is no longer simply to find a sign-symmetric mathematical model. It is to defend or challenge a **genuine-possibility preservation principle for the complete experiential whole**: what, in its nature, warrants the reversed global description as genuinely possible while retaining all actual qualitative and physical relations? An alternative is to derive a whole-level cancellation directly from internal constitution without global reversal. Either route must explain its own applicability and preserve the actual experiential datum, not replace the demand for complete intelligibility with an acceptable unexplained remainder.
