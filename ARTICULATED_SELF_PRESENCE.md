# Articulated self-presence: positive warrant and a composition consequence

## 1. The constitutive proposal

> Begin from concrete qualitative occurrence, not a bare actuality awaiting illumination. Where experience occurs, its qualitative character is actually present; it need not be presented to an additional, independently existing inner observer. Distinguishable contents can be jointly present in one experiential organization. The proposed ultimate nature is the complete, internally articulated actuality of which such organizations are real modes, rather than an undifferentiated experiential medium to which form and owners are independently attached.

This develops [necessary articulated unity](NECESSARY_ARTICULATED_UNITY.md); it is not a replacement ontology or a claim to have discovered a new essence. The governing extension to a necessarily existing, perfectly good, all-powerful, completely self-knowing absolute remains a working conjecture under the adopted requirements. The local starting point does not prove that extension.

The constructive purpose is to give self-presence a positive local reference, distinguish that reference from bare occurrence and reflective self-modelling, and derive a consequence for finite individuation. No quantum-register example or increase in software-test counts is used as warrant.

## 2. The positive reference: experienced differentiation within unity

An ordinary experiential datum is not merely that something happens. In a jointly experienced contrast, distinguishable contents are present together. The contrast itself can be experienced, not merely inferred by an external reader comparing independent records.

For example, consider the experience of two distinguishable regions in a visual field. The target is the experienced distinction and its joint presentation, not the external objects, retinal stimulation, verbal labels, or the claim that the judgment about those objects is infallible. One can misidentify the object while the mistaken appearance is still an actual qualitative occurrence.

This is ordinary defeasible first-person warrant, not a new experiment or a proof that all experience has a single sharply bounded field. The conditional core is sufficient for the argument: wherever an experienced contrast is jointly present, its account must include that joint qualitative presentation.

Three local conclusions follow:

1. The explanandum has positive qualitative content; calling it occurrence in general does not exhaust it.
2. Differentiation and experienced unity are compatible in actual experience. The claim that any differentiation automatically destroys experiential unity is too strong.
3. The content difference does not itself require two independent experiential owners. An experienced contrast is not, just by having two terms, an aggregation of two separately conscious subjects.

This does not show that the episode is ontologically simple, has no physical constituents, or is fundamental. It also does not show that every intrinsic quality is exhausted by its relations to other qualities. A particular quality need not be accompanied by an actually experienced contrasting quality in every occurrence.

The positive starting point is therefore **qualitative articulation within experiential unity**, rather than anonymous relata plus an unspecified relation or a featureless unity plus inexplicable later distinctions.

## 3. Self-presence without an added reader

Use self-presence in a restricted local sense:

    The qualitative occurrence is actually manifest in the experience;
    its manifestation is not a second copy made for another inner subject.

This does not mean that every experience represents itself, knows a proposition about itself, or possesses a reflective self-concept. Nor does it mean that all details are reportable, remembered, conceptually identified, or infallibly judged.

There are three different questions:

- Does a particular qualitative occurrence happen?
- Does the organism represent or judge that occurrence accurately?
- What concrete organization constitutes the occurrence?

The first does not wait for the second to become true. Misdescription can change a judgment without making the appearance it misdescribes never have occurred. The third remains an explanatory task; it is not answered by separating the first two.

An additional inner observer would simply become another experiencing target. Appealing to its already-present experience cannot be the entire account of the original presence. An infinite hierarchy or a cycle is not refuted merely by being infinite or cyclic, but its references alone do not independently establish qualitative occurrence.

This does **not** refute representational identity theories. An actual representational organization could constitute experience, if the identity is warranted. The objection is to explanatory substitution: a representation *of* experience or its accurate report does not constitute that experience solely because it refers to it. The inside/outside identity proposed in Lotfabadi's paper remains a candidate, not a conclusion of this argument.

This separates the preferred self-presence claim from both primitive bare happening and compulsory reflective self-modelling. It supplies no arbitrary rule that every event, information loop, or physical self-reference is aware.

## 4. Why the absolute cannot be inferred merely by enlarging the local datum

**Self-knowledge specification:** [The semantic continuation](COMPLETE_SELF_KNOWLEDGE.md) rules out requiring complete awareness to be a semantically total classical self-model supporting unrestricted negative epistemic fixed points. It does not prove that awareness is nonrepresentational; typed or otherwise restricted representational accounts remain possible. The positive local claim in this document still rests on the qualitative datum, not on a theorem that logical self-reference generates experience.

An actual finite experience warrants the occurrence of some qualitative actuality. It does not entail that every concrete occurrence is qualitative, that all experiences have one owner, or that a universal experiential field exists necessarily.

The strongest whole-account extension is nevertheless positively motivated:

- Experience cannot be excluded from a complete concrete ontology.
- The actual organization associated with experience belongs to the same world as public physical powers.
- A common concrete nature can avoid an independently stipulated matching of two otherwise complete realities.
- Under the adopted absolute requirement, neither local experience nor its explanation can have an independent ultimate foundation outside the whole.

The non-additivity argument supplies additional conditional support, not a proof of universal experiential identity. Neutral concrete constitutions and non-independent realization remain serious comparative alternatives unless their actual explanatory content fails.

The leading conjecture is therefore not that an observed human episode grows into the cosmos. It is that its actual qualitative character belongs to the intrinsic nature of the one reality, and that physical powers and finite scopes are determined within that same nature. The nature's particular determining content is still missing.

In particular, the ultimate ground should not be described interchangeably as completely undifferentiated and as containing a positive basis for every distinction. Necessary articulation has substantive content. Merely locating structure inside an experiential medium does not explain why that medium has this structure.

## 5. A composition consequence: maximal content containment cannot individuate finite modes inside an encompassing field

Consider the stronger whole-field version of the conjecture. Suppose:

1. There is a family F of candidate jointly present qualitative fields represented solely by their sets of content occurrences, with set inclusion as the proposed individuation order.
2. There is an encompassing content set W in F, and every candidate finite field m in F is contained in W.
3. Subjects are identified exactly with fields maximal under this content inclusion.

Then W is the only maximal content set: any proper finite field m is contained in the strictly larger W. Consequently, this rule cannot preserve finite subjecthood in that version of the whole-field account. If fields with the same content set can differ in mode or ownership, that already adds structure beyond the content-only individuation rule tested here.

This elementary argument is not a new mathematical theorem. Its philosophical consequence is important: one cannot invoke an encompassing experiential unity and then recover finite subjects merely as maximal collections of its contents under the same containment relation.

At least one premise must change. The relevant alternatives are:

- Complete absolute self-knowledge is not one encompassing field in the stipulated containment sense.
- Finite local ownership is constituted by a different, intrinsically grounded mode of acquaintance, not content maximality.
- The proposed finite fields are not subjects in the required sense, which would leave the actual local-ownership datum to explain in another way.

The governing account prefers the second direction while leaving the precise form of absolute acquaintance open. This is a constitutive commitment with an outstanding burden, not a solution by indexing alone. No finite mode is identified merely because the author or analyst selects a subset of contents.

This argument is specifically about maximality under **content inclusion**. It is not a refutation of all causal-maximality criteria, IIT, GWT, or other orders of comparison. Those candidates must be assessed using their actual definitions. Nor does the argument prove that local subjects are independent substances or that overlapping ownership is possible.

## 6. The strengthened candidate and its worked local meaning

The resulting proposal is:

> A finite mode is not a quantity of universal awareness removed from the whole, nor merely a proper subset of an all-containing field. It is a positively constituted manner in which qualitative contents are jointly present and locally owned. Its intrinsic organization must explain both that presence and the nontransfer of acquaintance to other contents. The whole's complete acquaintance includes the mode without requiring every content of the whole to be present under that mode.

A jointly experienced contrast supplies the local meaning of articulated presence. A thought or report about another person's contrast need not instantiate their qualitative occurrence. Two exactly matching actual episodes would still require a token/ownership account; type resemblance cannot make them one token. These distinctions are targets the proposed constitution must preserve, not proof that its concrete organization has been identified.

For a bounded mode m and a content d outside its direct scope, distinguish:

    Whole acquaintance with the fact that d occurs.
    Whole acquaintance with m's actual limitation.
    d being directly present under m's mode.

The first two do not entail the third unless a transfer premise is added. In a unified ontology, the transfer premise is not automatic. But rejecting transfer does not explain the local mode: its positive organization and warrant must be supplied.

Unlike an unexplained mask, the proposed local organization must actually constitute the scope. Existing maintained-workspace and causal-transport constructions specify some physical access relations. They have not justified the identity between those relations and qualitative acquaintance. Describing their information restrictions with this new vocabulary would not fill the gap.

## 7. What is advanced and what remains open

The defended local starting point is stronger in content than bare manifestation: actual qualitative differentiation and joint presentation are part of the evidence. The proposed self-presence is not a separate reflective observer. These commitments give a positive experiential reference to the whole-account conjecture without claiming an existence proof from definitions.

The composition argument also removes a specific tempting completion: maximal content containment cannot, by itself, constitute finite subjects inside an encompassing field. The same principle cannot be used both to establish a unique encompassing subject and to select proper finite subjects without changing its order or constitutive relation.

These results do not yet supply:

- a physical or intrinsic law of locally owned acquaintance;
- a derivation of particular qualities, physical laws, states, or finite boundaries;
- the necessary existence or unique determining essence of the whole;
- omnipotence or the complete reconciliation of perfection with actual privation.

Positive experiential occurrence is not thereby a positive evil constituent. Nor is every finite limitation a normative privation. Nothing in the local unity argument establishes that cruelty, suffering, or missing due regard is necessary or justified. Global acquaintance is not practical fulfillment.

The unresolved foundation is substantive: if qualitative nature is intrinsic, why does it have exactly this articulated organization? Identifying an actual experiential exemplar and eliminating an invalid composition rule does not answer that question. This is a philosophical refinement of the existing candidate, not the promised discovery of a complete essence or the requested non-incremental breakthrough.
