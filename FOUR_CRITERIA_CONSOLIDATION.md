# Four criteria for one complete account

## Governing proposal

Conditioned jointly on complete intelligibility, maximum elegance and parsimony, experience as valid datum, and beauty/perfection, the leading direction remains **one intrinsically articulated concrete actuality whose physical, phenomenal, and evaluative dimensions are internally constituted rather than connected by arbitrary external additions**.

This is a substantive common-constitution conjecture, not merely a warning about proof. It is stronger than compatibility among four unrelated theories. It is not yet a demonstrated unique solution, a proof of consistency, or a completed physical–phenomenal law.

Source: [the preserved exchange](sources/uploads/four-criteria-whole-account.md). Its user criteria govern; assistant elaborations are candidate interpretations, not additional user commitments. The exchange's corrective response remains relevant even though the final response reasserts some corrected claims.

## 1. Strongest jointly coherent readings

| Criterion | Full requirement | What does not follow merely by naming it |
|---|---|---|
| Complete intelligibility | No brute residue in actuality, laws, states, qualitative identity, individuation, value, or explanatory principles themselves. | That every truth is decidable by one effective formal calculus; that subjectivity must disappear; that a circular declaration explains itself. |
| Maximum elegance and parsimony | No dispensable independent commitments; maximum explanatory integration without loss of real distinctions or adequate content. | One written axiom, zero-bit source code, invariance under every imaginable transformation, or the elimination of concrete realization. |
| Experience as serious datum | Occurrence, quality, unity, duration, agency, suffering, joy, and experienced significance must be accounted for, not replaced by reports about them. | Infallibility of every interpretation; consciousness of every relation; one cosmic subject; or an additional nonphysical causal force. |
| Beauty and perfection | An unsurpassable, internally integrated fulfillment of reality, with no arbitrary defect excused as fundamental and no experiencer's reality discarded. | Actualization of incompatible total worlds, a demonstrated numerical value maximum, necessary bliss at every locus, or automatic justification of suffering. |

“Strongest” means no explanatory exemption, not attaching every superlative independently even when the additions conflict. If literal extras such as effective proof of every arithmetic truth are insisted upon, that package cannot be satisfied; deleting those extras does not weaken the demand for intelligibility itself.

The four requirements must be met together, not traded off by compensating experiential falsehood with mathematical simplicity or local cruelty with an attractive global description. Whether a jointly maximal account exists is itself an outstanding question, not established by writing four constraints.

## 2. What the exchange genuinely contributes

The strongest surviving insight is that these are **mutually constraining demands on the same actuality**:

- Intelligibility forbids leaving experience as an unexplained exception.
- Experience forbids obtaining closure by eliminating what is to be explained.
- Parsimony favors a common constitution over independently unexplained substances and bridges.
- Perfection requires an account of fulfillment and privation, not only mathematically consistent existence.

This supports the project's non-additive constitution direction. It does not deductively select idealism, panpsychism, cosmopsychism, pure structuralism, or a particular identity law. A common foundation could have differentiated intrinsic modes without every mode being an experiencing subject.

## 3. Necessary corrections to the proposed derivation

**Actuality.** Describing the absence of concrete beings does not automatically instantiate a concrete truth-bearing universe. Even a successful argument against absolute nothingness would not identify the actual world's constitution. Reflexive identity presupposes its logical interpretation; `A = A` supplies neither a concrete occurrence nor its physical content. Self-explanation must be shown, not substituted for the word “foundation.”

**Simplicity.** Conjoining many axioms into one sentence does not reduce their independent content. Kolmogorov complexity is relative to a reference machine up to additive constants; moving the explanation into an interpreter does not remove it. Explained effective constants may remain in an effective theory without becoming ultimate brute parameters. Maximal warranted symmetry is not invariance under all transformations: a symmetry must preserve specified structure.

**Necessity and probability.** Under a demand for exhaustive contrastive explanation, an unexplained selection among otherwise equivalent total possibilities would remain unpaid. It does not follow that the physical dynamics must be classically deterministic. Quantum probabilities, their interpretation, actual outcomes or branch structure, and observer evidence still need their own account. Necessary total structure and stochastic effective descriptions are different claims.

**Phenomenality.** Self-reference is not an independently warranted consciousness criterion. Neither a feedback loop nor a subject–object vocabulary derives a qualitative field. Relational geometry alone has not identified redness rather than merely the relations among color discriminations. The existing target remains a warranted constitution `F(P) = (Gamma(P), chi_P)`, including which episodes belong together and what their qualities are.

**Subjects.** One reality does not imply one experiencer. Calling local subjects perspectives of a whole leaves their actual boundaries, coexistence, and temporal incidence to be established. Coordinate descriptions of one event must not be confused with different bounded subjects. Conversely, physically distinct matching implementations must not be collapsed into one occurrence.

**Cosmogenesis.** Repeated “self-distinction” is not yet a generative construction. It needs specified objects, operation, constraints, and consequences. The Mandelbrot analogy shows how a specified rule can yield richness; it does not derive a universe, physical constants, or experience from reflexive identity.

**Agency.** Conscious deliberation can be an effective part of a necessary physical process. Material determination does not by itself make an agent a puppet, and membership in a cosmic consciousness does not by itself make an agent free. The account must preserve local reasons-responsiveness and causal organization rather than resolve agency by a label.

## 4. Perfection, privation, and the strongest remaining test

The user's surviving privation account is more precise than “pain is a dark note in a beautiful symphony.” Actual powers and experiences are positive actuality; wrongness concerns missing appropriate empathy, regard, integration, or fulfillment. Money and power are not intrinsically bad aims. The relevant defect can be neglect of others' needs and shares.

Badness-for-every-possible-goal is excluded by goal reversal; objective or interpersonal disvalue is not thereby eliminated. The due standard of fulfillment must still be justified.

Finitude is not identical with suffering: a bounded pleasant experience is an immediate conceptual counterexample to that proposed identity. Some goods presuppose adversity; this does not establish the necessity of every actual instance, severity, or distribution of adversity. Nor does common ontological identity cancel the standing of a harmed local subject.

The strongest perfection conjecture requires that no defect be ultimately brute or gratuitous. To establish that conjecture, one must explain the admissible alternatives, the standard of fulfillment, and why a less harmful constitution cannot satisfy the same complete requirements. Global necessity and local reasons to prevent harm can coexist; a successful account must include those reasons rather than neutralize them.

Value must not be introduced as an external selector acting on already possible worlds. If value belongs to the necessary common constitution, its connection with actuality must be explained internally. “The best exists because it is best” still leaves the actuality bridge unpaid unless that connection is established.

## 5. Connection to the mathematical and physical whole

The [unified constitution map](UNIFIED_CONSTITUTION_MAP.md) supplies the working interface: realized local observable algebras, state, dynamics, admissible interventions, and token realization. AQFT, quantum information, causal geometry, computation, contextual mathematics, and effective-scale organization must constrain this same constitution.

- **Intelligibility:** derive or justify the algebraic, state, dynamical, geometric, and actuality structure; do not rename them “self-reflection.”
- **Parsimony:** establish exact translations where warranted, explain shared invariants, and avoid counting redundant presentations as independent ontologies. Coarse-graining needs its own adequacy proof.
- **Experience:** the physical account must support the actual Gamma–chi constitution, not just report or discrimination capacities. AQFT localization and entanglement are not subject selectors.
- **Perfection:** explain fulfillment and due regard within that concrete organization, without pretending positivity of an operator or an optimization objective already supplies ethical goodness.

Causal sets, twistors, topos approaches, Langlands-type correspondences, scaling/fractals, and programming must have warranted roles in the atlas. No requirement makes mutually incompatible abstract models simultaneously actual, or turns every useful formalism into a fundamental law.

## 6. Consolidated whole-account conjecture

> Reality is one concrete, internally differentiated actuality whose mathematical-physical organization, genuine experiences, and qualitative-relational fulfillment are manifestations of a common necessary constitution. Its completeness would consist in explaining its own actuality and every genuine distinction without brute bridges; its elegance in having no dispensable independent commitments; its experiential adequacy in preserving qualities and distinct subjects; and its perfection in leaving no ultimately arbitrary privation. Unity is not sameness, mathematical description is not instantiation, and global integration does not erase local standing.

This is the preferred development direction under the four criteria, not a deduction that all relations feel, the cosmos is one blissful mind, or every suffering is already justified. The decisive work remains constructing the constitution and showing that it simultaneously meets the mathematical, physical, phenomenal, evaluative, and ultimate-explanatory obligations.
