# Structured void, neutrino oscillations and explanatory identifiability

## Source and scope

Brian Balke, [*Mass and the Structure of the Void*](sources/uploads/mass-and-the-structure-of-the-void-1.pdf), is a 19-page user-supplied conceptual paper, SHA-256 `aa3c13618f143953e678177ef2fd62c62f1dd2de421efc68c94db10112754c5f`. All pages, including references, were text-extracted and read. The embedded figure on page 8 was extracted and visually inspected; this is not a geometric reconstruction or an audit of the cited literature. We do not assume the paper's author is the commenter without independent confirmation.

On page 7, the paper provisionally links the W mass to charge-transfer processes; it does not derive a lattice cutoff from that mass. On pages 7–9 it proposes twisted polygon cells, loops, and a container, with neutrinos as loop-free excitations; page 9 explicitly says they are massless. Page 13 acknowledges that antimatter and weak-boson dynamics are incomplete. The model lacks specified lattice interactions from which oscillations or other quantitative predictions could be computed.

## Correction: massless is not by itself a model-independent refutation

In the conventional three-neutrino, approximately Lorentz-invariant oscillation framework, the two observed nonzero mass-squared splittings imply that at least two mass eigenstates have nonzero masses. But a different propagation Hamiltonian can generate flavor oscillations without standard neutrino mass terms. Thus the previous assessment's framing of Balke's massless neutrinos as its strongest *direct empirical contradiction* was too strong if read as interpretation-independent. Oscillations alone do not logically exclude all nonstandard massless models. They do impose a formidable, specific obligation: reproduce the complete flavor-, energy-, baseline-, direction- and matter-dependent dataset, as well as complementary kinematic and other tests.

Existing work must not be reinvented. Kostelecký and Mewes's [*Lorentz and CPT violation in the neutrino sector*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.70.031902) already presented nonstandard massless oscillations; their [repository record](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/dspace/items/554505e7-b503-4146-967b-c43ab2558e00) abstract was read, not their full article. Barger, Marfatia and Whisnant's [*Challenging Lorentz noninvariant neutrino oscillations without neutrino masses*](https://arxiv.org/html/0706.1085v2) gives the generalized bicycle Hamiltonian, analyzes solar, long-baseline and reactor constraints, and reports exclusion of the specific generalized bicycle cases it studies. We inspected the abstract, introduction, displayed Hamiltonian and selected results through search excerpts, not all eight HTML chunks, figures, underlying data or statistical analysis. Barger et al.'s later [*Lorentz noninvariant oscillations of massless neutrinos are excluded*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.6023v2) was read in abstract only; despite its broad title, its abstract qualifies the analysis to specified direction-independent effective-Hamiltonian structures with the stated high-energy behavior. Neither analysis is a proof that every conceivable massless or structured-void model is excluded. Their datasets are historical; no current global fit has been performed here.

## Finite check: a 1/E splitting is not a unique microscopic explanation

The following is an illustrative specialization of the existing avoided-crossing/seesaw structure, **not a novel neutrino theory or new derivation of the literature**. Let `E>0`, in arbitrary dimensionless units with `ℏ=1`, and in the basis `|+>, |->, |s>` choose

```text
H(E) =  [ 0  0  0 ]
        [ 0  0  1 ]
        [ 0  1  E ].
```

Set the two accessible basis states to `|e>=(|+>+|->)/sqrt(2)` and `|μ>=(|+>−|->)/sqrt(2)`. The unobserved auxiliary mode `|s>` couples only to `|->`. The two eigenvalues of its 2×2 block are `(E±sqrt(E²+4))/2`. Its lower eigenvalue is approximately `−1/E` at large E, while the uncoupled `|+>` eigenvalue is zero. Consequently the two predominantly accessible components acquire a relative phase approximately `L/E` when the propagation parameter is `L`.

For this *specified toy*, the corresponding leading two-level transition curve is `sin²(L/(2E))`, the same functional shape as a conventional two-state oscillation with an appropriate effective splitting. The auxiliary mode is not exactly absent: the verifier evaluates the full three-state unitary evolution, checks total probability and reports its occupation separately. In the example at `E=10, L/E=π/2`, the exact transition probability is `0.482686`, versus `0.5` for the leading approximation, and auxiliary occupation is `0.019226`. At `E=100` with the same ratio they are `0.499822`, `0.5` and `0.000200`, respectively. The [executable check](verification/structured_void_oscillation_identifiability_gate.py) and [receipt](verification/structured_void_oscillation_identifiability_gate.json) cover only twelve stipulated points and analytic normalization checks; the evolution formula is also cross-checked by direct numerical integration at one additional point. These checks are not a dataset or a uniform experimental error bound.

This mechanism is structurally related to the high-energy effective splitting in the bicycle models already analyzed in the cited literature. Nothing here establishes that the toy is a relativistic quantum field theory, that `|s>` is a lattice excitation, that its parameter `E` is a physically calibrated neutrino energy, or that it is compatible with neutrino measurements. The elementary example demonstrates a **conditional identifiability limit**, not a viable massless-neutrino theory: a curve with approximate `L/E` dependence, on its own, does not uniquely identify its microscopic cause. The actual bicycle-family exclusions show why matching one curve is insufficient.

## Obligations for the structured-void proposal

1. **Specify propagation and detection:** provide a Hamiltonian or equivalent law, an inner product, allowed states, neutrino production and detection couplings, and how ordinary matter modifies propagation. A word-picture of lattice transfer does not select the toy above.
2. **Predict jointly, then compare:** derive absolute and relative phase behavior across flavors, energies and baselines; account for solar, atmospheric, reactor and accelerator results and applicable kinematic observations. Specify deviations and uncertainty before comparing to data.
3. **Address the preferred structure:** a Cartesian medium and fast relaxation require an explicit account of observer-frame transformations, directional dependence and operational no-signalling. A preferred substrate is not automatically excluded; compatibility must be demonstrated quantitatively and under the assumptions of each experiment.
4. **Recover quantum measurement correlations:** a polarizer model should specify the field–material–detector interaction and show when it approximates the observed measurement instrument. Detailed dynamics and effective measurement descriptions are not mutually exclusive. Bell-test predictions and interference/which-way records must also be recovered; Bell violations constrain specified locally causal, measurement-independent models, not every deeper ontology.
5. **Close the explanatory boundary:** the paper's distortion-resistant container is a stipulated component, not automatically outside reality. But if the proposal is used as a self-sufficient whole, its existence and properties must be accounted for along with the lattice, rather than treated as an unexplained external condition.

## Consequence for the experiential whole

A nonstandard physical description may produce records similar to another while differing in microscopic ontology. This does not establish that the two descriptions have the same phenomenal subjects, nor that they differ phenomenally. For the present framework, a successful account must say which transformations are genuine redescriptions preserving physical interventions and experiential constitution, and which describe distinct physical possibilities. This complements the [auxiliary-variable representation gate](AUXILIARY_VARIABLES_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_GROUND.md): neither observational equivalence alone nor added microscopic components automatically fixes subject number, experience, or substrate independence.

The toy uses an ordinary evolution parameter; it is **not** a derivation of emergent time from a timeless whole. Balke's model and the toy also do not prove necessary existence, plenitude, intrinsic experience, or any particular subject boundary. The useful advance is narrower: an explicit correction to an overstrong exclusion, a source-grounded rival mechanism, and a precise list of what a physical and experiential completion would actually have to establish.
