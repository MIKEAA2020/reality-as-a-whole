# One reality, zero, experience and necessary plenitude

## 0. The proposal in one paragraph

The strongest integrated reading of the program is this:

> **There is one self-sufficient reality, whose intrinsic nature is experiential and whose physical description is its extrinsic articulation. Its whole-level energy is zero. It has no external space or time; spacetime, local dynamics and finite subjects arise within its constitution. The point is nothing in the whole-aspect, while the sphere expresses the internal differentiation of that same nothing—not another substance placed inside it. The genuinely possible and the actual coincide, without an external selection or temporal act of creation. The aim is an account in which the existence and determinate content of that reality are intelligible through its own nature, with no structural or experiential brute residue.**

This is the **research thesis**, not a claim that the repository has proved it. It does not eliminate matter: matter and its physical organization remain real, as the extrinsic appearance/description of the one reality. It does not make the whole a universal person or make every mathematical component a subject. It does not make suffering unreal, make mathematical symbols feel, or posit simulators.

The proposal is ambitious precisely because the listed requirements must work **together**. A zero-energy toy, an attractive ontology, a consciousness theory and a probability rule do not count as a solution if their decisive links are inserted separately. The purpose of this synthesis is to show what the existing work connects, what it actually establishes, and where to focus the next constructive derivation.

The new [gauge–incidence test](verification/zero_synthesis_gauge_incidence_test.py) below joins the latest conserved relational report to the subject/measure problem. Navigation to the wider work is provided by the mechanically generated [source register](FRAMEWORK_SOURCE_REGISTER.md). The register is an inventory, not a certificate that every historical claim was re-read or remains valid; earlier claims of closure must be read in light of later audits and the distinctions below.

## 2. A single organizing question: what is a determination of the whole?

Let \(\mathcal C\) denote the **unknown constitution**, not a newly posited physical object or a finished definition. A successful account should let us recover from it, in a mutually consistent way:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{physical structure and dynamics} & \mathsf P(\mathcal C),\\
\text{actual subjects, their unity and qualitative life} & \mathsf X(\mathcal C),\\
\text{incidence and conditional self-location} & \mathsf M(\mathcal C),\\
\text{the whole-level energy and any justified signed experiential total} &
\mathsf E(\mathcal C),\ \mathsf V(\mathcal C).
\end{array}
\]

The symbols are a **specification of the task**, not a derivation. In particular, no independent functions \(\mathsf P,\mathsf X,\mathsf M,\mathsf E,\mathsf V\) may simply be chosen and then called one explanation. Their relations must be grounded in the same constitution. The intended identity is not “a physical process causes a second nonphysical object called experience”; it is that the actual organized occurrence has an extrinsic physical description and, where a subject is constituted, an intrinsic experiential character. Which organizations do this, and why they have their particular qualities, are the substance still to establish.

This gives **parsimony a nonempty meaning**: not “there is only one word or one feature,” but no independently ultimate entities, selectors or bridging rules left unexplained. One whole can be internally rich. Conversely, repackaging several free choices under one symbol is not parsimony.

The point–sphere analogy fits here. The point is the user's representation of **nothing/zero as a whole without external extension**; the sphere represents that same whole's inner differentiation. The mathematical blow-up maps every internal direction to one external zero point. It gives an exact model of how a structured fiber can be forgotten by a projection, but the ordinary bare point does not mathematically recover its fiber. The real task is to show why the proposed zero's **own constitution** requires the differentiation, rather than simply appending a sphere to an otherwise featureless zero. See [point and sphere](ZERO_POINT_INTERNAL_SPHERE_CONSTITUTION.md).

## 3. Zero energy and zero experiential balance are related questions, not synonyms

The user's thesis that total energy is zero is a physical claim about the whole. It allows nonzero local structure and energy: zero is not being demanded of every branch or subsystem. In a controlled Einstein–scalar cosmology already calculated in the repository, the positive scalar term and negative gravitational term cancel in the Hamiltonian constraint, while the scalar's local energy density is positive and the geometry changes relative to the scalar clock. That shows *compatibility* of a zero constraint and nontrivial relational physics. It does not establish that this constraint is the universe's unique objective total energy; gravitational boundaries, normalization and the definition of whole energy must be addressed. See [the FLRW constraint](ZERO_TOTALITY_FLRW_CONSTRAINT_AND_ENERGY.md) and [the global-energy review](ZERO_ENERGY_UNIVERSE_DEEP_RESEARCH.md).

The experiential side requires equal seriousness. Suppose, for illustration, that actual subject incidences form a measured space \((\mathcal I,\mu)\) with an integrable signed felt value \(v\), and that a genuine involution \(\tau\) pairs incidences such that

\[
 \mu(\tau A)=\mu(A),\qquad v(\tau x)=-v(x).
\]

Then \(\int v\,d\mu=0\). Each negative contribution, including suffering **if that is what the value denotes**, remains locally real. This is not the claim that pleasure erases suffering or that negative experience means absence of experience. Nor is it a license to subtract infinities: an infinite whole needs a justified measure and convergence or an alternative well-defined pairing prescription.

The actual work is in the words **genuine**, **incidence**, **measure**, **felt** and **opposite**. An energy-sign counterpart is not automatically an experiential counterpart; a spatial mirror or time reversal does not generally reverse energy or felt valence. Nor may one sum energy over subjects using \(\mu\) without deriving the relevant energy allocation and measure. The proposed experiential intrinsic nature motivates seeking a deep connection between the physical and experiential cancellations, but the connection must be established, not assumed.

## 4. A new, unifying constraint: counterpart or gauge?

This is the most useful new connection between **zero, plenitude, subject identity, symmetry and measure**.

Suppose a physical transformation exchanges two labels \(x,\tau x\), and we want the experienced signs to be opposite: \(v(\tau x)=-v(x)\). If, however, the theory says \(x\) and \(\tau x\) are **the very same subject incidence under a gauge redescription**, a well-defined scalar felt value must also satisfy \(v(\tau x)=v(x)\). Together they force

\[
 v(x)=0.
\]

Therefore a nonzero felt sign cannot be obtained merely by declaring a gauge relabeling to be an opposite experience. For the pairing to explain real positive and negative experiences, the relevant operation must relate genuinely distinct incidences, or a further physical–experiential structure must explain how its action changes the felt value. Conversely, a symmetry of the whole does not automatically make its local partners gauge-identical; distinct occurrences can be exchanged by a symmetry. This distinction cannot be settled by terminology.

The [new verifier](verification/zero_synthesis_gauge_incidence_test.py) checks the issue **in the latest actual finite report circuit**. Its reference exchange and pointer flip \(T\) leave the unselected state invariant while exchanging two orthogonal recorded branches. The chirality \(\chi\) and pointer sign \(Z_M\) are each odd under \(T\); their agreement \(\chi Z_M\) is even and has value \(+1\) in *both* branches. If \(T\) is imposed as gauge and its two branches are identified as the same incidence, the absolute sign is not a gauge-invariant subject fact; the invariant agreement remains, but it no longer supplies opposite felt values. If the branches instead represent distinct incidences and the sign is genuinely felt, cancellation is possible under an invariant incidence measure. The calculation does **not** decide which interpretation is physically or phenomenally correct.

This exposes why the following are not interchangeable:

1. a symmetry of an unselected quantum state;
2. a physically distinguishable pointer outcome relative to a specified interface;
3. a mere change of description;
4. an actual subject incidence; and
5. an opposite felt quality.

A complete theory has to derive how these layers relate. This constraint is not an objection to necessary plenitude: it says what would have to be shown before a proposed counterpart is counted as an additional actual incidence.

## 5. One whole, all genuine possibilities, and determinism

The strongest whole is one: every genuinely actual occurrence belongs to it, even if it contains many finite subjects or effective universes. This does not make the whole one experiencer. Nor do incompatible claims about the **same whole in the same respect** become simultaneously true. Different local outcomes can coexist when their subject, record and context are correctly specified; a genuinely exclusive pair of global propositions cannot both hold in classical logic.

If the principle \(\Diamond\phi\Rightarrow\phi\) is applied to **every global proposition** \(\phi\) and also to its negation, classical modal logic yields \(\phi\Rightarrow\Box\phi\): every true global proposition is necessary. This is a conditional consequence of that formalization, not a derivation of the principle. The formal domain matters; a proposition about one particular observer is not automatically an exhaustive description of the entire whole. The account must not make plenitude trivial by simply declaring unwanted descriptions “not genuinely possible”; it owes an independent criterion of genuine possibility.

In the user's sense, necessary plenitude is globally **deterministic/necessitarian**: the complete actual whole and what occurs in it could not have been otherwise. That differs from the technical claim that a local differential equation plus initial data has a unique solution. Distinct local histories or viewpoints may occur within a fixed whole, and local statistical predictions can be meaningful without positing that a necessitated event was an irreducible objective random choice. The theory still needs to explain why individual occurrences and their weights have the determinate structure they do.

The essence–existence identity is the corresponding existential claim: the whole is not first a mere possibility that later produces or causes itself. Essence would need to be such that its actuality is intelligible from that very constitution. A mathematical stipulation that a model is “necessary” does not establish this identity or select a unique nonempty constitution.

## 6. Probability without simulators or arbitrary branch counting

The useful Bostrom connection is the self-location problem: given that multiple actual subject incidences may be present, what conditional weight should a subject give to being one rather than another? It does not require simulations, simulators, an external computer or Bostrom's ontology.

The prior [incidence-refinement result](ZERO_INCIDENCE_REFINEMENT_AND_BORN_MEASURE.md) shows why the answer cannot be “count the labels in any decomposition.” In the same finite example, different refinements produce 10, 16 or 34 positive mathematical slots and different naive counts while preserving the appropriate coarse physical probabilities. The refinements are not automatically three different actual subject censuses; physical measurements may create different apparatus records, while mere aliases must not multiply an incidence.

There is a conditional constructive route: normalized, positive, coarse-graining-additive, noncontextual weights on **all quantum effects** have the form \(m(E)=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma E)\) in the finite-dimensional case. With certainty of a specified pure physical state, \(\sigma\) is that state. This is a standard effect-algebra result, worked through in [operational probability reconstruction](OPERATIONAL_PROBABILITY_RECONSTRUCTION.md); its premises do not follow from zero and do not themselves identify an effect with a first-person incidence. They also do not mean that the same first-readout effect determines the complete later instrument.

Thus probability connects to plenitude only after the theory determines **what is an occurrence**, which descriptions are redundant, and which measure is appropriate. Equal support does not by itself mean equal weight. A necessary whole with infinitely many occurrences additionally needs a well-defined non-arbitrary measure or a justified alternative; there is no normalized uniform countably additive probability on all integers.

## 7. Time, subjects, and substrate

The chess analogy makes a concrete distinction. While the **player** thinks, the game has no new half-move: its position and half-move count stay fixed. The player is not the piece. Similarly, under the stated surface rule, the surface-restricted position remains A during the traveler's room journey and becomes B upon return; the traveler's actual room position traverses the chord. The surface inhabitant is not the traveler and does not automatically register room activity. There is no need to invent a holding force, a shared clock or an interface just to state the restriction. See [confined succession](ZERO_REALM_RESTRICTION_WITHOUT_INTERACTION.md).

This analogy shows why a local succession can be a restriction of a richer organization. It is not itself a derivation of physical time or of an experiencer. The existing stationary constrained histories, multiple-clock tests, operational instruments and actual record circuits demonstrate ways of deriving conditional changes without an external master time. Their clocks, couplings, preparations and boundary conditions remain specified inputs. No single preferred foliation follows.

Substrate independence is similarly an **organizational invariance claim about actual realizations**, not “any description of a computation is conscious.” An independently specified equivalence should preserve the relevant causal organization, state, boundary and context, while allowing the material realization to differ. It must say which environmental relations matter and distinguish an experiential type from the numerical identity of a subject or a count of occurrences. The [context test](SUBSTRATE_INDEPENDENCE_CONTEXT_AND_SUBJECTS.md) shows that matching a subsystem's local input–output behavior can coexist with different context-sensitive boundary verdicts. Enlarging the description until it explicitly contains the desired subject label would be circular.

## 8. The strongest physical–mathematical connections already obtained

The work has developed more than a slogan, but each result has a definite domain:

- **Sphere and cone:** a projective qubit sphere \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) is also the space of future null rays of two-by-two Hermitian matrices. A supplied internal reference makes a directional-balance equation covariant and normalizes the corresponding quantum effects. This connects geometry and measurement, but the projective/spinor structure and its physical interpretation are inputs, not consequences of a plain zero. See [sphere to Lorentz cone](ZERO_POINT_SPHERE_TO_LORENTZ_CONE.md) and [reference synthesis](ZERO_COVARIANT_REFERENCE_PHYSICS_EXPERIENCE_SYNTHESIS.md).
- **Reference from state:** in the paired-qubit complement, the reference can be obtained from the actual premeasurement reduced state, rather than defined by the chosen ensemble afterward. The paired state and the available instrument remain specified. See [internal reference and confined report](ZERO_INTERNAL_REFERENCE_CONFINED_REPORT_NECESSITY.md).
- **Conservation and real records:** noncommuting sphere effects have been constructed on 190 of 254 complement dimensions as exact logical qubits, or on 229 using a wider, unsharp higher-spin construction. These are different instruments, not identical sharp measurements. Their assigned finite-model lab charges and declared lab energies are conserved, and their statistics pass through the existing clock/work histories. See [noncommuting complement](ZERO_NONCOMMUTING_COMPLEMENT_SPHERE_HISTORY.md).
- **Relational signed record:** the chirality construction explicitly writes opposite signs into a pointer while preserving enlarged orientation charges and old work balance branch by branch. The extra reference pair and control are stipulated, and its sign is not the old component sign or automatically a feeling. See [conserving relational report](ZERO_CONSERVING_RELATIONAL_CHIRALITY_REPORT.md).
- **Limits of the representation:** an exact nontrivial continuous unitary representation of the full noncompact Lorentz group is not available on the finite lab. An infinite-dimensional hyperboloid reference is a mathematical route but needs preparation and a relational physical interpretation; a normalized packet is not an invariant constant state.
- **Independent subject/quality evidence:** earlier subject-rule comparisons, clinical and cognitive audits and imperfect classifier results must remain constraints, not be converted into claims that a graph, neural signal or quantum subsystem is itself a subject. Positive first-person evidence matters; failed report classifiers do not refute experience. The [dossier](CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_BATTERY_DOSSIER.md) and source register preserve those distinctions.

These results are mathematical and model-specific. They do not establish the unique laws of nature, the physical universe's global energy, actual subject boundaries or phenomenal valence. In particular, the reported finite-model sign cancellations use Born weights; their identification with a genuine experiential sum is not established.

## 9. How to use Langlands and other fields productively

The model for synthesis should be **specific correspondences with an explicit preservation claim**, not a loose assertion that every field is secretly the same. The geometric Langlands program has a genuine physics connection: Kapustin and Witten relate it to a twisted four-dimensional supersymmetric gauge theory, electric–magnetic duality and related mathematical structures [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0604151). Their framework uses specific theories and assumptions; it does not derive this project's experiential ontology, our observed universe or global zero energy.

A productive borrowing rule is:

1. identify the two precisely defined mathematical/physical structures;
2. give a map or equivalence and the hypotheses under which it holds;
3. state which dynamics, observables, symmetries, locality and measures it preserves;
4. explain whether it is a redescription, an effective approximation or a relation between distinct realized occurrences; and
5. connect it to a calculable consequence or a concrete obstruction.

This applies equally to category theory, dualities, statistical mechanics, quantum information, relativity, number theory and consciousness science. A valid mathematical possibility has an intelligible **mathematical** status even if it is not thereby an actual experiencer or a physical universe. The synthesis of all mathematics and physics is a long-term objective; the existing links do not prove a universal correspondence.

## 10. A generative and predictive research program

The next step is not another unrelated model. It is to make the one-constitution proposal earn its content by deriving several outputs **together**:

1. **Constitution and uniqueness.** Give a nonempty candidate constitution that specifies its own physical, experiential and incidence structure. Prove a precise existence/uniqueness or plenitude statement up to a justified equivalence; explain why rival admissible structures are included, equivalent or excluded without an external choice. The equilateral and unequal-weight sphere examples show that zero balance by itself does not do this.
2. **Dynamics and whole energy.** Recover a constraint-compatible, nontrivial local dynamics and a justified global energy definition, including gravitational/boundary and interaction contributions. State when it gives exactly zero and derive quantitative predictions that can be compared with established cosmology and particle physics, not merely fitted afterward.
3. **Subjects, qualities and access.** Derive subject boundaries and a qualitative law from realized organization. In particular, determine whether the conserving report's two signs are genuinely different incidences, gauge descriptions or neither, and whether the relevant sign is actually felt. Make testable, preregistered comparisons of competing experiential–physical models using ethically obtained reports and independently justified calibration; a report is evidence, not identical to experience by definition.
4. **Incidence and self-location.** Derive a measure invariant under actual redescription, compatible with physical coarse-graining where justified, and able to handle refinements and infinity. Show under what assumptions Born weights are subject weights, without treating probability as an objective random selector.
5. **Cross-scale and cross-description recovery.** Transport the *same* claims through change of representation, clock, material realization and relevant context. Recover local Lorentz/quantum behavior without a fundamental preferred foliation, and prove any claimed mathematical dualities at their actual scope.

A result should count as decisive progress when it reduces independent inputs **and** keeps the physical and experiential requirements intact, or when it proves that a previously assumed link fails under stated premises and constructs a better alternative. A finite circuit prediction is a test of that circuit, not automatically a new prediction about our universe. No present calculation has justified declaring the no-residue demand impossible; neither has it shown the demand already satisfied.
