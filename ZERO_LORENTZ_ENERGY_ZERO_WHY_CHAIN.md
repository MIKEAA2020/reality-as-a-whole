# Why would the whole's energy be zero? The Lorentz and Poincaré why-chain

## Result

The previous [non-Abelian zero-level analysis](ZERO_NONABELIAN_SYMMETRY_ZERO_LEVEL_WHY_CHAIN.md) found that a full noncommuting rotation algebra can fix the additive normalization of its generators. The next **why** is whether a comparable argument reaches **energy**, rather than stopping at angular momentum.

There is a positive conditional route. In a strictly represented Poincaré algebra, boosts and spatial translations have a commutator proportional to the time-translation generator. The complete algebra is generated by its commutators, so arbitrary scalar shifts of energy and momentum do not preserve its exact relations. If a normalized pure state is invariant as a ray under an **honest representation of the full connected group**, its four translation generators annihilate it. Under the additional identification of the time-translation generator with the relevant global physical energy, this gives a distinguished zero energy.

The same investigation also reveals why the inference is not yet a proof of the user's proposed zero whole:

* The existing point–sphere construction yields a **mathematical Lorentz cone** from supplied projective/spinor structure; it has not produced an actual global Poincaré action or energy generator on the complete echo, clock, reservoir and physical–experiential whole.
* A nontrivial exact unitary representation of the full Lorentz group cannot act on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space. The existing finite record devices and their covariance comparisons cannot simply be declared exact physical boost implementations. An infinite-dimensional carrier, a limited approximate regime, or a different account is needed.
* Lorentz invariance alone permits a constant vacuum stress tensor `T_μν=−ρ η_μν` with **arbitrary ρ**. It does not in itself make local vacuum-energy density vanish or settle the gravitational meaning of a global zero.

These are substantive deductions about stated mathematics, not a claim that the actual universe has or lacks a zero total. They distinguish a promising energy-normalization argument from the stronger, still open claim of a self-sufficient physical–experiential constitution. The exact algebra and controls are in [verification/zero_poincare_energy_why.py](verification/zero_poincare_energy_why.py) and its [tests](verification/zero_poincare_energy_why_test.py).

## 1. Why does extending rotations to boosts matter?

In units `c=ℏ=1`, choose a convention for Hermitian generators in which

```text
[J_i,J_j] =  i ε_ijk J_k,       [J_i,K_j] =  i ε_ijk K_k,
[K_i,K_j] = −i ε_ijk J_k,
[K_i,P_0] =  i P_i,             [K_i,P_j] =  i δ_ij P_0.
```

Here J generates rotations, K boosts, and `P_μ` spacetime translations. In this convention `P_0=H` is the generator of time translations for the specified inertial frame. The translation generators commute with one another; the remaining rotation–translation brackets give their usual vector transformation. Signs and factors change with conventions, but the dependency is the important point: **the time-translation generator is related to the commutator of a boost and a spatial translation**.

Suppose the same infinitesimal actions are described by shifted operators `P'_μ=P_μ+c_μ I`, with the same boosts and a strict, honest algebra. Then

```text
[K_i,P'_j] − i δ_ij P'_0 = −i δ_ij c_0 I,
[K_i,P'_0] − i P'_i      = −i c_i I.
```

All four shifts must vanish if the exact brackets are retained. The rotations and boosts have their own no-shift consequences from their noncommuting brackets. More generally the 3+1-dimensional Poincaré Lie algebra is **perfect**: its rotations and boosts arise from Lorentz commutators, and all four translations arise from brackets of boosts with translations. The earlier moment-map argument therefore applies to its strictly equivariant generators, provided the other hypotheses there are met.

This improves on applying the previous argument to time translations **alone**. The time-translation group is Abelian, so `H→H+cI` changes its unitary only by a global phase and leaves density-matrix evolution unchanged. The extra normalization information arises only when the time translations are embedded in an independently justified, honest boost–translation representation. If the full action is known only on rays, phase changes can instead produce a projective/cocycle description; a particular strict lift is not obtained merely by declaring phases unobservable. Nor is a shift by a non-scalar commutant operator covered by this calculation.

The verifier checks the complete commutator algebra and its ten-dimensional derived span using a faithful **real affine matrix representation**. It checks the offset defects exactly. Those matrices are not Hermitian quantum generators or a physical experiment; they verify the algebra, not its realization in nature.

## 2. Why could that imply zero for a whole?

Assume, for this argument, all of the following:

1. The complete candidate whole is represented by a normalized pure state in a Hilbert space carrying an **honest, strongly continuous** unitary representation of the full connected Poincaré group (or the appropriate connected cover).
2. Its ray is invariant under the entire group, not just under time translations or rotations separately.
3. The generators have the strictly normalized algebra above, and `P_0` is independently identified with the relevant global energy of that whole.

Then the group action on the invariant ray is a continuous one-dimensional unitary character. Its derivative vanishes on the commutator algebra; because that algebra is perfect, the derivative vanishes everywhere. Connectedness makes the character trivial. Thus, on the given vector,

```text
P_0|Ψ⟩ = P_1|Ψ⟩ = P_2|Ψ⟩ = P_3|Ψ⟩ = 0,
J_i|Ψ⟩ = K_i|Ψ⟩ = 0.
```

In particular the **four-momentum** is zero, not merely a zero energy in one chosen frame. The fixed vector lies in each generator's domain; no formal product of unbounded operators is needed for this group-character proof. An invariant state can contain nontrivial relational correlations; zero global four-momentum is not a proof that local operators, conditional records, or experiences vanish.

This is a conditional sufficient route, not a derivation of the premises. A symmetry of the **laws** does not imply the actual state is invariant. Invariance under time translations on rays alone permits a nonzero energy eigenvalue, because the vector can acquire a phase. And an actual physical system may have no normalized invariant vector in the chosen representation. Noncompactness itself does not forbid one: a representation can contain a trivial invariant sector, as in a suitably specified vacuum representation. Its existence and applicability must be established, not inferred from the name of the group.

The scope of `P_0` is essential. A generator of translations in an idealized background, a clock constraint, a boundary energy and the actual universe's total energy are not automatically the same observable. A positive-energy condition or a preferred energy reference cannot be used as an unexplained substitute for the requested derivation.

## 3. How far does the existing point–sphere construction get?

The existing [projective sphere-to-cone result](ZERO_POINT_SPHERE_TO_LORENTZ_CONE.md) starts with a complex two-dimensional spinor space. Its projective line is a sphere of null directions. Hermitian `2×2` matrices form a real four-dimensional vector space, with determinant of Lorentzian signature; `X→AXA†` for `A∈SL(2,C)` supplies the proper connected Lorentz action on that **constructed** cone.

One may mathematically add translations `X→X+a` with `a` Hermitian, producing the corresponding affine semidirect group and Lie algebra. This yields a coherent *representation candidate* relating the sphere, boosts and translations without selecting an external axis. But a projective direction sphere alone does not specify an actual affine spacetime, a choice of physical time translation, a Hamiltonian, or a unitary group action on the original quantum apparatus. The spinor structure, Hermitian lift, affine translations, scale and interpretation as physics must be justified.

The existing [reference-and-echo bridge](ZERO_COVARIANT_REFERENCE_PHYSICS_EXPERIENCE_SYNTHESIS.md) supplies a further concrete result: the directional reference and effects transform consistently under a change of Lorentz representation, and the relative-normalized physical-record tables agree. The new test repeats that comparison on three original clock labels. In that construction the nonunitary spinor boost is accompanied by a transformed reference and normalization; the resulting comparison is **covariance of descriptions and effects**, not a demonstrated active unitary boost of the entire lab, clock or subject. Treating it as the latter would conflate representation transport with a physical symmetry.

## 4. Why can't the existing finite record simply implement the boosts?

Assume a finite-dimensional Hilbert space carries Hermitian rotation and boost generators satisfying the exact Lorentz brackets. Trace cyclicity gives

```text
[K₁,K₂] = −i J₃,        [J₃,K₁] = i K₂,
Tr([K₁,K₂] J₃) = Tr(K₂ [J₃,K₁]),
therefore Tr(J₃²) + Tr(K₂²) = 0.
```

For Hermitian matrices both traces are nonnegative, so `J₃=K₂=0`. Cyclic repetition forces all rotations and boosts to vanish. The boost–translation brackets then force all translations, including H, to vanish as operators. Thus **there is no nontrivial exact finite-dimensional unitary representation of this full connected algebra**. A trivial representation is allowed, but cannot by itself implement the nontrivial spacetime transformations needed for the proposed bridge.

This does not rule out the finite echo or its internal reversible clock as a valid finite model. It rules out a specific overextension: equating its nontrivial finite unitary rotation/readout degrees of freedom with a complete exact finite-dimensional unitary Lorentz representation. The verifier checks that an explicit two-spin rotation generator has positive `Tr(J₃²)=2`, which is incompatible with the required identity for any Hermitian boost. The finite matrices used in §1 avoid the theorem precisely because they are **not** a unitary quantum representation.

Possible constructive routes remain open:

* Supply an appropriate infinite-dimensional unitary carrier and a normalizable invariant state, then couple its boosts and translations to the actual records, clock and constrained pair.
* Work with a finite, explicitly approximate reference and quantify its valid boost range and error, without claiming an exact group representation.
* Use local Lorentz/frame covariance or an emergent spacetime in which the global Poincaré assumptions do not apply, then give a different objective-energy account.

A relative clock can have nontrivial internal correlations even if an appropriate whole constraint vanishes. But the finite echo's cyclic clock shift is **not automatically** global physical time translation. None of these options follows solely from the algebra or the point–sphere picture.

## 5. Why doesn't Lorentz symmetry alone fix vacuum energy?

There is an instructive contrast between the generator argument and **local stress–energy**. In flat Minkowski geometry with `η=diag(−1,1,1,1)`, the formal constant tensor

```text
T_μν = −ρ η_μν
```

is invariant under every Lorentz transformation and is conserved when ρ is constant. Its energy density in the chosen rest-frame convention is `T_00=ρ`. The value of ρ is not fixed by Lorentz invariance: positive, zero and negative formal coefficients all have the same tensor symmetry. The verifier also solves the exact linear invariance conditions for a symmetric rank-two tensor and finds a one-dimensional family proportional to η.

This result goes deeper than an arbitrary reference shift: **the metric itself supplies an invariant tensor**, so a scalar multiple is allowed by the same symmetry. To require `ρ=0`, or to derive a relation between ρ and other gravitational terms, one needs an additional principle, field equation, boundary prescription, or a specific account of the complete vacuum. Symmetry alone is not that principle.

The comparison is not a constructed global cosmology. On infinite flat space, integrating a nonzero constant density can diverge; subtraction, regularization, boundary conditions and the definition of charges matter. In gravity, a vacuum stress contribution and a cosmological-constant term can be related through the field equations, but one cannot simply assert flat Minkowski spacetime with arbitrary uncancelled ρ is an Einstein solution. Nor can one infer that a normal-ordered global Poincaré generator is automatically the gravitational energy of a closed universe. The local tensor control and the invariant-ray theorem address **different quantities with different premises**; neither contradicts the other.

## 6. Where does the earlier gravity constraint fit?

The [closed FLRW analysis](ZERO_TOTALITY_FLRW_CONSTRAINT_AND_ENERGY.md) provides a different positive route. Its lapse equation imposes a vanishing Hamiltonian constraint while positive scalar-field energy density and relational evolution coexist. It does not require a global Poincaré-invariant state. Conversely, its compact, generally covariant cosmology is not the same setting as the global Minkowski generators above; the vanishing reparametrization constraint is not automatically a Poincaré energy or an invariant boundary charge.

The two routes may ultimately be aspects of one constitution, but this has to be demonstrated through a recovery map: what the fundamental quantity is, how local time/energy and spacetime emerge, which boundary conditions apply, and how the constrained and asymptotic descriptions agree where both are valid. A local Lorentz frame at each point is not automatically one global Poincaré symmetry of a curved universe.

Neither route supplies the physical–experiential identity. A positive first-person contrast is real evidence of local experience; a constraint, zero eigenvalue or symmetric tensor is not by itself a subject or a feeling. Distinct subjects and suffering remain real where present, not canceled as lived events by a whole-level equality.

## 7. Continue the why-chain to its present meta-level

1. **Why can a time-energy reference be distinguished?** Boost–translation commutators can tie it to a perfect strict algebra, unlike an isolated Abelian time-translation phase.
2. **Why would the whole have zero four-momentum?** A normalized pure ray invariant under an honest action of the full connected group gives zero generators, provided they are the physical global four-momentum.
3. **Why should that action exist on this whole?** A Lorentz cone and reference-covariance calculation do not establish it; a nontrivial exact finite-dimensional unitary lift is impossible, and a curved or closed universe need not possess a global Poincaré group.
4. **Why would that zero cover physical energy rather than only a convention or one generator?** The correspondence to stress, gravitational boundaries and clock constraints must be derived. Lorentz-invariant local vacuum stress can retain an arbitrary scalar coefficient.
5. **Why would the same nature be experiential and self-sufficient?** The physical representation, its state and its mathematical zero do not themselves constitute a local subject or an explanatory ground. First-person evidence constrains the desired identity but does not by itself determine the global generator or its measure.

The remaining meta-level question is not simply “can we add another boost model?” It is **what independently necessitates the relevant complete physical–experiential symmetry and the identification of its charge with the proposed whole-level quantity**, including the gravity and boundary case. A successful zero constitution could answer that question; the current conditional argument does not, and the failure to derive it here does not prove it impossible.
