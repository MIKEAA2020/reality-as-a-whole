# Plan: empirical bacterial chemotaxis and organization

This is a retrospective, exploratory source audit. Before this plan, search snippets and abstracts had already indicated an integral-feedback account, a 1999 robustness result, and a 2014 limitation at high attractant concentrations. The choice of sources and questions is therefore not blind or preregistered.

1. Read the available primary experimental paper on adaptation precision and limits, including its methods and figure captions; distinguish measured pathway readouts, organism-level behavior and model simulations. Record exactly what sources, images, supplementary data and underlying datasets were or were not inspected. Also inspect the original robustness paper to the extent accessible and a primary control-theory analysis or primary molecular model; preserve access limitations.
2. Reconstruct the receptor/CheR/CheB feedback and specific interventions with their controls and interpretation. Separate experimentally observed response from mechanistic models, proposed saturation mechanism and mathematical idealization; distinguish robust precision from changed baseline, kinetics, range or sensitivity.
3. Derive a minimal explicitly stipulated integral-feedback example and show its sufficient conditions, stability or convergence assumptions, and a constrained failure case. This is an illustration of an organizational explanation, not a fit to experimental data, an original detailed model, or a proof that the actual network meets exact assumptions.
4. Compare physicalist organizational realism and hylomorphism fairly: what the mechanistic and systems descriptions explain, whether each predicts anything distinct, and what would be required to settle a further ontological claim. Do not infer consciousness, phenomenal unity or the ontology of all reality from microbial adaptation.
5. Write a linked audit with source access and qualification, run any focused tests and selected regressions, refresh inventories, and commit/push only the active branch.
