# Self-explanatory actuality: what self-support, coherence, and necessity establish

## Direct result

Self-support and global consistency are not, by themselves, explanations of concrete occurrence. A necessary foundation would necessitate the particular actuality only if the connection to that actuality were itself necessary and sufficiently determining. That conditional result is rigorous; its premises have not been established for the proposed whole-account.

This audit sharpens the target of an intrinsically concrete constitution. It does not claim to have supplied one, nor to have shown that none can exist.

## 1. Test of self-support

[The complete-intelligibility comparison audit](COMPLETE_INTELLIGIBILITY_ARGUMENT_AUDIT.md) examines a full supplied two-stream deduction. Its contraposition argument does not establish reversed grounding; its being/determinacy identity and nondiscriminating-autonomy criterion remain unproved. Rejecting those shortcuts does not establish that all intrinsically determining explanation is impossible.

Consider the abstract Boolean condition x = x. Both x = 0 and x = 1 satisfy it. If 1 is interpreted as occurrence and 0 as nonoccurrence, self-consistency alone does not select occurrence.

By contrast, x = 1 has a unique solution, but the positive condition is now explicitly supplied. This does not establish why it applies to concrete reality. The contradictory Boolean condition x = 1 - x has no solution; excluding inconsistency does not select a concrete instantiation of some other consistent condition.

These elementary examples are not a physical simulation, a model of consciousness, or a claim that reality is Boolean. They identify an invalid inference: “can sustain itself if present” does not entail “must be present.”

Likewise, a rule that every event has a supporting event can be satisfied vacuously when there are no events unless a nonemptiness premise is added. A self-supporting cycle, where allowed, need not provide that premise. Dependence here is not being identified with an ordinary strict temporal-before relation.

The result does not refute every global or circular explanation. Independent, justified global constraints may explain more than a bare self-loop. The actual constraints and their concrete applicability must be supplied.

## 2. Test of global uniqueness

Suppose a formal specification has exactly one model up to isomorphism. This establishes a uniqueness result within the specification. It does not by itself establish:

- that the specification describes the actual world;
- that its model is concretely instantiated;
- that its axioms are necessary rather than stipulated;
- that its interpretation includes actual qualitative perspectives;
- that its normative properties are intrinsic rather than assigned.

Uniqueness could play a role in a successful constitutive theory, but it is not an independent actualization mechanism. This is the same limitation identified by the [earlier actuality diagnostic](research/rigorous_closures/REPORT.md) and the [axiarchic bridge audit](AXIARCHIC_BRIDGE_AUDIT.md), now applied to global closure rather than a unique value optimum.

## 3. A conditional necessity result

Let G state the proposed foundation and P the particular actuality to be explained. Write Box for metaphysical necessity.

Assume:

    Box G
    Box (G -> P).

Then:

    Box P.

Proof: at every accessible possibility, G holds and G implies P. Therefore P holds at every such possibility. This is the familiar distribution principle of normal modal logic, not a new physical or metaphysical discovery.

The philosophical burden lies in the premises. A necessary ground alone does not determine a contingent effect. If the connection leaves alternatives unsettled, Box(G -> P) has not been supplied for the particular outcome.

Conversely, if the premise P already contains “this complete concrete actuality exists,” defining G to contain P does not establish Box G. The desired necessity cannot be inserted into a name and then counted as derived.

## 4. What no-brute-facts does and does not imply

The user's objective requires a fully intelligible foundation and no unexplained residue. It motivates seeking a ground that does not itself remain an arbitrary contingency.

However, the statement “every fact is explained” does not by its logical form alone supply either modal premise above. The nature of explanation matters. A probabilistic explanation can constrain a distribution without selecting one outcome; an infinite or circular chain needs an account of why the entire arrangement obtains; an alleged self-explanation needs more than P because P.

A strong contrastive demand that the complete reason settle why this rather than another actuality obtains makes a determining constitution particularly attractive. It is an additional substantive interpretation of explanatory completeness, not a theorem obtained simply by writing the words sufficient reason.

This does not downgrade the objective. It identifies the kind of explanation needed to satisfy its most demanding interpretation.

## 5. The empty-reality argument must also be handled correctly

The statement that nothing concrete exists does not itself describe a concrete entity called Nothing. Denying such an entity therefore does not establish that concrete occurrence is necessary.

In a language with an abstract domain and a predicate Concrete, the sentence “no object is Concrete” is formally consistent unless other premises exclude it. This is not proof that an empty concrete reality is metaphysically possible; it shows that the mere grammar of negation does not refute it.

Similarly, representing a condition requires our actual representational activity. It does not follow that the condition represented contains that activity. The occurrence of this inquiry establishes occurrence here, not the impossibility of all nonoccurrence alternatives.

This is parallel to the privation distinction: absence need not be another substance, yet the absence claim can remain meaningful.

## 6. The positive requirement for a constitutive candidate

A substantive candidate must explain at least four linked features:

1. **Concrete occurrence:** why its nature is instantiated rather than merely described.
2. **Determination:** why its constitution yields the actual organization rather than unexplained alternatives.
3. **Phenomenal incidence:** why particular organized processes are actual qualitative perspectives.
4. **Axiological structure:** why their fulfillment has the claimed significance and normative relations.

A theory may make progress on some without solving all. The candidate must not rename the whole list “C” and treat that symbol as the sought constitution.

The strongest current architecture remains occurrence-first, non-additive common constitution. It starts from actual occurrence as a datum and seeks its necessary character. Starting there is an epistemic method, not a license to leave occurrence brute.

Nothing in this audit selects a cosmic subject, a particular physical formalism, or a specific material substrate. Those require their own constitutive arguments.

## 7. Relation to Good

If a justified necessary G explains P and value is an intrinsic feature of G, existence and value can have a common necessary foundation.

That still leaves two possibilities:

- value is indispensable to why G explains P, supporting strict axiarchism;
- G explains both P and value, while value is not the actualizing ground.

The goodness-of-being and privation proposals are compatible with the second. They do not automatically settle the first.

A candidate can therefore retain intrinsic value and privative wrongness without pretending that value has already been shown to produce existence. Conversely, strict axiarchism remains possible if its stronger dependence can be justified.

## 8. Comparative verdict

- **Bare self-support:** insufficient; it need not select occurrence.
- **Unique formal coherence:** insufficient by itself; formal uniqueness is not concreteness.
- **Necessary concrete ground with a necessary determining connection:** sufficient conditionally for necessary actuality, but the ground and connection have not been established.
- **Intrinsic Good:** a live constitutive extension, not an actualization proof merely by its attribution.

This narrows the research direction without claiming a completed solution. The relevant next discovery would be an independently justified nature with a necessary determining relation to concrete occurrence, not another general claim that the whole is coherent.

No empirical finding, executable model, new research test count, or calibrated probability is claimed.
