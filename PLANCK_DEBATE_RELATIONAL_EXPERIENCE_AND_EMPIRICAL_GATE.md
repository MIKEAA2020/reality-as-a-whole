# Planck, consciousness and the relational whole

## Source and scope

## What the debate contributes

- **Churchland:** explaining consciousness demands detailed neuroscience and targeted interventions rather than presuming that two mysteries explain each other. The approach has real empirical force: neurochemical interventions change perception, memory, responsiveness and reported experience. But demonstrating dependence on brain organization does not by itself show that experience is ontologically nonfundamental, or that a complete third-person dynamics explains why it is experienced. Comparing experience to life illuminates the research strategy; it does not settle whether the explanatory targets are identical.
- **Gefter:** the subject/object division and situated, embodied observation are worth taking seriously; formal physics need not offer every property as a pre-existing value independent of experimental context. But Bell/contextuality arguments exclude classes of models under specified assumptions, not all observer-independent descriptions. Quantum theory does not establish that reality is created by a human mind, nor that experiential relational ontology is uniquely correct. Embodied activity may explain access and action without automatically resolving felt character.
- **Greene:** familiar quantum measurements are physical interactions; invoking a conscious observer is not necessary to perform standard experimental calculations, and an ancient physical universe need not wait for humans. Decoherence helps explain suppression of interference in suitable reduced descriptions and stability of records; **by itself it does not settle every interpretation's single-outcome problem**. Consequently the quantum measurement problem is neither automatically consciousness-caused nor automatically solved just by saying “interaction.”
- **Mersini-Houghton:** asking why there is a felt side is legitimate, and the distinction between correlating processes and explaining experience matters. However, an objection that nonlinear neural firing cannot arise from linear quantum dynamics is not established: effective descriptions of interacting, open systems and their coarse-grained variables can be nonlinear even when the underlying quantum state evolves linearly. This is compatibility, not a completed quantum derivation of a brain or an explanation of experience. The participants' acceptance of brain dependence in humans does not establish brain necessity for every possible experience, or computational substrate-independence.

## The research constraint for our framework

The best synthesis is neither “quantum mechanics proves Planck” nor “neuroscience disproves an experiential ground.” Maintain three distinct claims:

1. **Ground:** reality has an intrinsically experiential nature. This is a metaphysical proposal, not yet derived from non-nothingness, structural coherence or quantum mechanics.
2. **Subject formation:** a particular relational organization constitutes a bounded subject, with a particular content and valence. This requires an explicit constitution rule and rival tests; it is not supplied by the word “relations,” generic entanglement, information, or a spatially localized quantum operation.
3. **Observable interface:** experimentally accessible brain/body/environment dynamics predict and control reports and behaviors. They constrain candidate constitution rules, but observation alone does not automatically fix phenomenal interpretation.

These are compatible with a timeless, self-sufficient relational whole and many local perspectives without a universal subject or external creator. Strong possibility=actuality is a proposed identity within the whole, not a temporal production process; it does not itself establish which organizations are possible, which are subjects, their qualitative character, or their weights. Neither this debate nor the following experiment proves the whole self-sufficient or derives physical law.

## A sharper empirical gate: organization, report and experience

A useful operational distinction is between **responsiveness** (can a subject respond to outside prompts?), **reported experience** (what is remembered and reported when communication becomes possible?) and **experience itself**. These do not coincide by definition. Any candidate which treats no external response, or absence of later recall, as decisive proof of absent experience fails this gate.

[Bajwa et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-12695-z), main text sections inspected including methods and discussion, measured EEG and TMS-EEG in 20 healthy participants under deep propofol sedation and used intermittent awakenings. Across 52 attempted awakenings, 24 generated reports suggesting experience, five reports suggesting no experience, and 23 were uninformative or unarousable. The authors found no statistically significant differentiation of the two tested complexity measures between the reported-experience categories within sedation; the categories were sparse and measurement/report timing uncertain, so this is not evidence that all complexity measures are useless, proof of equal distributions, or a perfect phenomenal label. The paper's supplementary files and raw recordings were not inspected or reproduced here.

This constrains a tempting version of our mismatch proposal: a single physiological mismatch or integration score cannot simply be renamed subjective unpleasantness or presence of experience. A better proposed discriminating design would compare, within persons and at matched sedation level, (a) neural organization, (b) responsiveness, (c) immediately elicited experience reports and separately (d) reported valence where available. Predeclare a neural mismatch measure, an intervention that changes it without merely changing sedation or report capability, competing predictors, missingness and timing uncertainty. Evidence of a reliable intervention-sensitive relationship with reported valence beyond those controls would be informative about the **candidate**, not an automatic proof of its constitutive or universal truth. Absence of significant difference would not establish equivalence.

The authors link [OpenNeuro ds005620 v1.0.0](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds005620/versions/1.0.0) and [analysis scripts](https://github.com/SugarLab/Anesthesia_PCI_Cont/tree/c3181cffdad638b88c0be249e6fbc692c2b8a01c). We inspected the dataset repository README and root listing at [commit a7ee507](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds005620/tree/a7ee50741b5625ee54478be73201ecfd4fd904dd) and the analysis-code file listing, not the raw recordings, experience-report files, full code or supplement. The dataset README lists awake, sedation, TMS and one-minute pre-awakening recordings. Whether usable individual valence labels and exact matching metadata are available remains unverified. A separate NEMAR listing reports a 77.3 GB dataset; no bulk download was attempted. The next responsible step would be bounded metadata/label inspection, not claiming a new dataset analysis or restarting the unrelated Roy work.

## Identity across anesthesia

Continuity of a person's identity after anesthesia is consistent with preserved biological organization, retained memories and causal continuity; neither moment-to-moment responsiveness nor uninterrupted recall is necessary to define personal identity. This observation does not prove that no experience occurred during the interval, or that psychological and biological continuity answer every philosophical question of subject persistence. These are separate hypotheses and separately testable aspects.

## What remains open

The debate strengthens the need to link local physical mechanisms with reported phenomenology while retaining a serious explanatory question about why experience exists. It does not establish the experiential ground, a unique subject boundary, a universal consciousness, an objective experience meter, an empirical solution to suffering, an emergent spacetime derivation, or a necessity proof for plenitude. Elegance is a useful selection criterion only after empirical adequacy, clear assumptions and the unresolved alternatives are made explicit.
