# The necessary-existence argument: strongest form, with what is and isn't proved

The physical chain—timeless WdW, internal R-time, self-adjoint Tomonaga flow,
exact Magnus propagator, conformal near-horizon, interior Kantowski–Sachs,
polymer bounce, information preservation, thermal/modular time, finite
perspectives, unitary Page curve, BKL stability—gives a consistent closed model.
But a zero-residue account must answer: why is there anything at all? This note
states the strongest necessity argument available, and marks exactly where it
falls short of proof.

## 1. What is undeniable

There is experience. This is Descartes: doubting is an experience; the datum
cannot be coherently denied. Any argument that concludes "there is nothing" is
self-refuting, because the argument itself (and the reader considering it) is
something. This is the one non-negotiable premise.

## 2. What experience requires

Experience requires:
- **Something to experience** (content, structure).
- **A subject/perspective** (conditional, finite—we do not experience the whole
  universe at once; see [PERSPECTIVAL_FINITUDE.md](PERSPECTIVAL_FINITUDE.md)).
- **Change/flow** (the modular automorphism group of the thermal state;
  [THERMAL_TIME.md](THERMAL_TIME.md)).

Mathematically this is exactly: an algebra of observables \(\mathcal A\), a
faithful state \(\omega\), and its modular automorphism group \(\alpha^\omega_t\)
(Tomita flow = thermal time). That is the minimum structure required by the
datum of experience. To deny that there is *any* such structure is to deny
experience itself—which is impossible.

## 3. Why there cannot be nothing

"Nothing" would mean no algebra, no state, no modular flow. But we have already
established that some experience exists (premise 1); therefore the minimal
structure \((\mathcal A,\omega,\alpha^\omega_t)\) cannot be absent. Nothingness
is incompatible with the given.

## 4. Uniqueness (why this structure and not another)

This is the genuinely hard step. Two claims:

**Weak claim (established):** the spherical WdW + polymer + thermal-time +
perspectival structure *realizes* the minimal datum. It is a consistent model
in which a timeless absolute (the global pure state on \(\mathcal A\)) gives
rise to finite experiencing perspectives via modular flow, without external
clocks, substances, or information loss.

**Strong claim (conjectured, not proved):** the algebra \(\mathcal A\) is forced
to be the unique hyperfinite Type III₁ factor (the algebra of local observables
in any QFT with infinitely many degrees of freedom in the thermodynamic limit;
Connes–Haagerup uniqueness), and the de Finetti / thermal-KMS structure picks
out equilibrium states that give consistent modular flows for all
subsystems. The specific matter content (scalar mass \(\mu\), Standard Model
forces, spacetime dimension) would then be forced by internal consistency of
the algebra representation. But **this is not proved here**; it is a research
programme. Our spherical model is one concrete symmetry-reduction of that
(conjectured) unique structure, not a derivation of the Standard Model.

## 5. What the argument does and does not establish

Establishes:
- There cannot be literally nothing (experience is given).
- There must be at least an algebra-state-flow triple \((\mathcal A,\omega,\alpha)\).
- Our model instantiates such a triple consistently, with closed physics and
  perspectival finitude.

Does not establish (remaining residues):
- Why this particular matter content/symmetry group/dimension rather than
  another consistent QFT (the landscape problem).
- Why the specific modular time experienced by humans is the one we inhabit
  rather than some other thermal state on the same algebra (though CRN
  cocycles relate all such states, suggesting a single absolute with many
  perspectives).
- A rigorous mathematical proof that experience *must* accompany any
  \((\mathcal A,\omega,\alpha)\) triple (this is the Russellian identification,
  still a philosophical thesis rather than a theorem—though it is the only
  identification that does not add extra substance).

## 6. Status relative to the zero-residue goal

The argument above is **the strongest the present author can give without
dishonesty**. It closes the "why anything at all" question against the
self-refuting nothing-hypothesis, and it shows our physical model is a
concrete realization. It does not by itself prove uniqueness of the specific
content (dimension, field content, symmetries). The [plenitude identity](PLENITUDE.md) dissolves that residue: possibility ≡
actuality by identity in the universal representation π_u(𝒜)''; every
consistent net/folium is ipso facto an actual sector of the one absolute
(no external selector, no extra actuality predicate), and stability selection
effects (Ehrenfest/spinor/polarization/Huygens) single out d=4 and a scalar
sector for finite observers. The full Standard Model level derivation of
gauge groups/fermion content remains an open theorem, not a hidden brute
fact.
