# Collective statistics, parameter gauge, and the limits of a relational inference

## The larger-network result and its scope

Tkačik and colleagues, [*Searching for Collective Behavior in a Large Network of Sensory Neurons*](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003408), study retinal population statistics, not phenomenal subjects. This reading covered the main article's accessible HTML prose and figure captions (chunks 0–24), including methods and supplementary captions. Equations are images and have **not** been systematically inspected; main or supplementary figure images, source data, and original code have not been audited or replayed. The paper is publicly available, not inaccessible.

The authors report a different recording from our 40-cell audit: 160 selected salamander retinal cells, a repeated natural movie, and models on subgroups up to 120. The methods describe 277 training and 20 randomly held-out repeats; a separate overfitting illustration describes a 90/10 split. These assess the same repeated movie, not generalization to arbitrary unseen movies. A separate retina and checkerboard stimulus appear in supplementary figure S7's caption; its image/data and methods are not independently checked here. Their comparisons suggest that at larger population sizes the ordinary pairwise model misses aspects of the distribution of total spikes, while adding the distribution of the total `K` improves several measured comparisons, including identity-specific triplet statistics. The `K` distribution is fitted, so reproducing it on the constraints used for fitting is not an independent prediction; independent triplet and other checks do matter. These are reported empirical results, not results of our data analysis.

They explicitly warn that a static MaxEnt distribution does not specify neural dynamics, that effective couplings are not necessarily microscopic interactions, and that minima depend on the model. Their descent procedure also has an ordering-dependent basin assignment; a Monte Carlo path through an energy landscape is not by itself an observed causal trajectory. Matching marginal statistics need not identify the complete joint distribution or uniquely establish a subject. The authors themselves call the K-pairwise model an intermediate, not an ultimate model and discuss possible additional constraints. Their global-activity constraint, statistical checks, and gauge issue are established work, not project discoveries.

## Exact finite diagnostic: even K-pairwise information can leave alternatives

[`verification/k_pairwise_constraint_and_gauge_gate.py`](verification/k_pairwise_constraint_and_gauge_gate.py) and its [receipt](verification/k_pairwise_constraint_and_gauge_gate.json) enumerate four binary variables. Two strictly positive joint distributions have **exactly identical** all singleton means, all pairwise coincidences, and the entire distribution of `K` (the number of ones). Nevertheless:

- the probability that variables 0, 2 and 3 are all one is `5/32` in one distribution and `3/32` in the other;
- their total-variation distance is `1/4`;
- each has entropy approximately `3.90564` bits. The uniform distribution satisfies those same constraints and is their unique maximum-entropy choice, at `4` bits.

Thus fitting those constraints selects a principled distribution under the maximum-entropy criterion, not a logical proof that the selected distribution is the actual joint law. This example is a simple null-space construction adapting standard marginal-underdetermination reasoning; it is not claimed as a novel general theorem or evidence that the published retinal fit is wrong. It uses a special uniform baseline, not retinal parameters; it provides no general error bound, no sample-size conclusion, and no measurement of subjects. In the paper, improvement on non-fitted statistics is empirical evidence beyond the fitted constraints, even though it cannot establish every rare pattern probability.

## Parameter gauge is more than a numerical nuisance

For binary `x`, write an unnormalized log weight as `Σ hᵢxᵢ + Σ Jᵢⱼxᵢxⱼ + V(K)`. The gate checks the published K-pairwise gauge freedom exactly: shifting every field by `a` and every pair parameter by `b`, while replacing `V(K)` with `V(K)−aK−bK(K−1)/2`, leaves **every log weight exactly unchanged**. Distinct parameter values therefore represent the same probability distribution. Tkačik et al. explicitly discuss this degeneracy and choose a gauge for comparing parameters; we are illustrating, not discovering, it. The term `V` and the apparent strength of an individual pair coefficient cannot be independently interpreted across gauges.

### Implication for the whole/perspective proposal

A candidate subject boundary, experiential quality, or other purported intrinsic fact should not change merely because the same statistical model has been rewritten in another gauge. That is a **necessary representation-invariance test**, not a sufficient criterion of reality, consciousness, or substrate independence. Even a gauge-invariant joint distribution leaves dynamical and causal models underdetermined; conversely a real mechanism may require richer, intervention-sensitive or temporal information than any static distribution. Substrate independence is still a proposal: one must say which transformations preserve the relevant causal organization, which experiential facts are claimed preserved, and how to test alternatives. Nor does an aggregate `K` term imply a universal subject, an agent, or that all local subjects fuse.

A disciplined next-step sequence is: (1) specify the equivalence relation and observable domain of each effective description; (2) state candidate perspective/experience mappings on equivalence classes rather than raw fitted coordinates; (3) test whether rival temporal or intervention models that share those statistics change the predicted mapping; (4) specify independently assessable experiential consequences and negative controls before claiming identification. These are requirements for future theory construction, not evidence that an experiential bridge, necessary plenitude, or self-subsistence has been derived. There is no solved problem of time or quantum gravity here.
