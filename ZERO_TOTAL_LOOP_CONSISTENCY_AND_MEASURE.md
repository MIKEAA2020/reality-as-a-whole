# Zero-total internal relations: a three-link consistency test

## Result

Extending the previous [one-link connection](ZERO_TOTAL_INTERNAL_CONNECTION_AND_MEASURE.md) to three linked neutral configurations reveals a genuine **internal loop relation**. Individual links can be relabelled independently without changing the observable relation; two links can be set to identity, but the third can retain gauge-invariant loop information.

The key comparison is exact: two fully supported measures can agree on **every single-link configuration distribution**, and on every pair of selected flip-event bits, yet disagree on a three-link event. For the specific measures below, three flips have probabilities `1/216` and `1/432`. A stricter flat-loop condition forbids them altogether. This is a concrete test of how a global consistency principle might constrain internal relations, not a derivation that the whole must be flat or that either measure describes reality.

The construction is finite and uses standard discrete holonomy and gauge ideas. Its charge sector, links, allowed configurations, measures and record interpretation are mathematical assumptions; no physical spacetime, energy, experience, subject or self-subsistence is derived. It is offered as a constructive step within the user's zero-totality programme, not a competing ultimate account. ## 1. Three internal configurations, three links

Reuse the six-state neutral sector `S`: the permutations of `(1,-1,0)`, each having zero signed sum. Let `G=S_3 x C_2` be the 12-element relabelling/sign group. Take three formal configurations `q_0,q_1,q_2 in S` and three group-valued links

```text
h_01: 1 -> 0,      h_12: 2 -> 1,      h_02: 2 -> 0.
```

Independent frame changes `g_0,g_1,g_2 in G` act by

```text
q_i -> g_i q_i,       h_ij -> g_i h_ij g_j^(-1).
```

The three selected flip bits are

```text
F_ij = 1  iff  h_ij q_j = -q_i,   else 0,    for ij=01,12,02.
```

Each is invariant under independent frame changes. The indices and directions supply internal relational bookkeeping; they do not prove the existence of physical time or require a clock outside the candidate whole. The three links are specified, not derived from zero.

## 2. The loop relation and what cannot be gauged away

Define the loop element in frame 0 by

```text
C = h_01 h_12 h_02^(-1).
```

It transforms as `C -> g_0 C g_0^(-1)`. Thus whether `C` is the identity, and more generally its conjugacy class, is frame-independent. Choosing `g_0=e`, `g_1=h_01` and `g_2=h_01 h_12` sets the first two links to identity; the transformed third link is `C^(-1)`. In contrast to a single link or a tree, a loop can retain gauge-invariant information. This is formal finite-group holonomy, not evidence that spacetime curvature has been derived.

**Flatness** means `C=e`, equivalently `h_02=h_01 h_12`: direct and composed transport agree. If `F_01=F_12=1`, then

```text
h_01 q_1 = -q_0,
h_12 q_2 = -q_1,
h_01 h_12 q_2 = q_0.
```

Under flatness, the last line gives `F_02=0` because `q_0` is nonzero. Hence the pattern `(F_01,F_12,F_02)=(1,1,1)` is impossible in this flat finite sector. Without flatness it is possible: with `h_01=h_12=e`, `h_02=-e`, `q_1=-q_0`, and `q_2=q_0`, all three flip. Here `-e` denotes the central global sign reversal in G.

Flatness is a **specified extra constraint**, not a consequence of zero total or of gauge invariance. The question whether an ultimate internal-consistency principle forces it—or instead permits nontrivial loops—remains a real task.

### Local comparisons can miss an internal loop

Let `q=(-1,0,1)` and let `u` swap the first and third vertices and reverse all signs. Then `u q=q`, but `u != e` and `u²=e`. Compare

```text
A = (q,q,q; e,e,e),
B = (q,q,q; e,e,u).
```

Every edge compares its endpoints as a **stay** in both A and B, so the three flip bits are all zero. Nevertheless `C_A=e` and `C_B=u`. The invariant `W=1` if `C=e` (else 0) distinguishes them; no frame change can turn a nonidentity loop into identity. The loop is invisible to this limited set of endpoint comparisons because u stabilizes q. A different observable or richer matter could reveal more, but no universal measurement claim follows.

## 3. Same local data, different global correlation

Compare the following finite mathematical measures; they are not claims that the whole is generated by an external random process:

1. **Haar product `mu_H`:** each `q_i` is independently uniform on S and each `h_ij` independently uniform on G.
2. **Flat conditional `mu_flat`:** each `q_i` is independently uniform on S, `h_01,h_12` are independently uniform on G, and `h_02=h_01 h_12`.
3. **Full-support comparison `mu_half=(mu_H+mu_flat)/2`.** This is a single specified measure on the *same* 373,248 labelled configurations as `mu_H`; it is positive on every one because `mu_H` is. The mixture notation is not a physical lottery creating one of two universes.

All three are invariant under independent frame transformations. Each single-edge triple `(q_i,q_j,h_ij)` is exactly uniform on its `6 x 6 x 12 = 432` possibilities. In the flat measure this follows because multiplication by a fixed group element is a bijection: any pair of links is jointly uniform. The verifier checks the complete single-edge marginals, not only the flip bits.

Every individual flip bit has probability `1/6`, and every pair is independent with joint probability `1/36` for two flips, under all three measures. Nevertheless:

| Measure | `Pr(F_01=F_12=F_02=1)` | `Pr(C=e)` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Haar product | `1/216` | `1/12` |
| Half-flat full-support measure | `1/432` | `13/24` |
| Flat conditional | `0` | `1` |

For `mu_H`, independent uniform links each yield a flip with probability `1/6` conditional on any fixed endpoints, giving `(1/6)^3`. For `mu_flat`, the algebra in §2 forbids three flips. The half-flat values follow by linearity. The total-variation distance between the two full-support **three-bit** distributions is `1/108`; between Haar and flat it is `1/54`. These are exact model probabilities, not measurements in nature. Full pattern distributions are recorded in the receipt.

The comparison separates three claims: local event data may agree; global consistency can change higher-order correlations; and **full support does not fix weights**. It does not establish that all formal configurations are genuinely possible or actual. If one adopts the user's possibility–actuality thesis, a theory must independently specify its genuine domain and measure; neither mathematical support nor the mixture establishes those facts.

## 4. Implications for the proposed self-sufficient account

This construction makes the next grounding problem more precise. A global relation can be internal, gauge-invariant and empirically consequential in a declared model, rather than a preferred external observer. But neither `Q=0` nor individual link symmetry derives flatness, a loop law, or the product/Haar measure. Independent uniform links actually have `Pr(C=e)=1/12`, not certainty. A fundamental principle may favor flatness, nontrivial holonomy or another measure; this example does not rule out such a derivation.

To connect the result to the user's strongest goal, the remaining work is to show why the neutral variables, local comparison and admissible loop relations are internally necessary or self-subsistent, then recover appropriate physical constraints and a justified measure without positing an external selector. The same account must establish actual experiential occurrences and their specificity: three bits, holonomy and mathematical entropy do not constitute subjects or explain suffering. A richer physical model must also distinguish this finite group loop from actual gravitational transport, demonstrate the appropriate coupling and account for backreaction.

The result is not a proof that the proposed zero-totality cannot meet the zero-residue demand. It is a specific higher-order consistency target that a successful derivation can meet or revise.
