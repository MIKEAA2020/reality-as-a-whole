# Best working synthesis under the seven criteria

> **Historical synthesis:** Its universal-subject target and literal-simulation discussion predate later corrections. Use the [current consolidated dossier](CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_BATTERY_DOSSIER.md) for the plural-subject, nonliteral actuality framework and current evidence status.

## Verdict

The strongest **provisional direction under the user's stated endpoint and the seven criteria** is a **non-optimal, experiential whole-first monism with an independently tested relational-physics and finite-perspective programme**. It combines:

1. one self-sufficient, timeless absolute whose intrinsic nature is experience and which is one universal consciousness;
2. real finite perspectives and physical processes as internally constituted, not independent ultimate substances or mere words;
3. non-optimality of our universe, and non-perfection of the whole conditional on the user's adopted Leibnizian implication;
4. empirically disciplined models of local physics and consciousness, treated as rivals to test rather than already-selected implementations.

This is a **working hypothesis and research architecture**, not a demonstrated synthesis satisfying all seven criteria. In particular, there is no derived self-explanatory concrete constitution, constitutive law of finite experience, emergence of spacetime, or new decisive physics prediction yet. “Best” means a defensible research preference under the user's commitments, not an exhaustive mathematical ranking, consensus conclusion, or measured probability that the view is true. The prior closure claims remain withdrawn.

## 1. The seven criteria, without renumbering

| # | Criterion | Non-empty demand | Present status of the preferred synthesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Experience is a valid datum | Phenomenal occurrence matters as evidence, even when judgments about it can be mistaken. | Starting datum; universal subject not established by it. |
| **C2** | Our universe is not best possible | Accept a genuinely better possible universe as the working premise; do not explain away harm. With the adopted `perfect whole → best universe` bridge, the whole is not perfect in that sense. | Adopted philosophical premises and valid conditional inference; no empirical proof of the bridge or value ordering. |
| **C3** | Full intelligibility without brute residue | **C3S:** intelligible structural, modal, physical and existential facts. **C3E:** experience is intelligible in its own experiential mode, not forced into a purely verbal equivalent. Neither tier exempts identities or scope from explanation. | Target remains unmet: actualization, structure, subject incidence and their identities are unresolved. |
| **C4** | Generative and predictive power | Generate risky, discriminating consequences, or at least a progressive programme with identifiable predictions and failure conditions. Metaphysical terminology alone is not a prediction. | Candidate empirical contrasts exist, but the whole-first metaphysics has not yet yielded a confirmed distinctive prediction. |
| **C5** | Alignment with promising physics | Engage with quantum and gravitational theory, relational and information-based approaches, and possible emergent spacetime while preserving tested physics. A research trend is not established fact. | Compatible as a direction only; no derivation of quantum/GR dynamics or emergent geometry. |
| **C6** | Alignment with plausible consciousness science | Explain real experiences while seriously testing competing neural theories, attention, causal organization and report limitations. | Existing study audits constrain proposed mechanisms; no validated universal consciousness or unique local constitution rule. |
| **C7** | Elegance and parsimony, in a substantive sense | Minimize independent ontological commitments, adjustable rules, unexplained mappings and post-hoc exceptions **subject to adequacy**; compare the total burden of explanations, not just the number of labels. | One ground is economical conditionally; the unfinished decombination and physics bridges carry real costs. No demonstrated unique winner. |

The user's earlier specified endpoint—one universal consciousness, intrinsic nature = experience, possibility = actuality, essence = existence, no fundamental spacetime or mass, and a self-contained account of Bostrom's question—is an additional governing target. It is not a theorem of C1–C7 alone. Alternatives that do not accept it must not be declared internally inconsistent merely for that reason.

## 2. What the candidate positively says

**Whole first, not mere neutral substrate.** On the working hypothesis, the whole is concrete and intrinsically experiential, not an abstract program waiting for an operator. Its unity is a hypothesis of one universal consciousness, not an inference from quantum entanglement or the number one. Unlike the neutral-ground DPMRS variant in the attached discussion, it does not deny a universal subject. It need not imagine a giant human-like ego. Ordinary experiences are real and locally bounded; the proposed relation between their incidence and the whole needs to be derived or independently warranted.

**One reality, two sorts of access.** A physical description addresses structural/causal relations and their testable consequences; first-person acquaintance supplies evidence of actual qualitative life. The proposed same-token identity between appropriate physical occurrence and experience is a **constitutive hypothesis**, not the assertion that words or equations reproduce what a particular experience feels like. Neither structural matching alone nor acquaintance alone fixes the relevant physical–phenomenal identity. Experience being given locally does not demonstrate that the complete ground is experiential.

**Timeless foundation, emergent local order.** The whole does not evolve in an external master time. Local succession, clocks, and spacetime must be recovered as relations in physically realized subsystems. This is the user's target, not a result of simply saying “timeless.” The [Barontini cold-atom comparison](COLD_ATOM_CLOCK_COMPARISON.md) is a finite experimental benchmark for internal clocks, not evidence of a universal clock or a proof of this ontology.

**An imperfect whole is not an unexplained whole by definition.** Self-sufficiency and explanatory completeness concern dependence and intelligibility; perfection concerns a distinct value claim. Under the user's adopted implication `perfect whole → best universe`, the non-best-universe premise gives a non-perfect whole by modus tollens. The user objects that a non-agentive whole does not meet their intended meaning of universal consciousness: the candidate must therefore address agency rather than invoke non-agency to evade the objection. Conscious experience and agency are conceptually distinguishable, but that distinction alone does not satisfy the user's stronger requirement or establish a viable account. For an agentive whole, if it could knowingly realize a strictly better universe and was decisively motivated to do so, our non-best universe would require at least one of those conditions to fail. Which fails is not established; the possibility/actuality and local-world comparison must also be made coherent. An explanation of evil cannot justify it merely because it occurred. Privation can describe a missing due good, but finite boundaries need not be harms, and pain remains real positive experience.

**Possibility and actuality remain a problem, not a slogan.** One possible research route is indexed plenitude: our non-optimal universe and better local possibilities are realized as distinct sectors within one whole. This requires actualization and comparison arguments and does not show that arbitrary logically consistent descriptions coexist. It is not the same as total-world modal collapse. Likewise, essence = existence is a desired result; defining a nature as necessary does not establish its concrete instantiation. Neither modal claim is derived by the present synthesis.

**Simulation relative to the whole.** Bostrom's simulated observer would require an actual implementation and relevant assumptions about observer counts, consciousness and probabilities. An internal simulation can have a simulator external to the *simulated domain* without anything being external to the absolute. Calling the whole itself a simulation is a metaphor unless an implementation relation is specified. See [Bostrom's original argument](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/) and [the existing audit](SIMULATION_ZERO_RESIDUE_ATTACHMENT_AUDIT.md).

## 3. Serious alternatives and what they cost

The comparison uses the strongest live versions, not caricatures. None has demonstrated C3 or all physics and experiential bridges.

| Candidate | Genuine strengths | Main unresolved cost relative to the user's target |
|---|---|---|
| **Experiential whole-first view** (preferred here) | Directly matches one universal consciousness and no external observer; may avoid deriving a macro-subject from independent micro-subjects. | Must establish the whole's existence and subjecthood, finite incidence/privacy, qualitative mapping and physics; one fundamental ground does not guarantee a simple derivation. |
| **Neutral-ground priority monism / DPMRS** | One basic ground and real local experiences; can adopt the same physics and neuroscience research programme. | How neutral structure gives rise to experience and selects subjects remains open. The particular attached version denies a universal subject and so conflicts with the user's additional endpoint, not necessarily C1–C7 alone. |
| **Micropsychism or panprotopsychism** | Takes experiential or proto-experiential intrinsic features seriously, and can engage neural evidence without one cosmic subject. | Combination or emergence of human-scale subjects and selection of basic entities remain difficult. It does not satisfy the user's one-universal-subject endpoint without more assumptions. |
| **Non-eliminative physicalism / structural realism** | Can take experience as a datum, use current successful physics and make detailed neural predictions without a fundamental experiential whole. | The full first-person/explanatory identity and necessary actuality still require a defensible account; it is not refuted simply because it uses physical vocabulary. It does not adopt the additional universal-consciousness target. |
| **Spinoza-inspired necessary substance / process views** | Serious whole-first antecedents that seek intelligibility without an external creator; can be developed in multiple ways. | The necessity of specific structure, lived subject boundaries, non-optimality and recovery of current physics need separate arguments. Strict Leibnizian best-world selection is incompatible with C2; Spinoza as such should not be conflated with Leibniz's optimalism. |

[Priority monism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/monism/) is prior art for one basic whole, but alone says nothing that establishes consciousness. [The panpsychism literature](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/panpsychism/) discusses both combination and whole-first approaches. The proposed absolute differs from standard cosmopsychism when “cosmos” means our spacetime universe rather than a pre-spatiotemporal whole. Avoiding bottom-up subject summing **replaces, rather than automatically solves**, the decombination/subject-incidence problem.

### Why the preference is conditional

Given the extra target **one universal consciousness**, experiential whole-first monism directly includes what the user seeks, and avoids adding a wholly non-experiential ultimate plus an unexplained extra realm. That is an intelligible reason to develop it. But a neutral or physicalist rival could use the same empirical models, and positing one subject may introduce its own unexplained incidence structure. C7 requires counting those costs too. The seven criteria alone, without weights or a demonstrated explanatory completion, do not uniquely select this candidate or prove it more empirically likely.

**Suboptimality and agency objection.** A suboptimal universe is a serious challenge to a universal agent expected to know, be able to bring about, and be decisively motivated to bring about a better universe. With the user's adopted `perfect whole → best universe` bridge, it entails a non-perfect whole, not the nonexistence of any universal subject. If possibility and actuality are identical, plenitude may instead require actual imperfect sectors; it need not follow that a universal agent selected this sector over better ones. But plenitude is compatible with a plurality of subjects as well as with one universal subject and thus does not itself support the latter. Moreover, necessary plenitude constrains what universal agency could change. A non-agentive whole cannot be substituted for the user's intended agentive universal consciousness to evade this issue. Whether an imperfect, timeless agentive universal subject has a coherent and explanatorily worthwhile account remains open.

**Likelihood versus usefulness for time:** Evidence currently supports physical models and correlations in their tested domains, not a calibrated ranking of experiential whole-first, neutral-monist and experience-respecting physicalist ultimate ontologies. The working metaphysical preference is not a verdict on which is most probably true. For the physics problem, the more directly useful strategy is an operational **relational quantum-gravity programme with physical clock/reference systems and clock switches**, constrained by locality, complete measurement records and gravity. It is compatible with the user's timeless endpoint but does not itself prove it. The [Höhn–Vanrietvelde clock-switching construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04153) is established prior work in a parametrized model, not a general solution; [Barontini's cold-atom experiment](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07745v2) tests an internal entropy-based ordering in an analogue system, not quantum gravity or the nonexistence of fundamental time. The next project-specific physics task remains L3: compare the same complete cutoff and continuum instruments on a common energy-constrained domain before claiming continuum convergence.

## 4. How to make C4–C6 do work

**Neuroscience:** preserve actual theory differences. [IIT 4.0](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465) supplies specific causal-structure proposals; global neuronal workspace theory proposes a different role for widespread access. Predictive processing is a broader modelling framework and is not by itself a proof of consciousness. The [COGITATE adversarial study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1) reported findings that challenged specified predictions of both IIT and GNWT; it did not directly test or confirm this metaphysics. Do not claim all three theories have been unified or that a generic `F(Φ, richness)` follows from experience.

For the existing concrete rivalry [H_M1 versus H_D1](MAINTAINED_DISCLOSURE_CANDIDATE.md), a separating intervention requires maintaining the nominated local basis (P3), removing the specified distributed organization including old records (P4), and an intervention-valid experiential observation (P6). Under their stipulated premises H_M1 predicts preservation and H_D1 predicts failure of the nominated target. None of the reviewed human experiments establishes all three gates; see [the pathway audit](SPECIFIC_VISUAL_PATHWAY_GATE_TEST.md) and [record-persistence test](DISTRIBUTION_CUT_AND_RECORD_PERSISTENCE.md). These are testable **local constitutive rivals**, not predictions uniquely generated by whole-first monism: either could in principle be embedded in different metaphysical frameworks.

**Physics:** the local field-instrument bound, gravity constraints, and cold-atom clock comparison in the [global plan](GLOBAL_COHERENCE_AND_DECISION_PLAN.md) provide limited checkpoints. They do not yet come from the proposed absolute, recover gravity or spacetime from it, or constitute a decisive open-physics solution. A successful implementation must recover existing experiment in its domain, state its resources and errors, and then give a discriminating quantitative consequence against a specified rival. It must not import an external time for the absolute.

## 5. Priority order and rejection conditions

1. **One constitution, not a bundle of labels.** Specify independently a concrete candidate with enough content to derive some physical structure and experiential incidence, and compare it with the neutral-ground rival. If the same physical and experiential observations are guaranteed under both, explicitly mark them empirically underdetermined.
2. **Physical continuity.** Pursue the existing L3 common-domain cutoff-to-continuum full-instrument task and its local-probe/gravity dependencies; do not describe a finite toy as a solution of quantum gravity.
3. **Finite perspective.** Give the candidate a non-circular, falsifiable mapping to a bounded human experiential contrast, with P3/P4/P6 and hidden-channel/record limitations made explicit. Failure to validate an assay means inconclusive, not evidence for a preferred rival.
4. **Modal and moral coherence.** Identify the domain of possible local worlds, actual sectors and the non-optimality comparison; justify the self-existence claim and the Leibnizian-style bridge or record them as assumptions. A framework entailing `best(U)` fails the adopted C2; one entailing whole perfection while accepting the adopted bridge also conflicts with C2.
5. **Reassess total explanatory cost.** Prefer this synthesis only if it actually supplies more constraints, bridges and empirical recovery than the rival without compensating ad hoc additions. If a necessary bridge is shown inconsistent or a distinctive prediction fails under valid measurement, revise the relevant claim rather than protect the label.
