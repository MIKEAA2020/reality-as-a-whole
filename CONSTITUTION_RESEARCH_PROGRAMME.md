## 1. The object to identify

For a concretely realized physical process P, seek a law

    F(P) = (Gamma(P), chi_P),
    Gamma(P) = (E, I, L).

E denotes actual phenomenal episode occurrences, if any. I specifies their qualitative and physical incidence and temporal extent. L specifies continuation or lineage without automatically identifying it with numerical identity. chi specifies qualitative character and organization for each episode.

A law must say which domains it covers and what counts as sufficient physical specification. It cannot merely accept an already supplied episode, call an output state a quality, and claim to have derived both.

The full explanatory task additionally requires an identity/constitution argument for F and an account of the foundation. An empirically successful local F would be real progress, but not yet a completed no-brute-facts whole-account.

## 2. The physical basis must be independently specified

The existing [realized-process candidate](REALIZED_PROCESS_CANDIDATE.md) remains the appropriate physical starting point: localization, state, dynamics, physically meaningful interventions, and actual token identity. In algebraic presentations this can be expressed through local observable algebras, a state, dynamics, and intervention maps. No new unexplained phenomenal variable is silently added to that basis.

Whether that basis is sufficient is itself a hypothesis. The programme must retain an explicit alternative: a discovered inadequacy may require an enlarged intrinsic nature rather than another relabeling of public response statistics.

A measurement protocol is a way to investigate the process. It is not automatically part of the process's phenomenal constitution. If an apparatus changes the process, that intervention must be represented physically; if it merely records or discards a feedforward output, it cannot acquire the right to redefine the same intrinsic episode.

## 3. First result: measurement completeness does not fix intrinsic geometry

The recent [geometry construction](research/constitutive_geometry/REPORT.md) used the two-response family with tensor, at x=1/4 and y=1/3,

    g_direct = diag(16/3, 9/2).

Now leave the source process unchanged and record its first output through a known bit-flip channel with error 1/4, while retaining the second output. The observed first-response probability becomes

    z = 1/4 + x/2.

The new tensor in the **same source coordinates** is

    g_noisy = diag(16/15, 9/2).

The noisy experiment still identifies both source parameters in principle: x = 2(z-1/4), and y is unchanged. Thus merely requiring parameter-identifying or informationally complete observations does not select a unique Fisher tensor.

These two tensors are not even proportional: one coordinate's information shrinks by a factor of five, while the other's does not. No single positive scale restores equality. This channel is invertible as a map for identifying source parameters, not reversibly invertible as a physical stochastic operation; its algebraic inverse would require negative transition weights.

Consequently, if one fixed intrinsic phenomenal tensor h and one fixed identification chi were required to equal each experiment's Fisher tensor up to a scalar, the two requirements would conflict. In the second coordinate they fix the relative scalar, while the first coordinate contradicts it.

This is a conditional obstruction to a **protocol-independent generalization** of the metric hypothesis. It does not refute the earlier hypothesis restricted to one declared protocol, nor prove that no intrinsic metric can be defined by a stronger physical construction.

### Consequence

Even success there would not automatically provide chi or Gamma.

The exact rational witness is checked in the existing geometry suite. This is a targeted consequence of the construction, not an additional toy consciousness assignment.

## 5. Milestones and their success criteria

### M0 — Cross-framework and physical adequacy throughout

[The unified constitution map](UNIFIED_CONSTITUTION_MAP.md) states the integration requirement: the candidate must be expressible on a physically justified realized-process basis and invariant under faithful changes of presentation. Any effective neural or computational reduction needs a justified microphysical relation; equal physical summaries do not automatically imply equal phenomenal facts. AQFT, quantum information, causal geometry, contextual logic, scaling, and implementation constrain the law from the outset, not only after a standalone brain theory is finished. Broader extension is staged, but physical compatibility is never postponed or waived.

**Success:** a candidate identifies its physical domain, exact presentation equivalences, and which coarse-grainings preserve its targets. A correct overall account must additionally account for the domains and correspondences of twistors, topos methods, Langlands-type structures, fractals, and the other mathematical resources without using their names as unexplained glue.


### M1 — A warranted narrow target

Start with an explicitly delimited, ordinary human qualitative contrast, such as two consciously discriminated visual orientations, without assuming that the stimuli or reports are the experience. This is a proposed target domain, not newly collected evidence.

Specify the target contrasts, temporal conditions, evidence channels, and known possible failures of memory, attention, comparison, and reporting. Separate mere changes of qualitative names from genuinely independently fixed differences.

**Success:** target identification remains warranted in the interventions used to discriminate theories. A synthetic labeled dataset does not satisfy this milestone.

### M2 — An interventionally adequate intrinsic basis

Specify candidate physical state variables and transitions, not just externally varied stimulus distributions. Distinguish active content realization, downstream report, copied monitoring, and actual counterfactual powers. Keep state estimation uncertainty visible.

**Success:** held-out interventions separate serious rival determinations, or explicitly leave an unresolved set. A response geometry shared by an active source and monitor does not select the basis.

### M3 — A concrete composition law

Propose a rule for togetherness, temporal extent, and continuation before seeing its preferred answer. State whether it permits overlap, multiple episodes, or a continuity structure other than a partition.

**Success:** the rule survives the existing weak-link, copying, temporal-origin, realized-token, and event-versus-mechanism tests, and has independently warranted phenomenal consequences. Whole connectivity, report convergence, or recurrence alone is not sufficient warrant.

GWT can contribute a mechanism and proposed constraints here, but “globally available” cannot simply be defined to mean “phenomenally together.”

### M4 — A qualitative law with independent constraints

**Exact sufficiency condition:** [Target factorization](COMMON_CONSTITUTION_CLOSURE.md#2-an-exact-sufficiency-criterion) requires the anchored target to be constant on the proposed basis fibers. This is necessary and sufficient for a map on the realized support, not for its constitutive warrant. Circular report codes and unique timestamps show why sufficiency alone is weak; the actual recorded-onset check establishes no experiential identity.

Specify chi on the candidate domain. Use independent anchors, qualitative relations, and interventions; reserve target conditions for predictions rather than fitting every case. Distinguish the calibration of a map from explaining its identity.

**Success:** the law excludes serious alternative identifications and predicts held-out contrasts without changing its parameters or equivalence relation to protect itself. Geometry covariance alone is not success.

### M5 — A constitutive justification

**No exclusion by incomplete survey:** [The complete-intelligibility comparison audit](COMPLETE_INTELLIGIBILITY_ARGUMENT_AUDIT.md) shows why no-brute-facts does not by itself select plenitude or identify structure with experience. Grounded distinctions are permissible; explanatory direction does not follow from implication or covariance. The occurrence/determination/adequacy obligations remain within W1–W6, not a new competing queue.

[The closure argument](COMMON_CONSTITUTION_CLOSURE.md) distinguishes uniqueness given a fixed basis/target from an explanation of why that determination belongs to the concrete nature. Token identity, property identity, and explanatory unification are distinct; neither declaring identity nor naming a dependence relation removes residue.

Compare the integrated account with non-independent realization, correlation-only, and enlarged-intrinsic-nature rivals. Explain why its powers, qualities, and incidence belong to this one occurrence rather than being linked by an unexplained dictionary.

**Success:** an independently motivated identity or constitution argument with rival-discriminating consequences, not an identity slogan. Scientific and philosophical justification can be explanatory and convergent; it need not pretend to be a deduction from physics alone. Its unresolved premises must remain explicit.

### M6 — Physical scope and ultimate foundation

Extend only where justified: realistic neural organization, alternative realizations, quantum/algebraic and relativistic constraints, and correspondences among mathematical presentations. Throughout, pursue the parallel foundational and evaluative tracks in the governing framework; neither the absolute nor the final local law is exempt from explanation. Broader physical extension is staged, but ultimate integration is not postponed until after an isolated brain theory.

**Success:** the domains and correspondences are actually established. Compatibility with a mathematical framework is not a derivation of physics, and a successful local identity is not yet a whole-account explanation.

## 6. Candidate rejection and revision rules

- A demonstrated contradiction or independently warranted counterexample rejects the stated version.
- An unresolved derivation remains unresolved, not automatically unlikely.
- A revision must state what changed and why; it cannot redefine every apparent counterexample as outside the domain.
- A failed report is not automatically absent experience; an intact report is not automatically intact unity.
- A physically duplicated implementation is another token if it actually occurs, not one token because it is isomorphic.
- No labels are quotiented away solely to create uniqueness; no symbol permutation is counted as a genuine quality difference without reference warrant.
- The number of passing software tests is never evidence that a consciousness assignment is true.
