# A concrete constitutive candidate: identity with a realized local process

## 1. Replace an unspecified “organization” with a declared basis

The minimal causal candidate can be sharpened as follows:

> **Phenomenal character and episode incidence are fixed by the complete realized local process: its concrete observable organization, state, dynamics, and physically available interventions. They are not fixed merely by behavior, a computational transition table, energy, stress, or a one-particle covariance.**

This is a specific sufficiency hypothesis about the kind of constitutive basis, not a discovered law identifying which processes are conscious. Its substantive risk is that some additional constitutive feature may be required beyond this basis. Calling the basis “complete” cannot be used to add such features after the fact without revising the hypothesis.

The candidate basis is a **realized process net**. A mathematical presentation is

\[
\mathfrak P=\bigl(\{\mathcal A(O)\},\alpha,\omega,\{\mathcal I(O)\},\mathsf R\bigr).
\]

Its entries mean:

- A(O): observable algebras assigned to declared physical regions or process locations, including inclusion and commutation relations;
- α: dynamics, with any external control or time dependence explicitly specified;
- ω: the state, including higher correlations rather than only selected summaries;
- I(O): the allowed instruments or interventions, their localization, probability rules, and state updates;
- R: the concrete realization and occurrence relations linking the presentation to actual processes, rather than merely to a formal specification.

This is an organizing specification, not a claim that a particular AQFT net is already a complete theory of a brain. In a quantum setting some of these data constrain or determine others; listing them does not postulate independent extra substances. A deterministic finite causal model is another possible presentation in a limited domain. Such alternative presentations require explicit bridges, not an assertion that they are identical by notation.

A state is not an additional hidden classical trajectory through every quantum observable. If a candidate needs actual records or a realized history, it must say what those mean in its physical interpretation. R does not automatically explain actuality: it marks an obligation that a mathematical net alone does not discharge.

## 2. Why each part is necessary to the proposed comparison

### State and higher correlations

The nonquasifree mixture and its covariance-matched quasifree replacement have the same two-point data but different four-point data. A complete observable state distinguishes them. This blocks the inference that a covariance-only description is already a constitutive basis; it does not establish that those four-point differences affect experience.

### Dynamics and interventions

A passive monitor can copy a source's ordinary trajectories without supporting the source's independently perturbable organization. The actual intervention domain distinguishes them. Behavioral similarity cannot stand in for an isomorphism of the full declared causal structure.

### Local organization

A bare global algebra or global transition map does not by itself identify physically local constituents. Changing mathematical coordinates can create different apparent decompositions. A candidate must preserve the physically specified localization and intervention structure, not select a decomposition because it produces the preferred subject boundary.

### Actual realization

Two presentations of one process do not become two processes simply because their notation differs. Conversely, two genuinely distinct implementations do not become one occurrence merely because their descriptions are isomorphic. The candidate must distinguish representation changes from relations between actual tokens. It cannot count descriptions as subjects.

None of these physical adequacy requirements alone establishes phenomenal sufficiency. They improve the antecedent of the proposed identity claim.

## 3. Exact equivalence conditions, not an unspecified “same computation”

A comparison must declare which maps count as constitutive equivalences. In the algebraic setting a proposed equivalence should at least preserve:

1. the observable algebraic structure, adjoint, and identity;
2. regional inclusions and the declared physical localization relations;
3. dynamics under the stated time correspondence;
4. the state on all observables in the claimed domain;
5. the intervention family, including outcomes, probabilities, and updates;
6. the difference between re-presenting one token and comparing separate actual tokens.

If Φ is an algebraic isomorphism and f the declared region correspondence, typical conditions include

\[
\Phi(\mathcal A_X(O))=\mathcal A_Y(f(O)),\qquad
\Phi\circ\alpha^X_t=\alpha^Y_t\circ\Phi,
\qquad \omega_Y\circ\Phi=\omega_X.
\]

Intervention intertwining must be added; equal unperturbed correlations alone are not enough. If these conditions hold in the specified domain, observable predictions transported by Φ agree. This is a physical equivalence statement. It is not a theorem that the phenomenal organizations agree.

The constitutive hypothesis adds the latter assertion:

\[
\mathfrak P_X\simeq\mathfrak P_Y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E(X)\simeq E(Y).
\]

For two distinct tokens, the right side means equivalence of phenomenal organization, not numerical identity of their episodes. For a re-description of one token, it must not manufacture additional episodes. The hypothesis needs both disciplines.

The domain must be declared in advance. An equivalence restricted to laboratory-accessible interventions need not be constitutively complete; an equivalence over physically impossible interventions also requires justification. This is an unresolved modeling choice, not a free escape from failed predictions.

## 4. What remains missing: a selector and a qualitative identification

A sufficiency claim still does not specify the episode assignment. A completed candidate needs a law of the form

\[
\Gamma(\mathfrak P)=\{\text{episode incidences and temporal extents}\},
\qquad
\chi(\mathfrak P,e)=\text{qualitative organization of episode }e.
\]

These are targets, not algorithms already supplied. Their introduction does not solve the problem. The theory must explain why these particular Γ and χ follow from the constitution or are warranted identities.

Invariance under process equivalence only requires that Γ and χ transport consistently between equivalent realizations. It does not determine their values on inequivalent processes. Many assignments can respect this requirement. Even an empty assignment respects equivalence before phenomenal occurrence evidence rules it out in its domain. An anchor for familiar human episodes does not by itself fix every other equivalence class or every subject boundary.

Thus the sharpened candidate passes a **physical-description adequacy audit**, but fails to be a complete constitutive theory: its phenomenal selector and qualitative identification remain unspecified. This is a finding about the current proposal, not a proof that a realized-process identity theory is impossible.

## 5. Do not smuggle Γ in through an apparently physical rule

Several natural completions are not yet justified:

- **Whole connected process:** would need to explain why connection implies phenomenal unity and why weak remote coupling does or does not enlarge an episode.
- **Maximally irreducible subnet:** needs a precise irreducibility notion, physical scale, intervention family, temporal extent, and exclusion rule. The label “maximal” supplies no phenomenal warrant.
- **Minimal process determining an anchored content:** can identify a target-specific support within a model, but support for one content is not automatically an entire subject or episode boundary.
- **Self-maintaining or recurrent process:** may characterize physical organization without yet explaining qualitative occurrence or individuation.

An open biological system need not have an exactly closed subsystem algebra or transition law. Insisting on exact autonomy could exclude plausible candidates for reasons that reflect an idealization rather than the phenomenon. Relaxing to approximate autonomy requires a justified approximation and relevance criterion, not a threshold chosen to obtain the desired answer.

This prevents the earlier exact-core examples from silently becoming a consciousness law.

## 6. What this does to the research programme

The next substantive advance must supply and justify an actual constraint on Γ or χ. It must do more than add detail to P. Three possible kinds of advance are:

1. A narrow, independently anchored phenomenal contrast that identifies a content-determining process across dissociations, together with an argument for why that identification is constitutive rather than only predictive.
2. A composition principle whose physical conditions and phenomenal implications are explicitly specified, and whose boundary consequences survive the weak-coupling, copying, and realization tests.
3. A genuinely explanatory identity argument showing why a specified intrinsic organization is a qualitative occurrence, rather than merely giving it the same name.

None is supplied by calculating another energy limit. None can be replaced by assigning consciousness labels to synthetic circuits and then testing whether a program recovers those labels.

The process-net specification does provide a concrete way to compare candidates and preserve all the physical distinctions already established. It is therefore a useful proposed basis for the missing law. It does **not** turn that law into a known result.

## 7. Current verdict

The strongest now-specified minimal hypothesis is **realized-process sufficiency**, with a local observable/state/dynamics/intervention basis and explicit token discipline. The missing constitutive principle is more precisely the justified Γ–χ law on that basis, or a justified demonstration that the basis needs some further intrinsic feature.

We have not identified such a law. The candidate cannot yet tell us which new realization has an episode, what it is like, or where its boundary lies. Claiming otherwise would simply reintroduce the arbitrary phenomenal annotation that the early audits exposed.

No new executable theorem or empirical consciousness finding is claimed. This assessment refines the constitutive proposal and records its failure to become a completed theory; the existing 1,012 tests remain checks of the earlier specified constructions.
