# The surviving thesis — consolidated statement and maximal steelman

**Status of this document.** This is the project's position stated at full strength, assembled from everything in the repository that has withstood its own tests. It is not a history. Provenance tags (`[R3 §4.4]` = Round 3 §4.4; `[WP2]` = Work Package 2; `[A1 §4.3]`, `[A2 §4.5]` = Audit 1/2; `[K2 Check D]` = the second chat's checks) are pointers for verification, not narrative. Every claim carries a grade:

- **E** — established: proved, or verified by a reproducible check in the repository;
- **C** — conditional: derived under premises that are stated and not themselves derived;
- **H** — hypothesis: defended as the best available option, not derived;
- **O** — open obligation: named, with the best-known route and a failure condition.

The grades are part of the thesis. A version of it that drops them is a different, weaker claim.

---

## 1. The thesis

> There is one concrete reality, and nothing stands outside it to run, create, or interpret it. Some of it is presented from within: that is a datum, not an inference, and its character is known by acquaintance rather than by description. Presented episodes are not shown to an inner observer and are not produced by a second substance; each is identical to a bounded concrete process, so that the process's public structure and its presented character are one occurrence under two kinds of access. The public structure is not decreed: within an explicit operational framework, spectral resolution and frame homogeneity force a Jordan state space, and either energy observability or local tomography with composition selects the complex quantum family, from which the Born form and reversible commutator dynamics follow. Which processes are presented is fixed by their actual intervention-structure, not by correlation or description; any third-person rule that tracks this returns a robust core and a graded margin, never a point selected by a single scalar. Imperfection, suffering and loss are real: each presented process has its own structural norm, and privation relative to that norm needs no best world and no fall. Existence as such is a given that every account shares and no account can explain non-circularly; it is therefore not a differential cost, and the thesis claims neither that nothing was impossible nor that this world is necessary. What remains unexplained is typed and listed: the premises of the structural derivation, the calibration of the actual dynamics, the determinacy of boundaries at the margin, the status of definite outcomes, and the standing of the explanatory framework itself. The thesis is the smallest such list the project has been able to defend, not the absence of one.

Working name: **Immanent Constitutive Monism (ICM)** — the `C → Π, Σ, Q` architecture of Rounds 1–6 read in the two-tier form that the second chat made explicit. Names used elsewhere in the record map onto it as follows (this table is the only concession to nomenclature):

| Name in the record | Relation to ICM |
|---|---|
| "Self-simulation", "zero-residue simulation hypothesis" (chat 1) | Predecessor; its immanence claim survives, its computational vocabulary and closure claims do not `[A1 §§3, 10]` |
| Immanent Constitutive Architecture `C → Π, Σ, Q` (Rounds 1–6) | Same thesis; this document adds the C3S/C3E reading, the typed residue, and the robust-core form of Σ |

| "Two-tier structural–experiential monism" (Audit 2) | Same thesis, descriptive name |

## 2. The criteria the thesis answers to

The filter is fixed here in the forms that are non-empty, defensible, and symmetric between candidates. Each reformulation was forced by a specific failure of the stronger reading.

| Criterion | Final form | Why this form |
|---|---|---|
| **D1 Datum** | Some presentation occurs; its occurrence is not inferred and its character is accessed by acquaintance. | The minimal form even a strict illusionist must address (the "illusion problem" is the datum under another description). Stronger forms ("phenomenal properties are real and non-physical") are conclusions, not criteria. |
| **D2 Imperfection** | Imperfection, suffering and loss are real, and no principle of the best explains what exists. | The modal-comparative form ("some possible world is better") is unsatisfiable by any necessitarian candidate and vacuous under global modal collapse `[K2 Check B; A2 §4.1]`. The axiological form is what the datum actually supports. |
| **D3S Structural intelligibility** | Every structural fact is derived, constituted, or shown to lack a well-posed contrast — by argument, not by naming `[R1 §1]`. | "Necessary", "self-grounding", "intrinsic" perform none of the three unless argued `[A1 §4.5; R4 §9.2]`. |
| **D3E Experiential intelligibility** | Presented character is intelligible by being presented; the demand for a structural derivation of what it is like is ill-typed. Its *arrangement* — which structures present, with what relations — is a D3S matter. | Acquaintance supplies the nature, not the arrangement `[A2 §4.4]`. The split is bounded by identity (§3), which is what stops it from immunizing the thesis. |
| **D4 Empirical constraint** | The structural tier must be constrained by physics and by consciousness science: it must forbid something, and its qualitative-structure claims must have a testable shadow. | Literal novel prediction is not available to any whole-reality account; a research-programme reading is the strongest honest one, and it is what polices D3E `[A2 §1]`. |
| **D5/D6 Alignment** | Coherence with current physics and theories of consciousness is a tie-breaker, not a licence. | "Trends" are heterogeneous and partly incompatible; the field's attention is evidence of plausibility, not of truth `[A2 §4.12]`. |
| **G0 Existence parity** (replaces the premise "nothing is incoherent") | The existence of a ground is a given shared by every account and explicable by none without circularity (every explanans exists). It is therefore excluded from *differential* assessment; nothing follows from it about which ground, or about necessity. | Whether an empty world is possible is a live dispute; the thesis does not need to win it `[A2 §4.2]`. Parity is weaker than "nothing is impossible" and sufficient. |

**The standard of residue** is the one set at the start and never improved upon: *no arbitrary unexplained actuality* — not "a description without concepts" `[R1 §1]`. A residue counts against the thesis only if it is **differential**: some rival could in principle discharge it non-circularly. This is not immunization; it is what makes comparison possible, and it is what §7 applies.

## 3. The architecture

```
                     C   one concrete reality (actuality; the only thing that is given)
                     │
        ┌────────────┼─────────────┐
        ▼            ▼             ▼
        Π            Σ             Q
   public structure  selection    presented character
   (D3S; D4)         (D3S; D4)    (D3E; its relations D4)
```

- **C** is not a substance behind the three; it is the occurrence of which Π, Σ, Q are aspects. Nothing external runs, creates, or reads it `[R1 §4; A1 §3]`.
- **Π** is the structure that any interaction with C exhibits: operational states, their symmetries, their dynamics. Its form is conditionally derived (§4.3). Its calibration is not (§7).
- **Σ** says which processes are presented episodes. It is a fact about actual intervention-structure (what influences what under intervention), not about correlation, description, or an observer's choice `[R3 §2; R2 §3.3]`.
- **Q** is the character of a Σ-selected process. It is not a further property attached to the process; it is what the process is, intrinsically. Π and Q are the same occurrence under description and under acquaintance respectively. This is the identity that makes the two tiers *epistemic* (two accesses) rather than *ontological* (two properties) `[R3 §8.1; R6 §7.1]`.

**Dependence direction** (priority monism versus micro-pluralism) is downstream of Σ, not a separate commitment: if boundaries are fixed by a global criterion over C, dependence is top-down by construction; if by local composition, bottom-up. The thesis is monist about *closure* (one concrete reality, one explanatory structure) and neutral about direction; whichever direction is adopted, its dependence relation is counted as a primitive `[A2 §4.9]`.

## 4. What stands, with the best argument for each

### 4.1 No external stage — **E**
Anything proposed to run, create, or interpret reality is, if real, part of reality; an explanation of a level by a host explains the level, not the whole `[R1 K2; A1 §§3.2–3.5]`. Pushing the question up a level is not answering it. The thesis is the only option that does not do so.

### 4.2 Presentation as datum; character by acquaintance — **E** (datum) / **H** (its reading)
The occurrence of presentation is the one fact that cannot be removed by any theory, because its removal would be an occurrence of seeming `[R1 K1]`. The illusionist can deny that the seeming has phenomenal character; the thesis then differs from illusionism by one premise, D1's acquaintance clause, and records the cost. What the premise buys is exact: the *nature* of what is presented is intelligible without derivation; its *arrangement* is not `[A2 §4.4]`. Every overreach in the record came from spending this premise on arrangement.

### 4.3 Identity rather than interaction or production — **H**, with its constraints established
- Two substances need a bridge law that is itself brute; production of experience from a ground defined as non-experiential needs a bridge of the same kind `[R1 §6; A1 §5]`. Identity has no bridge. That is a real simplification, not a slogan: it removes a *kind* of primitive, which is the only kind of parsimony the project counts `[R5 §7.2]`.
- The identity is constitutive, not merely nominal: if a process is a presented episode, its full actual state and organization *are* the episode's character `[R3 §2.2]`. This is also what keeps the view compatible with an exclusion principle (no nested subjects inside a selected whole): such a principle is compatible with a Russellian intrinsic-nature view only on its constitutive form, where micro-level natures do not persist as separate experiences within the selected whole — which is this form `[Mørch; A2 §5]`.
- "Identity" is a hypothesis because identity statements cannot be read off correlations `[R3 §8.3]`. Its defence is comparative: every alternative adds a bridge and explains nothing the identity does not.

### 4.4 The structure of Π is conditionally derived, and the condition is interpretable — **C**
Within an operational framework (real scalars, probabilistic mixing, finite sectors, full affine effects):

1. **Spectrality (SP)** — every state resolves within one distinguishing pure frame — and **frame homogeneity (FH)** — reversible equivalence of equal-size frames — force a simple Jordan or classical state space (Barnum–Hilgert) `[R5 §2]`.
2. Either **energy observability (EO)** — a faithful conserved-value assignment to reversible changes — or **local tomography with the stated composition and continuous-reversibility conditions (LT, CM, CR)** selects the complex quantum family from the Jordan classification `[R5 §§4–5]`.
3. Positive linear states then give the **Born trace form**; closed continuous reversible evolution has **commutator form** `[R4 §4]`.
4. Complex QM is the *locally tomographic* family (tensor deficiency `Δ_C = 0`, `Δ_R > 0`, `Δ_H < 0`): nature selects the theory with *no* irreducible global surplus, which refutes the presumption that unity of experience demands maximal holism `[R6 §4]`.
5. Counter-models fix the content of each premise (pentagon: FH without SP; point-joined-disk: SP without FH; square: pure-state transitivity weaker than FH; non-broadcasting is generic to every non-simplex state space and derives nothing) `[R5 §3; R6 §2]`.

**Why this is the strongest available form of "why quantum mechanics?"** The question is converted from *decree* to *conditional on three operational properties of presentation* — how states resolve, how frames relate, how change is labelled. These are statements about distinguishability and symmetry. They are exactly the kind of fact that a structural phenomenology could in principle constrain from the D3E side (§8, item 1). No other whole-reality account in the record offers a bridge of this shape; the theory landscape it answers to (reconstruction programmes, GPTs) is mainstream, not manuscript.

### 4.5 Σ: what any selection rule must be, and what it can return — **E** (constraints) / **H** (robust-core form)
Established constraints on any rule:
- It must be **interventional**: shared input and observational correlation do not make a unity; one-way transmission does not either; only mutual influence under intervention counts `[R3 §2.3]`.
- It must be **invariant under recoding with transported intervention structure**, and only then `[R3 §3]`.
- It cannot be read off **bare dynamics**: there is no universal unique binary decomposition of a state space from its dynamics alone; parts require an algebra or interface, and interfaces are recoverable only conditional on a stronger structural goal `[R2 §3; WP1 §2; WP2 §3.4]`.
- A **strict scalar maximum** does not select: symmetric overlapping candidates tie `[R1 §11]`; a strict-score boundary rule returns a non-dominated frontier `[R3 §4.4]`; on a six-node system nine functionals monotone in integration and richness yield three global winners, one to seven maxima, a three-member Pareto front, and one candidate no weighted sum can select `[K2 Check D]`.
- Five inputs enter every operational selection and are not derived: intervention algebra, target observation family, ports, event grain, temporal protocol `[WP4 §7]`.

**The robust-core form (H).** These are results about the *shape of the answer*, and the thesis adopts that shape rather than fighting it: a third-person selection rule returns, for a given system and admissible class of functionals, (i) a **core** — candidates selected under every admissible functional and stable under grain and protocol variation — and (ii) a **margin** — candidates whose status varies. Admissibility is itself constrained: unity is necessary for subjecthood, so any functional that lets a zero-integration set win is inadmissible. In the toy case this excludes two of nine functionals; under all seven remaining, the tightly coupled pair is a subject, and only how the loosely coupled chain is cut (one quad or two pairs) depends on the richness definition `[K2 Check D]`. The core is the prediction; the margin is reported as a margin, following the admissible-family discipline `[WP4 §9]`.

**Why this is not a retreat.** Boundary vagueness at the margin is the position every mature individuation concept occupies (organism, species, storm). The thesis locates determinacy where it is actually had: *first-personally*, each presented episode is determinate for itself (its occurrence is the datum), and the third-person rule is an instrument for attributing presentation to others, which can be graded without the presentation being graded. This combines the actuality-first route for the given case with set-identification for attribution `[WP4 §§5–6]`. The strongest objection — that "phenomenally conscious" admits no borderline cases (Simon 2017; Antony 2008, cited from memory) — targets the *presence* of presentation, which the thesis keeps determinate, not the *extent* of the process that bears it.

### 4.6 Q has a structural shadow, and the shadow is testable — **C** (geometry) / **H** (that it is phenomenal geometry)
From response distinguishability alone, a local Fisher metric on states follows, and smooth f-divergences give proportional local geometries `[R3 §6]`; maximizing over the algebra's sharp contrasts gives it without fitted distance parameters, with a definite consequence under added noise `[R3 §6.4; R4 §6]`. The hypothesis DM-Q — that this is the geometry of experienced differentiation — is exactly the kind of D3S/D4 claim D3E does not shield: it can be wrong. This is how the experiential tier avoids immunization: not by describing what it is like, but by committing to the *relations* among presented differences.

### 4.7 Existence: parity, not necessity — **E** (parity) / **H** (actuality-first)
- The parity point is a theorem about explanation: any explanans of "why anything exists" is itself something that exists; so no account explains it non-circularly, and the question is not differential `[R1 §19; A2 §4.2]`. The one rival that appears to escape — axiarchism, on which an abstract requirement that value be realized explains concrete existence — relocates the given rather than removing it: the holding of that requirement and its efficacy are its terminal givens. Parity holds at the level the trilemma fixes: every account has a terminal given `[R6 §6.1]`; this thesis places its own at existence as such and nowhere else.
- The thesis does **not** claim necessary existence, and it does not need to. Essence does not establish instantiation (`□∀x(C(x)→K(x))` does not yield `□∃x C(x)`) `[R1 §18; R4 §9.2]`; adding actual experience yields an actual occurrence, not the modal step `[R3 §7.2]`; a neutral theory has an empty-concrete expansion `[R3 §7.1]`. Every closure claim in the record that failed, failed here.
- What the thesis asserts is weaker and stable: actuality is the starting point, not a conclusion. The given appearance needs no separate actualization bridge *for itself* `[WP4 §5.1]`. Whether an empty world is possible is left open (G0).

### 4.8 Modality: a definite exit from the grounding trilemma — **H**
The trilemma is: a contingent foundation (brute); a necessary one (modal collapse or modal realism); or non-well-founded grounding `[R6 §6.1]`. Sharpened: *full necessitating closure plus well-foundedness entails no actual contingency* `[R2 §1.9]`. The thesis therefore cannot keep all three of (contingent facts exist; every fact has a full necessitating ground; grounding is well-founded). Its exit is to **drop universal necessitating explanation, not explanation**:

- **Structure** (the form of Π) is explained *necessitatingly*: given SP, FH and EO/LT, the complex family follows. The premises are inputs (§7), not brute-by-stipulation.
- **Outcomes** are explained *non-necessitatingly*: the law of outcomes (Born weights) is part of Π and derived; which outcome *this* episode registers is an **indexical** fact, i.e., a fact about where in the structure the asking episode is located, which has no well-posed world-level contrast (§4.9).
- **Existence** is exempt by parity (G0).

This neither collapses modality (the thesis is silent on whether other structures were possible) nor inflates it (no plurality of worlds is posited). Both overreaches in the record — "modal collapse is demonstrably false because alternatives are consistent" and "this world is the only possible one because its essence entails existence" — make the same error in opposite directions, confusing mathematical consistency with metaphysical possibility `[A1 §4.3; A2 §4.7]`. The thesis makes neither.

### 4.9 Outcomes: the branch-relative route is the best available, and it is not finished — **H**
An open subsystem's reduced state is an improper mixture; nothing in unitary structure says which component is actual `[R6 §5]`. The options each add a primitive (collapse parameters, trajectory variables, agent primitives) except the branch-relative one, which adds a measure interpretation. The thesis takes the branch-relative route because it is the only one in which Σ does the work it already has to do: subjects are selected *within* decohered structure, "why this outcome" becomes "why am I this episode", and the latter has no world-level contrast class — every episode asks it and every episode is answered by its own location. The obligation is the interpretation of Born weights as self-location probabilities (decision-theoretic or envariance arguments; contested). The failure condition is explicit: if no branch-relative derivation of Born weights survives, outcomes revert to a differential residue and the trilemma exit in §4.8 is weakened.

### 4.10 Imperfection: subject-relative privation, no best world, no fall — **H**
D2 is satisfied as follows. Decomposition into bounded episodes is positive expression, not loss; evil is privation *relative to the structural norm of the episode that suffers it* — the integration and differentiation its own organization supports — not relative to a world-optimum `[A2 §4.10]`. This makes the norm local and real, drops the global optimum (so D2's vacuity problem does not arise), and yields a generic but genuine D4 shadow: fragmentation and loss of differentiation should co-vary with suffering. The norm inherits whatever margin Σ has `[K2 Check E]`; where the margin is wide, the attribution of privation is correspondingly graded.

### 4.11 Computation and representation are internal achievements, not the ontology — **E**
Computation and meaning are things some processes within C accomplish relative to interfaces; they are not what C is. Behavioural equivalence yields a canonical type, never a realization population; hidden duplicates and padding are invisible to any interface `[R1 §8; WP3 §§4–5]`. Any formulation of the thesis that says reality "is" computational re-imports the implementation and semantic questions it dissolved `[A1 §3.4; A2 §4.8]`. The simulation vocabulary is dropped, with its one durable lesson kept: a host is part of the whole.

### 4.12 Leibniz, Spinoza, Whitehead, Bostrom — what the thesis takes and what it declines
- **Leibniz:** metaphysical evil as limitation (kept, in subject-relative form); a free divine choice among worlds (declined by D3S, not by D2) `[A2 §4.1]`.
- **Spinoza:** one reality with no external cause; attributes as modes of access (kept); mode-necessitarianism (declined as unneeded; see §4.8).
- **Whitehead:** no bare substrate behind activity; perishing and mutual obstruction as the price of plurality (kept); creativity as an underived ultimate (declined — and the same standard is applied to the thesis's own ground, §4.7) `[A2 §4.6]`.
- **Bostrom:** nothing except the host-inclusion lesson `[A1 §3]`.

## 6. What has been shown not to work (standing exclusions)

These are results, not preferences, and they constrain every future version:

1. Existence from consistency, fixed points, or essence `[A1 §4.3; R1 §18; R3 §7; R4 §9.2; A2 §4.7]`.
2. Subjects from strict maximization of a single scalar `[R1 §11; R3 §4.4; K2 Check D]`.
3. Parts from a bare state or bare dynamics `[R2 §3; WP1 §2]`.
4. Phenomenal unity from shared input or correlation `[R3 §2.3]`.
5. Holism as the signature of unity (local tomography is the selected feature) `[R6 §4]`; non-broadcasting as the signature of privacy `[R6 §2]`.
6. Contextuality or quantum structure from the mere occurrence of presentation `[R6 §3]`.
7. Realization counts from behavioural equivalence `[WP3 §§4–5]`.
8. "Nothing is impossible" or "modal collapse is refuted" from formal consistency in either direction `[A2 §§4.2, 4.7]`.
9. A best possible world under any necessitarian reading of D2 `[K2 Check B]`.
10. The whole as a simulation, programme, or interpreter-dependent computation `[A1 §3; R1 §8]`.

## 8. What to improve, in order of leverage

Each item has a success criterion and a failure criterion. An item whose failure criterion is met is to be downgraded in §9, not reworded.

1. **Close the tiers from the D3E side.** Show that spectral resolution, frame homogeneity and (preferably) energy observability are constraints on how *presented differences* can be structured — that a field of presented contrasts must resolve into distinguishing frames, that equal-size frames are reversibly equivalent for it, that its changes carry conserved labels. *Success:* a derivation from stated phenomenological-structural axioms to SP/FH, with counter-models showing which axiom excludes which GPT. *Failure:* the axioms needed are restatements of SP/FH in phenomenological vocabulary (the renaming test `[R5 §7.2]`), or they copy the observed structure into a definition (the nothing-new test `[R3 §8.4]`). Then the premises stay inputs.
2. **Prove the robust core exists.** Characterize the admissible class of selection functionals by invariance conditions (interventional, recoding-transported, unity-gated, grain-stable), and prove that in a stated model class the core is non-empty and the margin is bounded. *Success:* a theorem plus checks on realistic coupled architectures, not toys. *Failure:* every candidate is functional-dependent in realistic systems; then the vagueness reply fails and Σ needs a new principle, not a new scalar.
3. **Born weights as self-location.** Adopt or construct a derivation of the Born interpretation within branch-relative structure that uses only Π's derived form (trace form, unitarity) and Σ's selection. *Success:* a derivation whose assumptions are already in the thesis. *Failure:* a new primitive is needed; reclassify outcomes as differential and note the §4.8 exit is weakened.
4. **Test DM-Q.** The differentiation geometry makes a definite noise-dependent prediction `[R3 §6.4]`. Confront it with psychophysical discrimination data under controlled noise. *Success:* the proportionality survives. *Failure:* the geometry is operational but not phenomenal; Q loses its only derived relational content and D4 pressure on the experiential tier increases.

### 9.2 Regularities, and what they license

1. **Every closure claim in the record was withdrawn, usually by the same author, usually within one pass.** The strong reading of the criteria has never survived contact with the project's own tests. The consolidated thesis therefore states *no* closure claim; it states a typed residue. This is not pessimism; it is calibration to the record.
2. **The withdrawals always came from one of three tests,** which are now part of the thesis as reading rules (below): the contrast-class test, the symmetric-standard test, and the derivation-versus-naming test.
3. **Negative and structural results have never been reversed.** Not one impossibility or counter-model in the record has been overturned by a later pass. Confidence should track this asymmetry: the thesis's *constraints* are its most secure content; its *positive completions* are its least.
4. **Overreach is bidirectional.** The record contains both "this world is the only possible one" and "modal collapse is demonstrably false", both "nothing is impossible" and "logic refutes that". A correction protocol that only polices optimism will not keep the thesis calibrated.
5. **Renaming has been the chief vehicle of regression.** Each re-assertion of an excluded claim arrived under a new label (a new functional, a new acronym, a new essence). The map in §1 and the exclusions in §6 exist so that labels cannot do this again.

### 9.3 Reading rules for any future statement of the thesis

- **Grade every claim** (E/C/H/O). A sentence without a grade is not part of the thesis.
- **Contrast-class test:** for any "why X", state what the alternatives are; if there are none well-posed, say the question is dissolved and say why; do not answer a question that has not been posed.
- **Symmetric-standard test:** any standard that excludes a rival ultimate (creativity, a free choice, a brute law) is applied, in the same words, to the thesis's own ground.
- **Derivation-versus-naming test:** "necessary", "self-grounding", "intrinsic", "the nature of the ground", "representational redundancy" perform no explanatory work until the derivation, constitution or dissolution is shown `[R1 §1]`.
- **Non-empty reading, declared:** when a criterion is weakened to admit a survivor, the survivor is advertised at the strength of the weakened criterion.
- **Admissible-family output:** where a selector is not derived, return the family and the conditions under which it collapses; never force a point with an unexplained optimizer `[WP4 §9]`.
- **Renaming test and nothing-new test** `[R5 §7.2; R3 §8.4]`: a re-description that recovers the known structure without excluding anything is not an explanation.
- **Source weight:** attribution verified; peer status stated; manuscripts labelled as such; placeholders counted as absent.

### 9.4 Pre-registered retraction triggers

- If item 8.1 fails, §4.4's premises are reported as inputs and the sentence "the public structure is not decreed" in §1 is weakened to "is conditionally derived from stated inputs".
- If item 8.2 fails, §4.5's robust-core form is withdrawn and Σ is graded O without a candidate.
- If item 8.3 fails, outcomes return to the differential column of §7 and §4.8 loses its third clause.
- If item 8.4 fails, §4.6 is withdrawn and Q is left with no derived relational content.
- If any single-scalar or essence-based completion is proposed again, it is assessed first against §6 before any other merit is considered.
