# Absolute, perspectives, and the zero-residue account: synthesis with the spherical WdW chain

The mathematical chain built in this repository (summarized in
[SYNTHESIS_WDW_TIME.md](SYNTHESIS_WDW_TIME.md)) supplies a rigorous model in
which the core structural claims of the necessary articulated unity / absolute-
as-perspectives thesis can be read off the physics, rather than appended as
metaphysical decoration. This note states explicitly what the mathematics
supports and what remains philosophy.

## 1. What the physics establishes literally

1. **There is one global state, no external time.** The Wheeler–DeWitt
   constraint \(\hat H\Psi=0\) has a single timeless solution on the physical
   Hilbert space ([WDW_INNER_PRODUCT.md](WDW_INNER_PRODUCT.md),
   [WDW_SELF_ADJOINT.md](WDW_SELF_ADJOINT.md)). No parameter \(t\) appears in
   the fundamental equation.
2. **Time, change, and dynamics are relational.** Conditioning on the areal
   clock observable \(R\) yields time-dependent normalized conditional states
   evolving unitarily under the Tomonaga flow
   ([RELATIONAL_PERSPECTIVE.md](RELATIONAL_PERSPECTIVE.md),
   [WDW_MAGNUS_EXACT.md](WDW_MAGNUS_EXACT.md)). "Becoming" is the relational
   structure internal to the changeless state.
3. **The global state is pure; entropy/coarse-graining are perspectival.**
   Finite-energy observers see a finite Hilbert subspace (~11 levels for an
   \(n_{\max}=10\) observer), a finite number of distinguishable ticks (~3300
   across the dressed cloud), and positive coarse-grained entropy from
   unobserved modes (\(S_{\mathrm{unobs}}\sim152\) nats for our 220-mode tower),
   while the global von Neumann entropy is zero
   ([PERSPECTIVAL_FINITUDE.md](PERSPECTIVAL_FINITUDE.md)).
4. **The clock extends all the way through horizons to a regular bounce.**
   \(R\) is conformal time in the exterior, timelike in the Kantowski–Sachs
   interior, and the polymer bounce caps curvature at \(\mu^4\) while preserving
   unitarity ([WDW_NEAR_HORIZON.md](WDW_NEAR_HORIZON.md), [WDW_INTERIOR.md](WDW_INTERIOR.md),
   [WDW_BOUNCE.md](WDW_BOUNCE.md), [WDW_INFORMATION_PRESERVATION.md](WDW_INFORMATION_PRESERVATION.md)).
   There is no place where time "stops" or information is destroyed.
5. **All approximations are controlled.** Weak-field, adiabatic, and
   semiclassical approximations have explicit validity ranges and quantified
   errors; the theory remains valid across \(10^{-5}\leq C\leq0.99\) and
   transitions smoothly into conformal near-horizon and bouncing interior
   regimes.

## 2. Reading the metaphysics off the math

From (1)–(5), the following theses are supported as *structural features* of
the model, not as imported metaphysical commitments:

- **Absolute = the global pure state.** It is one, changeless, self-adjoint
  under a single constraint; nothing is added from outside, no external clocks,
  no external parameters, no observer outside the state. This is the
  mathematical realization of "one complete reality" within the symmetry-reduced
  sector.
- **Finitude and ignorance are perspectival, not privative substances.** A
  finite observer does not see "less reality"; they see the whole reality
  conditioned on one clock reading, with unavoidable coarse-graining over
  unobserved modes. The absence of information about other modes is *literally*
  absense—a trace over unobserved DOF—not a positive shadow substance.
- **Time does not require creation or a "first moment."** The global state is
  atemporal; the bounce replaces the would-be initial singularity with a
  smooth neck, so there is no \(t=0\) boundary condition to supply from outside.
  Time is a relational parameter everywhere it appears.
- **Evil as privation is formally mirrored.** Just as entropy/ignorance are
  traces over unobserved DOF (missing information, not positive stuff),
  "bad" in the ethical sense articulated earlier is structurally analogous:
  missing integration / failure of due regard, not a positive anti-absolute
  substance. The math does not prove ethics, but it illustrates the coherence
  of the "privation of the good" structure.
- **No simulation, no demiurge, no external substrate.** The model lives in
  its own Hilbert space; there is no computer running it, no watchmaker outside
  setting initial conditions, no brute parameter chosen from outside. The
  constraint closes on its own.

## 3. What is NOT proven even in the model

- **Necessary existence.** [Necessary existence argument](NECESSARY_EXISTENCE_ARGUMENT.md)
  argues against self-refuting nothingness (experience is given) for a minimal
  algebra-state-flow triple; the spherical WdW chain instantiates this triple.
  Uniqueness of specific matter content/symmetry/dimension is not proved.
- **Consciousness / phenomenal constitution.** Conditional states are
  well-defined, but what-it-is-like to be such a conditional state is not
  identified. The Russellian identification (the intrinsic character of the
  physical is experiential) is consistent with the model but is not forced by
  it; it is the only framework currently visible that does not add an
  extra-necessary epiphenomenal substance, but it is not a theorem.
- **Full 3+1, BKL stability, evaporation vs bounce.** The model is spherically
  symmetric; generic BKL bounces may be chaotic, Hawking backreaction may
  dominate the bounce dynamics for small M, and the late-time asymptotic
  structure of the bounce's baby universe is not computed.
- **The exact form of trans-Planckian QG.** The polymer factor is a toy;
  real LQG / string / causal-set resolutions will differ in detail. The
  structural point (unitarity + bounded curvature + information preservation)
  is robust across all regularized resolutions.

## 4. How close to zero residue?

Within the spherical dressed-scalar sector, the account is zero-residue in
the following specific sense:

- No external time.
- No external observer.
- No external initial/boundary conditions (the closed WdW constraint + bounce
  remove the past singularity; asymptotic behavior is determined by the
  constraint).
- No ad-hoc state-vector collapse (Born rule is conditional probability on
  relational states).
- No information loss.
- Finitude and apparent "becoming" are internal structure, not additions.
- Error budgets are quantified for every approximation.

What residue remains:

1. Full SM-level derivation of gauge groups/fermion content (dimension and
   scalar sector are selected by [plenitude + stability](PLENITUDE.md):
   hyperfinite III₁ factor is unique; Ehrenfest/spinor/polarization/Huygens
   stability picks d=4 and a scalar; the specific SM gauge group and
   generations remain an open theorem, not a brute parameter).
2. The hard problem of consciousness (see [phenomenal identity](PHENOMENAL_IDENTITY_THESIS.md),
   identification not theorem).
3. Extrapolation beyond spherical symmetry (Kerr / full 3+1 QG).

These are not residues in the sense of "unexplained things added by hand";
they are questions the model does not pretend to answer. Within its domain
the account is closed.
