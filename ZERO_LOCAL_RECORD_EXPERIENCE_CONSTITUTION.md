## What this adds

The [preceding steering result](ZERO_EXPERIENCE_STEERING_NO_SIGNALLING_CONSTITUTION.md) ruled out a direct identification of a **conditional quantum expectation** with a definite, locally accessible feeling. Here is a constructive alternative: an explicit local Hamiltonian turns the relevant qubit into a correlated physical record, preserves the appropriate local no-signalling statistics, and yields signed outcomes with zero weighted mean in the stipulated singlet state.

It also yields a sharper finding about the constitutive problem:

> Even knowing the complete signed first-moment operator and the local quantum state does not determine the full distribution of possible signed values. Two distinct local response instruments can have the **same signed operator for every state**, yet different positive/negative frequencies and magnitudes.

This is important for the user's demand: the physical–experiential identity must specify actual local organization, incidence and felt quality—not just a zero expectation or a traceless operator. The construction narrows the needed identity; it does not derive it from zero. Nor does it equate a record qubit with a conscious subject.

## 1. A local quantum record generated by an explicit interaction

Keep the previous assumptions: Alice's qubit `A` and Bob's qubit `B` are in a singlet, and the conserved five-ray relation supplies a local center frame and pair/triple measurement effects. Choose the intrinsic pair direction as the local spin `z` axis. Let `R` be a physical two-state pointer initially in `|0⟩`. Define on `B⊗R`

```text
P₊ = (I+Z_B)/2,       P₋ = (I−Z_B)/2,
H_int = ħ g P₋ ⊗ (I−X_R),     g>0.
```

This operator is nonnegative. Evolving for local effective proper-time parameter `τ=π/(2g)` gives

```text
U_BR = exp(−i H_int τ/ħ)
     = P₊ ⊗ I_R + P₋ ⊗ X_R.
```

The equality follows because `(I−X)²=2(I−X)`. A positive-spin state leaves the pointer in `|0⟩`; a negative-spin state flips it to `|1⟩`. This is a local quantum nondemolition **recording interaction**, not a postulated direct feeling. For an ideal degenerate pointer `H_R=0` and `H_B=(ħω/2)Z_B`, `[H_int,H_B+H_R]=0`; the signal qubit's bare energy is conserved under the coupling. This idealization does not model the preparation, switching, stable storage or thermodynamic costs of a real measuring device.

An effective local time parameter is used to describe this physical example; it is not assumed to be a fundamental cosmic time or preferred foliation. The coupling is held constant in this idealized calculation. It is reversible: for the negative-spin branch the pointer-flip probability is `sin²(gτ)`, and it returns at `gτ=π`. A permanent memory would require additional dynamics and an environment. Energy conservation for the bare signal does not make the whole's energy zero. In fact, in the initial singlet–pointer state the expectation of this positive `H_int` is `ħg/2`.

## 2. Physical records and no-signalling

With `A,B,R` ordered in that sequence, the initial state is

```text
(|010⟩ − |100⟩)/√2.
```

At the recording time the state is

```text
(|011⟩ − |100⟩)/√2.
```

The local joint state of Bob's signal and pointer is

```text
ρ_BR = (|00⟩⟨00| + |11⟩⟨11|)/2,
ρ_R  = I/2.
```

Thus the pointer is perfectly correlated with the signal in this basis, with equal Born weights for its two record alternatives. The global state is still a superposition: unitary evolution and the reduced density matrix do **not by themselves select a single actual outcome**, settle an interpretation of quantum mechanics, or establish a conscious subject. The earlier fixed-record table shows a limited deterministic compatibility example, but this Hamiltonian has not been derived as the mechanism producing that table.

For any trace-preserving operation `Λ_A` on Alice, including her pair or triple POVM when outcomes are not communicated,

```text
tr_A[(Λ_A⊗id_BR)(ρ_ABR)] = tr_A(ρ_ABR).
```

The identity follows from the Kraus completeness relation `Σ K_i†K_i=I` and the partial trace. The local interaction and Alice's operation act on separate factors and commute. Therefore not just one mean but the **entire local quantum state of `B+R`**, and hence every local quantum measurement distribution on it, is independent of Alice's uncommunicated choice. This is an operational statement within the assumed quantum model, not a proof that every conceivable additional experiential variable obeys no-signalling.

For the symmetric conserved five-ray case, the center-frame triple has `a·n=(-1/2,1/4,1/4)`, where `a` is the pair axis. Identify the physical record value `y=+1` with `R=0` and `y=−1` with `R=1`. The exact joint Born probabilities in the triple context are

```text
                         triple outcome i
                        0          1          2
P(i, y=+1)             1/4        1/8        1/8
P(i, y=−1)             1/12       5/24       5/24
P(i)                   1/3        1/3        1/3
```

The two pointer outcomes each have marginal `1/2`. In the pair context their marginal is also `1/2`; the corresponding conditional outcomes are perfectly anticorrelated with Alice's pair result. Thus this particular recording interaction realizes the full distributions used in the preceding argument—not merely their equal means.

## 3. Which part of an experiential law can quantum no-signalling constrain?

Suppose, conditionally, that a local physical/experiential theory identifies three local outcomes: positive felt value, negative felt value and no relevant subject incidence. If their statistics are represented by local quantum effects `E₊,E₋,E_∅`, then

```text
E₊, E₋, E_∅ ≥ 0,      E₊+E₋+E_∅=I,
P(v)=tr(ρ_R E_v).
```

Because these are local quantum effects on `R`, their full distribution is independent of an uncommunicated Alice operation in the model above. The local-state assumption is a **substantive** physical–experiential hypothesis, not a theorem that all experiences are quantum measurements. It does not follow from no-signalling between only the two conserved contexts.

More generally, if an operational probability for an experience outcome is preparation-noncontextual and affine under all relevant mixtures of local qubit states, then finite-dimensional linear representation gives an effect `E_v`: positivity and normalization imply `0≤E_v≤I`. This uses preparation noncontextuality and mixture-affinity in addition to no-signalling; neither should be smuggled in as a consequence of necessity. A context-dependent or non-quantum theory must supply its own no-signalling account.

For signed values `v₊=+1`, `v₋=−1`, let

```text
V = E₊−E₋.
```

In this example `ρ_R=I/2`, so the signed unconditioned mean is

```text
Q_local = tr(ρ_R V) = tr(V)/2.
```

Thus it is zero **if and only if** `tr E₊=tr E₋`. If an actual subject occurs with probability `p_s=tr[ρ_R(E₊+E₋)]>0`, then the *conditional* mean among those incidences is also zero. This is a statement about this local mixed state and these stipulated effects, not the whole's experiential total.

As a concrete family of valid effects, for `0<r≤1` and `0≤κ≤1` choose

```text
E₊ = (r/2)(I+κZ),  E₋ = (r/2)(I−κZ),  E_∅ = (1−r)I.
```

Then `P(positive)=P(negative)=r/2` in `I/2`, no relevant incidence has weight `1−r`, and the signed mean is zero. Yet the incidence rate `r` and the correlation strength `κ` can vary independently within this family. Zero and no-signalling alone do not pick the subject occurrence or its physical response.

## 4. A sharper underdetermination: the same operator, different distributions

Consider two local response instruments on the pointer, with different signed value scales:

```text
A:  E₊=P₊,       E₋=P₋,             values (+1, −1)
B:  E₊=P₊/2,     E₋=I−P₊/2,        values (+3, −1).
```

Both have the same first-moment operator **for every pointer state**:

```text
V_A = P₊−P₋ = Z,
V_B = 3(P₊/2) − (I−P₊/2) = Z.
```

But in `ρ_R=I/2` their full distributions are

```text
A: +1 with probability 1/2; −1 with probability 1/2.
B: +3 with probability 1/4; −1 with probability 3/4.
```

Each has mean zero, but the negative-outcome fraction and second moment differ: `1/2` versus `3/4`, and `1` versus `3`. Therefore even a universal identification of a signed **mean** with `tr(ρ Z)` does not determine actual outcome frequencies, magnitudes, or suffering incidence. The instruments are physically distinct; this is not a claim that the same fully specified physical instrument produces both distributions. The point is that the shared state and first-moment operator do not select between them. Actualizing either response as felt experience is another, independent hypothesis.

Sign orientation also remains an issue: assigning the record values to `Z` or `−Z` reverses which physical record gets which sign, while preserving the local zero and the no-signalling calculation. Physical calibration could help *identify* a proposed experiential mapping, but a complete identity must explain why that mapping holds, not just relabel the records. Mathematical alternatives are not asserted to be genuinely possible complete wholes.

## 5. When does a local response become fixed?

There is a useful **conditional uniqueness** result. Suppose a two-outcome local response is represented by effects `E₊,E₋` with `E₊+E₋=I`, and independent physical–experiential evidence establishes the calibrated conditions

```text
tr(P₊ E₊)=1,        tr(P₋ E₊)=0.
```

Positivity of `E₊` and `I−E₊` forces `E₊|+⟩=|+⟩` and `E₊|−⟩=0`, hence `E₊=P₊` and `E₋=P₋`. Once this calibration and the effect representation are justified, the local response on **every** qubit state is fixed by linearity.

This separates three issues that the no-residue account must eventually unify:

1. **Physical organization:** which local system, record interaction and states exist?
2. **Experience/subject incidence:** which physical states constitute a subject and which felt qualities do they have?
3. **Actual weighting:** why does the relevant whole-level experiential aggregation use the measure and value scale that produces zero?

The calibration is not derived from zero, and it cannot be assumed simply because the two records are mathematically distinguishable. It may be discoverable through physical and first-person evidence, but one local calibration is not yet the full no-residue explanation.

## 6. What remains of the point–sphere proposal

The line from the point–sphere mathematics to this local example is now explicit:

```text
assumed CP¹ sphere → generic 2+3 conserved null rays → intrinsic center frame
→ assumed singlet and ray POVMs → local recording Hamiltonian

```

Every arrow carries stated assumptions. In particular, a local pointer signed mean is neither total energy nor felt experience; the construction does not derive the `CP¹` structure, local subsystems, interaction, preferred measurement, definite outcomes, subject, actual incidence or the global zero from the constitution of zero. It does demonstrate exactly what a successful bridge must additionally determine: **the local response instrument and its phenomenal identity, not merely an operator whose expectation cancels**.

The result does not rule out the user's proposal that the entire physical–experiential constitution is self-sufficient, nor does it claim that a brute extra selector is acceptable. It identifies which needed structures must arise intrinsically if that proposal is to succeed.
