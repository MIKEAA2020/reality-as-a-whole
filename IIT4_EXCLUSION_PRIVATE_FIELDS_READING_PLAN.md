# Fixed plan: IIT 4.0 exclusion and private nested fields

## Known before reading

The project's [private nested comparison](PRIVATE_NESTED_FIELDS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_CONTENT_COMPARISON.md) stipulates a whole-field subject and two local pair subjects without shared experience tokens; it has no independent incidence principle. Its earlier four-spin selector L is **not** IIT 4.0. Previous work inspected the IIT 4.0 abstract, selected exclusion material and tie procedure; a preliminary search now reports that the 2023 IIT paper identifies maximal cause–effect substrates using system integrated information, excludes overlapping lower-scoring candidate substrates and distinguishes the resulting structure from a scalar. Those facts and source snippets were known before this plan. The current search also located an open critique of IIT's ontology; its main text has not yet been read for this follow-up.

## Planned source and analysis scope

1. Read the accessible 2023 primary IIT 4.0 paper with special attention to phenomenal axioms, physical postulates, system integrated information, exclusion and treatment of overlapping complexes, composition and quality, grain, tied cases, practical assumptions and stated limitations. Read all returned full-text chunks if feasible; explicitly report any unavailable equations, figures, supplement or code. Do not treat this as a reproduction of its formal proofs or as computation of IIT on the four-spin model.
2. Read the accessible named ontology critique in full if feasible and accurately separate its criticism from IIT's authors' actual claims. Note version, source and limits. Do not treat a counterexample or critique as conclusive without checking its assumptions, or equate non-selected systems with physically nonexistent entities without carefully reporting IIT's specific intrinsic/extrinsic distinction.
3. Formulate IIT's actual proposed argument from definiteness to exclusion and its explanatory identity sympathetically. Distinguish experiential evidence, physical formulation, disjoint complexes, intra-complex distinctions, intersubject experience-token sharing and quality. Specify which premises deny the nested physical-support proposal P and whether comparison can be made without calculating IIT scores. Keep the simple L selector and IIT mathematically distinct.
4. Apply concrete consequences to P, exclusive L and other rivals without assigning uncomputed phi values to the existing toy. Evaluate the proposed justification for exclusion versus private distinct fields: whether definiteness of each field logically rules out an overlapping substrate; other anti-redundancy and causal arguments the primary paper actually makes; the inferential step that remains contested. Preserve that IIT is an explanatory identity hypothesis with a detailed qualitative structure, not merely a phi-to-consciousness correlation.
5. Identify ethically practical evidence and its access, physical-model, quality and computational limits; report genuine likelihood or falsification conditions only for specified packages with prospectively fixed predictions. Do not infer no experience from silence or clinical nonresponse. Do not count information-labeling or a toy score as an empirical derivation. Retain C3, B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 unless new evidence genuinely settles them.

## Deliverable

A source-grounded incidence-principle comparison with source-specific access limits, links to relevant existing audits, refreshed inventories and relevant regression checks. No new invented toy regression or clinical data acquisition is planned.
