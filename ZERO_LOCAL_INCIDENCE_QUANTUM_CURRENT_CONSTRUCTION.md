# Local subject incidence and a conserved quantum current

## Constructive result

The [last correction](ZERO_INCIDENCE_NORMALIZATION_LOCALITY_CORRECTION.md) showed why a **globally** normalized incidence share cannot simply be identified with a local experience. Here is a more specific alternative:

> If subject-incidence indicators are local, mutually commuting, and commute with the local signed current, their overlap can be normalized **at each local site**. This gives local, simultaneous subject-indexed signed operators whose weighted sum is exactly the covered physical current. When the relevant domain is fully covered and the physical currents sum to zero, the subject-indexed operators sum to zero as an operator identity.

I construct an explicit positive-energy, closed quantum hopping system with two overlapping **candidate** incidence patterns. Its physical energy-current operators are nonzero, the locally allocated subject operators sum exactly to zero, and a coherent state gives them nonzero opposite expectations. No normalization by the number of distant subjects is needed.

The premises are not derived from zero, and the labels are not established subjects or feelings. In particular, an auxiliary register is not a phenomenological identity. The construction also has a sharp quantum limitation: a physically natural local excitation-presence projector does **not** commute with the hopping energy current. In that case support-preserving subject contributions cannot, in general, reproduce the original current operator. Both the construction and its limitation are derived below.

## 1. A local operator identity

On an effective physical network, let `h_i` be a Hermitian signed current contribution at site `i`. For candidate subjects `s`, let `N_{s,i}` be local orthogonal projectors indicating a proposed incidence of `s` at `i`, with prescribed positive weights `a_s`. Assume, for this construction,

```text
[N_{s,i}, N_{t,i}] = 0,
[h_i, N_{s,i}] = 0.
```

Define the **local** incidence operator and its coverage projector

```text
M_i = Σ_s a_s N_{s,i},       Π_i = projector onto support(M_i),
```

and use the Moore–Penrose inverse `M_i⁺`. Define the subject-indexed contribution

```text
v_{s,i} = N_{s,i} M_i⁺ h_i.
```

It is Hermitian because the factors commute. It is supported on the indicated incidence subspace, and two subject indicators may be simultaneously true; these are not exclusive normalized outcome probabilities. For each site,

```text
Σ_s a_s v_{s,i} = Π_i h_i.
```

This is an operator identity. If all relevant sites are covered, `Π_i=I`; if the physical currents obey `Σ_i h_i=0`, it follows that

```text
Σ_s a_s V_s = 0,     V_s := Σ_i v_{s,i}.
```

If coverage is incomplete, the exact remainder is

```text
Σ_s a_s V_s = −Σ_i (I−Π_i) h_i.
```

Thus the unexperienced or unassigned sector does not disappear. This algebra does not establish that `N_{s,i}` denotes an actual subject, that `v_{s,i}` is felt valence, that the weights `a_s` are the actual incidence measure, or that all physical energy belongs to subjects. It conditionally states what follows **if** those physical–experiential identifications and coverage are independently established.

### Locality of the construction

Unlike the prior global-frame normalization, `M_i` depends only on the subject incidences **at site `i`**. If the operators used to define `N_{s,i}` and `h_i` are in the local algebra of a region `R_i`, then `v_{s,i}` is in that algebra too. A trace-preserving operation on a disjoint tensor factor leaves its expectation unchanged. This is the finite-model operational no-signalling statement; it requires the incidence indicators themselves to be locally constituted and physically accessible in the stated way. Merely choosing their labels in a calculation does not establish that.

In a network, `h_i` may include interactions with adjacent sites; then `R_i` includes that interaction neighborhood, not only one coordinate. The finite nonrelativistic example below does not provide relativistic microcausality, a fundamental foliation, or a complete theory of emergent spacetime.

## 2. A closed quantum transport system

Take four physical qubits in a chain, with `n_i=|1⟩⟨1|_i`, and set `ħ=1`:

```text
H = 2 Σ_i n_i + Σ_(i=0 to 2) (σ⁺_i σ⁻_(i+1) + σ⁻_i σ⁺_(i+1)),
E_i = 2 n_i,
h_i = i[H,E_i].
```

This Hamiltonian conserves total excitation number and `Σ_i E_i=2Σ_i n_i`, so

```text
Σ_i h_i = i[H,Σ_i E_i] = 0.
```

Each `h_i` is generated by adjacent hopping terms. For example `h₁` acts on physical sites `0,1,2` and commutes with all operators on site `3`; the full four-qubit calculation checks this explicitly. The interaction itself is nonzero and the local current operators are nonzero.

The full Hamiltonian is nonnegative: on each edge the hopping term is bounded below by `−(n_i+n_j)`, and the site degree in the chain is at most two. This is a closed positive-energy effective system. The operator identity for the **exchange rates** is not a claim that its energy, much less the total energy of the whole, equals zero.

## 3. Overlapping local incidence patterns

At each site add an auxiliary local register qubit `R_i`, independent of the physical transport qubits. Let `P_i=|0⟩⟨0|` on register `R_i`. Make the following **stipulated** candidate-incidence assignments, with weights `a₁=a₂=1`:

```text
             i=0    i=1    i=2    i=3
N₁,i           I      I      I      0
N₂,i           0     P₁     P₂      I
```

At sites 1 and 2 both candidate incidences can coexist. The first pattern covers sites 0–2, and the second covers site 3 and can overlap the first. All sites are covered for every register state. Because the registers are separate from the hopping qubits, their indicators commute with the physical currents. That independence is an **assumption**, not a derived property of real subjects.

The local inverse `M_i⁺` produces finite shares `1` or `1/2`, depending on the local register at that site. The signed contributions for each candidate are local operators; changing a register at site 3 cannot change the allocation at site 1. Across the entire closed system,

```text
V₁+V₂=0
```

as an operator for every register state, not merely for a particular average or a chosen remote-subject count. This operator equation is conditional on the stated physical currents and incidence law. It does not say that the universe's experiential total is zero.

### An exact nontrivial state

In the one-excitation sector, label the states by the excited site and prepare

```text
|ψ⟩ = (|site 1⟩ + i|site 2⟩)/√2.
```

Take the local registers so that the second incidence is present at site 1 and absent at site 2. For the stated Hamiltonian and sharing rule,

```text
⟨h⟩ = (0,+2,−2,0),
⟨V₁⟩=−1,       ⟨V₂⟩=+1.
```

These are nonzero **quantum expectations**, not observed pain or pleasure, and they are not an assignment of definite values to all local current operators. In this two-index example `V₂=−V₁`, but actual definite records, simultaneous subjects and the phenomenal identity are not supplied by that fact. If a physical–experiential theory later justified those identities, zero of the total would not erase a genuinely negative local experience.

## 4. A concrete obstruction when the incidence is not independent

The separate register made the compatibility premise hold. It is not automatic for an incidence tied directly to the physical carrier. In the one-excitation sector of two coupled sites, take

```text
E_A = 2 P_A,      E_B = 2 P_B,
H_int = X,        J_A = i[H_int,E_A] = 2Y,
P_A=|10⟩⟨10|,     P_B=|01⟩⟨01|.
```

Here `P_A` and `P_B` are orthogonal excitation-presence projectors and `J_A` is the genuine energy-transfer-rate operator in this stipulated model. They do not commute: `[P_A,J_A]≠0`.

Suppose one requires two Hermitian signed operators `V_A,V_B` to be strictly supported on these two mutually exclusive subspaces:

```text
V_A=P_A V_A P_A,      V_B=P_B V_B P_B.
```

Then their sum is block diagonal in the `P_A,P_B` decomposition, whereas `J_A=2Y` is off-diagonal. Thus

```text
V_A+V_B = J_A
```

is impossible under these exact assumptions. This does **not** rule out all overlapping-support models, unsharp joint instruments, a different subject criterion, or a physical–experiential identity; it rules out this particular sharp support-preserving split of this current.

Simply symmetrizing does not solve the operational problem. The two expressions `V_A={P_A,J_A}/2`, `V_B={P_B,J_A}/2` do sum to `J_A`, but neither is supported on its named presence sector. In the state with `P_A=0`, the first expression has mean zero but nonzero variance, so its sharp measurement can produce a nonzero value despite the supposed absence of A. A zero mean alone is not a valid occurrence law.

The two projectors here represent **alternative excitation locations**, not two established simultaneously existing subjects. The example is a compatibility test of a possible physical realization, not a subject census or an empirical test of consciousness.

## 5. What this means for the zero proposal

The constructive gain is a precise sufficient route that combines the previously separated demands:

```text
local incidence + compatible local signed current + complete coverage
+ an independently established physical current zero
→ local subject-indexed operators with an exact zero sum.
```

The result preserves nontrivial local contributions, and it avoids making a subject's local allocation depend on a disconnected system. It does not by itself justify the experiential identity, the incidence weights or coverage, nor show that operator expectations are actual felt values. The no-residue programme must explain why the compatible incidence structure and physical current are intrinsically the **same** physical–experiential constitution, not insert independent registers or set an accounting rule merely to obtain zero.

The transport example also makes the next physical test concrete: determine whether a plausible subject-constituting organization supplies incidence operators compatible with its relevant energy or other signed current. The most obvious excitation-presence choice fails that test. A replacement must handle noncommutation, actual records and subject plurality without forcing a preferred fundamental time or assigning phenomenology to an unexperienced environment.

The later [zero-sector memory and causal-incidence model](ZERO_SECTOR_MEMORY_CAUSAL_INCIDENCE_CONSTRUCTION.md) tests actual-record multiplicity and a directed source–receiver signed candidate in the same conserved-sphere instrument. Its branchwise signed cancellation requires a stipulated physical–experiential rule; copying and storage alone do not establish subjects or felt character.

A complementary next construction asks whether a zero condition can fix the quantum organization itself rather than merely balance a current within a given organization. The [zero-spin constitution and internal sphere](ZERO_CONSTRAINT_TO_UNIQUE_ENTANGLED_CONSTITUTION.md) derives a unique two-spin singlet from total-spin zero and, for four spins, constructs a relational qubit whose observables preserve that zero. It distinguishes these conditional mathematical results from experiential incidence and an actual conserved-record mechanism.
