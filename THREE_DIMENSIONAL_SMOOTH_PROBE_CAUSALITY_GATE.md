# A 3+1-dimensional smooth-probe gate: what survives the ultraviolet obstacle

**Subsequent restricted advance:** [the nonperturbative 3+1 continuum analysis](THREE_DIMENSIONAL_COMPLETE_INSTRUMENT_CONTINUUM.md) uses a Hilbert–Schmidt frequency-sum primitive to obtain a full Bogoliubov and energy-constrained complete-instrument limit for this specified compact-volume model. Its bounds are qualitative and apparatus/gravity limitations remain. Statements below that the full limit remains open describe this report's original scope.

## Results

The prior [1+1 two-field continuum proof](TWO_FIELD_COMPLETE_INSTRUMENT_CONTINUUM.md) cannot simply be copied to three spatial dimensions: its instantaneous pair-creation kernel is not Hilbert–Schmidt. Three further results distinguish that failure from a failure of the physical proposal:

1. For the specified smooth, spacelike-separated 3+1-dimensional **finite-cutoff** source and two-field interaction, an energy-constrained bound on the difference of the **complete operation orders** tends to zero faster than any power of the cutoff. It includes both retained fields, arbitrary probe-only readout/records and arbitrary references. This is an order comparison, **not** a cutoff-to-continuum instrument theorem.
2. Integrating the interaction against a compact smooth time switch makes the **first-order** pair-creation kernel Hilbert–Schmidt in 3D, with finite first-order vacuum energy norm. The complete first-order quadratic operator converges on a number-operator domain. This does not by itself control higher Dyson orders or establish a full unitary/channel limit.
3. A two-oscillator counterexample shows why a first-order estimate cannot simply be promoted to a uniform energy-constrained Taylor estimate: at fixed mean input energy, the trace-distance derivative can be arbitrarily large.

The underlying local bilinear field–probe model and causal factorization are established work, e.g. [Fewster and Verch](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.06512). The Hilbert–Schmidt criterion for implementability of a **complete** bosonic Bogoliubov transformation is also established; see [Torre and Varadarajan](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9811222). A Hilbert–Schmidt *first-order term* is not that criterion for the complete transformation. This report specializes these tools and supplies the stated order bound and controls; it does not claim to solve 3+1 quantum field theory or quantum gravity.

## 1. Model and geometry

Use two free massive scalar fields, system `Φ` and probe `Ψ`, on a fixed flat spacetime with spatial three-torus of side `2π`. All positive/negative Fourier modes are present, with

```text
V=(2π)³,    ω_A,k=√(|k|²+m_A²),    ω_B,k=√(|k|²+m_B²),
m_A=.5,     m_B=.7,                 k∈Z³.
```

Let `f_1` be the normalized nonnegative even compact `C∞` bump of half-width `d=.4` used in the [1+1 test](SMOOTH_SWITCHING_ZERO_MODE_LOCALITY_GATE.md). Define the source spatial smear and probe-coupling smear

```text
f(x)=f_1(x₁)f_1(x₂)f_1(x₃),
F(x)=f_1(x₁−y)f_1(x₂)f_1(x₃),       y=π/2.
```

A normalized compact even time bump `b_a`, `a=.08`, switches the system-momentum source around time zero and the bilinear coupling around `T=.3`:

```text
A_s=∫ds b_a(s)∫dx f(x) Π_A(s,x),
H_int(t)=λ b_a(t−T)∫dx F(x) Φ(t,x)Ψ(t,x).
```

The minimal circular spatial distance between their supports is `y−2d≈.770796`, while the maximal temporal separation is `T+2a=.46`; all cross-support pairs are spacelike. The system source is `U_s=exp(−igA_s)` up to an irrelevant source-switching phase. The probe is subsequently read by any declared probe-only instrument; a smooth probe-field/qubit/two-bit readout is the example from the preceding report. Controller, qubit and memory costs are not modeled.

At cutoff N retain the cubic Fourier set `|k|∞≤N` in **both** fields, embed into the common Fock space by leaving all other modes untouched, and use the exact finite-mode time-ordered interaction. The input condition is `Tr[(H_A+H_B)ρ]≤E` with arbitrary entanglement between fields and references.

## 2. Complete finite-cutoff order bound in 3+1

Let `V_N` be the finite-cutoff system–probe unitary, `x` all retained real canonical field coordinates, and

```text
V_N† x V_N=S_N x,    A_s,N=a_Nᵀx,
D_N=(S_Nᵀ−I)a_N · x = Σ_j(u_j q_j+v_j p_j),
C_N=Σ_j(u_j²/ω_j²+v_j²),    D0_N=Σ_j(u_j²/ω_j+ω_jv_j²).
```

Exactly as in the 1+1 finite-cutoff argument, the difference of the two unitary orders is a Weyl displacement generated by `D_N`, up to a phase. Any probe-only readout commutes with the system source and is a common channel postprocessing. Hence, including all retained quantum outputs, records and an arbitrary reference,

```text
order distance ≤ min{2, 2|g|[√(2E C_N)+√(D0_N/2)]}.       (1)
```

This finite-N estimate does not use a Hilbert–Schmidt bound on the instantaneous continuum kernel. It is an unhalved trace-distance supremum over the common **two-field total-input-energy** class, not an unrestricted diamond estimate.

To bound the coefficients without assuming continuum unitary convergence, define the normalized source Fourier transform `G(k)=∫f(x)e^(−ik·x)dx`, the time transform `b̂(ω)=∫b_a(s)cos(ωs)ds`, and

```text
ε_N=(1/V) Σ_{|k|∞>N} |G(k)b̂(ω_A,k)|,
m_*=min(m_A,m_B),
Q_N=|λ| exp(|λ| ||F||∞/m_*) ||F||₂ ε_N/m_B,
Ω_N=max(√(3N²+m_A²), √(3N²+m_B²)).
```

The continuum source–system-field commutator vanishes at every point in the coupling support by finite propagation. Its truncated counterpart is bounded by `ε_N`, the omitted Fourier series. Smooth compact `f` makes that tail decrease faster than every inverse power. Projected multiplication by F is uniformly bounded by `||F||∞`; Grönwall's inequality in the two-field classical energy norm bounds each finite-N symplectic flow by `exp(|λ|||F||∞/m_*)`. Differentiating the source row along this flow, as in the [1+1 order proof](LOCAL_TWO_FIELD_PROBE_AND_CUTOFF_ORDER.md), gives

```text
√C_N ≤ Q_N,      D0_N ≤ Ω_N C_N,
order distance ≤ min{2, 2|g| Q_N [√(2E)+√(Ω_N/2)]}.       (2)
```

For fixed E, smooth shapes and finite g, λ, the right side vanishes faster than every inverse power. The continuum field theory's causal commutation is a separately established local-field result; (2) does **not** establish that `V_N` or either cutoff instrument converges to that continuum operation. The cubic finite-mode smears have global tails, so nonzero cutoff effects are not superluminal signals.

## 3. Smooth switching repairs the first-order pair kernel

Let

```text
F̂(p)=(1/V)∫F(x)e^(−ip·x)dx,   |F̂(p)|≤1/V,
χ̂(ν)=∫b_a(t−T)e^(iνt)dt,
C_r=||b_a^(r)||₁ <∞,
Z_m(s)=Σ_{k∈Z³} ω_m,k^(−s).
```

For integer `r≥1`, integration by parts yields `|χ̂(ν)|≤C_r/|ν|^r` for nonzero ν. The first-order Dyson pair-creation coefficient is, up to a phase,

```text
K_kl = F̂(k+l) χ̂(ω_A,k+ω_B,l) / [2√(ω_A,k ω_B,l)].
```

Since `ω_A+ω_B≥2√(ω_Aω_B)`, for any integer `r>2`,

```text
Σ_kl |K_kl|² ≤ C_r² Z_A(r+1) Z_B(r+1) / [4·2^(2r) V²] <∞.     (3)
```

For cutoff tails where either `|k|∞>N` or `|l|∞>N`, the same bound replaces one or the other Z sum by its tail. Since `Z_m(s)<∞` for `s>3`, these tails vanish; choosing arbitrarily large r yields faster-than-polynomial decay. This establishes existence of the first-order vacuum pair vector in the free Fock space. The instantaneous kernel's divergence is not a contradiction: time integration must occur **before** taking this bound.

The energy-weighted first-order vacuum pair norm also converges. For any integer `r≥4`,

```text
Σ_kl (ω_A,k+ω_B,l)|K_kl|²
 ≤ C_r² [Z_A(r)Z_B(r+1)+Z_A(r+1)Z_B(r)]/[4·2^(2r)V²] <∞.  (4)
```

At fixed finite cutoff this sum is the `λ²` coefficient of the vacuum free-energy change. Its continuum limit is a finite **first-order-response energy norm**; without higher-order control it is not a full nonperturbative energy or apparatus-work budget.

The integrated mixed creation–annihilation kernel has entries

```text
M_kl=F̂(k−l) χ̂(ω_A,k−ω_B,l)/[2√(ω_A,kω_B,l)]
```

(up to phase conventions). In one-particle notation it is a time integral of `ω_A^(−1/2) F ω_B^(−1/2)/2` conjugated by free phase rotations. It is bounded, and the norm of its cutoff tail is no greater than

```text
(||F||∞/2)[1/√(ω_A,N+1 m_B)+1/√(m_A ω_B,N+1)],
ω_X,N+1=√((N+1)²+m_X²).
```

Therefore the pair and mixed kernels together define a symmetric first-order quadratic operator on the finite-particle core, with cutoff convergence in the graph norm of total particle number. This is a first-order operator statement, **not** a statement that the exact 3D interaction unitary is differentiable in an energy-constrained channel norm.

## 4. A counterexample to uniform first-order reasoning

Consider just two oscillators, with free energy `H₀=N_A+N_B` and quadratic generator `T=a†b†+ab`. For `n≥3`, let

```text
p=1/n,     |ψ_n>=√(1−p)|0,0>+√p|n,n>,     <H₀>=2.
```

Here `<T>=0`, and the number states reached by T are distinct, giving

```text
Var(T)=(1−p)+p[(n+1)²+n²],
(d/dλ)|_{λ=0+} ||e^(−iλT)|ψ_n><ψ_n|e^(iλT)−|ψ_n><ψ_n|||₁
    =2√Var(T) → ∞.
```

Thus an all-state **uniform linear-in-λ trace-norm bound** cannot be deduced from a fixed mean energy alone, even in this two-mode quadratic example. At `n=1,000,000` the derivative is about `2828.43` while mean energy is exactly 2. This is a counterexample to a tempting proof strategy, not a no-go for energy-constrained continuity, for the specific 3D channel, or for a result under a stronger energy-moment assumption. A successful full proof may use different estimates, a low-energy projection and a non-linear modulus, or establish the required complete Bogoliubov and phase convergence.

## 5. Independent controls

The [verifier](verification/three_dimensional_smooth_probe_gate.py) performs full three-dimensional finite-mode calculations with both sine and cosine modes. It independently integrates the inverse classical symplectic response, cross-checks that method against the earlier full-matrix 1D calculation, and checks a tighter ODE tolerance. In the separated 3D example, the E=100 complete-order **upper bound** at N=1,2,3 is approximately `.2204, .3161, .4935`: these values are nonmonotone and do not themselves demonstrate asymptotic convergence. At N=3 the exact vacuum distance **before readout**, with both joint fields retained, is approximately `.03622`; it is not a two-bit record TV.

A source moved to the coupling's spatial center is no longer spacelike separated; at N=2 its unread joint-field vacuum order distance is approximately `.19928`, a nonzero positive control. This is a numerical model check, not an empirical observation.

For the first-order pair kernel, the finite cubic-cutoff sums and energy norms are recorded through N=6. Numerically evaluated time-derivative majorants also exceed the corresponding finite sums, but are extremely loose and not interval-certified. The `k+l=0` diagonal comparison through spherical cutoff N=96 shows the instantaneous squared-norm lower sum increasing to `.004856`, whereas the switched diagonal sum is approximately `.0006321`. The analytic estimates—not these finite sums—establish convergence of the **full switched first-order** kernel. The same script checks the fixed-mean-energy derivative example. Results are floating-point controls, not interval certificates; pinned dependencies are in `verification/requirements-field-probe.txt`.

## 6. Remaining gates

The next mathematical obligation is a 3+1 cutoff-to-continuum convergence result for the **complete interacting instrument**, not just its first-order operator or same-cutoff order difference. Establish full Bogoliubov implementability and phase/channel convergence under a stated energy domain, or give a precise obstruction. Then price a real local probe readout and record, control local stress-energy and switching work, and address dynamical gravity/refoliation. None of this establishes an experiential subject, necessary actuality, or a complete self-sufficient account of reality.
