# Supporting audit and battery protocol

This supporting document records the provenance, historical corrections, source links, bounded results and regression checks behind the standalone [framework](CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_BATTERY_DOSSIER.md). It is an audit and test protocol, not the main presentation of the theory. The framework is provisional and incomplete; the checks do not establish a zero-residue account or a solution to quantum gravity. The externally supplied v1 foundation-battery report is addressed in a separate [independent spot check](FOUNDATION_BATTERY_V1_INDEPENDENT_ASSESSMENT.md). Subsequent research on subject individuation is recorded separately in the [subject factorization gate](SUBJECT_CONSTITUTION_FACTORIZATION_GATE.md), its [weak-coupling and partial-observation extension](WEAK_COUPLING_PARTIAL_OBSERVATION_SUBJECT_GATE.md), and the [joint-access versus common-source decision gate](JOINT_ACCESS_COMMON_SOURCE_DECISION_GATE.md); none revises the frozen v1 framework.

**Audit scope:** the consolidation draws on the current source inventory and the linked audits and representative technical documents. The accompanying [source register](FRAMEWORK_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) inventories the repository's documents, original sources and verification assets for traceability. Inventorying all files is **not** a claim that every historical derivation, line, cited paper, source file or code path was independently reverified. Inspect the linked individual proofs and their limitations before relying on them.

## Reading conventions

- **D — datum:** experience occurs; other empirical claims require their own evidence and uncertainty. The occurrence of experience does not establish the scope of any other subject or the intrinsic nature of all reality.
- **H — working hypothesis or user-adopted premise:** a target the framework seeks to explain and test, not an established theorem.
- **C — conditional result:** follows under the stated assumptions, which remain open if not independently supported.
- **M — bounded model/check:** a stipulated mathematical example or executable test; passing it does not establish the physical or phenomenal interpretation.
- **E — empirical result:** an observed result in the cited experiment's actual domain, not an automatic metaphysical conclusion.
- **O — open:** a bridge, empirical result, physical derivation or justification not yet supplied.
- **R — retracted/superseded:** a previous stronger claim that must not be carried forward as a conclusion.

## 1. The candidate in one paragraph

> **H:** Reality is one self-sufficient, necessarily actual whole, timeless at the fundamental level, whose intrinsic nature is experiential and which is internally differentiated into genuinely distinct finite subjects and their experiences. Fundamental spacetime and mass are not assumed. Genuine possibility and actuality are identical in the user's strongest sense: if genuinely possible, actual; if not actual, impossible. There is no external creator or selector and no process by which merely possible things are made actual. The aim is to make the whole and its determinations completely intelligible, while accounting for our genuinely suboptimal universe and real suffering.

This is a positive **candidate**, not a derivation. “One whole” does not mean one universal experiencing subject. The current candidate relaxes the earlier universal-subject/agency target; local subjects and local agency remain. A non-agentive whole must not be relabeled “universal consciousness” to preserve the old target by definition. Plural subjects are posited in the candidate, not proved merely by different perspectives, multiple reports or operational observer labels.

The earlier requirement **essence = existence** remains a desired account of self-sufficiency: actuality would be intrinsic to the nature of the whole. It is not established by defining a nature as necessary. Likewise “self-actualization,” if used, means *no dependence on an external actualizer* in this timeless proposal, not a transition from nonexistence in external time. Local processes of coming-to-be remain possible within whatever account of emergent local time is established.

**Not a literal simulation framework:** Bostrom was a starting point for the question of actuality, not a premise that aliens, computers, simulators, simulated subjects, a cosmic programmer or a base world exist. A creator external to a universe, if proposed, would itself be part of reality as a whole and require explanation. Literal computational simulations are neither required nor assumed as an optional ingredient of the candidate. For the correction see [self-existent plenitude](SELF_EXISTENT_PLENITUDE_PLURALITY_CORRECTION.md).

## 2. Intelligibility, modality and nonexistence

### 2.1 What the modal commitment says

Use `P(d)` for genuine possibility and `A(d)` for actuality at the **specified scope**. The working thesis is `P(d) ↔ A(d)`. A coherent mathematical description or conceivability does not itself establish `P(d)`; the thesis does not say every consistent description is a concrete world. There is no ontological stock of merely possible worlds or choice event added between the sides.

A useful but conditional formalization treats global propositions with classical modal operators. If the schema `◇φ → φ` applies to every global proposition, including `¬φ`, then by contraposition and `□φ ≡ ¬◇¬φ`:

```
φ → □φ.       [C: classical logic, duality, and unrestricted global schema]
```

With reflexivity, `φ ↔ □φ`. This is **global modal collapse**, a consequence of that formalization, not a proof the formalization is correct. If `E` means “some experience occurs” and `E` is an actual global fact, the schema conditionally yields `□E`. It does not determine which subjects or qualities occur, show why the schema holds, or prove experience from logical truth alone. A possible-world semantic countermodel is a test of a proposed inference, not proof that the countermodel is a genuinely possible whole. Under unrestricted global collapse, earlier two-world countermodels with differing rigid subject-existence facts cannot be used without qualification. See [foundation gates](PLENITUDE_FOUNDATION_GAPS_AND_SYMMETRY.md) and [nonexistence/truth audit](NOTHINGNESS_TRUTH_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_NECESSITY.md).

### 2.2 Local variety versus global incompatibility

Different local experiences or histories can coexist when their subjects, regions and relations are properly distinguished. This does not make `φ` and `¬φ` true of the **same global whole in the same respect**. If both were genuinely globally possible, strong possibility = actuality and classical consistency would conflict. The theory still owes a non-circular account of genuine possibility, admissible differentiation and why incompatible global alternatives are not genuinely possible. “Everything consistent is actual” and “nothing was selected” do not by themselves settle that account.

The truthmaker route is also conditional: a necessary truth plus the independently supported premise that it must have a concrete experiential ground in every genuine possibility can imply necessary experience. The substantive truthmaker premise carries the existential burden; the truth of a mathematical sentence alone does not establish a concrete subject. **O:** no non-circular derivation of why there is an actual experiential whole, why its intrinsic nature and structure are what they are, or why essence and existence are identical has been completed.

## 3. The whole, subjects and experienced quality

### 3.1 Distinctions the account must keep

- **Whole:** the encompassing actuality, hypothesized to be self-sufficient and intrinsically experiential.
- **Subject:** a genuinely distinct experiencer; neither a coordinate position, abstract perspective, causal module nor mathematical label proves one exists.
- **Episode/content:** what is experienced, its valence, its bounded unity and its relation to other episodes.
- **Record/report:** physical evidence about an episode; valuable but fallible and not automatically identical to the episode.
- **Constitution/persistence rule:** what makes a subject this subject through local change, division, merging or deletion; not supplied by naming a boundary.

A fully specified account must show how the same whole contains real local experiencers without assuming their plurality follows from counting viewpoints. It must say whether subjects overlap, what makes an episode belong to a subject and why a particular structure is a particular felt quality. No claim that our formal variables exhaust experiential reality follows from representing them in a tuple.

### 3.2 What the finite gates actually show

- **M:** In the [triangle-plus-isolated graph](PLENITUDE_FOUNDATION_GAPS_AND_SYMMETRY.md), symmetry fixes equal weights *within* each orbit but not between orbits, and permits several subject selectors. Alternative invariant partitions give different stipulated subject counts while preserving the same toy report table. This is not evidence for real subjects or a no-go theorem for every richer theory.
- **M:** In the [four-spin coupled model](COUPLED_DYNAMICS_SUBJECT_BOUNDARY_GATE.md), two rules on the same stochastic dynamics disagree about one versus two subjects in a weak-coupling regime, but have the same physical outputs by construction. Identical kernels and report laws give identical finite adaptive observation histories; only theories with a sufficiently specified empirical/phenomenal bridge can be discriminated by those observations.
- **M:** The [causal-influence candidate](CAUSAL_INFLUENCE_SUBJECT_CONSTITUTION_GATE.md) replaces an arbitrary numerical threshold with a defined interventional cohesion score, but its scoring, unit grain, exclusion and interpretation are extra assumptions. At a symmetric parameter value six candidate subsets tie. [The tie/persistence audit](SUBJECT_TIES_PERSISTENCE_REDESCRIPTION_GATE.md) shows a restricted three-node symmetry obstruction to simultaneously selecting a nonempty, disjoint, invariant set of specified top maxima; the obstruction is not universal. For a symmetric two-to-one subject matching, strict identity and reflection symmetry do not select exactly one predecessor without another distinction. A proposed causal redescription must preserve the admissible interventions, not merely relabel states.
- **Prior art:** [IIT 4.0](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465) uses its own intrinsic-information, integration and exclusion machinery and has an explicit tie procedure. Our toy cut score is **not IIT** and does not refute or validate it. See the earlier source for its precise handling.

**O:** no rule has yet shown that our actual physical organization constitutes these rather than other subjects, explains their persistence, or gives the particular qualitative character of experience.

### 3.3 Discomfort and the user's misalignment idea

The hypothesis that a relevant misalignment is discomfort is retained as a testable **candidate**, not reduced to a slogan. [The phase-frustration study](VIBRATIONAL_DISSONANCE_EXPERIENCE_TEST.md) distinguishes total mismatch, unavoidable frustration, excess mismatch and dissipation. In its antiphase triangle the minimum frustration is `3k/2` while the gradient-dissipation proxy is zero at equilibrium. So those measures are not interchangeable. This is not a result about fundamental strings or a proof that frustration is pain.

A test must prespecify the relevant subject, interacting components and preferred offsets; independently estimate them; control stimulus intensity, attention and context; use anchored unpleasantness ratings and held-out comparisons; and test competing fixed maps. Existing cited EEG studies do not directly measure this frustration variable or establish its phenomenal identity. The [OPH alignment analysis](OPH_BOOK_EXPERIENCE_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT.md) supplies a related but distinct candidate and a conflict control.

## 4. Quantum observations, records and probability

The framework must recover tested quantum predictions, not assume that plurality or experience selects a branch. The finite Born-rule reconstruction in [operational probability](OPERATIONAL_PROBABILITY_RECONSTRUCTION.md) derives a positive linear state representation **after** assuming a unital C*-effect structure, normalized noncontextual effect probabilities and additivity. It does not derive those premises from the absolute or supply a subject measure.

The [one-friend gate](SUBJECT_IDENTITY_LOCAL_FRIENDLINESS_TEST.md) uses a specified Bell/memory circuit with correlations `(3/5,3/5,4/5,−4/5)` and `S=14/5`; a qubit memory is not a human subject. Subject plurality does not by itself fix a definite friend-event through reversal or evade the additional Local Friendliness assumptions. In a restricted [record-complementarity model](REVERSIBLE_FRIEND_RECORD_COMPLEMENTARITY.md), accessible untouched which-record information trades against later interference. [Sequential history](TEMPORARY_SUBJECT_RECORD_HISTORY_GATE.md) and [collapse-identifiability](CONSCIOUSNESS_COLLAPSE_IDENTIFIABILITY_GATE.md) audits identify other assumptions and confounds; they neither prove collapse nor show consciousness requires an indelible record.

For actual subject-locations with a justified measure `μ` and evidence `E`, a possible self-location rule is `Cr(Q|E)=μ(Q∩E)/μ(E)`, where the denominator is defined and positive. This is uncertainty **within** an already actual whole, not an ontological draw or a process that makes a world exist. **O:** subject reference class, multiplicity, evidence, inter-orbit weights and infinite limits are not derived. Equal counting is a further assumption; different infinite cutoffs can disagree. Bostrom's original simulation-versus-base trilemma and its numerical population formula do **not** carry over automatically to this nonliteral framework. Sources: [Bostrom](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/), his [population-formula correction](https://simulation-argument.com/patch.pdf), [corrected candidate](SELF_EXISTENT_PLENITUDE_PLURALITY_CORRECTION.md).

## 5. Timelessness, local clocks and gravity

The proposal does not posit an external time for the whole. It must nevertheless explain the actual local ordering, clock readings, records and experienced succession found in our world.

- **C/M:** [The finite relational/modular model](RELATIONAL_MODULAR_CLOCK.md) has a stationary constrained history with changing clock-conditioned states. Its Gibbs marginal gives modular-flow agreement after a stated rescaling; a non-Gibbs counterexample shows this does not follow automatically. The constraint, state, clock and calibration are supplied. Page–Wootters and thermal time are established prior art.
- **C/M:** A [regulated scalar-clock FRW model](FRW_MODULAR_CLOCK.md), [clock-switching checks](CLOCK_CHOICE_EQUIVALENCE.md), turning-clock/record examples and the [gravitating clock benchmark](TIME_DECISIVE_TEST_GRAVITY_RECORD.md) handle restricted cases, not the full gravitational multiple-choice/refoliation problem or a fundamental clock.
- **E:** Barontini's cold-atom internal entropy-clock work is an important experimental analogue and constraint on the programme; it is not a universal external clock or quantum-gravity derivation. The specific toy entropy laws and regulator assumptions are not established as that experiment's dynamics. See [comparison](COLD_ATOM_CLOCK_COMPARISON.md) and [audit](COLD_ATOM_ENTROPIC_CLOCK_AUDIT.md).
- **R:** Earlier claims that the problem of time, type III₁ classification, Shlaer reduction, bounce/singularity question or UV completion had been solved are withdrawn. Finite spectra do not establish an infinite-dimensional type; a chosen modular/proper-time identification is not its derivation. See [time evidence audit](TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md).

The gravity track contains a classical Einstein–matter action with local reference fields and a small-amplitude initial-data existence argument, as well as separate linearized and quantum-field benchmarks. Their assumptions and observables differ. They are not a completed local quantum gravitational detector or proof of spacetime emergence. A smooth compact-torus vacuum TT perturbation has a stated second-order obstruction; the matter-coupled family has different conditions. See [matter detector](RELATIONAL_MATTER_DETECTOR_GRAVITY.md), [torus gravity](TORUS_SECOND_ORDER_GRAVITY_MATTER.md), and [field-record embedding](QUANTUM_FIELD_RECORD_EMBEDDING.md).

**O:** common physical constraints, normalizable/reference states, clock/rod preparation, local detector, backreaction, continuum/refoliation control, laboratory calibration and emergent geometry have not been derived together from the experiential whole.

## 6. Cutoffs, locality and the common physical interface

In the specified [one-dimensional field record test](CROSS_FRAMEWORK_CAUSALITY_RECORD_TEST.md), a finite mode cutoff produces an order-dependent record distribution even for nominally disjoint smears: TV `≈0.04960` at the cited small cutoff. In that model this is a regulator-induced artifact, **not** a prediction of superluminal signalling; the continuum limit and a sine-inclusive control are separately discussed.

[The full-instrument bound](CUTOFF_INSTRUMENT_UNIFORMITY.md) gives a restricted energy-constrained comparison of the **two operation orders at a given cutoff**, including retained records and reference entanglement. It does not yet prove that either cutoff instrument converges to a specified continuum instrument. The cutoff-to-continuum common input/output embeddings, justified energy domain, local physical probes and full apparatus costs remain open. The quoted initial field-energy proxy excludes switching work, control/memory cost, renormalized stress and gravitational response; it is not a complete backreaction budget.

The targeted [FloatingPragma assessment](FLOATINGPRAGMA_FRAMEWORK_GAP_ASSESSMENT.md) pins OPH repository commit `98391cb29e965cc7338acf5852f6b95b15c27f3c`. Its finite instrument calibration and newer capacity-restriction and principal-cone interfaces are useful **conditional** tools. We independently checked the stated hypergeometric arithmetic, not the full OPH repository or Lean. A kinetic-energy-only low-energy subspace need not be finite rank, so one cannot automatically apply a finite-dimensional probe certificate to our full L3 problem. OPH's own pinned ledger leaves the same-source physical clock, interacting QFT, particle, gravity and empirical joins open.

## 7. Empirical consciousness programme

Experience is a valid datum; a report is a fallible communication of that datum, and a neural or eye-tracking decoder is not automatically experience. Proposed intervention tests must compare precisely defined rivals while preserving the nominated physical basis, removing relevant distributed routes and old records, and independently calibrating the experiential assay. A failed physical gate or unvalidated assay gives **inconclusive**, not a win for the favored theory.

[The random rivalry audit](RIVALRY_RANDOM_AUDIT_EXPERIENCE_BRIDGE.md) proposes frozen decoder–report error `d`, independently bounded report–experience error `r`, and physical-gate error `η`. Under its explicit hypotheses, a sufficient separation of two competing target predictions is `η_M+η_D+d_M+d_D+r_M+r_D < 1`. Its synthetic numerical example is not a human result, and report agreement alone does not establish `r`. The [three-probe analysis](EXPERIENCE_ASSAY_LATENT_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) shows how a latent variable can be identified under assumptions while its identity with phenomenal experience remains underdetermined. The [V4/FEF study audit](V4_FEF_ORIENTATION_GATE.md) reports observed neural effects but finds that the necessary joint subject/assay/intervention gates are not established.

The 2025 [COGITATE collaboration](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1) tested specified predictions of IIT and GNWT and challenged aspects of both; it did not prove or refute this metaphysics. OPH's self-reading identity is another hypothesis, not direct evidence. Empirical tests of local consciousness mechanisms can constrain components while leaving ultimate ontologies underdetermined.

## 8. Non-optimality, imperfection and value

The user adopts both `perfect whole → best universe` and `our universe is not best`. By modus tollens the whole is **not perfect in the sense of that adopted bridge**. This is a conditional philosophical conclusion, not an independent proof of the bridge or of a quantitative value ordering. Do not retain whole perfection as merely open while accepting both premises.

A better genuinely possible **local universe** need not be a better genuinely possible **entire whole**. If the better local alternative is genuinely possible under the strong identity, it must be actual at an appropriately compatible local scope; that location, its genuine possibility and the comparison are not established merely by describing it. Real suffering is not made unreal, justified or necessary by explaining its place. No aggregate moral functional, “privation” label, stable fixed point or repair objective automatically establishes that an injury is good or that the whole is perfect. See [whole implications](NONOPTIMAL_WORLD_WHOLE_IMPLICATIONS.md) and [privation stress test](PRIVATION_STRESS_TEST.md).

## 9. Seven acceptance criteria

| Criterion | Demand on the current candidate | Status |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Experience | Treat first-person occurrence as real; preserve finite subjects and their contents. | Datum plus unproved constitution/subject rules. |
| C2 Non-optimal universe | Respect real suffering, the adopted bestness bridge and non-perfect whole. | Conditional implication valid; detailed moral/comparative account open. |
| C3 Zero-residue intelligibility | Explain existential, modal, structural and **experiential** facts without an external brute selector. | Target unmet; necessary actuality, quality and identity are not derived. |
| C4 Predictive power | Make discriminating, prespecified predictions with valid failure criteria. | Local rival tests proposed; no uniquely confirmed whole-framework prediction. |
| C5 Physics | Recover tested quantum/relativistic physics, emergent spacetime and local clocks. | Separate restricted models; no complete common derivation. |
| C6 Consciousness science | Confront neural, behavioral and first-person evidence and calibration confounds. | Specific gates and observations; no established general phenomenal map. |
| C7 Elegance/parsimony | Minimize unexplained independent rules while satisfying the other criteria. | One whole may be economical, but unspecified subject/quality/measure maps count as real costs; no unique winner proved. |

C3 has structural/existential and experiential aspects: a correct structural formula is not automatically an explanation of what a quality feels like, and direct acquaintance does not automatically determine its physical constitution.

## 10. Supersession and non-claims

1. **Universal subject:** earlier [seven-criteria synthesis](SEVEN_CRITERIA_EXPERIENTIAL_SYNTHESIS.md), [nonoptimal-world revision](NONOPTIMAL_WORLD_REVISED_FRAMEWORK.md), and other whole-first texts retain one universal consciousness as an earlier target. The current **plural-subject** hypothesis supersedes that feature; nonagency does not meet the original agentive-universal-subject target.
2. **Perfection:** under the user's adopted bridge and non-best-universe premise, the whole is non-perfect. Earlier suggestions that whole perfection remains open *under those premises* are superseded; a better possible totality remains a separate question.
3. **Actualization/simulation:** no literal simulation or creator is a premise. The mandatory internal-simulation N6 in the earlier [necessary-plenitude simulation model](NECESSARY_PLENITUDE_PLURAL_SUBJECTS_SIMULATION.md) is withdrawn; its arithmetic is only an optional conditional comparison. Do not insert a temporal possibility-to-actuality mechanism.
4. **Closed frameworks:** [zero-residue retraction](ZERO_RESIDUE_CLAIM_CORRECTION.md), [time audit](TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md) and [WdW corrections](WDW_INTERFACE_CORRECTIONS.md) override historical claims of solved necessity, unique physics, full time resolution, UV completion or exact false oscillator links. A verifier that assigns `True` or aggregates exit codes is not a proof of those claims.
5. **Local mathematics:** new bounded checks can remain valid even when a historical interpretation is withdrawn. Read their own assumptions, source version and failure controls rather than treating file titles such as “closed” or “complete” as evidence.

Peer/literature review and real data are not replaced by an LLM battery.

### Executable regression subset

[`verification/framework_battery.py`](verification/framework_battery.py) runs 21 selected existing model verifiers; its [receipt](verification/framework_battery.json) gives individual exit statuses. It explicitly excludes historical scripts that label stipulated booleans or other scripts' exit codes as closure. A passing run means **only that the selected scripts ran successfully**; it is not an independent audit of their proofs, a test of every file, a measured experiment, or a verdict on B1–B12.

One clock check requires NumPy. For a reproducible local environment:

```bash
python -m venv .venv
. .venv/bin/activate
python -m pip install -r verification/requirements-framework-battery.txt
python verification/framework_battery.py
```

The first run in the present environment reported the NumPy-dependent clock check failed because NumPy was not installed; after installing the pinned requirement in a temporary location and rerunning, all 21 scripts exited successfully. This dependency issue was not a physical failure or an evidence update.

## 12. Dependency order and next work

1. **Foundation:** propose an intrinsic explanatory principle that can support actual experience and strong possibility = actuality without presupposing a creator, unjustified truthmaker, or its own conclusion. Check global consistency and alternative formal models.
2. **Subjects and quality:** compare explicit constitution/persistence rules on the same physics; address ties, admissible redescriptions and empirically anchored experiential content. Investigate the user's misalignment idea without equating it to an unmeasured proxy.
3. **Physics and time:** specify one source, states, clocks, interventions and records. Close the complete-instrument continuum gate before claiming the common quantum/gravity/emergent-spacetime bridge.
4. **Self-location:** only once subjects and evidence are specified, justify a measure and handle infinite limits; do not treat Bostrom's literal counting formula as the answer.
5. **Integration and adversarial review:** check the same candidate against non-optimality, actual observations, alternative theories and all seven criteria. Record what fails or remains underdetermined.

These tracks constrain one another, but no dependency diagram proves that a final theory exists or will be found. The result of this consolidation is a **clear, auditable starting point for a battery run**, not a claim that the substantive gaps have been solved.
