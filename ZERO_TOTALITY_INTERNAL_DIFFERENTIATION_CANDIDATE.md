# Can one relational constitution produce its own nonzero differences?

## The next constructive step

**Orientation continuation:** [intrinsic symmetry and organization](ZERO_TOTALITY_INTRINSIC_ORIENTATION_SELECTION.md) adds a permutation-symmetric term that conditionally reduces the continuous minima to one permutation orbit, and tests when that formal orbit might or might not represent one actual physical–experiential organization.

The [stationary-response model](ACTUAL_EXPERIENCE_VARIATIONAL_ZERO_CANDIDATE.md) produced zero-sum local responses, but needed unequal reference values as inputs. Can we remove those **fixed unequal references** and have nonzero differences arise within one symmetric constitution?

A finite candidate shows how this could work conditionally. Replace the fixed references with variables governed by the same functional, and give the relationally uniform configuration an instability stabilized by a higher-order term. Under an explicit parameter condition, every global minimum is internally differentiated; a corresponding signed response has zero total and nonzero local values. No externally specified list of unequal references, statistical ensemble, external creator or external time is required *within the model*.

This is not yet a derivation from zero's nature. The functional, its instability and coefficients, the actual minimum principle, the domain and its experiential interpretation are all specified as assumptions. Moreover, the differentiated minima are not unique. The example does not establish that a mathematical degree of freedom is an actual subject or feeling, nor that the universe has this energy functional.

## 1. One internal functional, not fixed unequal references

Take three coordinates x and three coordinates y. In a potential physical–experiential interpretation these would be two aspects of one internal organization; at this stage they are formal variables, not independently established experiences. Define

```text
xbar = (x_1+x_2+x_3)/3,
z_i  = x_i-xbar,
r²   = sum_i z_i²,

F(x,y) = 1/2 sum_i (x_i-y_i)²
       + 1/2 sum_(i<j) (x_i-x_j)²
       + 1/2 sum_(i<j) (y_i-y_j)²
       - (mu/2) r² + (lambda/4) (r²)²,
```

with `lambda>0`. All terms use internal differences or deviations from an internal mean. A joint shift `x_i->x_i+c`, `y_i->y_i+c` leaves F unchanged. The functional is also invariant under permutations of the three indices. Unlike the preceding toy model, y is varied along with x; no unequal vector a is fixed by hand.

We **assume** that the relevant actual configuration is a global minimum of this F, modulo its common-shift degeneracy. Neither an actual minimum principle nor this F follows from `Q=0`. Nor is F identified with the total physical energy of reality.

## 2. Exact minimization

For fixed x, the y-dependent part is strictly convex. Its unique minimum satisfies

```text
y_i = xbar + z_i/4,
q_i = x_i-y_i = 3 z_i/4.
```

Consequently `sum_i q_i=0` for every such y-minimized x, while `q` is nonzero exactly when `r>0`. The individual q-values need not occur in equal-and-opposite pairs. The zero is also visible by summing the y-stationarity equations: the derivatives of the y-difference term cancel, leaving `sum_i (x_i-y_i)=0`.

Substitution gives the reduced functional

```text
F_min_y(r) = (15/8 - mu/2) r² + (lambda/4) r⁴.
```

Since `r²>=0` and `lambda>0`, its global minima are:

- If `mu <= 15/4`, `r=0` and `q_i=0` for all i.
- If `mu > 15/4`, every global minimum has

```text
r² = (mu-15/4)/lambda > 0,
q_i = 3 z_i/4,
sum_i q_i=0,       sum_i q_i² = 9 r²/16 > 0.
```

With a nonzero finite list and zero sum, both signs necessarily occur. Thus **given this particular functional and the strict parameter inequality**, differentiated, balanced local responses follow at every global minimum. The uniform configuration still exists as a stationary point in the unstable regime, but it is not a global minimum. The strict inequality is the step that rules out the uniform minimum; zero total alone does not.

The common origin is undetermined because of the joint shift. It can be set to zero as a mathematical choice of representative without implying that physical differences vanish. This is not, by itself, evidence that joint shifts are gauge redundancies in nature.

## 3. An exact example and a test for selection

Let `lambda=1` and `mu=53/12`. Then `(mu-15/4)/lambda=2/3`. One zero-mean minimum is

```text
x = (2/3,-1/3,-1/3),
y = (1/6,-1/12,-1/12),
q = (1/2,-1/4,-1/4),    sum q=0,
F = -1/9.
```

There is no one-to-one permutation exchanging each q with its negative. As in the previous report, `F` itself is not zero merely because `sum q=0`; adding an arbitrary constant to make F vanish would not identify its zero with physical energy or explain its value.

Another minimum has `x'=(-2/3,1/3,1/3)` and `y'=x'/4`. It has the opposite q and the same F. The minima form a continuous set of orientations in the two-dimensional zero-mean subspace, besides the common-shift freedom. Thus the functional fixes a nonzero **magnitude** in the unstable regime, but does not by itself choose a unique labelled organization or a unique sign assignment.

Whether transformations among these minima are redundancies, actual symmetries relating distinct configurations, or differently experienced organizations depends on a further physical theory and subject-incidence account. A global symmetry is not automatically a gauge equivalence. It would be circular to declare all differences representational solely to remove the selection question. Nor does the model establish that all orientations are actual: the user's possibility–actuality thesis would need a justified domain and scope.

## 4. The gain and the remaining assumption

The improvement is precise: the previous unequal references a are no longer separately fixed; y is determined relationally with x. The model's assumed homogeneous functional nevertheless contains the necessary structure for differentiation. The nonzero q is not simply written into the minimum conditions as `q=(1/2,-1/4,-1/4)`: its magnitude follows from the assumed coefficients, while its orientation remains underdetermined.

But the decisive assumption has moved to the **negative quadratic relational term and its coefficient**. If `mu` is at or below the threshold, the same family has only a uniform global minimum. Thus a formal symmetry-breaking mechanism is not a reason that the actual whole must lie in the differentiated regime, and `sum q=0` does not select that regime. The quartic term and `lambda>0` stabilize the finite model; neither coefficient is derived from zero, from experience or from gravitational physics here. The negative term is a feature of an illustrative functional, not a demonstrated negative physical energy or a source of suffering.

For fixed positive lambda, any desired response magnitude can be reproduced by choosing a suitable mu. So independently fixing both coefficients and the functional is essential; choosing them after observing the desired difference would merely fit it. Even if their values were independently fixed, the model would still require an account of the particular actual orientation, unless the intended ontology genuinely makes it unnecessary.

## 5. Can actual experience help?

Actual experienced contrast is a legitimate starting datum, as developed in [experience to constitution](ZERO_TOTALITY_EXPERIENCE_TO_CONSTITUTION.md). Suppose, as an **additional bridge**, that the occurrence of some particular experienced contrast entails `r>0` in this very model, and that its actual configuration is a global minimum. Within the declared family, the exact minimization then implies `mu>15/4` and hence nonzero zero-sum q.

This is an abductive/conditional constraint on the model: it tells us which parameter regime could accommodate the stipulated datum. It does not derive the parameter or qualitative character from zero; nor does every real contrast automatically correspond to r, or to negative and positive valence. A jointly experienced light–dark contrast, for example, is not automatically a pair of pleasure and pain. An independently justified physical–experiential identity is needed.

A different bridge would identify q's positive and negative components with felt positive and negative aspects. If that identity and actual incidence were established, the model could describe actual suffering while its global signed contribution balances. No positive term erases or morally justifies suffering. Mixed or neutral experiences, separate subjects and their boundaries need further specification; the three indices need not be three subjects, and no universal observer is presumed.

## 6. What remains for complete intelligibility?

To approach the user's full claim, the candidate must show—not merely name—why one self-subsistent zero nature entails or constitutes:

1. the actual domain, its structural relations and its subject-incidence organization;
2. the stationary or minimum principle, its admissible variations and the differentiated rather than uniform regime;
3. the quantitative scale, orientation and relevant qualitative character as actual experience;
4. whichever facts distinguish the actual minima, or a justified account of why apparent distinctions are not independent facts;
5. the relationship to global physical energy or a gravitational constraint if energy is the relevant quantity;
6. relational time, local histories and records, if needed, rather than an assumed external clock.

The finite functional itself neither supplies these derivations nor demonstrates they are impossible. All ingredients might be aspects of one richer intrinsic constitution, not external additions, but that unity needs a substantive argument. The user's stronger thesis—that if zero cannot meet the demand no framework can—also remains a question to investigate, not a conclusion of this calculation.

## Result

A single **assumed** relational functional can, in a definite parameter regime, require nontrivial zero-sum internal responses without fixed unequal reference values. This is a more constructive version of self-differentiation than simply stipulating a balanced list. It remains an illustrative, conditional model: the instability, actual minimum, experiential character and determinate actual organization are not yet derived from self-subsistent zero.
