# Necessary plenitude, plural subjects and simulation: a constructed candidate

## The proposal in one sentence

**There is one necessarily actual, intrinsically experiential whole; genuine possibility is not something awaiting actualization; the whole contains multiple finite subjects and their internally ordered histories, including simulated subjects if the relevant implementations are genuinely possible; Bostrom's probability is a conditional self-location measure over those subjects or episodes, not a creator outside the whole.**

This is a **constructed research candidate**, not a proof that the described whole exists, that simulated consciousness is possible, that we are simulated, or that quantum gravity has been solved. It preserves the user's intended identity of genuine possibility and actuality; it does **not** introduce a mechanism that turns possibility into reality. It also does not replace the intended agentive universal consciousness with a non-agentive whole and claim they are the same: this candidate explicitly adopts **plural finite subjects and no universal subject**.

“Necessary plenitude” is appropriate as the name of the user's intended position: actuality is necessary and every genuinely possible item is actual. The two pieces should not be conflated, however. A bare equivalence between two predicates in one interpretation does not automatically prove the necessity of a concrete, nonempty experiential whole. Stronger modal premises can make necessity follow conditionally, as made explicit below.

The construction builds on the [subject-pluralist synthesis](SUBJECT_PLURALIST_EXPERIENTIAL_SYNTHESIS.md), the [simulation consolidation](SIMULATION_PLENITUDE_CONSOLIDATION.md), and the [nothingness/experience audit](NOTHINGNESS_TRUTH_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_NECESSITY.md). It replaces neither the user's stronger modal thesis nor the previously retracted claims of established zero-residue closure with a mere computational analogy.

## 1. What is fundamental, and what is not

Write `W` for the **entire actual whole**—all concrete reality, including any genuine simulations and their hosts. The following are postulates of this candidate, not derived facts:

- **N1 — Necessary whole.** `W` is nonempty and necessarily actual. There is no external time in which `W` begins, and no further world in which its actuality has to be produced.
- **N2 — Intrinsic experience.** Experience is intrinsic to the constitution of reality, not something added from outside to an otherwise complete structure. This does not assert that every physical subsystem is conscious.
- **N3 — Plurality.** At least two genuinely distinct finite subjects exist. A subject is not merely a named register or a different description of a single universal subject. The theory still owes a criterion for identifying subjects.
- **N4 — Necessary plenitude.** Genuine possibility and actuality are identical in the intended strong sense. There is no separate possibility-to-actuality transition or selector.
- **N5 — Internal physical order.** Finite subjects, their episodes and relevant physical structures stand in relations that can support locally ordered histories. Fundamental spacetime and an external universal clock are not presupposed; deriving observed spacetime and local temporal experience remains open.
- **N6 — Internal simulation.** As a *candidate additional factual premise*, the whole contains at least one realized implementation in which an internal host represents and evolves a modeled environment containing real experiencers. If such an implementation is genuinely possible under N4, it is actual; but neither a formal model nor ordinary conceivability proves it genuinely possible. This premise is adopted to incorporate Bostrom's hypothesis, not presented as established evidence.

The whole may have one common intrinsic nature without itself being a single experiencer. Conversely, `N3` is a substantive postulate: the mere existence of different perspectives does not prove irreducible plurality. Under the user's adopted `perfect whole ⇒ best universe` and `our universe not best` premises, **the whole is not perfect**. Nothing in N1–N6 assumes a perfect agent or a selection of the best universe.

## 2. Possibility = actuality, without weakening it into mere conceivability

The proposal distinguishes:

- `D`: a *description*—which may be coherent, incoherent, under-specified or genuinely possible;
- `P(D)`: `D` is genuinely possible in the user's strongest sense;
- `A(D)`: `D` is concretely realized in the whole, with the relevant scope and relations.

The central modal commitment is

```
P(D) ↔ A(D).
```

If `D` is genuinely possible, it is actual; if it is not actual, it was not genuinely possible. This is an identity of modal status, **not** an assertion that a previously unactualized thing becomes actual. Nor does it imply that every syntactically consistent description is genuinely possible. To establish that a proposed simulation, subject or world occurs, we still need to establish its genuine possibility or actuality; simply drawing it in a mathematical model is not enough.

This is not a redefinition that settles the research question: the theory still owes an independently intelligible account of `P`, `A`, and why they coincide. If the thesis is adopted as an essential necessity of `W`, that is a substantive commitment, not something established by the notation.

### Global truths and local variations

Scope is essential, but using scope must not be a way to evade the strong thesis. Suppose descriptions refer to **the entire actuality**, with classical negation and ordinary modal operators. The schema

```
◇φ → φ     for every global proposition φ
```

has a strong consequence: substituting `¬φ` and taking the contrapositive yields `φ → □φ`. With reflexivity, global truths are necessary (`φ ↔ □φ`). Thus, given actual existence of experience, this global schema makes experience necessary **conditionally on the schema and those modal/logical assumptions**. It is an instance of modal collapse, not a proof that the schema is true. In particular, the necessity is not deduced merely from a short definition or from the finite model below.

The schema does **not** prevent different local situations within `W`: one subject may experience red while another experiences blue. The global facts “there is a red experience here” and “there is a blue experience there” can both hold. Nor do two different subjects have to be temporal copies of one another. But `p` and `not-p` cannot both hold **of the very same item in the same respect** under classical logic.

There is a real further choice about purportedly different *complete* totalities: if each is an exhaustive maximal whole and their global claims contradict one another, treating both as simultaneously true of the same one whole is inconsistent under classical logic. One might argue that one of them is not genuinely possible, or introduce an explicitly indexed plurality of concrete domains within a wider whole; the latter is not the same thing as multiple incompatible exhaustively complete wholes. A nonclassical alternative is also possible to investigate but would be an additional commitment. The present construction does not pretend to decide this choice by relabeling every global proposition as local.

## 3. A subject–history constitution that can be refined

To make the target explicit, the candidate needs more than the statement “everything is experience.” A future full theory must define, rather than merely name:

1. **Incidence:** which configurations or relational histories have a subject, without presupposing brains are the only possible substrates.
2. **Individuation:** when two episodes belong to the same subject and when they belong to different ones, including copies and divergences.
3. **Content:** the relation between physical/relational organization and the particular felt character, not merely a bit, behavior, or report.
4. **Local succession:** what orders episodes for a subject and why that ordering is experienced temporally, without assuming a clock outside `W`.
5. **Inter-subject relations:** shared records, causal interactions and agreement, including what can and cannot be retained through a physical reversal.

As a formal starting shape, let `O` be the set of **actual subject-episodes** and `S` the set of subjects, with an incidence relation `owner ⊆ O×S`. For the simplest candidate each episode has exactly one owner; whether this is appropriate for split, shared or overlapping experiences is itself open. Give each subject's episodes an internally specified order where applicable. These structures are ingredients to be explained by the constitution of `W`, not independent floating substances or an external timeline.

The earlier [reversible-selector gate](TWO_SUBJECT_REVERSIBLE_SELECTOR_MODEL.md) shows why a single unconditioned quantum state and a stipulation of subject labels do not automatically give a physical subject law. No preferred branch basis, Born measure, general quantum dynamics, relativistic locality or subject identity is derived by the finite construction here. Plenitude does not license assigning all incompatible measurement outcomes as simultaneous facts of the *same* subject and context. The [history gate](TEMPORARY_SUBJECT_RECORD_HISTORY_GATE.md) and [record-complementarity gate](REVERSIBLE_FRIEND_RECORD_COMPLEMENTARITY.md) remain constraints on particular physical event and record proposals.

## 4. Bostrom incorporated as an internal case

In [Bostrom's 2003 paper](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/), advanced agents may run detailed ancestor simulations, and a substrate-independence assumption is used so that suitable simulations can contain conscious people. The argument then compares the abundance of simulated and nonsimulated observers and applies an explicit self-location principle. Bostrom and Kulczycki's [2011 patch](https://simulation-argument.com/patch.pdf) identifies a mathematical non sequitur in the original population formula and proposes two ways to address it. Their additional assumptions are not supplied by plenitude; population and evidence assumptions must be stated rather than silently imported.

Our incorporation keeps the important structure:

- **Internal host:** if a simulator exists, it and its resources are parts of `W`, not an explanation from outside `W` or a creator of the whole.
- **Implementation:** a genuinely simulated history must have an independently specified representational and dynamical relation to its host. A mathematical description alone is not automatically a running simulation or a concrete experience.
- **Experiencers:** a simulated person contributes to the relevant observer population only if the implementation actually has the relevant experience under a justified subject/experience rule.
- **Perspective:** its environment can be locally real to that subject without being the entirety of `W`; this does not make suffering illusory or morally insignificant.
- **Measure:** the probability of being in such an environment is a conditional measure over eligible actual subject-locations, not the mere assertion that they exist.

A further **nonliteral** reading—finite perspectives within the timeless whole without a host computing them—may preserve the insight of limited access and the mathematical self-location structure, but should not be called Bostrom's literal computer-simulation hypothesis. Our N6 adopts an internal simulation as a candidate in the literal sense; it does not assume that the whole itself is software running on an external computer.

The [Cicurel–Nicolelis audit](RELATIVISTIC_BRAIN_SIMULATION_SUBJECT_AUDIT.md) explains the open substrate issue. Their field-based challenge does not prove digital minds impossible, and Bostrom's substrate independence is not a proved theorem. Thus `N6` has real explanatory and empirical content; it is not established merely by N4 or by the existence of a correct program.

## 5. Exact finite construction: plenitude and Bostrom's counting coexist

The companion [verifier](verification/necessary_plenitude_simulation_model.py) builds a finite **relational toy model**. Its physical existence, subjects, subjective contents, admissible possibilities and host relation are stipulated. It shows that the proposed logical and probability structures can fit together; it does not show that our universe realizes them.

**Model:**

- There are 10 base-level toy civilizations, each with 2 eligible observer labels: `B=20`.
- Four reach a stipulated posthuman stage; three of those run 10 toy simulations each: 30 simulations in all.
- Each simulated history has 2 distinct eligible observer labels: `S=60`.
- Each observer's toy state follows `(b,1−b,b)` for `b=0` or `1`; the simulated program returns the same three symbolic states. This is checked for all labels. It is a finite implementation relation in a mathematical model, not an actual brain, host computer or conscious experience.
- The toy catalogue of four local types—base/0, base/1, simulated/0, simulated/1—is stipulated to be genuinely possible, and each type appears in the model. Hence its chosen `P` and `A` sets coincide **for this finite catalogue**. This does not prove that every genuinely possible description in reality is instantiated, or that every formal description is genuinely possible.
- All 80 labels are stipulated to be distinct subjects and compatible with the selected evidence. Actual subject identity, phenomenology and evidence compatibility are not established by the labels.

Under equal weight per eligible observer,

```
f_sim = S/(B+S) = 60/80 = 3/4.
```

The same result follows from the simplified Bostrom-style formula under this model's homogeneous assumptions:

```
f_P=4/10,   f_I=3/4,   N̄_I=10
f_sim = (f_P f_I N̄_I)/(1 + f_P f_I N̄_I) = 3/4.
```

This exact equality uses the same number of eligible observers per base and simulated history, a specified one-level population and the declared reference class. If history sizes and simulation numbers vary or correlate, the actual weighted sums—not a product of separate averages by fiat—must be used. Nested simulations and host descendants also need careful counting and avoidance of double counting.

To make the 2011 correction concrete, keep the same 10 civilizations, four posthumans, three interested civilizations and 10 simulations each. Now give each of the three simulating civilizations 2 eligible ancestral observers, but each of the other seven civilizations 1,000. Then the actual counts are `B=7,006`, `S=60`, so the correct simulated fraction is `30/3533 ≈ 0.00849`—not the unchanged simplified formula's `3/4`. This is an illustrative application of the **known patch problem**, not a new objection to Bostrom or an estimate of actual populations.

This is **not a prediction that we are 75% simulated**. Bostrom's further self-location step requires a warranted weighting and treatment of our evidence. The model has not established that its simulated labels denote experiencers.

## 6. Plenitude does not determine the probability measure

In the same finite toy model, suppose we give half the total weight to the 20 base observers and half to the 60 simulated observers, uniformly within each group. This yields `1/2` rather than `3/4`, while the actual labels, histories and symmetry *within each group* remain unchanged. This is a comparison of mathematical weighting rules to expose missing assumptions; it does **not** claim that both rules are genuinely possible actualities under N4, or that reality chooses between them by a cosmic lottery.

Bostrom's equal-per-observer/bland-indifference principle is a **specific candidate** for a justified self-location rule, not a consequence of plural subjects or plenitude alone. In this example, when all 80 observers are treated as equally eligible and evidentially equivalent, the group-weighted rule is **not** Bostrom's equal-per-observer rule. It shows underdetermination by the ontological premises, not an alternative result derived from his indifference premise. For a justified measure `μ` on actual eligible locations compatible with evidence `E`,

```
Cr(simulated | E) = μ(simulated ∩ E) / μ(E),
```

provided `0 < μ(E) < ∞` and the measure is appropriate for the inference. One must decide whether the relevant units are persons, lifetimes, subject-episodes, or another class; assign weights; handle duplication, memories and simulations that differ in detail; and explain why the resulting self-location rule is warranted. Counting structural descriptions, computer outputs or mathematical isomorphs is not automatically counting conscious subjects.

### Infinite plenitude makes this a sharper problem

If there are countably infinitely many base observers and countably infinitely many simulated observers, `∞/(∞+∞)` is not a number. No countably additive normalized distribution assigns the **same positive weight** to each of countably infinitely many individual observers: equal weight zero sums to zero, and equal positive weight diverges.

The verifier compares two exhaustive orders of the same two infinite populations. One takes three new simulated labels then one base label repeatedly, giving limiting simulated fraction `3/4`; the other takes one simulated then three base labels, giving `1/4`. Every label occurs eventually in both orders. These are regulator-dependent mathematical examples, not physically selected measures and not probabilities predicted by the framework. Infinite measures or non-uniform normalizable measures can exist, but a justified reference class, weighting and conditioning rule is still required. The cosmological measure problem has analogous cutoff sensitivity [2](https://arxiv.org/html/0812.0005); its particular proposed remedies cannot simply be imported as a proof for this timeless framework.

Because `W` is not assumed to evolve in an external universal time, a cutoff by “how many observers exist before cosmic time T” cannot be assumed fundamental. An intrinsically justified regularization, an appropriate finite local reference class, or another defensible measure is an **open dependency**, not a forbidden mathematical tool or a requirement that an external clock exist.

## 7. What the construction does and does not accomplish

**Achieved at the model level:**

- A coherent finite example has multiple distinct stipulated subjects, multiple realized local types, an internal symbolic simulation, and a Bostrom-style finite observer fraction in one overarching structure.
- The probability calculation does not require an external creator or a literal process that actualizes possibilities.
- Under standard classical modal semantics, an unrestricted global `◇φ→φ` thesis has the precise modal-collapse consequence stated above; internal differences remain consistent when properly scoped.
- Plenitude and group symmetry alone do not determine observer weights; the infinite case exposes additional regularization dependence.

**Not achieved:**

- A proof that nonexistence is impossible, that `W` necessarily exists, or that possibility is identical with concrete actuality. The substantive assumptions are explicit.
- A derived physical law, solution to the problem of time, emergence of spacetime, resolution of quantum measurement, or empirically grounded Born measure.
- A subject-identity/experience-constitution law, a proof that digital or analog simulations can feel, or evidence that our world is an internal simulation.
- A unique reference class or observer measure, a derived simulation probability, or a demonstration that this is the uniquely simplest or fully intelligible account.

These are **not cosmetic qualifications**: they mark exactly where the current construction must be strengthened to approach the requested zero-residue account. All a script can verify is that its stipulated finite mathematical rules are consistent and its arithmetic is correct.

## 8. Next dependency-driven steps

1. **Constitution, not enumeration:** propose a non-circular physical/phenomenal condition for subject incidence and identity, and compare it against brain fields, computational implementations and reversible-record constraints. Make its empirical or structural consequences explicit.
2. **Genuine possibility:** develop an independent account of why the proposed whole and its local realizations are necessary, including the status of global alternatives, instead of equating formal consistency with actuality.
3. **Implementation bridge:** decide what it takes for an internal simulation to realize a subject, including the Cicurel–Nicolelis substrate challenge. An ordinary digitally computed trajectory alone is not a demonstrated experience.
4. **Measure:** derive or justify a reference class and weighting/regularization from defensible principles; test stability under changes of descriptions, duplication and permissible refinements, without an assumed external clock.
5. **Physics and evidence:** recover relational dynamics, quantum probabilities and observed local spacetime, and state what could distinguish candidate subject/measure rules. The finite toy model supplies none of these results.
