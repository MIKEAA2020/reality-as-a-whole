# Recheck: older premises versus the current zero-energy programme

## Purpose and scope

The user identified that I had treated the older Claude-time non-dual framework as current. This recheck compares the **recent Claude-time audits and time workboard**, plus a small number of referenced framework documents, against the [zero-energy and intrinsic-experience programme](ZERO_ONE_REALITY_NECESSARY_PLENITUDE_SYNTHESIS.md), its [current physical–experiential gravity analysis](ZERO_EXPERIENCE_INTRINSIC_IDENTITY_RELATIONAL_GRAVITY.md), and the user's latest explicit correction. It is not a complete audit of the entire repository or all intervening conversations. Historical transcripts and their quoted claims are evidence of past positions, not automatically current instructions.

**Correction to this recheck:** The user has repeatedly made clear that **space and time are non-fundamental constructs**, not merely that there is no external space or clock. In particular, the user explicitly objected that “spacetime is a construct, emergent!” in the [experience/non-optimality transcript](sources/uploads/DeepSeek_Experience-and-non-optimal-world_20261123-3.txt) line 2311. The previous row incorrectly listed atemporality itself as an old premise to discard and replaced this stronger position with the narrower no-external-clock claim. That was my mistake. The older universal-consciousness ontology is superseded, but the non-fundamentality of space and time remains part of the current framework.

The current programme aims at a self-sufficient whole with objectively zero energy, intrinsic experience and extrinsic physical description, genuine finite subjects, and explanatory necessity without an external chooser; space and time are constructed or emergent rather than fundamental containers. The task is to explain how their physical and experienced character arises within that constitution, not to deny ordinary experience of space, duration or change. The precise whole-energy concept and detailed physical–experiential constitution are research targets, not proved consequences of a toy constraint. Its proposal that genuine possibility and actuality coincide is a substantive guiding thesis, not a license to call every mathematically consistent toy an actual possibility. No claim is made here that the programme is already completed.

## What was not found or not established

The finite modular calibration, Lüders/eraser and Fourier calculations are mathematical results conditional on their own definitions; they do not themselves require acceptance of an Absolute or universal subject. Their **relevance to the current project** is nevertheless a separate question. This pass does not show that every old time lead is obsolete or that the mathematical results have all been reverified. It also does not establish that every possible earlier assumption has been found; wider source and repository reconciliation remains unfinished.
