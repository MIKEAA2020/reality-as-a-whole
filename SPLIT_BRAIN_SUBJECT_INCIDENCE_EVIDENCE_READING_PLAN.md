# Fixed plan: split-brain first-person evidence and nested subject incidence

## Prior knowledge and scope

The existing [private-fields comparison](PRIVATE_NESTED_FIELDS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_CONTENT_COMPARISON.md) specifies a same-window existential target `N`: a local detailed experiencer and a numerically distinct global coarse-conjunction experiencer. P predicts `N=1` and the closed one-field C-layered package `N=0`, while both give `D=0, G=1`. These are stipulations about a four-spin scenario, not clinical findings. Previous project audits have already read the Pinto 2017 main article and its general reports of preserved subjective unity, studied Schechter's action-unity article and Lopez's layered proposal, and checked partial methods of some attention experiments. The 2017 supplemental first-person account and trial records were not inspected. Initial current search snippets point to Schechter and Bayne 2021 and a 2020 multidisciplinary review, including their caution that agency and experience can come apart; those snippets, not full texts, were seen before this plan.

## Tasks

1. Try to obtain and inspect the 2017 study's actual supplementary first-person account and task methods; record accessibility, exact inspection scope, participants and any source-specific qualifications. If unavailable, do not imply a full read or generate interview details. Search for a distinct accessible primary study that adds relevant simultaneous, first-person and/or separate response evidence. Inspect complete available source text if feasible and clearly distinguish full articles, abstracts and snippets.
2. Reconstruct what participants report, what task design and controls actually establish, and the source authors' own interpretations. Distinguish subject from agent, left/right report channels, distinct tokens versus matching content types, concurrent versus sequential evidence, stimulus versus experiential access, and across-patient or trial comparisons. Don't infer no experience from nonresponse, nonsignificance or inability to compare.
3. Apply the results to the **completed** P and C-layered packages only under explicitly stated content, window, subject, physical-domain and report-access mappings. Clearly separate direct empirical evidence from added bridges. Specify observations which could support `G` or `N` and failure conditions for each package; avoid claiming any observation validates a four-spin selector or supports P solely because descriptions use similar labels. Explain why a reliable report of one perspective cannot census inaccessible ones and why a purported second subject requires independent individuation.
4. Develop a practical, ethically constrained and noncircular evidence contract with positive and indeterminate outcomes, robust controls for leakage, memory, changing states and response channels, and realistic limits of target calibration. No surgery, new participant contact or unapproved data request is undertaken. Retain risk of interrogating/changing the experience. Do not reduce Lopez's experiential centrality argument to capacity interference.
5. Produce a page/section-specific source audit and relevant links, rerun existing checks, refresh inventories, and commit/push on current branch. Leave C3, B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 open unless independent new evidence genuinely resolves them; do not introduce new unrelated toy regressions.
