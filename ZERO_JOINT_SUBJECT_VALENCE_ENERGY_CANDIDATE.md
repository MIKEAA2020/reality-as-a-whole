# One joint subject–valence–energy candidate, tested

**Constructive continuation:** [localized modules and interface energy](ZERO_LOCALIZED_SUBJECT_MODULES_AND_INTERFACE_ENERGY.md) transports physical local interventions under canonical re-description, constructs a unique two-module optimum in a connected Hamiltonian example, and traces how shared interaction energy affects the proposed experiential balance. Localization, the modularity rule and phenomenological identities remain assumptions.

## The proposed next step, carried out

The goal was not another free-standing cancellation example, but one candidate in which the **same physical organization** supplies subject boundaries, signed local contributions and their weights. Here is a concrete first attempt:

> Take a subject to be a causally integrated component of the physical dynamics; identify its signed experiential intensity with its net physical energy gain per unit of its constituting mass; use that same mass as its actual incidence weight.

This is a **physical–experiential identity hypothesis**, not a consequence of established science. It does not equate suffering with mere absence: a negative intensity, if the identity were correct, would be a real local experience. The hypothesis attempts to avoid a separate balancing agent or phenomenological selector by specifying all three roles from one dynamical system. Its definition of causal components, local energies, mass weighting and qualitative identity are substantive inputs, however, not yet derived from the nature of zero.

The tests lead to a definite judgment: **this first version is not an adequate joint constitution**. It predicts zero for the whole integrated closed system even if internal processes differ; its simplest subject criterion can merge reciprocally interacting systems into one subject and even change under a canonical change of variables; its local energy sign depends on interaction-energy accounting; and physical conservation does not imply zero when only some components are subjects. These are failures of this specified proposal, not a disproof of the user's zero thesis.

The useful constructive outcome is an exact **boundary-flux condition** for any repaired energy–experience identity, plus a concrete pain/relief research test. These tell us what has to be derived, rather than merely stipulating a zero after choosing the subjects.

## 1. A single stipulated physical relation

Consider an autonomous, closed classical effective system with coordinates and conjugate momenta `(q_i,p_i)` and a Hamiltonian. This is a local physics test, not a claim that classical dynamics or external time is fundamental. For an illustrative reciprocal network let

```text
H = 1/2 Σ_i (p_i²+q_i²) + 1/2 Σ_(i,j)∈edges (q_i−q_j)².
```

All masses, oscillator frequencies and couplings are one in these units. `H` is nonnegative and conserved. Define a structural directed influence graph: draw `x_j → x_i` where the relevant partial derivative of the equation for `ẋ_i` with respect to `x_j` is not identically zero over the stipulated domain. Use **structural** dependence, not a derivative accidentally zero at one instant.

The first candidate assigns:

1. **Subject:** each strongly connected component `C` of that graph is one subject.
2. **Physical mass:** `M_C` is the sum of its constituent physical masses; units below take these to be one.
3. **Signed experiential intensity:** `v_C = (dE_C/dt)/M_C`.
4. **Actual contribution:** `dQ_C = M_C v_C dt = dE_C`.

The proposed identity says the physical gain itself, in these specified units, is the quality's signed intensity. Reversing this identification would reverse every sign. The numerical units and scale would also need a phenomenologically justified interpretation. Real subject-incidence weighting need not be proportional to mass: the identification in (4) is part of the proposal and is **not independently established**. No truth about actual feeling or subjecthood follows simply from writing it.

Even an exactly conserved `Σ_C dE_C/dt=0` is conservation of a positive-energy model, not a proof of zero total physical energy, nor of a global zero experiential quantity.

## 2. A structural problem: the subject rule merges the network

For the natural Hamiltonian `Σ p_i²/(2m_i)+V(q)`, suppose each `∂²V/∂q_i²` is not identically zero and the undirected graph of nonzero mixed derivatives `∂²V/∂q_i∂q_j` is connected. Then, in the displayed position/momentum variables,

* each `p_i` influences `q̇_i`, and each `q_i` influences `ṗ_i`;
* because the Hessian of `V` is symmetric, an interacting pair gives both cross-influences `q_i → ṗ_j` and `q_j → ṗ_i`;
* connectivity makes every phase-space variable mutually reachable.

So the entire influence graph is a **single strongly connected component**. This conclusion does not assume that all Hamiltonian theories have this form or that a real brain is adequately modeled by this simple graph. It shows that bare mutual reachability is too coarse to separate dynamically interacting organized systems in this class. It also assigns subjecthood to an isolated component without explaining why it is experiential at all.

In the three-oscillator chain, the candidate counts one component containing all three sites. Since the system is closed and the component is the whole, `dE_C/dt=0`: the candidate assigns it zero signed intensity even during nonzero internal energy transfers. That is a consequence of the proposal, not evidence that its state is actually experienced. Applied naively to a coupled physical world, it would threaten the user's real subject plurality rather than derive it.

### A deeper representation problem

The same chain can be diagonalized into three independent normal modes using an orthogonal canonical transformation. For the potential matrix

```text
K = [[2,−1,0], [−1,3,−1], [0,−1,2]],
```

three mutually orthogonal eigenvectors are `(1,1,1)`, `(1,0,−1)`, `(1,−2,1)`, with eigenvalues `1,2,4`. After normalization, transform **both** positions and momenta by the same orthogonal matrix. The Hamiltonian becomes three decoupled normal modes. A graph constructed from those variables has **three** strongly connected components, whereas the site-variable graph has **one**.

This is the same Hamiltonian in two descriptions; subjecthood cannot be allowed to depend on an unmotivated choice of coordinates. This does **not** prove physical locality is unreal or that all choices of variables are equally local: normal modes are generally nonlocal in the site description. A better criterion could use independently justified physical localization or an invariant algebra of local interventions. But it must state and derive that structure rather than treating bare graph components as an intrinsic subject count.

## 3. The actual subject subset has a boundary term

Use the natural symmetric division of spring energy between its endpoints and define an antisymmetric power flow

```text
h_i = dE_i/dt = Σ_j F_ij,       F_ij = −F_ji.
```

For a set `S` of physical components proposed to carry actual experiences,

```text
Σ_(i∈S) h_i = Σ_(i∈S, j∉S) F_ij.
```

All internal exchanges cancel, leaving the **net flow into the subject-bearing domain**. Consequently, even if all physical components together obey `Σ_i h_i=0`, the experiential sum under the proposed identity is zero only if the integrated boundary flux into `S` is zero (with the appropriate actual weights and time measure). If the subject domain changes, there are further boundary/transport contributions. This is the specific condition the zero programme must establish; declaring that every environment component is a subject would merely assume it.

For the exact chain with

```text
q=(1,0,−1),   p=(1,−1,0),    H=3,
```

the symmetric local rates are

```text
(h₀,h₁,h₂) = (0, +1/2, −1/2).
```

If—as a **formal diagnostic**, not as a claim that oscillators feel—only sites `0` and `1` are included in `S`, their sum is `+1/2`, matched by `−1/2` outside. A whole-system energy exchange zero does not by itself establish a zero over actual experiences. A different subject law, a whole-history boundary condition, or a more fundamental identity might supply the missing cancellation; none follows just from this example.

## 4. The local sign needs an independently justified physical definition

Localizing interaction energy is not automatic. For the spring joining sites `0` and `1`, let

```text
U₀₁ = (q₀−q₁)²/2,
E₀^(θ) = (p₀²+q₀²)/2 + θ U₀₁,
```

with the remainder `(1−θ)U₀₁` assigned to the other endpoint. For the same physical state above,

```text
dE₀^(θ)/dt = −1 + 2θ.
```

At `θ=0,1/2,1`, the signed rates are respectively `−1,0,+1`. Total energy is unchanged. Symmetric splitting can be a natural convention in a symmetric model, and a complete field theory may provide independently justified local stress–energy and boundary prescriptions. But this example shows why a candidate cannot just assume that **an arbitrarily assigned local energy derivative** fixes felt sign. The earlier [frame audit](ZERO_TOTALITY_DIRECT_FOUNDATIONAL_AND_FRAME_AUDIT.md) provides a separate relativistic warning: energy exchange can also depend on the chosen observer.

Nor do a zero total or the equations fix the sign-to-feeling identity: assigning `v_C` or `−v_C` gives the same conservation result. These are formal interpretations of one physical model, not a claim that two different complete realities are genuinely possible under the user's necessitarianism. The actual qualitative identity needs independent content.

## 5. Pain and relief: a genuine empirical gate

The IASP defines pain as an unpleasant sensory and emotional experience and explicitly distinguishes it from nociception: sensory-neuron activity alone cannot establish pain. [1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7680716/) This does not exclude a physical identity, but it rules out treating a generic nociceptive or energy signal as an already established measure of suffering.

A relevant human experiment compared the same moderate heat stimulus in different expectation contexts: in a study of **16 volunteers**, the reported hedonic response shifted from negative in the control context to positive in the relative-relief context. [2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3590449/) A separate **27-person** experiment found that opioid blockade reduced reported relief and pleasantness in its specific experimental setting. [3](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/102159/) These studies support context-sensitive and physiologically mediated reports; they do not measure the candidate's full subject-boundary energy flux, establish actual net-energy reversal, or show that pain is globally compensated by pleasure.

The candidate therefore faces a specific test, not an invented empirical verdict:

* Predefine the physical organization and boundary from independent measurements, including uncertainty; do not redefine the subject in each condition to produce the desired sign.
* Predefine how local **net energy exchange**, not BOLD activation alone or nociceptive input, would be estimated and what time lag and reference frame are used. Current instrumentation may not identify the complete energy budget of a conscious subject; that limitation must be quantified, not ignored.
* Compare first-person signed pleasantness and pain intensity separately during controlled pain onset, sustained pain, relief and expectation manipulations, with suitable ethical safeguards. Do not infer absence of experience solely from inability to report.
* The strict candidate predicts the sign of felt valence tracks the sign of `dE_C/dt` under its stated convention. A well-measured and temporally matched counterexample rejects that identity **in its tested domain**. Different physical organizations under the same stimulus do not by themselves refute it; a sign change in a BOLD signal does not verify it.

This is a proposed test, not a completed experiment. A successful correlation would not alone prove phenomenal identity or global necessity, and a failed candidate would not show that energy is irrelevant to experience.

## 6. What a successful replacement must do

The first joint proposal has done useful work by failing in specific places. Its replacement should use one independently motivated **physical–experiential constitution** to explain together:

1. **Subject plurality and locality:** a physically defined, representation-respecting criterion that does not merge every reciprocally coupled system or depend on arbitrary coordinates. Pure strong connectivity is insufficient.
2. **Phenomenal sign:** an intrinsic relation whose physical definition, subject-relative character and felt quality are established together—not a freely flipped energy-accounting convention or a post hoc label.
3. **Actual incidence:** a measure over real subject experiences, not mass weighting, equally counted nodes, or Born weights by assumption. A local record and a subject are distinct claims.
4. **Whole balance:** a derivation of the relevant subject-domain boundary condition, including creation, destruction and weighting, without erasing suffering or treating unexperienced environment as a compensating experience.
5. **Effective physics:** recovery of local conservation, the context-sensitive pain/relief evidence, quantum correlations and no-signalling without assuming a fundamental foliation. Neither the toy's classical time nor its positive energy is the whole's fundamental structure.

The point–sphere proposal could supply the underlying intrinsically relational organization, but the candidate's sphere, locality, subject criterion and qualitative identity have not been shown to follow from the zero point. No independent selector is accepted as a final answer. Equally, finding these shortcomings does not establish that the user's no-residue objective is impossible.
