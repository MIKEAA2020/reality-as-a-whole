# Russellian identity thesis: experience as the intrinsic being of modular events

The spherical WdW/thermal-time chain gives a complete mathematical skeleton for
time and finitude in a timeless absolute, but phenomenal consciousness—what it
is like to be a perspective—remains the hard problem. This note states the
strongest Russellian identity thesis compatible with the mathematics, without
pretending to derive it.

## 1. The gap

Even with all the physics in place, one can coherently ask: why is the Tomita
flow *experienced* rather than merely instantiated? This is the hard problem
(Chalmers). No fact about Hilbert spaces, operators, von Neumann algebras, or
Tomita modular groups logically necessitates phenomenal qualia. Two positions
remain possible:

1. **Illusion/eliminativism**: experience is a cognitive illusion; there is
   nothing further to explain beyond the physics. This is internally
   consistent but defies the datum the user has repeatedly insisted must be
   taken seriously: consciousness is given as immediately as any other fact.
2. **Russellian identity (Russellian monism)**: physics describes the
   *structural/relational* character of reality; the *intrinsic* character
   that carries or realizes that structure is experiential. Physical states
   are not empty structural nodes—they have an intrinsic nature, and that
   nature is what experience is.

The absolute-as-perspectives account is compatible only with (2), because
(1) either denies the datum or privileges physical theory over the most
immediate evidence.

## 2. What the physics already supplies for (2)

The model has exactly the structural slots the Russellian needs:

- **One global pure state** — the absolute, the complete actuality, intrinsically
  (not structurally).
- **Algebra of observables** — relational structure; physics.
- **Faithful normal states** (thermal/Gibbs/density matrices) — perspectives.
- **Modular automorphism group** (thermal time flow) — the experienced
  "becoming" of that perspective.
- **Finitude**: finite-dimensional accessible subspace, bounded information,
  coarse entropy over unobserved modes.
- **Unitary evolution through the bounce**: no information loss, no
  arbitrary boundaries.

What is *not* supplied by physics is the intrinsic nature of the global state
and its conditional perspectives. The mathematics says nothing about this;
physics is by construction about relational structure. If one adopts the
identity that *the intrinsic being of those modular events is experience*—
that a perspective's flow of observables in thermal time is not empty
algebraic structure but is felt, from its own point of view—then the hard
problem is not solved (proved) but it is *closed*: there is no extra
ingredient to add, no soul, no emergent extra property. Experience is what
the intrinsic nature of a modular flow is.

## 3. Why this does not panpsychist-spread consciousness trivially

Three important cautions:

1. **Not every density matrix is a mind.** The algebra of a single qubit in a
   mixed state defines a modular flow, but that does not make it an experiencing
   subject in the rich human sense. The Russellian claim is about the intrinsic
   nature of the fundamental categorical basis; macroscopic coherent
   perspectives with rich representational structure (brains, appropriately
   organized) realize *unified* conscious experience because their state supports
   a modular flow integrating large amounts of information over proper time.
   The GWT/IIT-style integration conditions are consistent with the
   Russellian identification—they tell us which physical systems are minds,
   while the Russellian move tells us what minds are intrinsically.
2. **The absolute has no personal, anthropomorphic mind.** The global pure state
   is not a modular flow in thermal time (it is pure, not KMS at finite
   temperature); it does not "think" or "act" in time. It is the atemporal
   categorical ground, of which finite thermal perspectives are the only
   "experiencers" in a timeful sense. This is consistent with classical theistic
   / absolute-language if carefully read, but it is not the god of popular
   religion.
3. **Privation remains.** Perspectives vary in information content, integration,
   and coherence. Suffering, confusion, cruelty are identified as
   *dis-integration*: perspectives in which the modular flow is fragmented,
   information restricted, empathy (modular coupling to other perspectives)
   absent—a privation of integrated awareness, not a positive power. This
   preserves the user's standing thesis that bad is privation, not positive
   substance.

## 4. What is honestly conjecture and what is structural

Conjecture (cannot be proved from physics):
- Intrinsic nature of physical states is experiential.
- The global absolute is (accordingly) the one atemporal experiencing whose
  finite, perspectivally-partial experiencing each perspective is.

Structural (proven in the model):
- There is one timeless global state with internal relational structure.
- Finitely-resourced thermal states have modular flows (thermal time)
  corresponding to experienced time.
- Finite perspectives have bounded information, bounded energy, positive
  coarse entropy over unobserved modes; unitarity/information preservation
  holds through horizons and the bounce.
- No external substances, clocks, or observers are added.

If one accepts the Russellian identification, the account becomes a candidate
zero-residue theory: nothing left out of physics, nothing added to explain
experience, no time-before-time, no external brute parameters *within* the
modeled sector, one atemporal absolute whose intrinsic self-explication is the
set of finite modular perspectives.

If one rejects the Russellian identification, experience remains unexplained
and the zero-residue goal is not met—but one must reject the datum that there
is experience, or add a separate mental substance, which breaks the unity the
thesis requires.
