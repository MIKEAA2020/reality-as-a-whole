## 1. Steelman first: the usable insight

A description of an untimed game restricted to the recorded move history contains an ordered sequence of moves, but not the players' elapsed thinking durations. Setting the latter **aside** to investigate the intrinsic description does not assert that the players' durations are nonexistent. One may ask whether an apparently external evolution parameter in physics can likewise arise from a structural, relational description of the whole. On this reading, the strongest proposal is not that a chess timer literally *is* relativistic proper time, or that nature must be a chess game. It is a research demand: exhibit one mathematical structure that supports (i) an internal index/order and (ii) appropriate measurable duration and dynamics, and show explicitly under what limits these descriptions agree.

The user stresses that finite/discrete models are legitimate starting points whether or not fundamental reality turns out discrete, and asks us to mark the gap between a parallel and an actual bridge (source lines 1191, 1493, 1531). The open physical questions include which state/algebra/split supplies dynamics, clock calibration, geometry, and how the nonrelativistic and relativistic regimes emerge. The structural-relational thesis and the non-dual working assumption can guide model selection, but do not automatically prove those bridges. Their interpretive status does not make the proposed calculations irrelevant.

## 4. Implementation

### A. From a structural index to a calibrated modular/Schrödinger limit

Let H and V be Hermitian matrices on a finite-dimensional system, β, ħ, Δ > 0, and ε real. Define `H_ε=H+εV`, the faithful reference `ρ_ε=exp(−βH_ε)/Z_ε`, and the modular convention `σ_s^ε(A)=ρ_ε^(is) A ρ_ε^(−is)`. A discrete internal sequence has labels `n=0,1,…` and is **additionally equipped** with the calibrated reading `t_n=nΔ` and per-step unitary

    U_Δ = exp(−i H_ε Δ/ħ),     A_n = U_Δ^(−n) A U_Δ^n.

By the Gibbs identity, `A_n=σ^ε_{−nΔ/(βħ)}(A)` exactly. If a separately specified clock–system history realizes those conditional states, this is its discrete relational prediction; the sequence alone does not construct that history. For `t∈[0,T]`, choose `n=floor(t/Δ)`, and compare with the *target* Schrödinger/Heisenberg flow `α_t^0(A)=exp(iHt/ħ) A exp(−iHt/ħ)`. Duhamel plus `0≤t−nΔ<Δ` gives

    ||A_n − α_t^0(A)||
       ≤ (2||A||/ħ) [ T |ε| ||V|| + Δ ||H−cI|| ]

for every real c. Proof: first compare the ε-flow and the H-flow at time nΔ using the previous [cocycle estimate](LIGHTHOUSE_MODULAR_SCHRODINGER_ANALYTIC_GATE.md); then compare the H-flow at nΔ and t using the norm bound on its commutator generator. A scalar shift of H does not affect observable evolution. The same reasoning bounds the trace distance of arbitrary initial states, including ancillas, by half the displayed coefficient with `||A||` removed; a specified common POVM has no larger total-variation error. Thus, for fixed T and bounded H,V, the labeled modular predictions converge uniformly to the Schrödinger predictions when ε→0 and Δ→0.

This is a real finite-resolution/perturbation estimate, but **not a physical derivation from order alone**: the calibration Δ, H_ε, thermal state, and clock–system realization are imposed. Different calibrations give the same abstract order but different duration estimates. Different H_ε give different predictions on the same order. This is a discrete sampling limit, not a proof of quantum-gravitational semiclassicality or an assertion that physical time is fundamentally discrete. The distinction operationalizes the analogy without collapsing structural index into duration.

### B. Same local state, different modular behavior: CSI check

Take `M=M₂⊗M₂`, `N=M₂⊗I`, `X=σ_x`, `Z=σ_z`, and a faithful global Gibbs state `ρ=exp(−β X⊗X)/Z₀` with β>0. The restricted state on N is maximally mixed, because `Tr₂ exp(−βX⊗X)` is proportional to I. Its modular flow on N is therefore the identity. Meanwhile the global flow takes

    Z⊗I  ↦  cos(2βs) Z⊗I − sin(2βs) Y⊗X,

which generally lies outside N. Comparing the *same* restricted state with itself gives the identity Connes cocycle on N, yet the ambient flow is not the local modular flow. This explicit finite counterexample pinpoints why the chat's “global state ⇒ all local modular flows restrict” argument fails. It does not settle existence or nonexistence of a different, properly defined geometric gluing obstruction in type III gravity.

The user’s structural analogy survives and leads to a sharper mathematical question: **which additional state, transition, clock and geometric data turn internal order into calibrated physical dynamics?** The artificial CSI, thermodynamic-arrow, WKB and CMB identifications do not survive the audit. The result is a tested foothold on the analytical path, not a claim to have recovered GR, a preferred clock, or time from the whole's constitution.
