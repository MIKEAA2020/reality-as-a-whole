# Clock poststates: a concrete obstruction to identifying two instruments

**Continuation:** [the same probe in an R-time representation](ZERO_SAME_PROBE_CLOCK_REPRESENTATION.md) now transports the actual four-outcome probe, retained cursor and clock by an explicit isometry. It also shows a robust two-state information-loss obstruction after a later R reading is made classical. The encoding is a same-theory representation, not a full gravitational clock-switch proof.

## Result

The [factorwise clock–memory probe](ZERO_FACTORWISE_CLOCK_MEMORY_PROBE.md) and the earlier [orbit-averaged instrument](ZERO_RETAINED_CLOCK_INSTRUMENT_AMBIGUITY.md) can give closely matching memory probabilities. They do **not** produce the same retained clock state. For the actual semibounded continuum R clock and the previously specified cosine-band preparation, we now prove a quantitative witness:

```text
old orbit-averaged instrument:  Pr(clock momentum outside Λ) > 1.06×10⁻¹⁰;
new factorwise probe, conditioned on a bin hit:  Pr(outside Λ) = 0.
```

Here `Λ=[5000,15000]` is the clock band used in the factorwise probe. The lower bound is deliberately conservative and tiny; it proves **nonidentity**, not a practically significant minimum discrepancy. It is compatible with the preceding `<0.00781` comparison of conditional *memory outcome probabilities*. The latter did not claim equality of retained poststates.

The witness is the physical spectral projector onto R-clock energy outside Λ, not a hidden orbit-label measurement. It commutes with the old root Hamiltonian and the new probe Hamiltonian. In the common R-clock identification, the full recorded old and new poststates therefore have trace distance greater than `1.06×10⁻¹⁰`, even if one also keeps the old apparatus and memory outcome. The models and operations are specified below. The new calculation does **not** show that an R-relative clock switch is impossible: these are distinct interventions, with different clock instruments. It identifies an invalid shortcut—treating their similar memory statistics as equality of the same complete instrument.

A separate finite cyclic control produces a much larger separation with **identical** memory statistics, but its numerical value is not a continuum estimate. [Checks](verification/zero_clock_probe_poststate_obstruction_test.py).

## 1. Two operations, one retained observable

Let

```text
H_R=L²([0,∞),dk),       K_R ψ(k)=kψ(k),
P=1_Λ(K_R),             Q=I−P,
Λ=[5000,15000],         B=[r−ℓ/2,r+ℓ/2],   ℓ=10⁻⁴.
```

The initial R clock is the same normalized cosine packet β used previously, supported in P:

```text
β(k)= (1/√b_R) cos[π(k−k₀)/(2b_R)] on Λ,
k₀=10000,        b_R=5000.
```

It is independent of the old apparatus A, whose system input may be arbitrary and whose old cursor/flag/memory are prepared as stipulated in the comparison. Let `E_B=E_R(B)` be the **uncompressed** covariant R-time effect and `A=√E_B`. The earlier chosen operation is

```text
T_m(ρ) = (1/ℓ) ∫_R dt U₀(t)† [Π_m⊗A] U₀(t)
                                  ρ
                              U₀(t)† [Π_m⊗A] U₀(t),
U₀(t)=U_A(t)⊗U_R(t),       U_R(t)=exp(iK_Rt).
```

The products are ordered as displayed, and the integral is the positive trace-class integral of the prior construction. T_m is one **particular mathematical instrument** with the previous relational effects; it is not derived uniquely from those effects or claimed to be the old memory device's local measurement interaction.

The new probe instead uses the compressed clock effect `P E_B P` and the clock gate

```text
Θ_R = arcsin√(P E_B P).
```

Its Hamiltonian has the old root, a fresh massive cursor and factorwise R/probe and old-memory/probe interactions as specified in the factorwise report. Since P commutes with K_R and Θ_R, and acts on no other factor, it commutes with the **entire** new Hamiltonian. Initially the clock lies in P, so its exact evolved state lies in P for both clock-probe results, both memory-probe results, and their sums. This remains true with the new massive cursor, not merely in its chiral or instantaneous comparator. Any subsequent partial trace, conditioning on an outcome of nonzero probability, or scalar evolution generated by H₀ preserves this statement. In particular

```text
Pr_probe(Q | clock-bin hit)=0.
```

The bin-hit probability is positive for the earlier β and fixed old apparatus: the factorwise bound gives it above 0.1205. The `P E_B P` clock gate has the **same bin-hit probability** as E_B for any initial state supported in P, but its poststate is a different operation.

## 2. The old operation necessarily populates the outside band

Sum both old memory outcomes and trace out A. Because the initial R clock is independent, `Σ_mΠ_m=I`, and U_A is unitary, the reduced old output is

```text
ρ_R^T = (1/ℓ) ∫_R dt A_t |β⟩⟨β| A_t,
A_t = U_R(t)† A U_R(t).
```

The old instrument's completeness ensures `Tr ρ_R^T=1`; there is no uniform normalized prior over t. Let

```text
L := Tr(Q ρ_R^T)
   = (1/ℓ) ∫_R dt || Q A_t β ||².
```

We prove L is strictly positive. An outline without constants is instructive. The functions `U_R(t)β`, for real t, span a dense subspace of P H_R: if f in P is orthogonal to all of them, Fourier uniqueness gives `conj(f)β=0`; β is nonzero almost everywhere inside Λ, so f=0. If L=0, continuity in t implies `Q A U_R(t)β=0` for every t. Boundedness then gives `QAP=0`. Since A is self-adjoint, this would make P commute with A and hence with `E_B=A²`.

But the clock POVM kernel is

```text
E_B(k,k') = [ℓ/(2π)] exp(i(k−k')r)
             sinc[(k−k')ℓ/2],
```

where `sinc z=sin(z)/z`. Its cross-band block Q E_B P is nonzero. Thus L>0. The following estimate makes this statement quantitative for the **actual** prior parameters.

## 3. An explicit continuum lower bound

Put `d=3.5` and let `P₀` project onto the interior band `[5000+d,15000−d]`; write `P₁=P−P₀` and keep `Q=I−P`. Define

```text
B₀=Q A P₀,          B₁=Q A P₁,          D=Q A Q.
```

Since `A²=E_B` and `||A||≤1`,

```text
Q E_B P₀ = B₀ P₀ A P₀ + B₁ P₁ A P₀ + D B₀,
||Q E_B P₀||_HS ≤ 2||B₀||_HS + ||B₁||_HS.       (1)
```

These Hilbert–Schmidt expressions are finite: `A P` is Hilbert–Schmidt because

```text
||A P||_HS² = Tr(P E_B P) = ℓ |Λ|/(2π) < ∞.
```

Similarly, the total width of the two edge intervals P₁ is 2d, so

```text
||B₁||_HS ≤ ||A P₁||_HS = √[ℓ(2d)/(2π)] ≈ 0.010555021.  (2)
```

Restrict the Q/P₀ kernel in the Hilbert–Schmidt norm to the rectangle

```text
k ∈ [15000,16000],       k' ∈ [13000,14000].
```

Each interval has width 1000, the first is outside Λ, the second lies inside P₀, and `|(k−k')ℓ/2|≤0.15`. Monotonicity of sinc on this interval yields

```text
||Q E_B P₀||_HS ≥ [1000 ℓ/(2π)] [sin(0.15)/0.15]
                   ≈ 0.015855878.                    (3)
```

Combining (1)–(3),

```text
||B₀||_HS ≥ (0.015855878…−0.010555021…)/2 > 0.0026504.
```

On P₀, the cosine packet is uniformly bounded below:

```text
|β(k)|² ≥ sin²[πd/(2b_R)]/b_R ≈ 2.41805×10⁻¹⁰.
```

Because `QAP` is Hilbert–Schmidt and β is bounded, Plancherel applied to the input frequency variable gives the exact identity

```text
L = (2π/ℓ) ∫_Λ dk' |β(k')|² ∫_(k∉Λ) dk |(QAP)(k,k')|².
```

Hence the finite-band interior alone gives

```text
L ≥ (2π/ℓ) [sin²(πd/(2b_R))/b_R] ||B₀||_HS²
  ≥ (2π/ℓ) [sin²(πd/(2b_R))/b_R]
       × { [1000ℓ sin(0.15)/(2π×0.15) − √(ℓd/π)] / 2 }²
  > 1.06×10⁻¹⁰.                                      (4)
```

The bracket is positive for the stated constants. This is a genuine continuum lower bound under the old time-POVM and product-preparation assumptions. It is *not* an estimate of the actual L, a lower bound on all other implementations, or a physical detector's accuracy. A better numerical or analytic estimate could be much larger or smaller than the finite-clock illustration below, subject to (4).

## 4. What the witness does and does not establish

Let `σ_old` be the full old instrument output with m retained, and `σ_new|hit` the normalized new-probe output with m retained after selecting its clock-bin hit. Trace out whichever old apparatus or reference factors are not being inspected. For the common spectral question Q,

```text
Tr[(I_m⊗Q) σ_old] = L > 1.06×10⁻¹⁰,
Tr[(I_m⊗Q) σ_new|hit] = 0,
D_trace(σ_old,σ_new|hit) ≥ L.
```

The two-outcome measurement `{Q,I−Q}` establishes the trace-distance lower bound; partial trace or retaining further systems cannot eliminate that witness. If the old dilation's scalar-fiber transport `U₀(t)` is performed before discarding t, Q commutes with every `U_R(t)`, so the same Q statistic is unchanged. This is not an assertion that every observable has the same matrix in every quantum reference frame: in a proposed frame transformation the observable must be transported along with the state.

The new probe has an explicit clock-no-bin result; the old T_m is a normalized relational instrument whose orbit-label environment is discarded and has no corresponding independent bin-hit flag. Thus the comparison is between the **old unconditional m instrument** and the **new m instrument conditioned on hit**, not between two realizations of an already-proved identical operation. The result confirms that the probability match did not close the retained-output gap. It is not a proof of an inconsistency in either instrument, nor a counterexample to frame equivalence for a single fixed instrument.

## 5. Finite cyclic control with equal memory probabilities

The [test](verification/zero_clock_probe_poststate_obstruction_test.py) also uses a different, finite periodic clock with 33 nonnegative energy levels, a seven-level selected band, `ℓ=1/7`, a cosine-like band state and a zero-Hamiltonian memory. It constructs the clock-bin effect from its discrete Fourier kernel and computes the square roots and a normalized orbit twirl directly.

For memory input probabilities `(0.3,0.7)`, both the old orbit-averaged and the hit-conditioned band-probe descriptions return **exactly** `(0.3,0.7)`. Yet the finite old clock output has outside-band probability approximately `0.728116`, while the band-probe output has zero; their clock-state trace distance is approximately `0.835189`. The finite clock bin-hit probability is approximately `0.130659`.

These numbers are for that expressly different finite periodic clock with a degenerate memory—not for the continuum R packet, the interacting two-window model or an experiment. The control demonstrates that a large poststate mismatch can coexist with identical record probabilities in a specified model. The continuum conclusion is only the proven positive lower bound (4).

## 6. Next step

There are two legitimate routes, each requiring an explicit common operation:

1. **Transport the factorwise probe itself:** retain its clock, memory, cursor and four outcomes, and construct the R-relative description of this same instrument and subsequent gauge-invariant measurements. Its band conservation and no-bin outcome must be preserved.
2. **Approximate the old T instrument:** replace its full clock square-root operation by a physical implementation and control its generated out-of-band disturbance, including the environment that carries the orbit label. If using a band-compressed approximation, include its leakage error rather than identifying it exactly with T.

Either route must retain the distinction between conditional probability agreement and full instrument/state agreement. The engineered clock operator, scalar-synchronized cursor, old global Q coupling, finite timing/resources, interacting and turning clocks, gravitational refoliation, actual physical energy and the experiential/constitutive bridge all remain open. This result narrows a concrete interface; it does not close the full frontier.
