# Why this zero level? Noncommuting symmetry, invariant rays and the sphere–echo

## The next why—and a positive answer with a definite scope

The preceding [experience-and-bedrock investigation](ZERO_EXPERIENCE_NECESSITY_AND_EXPLANATORY_BEDROCK.md) reached the question of how a single self-sufficient physical–experiential constitution could have an **objective zero**, without merely choosing a convenient reference level. The earlier [relational-gauge analysis](ZERO_TOTALITY_GAUGE_RELATIONAL_PRINCIPLE.md) found that an Abelian frame-shift generator can be shifted by a constant without changing the transformations. So why should its constraint level be zero?

There is an important conditional answer that goes deeper than imposing “zero” by hand:

> For a connected action whose Lie algebra is generated by its own commutators, a strictly equivariant set of generators—if it exists—cannot be shifted by arbitrary constants while retaining the same exact algebra. For the ordinary noncommuting rotation algebra, this fixes the additive zero of the three generators. If an honest representation of the full connected rotation group leaves a **pure state ray** invariant, the corresponding total generators annihilate that state, rather than merely having zero expectation.

This is a structural difference between a full non-Abelian symmetry and an individual Abelian shift. It supplies a genuine route to a distinguished **zero angular-momentum-type charge**, and allows nonzero internal relational structure. It does **not** show that the universe has this symmetry, that this charge is its energy, that its state is pure, or that the model's signs are experiences. The choice of group, its honest lift, physical action, and actual invariance remain to be explained by the proposed constitution.

The same common-rotation action is present in the existing spherical lift of the coherent echo. The new [verifier](verification/zero_perfect_symmetry_root.py) and [tests](verification/zero_perfect_symmetry_root_test.py) check its concrete angular-record vectors at the anchor, exact noncommuting generator algebra, alternative energy offsets, invariant mixed-state control, and a nontrivial local rotational example. This is a conditional mathematical advance on the specific **why zero rather than another level?** problem, not a completed no-residue account.

## 1. Why was the zero level arbitrary in the earlier mechanism?

For the previous local frame shifts, let `C_i` be a Hamiltonian generator of the ith transformation. Adding a constant `s_i` does not change its Hamiltonian vector field or Poisson brackets with observables:

```text
C_i  and  C_i − s_i
```

generate the same infinitesimal changes. If the components commute, the shifted generators also obey the same zero commutator algebra. But their zero-constraint surfaces differ: `C_i=0` versus `C_i=s_i`. Thus the frame symmetry alone cannot select the first surface. This was a specific, valid obstruction, not an argument that all symmetry mechanisms have the same freedom.

The deeper question is **why** this ambiguity occurs. The underlying shift algebra is Abelian: its commutators supply no relation that ties the value assigned to one generator to those assigned to others. A non-Abelian algebra can constrain the additive constants through its own composition law.

## 2. Why noncommuting generators can fix their own additive normalization

Let a connected symplectic model carry an infinitesimal Hamiltonian action of a Lie algebra g, with an **equivariant** moment map `μ`: for a consistent Poisson-bracket convention,

```text
d μ_ξ = contraction of the symplectic form with the vector field for ξ,
{μ_ξ, μ_η} = μ_[ξ,η].
```

Assume a second moment map produces the *same vector fields* and obeys the same exact equivariance relation. Because the phase space is connected, their difference is constant for each ξ:

```text
μ'_ξ = μ_ξ + c(ξ),
```

where c is a linear functional on g. Constants have zero Poisson brackets, so comparing the equivariance equations gives

```text
c([ξ,η]) = 0   for all ξ,η.
```

If the Lie algebra is **perfect**, meaning `[g,g]=g`, c must vanish. Thus the equivariant moment map, if one exists, has no additive ambiguity of this kind. The Lie algebra `so(3)` is perfect. An Abelian algebra has `[g,g]=0`, so this argument leaves all shifts available. A mixed algebra can retain shifts on its Abelian quotient.

This is a conditional mathematical theorem, not a claim that self-sufficiency creates a symplectic form or forces an equivariant action. The existence of a moment map, connectedness, strict equivariance, and which transformations are physically meaningful are assumptions. If the moment map has a central cocycle or the group action is only projective, the comparison must be reformulated; it is not valid to erase the cocycle without checking the physical representation.

### The same point in exact quantum algebra

For an honest finite-dimensional rotation representation, let the Hermitian generators satisfy

```text
[J_x,J_y]=i J_z,   [J_y,J_z]=i J_x,   [J_z,J_x]=i J_y,
```

in units with the algebra normalization fixed. Consider `J'_a=J_a+c_a I`. Then

```text
[J'_x,J'_y] − i J'_z = −i c_z I,
```

with the analogous cyclic equations. The primed generators obey the *same exact algebra* only if `c_x=c_y=c_z=0`. The verifier checks this symbolically for one, two and four spin-half factors. This fixes an additive reference **within that specified honest algebra and normalization**. It does not by itself fix the representation, its physical units, a Hamiltonian, or the existence of a zero-charge state. Additional operators in a commutant are not covered by the assumption that the two generators differ only by scalar constants.

## 3. Why an invariant pure ray can enforce a zero generator

Suppose a connected rotation group with perfect Lie algebra has an **honest, strongly continuous unitary representation** `U(g)`, and the pure ray of a normalized `|ψ⟩` is invariant under the complete group:

```text
U(g)|ψ⟩ = χ(g)|ψ⟩.
```

The group law makes χ a continuous one-dimensional unitary representation. Its infinitesimal character vanishes on all commutators; for a perfect Lie algebra it vanishes everywhere. Connectedness then makes χ trivial. Hence the vector itself is fixed and, for the specified honest generators,

```text
J_x|ψ⟩ = J_y|ψ⟩ = J_z|ψ⟩ = 0.
```

This uses the full connected group, not just one rotation axis. It strengthens the earlier warning that **ray invariance under a single Abelian generator** need not fix its eigenvalue: a spin-up ray is unchanged by z rotations while `J_z=+1/2` on its representative. It is not invariant under the full rotation group.

For finite matrices there is a direct proof. Full ray invariance implies `J_a|ψ⟩=λ_a|ψ⟩`. The expectation of `[J_x,J_y]` in this common eigenvector is zero, but the algebra says it is `iλ_z`; cyclically all three λ vanish. In an infinite-dimensional representation the group-character argument avoids applying unbounded products outside their domains; the resulting fixed vector belongs to each generator's domain and is annihilated by it.

The conclusion depends on the **honest lift**, not just a physical action on rays. A ray action can be described using phase-modified unitary representatives whose generators have central constants and no longer obey the displayed exact algebra. Establishing the appropriate strict representation and physical generator is part of the physics, not something inferred just from observing a ray. For spinorial examples, SU(2) is the natural cover; the two-spin integer representation also descends to SO(3). No claim about an arbitrary projective cosmological representation is implied.

### The mixed-state control

A rotation-invariant density matrix need not be supported in the zero-generator sector. For a single spin-half system, `ρ=I/2` is invariant under every rotation, while

```text
J² = (3/4) I,       Tr(ρ J²)=3/4.
```

Its first moments vanish, but it is not a singlet. Thus **mixed-state invariance**, a zero expectation and a pure invariant ray are three distinct claims. A closed whole may be represented by a pure vector in a candidate, but its actual purity is not established merely by writing one down.

## 4. Why zero need not erase internal differences

The simplest two-spin singlet is annihilated by all total `J_a`, while each local spin has nonzero variance. That was already established in [the zero-spin constitution](ZERO_CONSTRAINT_TO_UNIQUE_ENTANGLED_CONSTITUTION.md); the current result explains why the noncommuting algebra fixes its additive generator convention, and why full pure-ray invariance gives the kernel condition.

The same distinction appears geometrically in the **sphere carrier already used by the echo**. Let two internal unit directions be m,n and `u=m·n`. Common rotations leave u unchanged. For a smooth scalar state `ψ(m,n)=u`, let

```text
L_m = −i m×∇_m,      L_n = −i n×∇_n.
```

Then

```text
L_m ψ = −i(m×n),       L_n ψ = +i(m×n),
(L_m+L_n)ψ = 0,
```

while the individual terms are nonzero at generic nonparallel directions. In normalized product-area measure, `u` is uniform with density `du/2`, and

```text
||ψ||² = ∫ u² du/2 = 1/3,
||L_m ψ||² / ||ψ||² = 2.
```

The symbolic verifier checks the differential identity and these integrals. This smooth function is an **illustrative invariant-sector state**, not the actual discontinuous angular-bin preparation, and its local operators are model angular momenta, not felt values. A common frame rotation may be a gauge redundancy or an active symmetry depending on the theory; its mathematical invariance does not decide that interpretation.

There is no conflict with the positive physical pair already in the echo. That pair's exact zero is a separate constrained sign observable, whereas `L_m+L_n` is the generator of common rotations. Both can vanish in the same modeled history, but they are not thereby the same quantity, and neither is proven to be global energy or the net of actual experiences.

## 5. Does the actual modeled echo have the relevant symmetry?

In the existing [spherical invariant echo](ZERO_SYMMETRY_POINT_SPHERE_QUANTUM_PLENITUDE.md), the angular wavefunctions and their old-register amplitudes are functions of `u=m·n`; the pair-copy and record labels depend on u's bin. Common rotations leave u, its bins, the complete retained record vector, and the stated pair encoding unchanged. The 20-step clock, source and apparatus prefixes act on the other model factors and do not depend on the absolute directions. Thus the simultaneous-rotation representation acts trivially on the corresponding full lifted **candidate history**, under the assumptions of that lift.

The new tests independently evaluate the original complex echo vectors for three source labels, both pointer signs, four angular bins, and a nontrivial common rotation at the anchor. They reproduce equality of the complete old/echo amplitude slices, not merely equal probabilities. The continuous and full-history statements additionally rely on the earlier spherical-isometry and clock/pair constructions; they are not a new dense simulation of every source, apparatus and clock vector.

For a strongly continuous **honest** rotation representation on this spherical Hilbert space, invariance of that vector entails the common-rotation generators annihilate it. Even its step-function bin dependence does not obstruct this total-generator statement: a vector fixed by the entire strongly continuous group lies in each total generator's domain with eigenvalue zero. It need not lie in the domain of each individual sphere's generator separately; the smooth calculation above is not silently applied to the bin state.

This is a stronger statement than zero expectation of an arbitrarily defined signed value. Yet it is still conditional on the stipulated sphere lift and common rotational symmetry. It does not show that actual spacetime has a global angular momentum, that physical observers can implement the transformation, or that all correlations relevant to actual subjects are invariant under it.

## 6. Ask why again: what did this really explain?

The new result repairs one specific weak link: **the zero reference of a full noncommuting generator algebra need not be an independently chosen additive constant**, if a strict equivariant lift is already justified. But several deeper dependencies remain:

1. **Why this group and action?** The echo's `SO(3)` action and the spherical metric/measure are model structure. The prior [point-and-direction why-chain](ZERO_POINT_INTRINSIC_DIRECTION_WHY_CHAIN.md) showed that a point or generic first-order algebra does not itself determine them. No-preferred-axis motivation is not a derivation that the complete actual physical–experiential law has this group.
2. **Why is the complete state an invariant pure ray?** Invariance of laws alone permits nonsinglet states; an invariant mixed state need not have zero Casimir. Records and actual qualitative distinctions must transform appropriately. The construction supplies a particular invariant candidate, not its necessary actuality.
3. **Why this representation and unique organization?** The exact zero sector for two spin-half factors is one-dimensional, but for four factors it is two-dimensional. Both independent four-spin singlet pairings have zero total generators; their relative relational observables distinguish states within the sector. Zero charge and rotational invariance therefore need not determine the whole. This is an exact control, not a claim that those formal states are alternate actual universes.
4. **Why would this charge be energy?** Rotation charge is associated with an internal rotation, not automatically the generator of physical time translations. Even if both vanish in a larger theory, their identity needs an independent argument. A shift `H→H+cI` changes an ordinary time-evolution unitary only by a global phase and leaves its density-matrix dynamics unchanged. Time translations form an Abelian group, so the perfect-algebra normalization argument does not apply to H. Gravity, boundaries, an objective whole-level energy and any legitimate zero require their own analysis.
5. **Why experience and incidence?** A singlet or a rotation-invariant state is not automatically a subject, feeling or measure over subjects. A genuine first-person positive witness retains evidential force, but a physical generator, a formal inverse or equal Born weights does not turn into lived positive or negative value. Real suffering is not erased by a mathematical sum.

The foundational target remains **one** self-sufficient nature, not five external ingredients. The points above isolate which claims a proposed identity must actually derive or make intelligible. A mathematical representation and its logical constraints may be descriptions of aspects of the same nature; that possibility is neither proved nor refuted by identifying their roles.

## 7. The deeper root: choice of explanatory symmetry

Why could the algebra fix the additive constants? Because a commutator of two transformations reproduces a third, so the values assigned to the three generators cannot be shifted independently while preserving an honest representation. Why was there no analogous result for a shift? Because its commutator algebra has no such relation. Why does that still not constitute ultimate bedrock? Because the applicability of the algebra, its lift, the actual state and the meaning of its charges are not consequences of the algebra's internal consistency alone.

This is a productive change in the root-cause diagnosis. It is **not** true that every zero level is necessarily conventional or arbitrary: noncommuting symmetry can remove an additive ambiguity under precise premises. Nor is it true that a distinguished zero in one mathematical representation automatically is the objective zero of the whole. The next why is whether the same self-sufficient zero nature can require the complete symmetry and its invariant physical–experiential realization, and whether it connects that rotational result to the user's proposed whole-level quantity without assuming the desired answer.

The user's complete-intelligibility objective, actual experiential datum, and need to preserve local plurality and suffering remain intact. We have not proved the existence or impossibility of ultimate explanatory bedrock, or reopened the settled case against the relevant infinity proposal.
