# Fixed retrospective plan: laser-pain supplementary ratings

## Purpose and stop rule

Before examining the author-supplied single-trial CSV values, inspect their schema and provenance and determine whether the 95-person laser experiment can support a transparent baseline for single-trial pain **intensity** reports at varying individualized laser intensities. The dataset article states participants report pain intensity, not separate unpleasantness; it is therefore not a direct test of the candidate's unpleasantness postulate or the four-spin q. Raw/preprocessed EEG is not assumed accessible. The aim is to specify and, only if the fields genuinely support it, compute a stimulus-conditional reference for a future **same-trial** physiological competition. This is exploratory secondary analysis, not preregistration or evidence for subject constitution.

## Source and required checks

Use pinned `OpenNeuroDatasets/ds005293` version 1.0.0 at `66a97a6f92d49f1ac9742fdcafa052cfd2fcc3f0` and the article's author supplement `XiangyueZhao/Supplement-Data` at `daf9f0b83df047c506cc4714d39551b99980d537`. We have inspected repo trees, README and event schema but not event TSV values, supplementary CSV headers or rows. Read the article's methods/data/validation text and the author code that generates these CSVs. Verify downloaded bytes against pinned Git blob hashes. Establish meaning and independent source of each trial rating, stimulus condition, participant, channel/time or other variables before scoring; do not silently equate preprocessing metadata with actual labels or targets, use trial categories as reports, or concatenate data at the wrong unit.

If rows don't unambiguously identify participant, condition and corresponding actual trial rating on an interpretable common scale, **do not compute** scores. Report exact obstruction; inspect an aggregate CSV only as a separate source and do not treat it as a raw-data replay. Missing or unparseable values are excluded with recorded counts. Document every additional assumption and post-inspection amendment.

## Bounded comparisons, if eligible

Using only single-trial values, predefine leave-one-participant-out cross-validation and ordinary MAE in report-scale units (a) the pooled mean of other participants' ratings, (b) other-participant mean for the same stimulus intensity **category** (not a globally calibrated physical dose if individual calibration applies), falling back to pooled mean for unseen categories, and (c) when side is independently recorded, the other-participant mean for the same intensity-category-and-side pair with the same fallback. Equal weighting at trial level and additionally equal participant weighting; no EEG, response, person-specific calibration, validation or future held-out rows in training. Compute participant-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals for the paired difference (a) versus (b), with a fixed 20261031 seed and 2000 resamples, or explicitly suppress interval if too few participants. Show coverage, missingness, data granularity, rating distribution and sample counts. Association or prediction does not determine causality or the phenomenal basis. Separately report whether category is a nominal individualized condition rather than measured joules.

If the fixed interpretation is unavailable, the exercise remains a source-readiness audit, not a replacement selected after seeing values. No rating-derived grouping except target summaries. Never report an unvalidated extracted EEG measure or global EEG/experience association. Held-out participants are a stricter generalization setting than subject-calibrated clinical prediction; the baseline is not a performance ceiling. No selective change of sample or metric based on results.

## Implications and controls

Compare with ds008349's cross-modal intensity/unpleasantness distinction and the Rainville affect/intensity dissociation, explicitly citing prior scopes. Record that sensor phase, connectivity and coupling-preference identification remain unresolved; even a valid physiologic correlation would be compatible with report, salience, sensory, arousal and experiential-ground/neutral-ground alternatives. A stronger B4/B5/F2 or B10 verdict would require an independently anchored condition-valid subjective quality/unity assay, real physical mismatch operationalization and rival-discriminating prespecified evidence; a rating baseline or association does not supply these.
