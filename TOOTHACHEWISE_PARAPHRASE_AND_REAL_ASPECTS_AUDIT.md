# Toothachewise paraphrase: preserving pain without assuming the bridge

## Decision

Benovsky's optional eliminativism is best tested as a proposal to eliminate a **composite toothache object**, not as a proposal to deny that pain is felt. But his book's short toothachewise paraphrase does not itself specify a complete phenomenal-preserving translation. His general insistence on actual qualitative experience strongly motivates that reading; it is not the same as demonstrating exactly how the translation works. A successful paraphrase can treat pain as primitive, or as genuinely constituted without adding a separately existing pain object. It cannot establish preservation merely by renaming a physical configuration, predicting pain reports, or asserting that two descriptions concern one thing.

The most useful new comparison is with Le Bihan's realist-aspect rejoinder. His criticism puts pressure on whether non-reified aspects explain anything beyond two ways of describing a neutral ground; it does **not** prove that experience requires a second object, that Benovsky denies experience, or that realist aspects have solved the quality or subject-composition problem. Our own experiential-ground conjecture owes the same non-circular account of the particular quality and occurrence it claims to retain.

## 2. A preservation test that does not require a pain-object

Take a concrete case in which a person is actually undergoing a particular felt toothache, with its qualitative character and whatever concurrent lived relations are genuinely present. This is an experiential datum for the case, not something made true by a behavior code. Let `E` express the full relevant first-personal claim, **without deciding its metaphysical form**: no commitment to a separate subject, a composite object, an additional property, or the claim that every pain has the same hedonic character. Let `A` express a proposed toothachewise plural predication of the fundamental phental entities or their organization. These are schematic sentences, not an account of the underlying physics.

To evaluate a translation, ask for the following separately:

1. **Truth and occurrence.** What is the content of `A`, and on what basis does the account warrant that `A` is true exactly when the intended `E` holds in its declared domain? This is a biconditional *obligation*, not an assumption that the biconditional is true. The right-hand side need not quantify over a composite pain. A plural predicate, adverbial occurrence or suitably explained identity could be used, but its truth conditions and existential commitments need to be explicit.
2. **Qualitative discrimination.** If a relevant change in the actual feel is warranted, the translated account must represent the contrast, including temporal and joint-experiential organization where present. It must not assume that unpleasantness, sensory intensity, attention and report always coincide. It may use the phenomenal character directly as a primitive description; that preserves rather than derives it.
3. **Unity and incidence.** State which aspects occur together, which occurrences are distinct, and how their boundaries and persistence are handled, without assuming an extra owner or treating a spatial address as a subject. Do not require facts the experience does not disclose. The [episode-incidence analysis](EPISODE_INCIDENCE_REFORMULATION.md) separates content, temporal extent and lineage.
4. **Counterfactual and scope discipline.** Specify the domain and admissible interventions, including changes of organization or context, and ask what happens to both the felt characterization and the physical description. A match in one case is weaker than a lawful, justified relation; a stipulation about unobserved cases is not evidence they are metaphysically possible.
5. **Explanatory level.** Separate preserving a true claim, giving a sufficient domain-specific translation, establishing a constitutive identity, and explaining the ground and actuality of the relation. One can succeed at one and leave the next primitive. See [common constitution](COMMON_CONSTITUTION_CLOSURE.md).

These are adequacy conditions for this project's preservation target, not a theorem that every ordinary paraphrase must retain every conceivable detail. If the target is merely the truth of a coarser ordinary pain sentence, the preservation burden may be narrower; the full framework still needs the finer facts where warranted.

### Three readings of the same slogan

| Interpretation | What does the work? | Result and residual burden |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical/report substitution:** `A` is defined wholly by an independently specified physical configuration and pain-related dispositions. | A causal or behavioral description alone. | It can predict reports. By itself it has not justified `E ↔ A`; this is not proof that physicalism is false or that no physical identity could be justified. Calling the configuration painful does not supply that warrant. |
| **Phenomenal-preserving primitive:** `A` includes or is fixed by a genuinely felt, first-personal condition, perhaps primitive plural predication, with no composite object added. | Actual phenomenal occurrence is a basic truth condition. | It can preserve pain without adding a pain-object and without behavioral reduction. It does not derive that occurrence from a separately specified physical arrangement; any promised constitutive explanation remains to be given. If `A` is simply defined by `E`, translation is assured but the physical bridge is not. |
| **Constitutive phental account:** `A` is a concrete phental organization with physical and felt descriptions, and an independently motivated account identifies the felt occurrence with or as intrinsic to that organization. | A substantive account of the one concrete nature and its differentiations, not a second causal influence. | Potentially retains occurrence and removes a composite object. It must explain or independently warrant why *this* organization is *this* particular felt episode, and how the relevant incidence and interventions work. The present sources do not complete that demonstration. |

The second and third readings are candidate reconstructions, not claims that Benovsky explicitly supplies these formal definitions. His own position is more nuanced than either a raw behaviorist slogan or a requirement to add a separately existing pain. Eliminating an object does not automatically eliminate occurrence; conversely, using a phenomenally loaded word does not prove that a non-phenomenal physical description entails the intended occurrence.

## 3. What Le Bihan actually argues

In §1 Le Bihan contrasts an observed co-variation constraint with the apparent difference between phenomenal and physical descriptions. He proposes that aspects be distinct yet necessarily connected as aspects of a common neutral first-order property. His blanket remarks about brains and mental states are philosophical generalizations, not an independently established universal empirical law. Nor is necessity secured merely by calling two items aspects.

In §§2–3 he rejects identifying aspects with distinct first-order or second-order properties, arguing that doing so recreates property-dualist causal, closure and overdetermination problems. In §4 he offers instead primitive, mind-independent **aspects of first-order properties**. They are real but not extra first- or second-order properties in his scheme; 'mind-independent' is clarified as independent of an *observer*, not a denial of their mental character. §5 develops a weak panpsychist/panprotopsychist proposal: ubiquitous proto-mentality has qualitative character but not subjectivity, while organized systems can be subjective. He holds that this avoids summing microsubjects and that conditions for full-blown subjectivity are an empirical question. §6 concludes that anti-realism loses the distinctive explanatory work of aspects and collapses into neutral monism.

His reasoning is a substantive challenge rather than a demonstrated dichotomy:

- His §4 defines anti-realism strongly, as no aspect entities at all and two conceptual terminologies. Benovsky's earlier §4 also allows an **unknown nature given in two ways** and even suggests that neutral versus phental monism may be terminological. It is not enough to show that distinct, reified aspect-entities are absent; one must also show that a non-reified account cannot characterize actual qualitative appearance or any non-arbitrary difference between modes of access. Whether the result deserves the label *neutral monism* is partly classificatory; the explanatory issue persists under either name.
- The anti-realist can grant that felt pain is actual without claiming a separate mental property or independent aspect-object. Yet invoking actual appearance cannot be a complete explanation if the relationship between that appearance and its purported ground is left undeveloped. Realism also incurs a burden: postulating primitive aspects and necessary connection specifies a proposal; it does not establish the necessity, particular quality pairing, or causal relevance by definition.
- Le Bihan's §2 claim that separate properties must be contingently connected, and his causal dilemma, depend on contested modal and causal assumptions. A property dualist might instead posit necessary correlations or noncompeting realization; this does not automatically make that alternative correct. Likewise, rejecting separate causes can avoid an *additional* overdetermining event if the identity is justified; it does not establish which phenomenal differences are causally relevant.
- His §5 proposal makes a distinctive assumption: **qualitative character can occur without subjectivity**, and subjectivity arises through organization. That assumption and the claim that empirical science can settle the organizational conditions require argument. Avoiding combination of tiny subjects is a conditional benefit, not a demonstrated derivation of a particular unified point of view or qualitative organization. His proto-mental qualitative ingredients should not be silently equated with Benovsky's book-level non-experiential proto-phental basis.

## 4. Same pain case, rival explanatory commitments

| Account | How it can respect the felt case | What remains to be established |
|---|---|---|
| **Benovsky's book-level no-bearer / optional eliminativism** | First-personal experience need not have a reified bearer; on the natural preservation-oriented reading, toothachewise plurality is not just pain behavior. | Give the positive truth conditions for the optional paraphrase, the macro phenomenal character and its individuation from proto-phental organization. His basic no-bearer thesis does not entail the stronger elimination. |
| **Project's experiential ground / field-first candidate** | Treat a genuinely first-personal differentiated occurrence as basic or intrinsic to one concrete nature, without positing a second causal occurrence. | Justify occurrence, actual field boundary and the identity/constitution of a particular quality with the specified physical organization; explain or openly count primitives. The [non-additivity argument](NONADDITIVE_CONSTITUTION_ARGUMENT.md) is conditional and does not establish the identity or a quality law. |
| **Le Bihan's real aspects** | Observer-independent primitive aspects accommodate qualitative character; subjectivity is associated with suitable organization. | Explain the required aspect relation, quale-to-organization correspondence and the qualitative-without-subjectivity assumption; defend causal treatment. Ubiquity of mental aspects does not entail that rocks think. |
| **Neutral-monist alternative** | A common neutral basis, plus a defended account of how actual experiential occurrence is expressed or constituted, can retain pain; neutrality does not mean absence of experience. | Specify the neutral basis, emergence/constitution or primitive occurrence, and why the appropriate physical and first-person descriptions apply to the same case. Simply naming a neutral basis does not do this. |
| **Experience-respecting physical identity / dualism** | A physicalist can argue for a non-obvious identity without reducing pain to reports; a dualist can hold felt pain real and not identical with the physical. | Each must justify its identity or psychophysical connection and address the explanatory and causal objections appropriate to it. Neither is excluded merely by this paraphrase audit. |

The categories overlap: a theory may combine no-bearer subjectivity with aspects or a neutral ground. This table compares burdens, not exhaustive mutually exclusive metaphysical systems. Our preferred candidate receives no explanatory exemption for being one-natured.

## 5. A non-circular next step on an actual interface

A productive test is not another unconstrained model with invented pain labels. Use the existing [matched-stimulus unpleasantness analysis](MATCHED_STIMULUS_UNPLEASANTNESS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_BRIDGE.md) as a **starting data source**, not as an already measured physical-phenomenal bridge. Before any new analysis:

1. Specify a common domain of actual, time-aligned episodes and the intended contrasts: sensory pain intensity, unpleasantness and, if supported by the instrument, experienced temporal/joint context. Report scales are fallible evidence for these distinct targets, not the targets by definition. Audit source timing, missingness and response instruments before treating observations as paired.
2. Fix an independently measurable physical candidate and its interventions, including the project's specific mismatch quantity only if interaction preferences and histories can actually be identified. Retain a non-mismatch comparator and plausible context, expectation and intensity controls. Do not infer internal mismatch from stimulus level or a single EEG proxy.
3. Before looking at held-out outcomes, state distinct predictions on the same cases, exclusions, uncertainty, and a criterion for whether a candidate fails to transport across contexts or people. Use participant-aware validation and genuinely held-out contexts where feasible. Register a stop if the source lacks the required physical or experiential alignment; no result should be manufactured from labels alone.
4. Interpret outcome carefully: a robust report contrast inconsistent with a candidate's prespecified mapping challenges that mapping **conditional on measurement validity**. A good prediction supports a useful correspondence, but does not itself prove a constitutive identity, rule out all experience-respecting alternatives, resolve subject incidence, or explain why the quality is felt. Direct first-person evidence and third-person measurements contribute differently; neither is an infallible metaphysical oracle.

No such new data analysis or validated assay is claimed here. This is an actionable interface to existing research, not a preregistration, an experimental result or a claim that Le Bihan's and Benovsky's ontologies make different predictions as stated. To discriminate the ontologies empirically, they must first generate different testable predictions or stronger independent explanatory commitments on the same domain; otherwise comparative appraisal remains philosophical.

## Status

The source reading strengthens an experience-respecting comparison and corrects any suggestion that non-reification alone refutes phenomenal reality. It does not establish the pain translation, choose among ontologies, or derive the preferred ground. C3 still requires full structural **and** experiential intelligibility; B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 remain open. No new formal verifier is added: the already proved fiber-constancy result in [common constitution](COMMON_CONSTITUTION_CLOSURE.md) captures the relevant sufficiency/identity distinction, while further toy assignments would not warrant actual pain.
