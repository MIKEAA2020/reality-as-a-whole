## What should not be imported as an achieved deduction

1. **All formal structures are concrete.** The fact that no explanation of a restriction has yet been supplied does not show that every consistent description is concretely instantiated. An intelligibly determining constitution is another candidate and also needs justification. Actualization of all metaphysically possible total worlds is compatible with exactly one possible total world; it does not entail an abundance of distinct total worlds. See [COMPLETE_INTELLIGIBILITY_ARGUMENT_AUDIT.md](COMPLETE_INTELLIGIBILITY_ARGUMENT_AUDIT.md) and [SIMULATION_PLENITUDE_CONSOLIDATION.md](SIMULATION_PLENITUDE_CONSOLIDATION.md).
2. **Mathematical necessity establishes concrete existence.** The truth of an arithmetic statement or a formal consistency proof does not by itself demonstrate an experienced concrete occurrence. Actual experience is an epistemic datum; the impossibility of stating or entertaining nothing without an actual speaker does not prove that absolute nonbeing is impossible. The stronger thesis of necessary concrete actuality remains a target, not refuted or established by that move.
3. **Plenitude has zero information content.** 'All strings' can have a compact generative description in a specified encoding; that is not a zero-bit theorem in ordinary Kolmogorov complexity, nor does a short description make its concrete interpretation or observer weighting self-explanatory.
4. **Contradictions dynamically cancel and generate logic.** The source supplies no formal dynamics, cancellation rule or account of why it applies. A model that discards inconsistent states would already use a formal framework and a selection rule; it might illuminate a restricted model but would not, without further argument, ground logic itself. A literal process of things being 'attempted' also risks importing the external time the proposal meant to avoid.
5. **Self-reference or information integration is experience.** This is a philosophical identification hypothesis, not a derivation of the presence, quality, unity or ownership of experience. It must be argued and compared with alternatives; experience should not be dismissed when a computational model cannot supply it.
6. **Observers derive physics by compression.** Finite access and predictive compression can be useful internal models of perception. They do not imply that laws are merely mental algorithms, that quantum outcomes are observer-triggered rendering, or that the speed of light is a literal universal processor clock. Operational and empirical claims require a model that recovers tested physics.
7. **Plenitude or simplicity settles Bostrom's probability.** The original inference is conditional on observer implementation, population, evidence and weighting. Neither infinite computational descriptions nor a Solomonoff-style prior alone proves simulated observers dominate, or fixes the relevant measure. Local probability, where genuinely required, must be justified; it is not an automatic cosmic lottery.

## Concrete research use

When an account says that something follows 'without hardware', ask which of these it has actually established:

- **Specification:** a mathematically well-defined structure or relation.
- **Concrete occurrence:** what makes it an actual event, mode or aspect rather than merely a description.
- **Physical organization:** how its empirically successful physical relations arise, including their conditions of applicability.
- **Finite perspective:** how determinate quality, ownership, temporal character and limitation are constituted.
- **Ultimate intelligibility:** why the actuality and these relations have the stated nature without an independent unexplained selector.

These are obligations for evaluating an account, not assertions that five independent ontological objects or sequential causal stages exist. The goal is a common intrinsic constitution, not a stack of extra entities. For the present framework, the research priority remains constructing and testing the whole–perspective relation and recovering physical order from it; declaring the whole 'self-executing' does not perform that work. None of the five obligations is discharged merely by renaming the absolute as computation.
