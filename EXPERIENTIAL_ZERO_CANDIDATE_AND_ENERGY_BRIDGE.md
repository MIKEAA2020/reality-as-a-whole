# A candidate experiential zero: suffering, pleasure and the energy bridge

## The candidate

The user asks whether suffering could be **negative experience**, and agrees with pursuing relational or emergent time. The constructive candidate explored here is:

> Real experiences have intrinsically positive, negative, neutral or mixed qualitative character. Their appropriately defined contributions to a whole-level experiential quantity balance to zero. Suffering is real negative character within that whole; it is not the nonexistence of experience. The whole's zero, its experienced differences and their relations would ultimately be one self-subsistent constitution rather than separate externally imposed facts.

This is a proposal for the user's broader experiential quantity, not a claim that a standard psychological valence score already measures the whole of existence. I make one particular additive version precise below, then test what it explains and what would still have to be established. It is not the only way zero-totality could work, and it is not a completed zero-residue account.

## 1. A first explicit quantity

For a **finite provisional domain** of actual experiential occurrences, suppose each occurrence i has nonnegative quantities:

```text
p_i = its positive experiential contribution,
n_i = its negative experiential contribution,
r_i = any experiential contribution not captured by those two directions.
```

Here p and n are assumed to use a common, objective unit; r is included to keep neutral or otherwise non-valenced experience available. These are proposed intrinsic quantities, not readouts established by current psychology. The components may coexist within a mixed experience; a label or a formal number alone does not create an experiencer. The rule determining actual subjects, their boundaries and their contents is not supplied here.

The simplest signed candidate is

```text
q_i = p_i - n_i,
Q_X = sum_i q_i = P - N,
P = sum_i p_i,    N = sum_i n_i.
```

On the user's conjectured zero-totality, require `Q_X=0`. Then `P=N`. If some negative component occurs (`N>0`), a positive component occurs (`P>0`) as well. If an **entire local occurrence** has negative net contribution `q_i<0`, zero total also implies that at least one other occurrence has `q_j>0`. The latter does not follow merely from `n_i>0`: a mixed occurrence might also have an equal or larger positive component.

This supplies the requested negative-experience candidate: a painful occurrence can have `n_i>p_i` and contribute negatively, while its felt suffering remains fully actual. It does not show that the physical universe has a well-defined P or N, that their magnitudes match, or that suffering is needed or justified. Positive and negative contributions may be aspects of different subjects, or of different occurrences within one subject; no cosmic subject or interpersonal transfer of feeling is assumed.

### Zero is not absence

If r is also expressed in the same unit, a formal index of total represented experiential magnitude would be `A=sum_i(p_i+n_i+r_i)`. This comparability is a further illustrative assumption, not an established physical measure. In the stipulated representation, `A>0` records a nonzero contribution even when `Q_X=0`. For example, one occurrence with `(p,n,r)=(1,0,0)` and another with `(0,1,0)` give `Q_X=0`, `A=2`. A single mixed occurrence `(1,1,0)` also gives `Q_X=0`, `A=2`; so a zero global Q does not establish two subjects, two occurrences, or a particular pairing.

A genuinely neutral experience might have `(0,0,r)` with `r>0`: it has zero signed q while remaining actual in the proposed representation. This is why **the signed quantity by itself does not distinguish all possible experiential constitutions**. The user has not required one number to be an injective encoding of every fact; a scalar can apply to everything without individually identifying everything. But explaining the whole from zero requires more than noting that this particular scalar vanishes, because its particular qualitative organization is not specified by its sum.

## 2. What makes the number objective rather than fitted?

The candidate makes a nontrivial hypothesis: positive and negative intensities have a **common unit and relative calibration**, fixed by the actual experiential constitution rather than chosen afterward to make the sum zero. Multiplying all p and n by one positive constant changes units but preserves `Q_X=0`. Multiplying only n by an arbitrary constant generally changes the verdict.

To see the burden, suppose that only separate positive and negative totals P and N are known, both nonzero, and compare the family

```text
Q_alpha = P - alpha N,     alpha > 0.
```

There is always a choice `alpha=P/N` making `Q_alpha=0`, regardless of the original imbalance. Thus “some scaling yields zero” would be a weak post-hoc statement. By contrast, if the shared scale and relative coefficient were independently fixed by an intrinsic law or by well-anchored operational consequences, `P=N` would be a substantive constraint rather than a fit.

Deriving the relative scale from a deeper self-subsistent principle is not necessarily circular merely because it yields zero; it becomes uninformative if the only reason for the chosen scale is to make an already-assumed balance true and it has no other grounding or implications. We must not assume that pain and pleasure have equal magnitudes just because they are described using opposite signs. Subjective reports can anchor aspects of experience, but report scales, interpersonal comparison, token counting and magnitudes do not automatically supply a unique physical measure.

**Zero net is not the same as moral neutrality.** The equation does not make a subject's suffering less real or establish that an equal numerical positive contribution compensates it ethically. Nor does it imply that reality is a best possible world or contains an agent choosing to impose suffering.

## 3. Whole-level balance is not a law of local time

The user agrees with relational or emergent time, so the quantity should not presuppose an external clock. One proposal is to aggregate experiential occurrences across the **whole internal history** using an intrinsically defined domain and weighting. Whether such a measure exists, and how it avoids double counting subjects or infinite divergences, remains open.

Even if a meaningful total across an entire finite history is zero, it need not be zero at every internal time or for every local subject. For an illustrative relational ordering, two collections of contributions could have

```text
first internal slice:  +2 - 1 = +1,
second internal slice: -2 + 1 = -1,
whole history:                    0.
```

The labels here are arithmetic, not observations or a fundamental foliation. Nor does a zero sum over a history by itself prove conservation between slices, fix a current, predict local changes, or establish lived succession. Conversely, an assumed conservation law or a zero value on every slice would be a stronger condition and would need a physically and experientially justified clock or foliation where applicable. For an infinite history, convergence and measure must be addressed rather than cancelling infinities by hand.

## 4. An exact test of the simplest energy identity

To connect the experiential proposal to the user's global energy idea, consider the very strong **local** hypothesis that, for the same region A and a fixed nonzero conversion constant gamma,

```text
E_matter(A) = gamma Q_X(A),
```

where `E_matter` is a nonnegative local matter-energy quantity and `Q_X(A)` is the signed experiential contribution in that region. If the same rule applies to two admissible regions with `Q_X(A)>0` and `Q_X(B)<0`, one of `gamma Q_X(A)` and `gamma Q_X(B)` is negative regardless of gamma's sign. Therefore this simple proportionality cannot give nonnegative matter energy in both regions.

This is a conditional constraint on that **particular local identification**, not a disproof of your proposal. Positive local matter energy is not the same as a full gravitationally corrected energy, and a global energy balance can contain other terms. A stronger theory could relate energy and experience through a common underlying constitution, a constraint, gravitational or boundary contributions, or a more general map rather than a local proportionality. Such terms need not be external substances or brute additions—but the account must determine and independently identify them, rather than introduce an unconstrained correction solely to obtain any desired answer.

For instance, writing `E(A)=gamma Q_X(A)+B(A)` places all the explanatory work in B unless its content and transformation are specified independently. It could be a genuine intrinsically determined boundary/relational term; it could also be a post-hoc residual. The equation alone does not decide. Likewise, `Q_X=0` and a separate `Q_energy=0` do not prove that the two zeros are the same underlying fact.

The physical [FLRW constraint example](ZERO_TOTALITY_FLRW_CONSTRAINT_AND_ENERGY.md) shows that a zero gravitational constraint can coexist with positive local matter energy and relational change. It does **not** identify that constraint with this experiential Q or prove that the actual universe has either proposed global total.

## 5. How could this candidate gain evidence and explanatory force?

A useful development would specify, without assuming a cosmic observer:

1. **Phenomenal anchor:** what distinguishes a positive, negative, neutral or mixed component in the experience itself; how the proposal respects actual suffering and other qualitative content.
2. **Constitution and incidence:** what real organization constitutes an occurrence and its subject, including why non-experiencing descriptions are not simply assigned experiential tokens. The existence of one experiential whole does not automatically make every physical subsystem a distinct subject.
3. **Common scale and domain:** how positive and negative magnitudes and subject weights are fixed across contexts, and whether a whole-level finite or infinite sum is invariant and defined. If the quantity is supposed to apply to *all* existence, explain how non-subject aspects of the whole are included or otherwise accounted for; do not assume each is a subject.
4. **A genuine balance principle:** derive `Q_X=0` from the proposed nature of the whole, or supply independent grounds for the law. The present candidate *assumes* balance; no-external-creator and the presence of both pleasant and unpleasant experiences do not by themselves imply it.
5. **Physical recovery:** define a non-vacuous mapping to actual physical quantities and clock-relative records, including what the theory predicts beyond the balance it was designed to express. A local sign-proportionality need not be that map.

One might test a **particular** physical balance law in a restricted setting by predefining independently measurable experiential proxies, physical quantities and any boundary contributions, then comparing predictions under controlled conditions. Proxies and reports do not directly expose another subject's qualitative state, and no finite experiment measures the entire whole. A failed local proportionality would rule out that specified identification, not every zero-totality theory. Positive empirical support for a mapping would also fall short of proving its metaphysical necessity or self-subsistence.

## 6. Result and next step

The proposal now has a concrete form: real positive and negative experiential components can have a zero signed whole while neutral, mixed and suffering experiences remain real. Within that form, the crucial new issues are the objective relative scale and the connection to physical energy. Simple local identification of signed experiential contribution with nonnegative matter energy fails under the conditions in §4; a deeper identity remains open.

The decisive foundational question is whether the common scale, the balance and the particular experienced organization are all intrinsic consequences of one self-subsistent zero constitution. This report assumes them for analysis; it neither proves them nor establishes that they cannot be derived. It does not lower the no-residue demand, or establish that zero is the only viable self-subsistent candidate. The next substantive task is to propose and assess an intrinsic principle fixing the positive/negative relation **before** using it to declare the global total zero.
