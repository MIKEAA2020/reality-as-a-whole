# After the Roy data: decision and framework critical path

## Decision

**Move on from the Roy analysis as the main line of work.** The user reports that the authors cannot help with this study. Do not present author clarification as a pending dependency likely to be resolved. The supplied S09 data and S08 scans 1, 3 and 5 have yielded a useful *negative control result*: on the latter participant, the frozen S09-trained EEG rule matched the timing-only baseline overall, with different outcomes across scans ([results](ROY_S08_FIXED_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md)). This is not evidence against the whole framework, against the published SSVEP method, or against all report decoders.

Further work on those same runs—electrode selection, thresholds or exploring another feature—would be exploratory after the scores are known. It would not repair the missing MRI timing anchor or deliver the independent experiential calibration the framework needs. A subject-level analysis on genuinely uninspected participants could be worthwhile for a dedicated measurement study, but would require data, a frozen protocol and a realistic budget; another single participant is unlikely by itself to resolve the central framework gaps. Do not download further participants as an automatic continuation of this branch. The existing author-email draft is historical and unsent in this workspace; the user's statement does not establish who contacted whom or what specific records exist elsewhere.

A genuinely new, independently documented timing anchor or independently calibrated experiential assay could justify revisiting the data. Without that, no EEG–fMRI temporal contrast should be rescued by selecting an offset for fit. The MRI checks are format/consistency QC, not a completed fMRI analysis. Raw data remain private and the release's research/noncommercial conditions apply.

## What “pass the battery” means

The [frozen dossier](CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_BATTERY_DOSSIER.md) distinguishes 12 substantive gates from its executable regression subset. The latter's successful script exits only show that selected bounded checks ran; they are **not a pass of the philosophical, empirical or physics gates**. The project must not improve its battery score by stipulating premises, relaxing criteria after the fact, or treating a finite model as a universal proof.

These tasks can constrain one another in parallel. This ordering is a research strategy, not proof that B1 can be solved first, that the framework is true, or that a final account is attainable. The target remains a timeless, self-sufficient, intrinsically experiential whole, plural genuine subjects, strongest genuine possibility = actuality, and no external creator or temporal actualization mechanism. The non-optimality implication remains conditional on the adopted premises. The former universal-agent target is not silently satisfied by calling a non-agentive whole conscious.

## Immediate B1–B2 work contract

1. **Choose one actual candidate principle**, not a placeholder for the desired conclusion. Specify precisely whether its claims concern logical truth, mathematical consistency, metaphysical necessity, concrete occurrence or phenomenal incidence.
2. **Build an explicit premise ledger:** distinguish observed experience, assumed global principle, proposed constitution, and inference rules; identify whether any premise already asserts concrete experiential existence or necessity in the intended sense. There is no obligation to reject a primitive solely because it is primitive, but it cannot count as having been explained by a restatement.
3. **Challenge it with the same interpretations:** a formal description with no concrete instances; a unique formal model without an interpretation as concrete experience; and two distinct experiential assignments over the same physical structure. A formal countermodel shows logical non-entailment under the stated semantics, not that its world is genuinely metaphysically possible.
4. **Specify the bridge that would defeat each countermodel**, assess whether the bridge follows from the candidate rather than being renamed into it, and document consequences for plural subjects and strongest possibility. If the necessary bridge cannot be justified, retain B1/B2 as open and compare alternatives. Do not introduce a conversion of possible things into actual things.
5. **Run adversarial review and existing finite regressions** on the exact written claims, then update the gate verdict—not the frozen dossier or its original battery criteria. A novel claim requires checking relevant prior work before being characterized as new.

The first step is now in [the necessity-route comparison](FOUNDATION_NECESSITY_ROUTES_COMPARISON.md): it isolates the missing premises in actuality-first and truth-grounding routes and checks common finite controls. Neither route is established. The next task is the independent defense or refutation of the experiential truth-grounding bridge, not another restatement of the desired conclusion. A real result could be a conditional construction, a precise failure, or a new independently defensible bridge; none automatically closes physics or qualia.
