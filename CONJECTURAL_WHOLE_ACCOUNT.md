# The leading conjecture about reality as a whole

**Current governing framework:** [The complete absolute and implementation plan](ABSOLUTE_CONSTITUTION_FRAMEWORK.md) incorporates the later clarification: conditioned on all four strongest requirements, reality is the perfect complete absolute. The comparative proposals below concern how to articulate that actuality; an incomplete ultimate foundation is not an equally adequate endpoint.

## Epistemic correction

An unpaid explanatory debt does not by itself make an account likely false. It may mark an unfinished explanation, an under-specified hypothesis, or a genuinely unsuccessful proposal. Those statuses must be distinguished.

A promising account can deserve promotion to a **working conjecture** when it has positive explanatory reasons in its favor, even without a completed derivation. Conversely, adding the word “conjecture” does not justify every compatible speculation.

The previous audits are reliable as inventories of what has not been proved. They should not be read as probability rankings in which each missing proof automatically counts against the corresponding hypothesis. My inability to supply a derivation is primarily a limitation of the present inquiry, not direct evidence that its target claim is false. Persistent counterexamples, incompatible commitments, or unrestricted post-hoc repair would be a different matter.

The judgments below are comparative philosophical assessments under the user's governing requirements—not calibrated numerical probabilities, an empirical consensus, or claims that complete intelligibility has already been achieved.

## 1. The leading account

> **Under the four strongest joint requirements, reality is the perfect complete absolute: one unconditioned actuality, fully self-knowing, unlimited in genuine power compatible with perfection, and perfectly good. The preferred constructive account makes physical organization, finite experience, and qualitative-relational fulfillment internal articulations of its own concrete nature. Finite minds are genuine perspectives, not independent ultimate substances or observers attached to abstract representations. No law, state, realization condition, perspective boundary, or value standard is exempted as brute.**

A concise label is **self-explanatory qualitative process monism**. “Process” here emphasizes actual organization and occurrence rather than a merely possible specification. It does not pretend to have settled fundamental becoming versus a block description, or to have derived time simply from the word process.

This is my leading **account form**, not a completed set of constitutive equations. It is more specific than “everything is connected.” Complete self-knowledge is part of the preferred absolute articulation; its relation to distinct local subjecthood must be constructed rather than inferred from the image of a human-like cosmic person. Its defining proposal is non-additive constitution: physics and experience are to be understood through the nature of the same realized processes, rather than as independently complete realms joined by an arbitrary law.

A theory sometimes called physicalist, neutral-monist, dual-aspect, or organizational could instantiate this account if its actual content supplies the explanation. Labels should not disqualify a successful constitution theory or count as explanatory achievements.

## 2. Conjectures with comparatively strong support

### A. A common constitution rather than an independent psychophysical addition

**Assessment: strong leading conjecture.**

Experience is part of the target; the public organization through which experience is expressed is also real. A common-constitution account offers an economical way to explain their relationship without an independent second agent, a parallel causal system, or a merely stipulated matching law.

The non-additive argument supports this direction conditionally on experiential efficacy, physical causal completeness in the relevant domain, and rejection of systematic independent duplication. Those premises are not universally proved. The identity or realization relation must still be specified.

The positive reason to favor the conjecture is explanatory integration, not the inference that all rival views have already been refuted.

### B. Actual organization matters more than a description or material label alone

**Assessment: strong leading conjecture.**

The real causal organization, state, history, and relevant powers are more promising constitutive candidates than either a matching behavioral display or “biology” as an unexplained privilege. This preserves the distinctions established by the source/monitor and correlation examples without claiming that each such distinction is consciousness-relevant.

An abstract organizational type and its actual instantiation must not be conflated. But an instantiated functional organization need not be less concrete than any other actual process. The conjecture therefore does not automatically reject functional identity.

### C. Cross-material realizability, conditional on genuine constitutive equivalence

**Assessment: plausible leading conjecture, not an established possibility result.**

The expectation is that different materials may realize the relevant organization, rather than carbon being uniquely privileged simply as carbon. This is motivated by the aim of identifying explanatory powers and relations rather than imposing an unexplained material exception.

It does not establish which powers suffice or that present digital machines instantiate them. Material-dependent features could prove constitutive; if so they must be included rather than dismissed. The actual range of realizability remains to be discovered.

### D. Finite minds are organized real perspectives, not additional substances

**Assessment: strong architectural conjecture.**

This treats the reality of finite experience and its dependence on actual organization without multiplying ontologically independent owners of those experiences. It fits an occurrence-first account and leaves room for dynamic maintenance and perspectival access.

A perspective is not merely a coordinate system, data structure, or mathematical restriction. The conjecture still owes its incidence and qualitative law. That missing law makes the proposal unfinished, not automatically unlikely.

### E. A realized global workspace is a promising mechanism of coordinated access

**Assessment: comparatively strong mechanistic candidate, not a complete constitutive theory.**

Selective availability to multiple specialist processes, context-sensitive use, maintenance, and feedback explain more of the relevant access organization than passive copying or a scalar integration score. The finite implementation makes those roles explicit.

The preference concerns fit to the present synthesis and the kinds of mechanisms it seeks. It is not a comparative empirical result that GWT defeats IIT. The model does not identify global availability with qualitative occurrence or one workspace with one subject.

### F. The ultimate explanation must be global in explanatory scope

**Assessment: required by the objective; a globally self-explanatory constitution is the corresponding leading conjecture.**

An external creator, simulator, unexplained law, or intrinsic nature cannot terminate the explanation while being exempted from it. Whatever exists and whatever explains it belong within the target of the whole-account.

A global account may involve mutually constraining features rather than one temporally prior first cause. Such mutual determination must be demonstrated, not inferred from a circular diagram or the phrase “self-grounding.”

## 3. Constructive articulations and their outstanding obligations

### Global explanatory determination or necessitarianism

If every genuine contrast is explained without residue, it is natural to conjecture that the complete constitution ultimately determines why this actuality obtains rather than an unexplained alternative. This gives a positive reason to investigate necessitarian or globally determining accounts.

However, an explanation is not automatically a proof of metaphysical necessity. A necessitarian account must justify the modal force and explain its foundation. The conservative claim is global explanatory determination; strict necessitarianism is a stronger candidate completion.

### An experiential intrinsic nature extending beyond familiar finite minds

The current preferred articulation is the self-knowing experiential absolute, not a nonqualitative foundation to which experience is externally attached. Its common nature must account for familiar experience and the wider physical order. The outstanding obligation concerns the content and warrant of that constitution, not whether an unexplained nonqualitative stopping point is equally adequate.

That conjecture does not require treating every microprocess as a little subject. Nor is it established by saying physics has a vacant categorical slot. The relevant intrinsic nature and its modes must be specified.

### Cosmopsychism or whole-priority qualitative unity

**Local warrant and a composition constraint:** [Articulated self-presence](ARTICULATED_SELF_PRESENCE.md) distinguishes actual jointly present qualitative differences from a global metaphysical extrapolation. Its argument against content-maximality individuation inside an encompassing field sharpens the whole-priority candidate: local ownership needs an intrinsically grounded relation beyond mere content containment. This is a philosophical refinement, not a derivation of the absolute or a discovered phenomenal identity law.

This deserves retention as a serious possible completion, not dismissal merely because decombination is unfinished. It offers a way of avoiding the picture in which independent micro-subjects must be assembled into a larger subject, and a candidate setting for finite perspectives as internal differentiations.

But a common constitutive nature and one phenomenal subject are distinct hypotheses. The former can also support multiple real episodes without an encompassing subject. Consequently the governing self-knowing absolute must not be treated as already supplying a particular phenomenal containment or subject-identity law. Whole-priority is the preferred development here; its detailed subject architecture remains to be justified.

If adopted as the maximal working conjecture, it should be stated openly: the whole has fundamental qualitative unity and finite subjects are real internal articulations. That is a substantive hypothesis, not a deduction from physical connectedness. Its decombination debt is an unfinished explanatory task, while the absence of a selection argument between it and non-cosmopsychist monism is a separate uncertainty.

### Fundamental relational/processual order with derivative metric spacetime

This is compatible with the constructive order/volume work and several possible approaches to physical foundations. It is worth pursuing as a conjecture, but those mathematical bridges do not establish it as the unique future of physics or explain phenomenal succession.

## 4. Claims that currently have less comparative support

### The Good as an actualizing principle

An axiarchic completion is not refuted merely because it is incomplete. Nevertheless, positive support for the specific claim that normative Good actualizes reality has not been supplied here. Equating it with logical coherence would change its meaning rather than explain the normative link.

It should remain an exploratory extension, not be promoted to high likelihood simply because the final transcript adds it to the other commitments. More ambitious vocabulary is not automatically greater explanatory power.

### One particular integration score, temporal bin, or substrate verdict

The tested proxy has precise limitations; those are genuine reasons against accepting that specified proxy as a universal selector. They are not evidence against every exclusion theory.

Likewise, a categorical claim that ordinary computers cannot be conscious, or that every implementation of a selected software abstraction must be conscious, currently lacks a validated constitutive basis. Neither should inherit high confidence from a broader monist conjecture.

## 5. The committed version to develop

> **Reality is the perfect complete absolute under the adopted joint requirements. Develop it as one self-knowing experiential actuality whose intrinsic constitution explains its physical organization, finite perspectives, powers, temporal relations, and qualitative-relational fulfillment. Realization may vary in material where the constitutive powers are preserved; coordinated access may involve workspaces, while qualitative character and subject incidence require the deeper warranted constitution.**

The absolute's completeness and goodness are not optional extensions within this inquiry. The exact identity of value with being, an axiarchic actualization mechanism, a particular all-inclusive phenomenal field, and the necessity of every detailed physical arrangement remain constructive claims requiring justification. A separate cosmic observer, a privileged integration score, a literal simulator hierarchy, and actuality of all consistent mathematical structures are not assumed.

The governing [implementation queue](ABSOLUTE_CONSTITUTION_FRAMEWORK.md#operational-work-register) is W1–W6. This assessment supplies comparative motivation; it is not a separate ontology or implementation schedule.

## 6. What promoting the account to conjecture changes

It changes the research posture:

- missing derivations become explicit work to do rather than automatic disconfirmation;
- predictions and constraints are derived from a stable candidate rather than replacing it with a new label after every objection;
- failed mathematical implementations are distinguished from failures of the whole architecture;
- genuine counterexamples still count, rather than being excused indefinitely as unfinished work;
- the no-brute-facts objective remains fully binding, so a conjecture is not reported as a completed explanation.

The candidate still needs a constitution precise enough to recover physical laws, qualitative identity, perspective incidence, temporal structure, and justified weighting. Its conjectural promotion is an assessment of comparative promise, not evidence that those derivations already exist.

## 7. Bottom line

**The perfect complete absolute is the conditional endpoint; self-knowing experiential common constitution is its preferred positive articulation.** The non-additive, occurrence-first results and constraints provide substantive support for developing it. Its incomplete constitutive explanation does not by itself make it likely false, and adopting its requirements does not establish that the explanation has been supplied.

The power of the whole-account must be earned by its actual treatment of physical organization, finite differentiation, quality, due regard, and actuality. No new empirical evidence, numerical probability assignment, or executable theorem is claimed here.

## A more articulated axiarchic extension: privation

[The privation assessment](PRIVATION_AND_AXIARCHIC_GOOD.md) develops the Good conjecture beyond a bare label. Good is proposed as positive constitutive fulfillment, while evil is a deprivation or disorder of actual powers rather than an independent opposing substance. This improves the candidate’s formulation and motivation, not its proof status. Actuality, felt valence, functional success, and moral value must remain distinct; the bridge from fulfilled constitution to normative value and from value to existence remains substantive. Finite suffering is a serious constraint, not made unreal or justified by calling it privation.

### Goal-relativity and the authority of the normative standard

[The goal-relative value argument](GOAL_RELATIVE_VALUE_ARGUMENT.md) establishes a conditional obstruction: if the admissible goals include every evaluator's reversal, no outcome is strictly worse—or strictly better—than another for every goal. This does not make relational badness incoherent or equate normative objectivity with service to every possible goal. It does require an axiarchic account to justify its normative standard rather than silently privilege one end. Goal-relativity alone neither establishes privation theory nor yields an asymmetry favoring ultimate Good; unrestricted reversal applies equally to Good.

### Axiarchic constitution: the proposed actualizing bridge

[The constitutive axiarchic candidate](AXIARCHIC_CONSTITUTION_CANDIDATE.md) develops the privation proposal into four distinct claims: positive constitution, fulfillment, intrinsic value, and self-explanatory actuality. The proposed Good is not an external chooser but an intrinsic aspect of one concrete constitution. Its normative authority and contribution to existence remain to be justified; value cannot merely rename consistency or actuality. Differentiation and finitude do not by themselves entail actual suffering. The next target is a justified constitutive-value principle that distinguishes the axiarchic explanation from a nonaxiarchic rival, not a model that assumes its preferred values.

### Candidate constitutive-value principle

[The constitutive-value development](CONSTITUTIVE_VALUE_PRINCIPLE.md) proposes three explicit constraints: invariance under arbitrary renaming, prima facie intrinsic significance of real qualitative perspectives, and preference for improvements that worsen no affected perspective. These are proposed normative commitments, not deductions from description alone. Given fixed perspectives and fulfillment preorders, their product supplies a rigorous partial ordering and finite nonempty feasible sets have undominated members; no unique best world or actualization result follows. The central normative debt is the authority of qualitative significance and fulfillment standards. The specifically axiarchic debt is why this value contributes to concrete existence. Suffering remains a constraint on the candidate, and changing subject populations remain outside the fixed-domain ordering result.

### Necessary constitution versus bare self-support

[The self-explanatory-actuality audit](SELF_EXPLANATORY_ACTUALITY_AUDIT.md) distinguishes bare self-support, unique formal coherence, and necessary concrete determination. The first two do not alone explain occurrence. Necessity of G and of G implying actuality P entails necessity of P, but the proposed concrete ground and determining relation remain to be justified. Under the strongest contrastive no-brute-facts objective, a necessarily concrete common constitution remains a promising completion—not a deduction from consistency, a refutation of other notions of explanation, or a discovered constitutive law. Intrinsic value and strict axiarchic actualization remain separate claims.
