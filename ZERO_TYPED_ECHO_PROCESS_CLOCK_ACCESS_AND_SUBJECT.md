# A typed echo process: content, clock, access and subjecthood

## Result

The preceding [causal-access investigation](ZERO_ECHO_CAUSAL_ACCESS_AND_SUBJECT_REFINEMENT.md) showed that graph-based subject candidates change with time grain and encoding. This pass constructs the promised **operationally typed process candidate** on the same echo:

```text
physical carrier + maintained logical sign + actual gate sequence
+ specified clock interval + explicit allowed access algebra.
```

The candidate is executable as [`EchoContentProcess`](verification/zero_typed_echo_process.py). Its selected logical sign is the earlier echo-quality candidate, not a new phenomenal postulate. On the specified history it is maintained through the actual echo and inverse, can be read from a saved memory with the sign pointer, agrees with one component of the constrained opposite pair, and transforms consistently under the previously examined code refinement. The tests also check the original complex quantum vectors.

The constructive gain has equally important limits:

* The **physical** sign remains the same across the echo, while its minimal access sets change from tick to tick. One maintained logical content is therefore not one fixed physical support.
* Reading the sign into an extra bit can be implemented coherently and undone, but retaining and then ignoring that bit reduces the relevant pre-report pure-state fidelity to `1/2` on the tested state. The report is not an invisible observation or evidence that the sign is felt.
* The two existing quality signs are jointly accessible on the same carrier, with four equal physical cells; on a specified two-bin sector, explicit state-adapted operations flip either sign while keeping the other fixed. An independent interacting ancillary sign provides a separate control. None of these facts fixes the number of experiencers.
* An active type transformation exchanges this sign with the previously studied `Q_now` candidate; the two signs disagree on physical weight `1/2`. Treating the type transformation as a gauge identification would erase a real distinction in this model.
* A stationary relational effect can carry the sign on the entire reduced 20-tick cycle, but selecting the specific 7–13 window is not itself a stationary operation. A usable internal clock/reference and a subject/report bridge remain additional requirements.

This is a more precise candidate **interface**, not a proof of subjecthood, persistence of an experiencer, physical–experiential identity, actual incidence or the ultimate constitution. The implementation and checks are [here](verification/zero_typed_echo_process.py) and [here](verification/zero_typed_echo_process_test.py).

## 1. Type the candidate explicitly

Use the previously specified old sign registers B, J, R and M and the added saved-J register L. Let the anchor be tick 10. The process type is:

1. **Carrier and preparation:** the original angular/echo history, sign pointer, and an L memory initially ready and written from J at tick 7. Its preparation and coupling are extra physical inputs.
2. **Content algebra:** the two-dimensional commutative algebra generated by the anchor sign `K₁₀=M J R`; its sharp effects are `(I±K₁₀)/2`. This is a physical logical sign, not a declaration of felt valence.
3. **Actual modeled dynamics:** T–Q–T followed by its inverse at ticks 7–13. The saved L register remains coupled until after tick 13 in this test.
4. **Clock:** the earlier reduced 20-step stationary history and an explicitly selected 7–13 interval. The clock and interval have the restrictions in §5.
5. **Access:** the specified joint sign algebra generated by M and L; an operation able to form their product is assumed available. Alternative access sets are calculated below, not granted automatically to a confined observer.

The code checks the declared access at each tick. It does not attach an owner ID or an incidence weight to the candidate. A proposal that the process constitutes a subject, or that its sign is an experience, must add a justified bridge. In particular, maintaining an observable is not equivalent to having an experience *of* it.

## 2. One content, different physical expressions

On the reduced ledger, transport the anchor effect along the already specified clock dynamics. For **all** reduced basis states, not merely the ready support, its instantaneous expression on the echo interval is

| Tick | transported sign `K_t` |
|---:|---|
| 7 | `M J R` |
| 8 | `M R` |
| 9 | `M R` |
| 10 | `M J R` |
| 11 | `M R` |
| 12 | `M R` |
| 13 | `M J R` |

The gates carry the value forward, including through the inverse echo. On the **prepared ready** sector, `R₇=+` and the added memory records `L=J₇=z`, so throughout this interval

```text
K_t = M L = s z.
```

The equality with ML is specific to that ready/copy preparation; it is not an operator identity on arbitrary unready states. The distinct expression `MJR` or `MR` is needed if L is not supplied. For three original source labels and both sign branches, the tests propagate the actual complex quantum vectors and verify these equations at every checkpoint and in each saved-L slice. This is a check of the modeled physical history, not that the branches are already actual experiences.

At tick 10, the existing gauge-constrained pair has `Z₁=K₁₀`, `Z₂=−K₁₀`. The tests apply the actual pair encoding to recorded quantum slices and verify `Z₁=s z`, `Z₂=−s z`. The pair is an isometric encoding; its two signs need not correspond to two independent experiential subjects. If a later bridge gives both signs experiential meaning, the negative member remains real rather than being erased by the positive one.

## 3. Accessibility is time- and protocol-specific

From the actual reduced source distribution, enumerate the inclusion-minimal sets of commuting sign values sufficient to predict the maintained K exactly. L is physically retained and M is the sign pointer throughout:

| Tick | Minimal exact sign-access sets |
|---:|---|
| 7 | `{M,J}`, `{M,L}` |
| 8 | `{M,J}`, `{M,L}`, `{M,R}` |
| 9 | `{M,L}`, `{M,R}`, `{M,B,J}` |
| 10 | `{M,L}`, `{M,B,J}`, `{M,J,R}` |
| 11 | `{M,L}`, `{M,R}`, `{M,B,J}` |
| 12 | `{M,J}`, `{M,L}`, `{M,R}` |
| 13 | `{M,J}`, `{M,L}` |

These sets describe **possible exact classical-sign inference under the stipulated distribution**, not physically performed measurements or a proof that an observer has the specified combined operations. Without M, even access to all B/J/R/L sign outcomes yields optimal success `1/2` for K in this state. Thus the globally available algebra is not automatically available to a subject confined to J.

The formal availability of multiple implementations does not itself create multiple K tokens or subjects. Equally, the fact that two regions implement the same logical sign does not prove that they have one shared experience. This is the distinction the earlier [quantum access analysis](QUANTUM_ACCESS_STRUCTURES_AND_SUBJECT_COUNT_GATE.md) demanded in a different setting, now applied to the actual echo protocol.

## 4. Transport through a coherent encoding

Encode the old J record into J₁/J₂ as in the preceding pass and carry the pointer M along. Let V be the resulting 64-to-128-dimensional reduced-code isometry. At the grouped logical checkpoints—after the saved-memory write, T, both physical subgates of Q, and the final T—the tests explicitly verify

```text
U′_t V = V U_t,          K′_t V = V K_t,          V†V=I.
```

The quantum operator identities are checked on the **complete reduced logical spaces**, so coherent superpositions and reference entanglement are included. They use J₁ for the encoded representative of the original J operator and the two controlled B flips to implement Q on the code. The two physical Q subgates need not preserve the code *between* them; the equivalence is at the specified grouped checkpoints. The preceding pass additionally checked the old Q and T intertwinings on representative **original complex physical histories**, not on every vector of the unconstrained full Hilbert space.

This is a concrete notion of typed-process equivalence: transport the state, gates, content observable **and allowed access operations**, not just a label or a graph. It explains how the same specified logical content can survive an implementation change that altered naive factor-based module selection. It does not prove that every such physical extra register is phenomenally inert, that intermediate dynamics are irrelevant, or that all admissible causal interventions have been derived.

## 5. The clock supplies a real constraint

Let `W` be the existing reduced cyclic shift and let

```text
E = Σ_t |t⟩⟨t| ⊗ U_t U₁₀† [(I+K₁₀)/2] U₁₀ U_t†.
```

The existing relational-effect construction gives `[E,W]=0` over all 320 reduced clock/basis labels. Its physical weight in the specified stationary history is `1/2`. This is a stationary *physical* sign effect, not a Born-to-experience derivation. The earlier clock-dressed physical sign counterpart, rather than a bare sign flip, is needed for the original complex history.

A bare projector selecting ticks 7–13 does **not** commute with W. In the uniformly clock-labeled physical history the diagonal weights are

```text
P(window,K=+) = 7/40,       P(window,K=−) = 7/40,
P(window)     = 7/20.
```

Twirling that unreferenced window over all cyclic shifts produces `(7/20) I` on the clock and loses its absolute position. A relational clock/reference or a specified measurement protocol is therefore needed to talk operationally about this interval; the [earlier stationary POVM construction](ZERO_COHERENT_ANGULAR_RESERVOIR_CLOCK_ACCESS.md) provides a conditional example. These are physical probabilities over clock-labeled alternatives, not seven proven actual subjects or seven simultaneous experiences. The full 20-step relational-effect result above is for the prior reduced clock; no new proof of a complete L-and-report-dressed clock Hamiltonian or physical energy law is claimed.

## 6. An actual coherent report and its symmetry

Add a report bit C in the ready `+` state and coherently copy `K=M L` into it using the appropriate controlled flips. On the original complex post-echo source vectors, the two report signs each have physical weight `1/2`. If C is ignored, the state of the previously retained M/L/old carrier is dephased between K sectors; its fidelity with that **pre-report pure joint state** is

```text
(1/2)² + (1/2)² = 1/2.
```

The L memory is retained in this comparison; tracing it earlier is a different protocol. Applying the same coherent report write again uncomputes C and restores all tested complex amplitudes. It also removes the durable report. Neither readout nor reset is claimed to be thermodynamically free or available within a particular realm.

The sign counterpart flips M, hence flips K. Once C records K, preserving the recorded state requires **flipping C too**; leaving C fixed breaks its correlation. The tests apply the actual old exchange with its required tick-10 clock phase to both sign branches, pair it with `C→−C`, and verify the full recorded complex slices. This is a physical state symmetry on the specified support, not a proof of equal actual-incidence measures.

The active type swap is a separate test. Its reduced stationary lift maps the transported K effect to the transported `Q_now=MJ` effect. At anchor they disagree on physical weight `1/2`. The earlier full quantum type swap needs the saved-L dressing to preserve the recorded history. An active transformation that exchanges two distinct modeled values is not automatically a passive gauge equivalence of qualities or subjects. Which symmetries the complete physical–experiential constitution must respect is still open.

## 7. Two jointly accessible contents on the same carrier

The two earlier quality candidates already give a more stringent test **without adding an independent X**. At the anchor, with L saved,

```text
N = Q_now = M J,       K = Q_echo = M L,       N K = B.
```

They commute and can be jointly reported with two ready bits, using controlled flips from `(M,J)` and `(M,L)` respectively. The model's actual complex history has all four `(N,K)` cells at weight `1/4` for each tested source label. The verifier checks that a direct J-parity projection produces exactly the same complex vectors as grouping the existing B sectors. The four outcomes are statistically independent in this particular state; they are nevertheless related to the physical B sign and share carrier factors. A joint measurement is not a demonstration of two independent systems or two experiences.

Retaining both report bits and tracing them out dephases the four joint sectors: the fidelity with the pre-report pure M/L/old state is `4(1/4)²=1/4`, compared with `1/2` for the single-K report. Both parity writes can be coherently reversed; doing so removes the reports. These are state- and protocol-specific structural results, not intrinsic costs of awareness.

**Independent control is stronger than statistical independence.** A concrete restricted control exists on the actual two-bin subspace with saved `z=+`: negative bin 1 and positive bin 2. The earlier source-family-adapted unitary transfer maps their actual complex old/R vectors in both directions and, with s fixed, sends

```text
(N,K) → (−N,K).
```

Following that transfer by the old physical sign exchange and pointer flip sends

```text
(N,K) → (N,−K).
```

The test checks both maps on the original vectors for three source labels and both pointer branches. Thus the two structural signs are separately controllable **within this selected sector** by explicitly specified larger-carrier operations; they are not merely two names for the same value. This is not a proof that those controls are local, freely available, gauge-sector-preserving, or implementable on the complete clock/pair/apparatus history. The selected sector and state-family-adapted transfer are substantive restrictions.

This sharpens, but does not solve, the subject question. One joint physical content field and two coordinate-specific occurrences remain competing phenomenal/ownership bridges. Neither simultaneous reportability nor these restricted controls tells us whether any subject experiences N and K together, separately, or at all.

## 8. A genuinely independent active content is not an alias

Compare the binary algebra generated by K with a separately stipulated independent binary sign X. If X interacts with a ready Y bit by an X-controlled write, its joint classical sign statistics with K can be uniform over all four pairs, while the new X–Y interaction is genuine. The commuting operator span

```text
span{I, K, X, KX}
```

has dimension 4, whereas `span{I,K}` has dimension 2. The test constructs the coherent X–Y write explicitly. Thus an independently varying input is not eliminated merely because another region already represents K. This is a separate ancillary extension and preparation, not a claim that such an X exists in the original universe.

Still, even if both logical contents were actually maintained and accessible, a **joint-owner** proposal and a **separate-owner** proposal could assign them one or two subjects without changing the displayed quantum circuit. A rule could reject joint ownership of disconnected systems by requiring a specific causal relation, but that requirement—and the possibility of overlapping or whole-level subjects—must be argued rather than hidden in the count of logical generators. Logical rank is neither a subject census nor a phenomenal-unity measure.

Similarly, one subject persisting across the seven clock labels and seven successive subject labels can both be attached as formal ownership hypotheses to the same maintained K process. The actual process does not choose between them. A first-person identity claim or report can discriminate such proposals only with a separately justified identity/report error model, as shown in the [previous bridge test](ZERO_OWNERSHIP_REPORT_SYMMETRY_AND_INCIDENCE.md).

## 9. What this settles and the next dependency

The pass gives a single typed candidate with real gate-level dynamics, a stationary logical effect, an explicitly scoped access family, an implementation-equivalence test, a constrained opposite sign, a reversible report, and an independent-content control. It also establishes joint access and restricted independent control of the two existing quality signs on the same carrier. This moves beyond either counting graph components or simply declaring that an unspecified carrier is a subject.

It still does not derive the logical observable, prepared memory, clock, allowed operations, experiential interpretation or occurrence measure from zero. It does not decide whether the maintained content belongs to a persisting experiencer, or whether multiple contents share one. The next feasible step is to test an **explicit unity/ownership bridge** against this joint-control sector: it must distinguish shared versus separate ownership without depending on a chosen code factorization or treating every accessible copy as a new occurrence. Its report/phenomenal bridge and incidence weights must be specified independently before using it to argue for a signed experiential total or cosmic energy balance.
