## 1. The exact gap between a zero constraint and actual physics

Consider the spatially flat FLRW model on a fixed compact unit-coordinate-volume three-torus, with a homogeneous massless scalar `φ`. Its canonical constraint, using `κ=8πG` and lapse one, is

```text
C = −κ p_a²/(12a) + p_φ²/(2a³) = 0.
```

The volume is `V=a³`, the scalar momentum `p_φ` is conserved, and, for `p_φ>0` on the expanding branch,

```text
p_a = −√(6/κ) p_φ/a,
ρ = p_φ²/(2V²),
H = √(κ/6) p_φ/V,
R = −κ p_φ²/V².
```

Here `ρ` is the scalar-field energy density, `H` the expansion rate and `R` the Ricci scalar. These equations follow from the stated classical FLRW model; `p_φ` can be expressed as the integrated scalar momentum on a slice. Fix the same physical volume `V=1` in fixed units and the same `κ=1`. Then

| Conserved scalar momentum | Constraint C | Density ρ at V=1 | Curvature R at V=1 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 1/2 | −1 |
| 2 | 0 | 2 | −4 |

These are not just two labels for the same numbers: the curvature and density differ at the same relationally specified volume. A common time-coordinate change cannot turn one set of scalar observable values into the other. The use of a volume reading does not assume an external cosmic clock. Both cases satisfy the very same zero constraint.

**Consequence:** even this genuine gravitational constraint does not, by itself, determine the physical solution or its conserved scalar momentum. A Hamiltonian constraint is also not automatically the objective global energy intended by the user; the [FLRW analysis](ZERO_TOTALITY_FLRW_CONSTRAINT_AND_ENERGY.md) explains that distinction. This is not an argument for an arbitrary nonzero total or a reopening of the user's settled infinity case. It is a clear test of what the candidate zero **does and does not explain**.

A stronger self-subsistent zero nature could in principle select or constitute the relevant physics through further intrinsic relations. But those relations, their actual realization and why they leave no unexplained selection cannot be taken for granted merely because `C=0`.

## 2. A previously underemphasized problem: whose energy flow?

The recent energy-exchange candidate used an effective observer field `u` and

```text
E_μ = F_{μν}u^ν,
h_u = −j^μ E_μ = −j^μ F_{μν}u^ν.
```

For a *specified physical* observer field, `h_u` is a coordinate scalar. But changing the physical observer congruence changes the energy partition and can change `h_u`; this is not a mere passive coordinate change. Unless the subject's constitutive organization independently picks `u`, a frame-dependent sign cannot by itself be the intrinsic sign of one and the same felt experience.

A particularly sharp exact result follows if the charge current is a single co-moving current `j^μ=q n u^μ`. Antisymmetry of `F` gives

```text
j^μ F_{μν}u^ν = q n u^μ F_{μν}u^ν = 0.
```

Thus the proposed exchange is **identically zero in that current's rest frame**, even in a nonzero field. For a nonzero force, other physically different observer frames can attribute positive or negative power to the same local event. For example, at an event with `j=(ρ,0,0,0)` and an x-directed electric field `E`, observers moving with velocities `β=+0.6` and `β=−0.6` yield respectively `h=+0.75ρE` and `h=−0.75ρE`; the co-moving observer gets zero, using the sign convention above.

This does **not** show that all possible multi-component subjects have zero energy exchange. Their currents can have motion relative to a physically specified subject frame. It does show that selecting a frame and the relevant composite current is indispensable, and that bare `−j·E` has not been shown to be intrinsic valence.

## 3. Correction to the energy-locking proposal

The prior [energy-locked model](ZERO_TOTALITY_ENERGY_LOCKED_INCIDENCE_CONSTRUCTION.md) defined the electromagnetic energy current relative to a selected laboratory observer and aligned an auxiliary incidence current with that energy current. That conditional mathematics is valid in the specified frame. **It did not derive a subject frame or show that the same energy current remains aligned when defined relative to that subject.** The distinction is not cosmetic.

For a local transverse field `E_y=1`, `B_z=1/2`, an inertial laboratory observer sees

```text
u_EM = 5/8,      S_x = 1/2,      v_E = S_x/u_EM = 4/5.
```

The auxiliary current in the earlier proposal is aligned with this laboratory energy current. If one takes its velocity `4/5` as the proposed subject's physical rest velocity and **redefines** the electromagnetic energy current relative to that subject, the new current has components

```text
C_subject = (3/8, 0)   in the original laboratory coordinates.
```

It is not parallel to the proposed subject four-velocity. By contrast, the energy-frame velocity for this field is `1/2`. Thus “aligned with the laboratory energy flow” and “aligned with the energy flow measured in the subject's own frame” are different conditions. Curved or accelerating subject congruences also introduce the observer-deformation term already displayed in the prior report.

This does not invalidate the earlier fixed-frame theorem or its numerical checks; it limits their meaning. **They are not a frame-independent physical–experiential construction.** Claiming that they moved us materially closer to a derived subject–energy identity without highlighting this issue was misleading.

## 4. A second physical obstruction: passive dissipation

Suppose, in a candidate subject's independently specified rest frame, the current follows the elementary isotropic passive Ohmic law

```text
j^μ = ρ u^μ + σ E^μ,       σ ≥ 0,       E·u=0.
```

Then `j·E=σ E²≥0` and the proposed local valence `v=−α(j·E)/N`, for `α,N>0`, satisfies

```text
v ≤ 0.
```

If the whole represented experiential domain obeyed this law and its actual positive incidence measure gave an exactly zero total, its negative contribution would have to vanish almost everywhere on that domain. It could not describe both real negative and positive felt aspects by this particular mapping.

There are possible responses: physical regions can act as sources and return energy to the field; a different interaction or subsystem can matter; passive and active sectors can coexist; or the mapping may simply be wrong. None is selected by zero alone. A region with `j·E<0` is a net electromagnetic energy source in the chosen frame; this does **not** imply negative entropy production or automatically identify the region with positive feeling. Most importantly, the result is a conditional restriction on **this candidate**, not a theorem that the framework is impossible.

## 5. What probabilistic structure adds—and does not add

Even if a common experiential scale and the same measure for actual accounting and self-location were independently established, exact zero would not fix the probability of suffering. For any stipulated `0<r<1`, a two-class incidence distribution can have

```text
probability r:       valence −1,
probability 1−r:     valence r/(1−r),
mean:                −r + (1−r)r/(1−r) = 0.
```

This is a logical control, not a claim that every such distribution is physically possible. It shows that zero balance alone gives no numerical probability. An actual theory must derive which incidences exist, their qualities, their measure, and its relationship to self-location and quantum records. Different subjects' suffering remains real regardless of a signed total.

## 6. What counts as genuine progress from here

The project should stop presenting another closed accounting example as if it solved the foundations. A substantive next claim has to provide **one non-circular principle** that does explanatory work across the following, or an explicit derivation connecting independently motivated principles:

1. Why this actual whole has a zero constitution and why that constitution is self-subsistent—not merely a zero generator on a chosen model.
2. How it determines, or makes non-arbitrary, physically distinct observables such as the relational FLRW data above.
3. How the same physical constitution is genuinely experiential and individuates real subjects without declaring a mathematical current conscious.
4. How a common signed experiential quantity is objectively defined, if that quantity is part of the thesis, and why its zero applies to actual experiences rather than selected physical proxies.
5. How any probability rule is grounded in actual incidence and remains compatible with observed physical records.

There may be a successful route; neither this audit nor the preceding constructions proves otherwise. But the present record supplies **no derivation of that route**, and there is no honest basis for promising that the existing approach will eventually succeed. The conditional restrictions in this report are genuine results; they are not a completed solution.
