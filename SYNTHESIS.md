# Reality as a Whole
## A steelmanned synthesis of the complete inquiry

### Central thesis

> **The most promising common architecture is an occurrence-first, intrinsically articulated account of reality: public physical structure, phenomenal presentation, and perspectival organization must be explained as features of concrete actuality, not assembled through independently unexplained bridges. Genuine differences, implementations, and observers must be preserved, while merely representational duplication must not manufacture additional reality. Physical and computational constructions explain portions of that organization; they do not alone establish actuality, experience, or observer number. Probability enters only where a justified population, evidence relation, and weighting rule support it. The goal remains a fully self-accounting reality, with its foundation included in—not exempted from—the explanatory demand.**

This is the strongest **integrated candidate architecture** supported by the inquiry. It is neither an established zero-residue theory nor simply a list of objections. Its positive proposal is that **constitution should unify what simulation narratives, psychophysical bridge laws, and observer lotteries treat as separate explanatory additions**.

The proposed unification has not yet been demonstrated. That qualification limits the claim of success; it does not weaken the objective.

**The constitution task is part of the mathematical unification, not a separate addition.** [The unified constitution map](UNIFIED_CONSTITUTION_MAP.md) connects the Gamma–chi target to the actual local observable/state/dynamics/intervention basis, quantum channels, causal geometry, contextual descriptions, scaling, and realized computation. Exact re-presentations must preserve the same occurrence and phenomenal facts. A noninvertible effective description is constitutively sufficient only if it retains the relevant distinctions; physical approximation or universality alone does not prove phenomenal preservation. All named frameworks must be accounted for in their proper domains, without declaring every incompatible abstract model to be the same actual physics.

## 1. The governing standard

**Four joint completion conditions:** complete intelligibility, maximum explanatory elegance and parsimony, full fidelity to experience, and beauty/perfection. These constrain one account simultaneously; none licenses sacrificing another. [The four-criteria consolidation](FOUR_CRITERIA_CONSOLIDATION.md) preserves their strongest jointly coherent reading without treating self-reference as awareness, one reality as one subject, or optimality as an explanation of actuality.

The target is a completely intelligible account of reality **as a whole**: nothing genuinely explanatory is left unpaid merely because it is called fundamental, necessary, intrinsic, mathematical, experiential, or self-grounding.

The settled methodological ordering is:

1. Pursue complete intelligibility without exempting the whole or its explanatory principles.
2. Preserve successful content and avoid gratuitous revisions while pursuing it.
3. Treat elegance as economy of independent explanatory commitments, not verbal brevity or a small inventory of entities.
4. Treat fidelity to Bostrom as a guide and correspondence test, not a constraint that limits completion.

The uniqueness conjecture is substantive: perhaps a fully completed inquiry converges on one framework up to explanatory equivalence, and its elegance expresses that absence of arbitrariness. It remains a conjecture, not a license to force every local construction to return one answer.

Three questions must not be collapsed:

- **What counts as complete?** No unexplained residue.
- **Can reality satisfy that standard, and does it?** A metaphysical question.
- **Which account satisfies it?** A constructive and comparative question.

The project can adopt completeness as its governing requirement without endlessly renegotiating the word. But an unconditional proof about reality still needs an argument that the proposed requirement is satisfied. Conversely, no supplied argument establishes that completion is impossible.

A sufficiently justified dissolution of a question can count as explanatory progress. Failure to answer it, inability to imagine an answer, or declaring it “bedrock” cannot substitute for that justification.

## 2. The basic turn: from descriptions to occurrences

The durable advance beyond simulation is to distinguish four things:

1. **An abstract specification:** what a process would be like if realized.
2. **A concrete occurrence:** something actually happens.
3. **An implementation relation:** one actual organization realizes the relevant dynamics of another description.
4. **An explanation:** an account of why the occurrence and the relation obtain.

A mathematical description does not need to be executed by another mathematical description. An actual process does not require a further external processor merely because it admits computational representation. But neither observation explains why it is actual.

The whole includes every real implementation, observer, law-bearing process, and ground. An existing explainer cannot literally stand outside everything that exists. This blocks explanatory evasion through an excluded simulator; it does not imply that inclusion is explanation.

> **Nothing outside the whole is not the same achievement as nothing unexplained within it.**

A constitutive account asks what actuality is such that its determinations follow from its nature. It need not be a temporal story in which reality produces itself. Nor need it be a single substance, an undifferentiated One, or a cosmic person.

An internally articulated whole can contain genuine distinctions. The standard excludes **unexplained differences**, not differences as such. Erasing plurality, change, or finite experience by calling them illusions merely relocates the burden unless their occurrence is explained.

## 3. The leading candidate: intrinsic qualitative-relational constitution

A useful name for the common constructive direction is **occurrence-first qualitative-relational constitutivism**.

The name summarizes a proposal, not a proof:

- **Occurrence-first:** begin with what actually obtains, including the undeniable occurrence of experience, rather than treating a formal specification as already concrete.
- **Relational:** public physics describes structured distinctions, dynamics, interactions, and invariants that must be recovered precisely.
- **Qualitative:** actual phenomenal character is not discarded, reduced to the existence of reports by definition, or left outside the explanandum.
- **Constitutive:** seek an account of how these features belong to the same actuality, not a pairing of independently unexplained ingredients.

The minimal commitment is that reality includes both physical organization and phenomenal occurrence. The stronger candidate is that, in conscious episodes, the public process and the phenomenal occurrence are **one concrete event**, available under different descriptions or modes of acquaintance. A local identity with a particular brain-process token does not imply that every possible conscious realization is biological: another episode could be identical with a different concrete realization. Sharing a phenomenal kind is not sharing numerical token identity.

That stronger claim is not forced by the minimal one. Nor does it establish that every occurrence is experiential. The architecture leaves open whether the ultimate nature is experiential, neutral, or otherwise internally explanatory; those alternatives cannot be merged merely by calling them aspects of one whole.

The positive aspiration is precise:

> **What makes the relevant actuality determinate should also account for its organization and, where applicable, its phenomenal presentation—without a separately adjustable consciousness assignment.**

This is more demanding than saying “everything is consciousness” or “experience is information from inside.” It asks for consequences that were not already inserted into those descriptions.

## 4. The required explanatory connections

Let \(C\) denote the proposed complete concrete constitution. A successful account needs justified connections such as

\[
C\longrightarrow\Pi,
\qquad C\longrightarrow\Omega_{\rm realizations},
\qquad (C,r)\longrightarrow\mathcal E_r,
\]

where \(\Pi\) is public physical organization, \(\Omega_{\rm realizations}\) the actual occurrence population, and \(\mathcal E_r\) the experiential organization associated with realization \(r\), if it is experiential.

It must also explain the actuality and sufficiency of \(C\), and justify the scope of epistemic inference from a perspective’s evidence.

These need not all follow from a single short axiom. A mutually supporting explanatory system may be more adequate than one sentence bearing an impressive label. What matters is whether the links are internally justified rather than independently adjustable.

The required unity is **one explanatory achievement**, not identity of all its targets. Actuality, feeling, realization multiplicity, and probability are not interchangeable phenomena.

The central convergence test is this: do independently pursued explanations of actuality, experience, physical organization, and perspective constrain and support the same constitution? Or have several unresolved assumptions merely been packaged together?

## 5. Physical difference without a permanent archive

The surviving traceability principle is modest but useful:

> A physical existence claim has physical content only insofar as its posit makes some physical difference in principle.

“Difference” includes states, relations, lawful behavior, correlations, or appropriately grounded counterfactual consequences. It does not require a human observer, a permanent readable record, or recovery of every past event.

The principle therefore preserves both sides:

- An inaccessible or unobserved difference is not no difference.
- Local erasure does not by itself establish destruction of global information.
- Reversible or unitary dynamics do not by themselves establish a separately recoverable archive of every historical occurrence.
- A posit stipulated to change nothing physically whatsoever contributes no physical content.

Empirical equivalence alone is not a general theorem of metaphysical identity. Nor does causal registration entail conscious registration. Traceability cannot carry the later argument for an experiential ultimate on its own.

Its role in the synthesis is methodological: resist empty distinctions while preserving real differences and not confusing lack of access with lack of occurrence.

## 6. Identity: the strongest local hypothesis and its proof obligations

Mutual implication, necessary co-occurrence, and numerical identity differ. “Consciousness equals physicalism” also confuses a phenomenon with a thesis. The relevant proposal concerns an episode and a concrete process.

Different phenomenal and physical concepts need not have the same meaning to refer to the same event. An empirically discovered identity can be necessary if the requisite identity and reference assumptions hold. But necessity of identity does not establish the initial identification, and token identity alone does not settle every property-level or modal question.

The strongest formal research target is:

> Independently specify the relevant process and phenomenal relations, then test whether the process determines the phenomenal structure without an arbitrary selector.

Let \(T\) connect a process vocabulary with a phenomenal vocabulary. For each fixed physical model, ask how many admissible phenomenal expansions satisfy \(T\): none, one, or several inequivalent alternatives.

This becomes meaningful only if:

- “complete process” does not already include the desired phenomenal answer;
- phenomenal distinctions are not defined solely as report or control equivalence;
- the connecting constraints are independently justified;
- and “harmless relabeling” is not used to dismiss an unresolved qualitative alternative by assumption.

First-person judgments about similarity, valence, co-presence, ownership, and temporal organization are evidence, although fallible and often mediated by reports. Their independence means that they are not defined into the physical model—not that they are causally independent of the brain.

Three achievements remain distinct:

1. **Determination:** the process fixes the phenomenal profile.
2. **Identity:** the phenomenal episode is that process, not merely necessarily dependent on it.
3. **Explanation:** the account makes the determination or identity intelligible.

Unique correspondence can support identity strongly without entailing it by logic alone. Beth-style definability results also have exact formal hypotheses; uniqueness up to abstract isomorphism is not automatically implicit definability over a fixed reduct. Formal definability does not establish that its predicates capture actual experience.

## 7. Brain anchoring without unjustified biological exclusivity

The local programme must explain the actual dependence of familiar experience on brain organization. Workspace integration, recurrent dynamics, self-modeling, memory, affect, and control are candidate components, not a proven complete list.

Three claims should remain separate:

- Human conscious episodes are empirically tied to concrete brain processes.
- Particular biological or brain-like dynamics may be indispensable to them.
- No nonbiological system could ever be conscious.

The first motivates investigation. The second is testable. The third has not been established by the supplied work.

Likewise, consciousness localized to particular organizations does not imply that all matter is conscious. Conversely, the fact that familiar unified subjects require special organization does not establish the absence of every conceivable rudimentary phenomenal aspect elsewhere.

GNWT and causal self-reference are useful candidate mechanisms. A causally calibrated self-model can anchor “I,” self-directed action, and local records without an extra disembodied ego. That is a substantive account of functional first-personness. It does not yet establish feeling or show that every experience requires an explicit self-model.

The strongest current local proposal is therefore **a brain-anchored, phenomenal-realist process identity programme**, not a certified equation of consciousness with workspace ignition.

The extended identity transcript clarifies four separate questions: whether an intrinsic nature beyond structural description is posited; whether that basis is itself experiential; which substrates can realize the relevant organization; and how unified subjects are constituted. A thick intrinsic ontology need not make phenomenal consciousness fundamental, but an explicitly fundamental experiential version does. Neither thinness nor thickness settles substrate independence. An organizational condition also needs two arguments: that another substrate can physically implement it, and that such implementation preserves the relevant phenomenal character. “Organizational” and “biological” are not exhaustive alternatives, and a local neural token identity is not a universal biological exclusion. See [the updated identity analysis](identity-and-whole-thesis.md).

The brain-specificity transcript sharpens the distinction further: **brain-anchored and realization-open** is warranted as a research stance; neither a mandatory organizational checklist nor a general exclusion of current computers is established by its arguments. Digital hardware is not uniformly feedforward, physical computers are dissipative, and external energy dependence is shared with biological systems. More specific hypotheses about self-production, criticality, quantum resources, or maintenance require independent target evidence. A verified contract proves compliance with the chosen specification, not that the specification captures experience.

Near-decomposability and self-maintenance are useful boundary hypotheses, not replacements for the missing unity argument. Strong internal and weak external coupling over a relevant timescale may identify a robust physical module without exact isolation. The temporal window, constitutive relevance, and correspondence to an actual episode remain to be justified. See [the contextual brain-specificity audit](brain-specificity-thesis.md).

## 8. Occurrences must not be counted as descriptions or possible states

A single computational type can have several executions; one execution can have several descriptions. Any occurrence account must preserve that distinction.

It must also distinguish identity from continuation. If one predecessor is declared identical to each of two distinct successors, transitivity merges the successors. Branching survival, temporal continuation, overlap, and part–whole relations should not be silently encoded as description-equivalence.

The strongest formal distinction developed across the material is

\[
q:S_{\rm implementation}\longrightarrow S_{\rm predictive\ quotient}
\]

versus

\[
\tau:\Omega_{\rm actual\ realizations}\longrightarrow
\mathcal T_{\rm organizational\ types}.
\]

The first quotients **alternative states** by selected behavior. The second classifies **actual occurrences**. Many occurrences can have one type. Many states can represent alternatives of one compound system.

Hence none of the following automatically counts observers:

- distinct programs or descriptions;
- machine states;
- reachable states;
- quotient-fiber sizes;
- available readout maps;
- paths without provenance;
- or possible model annotations.

The formal work specifies useful conditional versions of \(q\). It does not derive the entire actual realization domain or its phenomenal constitution.

## 9. What the operational constructions actually contribute

The constructions are not merely negative. They identify organization without some arbitrary choices, while exposing the inputs still doing work.

### Stable macro-organization

A connected four-component diffusion model with strong within-pair and weak between-pair coupling yields distinct relaxation scales and exact aggregate dynamics. The identity \(PL=L_MP\) supplies a macro-law valid for every microscopic initial state in that model.

This demonstrates conditional emergence and explanatory compression. It does not turn the modules into minds or explain the supplied micro-laws.

### Intervention-relative subsystems

Bare dynamics need not select a unique factorization. An appropriate unlabelled reset algebra can distinguish factors, and a target’s ancestor closure can give its unique smallest autonomous envelope in the deterministic full-product setting.

The intervention algebra and target are substantive inputs. Dependence is not automatically constitution.

### Moving storage and shared machinery

A rotating-register model exposes processes that traverse storage sites. Time-indexed provenance preserves their paths when endpoint sampling obscures them. But forks and merges defeat naive path counting.

A shared-controller model identifies a distinctive control role without settling whether it is internal constitution or external enabling machinery. Multiple interface assignments satisfy the stated causal constraints.

These cases together rule out both pure spatial support and unrestricted causal connectedness as sufficient subject criteria.

### Predictive minimization and hidden copies

For a fixed finite-state interface, behavioral minimization yields a unique minimal predictive machine. Append uncoupled hidden dynamics \(Z\) and observe only the original machine \(A\); then, under the stated product assumptions,

\[
\operatorname{Min}(A\times Z)\cong\operatorname{Min}(A).
\]

The hidden system can itself be another execution of \(A\). Therefore local predictive equivalence does not imply equal total realization population.

A nonzero finite count cannot obey both unrestricted local-abstraction invariance and exact duplicate preservation:

\[
N(A\times A)=N(A),\qquad N(A\times A)=2N(A).
\]

This excludes that conjunction, not all observer measures. Extended infinite values also require separate treatment.

The positive lesson is **canonicality relative to inputs**, not automatic canonicality of the inputs.

## 10. Recovering physics: conditional reconstruction, not metaphysical deduction

The constitutive programme offers real opportunities for explanatory compression.

Within appropriate finite convex operational frameworks, spectrality and frame homogeneity, supplemented by the precise dynamical-label or composition assumptions, can constrain state-space types toward complex quantum theory. The supplied work explores energy-observability and local-tomography/capacity routes.

These are substantive assumptions. They do not follow merely from the words “concrete,” “private,” “unified,” or “self-presenting.” General theorem applications must match their effects, transformation groups, dimensions, and composite hypotheses exactly.

Some algebraic steps are directly checkable: Jordan and commutator products recover complex multiplication; positive-linear states yield trace probabilities in the selected framework; suitable reversible dynamics yield commutator form. None selects the actual Hamiltonian, initial state, coupling constants, or experienced outcomes.

Likewise:

- Fisher geometry is a geometry of specified statistical discrimination, not automatically experienced similarity.
- Qubit positive cones have Lorentz-cone mathematics, not automatically spacetime interpretation.
- Chronological order plus volume can recover metric geometry under suitable continuum and causal assumptions, not derive those assumptions or gravitational dynamics.
- Local tomography concerns the full collection of product-measurement correlations, not merely separate reduced states. It remains compatible with entanglement.
- No-broadcasting does not select complex quantum theory, and phenomenal privacy is not operational no-broadcasting.
- Contextuality is a specific constraint on value assignments, not a consequence of phenomenal co-presence alone.

A future account may incorporate AQFT, causal sets, twistor constructions, quantum information, topos methods, and Langlands-type correspondences in different roles. They should neither be stacked as independently achieved foundations nor ruled out as necessarily incompatible competitors. Integration requires explicit domains, mappings or limits, prediction agreement, and occurrence interpretation. A shared mathematical vocabulary does not derive an experiential inside, concrete actuality, or the necessity of the laws.

Likewise, decoherence and redundant records do not alone settle definite outcomes or Born weighting; self-location introduces further justified assumptions rather than removing them automatically. Gödel, Turing, and Lawvere impose precise formal constraints, not a theorem identifying the phenomenal explanatory gap or refuting complete ontological intelligibility.

The decisive constructive target is therefore

\[
\text{independently characterized concrete nature}
\overset{?}{\Longrightarrow}
\text{operational principles}
\Longrightarrow\text{physical structure}.
\]

The second connection has conditional mathematical support. The first remains the principal constitutive obligation.

## 11. Probability: preserve Bostrom conditionally, not compulsorily

Given an established realization population \(O_E\), a simulated subset \(S_E\), and a justified measure \(w\), a finite or otherwise well-defined self-location problem may support

\[
\operatorname{Cr}(\mathrm{SIM}\mid E,w)
=\frac{w(S_E)}{w(O_E)},
\qquad 0<w(O_E)<\infty.
\]

Equal positive weight and additivity for \(N\) intrinsically equivalent realizations give \(k/N\) for a \(k\)-member subset. The lemma is valid; the weighting principles are not thereby proved.

Equal reality, equal consciousness, shared evidence, and symmetry within a group do not automatically fix relative weights between groups. One base observer and 99 simulated observers admit normalized within-group-symmetric weights

\[
w(b)=a,\qquad w(s_i)=(1-a)/99.
\]

The weak constraints leave \(a\) open. This shows underdetermination from those constraints, not that every choice is rationally warranted.

A result for intrinsic duplicates also does not establish equal weighting across heterogeneous human-type observers. Units of contribution—subjects, lives, episodes, moments, executions, duration—must be stated.

Three probabilistic tasks should remain separate:

1. A physical history measure \(\mu\).
2. Conditioning on accessible records \(\mathcal F_r\).
3. A justified weighting over centers when the question actually requires one.

Conditional expectation operates on a given measure; it does not derive that measure or identify subjects. A centered kernel \(\lambda_\omega\), where appropriate, introduces further assumptions that require justification.

No universal ego lottery is needed to explain indexical reference. That does not eliminate population-sampling questions. Conversely, the existence of many candidates does not demand a normalized global prior where none is justified.

Bostrom’s demographic reasoning survives as a conditional correspondence case. Recovering its numerical conclusion is not the success condition imposed on the deeper inquiry.

## 12. Algorithmic reconstruction: a modeling branch, not a shortcut

Algorithmic priors provide useful description-based models. Prefix-free coding constrains total description weight; appropriate universal semimeasures have dominance and invariance properties; complexity-rate limits can survive bounded machine changes.

But these facts do not establish:

- a unique physical observer measure;
- exact finite machine-independent odds;
- that copies have no relevance;
- that all abstract programs are actual;
- or that computational consistency explains existence.

A bounded address-weight construction caps weight through a chosen world description. That cap does not establish multiplicity independence under every observer measure. Changing an algorithmic reference machine can change finite predictions; calling the change a gauge does not remove that substantive difference.

Substrate independence concerns realization of consciousness, not necessarily runtime-independent weighting. Time-blind and resource-sensitive priors are distinct theoretical commitments.

Ancestor-simulation does not entail unlimited self-similar depth. Resource bounds can constrain depth under an explicit overhead model; they do not yield a universal numerical depth limit without justified physical inputs.

Fixed-point equations and recursion theorems can support coherent self-reference. They do not establish a unique physically actual universe or demonstrate that self-reference explains its own actuality.

These results are useful precisely when their scope remains explicit. They should not replace Bostrom’s weighting assumptions with a different unexplained prior while claiming the measure problem has disappeared.

## 13. The ultimate-ground alternatives: retain the common demand, not a premature merger

The inquiry contains several distinct global candidates. They share an aspiration toward internal explanation but cannot all be affirmed as the same completed ontology.

### A. Explanatory necessary constitution

A nature that genuinely accounts for its own actuality would not be brute merely because our theory of it remains uncertain. Epistemic justification and ontological explanation differ.

Bare necessity is not enough; necessity may explain if the nature and its sufficiency are actually established. Mathematics illustrates noncausal modes of explanation without by itself proving concrete self-grounding.

### B. Experiential or self-luminous ultimate

Self-presence is a serious candidate for actuality that is not wholly opaque from within. It motivates an account in which being and intrinsic manifestation belong together.

But self-explanation does not yet entail awareness, and awareness does not by itself explain its own existence or every detail. The required double argument remains: why a non-experiential self-explanatory nature is insufficient, and why the proposed self-presence is sufficient.

### C. Neutral intrinsic constitution

A neutral or proto-experiential intrinsic nature could underlie physical organization without making every object a conscious subject. But a nonconscious basis still owes phenomenal constitution; a basis of micro-experiences owes combination; a globally experiential basis owes local subject differentiation.

A globally substantive intrinsic nature makes the overall account thick even if its local access relation uses thin vocabulary. It cannot simultaneously be advertised as adding no intrinsic ontology anywhere.

### D. Structural plenitude

Actuality of every admissible coherent structure could remove selective realization of only one world. It does not explain its own admissibility criterion or the coherence–actuality principle merely by stating them. It also inherits phenomenality, duplicate-description, and measure obligations.

Plenitude is not forced by the inadequacy of arbitrary selection: a uniquely explanatory necessary constitution would be another candidate.

### E. Non-well-founded explanatory organization

An endless constraint network can uniquely determine its values; the bounded recurrence example demonstrates that absence of a first member need not imply mathematical underdetermination.

That does not establish instantiation or ultimate explanation. But it prevents dismissing every rootless proposal as a tautology without examining it.

**Best present choice:** adopt the occurrence-first constitutive architecture as the common research framework, while keeping these global completions as explicitly competing hypotheses. No supplied argument warrants selecting one as the uniquely achieved answer. Their next test is explanatory work, not another metaphysical label.

## 14. Suspension without retreat

For a declared admissible model class \(K\), an identification set

\[
\operatorname{Id}_K(Q)=\{Q(k):k\in K\}
\]

states what the available constraints determine. A family is not a probability distribution and its cardinality is not an observer count.

The final realization-token work earns this intermediate position: causal and observational constraints identify structural facts and candidate families but do not identify the phenomenal selector or global weights. Actual appearing establishes an occurrence but does not, by performative undeniability alone, establish necessary appearing.

Logical countermodels diagnose missing entailments; they do not establish metaphysically possible complete realities. The absence of a currently justified selector does not prove that no further evidence or constitutive principle could supply one.

Nor is suspension automatically complete explanation. It counts as success in delimiting a particular inference. To dissolve a global explanatory demand, one must justify why there is no additional determinate fact or warranted number to recover—not merely observe that present premises are insufficient.

This is the appropriate combination of ambition and restraint:

> **Do not lower the completeness standard, and do not manufacture completion to satisfy it.**

## 15. The remaining work, consolidated rather than counted

No justified “three residues,” universal residue score, or optimality ranking emerges from the material. Several burdens overlap and may have a common solution. The useful object is a dependency graph:

| Explanatory target | What has been earned | What remains to establish |
|---|---|---|
| Whole-inclusive explanation | External hosts and observers cannot be omitted | Actual explanatory sufficiency of the whole |
| Concrete actuality | An occurrence is actual; abstract specification alone need not entail it | Why occurrence and this constitution obtain |
| Public organization | Conditional macro-laws, quotients, geometry, and quantum constraints | Why their physical inputs and principles hold |
| Phenomenal character | A coherent process-identity and intrinsic-constitution programme | Determination, identity, and explanation of feeling |
| Subjects and occurrences | Constraints separating types, descriptions, paths, and tokens | Actual realization population and phenomenal unity |
| Outcomes and histories | Explicit quantum and modal dependency questions | A justified account fitting definite experience, records, and probability |
| Self-location | Conditional counting and conditionalization results | Weighting, contribution unit, and justified scope |
| Reflective closure | Explanatory principles themselves are included in scrutiny | Why the reasoning, modality, representation, and epistemic norms used are adequate |

The last row does not require treating logic or every norm as an event requiring an earlier cause. It requires not invoking their supposed authority as unexplained machinery while claiming that every explanatory condition has already been accounted for.

A fair comparison applies the same standard to the preferred constitution and its rivals. A proposed identity cannot be counted as solved while a rival’s proposed identity is counted as brute.

## 16. The best next research move

[The 36-step integrated-account construction](INTEGRATED_ACCOUNT.md) develops the possibility that **all** the named mathematical resources can be incorporated through defined domains, explicit translations, and controlled limits. It distinguishes an integration architecture from a demonstrated common physical model, and both from a completed explanation of actuality and experience. No existing result here establishes that the full proposed integration exists, is unique, or meets the zero-residue requirement.

The highest-leverage task is **one explicit constitutive connection with consequences on both sides**.

1. Independently characterize a concrete occurrence or phenomenal relation without inserting quantum axioms or a desired boundary.
2. Derive a necessary organizational condition and attempt the explanatory return: show why it constitutes, rather than merely accompanies, the relevant feature.
3. Seek countermodels satisfying the proposed constitutive premises while violating the claimed consequence.
4. Test the rule on moving storage, shared control, genuine duplication, nested description, branching, merging, and temporal resampling.
5. State which occurrence and interface facts it derives, and which it still assumes.
6. Only then determine what epistemic constraints actually follow. Do not reward the rule merely for reproducing a preferred simulation probability.
7. Pursue the actuality/necessity argument in parallel so that the local construction is not later embedded in an unexplained global foundation by fiat.

The recent boundary work suggests a focused refinement: independently characterize a **whole-episode contrast**, including co-presentation, then test whether episode-relevant near-decomposability or maintenance predicts its transformations across compatible physical and access models. A spectral gap, a produced boundary, or a criticality signature may explain stable organization without establishing subjecthood. Keep that bridge explicit rather than identifying unity with the selected module.

A bounded phenomenal domain—such as valence and its dissociation from sensory intensity—can make the local identity test concrete. An operational principle—such as spectrality—can make the public-physics connection concrete. Neither local success should be mistaken for the whole; their independent convergence would be a substantive advance.

### Final statement

> **Reality is to be understood through its actual constitution, with physical organization and experience both included and neither reduced to an unexplained accompaniment of the other. Realization, not a universal simulation metaphor, is the crucial bridge: it must distinguish occurrences from types and descriptions, explain experiential individuation, and delimit justified self-location. The strongest existing work supplies conditional organization theorems and exact limits on what abstractions preserve. It has not yet explained the complete actuality that makes those constructions physically and phenomenally true. The surviving programme is therefore a disciplined search for one internally justified constitution that unifies these explanatory roles, while retaining plurality where real, suspension where warranted, and the full zero-residue standard throughout.**

## Whole-account conjecture and axiological completion

**Epistemic standing.** [The leading whole-account](CONJECTURAL_WHOLE_ACCOUNT.md) is a working conjecture, not a completed theory: one concrete constitution explains public causal organization and qualitative occurrence without independent observer attachment. Finite minds are realized perspectives, not additional substances. Missing derivations are not automatically evidence of probable falsity; counterexamples, contradictory commitments, and unrestricted repair are different matters. Comparative philosophical preference is not a calibrated probability or empirical consensus.

**Strong completion under the objective.** Complete intelligibility applies to laws, states, realization, subjects, weighting, and the foundation itself. A necessarily concrete, globally determining nature is a promising completion under the strongest contrastive reading of this objective. Neither bare consistency nor the words sufficient reason logically establish that completion. Actual causal organization remains compatible with cross-material realization if the relevant constitutive powers are preserved. A realized workspace remains a promising access mechanism, not a demonstrated law of qualitative constitution. Common nature does not entail a cosmic subject, and process does not by itself settle becoming versus a block presentation.

**Goodness of being and privation.** [The privation assessment](PRIVATION_AND_AXIARCHIC_GOOD.md) develops Good as positive fulfilled constitution and badness as deficient due integration, rather than an independent opposing substance. Cold is a studied physical condition; heat is energy transfer, not a separate material substance. The physical analogies motivate avoiding opposed substances, not a proof about moral value. Denying an evil substance is weaker than showing that every bad-making property or relation is privative. If Good only renames actuality, intrinsic normative worth and actualization have not been explained. If it attributes value, the identity is substantive.

**Goal reversal.** [The exact goal-relative argument](GOAL_RELATIVE_VALUE_ARGUMENT.md) shows that no outcome is strictly better or worse than another for every goal when admissible evaluations include reversals. This addresses the user's intended badness without qualification. It does not make qualified relational badness incoherent, establish universal instrumental Good, or identify normative authority with usefulness for every possible goal. The proposed goodness of being is a separate thesis, not a consequence of that quantifier argument.

**What a privation would be.** [The stress test](PRIVATION_STRESS_TEST.md) indexes fulfillment to a bearer, respect, and circumstances. Mere absence is insufficient: a stone is not blind, and finitude is not automatically wrongdoing. Functioning protective pain can involve successful warning and a local cost simultaneously, so it is not by itself a counterexample to a richer privation account. But absence of pleasure does not distinguish neutrality from suffering. A nontrivial theory must independently specify positive fulfillment and justify why it is due; defining Good as not-Bad cannot do this. The actuality of pain or a harmful act is not denied by analyzing its proposed badness privatively.

**The user's corrected cruelty example.** Seeking money or power is not automatically wrong. The proposed deficiency is missing due practical integration of affected beings' legitimate needs and claims, not merely a destructive direction. Lack of empathy is an example, not a universal psychological diagnosis. An informed exploiter can reason effectively while disregarding others, and someone with little empathic feeling can act fairly. Thin agency and accurate representation do not alone establish moral concern. [The worked entitlement case](DUE_REGARD_ARGUMENT.md) derives payment being due from an applicable valid agreement and qualifying completed work. This is ordinary conditional application, not proof of the rule's moral authority. Consistent institutions can be unjust, and an agent rejecting the rule is not thereby logically contradictory.

**Preferred normative extension.** [The foundation comparison](FOUNDATION_OF_DUE_REGARD.md) favors qualitative fulfillment as the intrinsic-value candidate to develop over arbitrary chosen goals or thin agency alone. It addresses the affected being directly and does not make sophisticated rational agency the gatekeeper of welfare. The central conjecture is that actual qualitative fulfillment supplies prima facie reasons not created by another agent's project. Unchosen valence, reason-giving significance, and interpersonal authority remain distinct; the first does not alone prove the others. Symmetry blocks arbitrary label privilege but does not refute a uniformly agent-relative egoism. A rival accepting all described experiences and needs while denying interpersonal reasons has not been excluded. Under no brute facts, the reason-giving identity itself must be explained.

**Actual fulfillment, not merely representation.** [The welfare/standing/claims analysis](ACTUAL_FULFILLMENT_AND_STANDING.md) distinguishes how a being fares, its proposed standing, and specific legitimate claims. A pleasant belief that payment occurred does not discharge an obligation requiring an actual transfer. The candidate is therefore qualitative-relational fulfillment, not only a pleasure score. An exploiter's pleasure is actual but does not alone settle the affected being's claims. Legitimacy and the relations among these goods require grounding; listing them is not a completed constitution. Conditional on intrinsic value depending on relevant actual constitution, a material label alone cannot alter it. This does not establish that existing software is conscious.

**Limits of fulfillment-based selection.** [The constitutive-value analysis](CONSTITUTIVE_VALUE_PRINCIPLE.md) supplies conditional product-order results, not moral laws: fixed fulfillment preorders yield a partial order after quotienting equivalent profiles, and finite nonempty feasible sets have undominated members. Anonymity and Pareto improvement leave incompatible rankings possible, as the abstract levels (2,0), (1,1), and (0,2) demonstrate. They do not determine a unique best world, settle changing subject populations, or derive priority rules. Under the stronger hypothesis that actuality is undominated, an independently established admissible improvement with no relevant loss would contradict its required status. Mere imaginability does not establish that repair. Finitude does not justify the actual extent of suffering. Defining admissibility as the actual world alone makes undominated status trivial unless uniqueness has an independent constitutive explanation.

**Value and actuality are separate explanatory questions.** [The axiarchic candidate](AXIARCHIC_CONSTITUTION_CANDIDATE.md) distinguishes positive constitution, due fulfillment, intrinsic value, and self-explanatory actuality. [The bridge audit](AXIARCHIC_BRIDGE_AUDIT.md), applying the earlier unconstrained-actuality diagnostic, grants even a uniquely best candidate and shows its realization does not follow without a bridge. Excluding inferior realization also differs from establishing any realization. This does not prove metaphysical independence; it delimits the stated premises. A common ground of occurrence and value does not by itself make value the asymmetric actualizer. Normative and existence inquiries should proceed in parallel.

**Concrete necessity.** [The self-explanation audit](SELF_EXPLANATORY_ACTUALITY_AUDIT.md) distinguishes self-support, unique formal coherence, and necessary determination. The first two do not establish occurrence. A necessary ground G plus a necessary implication from G to actuality P entails necessary P, but the ground and connection have not been established. [The necessary-occurrence candidate](NECESSARY_OCCURRENCE_CANDIDATE.md) adds the essence–instance distinction: every instance being necessarily concrete does not establish any instance. In S5, possible necessity implies necessity; mere possibility does not. Beginning from known occurrence avoids manufacturing an instance from a definition, but still requires an independent explanation of why its nature is noncontingent. No concrete nature satisfying all these requirements has yet been supplied.

**Current integrated conjecture.** One concrete nature constitutes causal powers and qualitative perspectives; its realized qualitative-relational fulfillment may have intrinsic significance, with wrongdoing privative of due integration. A necessarily concrete determination of the whole is the preferred strong completion to investigate. Universal privation, the reason-giving identity, and Good as indispensable to actualization remain separate live conjectures. [The consolidated axiological assessment](AXIOLOGY_STATUS.md) distinguishes their support and rival accounts. These developments claim no new empirical findings, executable research results, numerical likelihoods, or increases in the established test count.
