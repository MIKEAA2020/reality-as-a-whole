## Why the restricted pass is meaningful

The packet is not merely a prose declaration that a framework has answers. Its battery exercises specific connected interfaces:

1. **A global domain and finite test closure:** accepted code strings are kept separate from rejected strings and decoded records; quotient operations act on the typed records. Named cases, successor records and content patterns have a stated finite closure and global/locality comparison.
2. **Actual-bearer preflight:** positive cases are carried by declared actual structures; the positive phenomenal case also uses a distinct actual-experience token and realization relation. Nonactual controls are not substituted for actual negative cases.
3. **One interface, nontrivial controls:** one actual locus hosts both correct and incorrect presentations; one actual structural setting supplies positive/negative phenomenal controls; action comparison is scoped to the host; and the finite physical target is checked by a rule rather than just restated.
4. **Explicit scope and residues:** instantiation and experience-realization are acknowledged as primitives. The R3.c record explicitly says the restricted pass does not establish public truth, selector discharge, zero residue or empirical physical recovery.
5. **Frozen operation and lineage:** post-result changes in the candidate's core are treated as a fresh candidate, not quietly imported into a previously adjudicated one.

Those are practical design insights for passing an interface battery. They do **not** mean our point–sphere system can inherit PSR-5's verdict, nor that every formal battery has identical entry rules.

## How to apply the insight to our zero candidate

The existing [sphere–echo candidate](ZERO_SPHERE_BATTERY_INTEGRATED_BRIDGE_CANDIDATE.md) begins with one shared history and gives a typed physical content/target relation, a true/error control, the same two subject-quality hypotheses and incidence measures, all on that one system. Its tick-10 record correlation and inverted-decoder control are verified. That is a useful start, but it is **not** yet an externally frozen, admitted or completed counterpart to PSR-5's battery.

A serious successor packet should explicitly provide, on **one shared zero-constitution model**:

| Interface | Minimum packet content | What must not be confused with a pass |
|---|---|---|
| R0-like global and local model | Typed carriers and accepted/rejected codes; every fixture's full fields and carrier order; operations, quotient transport, complete dependencies; a proved finite-slice/global link or an honestly different model regime. | Naming a zero, writing a finite circuit, or claiming totality without the closed definitions. |
| R1-like actuality and identity | A fixed criterion for actual structures and locus identity, preserving the chosen equivalence; positive actual and negative nonactual controls. | Declaring every mathematical description genuinely possible or treating a model's positive support as proof of metaphysical actuality. |
| R2-like content and error | A typed content/reference channel with a fixed target and correct/error controls at an appropriate actual bearer. If public truth is claimed, separate acquisition, provenance and witness criteria. | A label `true`, or the ideal sphere-model decoder alone, as a public-truth bridge. |
| R3.a-like experience | Distinct domain, realization relation and positive/negative bearer controls; explain the link to the proposed subject and quality law. | A physical parity being automatically felt, or an explicitly primitive bridge being presented as no-residue success. |
| R3.b-like normative interface | Host-scoped action domain, total successor, feasibility, veto and ranking operations, fixed controls and precise normative scope. | A stipulated ordering proving objective moral truth or cancelling suffering. |
| R3.c-like physical interface | A genuinely fixed domain, dynamics, initial/target data and independent calculations; state the physical regime actually tested. | A finite dynamics fixture as recovery of quantum theory, relativity, gravity or real-world physics. |
| Stronger zero objective | Explain why the same constitution, its experiential realization and its incidence measure are actual and self-subsistent, without an extra unexplained selector. | Restricted formal-interface completion as a no-residue pass. |

This is an **application checklist**, not a claim that this project is already registered for PSR-5's independent rival battery or that satisfying these example bullets would automatically pass another external battery. The actual external rules and preflight matter. In particular, the finite candidate can openly display assumptions while being evaluated as an interface; the user's stronger aim requires either deriving those assumptions from the proposed zero constitution or showing their self-sufficiency. Disclosure alone is not discharge, and an unresolved demand does not establish impossibility.

The next constructive task is to produce a versioned, genuinely closed point–sphere packet using this integrated methodology, and then test it against the actual applicable battery. For the stronger aim, the decisive bridge must tie the same boundary, `sj` versus `sjr` quality distinction, and incidence weighting to a defensible common constitution rather than leave three independent choices. The PSR example gives a model of how to make that work inspectable; it does not by itself supply the missing bridge.
