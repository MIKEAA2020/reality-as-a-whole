# Fixed plan: privately related local and global fields

## Prior knowledge and motivation

The [Basile audit](BASILE_PANPSYCHISM_HOLISM_AND_SUBJECT_QUALITY_AUDIT.md) has already identified the alternatives of shared experiential tokens, private related occurrences and one field with multiple descriptions. The [Lopez-inspired comparison](LAYERED_UNITY_AND_SUBJECT_QUALITY_COMPARISON.md) already constructs L-exclusive, C-full and C-layered stipulations on the same four-spin physical kernel, with global/local conjunction predictions. E-L already postulates pair-level q quality. These are known, and this analysis is not a preregistered experiment or a blind discovery.

## Fixed task

1. Develop one explicit **private-token nested** completion that postulates two locally selected pair subjects and a distinct whole-level subject at `t=1/4`, without requiring those local experiences to be shared tokens or claiming that the physical selector entails subjecthood. Fix exactly which local and global qualities and conjunctions each subject has; keep distinct physical overlaps, experiential ownership, information content and actual subjects.
2. Choose a narrowly defined whole-quality rule, label it as an additional postulate, and say how pair-level q contrast and experienced context are related or distinguished. Compare the proposal to exclusive L, whole-only C-full and C-layered using common *type/content* targets (not falsely common token identity) and the same hypothetical observation model. Explicitly mark every postulate that has no supporting evidence.
3. Identify at least one difference between the private nested completion and each rival, but do not promise observational discrimination if the necessary first-person subject identity, access or physical mapping cannot be established. Make no inference from an unresponsive channel to absent experience. The same person, agent, behavior or reporter need not establish one phenomenal subject; multiple response channels do not automatically establish multiple ones.
4. Analyze whether the completion addresses Basile's holism and Lopez's partial unity fairly. In particular, matching qualitative or content types across distinct occurrences is not identical to literal token sharing; a link between duplicate content does not itself show a unity relation across fields. Distinguish q, its coarse pleasantness contrast and full qualitative character.
5. State the strongest positive content, remaining noncircular constitution and individuation costs, and the conditions under which the candidate would fail or remain observationally underdetermined. Avoid another arbitrary numerical toy exercise and do not claim the model, a phenomenological assay or a global world is empirically established or genuinely possible merely because described.

## Deliverable

Produce a focused conceptual comparison and precise evidence gate, cross-link it from the Basile and layered audits and project status, refresh the mechanical inventories, and run relevant existing checks. Preserve C3 as full structural and experiential intelligibility; B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 remain open.
