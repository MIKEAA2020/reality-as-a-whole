# Universal consciousness, shared experience and persistence through quantum reversal

> **Status correction:** A universal subject is a tested rival, not the current target. The user had already pivoted to the [experiential whole with plural finite subjects](SELF_EXISTENT_PLENITUDE_PLURALITY_CORRECTION.md) without assuming a universal agent. This paper's one-observer theorem may still constrain candidate finite-subject histories under its assumptions.

## The advance and its limit

A previously unincorporated result changes the next step: the problem is not only whether **different observers'** outcomes can be pooled. Even two successive perceptions attributed to **one observer** can resist a single, preparation-independent quantum probability law under a Wigner's-friend intervention. This was established by [Allard Guérin, Baumann, Del Santo and Brukner (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-021-00589-1), not discovered here.

This matters for our target because a genuine universal subject should not be a mere set of unrelated subject labels. If its unity is claimed to include a single enduring, probabilistically connected history of the finite experiences it shares, then the account must say how that history survives interventions that coherently reverse its physical correlates. **The theorem does not establish that subject identity is impossible, that no experience occurred, or that time is fundamental.** It constrains a specified combination of joint probabilities, single-time quantum predictions and preparation independence.

We apply that published result to the existing whole–finite-subject problem and give an exact rational example. The example is a quantum-memory model, not an actual experience, proof of a universal agent, or a new no-go theorem.

## 1. The established one-observer theorem

The cited authors consider a friend whose recorded outcome is described at two stages, before and after Wigner's intervention. Their three assumptions, in abbreviated form, are:

- **P1 — joint two-stage probability:** the early and late perceived outcomes have a joint distribution, with the expected single-stage marginals.
- **P2 — unitary one-stage marginals:** each marginal is calculated from the relevant uncollapsed, unitarily evolved state.
- **P3 — preparation-affine joint law:** the joint distribution depends convex-linearly on the initial system density matrix, with the measurement setup fixed.

For general choices of Wigner's measurement, the three cannot all hold. The argument does not rely on two spatially separated laboratories or Local Agency. P3 is a genuine extra assumption, not simply the assertion that probabilities obey ordinary arithmetic: it requires the density matrix to contain all preparation information relevant to the joint two-time law. The paper discusses, among other alternatives, dropping this assumption through additional hidden information, or restricting the joint attribution. Its conclusions about particular interpretations are attributed to the authors, not established here as uniquely correct.

This is **not** a theorem against joint distributions for one fixed state. Given any two marginal distributions for a fixed state, ordinary couplings exist. The result is that no *single preparation-affine rule* can give the stipulated marginals for **all** inputs in the specified scenario. Nor does it say a friend's memory cannot be genuinely stable in ordinary, decohered circumstances; the difficulty concerns the special coherent intervention and its stipulated predictions.

## 2. Why a relational universal subject must face this too

Suppose, as a **bridge assumption**, that a proposed universal subject `g` genuinely shares a finite friend's early experience and the friend's later experience, and that both have the same definite binary content as the modeled memory at their respective stages. Add the further claims that:

1. These two shared contents belong to one joint subject-history with an ordinary joint probability distribution.
2. Their one-stage probabilities agree with P2 for the stipulated laboratory circuit.
3. Their joint distribution obeys P3 for all relevant input preparations.

Those three claims inherit the published contradiction for the specified intervention. Calling the contents “relative to `g`” does not by itself remove it: P1–P3 can be stated **within the one proposed perspective**. Neither Local Agency nor a cosmic narrator is needed for this particular argument.

Crucially, **sharing tokens, being one subject, and having an affine joint history are distinct claims**. The quantum memory projectors do not establish either experiential content or sharing. The bridge is hypothetical; without it the theorem cannot be applied to `g`. Nor does the failure of the three claims imply that `g` ceases to exist, that the friend did not experience anything, or that every experience lacks a history. It means the proposed bridge must identify which assumption changes and give an alternative that is consistent and explanatory.

### Two different pressures, not one

- The [Local Friendliness gate](UNIVERSAL_SUBJECT_QUANTUM_COMPARISON_GATE.md) tests a joint friend/Alice/Bob account plus specified intervention-independence conditions. A single common perspective does not automatically bypass it.
- The present published theorem tests **one subject at two stages**, unitary one-stage predictions, and an affine two-stage rule. It does not need the Local Friendliness causal assumptions.

An acceptable proposal must address **both** when both sets of assumptions apply. Dropping absoluteness in the first argument does not automatically establish an acceptable two-stage probability law in the second. Conversely, dropping P3 here does not by itself make Local Agency hold in the first.

## 3. One exact instance

Let the system and memory be qubits, and encode the system's `|0>` and `|1>` as `|00>` and `|11>` in system–friend order. Let Wigner measure the two encoded states in the orthonormal basis

    |φ₁> = (3|00> + 4|11>)/5,
    |φ₂> = (4|00> − 3|11>)/5.

Ignore the Wigner outcome. Compare the early and later single-stage memory-up probabilities as functions of the same initial system density matrix `ρ`. The corresponding effects on the initial system are

    E₁ = [ 1  0 ]          E₂ = (1/625) [ 337  −84 ],
         [ 0  0 ]                          [ −84  288 ]

and

    [E₁,E₂] = (1/625) [  0  −84 ].
                         [ 84    0 ]

The early effect is a sharp projector. By the joint-measurability result used in the published paper, a joint POVM with these marginals exists **if and only if** the two effects commute. Here they do not. The no-go follows for a joint law affine in `ρ`, because such a law over all density matrices is represented by a joint POVM. This is the published proof method instantiated at rational coefficients, not an independent discovery.

For a concrete intervention comparison, let the input be `|+>=(|0>+|1>)/√2`. The later memory-up probability is `457/1250` in the stated uninterrupted unitary model. If one actually dephases the memory alternatives between the two stages, it becomes `1/2`; the difference is `84/625`. Thus **inserting a physical record-reading intervention is a different experiment**. The calculation does not imply that experiencing an outcome itself performs such a dephasing operation.

## 4. A finite control: a joint distribution is possible for one state

For the fixed `|+>` input, the early memory-up marginal is `1/2` and the later memory-up marginal is `457/1250`. One possible mathematical coupling is the independent table

| | Late up | Late down |
|---|---:|---:|
| Early up | 457/2500 | 793/2500 |
| Early down | 457/2500 | 793/2500 |

It is nonnegative, normalized and has both desired single-stage marginals. This explicitly rules out the mistaken claim that those marginals alone are contradictory for this particular preparation.

But extending this **product rule** to every input makes the joint law nonlinear in the density matrix. For the maximally mixed input it gives `P(up,up)=1/4`. Preparing that input as an equal classical mixture of the `|0>` and `|1>` inputs and averaging their product-rule joint probabilities instead gives `337/1250`, a difference of `49/2500`. The single-time marginals agree between the two descriptions; the proposed joint rule is preparation-sensitive or incomplete as a function of `ρ` alone. Classical probabilities still combine linearly when the actual preparation is included; this is not evidence of nonlinear quantum dynamics.

The product table is **only a logical control**. It arbitrarily makes earlier and later outcomes independent, does not account for normal memory continuity, supplies no physical or experiential law, and does not resolve Local Friendliness. It shows precisely what is gained and lost by dropping P3, not that this rule is a plausible ontology.

## 5. A concrete candidate and its remaining test

The least revisionary candidate consistent with the user's goal is to retain a genuinely **one, agentive, intrinsically experiential** whole while making the following claims separately, rather than identifying them:

- The same universal subject can be intrinsically related to multiple finite subjects, and sharing a token is allowed where an independently justified constitution warrants it.
- A finite agent may possess a stable experiential history in ordinary circumstances with sufficiently stable records. A quantum-interference intervention may make a proposed **joint** narrative of early and later contents unavailable under P1–P3, or may require a richer law with additional state/preparation information. Neither option says the earlier experience was unreal.
- The universal subject's identity is **not defined as** one context-free, affine probability distribution over all finite subjects' experiences. Its unity and agency need their own positive constitution; refusing that definition alone does not establish either one.

This is a disciplined **candidate**, not a demonstrated solution. It does not yet specify the subject-incidence law, phenomenal qualities, a usable non-affine/relational two-stage dynamics, cross-perspective agreement, emergent time, or an actual physical implementation. It must also say whether shared experiences permit overlapping subjects and how contextual scope interacts with shared tokens; labelling the same subject in incompatible contexts is not a proof of a real agent.

The laboratory stages `t₁,t₂` are internal orderings used to describe a conditional physical experiment, not an external clock for the absolute. The proposed whole remains timeless at the fundamental level; the derivation of local temporal order and experienced duration is still open. Strongest possibility=actuality likewise does not establish P1–P3 or their replacements. If the existing self-subsistence and essentially experiential premises are granted, they already entail the corresponding self-existence and experiential nature; the unresolved question is whether those premises and this subject constitution are justified, not what causes an already self-existent whole. The adopted non-optimality premises still imply a non-perfect whole.
