# IIT 4.0 exclusion versus private nested subjects

## Verdict

Follow-up: the [local-net classical-substrate bridge](LOCAL_NET_CLASSICAL_SUBJECT_INCIDENCE_BRIDGE.md) tests a particular quantum measurement record for effective-state closure. Its failure is not a refutation of IIT, which does not require that record to be a unit.

IIT 4.0 supplies a substantive, independently motivated competitor to the private nested-fields proposal P: for a specified physical universe and grain, it selects non-overlapping maximal complexes, rather than granting a separate experience to every overlapping irreducible candidate. Its motivation is not merely a preference for the largest object: the authors seek a definite, irreducible substrate **from the perspective of the entity**, governed by a maximal-existence principle. They also propose that the experience *is* the complex's structured cause–effect power, not a numerical phi score.

But the claim that **each experience is definite** does not, by itself, logically exclude two distinct, definite experiences with overlapping physical supports. IIT's exclusion conclusion needs its further physical translation, its competition rule for overlapping systems, and its claim that this rule uniquely determines intrinsic existence. P rejects that competition rule while leaving its own incidence and quality rules largely unexplained. Neither the description of P nor this logical distinction shows that P is metaphysically or physically possible. IIT's coherent alternative is a challenge to P, not a calculation that the four-spin toy selects either pairs or whole.

This is a retrospective source audit under the [reading plan](IIT4_EXCLUSION_PRIVATE_FIELDS_READING_PLAN.md). It does not implement IIT, inspect a patient, or close the project's experiential gates.

## 2. Where the exclusion argument comes from

The primary paper's five purported essential phenomenal axioms are intrinsicality, specificity, unity, definiteness and composition. Its definiteness example is a particular current experience including the visual scene, not a smaller experience containing only its left side or a larger one containing the wall behind the observer. The paper calls these properties immediate and irrefutable, then says translating them into physical postulates and mathematical expressions is an **inference to a good explanation**, under realism, operational physicalism and operational atomism—not a deduction from introspection alone ([axioms and postulates](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465#sec002), Box 1).

Its positive reasoning is stronger than the bare slogan 'there must be a boundary': a substrate must take and make a specific difference **over itself** as an irreducible whole, and it must be *this* definite whole. Overlapping candidates can each have positive irreducibility. Box 2's **principle of maximal existence** proposes that, where candidates compete for the same substrate over an essential requirement for existence, what exists is what exists most: choose the candidate with the strongest claim to being one system, measured by system integrated information. Lower-scoring overlapping candidates are excluded as independent complexes. The corresponding minimal-existence principle assesses a claim to unity at its weakest relevant partition and on the weaker of cause and effect. These principles are offered as explanatory inferences; the paper does not establish that they are the only metaphysically coherent principles.

The inferential gap can be made explicit without pretending to exhibit a possible world: (i) suppose field A has definite contents and support `{0,1}`; (ii) distinct field B has definite, private contents and support `{0,1,2,3}`. Neither definition says A's experience is also B's, has fuzzy boundaries, or is a less-than-complete version of B's. Thus individual definiteness plus *mere* physical overlap gives no formal contradiction. To rule it out one must add, for example, IIT's rule that overlapping substrate candidates are mutually exclusive as independent intrinsic entities and that the maximum wins (with tie provisions), plus its causal-structural identity. Merely renaming B as a larger description of A would assume rather than establish their identity. Conversely, P's stipulation of separate fields does not show that an overlapping causal substrate can really support them or explain why there are exactly three.

IIT's overlapping **distinctions and relations within a single complex** do not themselves constitute overlapping subjects. Similarly, a subset can figure in a whole's causal structure without being an independent complex. This is not an argument that one numerical experience token belongs to two subjects; P's private tokens avoid Basile's specific shared-token contradiction but do not evade IIT's substrate-level competition. Compare the [Basile audit](BASILE_PANPSYCHISM_HOLISM_AND_SUBJECT_QUALITY_AUDIT.md) and [private comparison](PRIVATE_NESTED_FIELDS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_CONTENT_COMPARISON.md).

## 3. What IIT actually computes—and what it identifies

The ideal input is an interventional transition probability matrix for finite discrete units, their current state, a discrete update, and conditional independence of unit transitions given the previous state. For each candidate set, units outside it are treated as background, causally marginalized conditional on the universe's current state; cause and effect background treatments differ. Uniform distributions over possible states enter the intrinsic calculation, rather than substituting the observed frequency distribution. For each current candidate state, IIT selects cause and effect states by **intrinsic information**: selectivity times informativeness. It compares them with directed partitions in which affected connections are noised; system `φ_s` uses the weaker cause/effect value at a minimum partition. Partition selection is normalized by its maximum possible value, while the selected system value is unnormalized ([system formalism](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465#sec025)).

The recursive procedure selects a maximal `φ_s` system and removes its units from subsequent selection, retaining removed units as background, and continues on the rest. The authors describe resulting complexes as disjoint. The full program also compares spatiotemporal and unit grains; a selected macro-unit must meet its own internal irreducibility condition. This is not simply “the largest system wins,” nor equivalent to the project's L score. Interventions, admissible units, background, state and grain matter. The primary paper acknowledges that background handling and tie resolution remain open to evaluation and that exhaustive realistic calculations are infeasible.

For a selected complex, composition unfolds mechanisms (unit subsets), their irreducible cause/effect states over purviews, and congruent overlaps as relations. Distinction and relation integrated-information values contribute to **structure integrated information `Φ`**; the resulting *Φ-structure* is the structured entity IIT identifies with experience. `φ_s` selects a substrate, whereas `Φ` summarizes the unfolded structure's quantity and the arrangement of distinctions and relations is meant to account for quality. A scalar alone cannot recover color, affect, conjunction or ownership. The paper proposes an **explanatory identity**, not a mere predictive correlation or a correspondence between two separate substances: subjective feeling and the objective operational account concern one entity under different perspectives. Whether the proposed structure actually explains specific feels and why this identification rather than a rival is correct remains a major open question. IIT itself points to work in progress on particular qualities and content; no derivation of the present project's `q`-to-unpleasantness rule follows.

**Ties:** S1 says overlapping systems tied on maximal `φ_s` are compared by their structure `Φ`; if still tied, the tied candidates do not qualify and the next-best unique system is considered. It also describes separate cause/effect-state and mechanism ties, including an exception when apparently different states specify intrinsically identical structures. These are IIT-specific provisions, not a license to choose a winner arbitrarily or a demonstration that toy-selector ties have been resolved. See the prior [tie audit](SUBJECT_TIES_PERSISTENCE_REDESCRIPTION_GATE.md).

## 4. Comparison at the four-spin interface

At `t=1/4` the project's L selector chooses the two designated pairs and C chooses the whole. P adds **both** pair fields and a distinct whole field with private corresponding coarse tokens. L-exclusive stipulates just pair fields; C-full and C-layered each stipulate a single whole field with different content-unity relations. The [common target table](PRIVATE_NESTED_FIELDS_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_CONTENT_COMPARISON.md) gives `D,G,N` respectively: L-exclusive `(0,0,0)`, C-full `(1,1,0)`, C-layered `(0,1,0)`, P `(0,1,1)`. These are **package stipulations**, not values of IIT quantities.

| Proposal | Conditional relation to IIT 4.0 |
|---|---|
| P | If the same pair and whole supports are treated as competing overlapping IIT substrates in one correctly specified universe and grain, IIT's exclusion does not admit all three as distinct complexes. This is a rule-level conflict, independent of which wins. A different interpretation of 'support', causal grain, time, or ontology would need a precise mapping before declaring a contradiction in a real case. |
| L-exclusive | Non-overlapping pair incidence resembles a possible IIT selection, but its selector and q rule are not IIT. Agreement would require the actual IIT calculation and content/identity assumptions; the table's absence of global coarse conjunction does not follow merely from pair selection. |
| C-full / C-layered | A single whole complex is also a possible kind of IIT result, not an established result for this sector. Its structure must be unfolded to assess either content assignment; IIT cannot simply be labeled C-full or C-layered on a score. The layered package's partial-unity assumptions also need independent assessment against IIT's unity axiom. |
| IIT 4.0 itself | No `(D,G,N)` row can be honestly filled for this toy without an admissible causal model, a completed competing-system and grain analysis, Φ-structure unfolding, and an independently justified map to the specific experiential targets. The spin kernel and abstract labels alone do not supply these. |

Even an IIT result favoring the whole would not itself prove the subjective result by mathematical calculation alone: the explanatory identity and mapping from physical units to a genuinely applicable causal model remain premises. Likewise, the project's other [IIT-style causal proxy](research/causal_exclusion_candidate/REPORT.md) has documented limitations and is not IIT 4.0. At `t=1/2` L's own tie remains unresolved; no IIT tie result is implied.

## 5. Ontology challenge, with possible reply

The primary paper calls experience intrinsic existence, defined as for itself, and characterizes physical existence operationally in terms of observed/manipulated cause–effect power. It also says a complex exists unfolded as a structure, absolutely rather than relative to an observer, while overlapping lower-`φ_s` candidates are excluded as complexes. It assumes a world independent of *our* experience. Do not flatten these distinctions into the unsupported claim that the selected complex makes every overlapping physical unit or ordinary causal description disappear from all perspectives. Its grain section explicitly treats units as constituents of a complex without their existing as complexes in their own right.

Cea et al. interpret a broader IIT ontology, using both the 2023 formulation and a 2022 ontological text, as denying objective independent existence to nonconscious extrinsic entities. Their **intrinsicality 2.0** challenge is that a complex's intrinsic existence can depend on changes to excluded overlapping competitors or outside units. Their example reports `AB` 0.32 versus `AC` 0.28, then after changing C `AB` 0.29 versus `AC` 0.30; they regard this as a change of genuinely existing complex. Note that AB's reported `φ_s` itself changes: unchanged internal AB wiring does not imply unchanged computed intrinsic value under changed background. Their **engineering** challenge asks how conscious intrinsic systems can be assembled from parts said not to exist independently; their three-gate example and assumed nonconscious components are theirs, not experimentally established facts here.

They recognize an IIT reply: *intrinsic* as “for itself” differs from the standard philosophical requirement of independence from all external facts; hence a relational maximum does not by itself contradict IIT's usage. A further reply distinguishes operational dependencies from ontological constitution or appeals to independently existing micro-complexes (“ontological dust”). The critics argue these responses leave real dependence unexplained and object to a fundamental partless monad; the latter objection and their broader eliminativist reading are contested, not settled by the comparison. An IIT defender could deny that an excluded aggregate is literally a mere idea, distinguish a model variable from a separately fundamental entity, or ground changes in independent intrinsic constituents and relations—but must then say what makes the modeled interventions and comparisons real. Neither response automatically refutes the other. P must likewise explain why a change in surrounding causal organization should or should not alter local subjecthood, not merely say its fields are private.

## 6. What could discriminate, and what cannot

A prospective comparison needs all of the following before turning the table into an empirical test:

1. **A bounded physical population and mapping:** specify participants, episode, simultaneous time window, candidate units, admissible interventions, environment and grain; defend correspondence to the declared supports. Estimate an interventional model with uncertainty and check whether the discrete, conditional-independence and background assumptions are appropriate. A patient with divided processing is not thereby an instance of the spin sector. An IIT approximation needs a validated error bound or sensitivity analysis sufficient to preserve the ranking across plausible models and grains; ordinary connectivity or complexity is not `φ_s` or `Φ`.
2. **Pre-register complete competing packages and target probes:** fix `D`, `G`, `N`, reporting channels, expected behavior including misses, and each package's subject and content rules. A calibrated dual-field task might seek simultaneous first-person conjunction by confidence-rated trial reports, controls for memory and response interference, channel and timing controls, and independently justified access to candidate local and global fields. This would be a proposal, not an already validated subject census. First-person reports are evidence, not noise to be explained away; silence or failure to report leaves experience uncertain. Multiple channels alone do not prove distinct subjects.
3. **Preserve the actual contrasts:** sufficiently warranted `G=1` challenges the *specified* L-exclusive package, not the L physical selector alone or IIT generically. `D=0,G=1` does not distinguish P from C-layered; a well-supported `N=1` would challenge the specified one-field C completions and, **if** an independent mapping showed those subjects have overlapping IIT supports in the same window and grain, IIT's disjoint-incidence claim as applied there. That conjunction of subject individuation and support mapping is particularly demanding; neither two reports nor a global report establishes it. `D=1` challenges P and C-layered as stipulated, not all nested-field theories. A null requires justified population coverage and assay sensitivity; otherwise report unknown.
4. **Test quality separately:** prospectively specify and independently calibrate the proposed `q` and subjective contrast, avoiding conflation of pain intensity, unpleasantness, anxiety, expectation or valence. A good report predictor or perturbational complexity classifier is not by itself a proof of the phenomenal identity, subject boundaries, or the four-spin `q` law. The primary paper's published predictions are theory claims, not evidence independently reviewed here.

These tests are ethically constrained: use non-invasive or clinically indicated measurements with consent and validated access; no intervention is warranted just to force a subject-boundary determination. Existing split-brain first-person testimony remains genuine evidence and the limitations in the [attention audit](ATTENTION_CAPACITY_VERSUS_LIVED_UNITY_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md) remain. Shared control or capacity is not identical to experienced unity, and nonresponse is not evidence of absence without an access model. Current observations and toy assignments do not establish a discriminating `N` result.

## 7. Consequences for the project

P now has an explicit rival with a motivated incidence rule; it has not acquired one of its own. IIT provides a detailed causal-structural quality hypothesis and a non-overlap rule, but neither source provides a proof that definiteness alone entails exclusion or that the project has reproduced the IIT analysis. The most useful next step is to specify admissible interventions and competing complete causal/phenomenal models before claiming any discriminating subject observation. C3, B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 remain open; no substantive framework battery pass follows.
