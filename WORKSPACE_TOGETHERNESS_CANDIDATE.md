# First togetherness candidate: common recurrent access, with an empirical target

## Direct result

A **common recurrent-access hypothesis** can be stated as a specific local candidate for togetherness, but it is not yet a complete episode law. It distinguishes active source organization from passive copying. It still needs independently justified workspace boundaries, temporal delimitation, and phenomenal evidence.

The next step is therefore not another synthetic consciousness assignment. A primary experimental protocol has been located and inspected to constrain the evidence problem: COGITATE Experiment 1 independently records stimulus content, duration, task role, and button response. The documentation does not itself establish the candidate or measure phenomenal unity.

No participant recordings were downloaded, no empirical results were reanalyzed, and no verdict about GNWT versus IIT is drawn.

## 1. Freeze a local hypothesis instead of hiding the missing claim

Let P be the physically specified process. Let W be an independently identified recurrent-access organization, not a region named conscious because it produces the desired result. Let tau be a physical access interval delimited by declared gating or maintenance events.

Let C_W(tau) be the physically identified content-token distinctions concurrently maintained and available within W during tau. Availability means an implemented ability to influence flexible consumer operations and the ongoing control organization under the declared intervention family. It does not mean that an experimenter has labeled a stimulus task-relevant.

The local hypothesis H_W is:

> Within the declared domain, independently anchored qualitative contents belong to one episode exactly when their realized content distinctions participate in the same recurrent-access organization during the same access interval.

A schematic relation is

    J_W(c_i,c_j;tau) iff c_i and c_j belong to C_W(tau).

The physical membership rule and the assertion that it is phenomenal togetherness are separate. H_W explicitly adds the latter assertion. The formula is not a derivation of it.

Without an independent way of selecting W and tau, this remains a local conditional candidate, not Gamma(P). The full law cannot take its subject boundary as a hidden input.

## 2. What the hypothesis gets right in the existing physical models

### Active source versus passive copy

The source can causally affect memory, action, comparison, and feedback; the passive monitor cannot. The already implemented intervention witnesses therefore distinguish the two physical organizations even when their records and Fisher geometry match.

This is a reason to include causal organization in a candidate basis. It is not a proof that the source is conscious or that the passive monitor cannot have any possible experience.

### Report independence

A report-only lesion need not interrupt the remaining access organization. Conditional on H_W and preserved W, it predicts preserved togetherness despite loss of that evidence channel. This is a proposed consequence, not an observed no-report experience in the toy system.

### Independent content distinctions

Independent variation of contents does not imply independent dynamics, as the earlier differentiation-unity construction established. H_W can accommodate differentiated contents within one organization without identifying differentiation with division into subjects.

The existing one-slot workspace is not a validated implementation of several conscious contents. These points concern prerequisite physical distinctions and compatibility, not a passed test of phenomenal unity.

## 3. Boundary stress tests

### Weak coupling

If any nonzero influence between two systems is counted as common access, H_W inherits the earlier exact-core discontinuity: arbitrarily weak effective coupling changes the boundary verdict. Renaming interaction access does not fix this.

A stronger implementation must specify which currently realized gate, maintenance, or control structure makes the contents jointly available. Conditioning on an actual gate event can distinguish realized transactions from their ensemble frequency, but it is an additional realization-sensitive proposal. It is not a general solution for open stochastic or quantum systems, and it does not establish that a rare transaction merges subjects.

**Result:** bare coupling is rejected as the definition of C_W; a complete physically justified access rule is still missing.

### Temporal boundaries

Using actual entry and release events is more physically specific than an arbitrary coordinate bin. But a continuously maintained neural process may have no unique transaction boundary. Nested gates and asynchronous updates can generate several plausible intervals.

**Result:** a declared transaction is usable for a local hypothesis, but cannot yet be promoted to an intrinsic universal episode clock.

### Shared hardware and overlapping transactions

Two transactions can share a controller without sharing the same phenomenal content-occurrence. Taking the transitive closure of overlapping causal supports would repeat the earlier false-amalgamation error.

H_W must retain transaction provenance, distinguish stored representations from the earlier occurrences represented, and not equate lineage with numerical identity. Returning all possible access intervals would yield a family of functional supports, not automatically actual episodes.

**Result:** neither common hardware nor maximal connectedness supplies Gamma.

### External task conventions

A purely verbal relabeling of a task cannot change a fixed intrinsic occurrence. Changing instructions, attention, or motor requirements can change the physical process, so invariance under those interventions must not be assumed. A definition based solely on the experimenter's task category is neither an intrinsic access rule nor a consciousness law.

**Result:** task relevance is a variable to separate and investigate, not an accepted label of phenomenal presence or unity.

## 4. Independent primary protocol located

The COGITATE documentation inspected here is pinned to commit

    6eb5d9daa664211ad3573e2a369b340979e9e926

of `Cogitate-consortium/cogitate-data`. Its [overview](https://github.com/Cogitate-consortium/cogitate-data/blob/6eb5d9daa664211ad3573e2a369b340979e9e926/docs/02_overview.md#experiment-1-conscious-perception) describes Experiment 1 as varying task relevance and duration for clearly visible stimuli, with content and duration questions distinguished from task consequences.

The [detailed procedure, lines 175–201](https://github.com/Cogitate-consortium/cogitate-data/blob/6eb5d9daa664211ad3573e2a369b340979e9e926/docs/03_cogdataset.md#L175-L201) specifies sequential suprathreshold images, one at a time; task-relevant targets, task-relevant non-targets, and task-irrelevant stimuli; and durations of 500, 1000, and 1500 ms. It includes category and orientation manipulations. These are documented design facts, not conclusions about which trial was experienced.

The [event scheme, lines 243–295](https://github.com/Cogitate-consortium/cogitate-data/blob/6eb5d9daa664211ad3573e2a369b340979e9e926/docs/03_cogdataset.md#L243-L295) records stimulus information, duration and relevance triggers, a button-press trigger, and stimulus offset separately. That makes it a plausible resource for distinguishing content, task, and response explanations.

It does **not** make button events a direct consciousness measure. Nor are task-irrelevant trials automatically unconscious trials, or this entire protocol an absence-of-report paradigm. One sequential stimulus design also does not directly manipulate subject splitting or establish a togetherness relation.

## 5. A concrete evidence question

For the same stimulus identity or features across different task roles, ask whether a candidate neural organization tracks:

1. the stimulus and its presentation duration;
2. its independently warranted experienced features;
3. task selection and maintenance;
4. the button response or later report.

These are potentially different targets. A representation that decodes stimulus identity may concern an input, a memory, or a report-related state. Its mere decodability does not establish constitution.

A proposed analysis should preserve stimulus identity, duration, task condition, and response timing as separate variables, use held-out identities or task conditions where appropriate, and account for actual sampling and modality differences. Task assignment need not be treated as a clean causal intervention on only one neural component; instructions and attention have multiple possible effects.

To test H_W itself, one would additionally need warranted incidence evidence: which particular content-occurrences were together. Joint decoding or a tuple of reports cannot supply that by definition. Thus this resource is a useful first constraint on the content/access/report separation, not a complete experiment on Gamma.

## 6. Source discrepancies that prevent premature timing claims

Two documentation issues were observed:

- The overview asks for responses as quickly and accurately as possible, while the detailed procedure describes a non-speeded response.
- The trigger description mentions 50-ms spacing, while its headings describe successive two-frame offsets. Timing cannot be reconstructed by treating those descriptions as equivalent without checking actual modality records and acquisition specifications.

These are source-documentation discrepancies, not demonstrated faults in the experiment or analyses. They prevent claiming a clean latency result from documentation alone. Actual timestamps and modality-specific records would take precedence after validation.

## 8. Current decision on H_W

**Retain it as a local candidate to investigate, not as a selected complete law.** Its appeal is that it connects actual content availability with recurrent control while distinguishing passive copies and report channels. Its unresolved liabilities are physical workspace selection, actual temporal delimitation, and independent phenomenal incidence.

The earlier GWT preference survives as a mechanistic research preference. The current work does not establish empirical superiority over IIT or another consciousness theory.

The substantive next task is to obtain or identify independent evidence that can separate recurrent access from phenomenal incidence, while also specifying a physically intrinsic W and tau. A complete answer must determine these rather than accept them as unexplained inputs.

## 9. Progress and stopping rule

Progress here consists of a frozen local togetherness hypothesis, explicit boundary tests, and an independently sourced experimental target with provenance and limits. It is not a newly discovered Gamma law, qualitative identity, or human-data result.

No new synthetic model is added because it would not resolve the missing evidence or constitutive warrant. The established software count remains 1,149 tests across 43 folders. Source integrity checks are separate from that count.
