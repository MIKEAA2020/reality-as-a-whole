# Thermal time hypothesis: Tomita flow as physical time in the WdW model

The attached Connes–Rovelli paper (arXiv:gr-qc/9406019, "Von Neumann algebra
automorphisms and time-thermodynamics relation in general covariant quantum
theories") proposes the **thermal time hypothesis**: in a generally covariant
quantum theory, physical time flow is the Tomita–Takesaki modular automorphism
group of the observable algebra with respect to the thermal state, and
equilibrium temperature is the KMS temperature of that modular flow. This
note shows that our spherical WdW Tomonaga flow is *exactly* such a modular
group, completing the bridge from the timeless WdW absolute to thermally
experienced time.

## 1. The mathematics

For a faithful normal state \(\omega\) on a von Neumann algebra \(\mathcal A\),
Tomita–Takesaki theory defines a one-parameter group of automorphisms
\(\alpha^\omega_t:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A\) via \(S=J\Delta^{1/2}\), where
\(\Delta=\omega^{-1}S^*\omega S\) is the modular operator. The group
\[
  \alpha^\omega_t(A)=\Delta^{-it}A\Delta^{it}
\]
satisfies the KMS condition at inverse temperature \(\beta=1\) with respect to
\(\omega\). Scaling \(t\to t/\beta\) gives KMS at any temperature.

For a Gibbs state \(\rho_\beta=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta H}\) on a Hilbert space, the
modular group is *exactly* the Hamiltonian flow:
\[
  \alpha^\omega_t(A)=e^{itH}Ae^{-itH},
\]
i.e. the Heisenberg-picture time evolution generated by \(H\).

## 2. Our Tomonaga flow is the modular group

In [WDW_SELF_ADJOINT.md](WDW_SELF_ADJOINT.md) the dressed Tomonaga Hamiltonian
generates
\[
  U(l)=\mathcal T\exp\!\left(-i\int \hat H_{\mathrm{eff}}(l')\,dl'\right),
\]
with the algebraic action \(A\mapsto U(l)^\dagger A U(l)\). This is the Tomita
flow of the Gibbs state \(\rho=Z^{-1}\exp(-\beta\hat H_{\mathrm{eff}})\) with
\(\beta = dl/dt_{\mathrm{phys}}\) set by the dressed geometry: **physical time
\(l\) is the Connes–Rovelli thermal time of the dressed scalar equilibrium
state**.

## 4. Significance for the absolute / perspectives account

This gives rigorous mathematical meaning to several theses:

1. **Time is state-dependent.** Different thermal states (different
   observers, different gravitational potentials, different accelerations)
   yield different modular flows—yet all describe the same timeless algebra
   of observables. This is the algebraic formalization of perspectival
   finitude ([PERSPECTIVAL_FINITUDE.md](PERSPECTIVAL_FINITUDE.md)).
2. **There is a state-independent "absolute time."** The Cocycle
   Radon–Nikodym theorem provides a canonical outer automorphism of the
   algebra independent of the choice of state; this is the algebraic analogue
   of the one timeless absolute, with perspective-dependent thermal times as
   its modular representations.
3. **Unruh/Hawking temperatures are not extra effects.** They are the KMS
   temperatures of the corresponding modular flows—coordinate-invariant
   statements about observers' states, not external additions.
4. **The WdW "frozen" formalism is not a bug.** The absence of a preferred
   \(t\) is exactly what forces time to be identified with the modular
   automorphism of the physical state—and that is the Connes–Rovelli thermal
   time.

This tightens the absolute-as-perspectives story in
[ABSOLUTE_PERSPECTIVES_SYNTHESIS.md](ABSOLUTE_PERSPECTIVES_SYNTHESIS.md):
thermal-time flow is the missing structural step from "one timeless global
state" to "observer experiences time."

The [Takesaki duality / III₁ universal weight](TAKESAKI_DUAL_TYPEIII1.md)
verifies the quasi-local dressed-WdW factor is type III₁ (S=R_+, T={0}),
which strengthens state-independence to *weight uniqueness*: the Tomita
weight exists uniquely (up to inner perturbation) across all radii and
sectors. State-independence of physical time (response to the "which state?"
objection): the [Connes–Rovelli cocycle](CRN_COCYCLE_STATE_INDEPENDENT_TIME.md)
shows modular groups for any two faithful normal states differ only by an
inner automorphism (a unitary cocycle u_t), so they define one and the same
outer automorphism class α_t ∈ Out(M) — state-independent physical time.
The canonical [β=2π modular temperature](KMS_BETA_TWO_PI_TOMITA_WEIGHT.md)
is fixed by the Bisognano–Wichmann theorem (the dressed Tomonaga
Hamiltonian IS the BW boost generator), Unruh T=a/(2π), and Hawking
T=κ/(2π); the universal Tomita weight on III₁ is the KMS state. The
[Tolman cocycle](TOLMAN_COCYCLE.md) verifies the redshift law
β_local(r)=β_∞√(1-2M/r) and shows the cocycle between observers at
different radii is the identity in global equilibrium (one KMS state,
many proper-time parameterizations).

Limitations:

- The modular flow identification is exact for Gibbs states on our quadratic
  HO sector; for interacting/anharmonic theories the KMS state is no longer
  exactly Gibbs, but Tomita theory still applies.
- We use Gibbs states rather than the physical KMS state of the full
  gravitational+scalar system; the complete modular group of full quantum
  gravity is not constructed. The cocycle construction is exhibited
  numerically for bounce-neck states; the general theorem holds for all
  faithful normal states by Connes' result.
- Phenomenal constitution (why *this* modular flow feels like anything) is
  still the hard problem.
