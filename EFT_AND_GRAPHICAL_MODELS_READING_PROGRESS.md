## What the thesis adds so far

1. **Methods versus ontology.** Chapters 1–2 frame theories as situated, partly overlapping practices and argue for a constrained methodological realism. Their conclusion is an interpretation, not a proof that relational structures, experience, or any proposed subjects exist. Realism can be pursued without a prior guarantee of a unique fundamental theory; conversely, a successful effective description does not exclude one. RG robustness alone does not settle what refers to the world (chapter 2, especially §§2.2–2.4).
2. **Naturalness is not just one proposition.** Chapter 3 distinguishes sensitivity, symmetry protection and probability-based fine-tuning. A useful separation is whether predictions at a scale are insensitive to omitted high-energy *states* versus insensitive to changes in high-energy *laws or parameters*; Athanasiou discusses Franklin's distinction in §3.5.3. Treating empirically fixed low-energy parameters as usable is not a derivation of them, and successful prediction does not prove law-insensitivity. Neither a speculative UV completion nor EFT failure follows simply from the presence of an unnatural parameter.
3. **Reconstruction is not risky prediction.** In §3.3.1 the author questions some retrospective historical claims that naturalness drove discoveries. This motivates an explicit project rule: label post-hoc fit, previously published method, and prediction frozen before new observations separately. It does not establish that naturalness has no scientific value.
4. **Elegance and parsimony remain preferences, not evidence of truth by themselves.** The thesis's discussion of aesthetics (§3.4.3) offers a useful challenge to any attempt to derive existence or necessary plenitude from elegance. For the project, economy may rank otherwise viable proposals, but it cannot replace physical recovery, an experiential bridge, or a justified measure over subjects.
5. **Formal analogy is not physical identification.** Chapter 3 (§3.5.2) discusses Doreen Fraser on similarities and differences between statistical-mechanical and field-theoretic renormalization. The same caution applies to our fitted retinal pair couplings: probabilistic interactions, causal interactions, and phenomenal relations are different claims. A mathematical analogy or fit alone does not turn one into another.

The thesis also discusses the cosmological constant and limits to EFT in chapter 4 and revises its appraisal in chapters 4b–5. Those sections have only been partially inspected so far; their conclusions should not be represented as verified results. In particular, claims that EFT techniques fail for a cosmological problem are contested and should not be generalized to all EFTs of gravity or treated as a solution of the cosmological constant problem.

## A mathematical correction to a source example

On PDF page 176, printed page 164, equation (3.50) labels the triangular function `1−|s|` on `[-1,1]` as the cumulative distribution function of `Y=X1−X2` for uniform variables. Assuming independent uniform variables, this is instead the **density**. The correct cumulative distribution is `0` below `−1`, `(s+1)^2/2` on `[-1,0]`, `1−(1−s)^2/2` on `[0,1]`, and `1` above `1`. Thus `P(|Y|<ε)=2ε−ε²` for `0≤ε≤1`: a density maximized at zero does not make an arbitrarily narrow cancellation event probable. The page then describes small differences as atypical under an independent standard-normal example, whose density is also maximized at zero; the probability of a small interval depends on its width and the variance. This does not invalidate the broader point that a fine-tuning probability depends on its specified measure, but the example must not be copied as written. These are corrections to the displayed dissertation, visually checked, not attributed to Wells without checking his original work.

PDF page 165 (printed 153) also prints a rho-meson mass near `770 GeV`; [Particle Data Group tables](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2018/tables/rpp2018-sum-mesons.pdf) give about `775 MeV`. The page's named charged–neutral kaon mass ratio is also inconsistent with its stated `7×10⁻¹⁵`: the PDG table lists a mass difference near `3.934 MeV` relative to a charged mass near `493.677 MeV`, a magnitude around `0.008`. This may be a confusion with a different neutral-kaon splitting; the intended historical formula has not been independently reconstructed. These transcription/technical errors call for independently checking equations before relying on the thesis as a physics source.

## What the monograph adds so far

Its introduction and early background distinguish probabilistic factorization, inference and computational strategy. For our ten-cell exact-enumeration calculations, the state space has `2^10` configurations; this is feasible here but should not be casually extrapolated to large networks. Trees permit exact message passing, whereas generic loopy graphs need other methods and loopy message passing is not automatically exact or convergent (§§2.3–2.5). The distinction between pairwise factorization and a general triple factor in §2.4.2 matters: a fully connected ordinary three-node graph alone does not identify the order of interaction. These are established results, not new findings of this project. An undirected statistical factor is not, without additional assumptions or interventions, a causal connection.

The extended monograph's exponential-family duality, variational bounds, parameter estimation, and relaxations are worth reading against the actual analysis before proposing any scaling change. Existing exact enumeration remains preferable for the small groups already analyzed. No variational approximation, error bound, causal result, or new neuroscience claim has been established here.
