# Fixed analysis plan: first-person affect under intracranial stimulation

## Question

Apply the candidate's cross-scale quality comparison to actual first-person affect reports, and audit the original paper's frequency and charge details before drawing a conclusion. Source: Yih, Beam, Fox and Parvizi, [*Intensity of affective experience is modulated by magnitude of intracranial electrical stimulation in human orbitofrontal, cingulate and insular cortices*](https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsz015), and its [supplement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/6537947/bin/supplementary_data_nsz015.docx). Main text, table 3 and supplement text were inspected before fixing this plan; the intended first-person example and possible inconsistencies were already visible. This is retrospective, not blinded or preregistered.

## Fixed extraction and checks

Transcribe the 15 supplement table 2 paired frequency-comparison rows, including each side's stated frequency, duration, current, charge per pulse and reported charge per trial. Compute `charge per trial = stated charge per pulse × stated frequency × stated duration`, separately for each side, exactly with rational arithmetic. Report all mismatches, their number and factors. This is a check of the supplement's stated formula against its displayed numbers; the calculations rely on the stated units and settings, and do not independently determine delivered charge, implementation, tissue activation or accuracy of underlying data. Check the given per-pulse value against the supplied stimulation parameters only where the stated or inferred width is supported; do not assume undocumented pulse shape, rounding conventions or phase-counting rules. No selective claims about the clinical data.

Take the single main-paper/supplement example of one participant's reported smell distastefulness at four increasing frequencies (2,5,10,20 Hz; quoted values 10,20,40,60 percent) and audit exactly what the table says, including the last comparison's sham count and duration. Treat the four values as published verbal report summaries, not independent trial outcomes, calibrated affect magnitudes, or evidence that the same subjective sensation was perfectly isolated. The cases were selected retrospectively and EEG or trial data were not replayed. Explicitly acknowledge sham responses and possible order or report effects.

For a *strengthened and separately stated* two-level phenomenological law, ask what numeric fit error remains if each reported value must be one of two values under a fixed truthful readout, even with an oracle choosing the two values after seeing the four numbers and selecting whichever of the two values it prefers for each number. Enumerate all 16 assignments with exact rational arithmetic and compute minimax absolute error, minimum mean absolute error and minimum root mean square error. Compare with a four-level lookup and explain that this is in-sample arithmetic on the published quoted numbers, not statistical evidence, cross-validation, a calibrated error bound for subjective experience, or evidence that the q bit was measured. The original E-L pair rule is a binary **contrast only**, so it is not falsified as an intensity law; its whole-sector binary E-L+ intensity extension is only compared if one additionally specifies a real physical realization, a fixed mapping and accurate readout. Unmodeled report-channel variation can reproduce more than two numbers even with a binary latent quality, at the price of further assumptions.
