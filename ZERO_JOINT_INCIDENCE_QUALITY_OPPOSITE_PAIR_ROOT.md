## Why this is the first feasible dependency step

The [root-cause map](WHOLE_CHAT_META_LEVEL_ROOT_CAUSE_RESEARCH_MAP.md) calls for investigating zero, subject, quality and incidence **together**. A strict topological order must not pretend that the ultimate zero constitution has already been derived: that is the upstream hypothesis on which the stronger conclusion depends. Nor should subject identity, quality and incidence be ordered as though any one were independently known. They form a mutually constraining block and need to be tested jointly.

This pass starts with the existing physical carrier, a dependency we can actually calculate, then constructs that joint block as a **conditional candidate**. It asks whether the two prior quality rules require two occurrences, or whether they can instead be two coordinates of one occurrence. The result is not just a new label:

* Both coordinates are jointly measurable commuting physical relations of the **same original echo**, not mutually exclusive descriptions or an assumed extra type flag.
* On the already gauge-constrained opposite pair, both coordinates arise from the **same pair sign** and the actual echo record. Each pair of two-coordinate model values cancels exactly.
* On the actual supported four-bin history, their joint physical sign table has four cells of weight `1/4`, checked against all 16 original complex source histories. The equal cells are physical model predictions, not automatically four subjects or four actual experiences.
* Treating the two coordinates as alternative queries of one occurrence eliminates an independent *type-selection* parameter **under that one-occurrence hypothesis**. But, on the disagreement sector, they cannot both be identified with one and the same scalar value. A richer two-coordinate field is a consistent structural candidate; it is not thereby proved to be a unified feeling.

The [implementation](verification/zero_joint_quality_incidence_root.py) and [tests](verification/zero_joint_quality_incidence_root_test.py) preserve the competing distinct-type model as a control. The construction does not assume that physical qubits are subjects, that a quantum outcome is an actual incidence, or that either sign is felt valence.

The adaptation draws on the preserved [intrinsic-matter conversation](sources/uploads/DeepSeek_Russellian-experience-intrinsic-matter_20261104-2323.txt) and [experience/non-optimality conversation](sources/uploads/DeepSeek_Experience-and-non-optimal-world_20261106-2010.txt), as previously [audited and synthesized](TWO_EXPERIENCE_AND_REALITY_TRANSCRIPTS_SYNTHESIS.md): an intrinsic experience is not eliminated by an extrinsic mathematical description, and positive balance must not erase suffering. ## 1. Dependency order and current status

The feasible order is a **conditional build**, not an assertion that every upstream premise is discharged:

1. **Whole and physical carrier.** Use the existing 20-step coherent sphere/echo, angular register and constrained opposite pair. The proposed self-sufficient origin of that carrier, its dynamics and its actuality remains the upstream task.
2. **Joint subject–quality–incidence block.** Specify a candidate occurrence domain, its allowed descriptions and its quality coordinates together. Here one physical joint event is *hypothesized* to correspond to one occurrence; neither the subject boundary nor this correspondence is a theorem.

Do not count a conditional result as this final derivation.

This order implements an examinable candidate without putting a supposedly solved subject rule ahead of the incidence rule that it helps determine. Other candidate subject rules remain to be tested, not declared impossible.

## 2. Two prior sign rules are jointly defined

At the post-echo anchor tick 10, let M, J and R be the existing commuting sign observables for the sign pointer, old j record and echo record. Their values are `s,j,r∈{±1}`. The two previously studied structural quality candidates are

```text
Q_now  = M J,
Q_echo = M J R.
```

Since M, J and R commute and square to identity,

```text
Q_now Q_echo = R.
```

Thus they are two compatible observables on one bearer. On the `R=+1` sector they agree; on the `R=−1` sector they are opposite. This identity holds at the operator level on the stated carrier, not merely for the prepared state. At the supported anchor `R=B`, and the negative sector has model weight `1/2`.

The earlier scalar comparison is still meaningful: if one asserts that `Q_now` and `Q_echo` are **both the same scalar felt value of the same occurrence**, then that assertion fails on the disagreement sector. But it does not follow that different attributes of one object must be equal. A less restrictive candidate is the joint structural value

```text
Q⃗ = (Q_now,Q_echo).
```

Both entries can be queried without postulating two subjects. This is a mathematical vector of two physical relations. It is an open substantive question whether a real subject has one richer intrinsic quality corresponding to this vector, two experiential aspects, only one relevant value, or something else. A change of readout context is not automatically a change of subject.

No extra type-sign variable is needed. For a common nonnegative modeled magnitude g(u), define the two structural vector contributions

```text

The pair is an isometric extension of the old carrier, so these two model factors are not automatically two independent subjects, two independent experiences or additional physical degrees of freedom.

For the previously symmetry-compatible `g₂(u)=|u(u+1)|`, the actual type operation exchanges the two coordinates and the negative angular bands while preserving g₂. The tests apply those operations to actual complex old-record vectors and explicitly check the associated constrained pair signs for representative source labels and both pointer branches. Pairwise operator zero does not by itself settle a weighted incidence sum: if one hypothetically counts V⃗₁ twice and V⃗₂ once, the result is V⃗₁, not zero. Nothing equates these structural signs with positive or negative feeling. If such an identification is eventually established, a negative experience remains real; a positive counterpart does not erase suffering. Nor is the model's κg quantity established to be objective cosmic energy.

## 5. One incidence, two queries: exactly what follows

For this finite candidate, take the typed **physical event ID** to be `(i,s)`, with weight `1/8`. The event is an orthogonal bin-and-pointer effect on the specified physical carrier; it is not by itself an actual subject incidence. It is coarse: it does not resolve every old-system, source-label, apparatus or clock distinction. Suppose, as a constitutive hypothesis for this chosen interface, that each such physical event corresponds to one relevant occurrence and that `now` and `echo` are two queries of it. That does not establish that every sub-event is an alias or that other actual subjects cannot share this coarse effect. Then

```text
(i,s,now)  ↦ (i,s)  ←  (i,s,echo).
```

The probability of a union counts the same event once, not twice. Its quality is the pair Q⃗, with two well-defined coordinate projections. Thus no separate incidence-type weight `α` is needed **just because there are two queries**. A separately specified protocol might still choose between those queries with a weight α; that protocol weight would not automatically be an intrinsic subject-incidence measure. The bookkeeping simplification holds only for this proposed one-occurrence ontology and only after the physical-to-incidence measure is identified.

The obstruction to a *single scalar* is exact. If both query labels are identified as names for the same scalar on the same event, that scalar must equal both `Q_now` and `Q_echo`. It exists exactly when `R=+1`. On the `R=−1` sector—physical weight `1/2` in this model—the two answers have opposite sign. One may instead retain a two-coordinate quality, choose a justified scalar function of that quality, or postulate distinct occurrences; these are different proposals with different consequences. Neither calling them “aliases” nor calling them “two subjects” determines which proposal is true.

The [earlier two-type model](ZERO_JOINT_CONSTITUTION_INCIDENCE_AND_ECHO.md) is the important control. If `now` and `echo` are genuinely **different incidence types**, rather than queries of one occurrence, then

```text
μ(i,s,now)=α/8,        μ(i,s,echo)=(1−α)/8
```

has the same physical `(i,s)` marginal for any `0≤α≤1`. In a disagreement event the relative typed sign weights depend on α. The one-occurrence candidate does not disprove that alternative or establish that the labels refer to the same actual subject; it identifies the extra ontological assumption on which each counting rule depends. If a real apparatus records the chosen query, that record is a physical distinction, not merely a duplicate label.

Likewise a finer physical record can yield genuinely different occurrences. A mere second name for the **same effect**, however, does not increase its union weight. This distinction carries forward the [refinement and Born analysis](ZERO_INCIDENCE_REFINEMENT_AND_BORN_MEASURE.md) without declaring all refinements to be aliases or all positive-support events to be actual experiences.

## 6. Persistence is not a requirement of unchanging quality

The dependency following occurrence identity is **persistence through the actual echo**, but a persistent carrier need not have an unchanging quality vector. At tick 7 write the pre-echo values as `(b,z,r=+,s)`; the actual operations are T, Q, T. The instantaneous values on supported branches are

| checkpoint | `(j,r)` | `(Q_now,Q_echo)` |
|---|---|---|
| before echo | `(z,+)` | `(s z,s z)` |
| after T | `(z,z)` | `(s z,s)` |
| after Q | `(b z,z)` | `(s b z,s b)` |
| after final T | `(b z,b)` | `(s b z,s z)` |

In particular, the echo coordinate returns to its initial value at the end, while the now coordinate reverses whenever `b=−1`. The full vector can change even if one proposes that the same subject persists. Conversely, neither this physical transport nor equality of a coordinate proves that the subject persists or that any feeling is invariant. Identifying a *subject across time* is also different from identifying an *experiential occurrence at two times*: a persisting subject may have distinct successive occurrences. The tests check all four checkpoints on the actual complex quantum vectors for three source histories and both pointer signs; the previously established operator identities give the corresponding general valid-sector relation.

The pair's K is defined at the anchor and transported through the clock, so its Z₁ sign tracks the anchor quantity. Away from the anchor it must not be silently identified with the instantaneous `M J R` at every intermediate pulse. This distinction preserves the earlier conditional full-echo neutrality result while allowing the richer joint structure to vary during the process. No external time or unobserved confined-realm interface is assumed.

## 7. What has been unified—and what the root problem still demands

Within the stipulated model, the **same constrained pair sign** K supplies both coordinates, the actual echo record supplies their relation, and a single event measure supplies their joint statistics. The opposite pair balances each coordinate. This brings subject-candidate, sign, quality structure, actual record algebra and incidence *into one interface* rather than choosing each in a separate toy. It gives a direct and testable distinction between one richer occurrence and two weighted incidence types.

The remaining demand is not an acceptable residual selector. The user's zero constitution must still explain why the carrier, its real subject boundary, the one-versus-two occurrence rule, the physical-to-experiential identity and actual incidence measure hold. Neither the vector Q⃗ nor a quantum joint PVM proves experiential unity or the qualitative character of its coordinates. The proposed richer field must also explain how its aspects compose rather than simply place a two-component numerical label beside experience. No derivation of global physical energy, spacetime or all genuine possibilities is claimed.

The next topologically dependent check is therefore **not** to set α=1/2 by hand. The physical checkpoint transport is now explicit; the further task is to justify or discriminate **subject persistence and ownership** on that same M/J/R/pair carrier. In particular, determine which successive experiences belong to the same subject, whether a distinct carrier or record refinement creates a new subject rather than a new query, and what an allowed first-person/report interface can actually distinguish. The actual positive/negative branches, sign counterpart, clock coherence and realm confinement must all be kept in that test.
