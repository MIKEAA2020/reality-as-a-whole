# Project-wide coherence audit and constructive integration

**Objective:** develop the user's intended complete account: a self-sufficient, timeless absolute, intrinsically experiential, with one universal consciousness, essence–existence and possibility–actuality identities, and internally intelligible finite perspectives and physical reality. Bostrom's hypothesis is a point of departure, not a commitment to a literal simulator. This is the **specified target**, not a result established by the project.

**Scope and honesty:** [verification/project_inventory.py](verification/project_inventory.py) reads and hashes every root and `research/` Markdown document and reports local links and risk-word locations in [project_inventory.json](verification/project_inventory.json). It excludes user-supplied source transcripts and verification assets from the *authored-document count*; these are dependencies, not silently treated as authored theory. The program inspects file text mechanically, and I read a selection of central documents and critical interfaces in detail. **This is not a line-by-line expert verification of every claim in every document or all historical conversations**, and the inventory's keyword hits are neither errors nor a scientific correctness score. This limitation matters: some historical chains still contain unsupported assertions.

## 1. One account, not an additive collage

The project can be organized around a single concrete actuality while keeping **different explanatory tasks** distinct:

1. **Ultimate constitution:** why the absolute is actual, necessary, intrinsically experiential and self-sufficient, including the proposed essence–existence and possibility–actuality identities.
2. **Finite disclosure:** how genuinely distinct, locally limited experiences and their ownership occur within one universal consciousness without an extra independent selector or a created copy.
3. **Operational physical order:** how physical systems, effects, state assignments, interventions and persistent records arise or are represented; what can be measured and compared.
4. **Internal time and spacetime:** how correlations, clock readings, causal order and effective metric/field dynamics are obtained in relevant domains without an external clock for the whole.
5. **Value and evidential access:** how positive fulfillment and real local suffering are understood, and how independent physical and phenomenal evidence constrains proposed identifications.

These are **explanatory interfaces**, not five independent substances or an external causal production sequence. None should be declared derived merely because it appears in the same diagram. The whole is timeless in the intended account; internal histories and local succession are not thereby denied or trivially derived.

## 3. First constructive bridge and its limit

[TURNING_ENTROPY_RECORD_BRIDGE.md](TURNING_ENTROPY_RECORD_BRIDGE.md) explicitly maps a cosine turning clock to a Barontini-form path functional for the chosen toy relation `S=S₀+aq`. It shows that two visits with identical reading and orientation can have accumulated clock readings separated by `4a`. A finite decoder that must resolve N+1 repeated cycles to error `<2a` needs at least N+1 distinguishable classical labels under its stated assumptions. It also relates a normalized classical recollapsing Einstein–scalar trajectory to an analogous two-branch path, without asserting quantum or experimental equivalence.

This makes a useful missing interface explicit: **an ordered internal path is not automatically a single-valued observable of an instantaneous recurring configuration; a record or restriction of the target may be needed.** It does not establish that Barontini's atomic experiment has this toy entropy law, that time is entropy, that the record exists autonomously, or that such a record constitutes awareness. The counting and path mathematics are elementary; novelty has not been established.

## 4. Critical contradictions that must not propagate

[WDW_INTERFACE_CORRECTIONS.md](WDW_INTERFACE_CORRECTIONS.md) documents an independently checked error at a real document boundary:

- `x⁴` is **not** operator-bounded relative to the ordinary harmonic oscillator H₀. Its number-state expectation grows quadratically while H₀ grows linearly. The historical Kato–Rellich argument and claim of ordinary perturbation-series convergence are invalid.
- The stated oscillator matrix element `<2|x⁴|0>` was half the correct value.
- The exact commuting Hamiltonian `ω(l)(N+1/2)` is a valid **stipulated model**, but the earlier operator `p²+ω(l)²x²` generally does not commute across distinct frequencies. A square-root oscillator likewise has different spectral spacing. The claimed exact links between these Hamiltonians were not established.
- Stone's theorem for a fixed self-adjoint generator does not alone establish a propagator for an arbitrary time-dependent family.

These corrections do not prove all harmonic or quartic models inconsistent, and standard positive quartic Hamiltonians can be treated by other methods. But downstream quantitative gravitational, horizon/bounce and phenomenal claims cannot inherit the older chain without a fresh derivation.

Other invalid promotions are already retracted in [ZERO_RESIDUE_CLAIM_CORRECTION.md](ZERO_RESIDUE_CLAIM_CORRECTION.md) and [TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md](TIME_SOLUTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md): mathematical representation → concrete actualization; finite spectra → type III₁; cocycle equivalence → unique physical time; a laboratory analogue → Einstein quantum gravity; and code assigning `True` → proof. The project-level README and main programme previously repeated some such historical assertions; their warnings now point to these audits. Further individual claims may still contain errors.

## 5. An interface contract for future integration

For every proposed connection, record:

1. **Objects and domain:** physical state/algebra, constraint, preparation, available records, clock instruments, effects, and allowed interventions, or the corresponding experiential target.
2. **Map and preserved content:** exact unitary/isomorphism, coarse-graining channel, approximation, asymptotic limit or analogy; specify which probabilities, causal relations and distinctions survive, and which are lost.
3. **Assumptions and resources:** selected state, clock, orientation, normalization/rigging, coarse-graining, boundary data, energy/memory, and access to records. Do not import a laboratory time into the absolute as a fundamental clock.
4. **Proof and evidence:** theorem with domains and quantified errors, numerical check of a declared model, experimental result with acquisition/uncertainty, or philosophical hypothesis. Mark which it is.
5. **Failure test:** a rival state, turning point, intervention, representation change, finite-resolution case, singular limit or alternative qualitative assignment that would expose an invalid identification.
6. **Prior art:** cite the closest established derivations; assess whether a proposed contribution is already present before claiming novelty.

In particular, two clock descriptions must transform the **same preparation, physical events, instruments and conditioning**. Mere equality of selected formulas, one-time marginal densities, or compatible metaphors does not establish operational equivalence.

For a cross-project prior-art trail, candidate interface and differences in metaphysical commitments, see [CLUES_CROSS_PROJECT_ASSESSMENT.md](CLUES_CROSS_PROJECT_ASSESSMENT.md). Its assessment is a selective triage, not independent verification of the linked manuscripts.

## 6. Ordered research gates

### Gate A — repair and audit the physics dependency chain

Re-derive one chosen constraint and its physical inner product without replacing the square-root dynamics by an ordinary oscillator by assertion. State domains and any self-adjoint extension; prove or bound the reduced dynamics under time-dependent conditions. Perform independent analytical and numerical checks. Until then, discard dependent claims that need those invalid steps. Audit the other research reports, including classical-to-quantum and continuum claims, with the same discipline.

### Gate B — turn the common bridge into an explicit recorded experiment

[FINITE_TURNING_COUNTER_INSTRUMENT.md](FINITE_TURNING_COUNTER_INSTRUMENT.md) supplies a first finite history, prepared counter, overflow flag, complete erasure instrument and backaction witness, and checks same-event probabilities in two vertex-conditioned frames. [INFINITE_WINDING_COUNTER.md](INFINITE_WINDING_COUNTER.md) implements an ideal bilateral infinite lift and quantifies offset uncertainty between two reductions. [FINITE_WINDING_READOUT.md](FINITE_WINDING_READOUT.md) follows with a finite open-path counter and noisy instrument, recovering the ideal result on an interior window and quantifying a specified readout bias. These models supply an unwrapped clock and prescribed dynamics; none is an autonomous origin of temporal order. Extend them to a physically motivated, possibly interacting clock/accumulator with explicit joint outcomes and no assumed independent origin of order. Include counter overflow, record loss and failure branches. Compare against prior work on quantum reference frames, clock instruments and [record-based internal time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25307); no novelty is assumed.

### Gate C — confront the measured internal clock

Use the Barontini construction as a **published experiment**, not a proof of a preferred cosmic time. Design a prespecified uncertainty-aware comparison of the same observed events using τ, clock branches and record-augmented alternatives. State that released processed traces do not reconstruct the entropy, and obtain or identify necessary independent data before treating any held-out analysis as a prediction. The expanding BEC data are a separate experimental setting; do not combine them as if they were the same apparatus.

### Gate D — one gravity model, then harder constraints

Promote the classical recollapse interface only after specifying its quantum physical Hilbert space, actual clock POVM, record instrument, detector stress energy and matched probabilities through the turn. Then test an inhomogeneous gravitational model with the appropriate local constraints, refoliation/observable questions and semiclassical limit. Alternative null-boundary/anomaly programs may modify these requirements, but their constraints and consistency must likewise be explicit. No fixed universal internal clock is required.

### Gate E — connect physical time to finite experience

Give the physical–phenomenal candidate a precise target for content, unity, local ownership and temporal incidence. Test against record/source dissociations, representational changes, temporal-translation symmetry and independently anchored phenomenal evidence. Neither an accumulated clock nor an interval of data is automatically an experience. A successful physical account of ordering and a warranted constitution of experienced succession are separate necessary achievements of the overarching programme.

### Gate F — discharge ultimate explanatory obligations

Give non-circular arguments for the user's identities and the actuality/nature of the absolute, and show how the same constitution determines (rather than merely accommodates) the physical, phenomenal, modal and value structure. An experiment on cold atoms cannot prove these identities, but a developed account cannot ignore what well-supported experiments show. Full zero residue remains unproved until these connections are supplied.

## 7. Current verdict

The project's strongest current contribution is a **collection of constrained models, audit methods, experimental comparisons and a coherent research target**, with one explicit bounded turning/path/record bridge added here. These parts genuinely constrain one another when their interfaces are declared. They do not yet compose into a decisive solution of the problem of time or a demonstrated completely intelligible account of reality. The best next advance is to build and challenge the finite recorded clock of Gate B while repairing Gate A; success would be meaningful but explicitly model-limited, not automatic zero-residue closure.
