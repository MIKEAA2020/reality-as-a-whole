# A finite-window localized response in the gravity–identity candidate

## Result

The previous [localized identity interface](ZERO_LOCALIZED_RELATIONAL_SUBJECT_IDENTITY_CANDIDATE.md) derived an immediate relational acceleration difference for opposite signals. This continuation asks a more demanding question: **does a localized response remain distinguishable after the coupling is switched off?**

In the same declared gravity–clock–rod–signal–detector action, I numerically evolve the leading-order signal-dependent detector response with a smooth coupling localized in **all three rod directions** and switched off after a finite interval. On the exact gravitating clock-and-rod background, two signal signs give opposite leading response differences at the sampled clock times `T=0.4, 0.6, 0.8`; the pulse is off after `T=0.4`. The selected local point and a local weighted average both distinguish the alternatives at those samples.

This is a **finite-window physical response, not a durable memory**: the point response continues to change after switch-off. Nor is it a measured outcome, a constructed sign-reference apparatus, an actual subject, or a felt quality. It is computed at first order in both signal/detector amplitude and coupling, with numerical resolution checks—not by evolving the full nonlinear Einstein–matter system. The exact compact Hamiltonian constraint belongs to the underlying model; it is not shown to be the actual universe's total energy. [Code and tests](verification/zero_localized_gravity_finite_response_test.py).

## 1. Why this is the right next physical question

The user asks for a self-sufficient constitution in which zero, physical organization and experience belong together necessarily, with experience as an **intrinsic identity**, not an extra substance generated by physical activity. The previous pass made a local candidate interaction part of the same gravity–matter action. But instantaneous responsiveness is not the same as retaining a distinction through an interval. An identity candidate involving an organized subject and its access must ultimately specify which distinctions persist in its own physical organization and at what grain.

That does **not** mean an experience requires an external observer, a verbal report, or permanent storage. It means that if a proposed physical organization claims to supply access to a distinction at a later relational event, this claim must be physically demonstrated rather than assumed. A modeled response remains physical evidence about the model; it does not create or prove experience.

## 2. The same underlying action, now with a 3D-local pulse

Use the full action and compact three-torus initial-data framework stated in [the preceding physical interface](ZERO_LOCALIZED_RELATIONAL_SUBJECT_IDENTITY_CANDIDATE.md) and [its original derivation](RELATIONAL_MATTER_DETECTOR_GRAVITY.md). It contains Einstein gravity, scalar clock T, circle-valued rods `θ¹,θ²,θ³`, signal Φ and detector χ, with interaction `−λW(T,θ)Φχ`. The field and clock/rod stresses enter the same constraints.

At the small-field background `ε=0`, take the **expanding** branch and the reference parameters of [the earlier finite-window analysis](RELATIONAL_SIGNAL_GEOMETRY_NO_GO.md):

```text
f=1/2, τ=3/2, a(0)=1,
da/dT = a³/τ √(f²/4 + τ²/(12a⁴)),
θⁱ=xⁱ mod 2π,       dT/dt = τ/a³.
```

The clock and rods gravitate even at ε=0; this is not a Minkowski background. This expanding branch has `α₀=−1`, unlike the `α₀=+1` initial-acceleration example in the immediately preceding pass. The same implicit-function argument works on either branch because `α₀≠0`. Thus we are using the **same action and initial-data ansatz**, not claiming to have numerically evolved that previous contracting-branch example.

Choose a smooth spatial bump of half-width `w=1.4<π` on each angular rod coordinate:

```text
b_w(x) = exp[1−1/(1−(x/w)²)]   for |x|<w,
         0                         otherwise,
B(θ)   = b_w(θ¹)b_w(θ²)b_w(θ³).
```

Angular arguments are represented in `[-π,π)`; B is smooth and supported in a finite three-dimensional rod patch. Let

```text
F(T) = exp[−1/(s(1−s))]/I   for s=T/0.4 in (0,1),
       0                      otherwise,
I    = ∫₀¹ exp[−1/(s(1−s))] ds,
W(T,θ)=F(T)B(θ).
```

F and all its derivatives vanish at the switching endpoints. The coupling is zero outside `0<T<0.4`, but the signal field itself is not localized: its initial profile is proportional to `cos θ³`. W is a **chosen, physically covariant profile**, not a detector self-control law or a derived subject boundary. Clock, rods, background solution and this profile remain assumptions.

## 3. The perturbative physical prediction

Consider the same-sign-prepared detector comparison of the previous pass:

```text
Φ_s(0,z)=s ε b cos z,   ∂T Φ_s(0,z)=0,       s=±1,
χ_+(0,z)=χ_−(0,z)=0,
Pχ,+(0,z)=Pχ,−(0,z)=ε p(1+cos z),
b=2, p=1.
```

The original constraint-compatible initial data include an inhomogeneous conformal factor and extrinsic curvature; they agree for the two signs. For sufficiently small ε the earlier implicit-function argument gives exact constrained data, but it does not provide a quantitative radius or a nonlinear solution here.

The computation below is **leading order in ε and λ** on the exact ε=0 background. Put `Φ_s=s ε b u(T) cos z + …`, where

```text
u'' + [a(T)⁴/τ²] u = 0,       u(0)=1, u'(0)=0.
```

For the half-difference of the two detector fields, define

```text
D(T,θ) = [χ_+(T,θ) − χ_−(T,θ)]/(2 ε λ)
```

at nonzero ε and λ, interpreted at **leading order**. It solves

```text
D'' = (a⁴/τ²) ∇²_θ D − (a⁶/τ²) F(T) B(θ) b u(T) cos θ³,
D(0,θ)=0,       D'(0,θ)=0.
```

The equality of initial detector preparations makes their common free evolution cancel in the half-difference. It does **not** mean the individual detector fields start with zero momentum. The O(λ) differential response is independent of p in this linearized comparison; the common detector trajectory is still physically present. At higher orders signal–detector feedback, the quartic interaction and inhomogeneous gravitational/reference backreaction matter. Within the linear-amplitude coupled equations the correction to the sign-difference response begins at higher odd powers of λ; the present calculation does not quantify it. We also have no bound on finite-ε nonlinear corrections.

The field equations and the stated perturbative reduction are those of the cited model, not a new fundamental law derived from zero. A finite Fourier discretization solves the displayed leading equation. As in the source derivation, the rod chart is only assumed valid over the chosen patch and window; this approximation does not establish that a complete nonlinear clock–rod map remains invertible there.

## 4. What the calculation actually finds

Use a periodic spectral discretization with `32³` grid/mode points, `800` RK4 steps from T=0 to 0.8, and a temporal pulse normalized as above. Measure both the point value `D(T,0,0,0)` and the normalized local weighted average

```text
D_B(T) = ∫ B(θ)D(T,θ)d³θ / ∫ B(θ)d³θ.
```

This is a **local averaging prescription**, not a physically built averaging detector. The result is:

| Internal clock T | scale factor a | point `D(T,0)` | local weighted `D_B(T)` | point slope `∂T D(T,0)` |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.4 (switch-off) | 1.148786 | −0.101123 | −0.048559 | −0.501830 |
| 0.6 | 1.237917 | −0.191763 | −0.091781 | −0.387641 |
| 0.8 | 1.341012 | −0.247825 | −0.119531 | −0.144299 |

These are dimensionless model values for D, **not** individual detector readings. The leading prediction for the actual two-preparation difference is `χ_+−χ_− ≈ 2 ε λ D`; it includes neither finite-coupling correction nor quantified error from nonlinear gravity. For illustration only, with `ε=λ=0.01`, the leading point difference at T=0.4 is about `−0.00002022`. Those parameter choices are not a physical calibration or a proven error-controlled approximation.

The coupling has been off since T=0.4, but the detector field continues to evolve. Its point slope is plainly nonzero at all three samples. Thus these values show a **temporarily retained, evolving physical distinction**, not that a robust stable memory has been constructed. The three sampled values do not prove that the sign remains unchanged continuously on the whole interval; local smoothness does ensure a sufficiently short nonzero neighborhood around each nonzero sample within the numerical model.

### Resolution and controls

- At T=0.8, `24³`/600 steps gives point `−0.2476996` and local average `−0.1198321`; `32³`/800 gives `−0.2478253` and `−0.1195310`; an additional `40³`/1000 run gives `−0.2476677` and `−0.1197453`. These are empirical discretization checks, not certified continuum error bounds. Spatial convergence is not perfectly monotonic for the smooth bump.
- For `24³`, doubling the time steps from 600 to 1200 changes the displayed point values by less than `10⁻⁹`. This is a time-step comparison at fixed spatial resolution, not an independent nonlinear GR calculation.
- Zero λ gives exactly zero D in the numerical construction; reversing λ reverses the leading D. W vanishes outside its declared spacetime support, but the detector's free field can propagate and evolve afterward. Coupling localization does not imply the detector field is forever confined to the patch.
- The code checks the compact spatial support of B and its reflection symmetry. The Fourier representation is finite-band and so approximates, rather than exactly preserves, the smooth bump's compact support.

The [tests](verification/zero_localized_gravity_finite_response_test.py) calculate the first three resolutions and the fixed-spatial-grid time refinement. The `40³` check is independently reproducible by calling `run(40,1000)` in that module.

## 5. What is and is not an accessible distinction

At leading order, opposite signs give nonzero opposite contributions relative to the common detector baseline. **Access to that contrast still needs an operational reference.** An observer using a local field reading must know or physically encode the relevant baseline and orientation; subtracting two calculated solutions is not itself a device in either solution. The sign calibration, its dynamics and gravitational stress are not built here.

A useful conditional discrimination statement is possible. If a calibrated device had a known local baseline and its total absolute reading error were strictly less than `ε |λ D_B(T)|` at a given sample, then the two leading predictions, separated by `2ε|λD_B(T)|`, would lie on opposite sides of the baseline. That is an algebraic margin **assuming** the baseline, calibration, perturbative error and instrument error are all bounded accordingly. No such bounds, probability of error or actual device have been established.

The sign-reference issue also illustrates why physical properties must be recovered at the right descriptive grain. In the separate `p=0` sign-symmetric preparation, the exact full-action transformation `(Φ,χ)→(−Φ,−χ)` gives opposite χ but identical geometry and references, as shown in [the earlier no-go](RELATIONAL_SIGNAL_GEOMETRY_NO_GO.md). A readout retaining only χ² cannot distinguish those opposite χ values. This is not a claim that **all** physical information or all experiential differences disappear: it is a loss under that particular coarse-graining. Our current `p=1` comparison is not that global symmetry pair; its common baseline generally prevents assuming equal χ² or identical later geometries.

## 6. Relevance to intrinsic experience

The calculation brings a localized, clock-relative physical distinction further into a single constrained gravity–matter model. It does not make D, a field sign or a memory bit into experience. A viable intrinsic-identity candidate would need to specify a genuinely organized occurrence, a subject boundary and a directly anchored qualitative contrast, and show how the same occurrence is accessible both through its physical relations and through the subject's lived character. No extra mental object or external force is required by that goal; neither is the correspondence selected by the present PDE.

Nor should the global zero be confused with loss of local actuality. The constraint's vanishing does not cancel the detector difference, or the reality or moral importance of a subject's suffering. But the compact canonical generator has not been established as actual whole-universe energy, and no experiential balance follows from it. The user's structural–experiential understanding remains the target: structural equations can illuminate the relations while direct acquaintance is not replaced by the symbols; both would concern the same intrinsic occurrence in a successful theory.

## 7. Next gate

A genuine next step is an **autonomous, calibrated finite record**: give the sign reference and localized memory their own physical fields, initial data and couplings; show readout separation and bounded error over a declared interval; and include their contribution to the gravitational constraints and reference-field dynamics. The current transient response is useful input, not a record by definition. In parallel, formulate a subject-boundary and experiential-incidence criterion independently motivated by the actual qualitative organization—not by fitting the desired answer to the field signal.

The [physical sign-reference continuation](ZERO_GRAVITY_LOCAL_PHYSICAL_SIGN_REFERENCE.md) adds a dynamical reference field, its coupled gravitational initial data and a reference-relative finite-window composite. Its numerical dynamics remain linearized and drifting; no autonomous record or subject is established.

Ultimately the action, profile, physical global energy and actual experiential constitution must follow from the self-sufficient nature of the whole. Nothing in this numerical exercise supplies that derivation, and no external causal creator is proposed.

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=verification:. \
 python -m unittest verification.zero_localized_gravity_finite_response_test -v
```

The test runs 16³, 24³ and 32³ spectral grids, a time-refinement comparison, and zero/reversed-coupling controls.
