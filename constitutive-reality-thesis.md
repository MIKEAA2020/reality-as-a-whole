# Concrete constitution and reality as a whole

## Consolidated surviving thesis

> Seek a concrete constitution of reality whose nature explains its public physical organization, phenomenal character, and bounded perspectives without an external executor or an unexplained bridge between independently existing mental and physical events. Conditional mathematical constructions show how specified interaction structure yields stable macro-organization, how specified operational principles constrain quantum state-space types, and how specified probabilistic distinctions induce geometry. These are genuine explanatory dependencies. The decisive unfinished task is to justify why the concrete constitution has those properties, why it actually obtains, and how its organization constitutes particular experiences and subjects. The zero-residue goal remains intact: neither its achievement nor its impossibility has been established.

This is stronger than a simulation narrative and more disciplined than a declaration that reality is self-explanatory. Its achievement is **a partially constructed dependency architecture**, not an already completed ontology or a proven optimum among all metaphysical theories.

## 1. The target and the candidate must remain distinct

The governing objective is complete intelligibility of reality as a whole. Minimal departure from a starting theory, simplicity, and explanatory economy are subordinate comparison criteria—not permissions to accept an unexplained remainder as complete.

The candidate is **immanent concrete constitution**. “Concrete” means actually occurring, not necessarily material. “Immanent” means that no existing executor, observer, interpreter, or ground is excluded from total reality. “Constitution” asks what the occurrence is and how its features follow from its nature, rather than assuming an earlier efficient cause is the only possible explanation.

This removes an unnecessary demand that reality require another machine to execute it. It does not establish actuality, necessity, qualitative character, or explanatory completeness. Likewise, a single total reality does not entail a single cosmic subject, one fundamental substance, or a unique possible world.

The strongest experiential version takes public physical roles and phenomenal character to belong to the same occurrence. That is a constitutive identity proposal, not a conclusion derived from the words “concrete” or “presentation.” It must earn the identification.

## 2. The explanatory architecture

Let \(C\) denote the proposed complete constitution, not merely an abstract structure given that name. The project seeks justified dependencies of the following kind:

\[
C\longrightarrow\Pi,\qquad C\longrightarrow\Sigma,
\qquad (C,s)\longrightarrow Q_s.
\]

Here \(\Pi\) is public physical organization, \(\Sigma\) specifies subjects or episodes, and \(Q_s\) characterizes episode \(s\).

A fourth obligation is not represented by those arrows: explain why such concrete constitution obtains at all. Merely quantifying over a hypothetical \(C\) does not supply it.

The arrows are research obligations. They cannot be implemented as arbitrary lookup tables and counted as explanations. Nor does calling \(Q_s\) “intrinsic” establish that it is uniquely fixed, or calling a subsystem “integrated” establish that it is a subject.

The useful methodological rule is:

> A gap closes only through a justified derivation, a defended constitutive identity, or an argued dissolution of a genuinely misplaced question—not through inclusion, renaming, or successful notation.

## 3. Actuality, necessity, and grounding

### Actual occurrence is not necessary occurrence

Experience supplies evidence that something occurs. It does not alone establish that concrete reality exists in every metaphysically possible situation.

Similarly, an abstract characterization that contains no instantiation commitment can leave concrete instantiation open. The empty-concrete interpretation is then a model-theoretic demonstration of what that particular theory fails to entail—not a proof that an empty reality is metaphysically possible under every adequate ontology.

A successful necessary-existence argument must justify the principle excluding absence rather than insert necessary nonemptiness as a disguised premise. Abstract or essential explanation need not be categorically disallowed; it must establish actual explanatory sufficiency.

### The rooting argument is conditional

Suppose contingent existential facts require existentially committing support, and an actual relevant support line has a root. If the root were contingent and covered by that backing principle, it would require preceding support and would not be a root. Under a suitable modal classification, that supports a necessary existential root.

The inference can be valid without S5. The substantive questions remain whether the support principle applies as stated, whether the support is existentially committing, and whether a root exists. It does not independently establish a unique necessary individual or the necessity of every actual detail.

### The stronger grounding incompatibility also survives conditionally

Assume actual contingent existential facts, full necessitating grounds for every such fact, and strict well-foundedness of the relevant support relation. If every full ground belongs to the relevant existential domain, a necessary full ground would necessitate its consequence; hence a contingent consequence must have contingent full support. Every contingent member then has a preceding contingent member, conflicting with well-foundedness.

The domain qualification matters. A full ground may be plural or conjunctive. The proof must include those grounds in the domain rather than silently treating each explanation as a single fact of the same kind.

This exposes an incompatibility between specific assumptions. It does not show that every legitimate explanation necessitates, that all explanatory relations are well-founded, or that maximal intelligibility is impossible.

### Rootless does not mean mathematically undetermined

For a sequence indexed by nonnegative integers satisfying

\[
x_n=(1+x_{n+1})/2,\qquad 0\leq x_n\leq1,
\]

iteration gives

\[
1-x_n=2^{-k}(1-x_{n+k}),
\]

forcing \(x_n=1\). Thus an endless constraint network can uniquely determine its values. Without boundedness, \(x_n=1+c2^n\) gives further solutions.

This is a concrete reason not to dismiss every rootless account as vacuous circularity. It does not explain why this network is actual or why these constraints have authority. A finite foundational account faces corresponding explanatory questions about its own principles.

### Necessitarianism has not been refuted by formal alternatives

The inference

\[
\Box F\land\Box(F\rightarrow p)\Rightarrow\Box p
\]

is valid in the relevant normal modal setting. A proposed necessary ground needs a necessary sufficient connection to produce necessary consequences.

Formal consistency of classical models, alternative probabilistic theories, or an empty-concrete extension does not refute that inference or establish their metaphysical possibility. It shows that weaker premises do not exclude them. Conversely, a preferred modal model does not establish that its necessity claims are true.

The global branches remain open: necessary determinate constitution, a sufficiently justified non-well-founded account, principled plenitude, or an adequately defended nonnecessitating explanation or dissolution. Their explanatory adequacy must be assessed, not decided by a trilemma slogan.

## 4. Stable parts and macro-laws: a genuine construction

A connected four-component diffusion system can have strong internal coupling within pairs and weaker cross-pair coupling \(\varepsilon\). For the supplied architecture, the relaxation rates are

\[
2+2\varepsilon\quad\text{and}\quad4\varepsilon.
\]

For small positive \(\varepsilon\), this distinguishes fast internal relaxation from slow between-module change. At \(\varepsilon=0.01\), the corresponding timescales are approximately \(0.495\) and \(25\).

For pair totals \(S_A,S_B\), the aggregate laws are

\[
\dot S_A=2\varepsilon(S_B-S_A),\qquad
\dot S_B=2\varepsilon(S_A-S_B).
\]

An intertwining identity \(PL=L_MP\) makes the aggregation valid for all microscopic initial states, not just a selected trajectory.

This is meaningful explanatory compression: stable organization and autonomous macro-evolution can follow from given microscopic dynamics without an external observer deciding the dynamics after the fact. The physical significance of the variables, coupling, subsystem choice, and timescale still requires justification. The exact partition is not uniquely privileged for every coupling value.

These modules are **not thereby minds**. Persistence, mutual influence, approximate autonomy, and integration constrain candidate subject boundaries; they do not establish phenomenal unity.

## 5. Interventions, causal integration, and subject boundaries

The prototype integration work supports several useful requirements:

- Shared input is not the same as mutual causal influence.
- A causal account needs meaningful intervention structure as well as observational transition probabilities.
- A passive coordinate transformation must transport that intervention structure, rather than replace physical interventions with coordinate-local substitutes.
- A scalar whose maxima tie does not automatically determine a unique subject boundary.

The prototype’s ties diagnose inadequacy of that selection rule. They do not establish that experience disappears, that no causal theory of subjecthood can work, or that all integration measures fail.

A proposed co-consciousness equivalence relation would determine classes if it were reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. The real task is to justify that relation and its applicability. The mathematics of equivalence classes does not establish that phenomenal unity obeys the assumed relation or fix its temporal grain.

A future constitutive account should predict how subject boundaries behave under changes in coupling, noise, memory, and temporal integration. Appending an arbitrary tie-breaker produces an output, not an explanation of why that output is phenomenal.

## 6. Geometry: retain the dependency, not the identification

### Statistical differentiation

For specified response probabilities, Fisher information supplies a local distinguishability geometry. For a Bernoulli variable,

\[
ds^2=dp^2/[p(1-p)],\qquad u=2\arcsin\sqrt p,
\]

so \(ds^2=du^2\). Symmetric noise contracts the relevant statistical distinguishability.

This is a derived geometry of the stipulated probabilistic model. Identifying it with experienced similarity requires independent evidence. Noise can impair discrimination without making the phenomenal qualities themselves closer. A successful bridge must distinguish those possibilities rather than accommodate every outcome retrospectively.

### Spacetime geometry

Under appropriate causal, dimensional, regularity, and continuum hypotheses, chronological structure can determine a conformal metric class. An appropriate volume measure can then fix the scale: if \(g=\Omega^2\bar g\) in dimension \(d\), then

\[
dV_g=\Omega^d dV_{\bar g}.
\]

This is a conditional reconstruction, not a derivation of causal order, volume, dimension, Einstein dynamics, or their actuality.

Likewise, the qubit identity

\[
\det(tI+xX+yY+zZ)=t^2-x^2-y^2-z^2
\]

exhibits Lorentz-cone mathematics. It does not identify qubit coefficients with spacetime events. Proper time along a specified worldline is invariant; metric components transform; causal structure alone generally does not fix conformal scale. None of these geometric facts establishes metaphysical necessity or phenomenal constitution.

## 7. Conditional quantum reconstruction is substantive progress

The most developed constructive claim is that operationally interpretable assumptions can constrain quantum state-space types without simply declaring a complex Hilbert space at the outset.

The candidate dependency is

\[
\text{finite convex operational framework}
+\mathrm{SP}+\mathrm{FH}
+\begin{cases}
\mathrm{EO},\\
\text{specified composition constraints}
\end{cases}
\Longrightarrow\text{complex quantum state-space types}.
\]

Here spectrality concerns decompositions into jointly perfectly distinguishable pure states; frame homogeneity concerns active reversible transformations between frames, not mere relabeling. Energy observability concerns an appropriate injective conserved-observable assignment to generators. The composition branch includes local tomography, capacity multiplication, product preparations, and the stated reversibility conditions.

The transcript attributes the general reconstruction results to published work. Their precise theorem hypotheses have **not** been independently source-checked here. In particular, the actual transformation group, effects, centered observables, finite-dimensional scope, and composite assumptions must be matched exactly before claiming a theorem applies. Finite calculations cannot prove the general classification.

Several algebraic dependencies can nevertheless be checked directly. In the complex Hermitian setting,

\[
a\circ b=(ab+ba)/2,\qquad \psi_a(b)=i[a,b]/2
\]

satisfy

\[
ab=a\circ b-i\psi_a(b).
\]

Normalized positive-linear states yield trace-form probabilities, while appropriate continuous reversible dynamics yield commutator evolution. Those statements do not derive probability from nothing, select the actual Hamiltonian, or settle outcomes.

Covariance also requires specifying the admitted symmetry group: the usual commutator correspondence behaves differently under complex conjugation than under connected unitary transformations. “All reversible transformations” must not silently broaden the hypothesis.

The correct achievement is **conditional structural selection and compression**. The decisive missing connection is still

\[
\text{concrete constitutive nature}
\overset{?}{\Longrightarrow}
\text{operational reconstruction premises}.
\]

Recovering familiar mathematics is evidence that a package is useful. It does not show that it follows from actuality, phenomenal unity, or the absence of an external executor.

## 8. Countermodels discipline the constitutive bridge

The operational countermodels have a coherent role: they prevent weak slogans from silently supplying strong axioms.

- Mixing pure states is weaker than spectrality.
- Pure-state transitivity is weaker than frame homogeneity.
- Observable motion need not carry the stipulated conserved affine label.
- Interaction and entanglement do not imply local tomography.
- Nonclassicality or no-broadcasting does not uniquely select complex quantum mechanics.

Each claim depends on its effect and transformation framework. For example, universal broadcasting is related to simplex structure under standard operational assumptions and an appropriate composite. A simplex with artificially restricted allowed channels need not physically admit every classical broadcasting operation. No-broadcasting is therefore not simply the *definition* of every non-simplex geometry independent of operational assumptions.

Phenomenal privacy is also not operational no-broadcasting. The latter concerns reproducing state statistics, not numerical duplication of an occurrence or sharing first-person acquaintance.

For contextuality, the Mermin–Peres square establishes an inconsistency of a specific noncontextual value assignment with quantum operator constraints. It does not follow merely from experiencing several contents together. The independent exact check confirms the six context products, the deterministic bound of four for the signed witness, and the quantum value six.

These checks restrict a proposed bridge. They do not disprove the experiential constitution candidate as such, nor establish complete metaphysical possibility for every operational toy model.

## 9. Local tomography is not a refutation of holism

The real-quantum example is exact:

\[
\rho_\pm=(I_4\pm Y\otimes Y)/4
\]

have identical real local-product statistics but are distinguished perfectly by a suitable joint effect. This illustrates failure of local tomography for that composite model.

Standard finite complex quantum composites are locally tomographic: joint states are determined by all local-product measurement statistics. This includes **correlations**, not merely each subsystem’s reduced state. Entangled states need not be determined by their separate marginals.

Thus local tomography is compatible with entanglement and nonseparability. It is not a theorem that complex quantum theory is the “least holistic” nonclassical theory or that a holistic constitution should prefer real quantum theory. No universal ordering of holism was defined or proved.

The stated rank-dimension identities are correct as algebraic comparisons:

\[
D_{\mathbb R}(n^2)-D_{\mathbb R}(n)^2=n^2(n-1)^2/4,
\]

\[
D_{\mathbb C}(n^2)-D_{\mathbb C}(n)^2=0,
\]

\[
D_{\mathbb H}(n^2)-D_{\mathbb H}(n)^2=-2n^2(n-1)^2.
\]

But a dimension comparison presupposing a same-family rank-\(n^2\) composite is not itself a construction of that composite. Quaternionic composition is particularly nontrivial. A negative difference diagnoses tension with the assumed product structure; it does not by itself establish a viable “composition-deficient” physical theory.

## 10. Definite experience and quantum outcomes

The reduced density matrix of an entangled subsystem does not, by itself, identify a unique globally selected outcome. Local statistics can agree with a classical ensemble while the global state retains correlations.

This motivates an explanatory obligation, not an interpretation-neutral declaration that no objective definiteness is possible. Everettian accounts pursue branch-relative records; hidden-variable accounts add definite variables; collapse accounts modify dynamics. Each requires its own detailed assessment, including probability and phenomenology.

Calling a presentation definite does not automatically solve its relation to an entangled description. Conversely, describing a subsystem by an improper mixture does not prove the absence of definite experience. The constitutive candidate must state how experiences, records, and probabilistic outcomes fit together and what predictions follow.

Fixed numerical collapse parameters are model assumptions requiring justification, but they are not uniquely disqualifying: a fair comparison also tracks the proposed constitution’s assumptions, Hamiltonians, states, and linking principles.

## 11. The residue count and impossibility conclusion do not survive

The transcript’s final scorecard favors its own framework by treating proposed identities and boundaries more generously than analogous commitments in rivals. There is no justified common unit of “residue,” demonstrated independence of the categories, or exhaustive comparison of the six paradigms.

In particular:

- Phenomenal identity remains unestablished; it cannot be marked solved by its assertion.
- Subject boundaries remain unestablished; a tested scalar did not determine them.
- Reconstruction premises still require justification.
- Conditional state-space recovery does not select dynamics, actual states, dimensionality, or global actuality.

Accordingly, “exactly three primitive burdens” and “lowest residue score” are not established. A dependency graph is more informative than a scalar score: it shows which achievements rely on which unpaid premises and where a principle might explain several targets together.

The final claim that absolute zero residue is mathematically or modally unattainable also does not follow. Formal alternative models do not refute metaphysical necessity. The earlier rootless construction prevents treating every infinite determining structure as a tautology. Failure of the present candidate to close its gaps establishes neither impossibility nor an absolute minimum remainder.

**The correct conclusion is not to replace zero residue with a weaker ultimate goal.** Track partial progress while retaining complete intelligibility as the target. Explanatory minimization is an interim research discipline, not a demonstrated ceiling on explanation.

## 12. Constructive next work

The highest-value next step is to formalize the missing constitutive implication, not add more labels or repeat the reconstruction theorem.

1. **Specify the proposed nature of \(C\).** Distinguish experiential claims from generic actuality and provide criteria that do not already encode quantum theory.
2. **Choose one operational principle to derive.** For example, state exactly why that nature requires spectral decomposability rather than arbitrary convex decomposability.
3. **Build a countermodel search.** Seek structures satisfying the proposed constitutive premises while violating that principle. A countermodel shows missing assumptions, not failure of the whole project.
4. **Keep the phenomenal branch independent.** Establish or test identity, qualitative relations, and boundary rules using evidence not defined into access behavior.
5. **Integrate composition and locality.** Identify the physically distinguished algebras and interventions rather than selecting a convenient tensor factorization.
6. **Work on actuality in parallel.** Develop the existential/rooting or non-well-founded branch without assuming mathematical determination equals instantiation.
7. **Require predictive accountability.** State which empirical or formal result would disconfirm a specific bridge and which revision would count as an added assumption.

A major advance would be one justified constitutive principle that independently constrains both operational physics and phenomenal organization. Its explanatory leverage must be demonstrated, not inferred from the ambition to unify them.

## Final formulation

> Reality should be investigated as an actual, internally articulated constitution, not as a simulation needing an external executor. Public physics and experience may be aspects of one occurrence, but their identity and articulation into subjects require justification. Conditional results already show how interaction yields stable macro-laws, how differentiation yields statistical geometry, and how operational assumptions constrain quantum state-space types. These results expose rather than discharge the foundational obligation: explain why the actual concrete nature has those properties. Necessary, plenitudinal, and non-well-founded global completions remain candidates; neither completion nor impossibility has been proved. Preserve the zero-residue objective and count only justified dependencies as progress toward it.
