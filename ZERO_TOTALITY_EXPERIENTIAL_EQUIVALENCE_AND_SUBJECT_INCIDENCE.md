# When does a symmetry preserve actual experience?

## The problem

**Incidence continuation:** [subject fields and experiential accounting](ZERO_TOTALITY_SUBJECT_INCIDENCE_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_ACCOUNTING.md) proposes a field-first incidence candidate and shows how token-based and subject-incidence totals can differ when a token is hypothesized to be shared.

The [previous candidate](ZERO_TOTALITY_INTRINSIC_ORIENTATION_SELECTION.md) reduced a finite zero-sum model to one orbit of permutations. That is not enough to establish one **physical–experiential** whole: a list of signed contributions does not say who experiences what, how experiences are related, or whether they happen at all.

This report makes the missing condition explicit. A legitimate change of description must carry along the relevant qualitative character, subject incidence and internal relations—not just permute numbers. In a finite control, the same signed list and the same global zero admit **five non-isomorphic subject-incidence organizations**. Even fixing the number of subjects and their signed totals does not determine all the qualitative incidence facts.

These are conditional distinctions between specified representations, not a claim that nature realizes all five organizations or that experience can be reduced to a graph. They identify what an intrinsic zero-totality account must actually explain, while leaving open that one self-subsistent constitution could determine all of it together.

## 1. A candidate for experiential equivalence

Suppose, for a finite illustrative sector, that the theory has already established:

- a set E of actual experiential aspects or occurrences, with their qualitative contents;
- a set S of actual subjects and an incidence map `b:E->S` saying which subject has each aspect;
- any physically and experientially relevant relations among occurrences, including jointly presented contents and, where appropriate, internally recorded order;
- if the signed quantity is applicable, a contribution `q(e)` and a justified weight `w(e)`.

This is a bookkeeping representation of a **proposed already-experienced reality**. Assigning a subject label or a q-value does not create a feeling, establish a shared scale, or determine subject boundaries. Nor is it asserted that all experiential phenomena can be exhaustively captured by these finite annotations.

A passive relabelling consists of bijections `pi:E->E'` and `sigma:S->S'` that carry the represented structure with them. In particular,

```text
b'(pi(e)) = sigma(b(e)),
q'(pi(e)) = q(e),       w'(pi(e)) = w(e),
```

and the relevant qualitative, incidence, joint-presentation and history relations must also be preserved. This is the model's **necessary preservation test** for a claimed mere relabelling. A full physical theory might have a richer and differently formulated notion of equivalence; the toy isomorphism is not automatically sufficient to establish physical equivalence.

This criterion does not say that pain and pleasure are the same. Under a passive change of labels, a negative aspect maps to its corresponding negative aspect; the signs and qualitative roles travel with their occurrences. Nor does it require subject names themselves to be fundamental: the correspondence can rename subjects while preserving each subject's whole experiential organization.

If a subject has a physically meaningful relational role, memory or record, that feature must be transformed and preserved too. One cannot justify equivalence by simply discarding the features that distinguish whose suffering is present.

## 2. Same zero, five incidence structures

Assume, **for this control**, three actual occurrences: `P` with q `+1`, `N` with q `-1`, and `Z` with q `0`. Explicitly assume that Z is a genuine neutral experience here; a zero value alone would not imply this. All weights are one, so

```text
Q = +1 - 1 + 0 = 0.
```

Suppose that each occurrence belongs to exactly one subject, with no other occurrences in the finite domain. The possible incidence partitions are:

| Subject incidence (each brace is one subject) | Number of subjects |
|---|---:|
| `{P,N,Z}` | 1 |
| `{P,N}`, `{Z}` | 2 |
| `{P,Z}`, `{N}` | 2 |
| `{N,Z}`, `{P}` | 2 |
| `{P}`, `{N}`, `{Z}` | 3 |

These five structures are pairwise non-isomorphic under relabellings that preserve the stipulated positive, negative and neutral roles and incidence. For instance, `{P,N},{Z}` has a single subject with both positive and negative occurrences, whereas `{P,Z},{N}` does not. They have the same q list, global Q and incidence weights. The table does not establish that each partition is physically possible, that P and N are simultaneous, or that their aggregation has a moral meaning. A subject might have occurrences at different internal times; relations of joint presentation would need separate specification.

There is a sharper comparison. In both `{P,Z},{N}` and `{N,Z},{P}`, the multiset of **subject-level signed totals** is `{-1,+1}`. Yet in the first the neutral occurrence belongs with the positive one, while in the second it belongs with the negative one. Equal global Q, equal number of subjects, and equal multiset of their totals still do not fix which qualitative content occurs for which subject.

This does not make subject incidence automatically brute. A correct constitutive principle could determine it. The finite control shows only that **the signed total or its bare orbit does not determine it by itself**. The earlier stationary functional does not supply an incidence rule, so its one q-orbit cannot simply be counted as one fully specified experiential whole.

## 3. Temporal relations are further structure

Take the `{P,N,Z}` incidence case and compare two stipulated relational histories: in one, the positive occurrence precedes the negative occurrence; in the other, the negative precedes the positive. If the two occurrences retain their distinct qualitative roles and the ordering is a genuine preserved relation within that subject's history, no role-preserving isomorphism exchanges those orders. Both histories still have Q=0.

This is an elementary control, not a derivation of lived succession, memory or a fundamental time coordinate. The order relation would need a physical and experiential account, potentially via relational clocks and records. It does not require a preferred cosmic foliation. If the sign role, order or subject mapping is only a descriptive convention rather than a physical/experiential fact, the appropriate quotient would differ; that is a substantive issue for the theory, not a choice settled by this toy example.

## 4. A constructive constitution to investigate

A stronger candidate than a bare sum would say:

> The whole's intrinsic nature simultaneously constitutes the actual qualitative occurrences, their subject incidence and relational histories, and the signed quantity whose whole-level total is zero. Mathematical relabellings are redundant only when they preserve that entire constitution.

This proposal does not insert a second inner observer. Where a qualitative occurrence is actual, its being present is not made real by a further observer reading an annotation. Yet local self-presence does not by itself tell us what physical organization makes the occurrence belong to one subject rather than another. That is the link the candidate must explain, rather than merely adding a subject table after specifying the structural state.

For example, a prospective theory might seek an independently defined organization that supports *joint qualitative presentation* within an actual subject and distinguishes it from two separate local subjects. To support the proposed zero, it would also need to derive a common signed quantity and its global composition from the same constitution. Physical integration, a mathematical equivalence relation, or a report of joint experience cannot simply be asserted to be that constitution; each needs independent warrant and must handle ambiguous cases, mixed feelings, temporal persistence and subject boundaries.

The positive explanatory target is not to choose between structure and experience. The aim is an identity or other constitutive relation in which the structure is genuinely experiential and the subjective differences are intelligible from the whole's nature. A separate external maker or universal experiencer is not assumed. At present, neither the local identity nor its global self-subsistence follows from the finite symmetry argument.

## 5. Consequences for the zero-totality programme

1. **Zero does not erase suffering.** In the stated representation N remains an actual negative experience regardless of the Q total or whose other experience is positive. The calculation does not establish moral compensation or an optimal world.
2. **No cosmic subject is needed.** A single whole can contain several subjects in the representation. Nor does a partition itself establish the existence or plurality of actual subjects.
3. **A scalar need not encode everything.** The user proposes a well-defined global quantity applying to the whole, not necessarily that reality itself is a scalar or that Q uniquely encodes every relation. The stronger no-residue question is how those relations are determined by the whole's constitution.
4. **Physical and experiential zeros remain distinct claims.** No value of q here is identified with physical energy, and a permutation-invariant Q is not automatically a gravitational constraint.
5. **Possibility–actuality needs its own scope.** Whether any or all of the five structures are genuinely possible or actual is not settled by the enumeration. Local alternatives cannot be promoted to one complete global actuality merely by listing them.

The next foundational task is to propose an actual **incidence principle**—a specific relation by which a physical–experiential organization constitutes a subject and its content—and test whether it is independently motivated and compatible with the zero constraint. The present report supplies a requirement and discriminating controls, not such a derived principle. It neither proves nor disproves that zero can meet the user's full demand for complete intelligibility without residue.
