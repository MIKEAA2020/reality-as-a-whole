## 2. Arithmetic lead: a normal Gibbs model with prime-sensitive observables

This is an explicit auxiliary spectral model, **not** the full Bost–Connes system or a derivation of physical time. Let `H=ℓ²(N_{≥1})`, `H_ar|n⟩=(log n)|n⟩`, and β>1. Then

    Z(β)=Tr e^(−βH_ar)=Σ_(n≥1) n^(−β)=ζ(β),
    ρ_β=Z(β)^(−1)Σ_n n^(−β)|n⟩⟨n|.

For each prime p let `P_p` project onto multiples of p. Unique factorization and absolute convergence give

    Tr(ρ_β P_p)=p^(−β),
    Tr(ρ_β P_p P_q)=(pq)^(−β)  for distinct primes p,q.

More generally, for any finite set of distinct primes S, the joint probability that every p in S divides n is `(∏_(p∈S)p)^(−β)=∏_(p∈S)p^(−β)`. Thus the prime-divisibility events are jointly independent in this specified Gibbs distribution (not merely pairwise independent); prime valuations have `P(v_p=k)=(1−p^(−β))p^(−βk)`. These are genuine arithmetic structural facts, not proof that primes are constituents of physical time. The partition function and energy spectrum were chosen, not derived from the superseding zero-energy programme or from the source's older non-dual assumption.

For bounded A, the standard modular action of this faithful normal state on `B(H)` is `σ_s(A)=ρ_β^(is)Aρ_β^(−is)`. Therefore, with a **chosen** positive ħ,

    σ_(−t/(βħ))(A)=e^(itH_ar/ħ) A e^(−itH_ar/ħ).

No external clock subsystem is assumed, but the dynamical Hamiltonian, state and calibration are inputs. On a matrix unit `|n⟩⟨m|`, the phase is `exp[i t log(n/m)/ħ]`. Prime arithmetic affects particular frequencies; it supplies neither spacetime dynamics nor subjective succession.

A useful regulator bound is explicit. Set `Z_N=Σ_(n≤N)n^(−β)` and let `ρ_β,N` be its normalized finite-cutoff state extended by zero. As the measures are diagonal,

    ½||ρ_β−ρ_β,N||₁=(ζ(β)−Z_N)/ζ(β)
                       ≤ N^(1−β)/[(β−1)ζ(β)].

At β↓1, ζ(β) diverges and every fixed finite-level probability tends to zero. This family has **no trace-norm limit to a normal state on B(ℓ²)**: trace-norm convergence would force the limit to assign zero mass to every finite-rank spectral projection, contrary to unit trace. This is a statement about this chosen representation, not proof that KMS states do not exist in other representations or in the BC algebra. The distinction explicitly repairs the source's phase-diagram error and illustrates why type/representation and normality matter.

## 3. Fractal lead: a multiscale finite Fourier bound

This is a fully worked **discrete product-group** illustration of multiscale localization, not Cohen's Euclidean theorem and not a claim that spacetime or modular spectra are fractal. Work on `G_k=(Z/5Z)^k`, whose size is `N=5^k`. Let `F_k=F_5^(⊗k)` be the normalized finite Fourier transform and let `X_k=Y_k={0,1}^k`. Define coordinate projections `P_X,P_Y`.

The one-digit restricted Fourier matrix has operator norm

    λ = ||P_{0,1}F_5 P_{0,1}||
      = sqrt((2+2 cos(π/5))/5) ≈ 0.8506508 < 1.

Tensor-product multiplicativity gives the exact result

    ||P_X F_k P_Y|| = λ^k = N^(−δ),
    δ = −log λ/log 5 ≈ 0.100503 > 0.

Consequently any normalized function supported on Y has at most `λ^k` of its Fourier **amplitude norm** in X; squared probability is at most `λ^(2k)`. The estimate is sharp: tensor powers of a maximizing one-digit singular vector attain it. It explicitly demonstrates how a scale-by-scale bound can produce a power law.

Control: nontrivial scale and support alone are insufficient. If `H` is the subgroup with its last coordinate zero and `H⊥` the dual subgroup supported on the last coordinate, the normalized indicator of H transforms into the normalized indicator of `H⊥`; hence `||P_{H⊥}F_kP_H||=1`. No universal decay follows from “fractal,” “discrete,” or “self-similar” as a label. Nor is this product Fourier transform automatically the cyclic Fourier transform on `Z/(5^k)Z`: the groups differ.

To apply a genuine FUP to the problem of time, one must identify actual clock/record or spectral localization sets, an independently derived Fourier/oscillatory transform, the applicable porosity/scaling conditions and a physical observable controlled by the bound. The source's single uploaded paper does not provide those maps. The finite check below is evidence that a well-typed *borrowing* can be investigated, not evidence that this bridge already holds.
