# Post-schema amendment: actual laser energy, not condition rank

The [fixed plan](LASER_PAIN_SAME_TRIAL_READINESS_FIXED_PLAN.md) preceded access to the CSV; we have now inspected its header and first six data rows, but not computed aggregate scores. The file identifies `ID`, `Trial_Num`, `laser_power`, and `Painrating`; it has no stimulation side. The article identifies ratings and energies at trial level, but the inspected author-supplement code selects `.set` epochs and does **not** establish how this CSV was generated. Thus the interpretation is source-attributed and conditional; no raw-data verification is claimed.

The requested ordinal condition-rank predictor cannot be recovered unambiguously from the CSV alone: the column records numerical laser energy, not explicit L1–L4 rank, and subject-specific missing trials and calibration preclude assuming rank from the observed distinct values. The conditional adaptation is a leave-person-out group mean at the **exact recorded energy**, with the original pooled fallback when no training row has that energy; it is *not* the plan's nominal-rank model. Use exact decimal categories, not binned or tuned values. Retain the preplanned pooled baseline, metrics, participant-weighted version and fixed bootstrap; suppress the side model (no side field). Treat energies as individualized/selected and calibration as an additional information input potentially involving prior pain reports, not an untouched external randomized dose.

This is a post-schema amendment, not preregistration; it cannot repair missing target/condition provenance. Count duplicate participant/trial keys and stop if any; count missing or invalid fields and exclude only invalid rows. Require at least two distinct participants and at least one usable row for every scored test participant. Ensure held-out predictions are trained exclusively on other IDs. The confidence interval resamples participants' fixed out-of-fold losses, and is conditional on the jointly trained leave-one-out folds rather than a full refit bootstrap; report this limitation. No uninspected EEG or questionnaire data will be inferred. If additional unexplained ambiguity becomes material, stop rather than silently change the target or data unit.
