## 1. Physical definitions and exact conditions

Work in a smooth effective spacetime with signature `(-,+,+,+)` on a finite region `D` between two regular complete compact boundaryless levels of `T`. Require `T` to increase along every future-directed causal curve in the patch, and `∇·J=0`. Let the total flux of `J` through a complete clock slice be zero. These are the hypotheses of the earlier [clock–current history theorem](ZERO_TOTALITY_COVARIANT_CLOCK_HISTORY_BRIDGE.md); by the divergence theorem,

```text
∫_D B dV = ∫_D J·∇T dV = (T₂ − T₁) Q_J = 0.
```

Assume a distinct future-directed causal current `K`, positive where the proposed actual incidence is present, and define `N=K·∇T≥0`. If `K` is conserved, its flux through complete slices is constant; this is a restrictive no-net-source assumption, not an automatic property of subject births, deaths, branching, or merging. The mathematical zero above does not itself require `K` to be conserved. A realistic theory would need a constitutive source/transport law and an independently grounded interpretation of its currents.

Let `S={x∈D:N(x)>0}` be the represented experience domain. The necessary support condition for the construction is

```text
B=0 almost everywhere outside S;    ∫_S N dV is finite and positive.
```

Then `dμ=N dV` is a nonnegative finite measure on represented actual incidences, `v=αB/N` on S, and `∫_S v dμ=α∫_D B dV=0`, assuming the signed integral exists. Where `N=0`, `v` is not defined; `0/0` must not be treated as an experience. If `B` is nonzero outside the experience domain, the zero theorem includes non-experiential contributions and **does not imply** that the experiential total is zero. One must derive an appropriate experiential current, show a genuine support identity, or account for the non-experiential and boundary terms independently—not relabel them as subjects.

`N dV` is a covariantly expressed candidate measure, not an actual subject-counting law. A spacetime point need not correspond to one experience. Overlapping, spatially extended or shared subject fields may demand a multiplicity/allocation construction over a richer incidence space. A future-directed current and positive density do not establish subject unity or phenomenal identity.

## 2. Why the ratio is substantive, not a free fix

The ratio ensures the correct physical signed density is integrated **once**, rather than multiplying it by an additional incidence factor. But it can be written algebraically for any `B` and positive `N`; this is not a new physical proof of the experiential bridge. The substantive claim is the independent identity `v=αB/N`, including its sign, magnitude, units, clock and support. It makes the following restrictions:

- Holding `B` fixed, increasing `N` decreases the proposed magnitude of valence per incidence; this is a physical prediction of the identification, not a general truth about experience.
- Where `N` becomes small, `B/N` can become large. A physically adequate model must control this and its integrability, or supply a different constitutive law.
- If `J` is an ordinary electric-type charge current, the proposal associates valence with its clock-frame sign. Charge conjugation reverses this proposed valence when `K` and `T` remain fixed. There is no evidence that ordinary charge sign determines pleasure versus suffering; this is a serious physical/experiential gate. Alternatively `J` must be a separately derived experiential current, not merely called one.
- The theorem gives zero signed total over **every complete regular clock slab** within the domain, not only over one specially selected history. On each complete regular clock slice the signed flux vanishes as well. If the incidence/support identity holds on all such slabs, it imposes correspondingly strong slice-by-slice balance on the proposed valence density. Individual subjects and restricted regions need not balance.
- If an independent measure or calibration contradicts the inverse-density relation, sign mapping or the relevant complete-slab implication, this specific bridge fails even if the signed current theorem remains true.

By contrast, saying `v=αB` and using `dμ=N dV` gives `∫v dμ=α∫NB dV`, which is generally **not zero**. The ratio candidate is one precise way to confront the previously identified weighting obstruction, not permission to alter the physical meaning of `B` or `N` after the fact. In particular `B` is a density per spacetime volume, while `v` is stipulated as intensity per incidence; their units and interpretation must be fixed from independent physics.

## 3. Conditional probability and an exact distribution relation

Suppose, in addition, that the justified self-location measure is precisely `μ` up to a common positive factor and that the whole domain is the relevant reference class. Set `p_+`, `p_-`, `p_0` for positive, negative and neutral valence incidences, and let

```text
u_+ = E[v | v>0] > 0,
u_- = E[−v | v<0] > 0.
```

For finite nonzero positive and negative portions, `E[v]=0` implies

```text
p_+ u_+ = p_- u_-,
p_- = (1 − p_0) u_+ / (u_+ + u_-).
```

Therefore zero balance neither fixes the fraction of suffering nor requires equal frequencies or paired individual experiences. If negative incidences are, on average, twice as intense as positive ones and there are no neutral incidences, the conditional negative-incidence probability is `1/3`. This inference needs the common signed scale, actual total, finite moments and equal accounting/self-location measures. Without these, it is not a prediction. The relation describes signed balance; it does not cancel, justify, or reduce the moral significance of actual suffering.

An individual may have evidence `D` selecting a restricted part of the whole. Let `L(x)=P(D|x)` under a specified generative model. If `E[L]>0`, then

```text
P(A | D) = E[1_A L]/E[L],
E[v | D] = E[vL]/E[L] = Cov(v,L)/E[L],
```

where the last equality uses `E[v]=0` under the **unconditioned whole measure**. Thus the zero whole can coexist with systematically positive or negative experiences in a selected population. The evidence likelihood is a separate input, not a mechanism that changes the actual total. Self-location is an epistemic proposal; applying a different weighted law to unconditional physical records requires an additional bridge and no-signalling controls.

## 4. Worked covariant-current control

Consider a two-dimensional flat cylinder with metric `−dt²+dx²`, `0≤x<2π`, and `0≤t≤1`. This is a mathematical control, **not** an Einstein–matter cosmology or model of real experiences. Stipulate

```text

```

Both currents have zero divergence. `K` is future timelike and its time component is strictly positive; `J` has zero total slice flux. Hence

```text
N=1+½ cos 2x,             v=cos 2x/(1+½ cos 2x),
∫dμ = 2π,                 ∫v dμ = ∫cos 2x dt dx = 0.
```

Under the separately assumed self-location rule `dP=dμ/(2π)`, the zero set has zero measure, and

```text
P(v>0) = 1/2 + 1/(2π) ≈ 0.6592,
P(v<0) = 1/2 − 1/(2π) ≈ 0.3408.
```

The mean positive magnitude is `u_+=2/(π+1)`, and the mean negative magnitude is `u_-=2/(π−1)`. Their probability-weighted contributions are equal and opposite. This is a nontrivial mathematical example where a zero signed whole and asymmetric incidence probabilities coexist, not a measurement of human experience.

For comparison, identifying valence directly with `B=cos 2x` while using the same `dμ=N dV` yields a total `∫NB dV=π/2`, not zero. The difference is an explicit consequence of the competing physical identifications, not evidence favoring either one.

As an evidence-selection illustration, take `L=(1+½ cos 2x)/(3/2)`, which lies between `1/3` and `1`. Then


This selection does not change the actual signed total, and the choice of `L` is a stipulation, not evidence about our universe. ## 5. Relational time and scope

The expressions `J·∇T`, `K·∇T` and `dV` are coordinate scalars/measures, so coordinate changes do not change the construction. The ratio `v=α(J·∇T)/(K·∇T)` is pointwise unchanged under `T→f(T)` for increasing differentiable `f`. But a **physical measure** `K·∇T dV` changes by `f'(T)` if one replaces the physical clock by a new one and does not include the inverse Jacobian. A mere relabelling of the same clock measure includes that Jacobian; an inequivalent clock weighting is a different proposal. This construction does not derive a unique physical clock, show that all clocks agree, or introduce a fundamental preferred foliation.

The clock, currents and metric are effective structures. A fundamental pregeometric theory would have to derive them and their measure. Moreover, a complete regular clock slab is not automatically the complete history of reality or of every subject. Regions with boundaries, sources, noncompactness, absent clocks or unrepresented experience need separate accounting. Zero gauge charge, a gravitational Hamiltonian constraint and signed experiential total remain distinct until their identity or relation is independently established. The stipulated `α` alone does not establish that experiential valence is energy.
