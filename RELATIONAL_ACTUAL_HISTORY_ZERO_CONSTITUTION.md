# A joint candidate: actual relational histories with a zero signed response

## The constructive question

Can the recent strands be brought together rather than leaving an actual-history total and a zero-producing principle as unrelated assumptions? One explicit candidate is an **atemporal functional of the actual subject-incidence histories**. Its stationarity determines a signed response on those histories; under a further condition, every global minimum has genuinely nonzero positive and negative responses whose actual whole-level integral is zero.

The construction uses separate local clock measures and does not require a preferred cosmic slice or an ensemble of merely possible outcomes. It replaces fixed unequal reference values by a second internally varied field. This is a conditional mathematical model: it does not establish that the fields are feelings, that its measure counts actual experience, that its functional is a law of nature, or that its assumptions follow from zero's self-subsistence. Its global coupling and its many possible minima also require interpretation.

## 1. The actual domain and measure

Let `Omega` be a stipulated finite-measure domain of **actual subject–experience incidences**, represented for illustration as a disjoint union of internally clocked subject-history pieces. Let `d mu` be their combined, independently specified positive measure, `M=mu(Omega)` with `0<M<infinity`, and write

```text
<g> = (1/M) integral_Omega g d mu.
```

The brackets average over the stipulated **actual domain**; they are not a quantum expectation or a probability distribution over alternatives. The normalization is mathematical convenience. It does not make the actual incidence measure, clock durations or cross-subject weights objective by itself. Repeated window descriptions of the same incidence must be identified rather than counted twice. If the intended whole-level quantity instead counts shared experiential tokens once, the domain and measure need the distinct treatment in [subject incidence and accounting](ZERO_TOTALITY_SUBJECT_INCIDENCE_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_ACCOUNTING.md).

Assume real square-integrable internal fields `x,y` on Omega. Define

```text
z = x - <x>,       R = <z²>,       q = x-y.
```

For now x, y and q are formal variables on a stipulated domain. A **substantive experiential identity** would be needed to interpret `q` as actual positive or negative felt intensity at each incidence. Calling a negative q “suffering” is not that identity.

## 2. One explicitly stated relational functional

Choose real parameters `a,m`, with `k>0` and `lambda>0`, and consider

```text
F[x,y] = 1/2 <(x-y)²>
       + k/2 <(y-<y>)²>
       + (a-m)/2 R
       + lambda/4 R².
```

All displayed terms are unchanged by simultaneously adding the same constant to x and y. There is no separately prescribed unequal reference profile. The y-field is varied as part of the same proposed constitution. The constants, the whole actual domain, the common measure, the functional and the stationary/minimum principle are nevertheless **assumed inputs**, not consequences of `Q=0`.

For fixed x, the y-part is strictly convex. Assuming the necessary variations are admissible, its minimum satisfies

```text
y = <x> + (x-<x>)/(1+k),
q = k/(1+k) (x-<x>).
```

Therefore

```text
integral_Omega q d mu = M <q> = 0.
```

This is an actual-domain identity under the stipulated interpretation, not the statement that an average over alternative outcomes happens to be zero. The zero can already be obtained by varying y along a common shift while holding x fixed: the centered y-term is unchanged, and stationarity of the first term gives `<y-x>=0`. This variation must genuinely be allowed; fixed boundary values, constraints or a different variational domain could invalidate the step. Full y-minimization is needed for the more specific relation and nontriviality result below.

The actual duration-weighted contribution could then be `Q_X=integral q d mu`, **if** q is independently identified with the proposed experiential intensity and mu with the correct actual-incidence measure. Finite mu and square-integrable x,y make q absolutely integrable by Cauchy–Schwarz, so this particular integral is well defined. This finiteness assumption does not settle an infinite universe or justify subtracting divergent totals.

## 3. Conditional derivation of nonzero differences

After minimizing y, the functional depends on x only through `R=<z²>`:

```text
F_min_y(R) = 1/2 [a + k/(1+k) - m] R + lambda/4 R².
```

Suppose Omega admits at least one nonconstant square-integrable function—e.g. it contains two disjoint subsets of positive measure—and that such functions and their rescalings are admissible. Then:

- If `m <= a+k/(1+k)`, the minimum occurs at `R=0`, so q vanishes almost everywhere.
- If `m > a+k/(1+k)`, every global minimum has

```text
R = R0 = [m-a-k/(1+k)]/lambda > 0,
<q> = 0,
<q²> = [k/(1+k)]² R0 > 0.
```

A nonzero square-integrable q with zero mean takes both positive and negative values on sets of positive measure. Thus the stated constitution conditionally gives actual-domain zero **and** nonzero local signed differentiation. It does not require a pairwise equal-and-opposite match or simultaneous experiences. The parameter inequality, existence of nonconstant admissible fields and global-minimum premise do the work; none follows from a bare zero equation.

The proof can be written as a sum of nonnegative terms. Let `y*(x)=<x>+(x-<x>)/(1+k)`, `d=y-y*(x)`, and assume the differentiated regime. Then

```text
F[x,y] + (lambda/4) R0²
 = (lambda/4)(R-R0)²
 + 1/2 <d²> + k/2 <(d-<d>)²>.
```

This establishes the global lower bound and equality conditions for the specified function space. It does not establish that the real whole minimizes F.

## 4. A two-history example

Use the previous report's two disjoint stipulated local-clock histories: A has two unit-duration segments, B one unit-duration segment, each with the same measure weight. Their total measure is `M=3`. Choose

```text
k=1,  a=1,  m=7/2,  lambda=1,
x on the three segments = (2,-1,-1).
```

Then `<x>=0`, `R=2`, and `R0=2`, so this is one global minimum. Its induced y and q are

```text
y = (1,-1/2,-1/2),
q = (1,-1/2,-1/2).
```

A's integral is `+1/2`, B's is `-1/2`, and their actual-domain total is zero. Local contributions are nonzero; q values are not all individually paired by equal magnitude. The example has been selected to illustrate the minimum; x and q are not measured experiences, and the two histories are not derived physical subjects. Choosing q to denote valence does not make the negative segments unreal or morally compensated.

The same minimum also has `F=-1` in these units. The vanishing quantity is the integral of q, **not F itself** and not a demonstrated physical energy constraint. Changing an arbitrary zero of F would not explain a global physical zero.

## 5. Relational-clock invariance and its limits

Suppose one history is parametrized by `tau=f(lambda)` with f increasing and the same measure transported correctly. The fields transform by composition and the measure acquires the corresponding Jacobian. The integrals defining M, means, F, Q and the minimum conditions are unchanged. Independent reparameterizations of different histories therefore require no common temporal slicing.

This is invariance under **redescription of the same measures**. It does not show that different physical clocks, different clock-dependent quantum constructions, or arbitrary choices of subject weights are equivalent. If the actual measure is altered, the mean `<x>`, the resulting q and the candidate qualitative predictions can change; an independently warranted measure is indispensable. Nor does an atemporal functional derive local succession, clock records, or a viable relativistic causal law.

The terms involving whole-domain means couple all admitted parts of the model. As a whole-history constitutive comparison this is mathematically coherent without positing an external clock. But it is not automatically a physically local interaction: an actual theory must say how its relations fit relativistic causality, subject-local phenomenology and any emergent spacetime. One cannot infer an operational influence across subjects or into the past from this functional, nor declare such concerns solved merely because it is written atemporally.

## 6. The anti-fitting and determinacy tests

The model has implications beyond the zero if its functional, measure and parameters are independently fixed: at a differentiated minimum, the q field is proportional to the centered x field and has the prescribed positive mean square. These can be compared with an independently specified physical–experiential mapping; the bare mathematical statements are not evidence that such a mapping holds.

However, this family is still flexible. Given any nonzero square-integrable field q with `<q>=0` on the stipulated domain, one can set

```text
x = (1+k) q/k,   y = x/(1+k),
m = a+k/(1+k) + lambda <x²>.
```

Then that q is reproduced at a differentiated global minimum. If the field or parameter m is chosen after seeing the desired balance, the model can simply redescribe an already balanced answer. If m is fixed independently, the q mean square is constrained, but there are still generally many profiles of that same norm. The functional does not determine their qualitative organization, subject incidence, or the exact pattern of actual experiences.

This is why a finite construction can be a useful candidate without yet being a self-explanation. It shows one coherent route from an **assumed** relational constitution to actual-domain balance and nontriviality. It does not show why the measure, variation, parameter regime, actual histories or experiential identity follow from zero's nature; nor does it establish that they could not do so in a better theory.

## 7. What a complete account would still have to derive

The no-residue task remains to explain, together, why there are actual experiential fields and distinct subjects; why their particular contents, local clocks and relative weights occur; why the proposed response is felt valence; why the functional and its nontrivial regime belong to the whole's own nature; and how physical energy, relational dynamics and qualitative presence are unified. A minimum of an invented functional is not a substitute for any of these explanations.

The candidate also does not assume a best world or universal subject. Negative experiences, if represented by negative q, remain genuinely suffered by their subjects. No positive contribution erases them. Other additive or non-additive zero-totality proposals remain open.
