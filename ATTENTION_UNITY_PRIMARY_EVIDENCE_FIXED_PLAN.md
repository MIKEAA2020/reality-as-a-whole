# Fixed plan: attention evidence and the subject bridge

## Question and prior knowledge

Lopez's previously read layered-unity proposal uses residual attentional capacities, especially executive control and top-down orienting, to argue for an experienced centrality structure and attentional agency. The prior [layered comparison](LAYERED_UNITY_AND_SUBJECT_QUALITY_COMPARISON.md) did not independently audit the cited primary attention studies. A search has already shown the Arguin et al. 2000 abstract and portions of its introduction and results: 10 controls, 10 callosotomy patients (seven anterior, three complete), two arrows, 500 ms cue–target interval and group/individual heterogeneity. These observations are known before this plan, as are prior split-brain behavior and project model results. This is a retrospective evidence audit, not a blinded literature screen.

## Scope and fixed checks

1. Read the available primary text or abstracts of the two attention studies Lopez uses to support *residual executive attention*: Holtzman and Gazzaniga 1982 (dual-task interference) and Ptito et al. 2009 (attentional blink). Also cross-check Arguin et al. 2000, which addresses spatial orienting and is not the same task or patient group. Explicitly report what material was accessible; never claim full articles, sample sizes, quantitative estimates, experimental controls or phenomenological reports without having inspected the relevant material. If only abstracts or excerpts are accessible, give a bounded evidence appraisal and identify the missing methods.
2. Preserve whether callosotomy was complete versus partial, within-study person and control denominators, and task/condition distinctions when actually available; do not pool, infer patient independence across papers, or turn no significant difference into equivalence. Present a common evidential chain: manipulated task demands → recorded behavioral/neural outcomes → shared capacity or control hypothesis → single experiential centrality or voluntary agency → the L/C incidence rule. Identify at least one alternative explanation and one empirical discriminator for each critical inferential step; distinguish physical sharing, information flow and phenomenology.
3. Return to the [existing common-domain comparison](LAYERED_UNITY_AND_SUBJECT_QUALITY_COMPARISON.md): explain whether these tasks provide `Jlocal`, `Jglobal`, a q-quality measure, or a justified mapping to the four-spin candidate. Keep genuine first-person reports separate from behavioral accuracy and do not treat nonresponse as absent experience. Fairly represent Lopez's experiential centrality premise rather than reducing it to a common processing capacity.
4. Identify a practical noninvasive, no-new-surgery next evidence gate, including independent source-domain calibration and transfer to the same condition, uncertainties, cue and timing controls, heterogeneity and outcome that leaves the competing full packages unresolved. If data or assay conditions are absent, stop with an explicit impasse; do not construct another finite model solely to have a regression.

## Deliverable and validation

Write a concise source-anchored report with links and reading limits, and update project status and the prior layered audit. Test linked existing scripts as relevant, refresh inventories and verify links. No raw participant records, clinical interventions, numerical q estimates or experiential subject census. C3, B4–B5/F2, B9/F3 and B10 remain open unless genuinely new evidence warrants otherwise. Do not imply a full reading of Klinge's book.
