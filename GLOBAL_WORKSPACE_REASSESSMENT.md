# Global Workspace Theory as a candidate implementation

## Assessment

Global Workspace Theory is a plausible better **primary mechanistic starting point** for this synthesis than the finite causal-exclusion proxy. This is an assessment of conceptual fit and research usefulness, not an empirical verdict that GWT is correct or a claim that it completes the unified account.

The earlier IIT-style choice addressed a narrow question: could an explicit exclusion rule distinguish a source from its copy and avoid merger under every positive coupling? That justified a bounded test, not prioritizing IIT as the ontology of the synthesis. The proxy was neither IIT 4.0 nor a comparative test against GWT.

## What fits

The relevant GWT proposal is not that a buffer contains several items. It is that selected information becomes globally available to multiple otherwise specialized processes, allowing coordinated flexible use. Neural global-workspace proposals add specific implementation commitments; broad GWT and those neural variants should not be treated as identical in every detail.

This architecture connects naturally to several surviving requirements:

- **Differentiation with coordination:** specialized contents can participate in a common functional episode without erasing their differences.
- **Actual implementation:** global availability must be realized through effective causal routes, not inferred from a matching display or an observer's state encoding.
- **Source versus monitor:** a passive copy is not automatically a workspace. The question is whether its contents can causally influence the relevant distributed processes and participate in their coordination.
- **Access versus overt report:** reporting is one possible use of globally available content. Blocking a report channel is not by itself evidence that the broader availability has vanished.
- **Temporal organization:** competition, maintenance, updating, and access can be studied in their actual causal dynamics instead of stipulating a universal partition into fixed-duration bins.

“Global” is relative to the relevant functional architecture, not every physically connected event in the universe. A weak physical bridge does not automatically establish global workspace participation. Whether and when it does must be determined by the implemented causal organization.

These are reasons to investigate a realized-workspace model. They do not show that functional availability is sufficient for phenomenality or that workspace participation is a proven subject-boundary criterion.

## What does not follow

GWT's mechanistic strength concerns access and flexible use. Proponents can offer it as a theory of consciousness, not merely behavior; nevertheless, the mechanism's success does not by itself settle the constitutive claims sought here:

1. Why is globally available content experienced rather than only processed?
2. Why does it have a particular qualitative character?
3. Why do specified contents belong to one episode rather than several coordinated experiences?
4. Which implementation differences preserve phenomenal constitution?
5. Why are the physical laws, realized processes, and their qualitative constitution actual?

These questions are not automatically resolved by IIT either. More explicit phenomenal postulates are not the same as independently justified answers.

## The right next model

A GWT-directed construction should include specialist processes, selective workspace entry, distribution to multiple consumers, flexible causal use, and an actual report mechanism distinguished from the workspace. Feedback and recurrence should be declared where the selected version requires them, not smuggled into every use of the GWT name.

The critical comparisons should preserve ordinary output where possible while changing workspace availability or its downstream uses. Candidate interventions include disconnecting a report route while leaving other access paths intact, replacing an active workspace with a passive copy, and disrupting cross-module availability while preserving local discrimination. These are proposed model contrasts, not claims that clean human interventions or phenomenal measurements already exist.

The evidence and interpretation must distinguish:

- actual global functional availability;
- evidence for conscious access;
- a hypothesized constitutive identity;
- subject/episode incidence;
- and the ultimate account of actuality.

The existing episode-first toy models distinguish persistent storage, sequential processing, and later replay; they are not implementations or empirical tests of GWT. A full GWT-directed model must add the missing distributed causal architecture rather than relabel its buffer.

## Revised working position

Use GWT as the leading **candidate mechanism for coordinated conscious access** in the next constitutive comparison. Retain occurrence-first, non-additive realization as the wider candidate ontology. Do not require an IIT-style score to define the workspace, and do not identify functional globality with phenomenal unity without an argument.

This is a change in the candidate to develop, not a discovery that GWT unifies mathematics, physics, experience, and ultimate explanation. No new executable result or comparative empirical evidence is claimed.

## Substrate and identity qualification

[The full substrate transcript audit](substrate-independence-thesis.md) distinguishes actual realization from material-specific necessity. GWT need not identify experience with an uninstantiated abstract program; a realized organizational identity remains a live hypothesis. Conversely, the existing functional architecture does not prove phenomenal sufficiency. The SR analogy supplies a standard for a possible constitutive explanation, not a completed identity argument or a reason to substitute cosmic consciousness for functional global access.

## Explicit local geometry candidate

[The SR-inspired construction](research/constitutive_geometry/REPORT.md) supplies physical response probabilities, a derived metric, coordinate transformations, channel contraction, and workspace dissociations. It proposes a calibrated local phenomenal-geometry constraint but does not establish qualitative occurrence, an intrinsic protocol, or episode incidence. In particular, copied monitoring can share the active source's metric, and the implemented two-reset-trial calibration is not one claimed experience. This adds mathematical content to the analogy without treating geometric covariance as proof of constitution.

The actual data and result paper have not been analyzed. GWT remains the mechanistic research preference, not a demonstrated constitutive law or superior empirical winner.
