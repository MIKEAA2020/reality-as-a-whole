## Working rule

Whenever a source matters to an answer, report any material access or reading limitation **in that answer**, not only in an internal note. Distinguish: (1) full version of record, (2) author preprint or supplied manuscript, (3) abstract or excerpt only, (4) missing supplement, figures or external files, (5) raw data/code available versus actually inspected, and (6) reproduction versus summary. Do not call a preprint the full published version without verifying their identity. If a source changes, state what was compared and what was not; don't make blanket claims from a title, a successful HTTP response, or an inventory.

## Attachment: what it changes for QPV

`quantum_position_verification_main.txt` is a LaTeX main manuscript for the QPV experiment, **not the debate transcript**. Its main sections and methods match the revised preprint closely on key inspected points: conditional security, untrusted quantum devices, pre-shared-entanglement restrictions, XOR implementation and the identified two-pair attack. That supports the previous substantive interpretation, but it is not independently authenticated as the definitive journal text and does not include the referenced full supplement or missing macro definitions and figures.

A newly noticed detail needs explicit qualification: the supplied manuscript and arXiv v2 main experimental section state challenge arrival within `3 ± 2 ns`, whereas the arXiv v2 supplementary implementation section reports `9 ± 2 ns`. We have not reconciled the statements, checked primary timing records or inferred that they measure the same timing quantity. They should not be silently combined or cited as an independently verified latency.
