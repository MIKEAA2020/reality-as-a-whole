# Necessary articulated unity
## A constitutive proposal for qualities, finite perspectives, and their relations

## 1. The proposed constitution

> **The absolute is one intrinsically determinate, self-present actuality. Its completeness is not blank identity but the noncomposite fullness that grounds every genuine difference. Qualities are determinate manners of that self-presence; finite perspectives are actually bounded organizations of its disclosure; their physical and experiential relations are internal determinations of the same actuality, not links between independently existing ingredients.**

This is the preferred **intrinsic-articulation hypothesis**. It develops the [disclosure account](COMPLETE_AWARENESS_FINITE_DISCLOSURE.md) rather than adding another ontology or simulation story. No absolute becomes finite, creates a second reality, or runs an experiential representation. Local succession and causal dynamics describe internal order, not an external history of the absolute's production.

The proposal is substantive in three ways: it excludes strict undifferentiation as an adequate completion; it rejects an independently adjustable assignment of qualities and subjects as the final explanation; and it requires the same realized nature to ground qualitative specificity, bounded disclosure, and their relations. Its particular identity laws have not yet been established. It is an explicit constitution hypothesis and its consequences, not a discovered complete essence of actuality.

## 2. What completeness requires—and what remains to be determined

The argument for articulation is:

1. Actual experience has determinate differences: pain is not joy, one local disclosure is not another, and an appearance of a difference is itself a determinate occurrence.
2. Complete actuality cannot omit those facts; complete self-knowledge cannot confuse them.
3. Nothing outside the absolute supplies their ultimate ground.
4. Its own nature must therefore ground their determinacy.

**Conclusion:** under the adopted completeness and experiential requirements, the absolute must be sufficient for genuine articulation. Distinction is not merely tolerated; its intelligible grounding is required.

This is **necessity of articulation**, not yet **necessity of every particular articulation**. It does not select an actual color space, count of subjects, physical Hamiltonian, history, or distribution of suffering. To establish those as necessary, one must identify the intrinsic nature and justify the connection from that nature to those particular facts. Replacing “some determinate content is required” with “this precise content has been derived” would be invalid.

### Fixed meaning of simplicity

Simplicity here means no dependence on an outside, no assembly from independently ultimate constituents, and no separately adjustable foundations. It does **not** mean absence of every difference grounded in actuality.

Strict undifferentiation is not retained as an interchangeable description. A theory using “virtual” or “eminent” containment must explain the same determinate facts; if it has no intrinsic basis for their differences, it fails the articulation requirement. If it has such a basis without composite parts, it agrees with the relevant explanatory demand even if its terminology differs.

## 3. Qualities: intrinsic specificity, not names attached to structure

### Positive account

A quality is **how a determinate occurrence is self-present**, not an additional object observed inside it. Redness is not an abstract coordinate awaiting illumination by a separate observer. An actual red experience is already a qualitative occurrence. Its contrast, intensity, saturation, spatial organization, affective relations, and incidence concern that same occurrence.

On the proposed constitution, these relations are not external descriptions laid over an otherwise anonymous quale. They express its articulated nature. Conversely, a bare network of unspecified relations is not automatically that quality: its concrete intrinsic character must be identified, not supplied by a label.

The relevant identity claim is:

    a warranted qualitative occurrence
       = the intrinsic self-presentation of this realized determination.

The right-hand side must be specified independently enough to constrain the left. “The determination that feels red” is not an explanation of redness.

### What can actually explain a particular quality

An adequate account must jointly specify:

- the realized physical/intrinsic distinction, including state, organization, and relevant counterfactual powers;
- the independently anchored qualitative contrast and its relations;
- a warranted constitution argument joining them as one occurrence;
- consequences that discriminate an alternative identification under held-out conditions.

For example, a visual-orientation contrast is a bounded starting target. It is not a derivation of the absolute character of redness, and stimulus orientation, decoded orientation, reported orientation, and experienced orientation cannot be equated by definition.

**Reference discipline:** renaming unused symbols changes nothing. Exchanging independently anchored qualities may change the proposed constitution. Agreement on measured similarity or discrimination relations does not by itself prove that every qualitative fact is exhausted by them. The [qualitative-equivalence audit](QUALITATIVE_EQUIVALENCE_AUDIT.md) remains binding.

### Why the whole does not need extra quality copies

The whole's self-presence includes the occurrence itself, with its locally owned character. No second red experience is manufactured for a cosmic spectator. Whether complete acquaintance has a nonaggregative phenomenal character of its own must be explained; it is not a license to count the same local token twice or erase aversive qualities by reclassification.

## 4. Finite perspectives: bounded acquaintance grounded in actual organization

### Positive account

A finite perspective is **a determinate organization of directly present contents, ownership, and locally effective relations within the one actuality**. It is neither a separately created owner nor merely a coordinate view of the whole. Its boundedness is constitutive: it does not first possess all knowledge and then lose it.

The whole completely knows that a mode has content c and lacks acquaintance with d. This fact does not make d locally present. Complete acquaintance, local acquaintance, warranted knowledge, memory, and usable information are different relations grounded in the same actuality, not independent substances.

**Candidate physical route:** investigate the actual organization maintaining content and its counterfactual integration, rather than an observer's ability to decode it. Recurrent maintenance, selective access, and context-sensitive control are candidate mechanisms for locally effective content. They are not yet sufficient conditions for subjecthood.

The assertion is stronger than “we can restrict a global description”: not every mathematical restriction is an actually constituted perspective. A viable law must pick out the actual local organization without taking its desired subject boundary as an input.

### Explicit target

For a physically specified realized process P, retain:

    F(P) = (Gamma(P), chi_P)
    Gamma(P) = (E, I, L).

E identifies actual episodes where warranted; I relates content and physical occurrences to their incidence and temporal extent; L specifies continuation or lineage without automatically identifying it with numerical identity; chi specifies qualitative organization. Neither a disjoint partition nor a universal episode clock is presumed.

The intrinsic-articulation hypothesis says these are jointly grounded in P's concrete nature, or in a declared enlarged intrinsic basis if P proves insufficient. It does not make them separate foundational choices. The proposed physical basis remains [the realized process net](REALIZED_PROCESS_CANDIDATE.md), not a state label containing the desired phenomenal answer.

### Boundary cases and consequences

- **Passive monitor:** carrying a copy of a content-bearing trajectory does not establish the same ownership or constitutive organization. A monitor's possible experience remains a separate question.
- **Matching realizations:** matching qualitative kinds need not share a token; distinct actual occurrences cannot be collapsed by isomorphism.
- **Shared hardware:** sharing a controller does not by itself establish one field of acquaintance.
- **Weak coupling:** nonzero influence does not automatically merge subjects. A physically meaningful integration rule must justify any boundary change.
- **Maintenance versus distribution:** preserved reduced maintenance does not establish preserved whole constitution. Treat the existing intervention audit as a controlled distinction, not a phenomenal verdict.
- **Memory and lineage:** later representation of an earlier occurrence is not the original occurrence. Continuation, overlap, and ownership need their own criteria.

These consequences constrain a proposed scope law; they do not establish one. The common recurrent-access candidate remains available for a declared narrow domain, with its boundary and timing debts exposed rather than renamed as solutions.

## 5. Relations: internal determinations, not independently actual connectors

Three relations must not be confused:

1. **Constitution/incidence:** what belongs to an occurrence as its quality, organization, and local scope.
2. **Internal physical dependence:** interaction, maintenance, intervention, and causal organization within actuality.
3. **Knowledge/representation:** one mode's access to, or representation of, another occurrence.

Causal influence does not automatically constitute togetherness. Representing another's pain does not automatically make it the same locally owned pain. The absolute's self-presence is not an external measurement of a separate being.

Finite perspectives can therefore be distinct without ultimate separation. Their interaction can alter locally present content without manufacturing a new ontological substance. A physical law describes that lawful organization; it need not be an external command governing intrinsically unrelated things.

This is not “relations all the way down” in place of concrete actuality. Nor does identifying one actuality eliminate the distinctions among these relations. The complete nature must ground them; merely placing them in a common diagram is not their explanation.

## 6. A precise explanatory requirement: no independent selector at completion

Let B be a candidate intrinsic basis specified without the desired qualitative or subject labels. Let T be the proposed constitution constraints, including independently warranted qualitative reference and physical evidence.

Ask whether a fixed realized B admits:

- no admissible phenomenal articulation: the candidate fails for that target;
- one articulation, up to justified re-presentation preserving reference and tokens: a determination result;
- more than one inequivalent articulation: the stated basis and constraints have not yet selected the actual target.

A completed constitution cannot leave an independently adjustable choice among genuinely different chi or Gamma assignments. If extra intrinsic structure is needed, it must be independently justified and declared as a revision, not hidden inside “complete.”

**Important distinction:** a unique mathematical expansion is not alone an identity or an explanation. The predicates must capture actual qualities and scopes, the realized basis must be warranted, and its actuality and necessity must be explained. Determination, identity, and explanation are separate obligations.

### Two underdetermination arguments

**Qualities:** if the constraints use only relations preserved by a target permutation g, then a proposed map f and the transformed map g composed with f satisfy those same relational constraints. If g is merely renaming, there is no second fact. If it exchanges independently anchored qualities, the constraints have not selected between genuinely different assignments. Choosing a quotient solely to force uniqueness assumes the answer.

**Perspectives:** if T contains no constraint connecting actual organization to incidence, assigning one joint episode or two separate episodes is not distinguished by the common-actuality claim alone. This is a logical demonstration of missing premises, not proof that both assignments are metaphysically possible, empirically adequate, or equally plausible.

These arguments explain why the general requirements cannot, without more content, deliver particular articulation. They are not a reason to abandon the stronger endpoint; they identify exactly what its constitutive explanation must add.

## 7. Worked explanatory target: two contents, one practical context

Consider a person pursuing payment or profit while encountering another's distress.

**Qualities:** the visual appearance, affective concern, and thought of gain are determinate actual contents. Their differences belong within the absolute's fullness. They are not deficiencies merely because each is not every other content.

**Perspective:** only some contents may be locally present; others may remain unattended, unremembered, or unavailable for use. Whether a particular omission is unconscious processing, absent acquaintance, or lack of action must be established rather than inferred from missing report.

**Relations:** the other person is actual whether noticed or not. Their need can become represented locally; that representation can influence action; or effective regard can remain absent even when the need is known.

**Privation:** the intelligence, effort, goal, acquisition, and possible pleasure are positive actuality. Where justified consideration is due and absent, the bad-making feature is missing integration, not an additional evil constituent. The missing regard is not turned into a positive thing by its being truly absent.

**Fulfillment:** a considerate action realizes the relevant relation where its conditions are met. Merely contemplating that relation, feeling sympathy, or being globally comprehended is not the same actual organization as fulfilling it.

This supplies a concrete schema linking quality, local scope, physical action, and practical integration. It does not establish every due standard, that every pursuit of gain is wrong, or why all actual privations belong to perfect reality.

## 8. Completeness, perfection, and privation without equivocation

[The decombination/privation consolidation](DECOMBINATION_PRIVATION_CONSOLIDATION.md) strengthens the dependence direction: positive constitution must ground actual scope, while non-disclosure and missing due fulfillment are understood relative to it. The dependence of lack on positive actuality does not imply that fullness needs an actual defect for self-knowledge. Modes and appearances preserve the experiential datum without automatically settling subject incidence.

The recent clarification governs the stress test: **badness is not a positive rival ingredient to be accommodated alongside the absolute**. A real absence is not a positively existing constituent. Darkness can be truly present as a condition of absent illumination without a second substance of darkness; similarly, lack of due regard need not be another power.

Distinguish:

- **Determinacy:** this quality or relation is not that one.
- **Local limitation:** a mode does not disclose the whole.
- **Privation:** something appropriate or due is absent in a specified respect.

The first is required by complete self-knowledge. The second does not subtract knowledge from the whole. The third is not established merely by either of the first two. The authority and applicability of the due standard still require explanation.

A local omission of regard does not by logic alone entail ignorance, lack of empathy, or missing knowledge in the absolute. Nor does the whole's completeness automatically mean that every local activity has enacted every due relation. These are different predicates of different respects, not a positive substance of evil plus a good substance.

The remaining perfection question is therefore **how these particular local limitations and absences are grounded in unsurpassably complete actuality**, while preserving the significance of actual consideration, prevention, and repair. It is not a demand that the theory incorporate a second positive principle of badness.

The earlier disclosure account's finding remains narrowly valid: knowing that a relation is missing does not realize that relation. It neither refutes privation nor proves the absolute deficient. Do not turn this local conditional result into a contradiction without an independently warranted connection between local omission and absolute deficiency.

Likewise, asserting that all actuality is positive does not independently prove that every local omission is necessary, that cruelty is justified, or that a less harmful complete constitution is impossible. Those are separate claims. Necessity, where warranted, must include the genuine roles of prevention and repair, not neutralize them.

## 9. Mathematical and scientific articulation

The physical interface remains the realized net of observables, state, dynamics, localized interventions, and tokens. Its algebraic, causal, geometric, contextual, computational, and multiscale presentations must constrain the same constitution.

- Exact faithful re-presentation preserves actual quality and incidence; it does not create a new subject.
- Effective reduction requires preservation of target distinctions. Equal reports, covariances, stress limits, or reduced transition tables are not automatically complete equivalences.
- AQFT localization and state restriction do not select a subject. Type-III issues and justified finite approximations remain relevant.
- GWT can explain access mechanisms; IIT, predictive organization, self-modeling, and valence candidates must be compared in their actual domains. A shared vocabulary does not make their sufficiency claims agree.
- Quantum information, causal sets, twistors, topos, Langlands-type correspondences, scaling/fractals, complexity, and programming retain the roles and open bridges recorded in the atlas. None is discarded because the absolute is not an external process.

Thus positive intrinsic articulation is not an escape from physical specificity. A purported complete nature must explain why these structures and laws apply, rather than baptize an arbitrary mathematical structure as awareness.

## 10. Work performed and next constitutive obligation

**Now supplied:** a fixed noncompositional meaning of simplicity; an argument that complete awareness requires intrinsic articulation; a proposed account of quality, scope, and relations; a no-independent-selector criterion; explicit underdetermination arguments; and a worked privation case preserving the user's distinction between actuality and absent due integration.

**Not supplied:** the concrete essence that necessitates the actual quality space, a validated subject/temporal incidence law, the microphysical-to-neural bridge, the authority of all due standards, or an explanation of every actual local privation. There is no new empirical discovery or claim that the whole framework has been formally proved consistent.

**Next test, within the existing W1–W6 queue:** freeze a bounded realized basis and independently warranted qualitative/incidence contrasts; state the constitutive premise that excludes the strongest surviving alternative; show why that premise follows from the proposed intrinsic nature or revise the nature openly. If two anchored assignments survive, identify the missing relation rather than declare their difference unreal. In parallel, justify what makes regard due in one concrete practical case without assuming that all absence is defect.

The advance is from “one whole can have distinctions” to **one complete nature must ground determinacy, local scope, and their organization without independently adjustable selectors**. This makes the task more explanatory and testable without pretending that specifying its obligations already completes it.
