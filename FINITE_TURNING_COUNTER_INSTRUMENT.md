# A bounded turning-clock record in one constrained history

**Result:** the earlier [turning/path bridge](TURNING_ENTROPY_RECORD_BRIDGE.md) can be represented by a finite positive propagation constraint with a cycle counter, explicit saturation flag, a complete noisy readout and measurement backaction. The construction uses the existing [relational-history](RELATIONAL_TIME_CONSTRUCTION.md) and [clock-frame](CLOCK_CHOICE_EQUIVALENCE.md) techniques. It is an application and consistency check, **not a claimed new theorem, an autonomous device, a Barontini data analysis or a solution of the problem of time**. See the [exact rational check](verification/finite_turning_counter.py) and [output](verification/finite_turning_counter.json).

The finite history register already supplies an ordered, unwrapped 13-vertex path. The counter update is scheduled by those vertices. Thus the construction **does not derive chronology from a cyclic coordinate alone**: it makes the extra order and finite memory explicit, and provides a test target for a later autonomous realization. The label is an internal finite system, not an external clock for the absolute.

## 1. Model and supplied ingredients

Let the clock Hilbert space have orthonormal basis `|j>`, `j=0,...,12`. Let the counter–flag space be `C³⊗C²` with basis `|n,f>`, `n∈{0,1,2}`, `f∈{0,1}`. The flag `f=1` denotes overflow for the specified prepared trajectory; it is not a magic irreversible latch.

For each path edge `j→j+1`, define a unitary W_j on this six-dimensional register:

- j=3: swap counter levels 0 and 1, with f unchanged;
- j=7: swap counter levels 1 and 2, with f unchanged;
- j=11: flip f conditional on n=2;
- every other edge: identity.

These reversible gates are **only an increment on the designated initial path** `|0,0>`; they are not a cyclically reusable increment for every counter input. In particular, a second application of a swap or flag flip can undo it. The edge-dependent gates, initial state, clock register, q readout, instrument and unit a below are supplied. No energy cost, local Hamiltonian engineering or robust reset is derived.

Define the propagation maps

    B_j = |j>⊗I − |j+1>⊗W_j,
    C = sum_(j=0)^11 B_j B_j† ≥ 0.

Here B_j maps the register space to the joint clock–register space. With `V₀=I` and `V_(j+1)=W_j V_j`, the standard finite history isometry is

    J|ψ> = (1/√13) sum_(j=0)^12 |j>⊗V_j|ψ>.

As in the cited Feynman–Kitaev construction, `ker C=range J`: positivity makes every edge residual vanish, giving `ψ_(j+1)=W_j ψ_j`, and conversely every such path is in the kernel. Thus `J†J=I` and `dim ker C=6`. Every `JρJ†` is stationary under the auxiliary flow generated by C. This uses known history-state mathematics; see also [the Feynman–Kitaev clock review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.07395).

## 2. Turning readout and finite counter

Let the **coarse clock effect** report `q_j=cos(πj/2)`, with branch/turn label r=`j mod 4`: the four distinguishable phase labels have q values `+1,0,−1,0`. The two zero readings have opposite orientation. For the prepared counter `|0,0>`, conditioning the history on vertex j gives:

| Vertices j | Counter n | Flag f | Reading τ/a |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–3 | 0 | 0 | 0–3 |
| 4–7 | 1 | 0 | 4–7 |
| 8–11 | 2 | 0 | 8–11 |
| 12 | 2 | 1 | overflow; no valid decoded reading |

For `f=0`, the classical decoder uses `τ/a=4n+r`. With the illustrative assignment `S_j=S₀+a q_j` and `a>0`, the discrete total variation of the prescribed q path is `a j`, agreeing with the decoder for j=0,...,11. S is an **assigned toy quantity**, not a measured entropy, and the equations do not identify physical time with entropy.

On the selected trajectory j=0,4,8 have the same q and the same phase/turn label, but retained n distinguishes the three readings 0,4a,8a. At j=12, the next update flips the overflow flag rather than falsely returning a fresh valid cycle. The full 13-state clock register itself distinguishes the vertices if it remains accessible; the loss of distinction arises only when the allowed clock readout is restricted to the coarse q/phase label and other distinguishing records are discarded.

Clock vertex projectors need not commute with C. Their conditional distributions are partial-observable formulas; the constraint-compatible representatives for an **anchored register event** are constructed below. Do not identify the raw clock PVM with a gravitational Dirac observable.

## 3. Complete readout and its disturbance

At a fixed chosen frame j, let `P_(n,f)=|n,f><n,f|`. For an erasure probability `0≤ε≤1` define a declared measurement instrument on the register:

    I_(n,f)(ρ) = (1−ε) P_(n,f) ρ P_(n,f),
    I_?(ρ)     = ε sum_(n,f) P_(n,f) ρ P_(n,f).

The effects are `(1−ε)P_(n,f)` and `εI`, so their sum is I. Erasure is retained as an explicit outcome. This instrument first records the projective counter/flag outcome and then erases its *reported label* with probability ε; the microscopic record or environment is not reset or shown absent. It is not the only implementation of the same POVM.

For ε=1/5 and the prepared path, a valid reading is reported with probability 4/5 and is erased with probability 1/5. At j=12 the overflow flag is reported with probability 4/5; with probability 1/5 the output is explicitly unknown. Thus overflow is **not** certified in the erased runs and no silent perfect detector is assumed.

Backaction matters. Start from the coherent root register state

    |+> = (|0,0>+|1,0>)/√2.

At j=4 the gate swaps the two counter levels, leaving this superposition unchanged. The instrument's nonselective channel removes its off-diagonal coherence. The trace distance between the pre-readout pure state and the nonselective post-readout state is 1/2. At ε=1/5 the outcomes `(0,0)`, `(1,0)`, `?` have probabilities 2/5, 2/5, 1/5. That disturbance is a feature of this particular instrument; a different implementation or retained environment can change the channel.

## 4. One physical event represented in two ideal frames

Conditioning at vertex j gives the ideal register frame `ρ_j=V_jρV_j†`. Let an anchored root effect be E. In frame j the **same event** is represented by `E_j=V_j E V_j†`, and

    Tr(ρ_j E_j) = Tr(ρ E).

More generally, transport every Kraus operator `K_m` as `K_m^(j)=V_j K_m V_j†`. This gives the same outcome probabilities and transported conditional post-states, not merely equality of one selected expectation. This is a standard conjugation result within the declared common root space; it is not a general theorem for interacting quantum clocks with different subsystem factorizations.

For a concrete counterexample to reusing an untransformed matrix, choose root preparation `ρ=|1,0><1,0|` and root event `E=|1,0><1,0|`. In frame j=0 the event is `P_(1,0)`; in frame j=4 it is `P_(0,0)` because W_3 swaps levels 0 and 1. The same event has probability 1 in both frames. Asking for `P_(0,0)` already in frame 0 instead has probability 0: that is a different event, not a failure of covariance.

As a two-event check, perform the noisy counter instrument on the superposition |+>, then measure the noncommuting effect `|+><+|`. The joint probabilities for first outcomes 0, 1 and erasure followed by the plus outcome are respectively 1/5, 1/5 and 1/10. Constructing both instruments and post-states independently in each frame gives the same three fractions. This tests the declared finite instrument, including erasure and disturbance, not arbitrary interventions in general quantum gravity.

A bounded physical-space representative is

    D_E = J E J†,

extended by zero on the orthogonal complement of `ker C`. Because `C J=0` and C is self-adjoint, `[C,D_E]=0`; expectation in `JρJ†` equals `Tr(ρE)`. For the complete instrument, the physical Kraus representatives `J K_m J†`, together with the complementary projector `I−JJ†` as an off-physical-sector outcome, form a complete instrument on the whole finite kinematic space. On the physical sector the complement has probability zero. The code checks the relevant commutator on all 78 kinematic basis vectors; this numerical check accompanies the analytic identity rather than replacing it.

This is a comparison of **two vertex-conditioned descriptions of one ideal parent clock**, not two independently interacting clocks. Neither arbitrary temporal delocalization nor gravitational clock switching follows. [Baumann–Lock](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08845) already analyze interventions and causal comparisons across distinct temporal quantum reference frames; their framework is a necessary comparison for any stronger extension.

## 5. What happens if the parent clock is made genuinely periodic?

The open path above already records an unwrapped order. To test whether one can remove it without consequence, replace the 13-vertex path by four vertices on a circle. Use the identity on the first three edges and shift the counter cyclically on the wrap edge:

    X|n,f> = |(n+1) mod 3,f>.

The same positive propagation constraint now enforces `ψ₁=ψ₀`, `ψ₂=ψ₁`, `ψ₃=ψ₂` and `ψ₀=Xψ₃`. Hence a zero-constraint history exists only when its root register lies in `ker(X−I)`. This is exactly the known holonomy consistency condition reviewed in [RELATIONAL_TIME_CONSTRUCTION.md](RELATIONAL_TIME_CONSTRUCTION.md), applied to this concrete counter; it is not a new general theorem.

For this X, `ker(X−I)` is two-dimensional, spanned by the uniform counter state for each flag. Conditional on any sharp clock vertex, the counter distribution in a physical state is uniform, so it cannot be initialized sharply in `n=0`. The normalized joint state with a uniform four-vertex clock and sharp `|0,0>` counter has constraint expectation 1/2, not zero. The rational verifier checks both examples and the fixed-space rank.

**Consequence and scope:** simply closing this particular phase graph while requiring an exact zero-constraint history prevents the intended sharply initialized counter from satisfying the wrap constraint. This does not rule out other relational clocks or all counters. Possible alternatives include a longer/nonclosed history, an enlarged clock whose states retain winding, a different allowed holonomy, or a generalized non-normalizable physical history with appropriate rigging. Each changes an assumption and must be constructed rather than declared equivalent. In particular, a finite cyclic shift ultimately wraps and is not an unbounded counter.

**Infinite continuation:** [INFINITE_WINDING_COUNTER.md](INFINITE_WINDING_COUNTER.md) constructs one explicit infinite lift and group-averaged physical space. It avoids this finite obstruction by supplying a bilateral unwrapped degree of freedom and a correlated counter; it does not derive a finite, autonomous apparatus or a fundamental time.

## 6. Where this advances—and where it stops

- The earlier abstract winding-memory requirement now has a finite constraint, explicit register states, reversible updates, a complete noisy instrument, an overflow condition, and a concrete disturbance witness.
- It reuses established history-state and clock-transport ideas; closely related record-based time constructions already exist, including [Martellini's record-clock proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25307) and [finite quantum histories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05748). **Research novelty is unestablished.**
- The prescribed 13-step graph and controlled gates encode the order being investigated. Counter preparation and interrogation remain assumptions. No continuously running physical counter or cyclic resource-reuse law has been derived.
- To connect to [Barontini](https://doi.org/10.1103/1h9j-df4k), specify physical observables and an apparatus that measures the experiment's actual coarse-grained estimator, and compare the same data and uncertainties. This toy S is not that estimator.
- To connect to [recollapsing Einstein–scalar gravity](RECOLLAPSING_GRAVITATIONAL_CLOCK.md), replace the engineered path by its declared constraint, choose an actual clock/record instrument and include detector backreaction. No such quantum mapping is supplied here.
- Neither the history state nor its counter demonstrates experienced passage, one universal consciousness, essence–existence, or zero-residue closure.
