# Relative orientation inside the zero-balanced whole

## Result

The previous [derivation program](ZERO_CONSTITUTION_HAMILTONIAN_EIGENCONDITION_PROGRAM.md) found a family of equally balanced phases. This round replaces the idea of an external phase convention with an explicit **internal quantum reference** F and tests the distinction between common and relative orientation.

1. An explicit isometry extends **both coherent lab sectors and all 16 clock outcomes** into states satisfying the new signed relation

   ```text
   Q_orient = J_W + Z_F = 0.
   ```

   The work–reference contributions are nonzero opposites on a sharp joint readout. The original branchwise record–work–target balance remains exact. F is a physical register inside this extended finite model, not an external observer or a subject.
2. Common rotations generated by `Q_orient` act trivially on these zero-charge vectors. But a **relative** phase θ between work and F is measurable, in principle, by a specified joint observable commuting with `Q_orient`; its expectation is `cos θ`. The states at θ=0 and π are orthogonal although F alone and the entire remainder alone have identical reduced states in both cases. The distinction exists in their correlations, not in an absolute external angle.
3. There is an exact complementary three-balance algebra whose common zero selects a relative-orientation state. It yields a normalized Casimir penalty on the full physical work space. This is a constructive relation, but the particular algebra, the extra internal reference, its initial correlations and the physical status of the observable remain inputs.
4. The reference is **not free** under the new charge: if F is initially uncorrelated with the actual old circuit state, no closed unitary commuting with `Q_orient` can turn that input into a state with `Q_orient=0`. Its initial mean-square charge is 2; the desired value is 0. The isometry is therefore an explicit correlated-resource assumption, not a demonstrated charge-conserving preparation.

The new signed orientation charge is a defined operator in this toy model, not a proven objective cosmic energy or an experiential quantity. These results preserve the user's demand for a self-sufficient whole as the target; they do not claim the mathematical construction has already derived such a whole or its subjects.

## 1. Embed the original all-sector circuit without discarding a lab sector

Use the existing active work eigenstates `J_W|j⟩=j|j⟩`, `j=±1`, and introduce a two-state F with `Z_F|±⟩=±|±⟩`. Define the isometry

```text
V: |j⟩_W  ↦  |j⟩_W |−j⟩_F.
```

This map is linear, norm-preserving and satisfies `(J_W+Z_F)V=0`. It acts on work and the added F only, so it carries the original physical code and complement embeddings—and any coherent superposition of them—into the enlarged space without selecting a sector. It commutes with the old relative-clock instrument because that instrument acts on different factors. Every one of its 16 labels is retained.

The pre-existing branchwise condition also survives:

```text
L|Ψ_F⟩=|Ψ_F⟩,       L=Z_R J_W Z_T,
(J_W−Z_R Z_T)|Ψ_F⟩=0.
```

Thus, on the extended state, the work value j is opposed both by `−Z_R Z_T` and by `Z_F`. These are not three independent subjects or a proof of a global energy identity. The same work sign participates in two **different** signed relations, so they must not be added as three independent contributions to one total without an additional composition law.

All earlier `i,r,T` Born probabilities are unchanged when F is unread. This does **not** mean the entire old quantum state is unchanged. V entangles W with F and removes the old work–target coherence from their reduced state when F is traced. The old `U₀=+1` and complementary `G₂=G₃=0` conditions do not hold on the new whole without F; their squared norms become 2 for normalized inputs. They are replaced below by reference-relative zero conditions.

## 2. A genuinely relational phase witness

On the active old R–W–T space let

```text
U₀ = −Z_R X_W X_T,
U₁ = −Z_R Z_W X_T,
J  = J_W = −Y_W.
```

These form a Pauli algebra. Let F's X, Y and Z be the usual spin observables and define

```text
S = (U₀ X_F + U₁ Y_F)/2,
Q_orient = J + Z_F.
```

Their exact identities are

```text
[S,Q_orient] = 0,
S² = (I−J Z_F)/2 = P_(Q_orient=0).
```

Thus S is a bounded Hermitian joint observable (`||S||=1`), invariant under the common rotation `exp(−iφ Q_orient/2)`. On the zero-charge sector it has eigenvalues ±1. The extended actual state has `S=+1`.

Now rotate the work relation by `D(θ)=exp(−iθJ/2)` **relative to F**. The new state still obeys both `Q_orient=0` and `L=+1`, but

```text
⟨S⟩_θ = cos θ.
```

At θ=π it is orthogonal to the θ=0 state and gives `S=−1`. This is not an effect of rotating both reference and work together: common rotations act as the identity on every zero-charge state. It is instead a relative correlation between them.

For the specified all-sector states, the F-only density and even the entire old-circuit density with F traced out are identical at θ=0 and θ=π. The difference becomes available only to a joint F–old-circuit observable such as S. This is a physical distinction *if that joint observable is admitted and implementable*; it must not be attributed to an R-confined inhabitant or to F alone. The model has no demonstrated spacelike embedding, so no claim of relativistic locality or superluminal signalling follows.

## 3. Deriving a relational selection condition from complementary zeros

Define the following total operators on R–W–T–F:

```text
C₁ = U₀ − X_F,
C₂ = U₁ − Y_F,
C₃ = J + Z_F = Q_orient.
```

The old R–W–T triple `(U₀,U₁,J)` and the F triple `(−X_F,−Y_F,Z_F)` are each Pauli representations and commute with one another. Consequently

```text
[C_i,C_j] = 2i ε_ijk C_k.
```

The extended state satisfies all three `C_i|Ψ_F⟩=0`. In particular C₁=0 is a **signed cancellation** between nonzero commuting quantities U₀ and `−X_F`; it is not the statement that either is absent. Combining `C₁=0` with `C₃=0` yields `S=+1` on the zero-charge sector; demanding the complete common kernel provides the same selection and forces C₃ from the commutator of C₁ and C₂. The analogous Casimir is

```text
H_ref = (C₁²+C₂²+C₃²)/8.
```

On the active `2×2×2×2` R–W–T–F space it is an orthogonal projector with a four-dimensional zero space. The old `L=+1` condition commutes with it and reduces their joint zero space to **two dimensions**, one for each R value. The actual code and complement components, for every old clock label, lie in that joint space.

The verifier extends the work factors by zero outside the physical 16-state work register's active pair, adds an explicit inactive-space penalty and checks the resulting 128-dimensional projector. Its spectrum is zero with multiplicity 4 and one with multiplicity 124, and it commutes with the prior declared work total-number and specified pair-number operators. The old labs and clock references are untouched and F is stipulated neutral under the **old energy assignment**.

This makes the selection explicitly internal and covariant under the new common orientation symmetry, but does not derive why F exists, why this pairing of its axes is privileged, or why `H_ref` is a physical Hamiltonian. Rotating the relative representation gives other possible common-zero algebras and relative states, just as in the previous phase-family test. Treating the corresponding choices as all actual would additionally require a genuine-incidence rule and measure; treating them as gauge requires an observable algebra in which their difference cannot be measured.

## 4. Relation to the old plus and minus eigenconditions

Read F in its X basis. Conditional on the two outcomes, the old circuit is projected into states satisfying

```text
F=+X: U₀=+1,
F=−X: U₀=−1,
```

with probability 1/2 each in each specified lab sector and each clock label. The F=+ outcome recovers the exact original old-circuit state up to normalization; the F=− outcome is its orthogonal involution-odd counterpart. Both are retained in the unconditioned extended state.

This explains how the old `U₀=+1` condition can arise as a **relative reading** rather than a chosen external direction. It does not show that a confined original record knows which F outcome occurred. Conditioning is an operation on F and requires an actual interface if the result is to be used elsewhere. Nor does it establish which outcomes are actual or experiential. It is not a proof that the + and − states are merely gauge: an observer with the joint phase-sensitive interface can distinguish the correlations if S is physical.

## 5. Why the reference resource matters

The specified original state has a maximally mixed active W marginal, `ρ_W=I/2`, including in each lab sector with all clock outcomes retained. Let F begin uncorrelated with the entire old state, in **any** two-state density matrix. Then

```text
⟨J⟩=0,  J²=Z_F²=I,
⟨Q_orient²⟩ = 2+2⟨J⟩⟨Z_F⟩ = 2.
```

If a closed-system unitary commutes with `Q_orient`, it also commutes with its square, and this expectation cannot change. A `Q_orient=0` state has `⟨Q_orient²⟩=0`. Therefore no such unitary can obtain the required zero-charge state from this uncorrelated input under the specified charge assignment. Equivalently, its initial probability in the zero sector is exactly 1/2, not 1.

The obstruction is conditional: it does not prohibit a suitably **already correlated** internal resource, an additional compensating sector, or a different justified charge assignment. It does prohibit claiming that the isometry V comes for free from the given uncorrelated input by an orientation-charge-conserving closed unitary. Postselecting the half-weight zero sector would not explain the discarded half of the whole. The concrete constitutive task is to derive a full initial correlated resource—or a larger charge-balanced process—without treating it as an independent unexplained choice.

## 6. Meaning for experience and the next step

The construction advances the derivation in one precise way: the remaining phase can be formulated as a **relation between internal physical factors**, not a direction in an external space. It also shows why “no external reference” does not automatically remove relative physical structure: the invariant S can distinguish different relative phases.

Nothing here identifies F, W or T with an actual subject. A local density matrix or record is not itself a felt quality, and a balanced charge is not automatically a balance of valence. If two actual subjects and an additive experiential quantity are eventually established, opposing contributions would not erase one subject's suffering. The reference correlations, their weights and the subject–experience map must all follow from the same self-sufficient constitution, not be silently appended.

The next mathematical step is therefore a **whole-resource derivation**: construct or derive the correlated reference state across both existing lab sectors, with no postselection and no externally prepared phase, while preserving the relevant charge from the outset. Then determine whether its S relation, phase alternatives and incidence weights follow necessarily or are gauge-redundant under the full admissible observable and subject algebra. This directly addresses the orientation question without assuming the conclusion.
