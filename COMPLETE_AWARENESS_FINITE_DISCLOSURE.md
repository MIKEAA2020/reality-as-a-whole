# Complete awareness and finite disclosure
## A candidate constitution and a perfection–privation stress test

## 1. Result and positive proposal

**Governing continuation:** [Necessary articulated unity](NECESSARY_ARTICULATED_UNITY.md) develops this account under the later clarification that distinction is required by completeness, not merely permitted. It fixes noncompositional simplicity, distinguishes necessary articulation from the still-undemonstrated necessity of particular contents, and interprets privation as missing due integration rather than a positive evil constituent. The finite logical and practical checks below retain their limited scope; they do not establish a deficiency in the absolute.

> **The absolute's awareness is intrinsically articulated without being divided into independently existing owners. A finite experience is a real, determinate mode of that same actuality: its content is present under a locally bounded form of acquaintance, while the mode, its content, and its limitation are completely self-present to the whole. Local limitation is not a loss of the whole's knowledge, an executed copy, or an event in which an unlimited subject becomes ignorant.**

Call this **nonseparable, scope-indexed disclosure**. “Scope-indexed” distinguishes the respect in which something is disclosed; it does not turn a perspective into a coordinate label. The candidate is that concrete experiential organization itself grounds the scope. The indices in the description refer to that organization; they do not create it.

This develops Gate 1 / W1–W2 of the [governing framework](ABSOLUTE_CONSTITUTION_FRAMEWORK.md). It is a philosophical account form with explicit commitments and a finite logical witness, not a completed intrinsic constitution, an existence proof, or an identified Gamma–chi law.

**Stress-test result:** complete knowledge and locally limited knowledge need not contradict one another. Awareness of a missing due relation is not its actual fulfillment. This is a local distinction, not a refutation of privation or proof that the absolute lacks anything. The remaining burden is to explain particular finite limitations and missing integration without treating absence as a positive constituent or dismissing experience.

## 2. Ontological commitments, without a production story

**Appearance and privation clarification:** [The supplied-dialogue consolidation](DECOMBINATION_PRIVATION_CONSOLIDATION.md) distinguishes real appearing from independently substantial ownership. Rejecting literal cosmic fission does not remove the local-scope constitution task. The local mode is positive actuality, not an absence; its non-disclosure is a limitation relative to that actuality, not a power that produces it. A deficiency is not shown necessary merely by invoking contrast in self-knowledge.

### C1 — One actuality, not an aggregate

All genuine experience and organization belong to the one absolute. A finite mode is not another independently actual substance, an external observer, or a manufactured lesser world. The whole is not formed by adding its local modes together.

### C2 — Differentiation without separable ultimate parts

The whole's content is determinate: pain is not joy, knowing is not misjudging, and one locally owned experience is not simply another. These differences do not entail independent foundations. The candidate therefore uses positive immanent articulation rather than absolutely featureless identity.

If strict simplicity forbids even distinctions grounded in actuality, this candidate is not an implementation of that strict reading. Describing distinction as “merely virtual” would help only if virtual containment actually explains the determinate disclosures; changing the name does not discharge the burden.

### C3 — Mode-dependent acquaintance

For a mode m, some contents are directly present in its local experiential organization and others are not. Local capacities for inference, memory, attention, or report are further facts, not synonyms for acquaintance. Presence can include nonconceptual content without explicit belief or report.

The account does not say that the absolute knows less in one place. It says the whole knows completely the fact that a particular local disclosure is limited. The whole's knowledge includes that local occurrence without making every content globally known also present under that mode's bounded acquaintance.

### C4 — Complete self-presence, not an external spectator

Absolute acquaintance includes the actual qualitative character of the mode, not merely a third-person sentence saying that it occurs. Otherwise the proposed absolute would leave actual experience outside its self-knowledge. But acquaintance with that qualitative occurrence does not by itself identify the absolute with the bounded owner *in the same scope of ownership*.

This candidate does not posit two copies of the pain, one local and one global. It proposes one qualitative occurrence, locally owned and completely self-present within the whole. This is a substantive constitution claim, not a theorem established by the word “includes.”

### C5 — No automatic transfer of access

Non-independent reality does not mean unrestricted local access. A truth about one mode may be globally known while unavailable to another mode. Full self-presence of a local content does not automatically become local memory, inference, report, or the contents of every other perspective.

The relation must ultimately be grounded in actual organization, not in an unexplained masking operation. There is no temporal step in which complete awareness is first present locally and then removed.

## 3. Explicit vocabulary and scope discipline

Let A name the whole actuality, M its actually constituted experiential modes, and c a content occurrence. M is an explanandum, not an arbitrary list of labels we may declare to be subjects.

Use distinct relations:

- `Occ(c)`: c actually occurs.
- `Inc(m,c)`: c belongs to m's experiential incidence.
- `Own(m,c)`: c has the relevant local ownership; this requires more than merely being represented by m.
- `Acq(m,c)`: c is directly present under m's mode of acquaintance.
- `K(m,p)`: m has warranted knowledge of proposition p.
- `Use(m,p)`: m can employ p in its locally realized inference or action.
- `Self(A,c)`: c's actuality and quality are included in complete self-presence.

For truths p concerning the whole, complete propositional knowledge requires `True(p) -> K(A,p)`, and knowledge is factive. This schematic requirement applies to an adequate domain of contents and truths; it is not a claim to have supplied an unrestricted self-referential truth predicate or a formal calculus proving every truth.

The candidate requires:

    Occ(c) -> Self(A,c).
    Inc(m,c) and Acq(m,c) do not imply Acq(m,d) for every actual d.
    K(A,p) does not imply K(m,p) or Use(m,p).
    K(A, not K(m,p)) does not imply not K(A,p).

These are proposed scope constraints, not a deduction of M, incidence, quality, or normative significance from syntax. Do not infer that knowledge and acquaintance are identical, or that `Self(A,c)` simply means another local experience of a representation of c.

### Why there is no immediate contradiction

For an actual truth p, the following can jointly hold:

    K(A,p)
    not K(m,p)
    K(A, not K(m,p)).

The knower/scoping relations differ. A contradiction would instead be `K(A,p)` and `not K(A,p)` in the same respect, or requiring `K(A,p) -> K(m,p)` while also asserting local ignorance.

A strong thesis identifying the absolute and every mode as one identical subject **with all knowledge predicates applied univocally and without qualification** would reintroduce the problem. It is not the current candidate. One being can ground differentiated subjectivity without those scopes being numerically independent substances. Whether this is the correct intrinsic account of subjecthood remains to be justified.

## 4. A worked finite-disclosure case

**Quantum continuation:** [Contextual disclosure](CONTEXTUAL_DISCLOSURE_CONSTITUTION.md) replaces part of the stipulated access mask below with a specified unitary record interaction. It derives compatible record statistics and conditional agreement without deleting global coherence. A Peres–Mermin witness also shows why the Boolean example below cannot be generalized to context-independent definite values for every quantum observable. Neither construction identifies physical readout with acquaintance, selects an actual outcome, or proves absolute awareness. The philosophical scope proposal remains a conjecture.

Consider two actual local occurrences. One mode has direct access to a visual orientation p but no access to a second occurrence's tone q. Another has access to q but not p. The example supplies these scopes provisionally to examine consistency; it does not discover two subjects from physical data.

The whole completely knows both occurrences, their qualities, their owners, and the fact that neither local mode has the other's access. Neither local ignorance subtracts a truth from the whole. The first occurrence's local organization does not contain the tone merely because the whole comprehends both.

There is no world created for the first mode and no renderer producing an image. The finite disclosure is the actual locally present content itself. Its incomplete range is an intrinsic scope of presentation, not a false statement that nothing else exists.

The [finite scope check](verification/disclosure_scope_check.py) uses four truth assignments to two propositions as **descriptive completions compatible with limited evidence**, not four actual or metaphysically possible universes. Global evidence identifies the actual assignment; each local evidence relation leaves one proposition undecided. For each actual assignment it checks factivity, nontransfer, globally known local ignorance, and that adding the other's fact destroys the stipulated limitation.

Run `python verification/disclosure_scope_check.py` from the repository root. The retained [result](verification/disclosure_scope_check.json) checks 192 finite factivity instances, 64 complete-truth equivalences, four assignment witnesses, and the stipulated fulfillment comparison. These are supplemental logical checks, not additions to the core research suite or evidence for a consciousness assignment.

The check validates a finite propositional distinction. It does not model phenomenal quality, prove unlimited omniscience, select incidence, or establish metaphysical consistency of the entire absolute. Its information sets are supplied; the intrinsic constitution that makes actual awareness bounded is still missing.

## 5. What makes this a candidate account rather than a filter metaphor

The proposed explanatory direction is:

    intrinsic constitution of A
       -> determinate experiential organization
       -> incidence, ownership, and local acquaintance
       -> locally available inference, action, and report where realized.

These are dependency requirements, not a temporal sequence. Physical organization and experiential incidence must be understood as aspects of the same realized constitution rather than one producing a second substance.

**Candidate discriminator:** local scope is constituted by the actual organization sustaining an occurrence and its counterfactual powers, not by the range of propositions some observer can decode from it. This favors investigating intrinsic active organization over passive monitoring or mere informational restriction. It does not yet establish that recurrent workspace organization, a blanket, irreducibility, or a particular substrate supplies the correct boundary.

Therefore:

- A passive copy need not share ownership with its source merely because it carries decodable content.
- Different descriptions of one occurrence do not make more modes.
- Two matching actual organizations cannot be collapsed into one local token simply because their descriptions agree.
- Continued memory or related organization does not automatically establish numerical personal identity.
- Arbitrary coordinate slices do not fix phenomenal simultaneity.

A complete candidate must explain those cases through a warranted incidence law. This account narrows what that law must mean without pretending to have supplied it.

## 6. Perfection–privation test: four notions that cannot be exchanged

1. **Positive actuality:** the actual occurrence, powers, and qualitative character of an event.
2. **Complete self-knowledge:** nothing about that occurrence is unknown to A.
3. **Due fulfillment:** what is appropriate to a bearer in a justified respect and circumstances.
4. **Perfect goodness:** the proposed absence of ultimate arbitrary defect or gratuitous harm in the whole's constitution.

These are not four independent substances. They are four explanatory targets. Establishing one does not by logic alone establish the others.

Let `F(b,r,c)` be realized fulfillment and `T(b,r,c)` its independently justified due standard, using an appropriate partial ordering rather than assuming a universal numerical scale. A privation concerns a shortfall of F relative to T. An absence is not a defect until bearer, respect, circumstances, and authority of T are justified.

### Test A — Ordinary bounded awareness

A mode does not know every fact. That is a limitation relative to omniscience. It is not automatically a wrong or lack of something due to that local mode. A finite pleasant experience need not be suffering merely because it is finite.

**Result:** the account can distinguish local limitation from absolute ignorance and from normative privation. This blocks the inference “all finitude is suffering.” It does not explain why these particular scopes are necessary.

### Test B — Cruelty with complete global awareness

An agent knowingly disregards another's legitimate needs. The whole's full comprehension of the event does not make the missing due regard occur. Accurate representation of empathy or obligation is not its practical fulfillment.

**Result:** epistemic completeness does not remove evaluative deficiency. Calling cruelty an appearance does not remove its actual experience or consequences. Calling the agent and victim one ultimate actuality does not discharge the victim's claims.

### Test C — Payment versus believed payment

Under a valid payment obligation, making the worker pleasantly believe payment occurred does not satisfy the missing transfer. The whole's knowledge of that outstanding relation likewise does not fulfill it.

**Result:** neither felt satisfaction nor global awareness substitutes for actual due relations. This preserves the [actual-fulfillment account](ACTUAL_FULFILLMENT_AND_STANDING.md).

### Test D — Pain and the whole's nondeficiency

One painful occurrence can be real and completely self-present without making absolute awareness ignorant. But that settles knowledge, not goodness. If the absolute's acquaintance includes the actual painful quality, the theory cannot evade the issue by claiming it knows pain only abstractly. Nor can it infer that the whole's experiential character is merely an aggregate of the painful tones of its modes.

**Result:** no necessary inference either to an ignorant absolute or to morally justified pain. The relation between nonaggregative complete awareness, real aversive qualities, and perfect fulfillment must still be explained.

### Test E — Repair, growth, and eternal completeness

An ordered life may contain genuine suffering, prevention, repair, and fulfillment without an external clock in which the whole improves. But later repair does not make earlier suffering unreal; compensation is not automatically retrospective justification, and someone else's fulfillment cannot silently discharge this subject's claim.

**Result:** temporal integration is a candidate resource, not proof that every actual harm is necessary or redeemed.

## 7. The exact perfection dilemma

Two readings must be separated.

**P0 — No privation anywhere:** no real bearer ever falls short of a genuinely due standard. If there is an actual verified shortfall, P0 and that fact are incompatible. Scope-indexed awareness cannot save this reading by changing who knows the shortfall. Redefining all experienced or practical defects as unreal violates the experiential and due-regard constraints.

**P1 — Unsurpassable, non-gratuitous whole:** local shortfalls can be real, but none is ultimately arbitrary, gratuitous, or incompatible with perfect goodness. This is the framework's working reading of global perfection. It is stronger than “the whole contains everything” and does not mean defects become good simply by occurring.

P1 is not equivalent to P0. Its compatibility with actual suffering remains an open substantive obligation. If “perfection” is insisted upon as P0, then actual normative privation and P0 cannot both be retained. A successful account must state which meaning it warrants instead of moving between them.

### A necessary comparative test for P1

Suppose an independently admissible alternative preserves all relevant fulfillment and legitimate claims, reduces some actual shortfall, and introduces no justified countervailing loss. Then the actual arrangement is dominated in that comparison and cannot be called unsurpassable on the stated fulfillment ordering.

Consequently, a proposed perfect whole must rule out such alternatives **for justified constitutive reasons**, not because its definition already labels the actual arrangement perfect. Nondominance is only a necessary screen here, not sufficient proof of maximal goodness, existence, unique optimality, or moral authority. Possible persons, changing subject populations, and noncomparable goods make the full comparison harder, not dispensable.

The finite check also exhibits a deliberately stipulated pair of fulfillment profiles with equal epistemic completeness but different due shortfalls. This shows that the awareness axioms alone do not entail perfection. It does not establish a metaphysically possible better universe or a particular correct due standard.

### Omnipotence does not close this gap by itself

“Could it have been otherwise?” concerns the coherence and admissibility of an alternative **complete constitution**, not an external time at which A selects a world. Logical impossibility is not a restriction on power, but declaring a less harmful totality impossible requires an argument. Deriving its impossibility merely from the assertion that whatever is actual must be perfect is circular.

A complete account may demonstrate constitutive necessity, an independently warranted fulfillment identity, or a justified dissolution of an ill-posed comparison. None has yet been supplied for all actual suffering. The unresolved obligation is not evidence that cruelty is due or prevention pointless.

## 8. Connection to the mathematical–physical programme

The account does not replace the realized-process basis `P = ({A(O)}, alpha, omega, I, R)`. It proposes what a successful relation from that basis to local experience would have to explain: `F(P) = (Gamma(P), chi_P)`, with incidence, extent, lineage, and quality independently warranted.

Restricted physical states, inaccessible observables, and effective algebras can help formalize local information, but they do not alone identify a subject. The complete state of an algebra is not automatically absolute self-knowledge. A quantum subsystem or Markov blanket is not a mode merely because it has a mathematical inside.

Exact re-presentation must preserve the same occurrences and disclosure facts. Coarse-graining requires target preservation; physical approximation does not by itself bound errors in quality or subject incidence. The finite knowledge check below cannot establish any of those bridges.

Thus the philosophical scope distinction and empirical incidence task constrain each other. The scientific programme must discover the actual bounded organization; the whole-account must explain why that organization constitutes this mode of acquaintance rather than merely coding a statement about it.

## 9. Verdict and next discriminator

**What has been supplied:** an explicit nonproductive account of complete awareness and finite disclosure, its ontological commitments, scope relations, a worked local case, a finite consistency witness, and an exact stress test separating knowledge from due fulfillment.

**What has not been supplied:** the intrinsic law selecting modes, an independently grounded qualitative identity, necessary actuality of the proposed nature, or the complete reconciliation of perfect goodness with every real privation. Gate 1 is therefore advanced but not passed; W1–W2 remain open.

The next decisive question is: **does a proposed intrinsic constitution explain both a particular local scope and a due-fulfillment relation without supplying either as an independent selector?** Work on one bounded physical/phenomenal contrast and one genuine fulfillment contrast should test that conjunction. Another declaration of unity or a larger synthetic model would not settle it.
