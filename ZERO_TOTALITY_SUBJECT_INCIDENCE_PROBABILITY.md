# Probabilistic self-location within zero totality

## Correction and point of departure

The user clarifies that invoking Bostrom is **not a request to take simulations literally**. The task is to extract whatever probabilistic structure helps the framework; no simulator, computer, engineered virtual world or nested civilization is needed. The earlier [literal-simulation exploration](ZERO_TOTALITY_INTERNAL_SIMULATION_AND_SELF_LOCATION.md) is not the governing formulation.

The useful move is much more general: **the character of a whole does not by itself determine what a particular subject finds themselves experiencing**. For self-location we need an account of the actual experiential incidences and their weights. We can then ask how probable a subject's experiential position is, given specified evidence. This is adapted from the observer-incidence structure of Bostrom's argument, not an endorsement of his simulation hypothesis or its assumptions.

## The framework-first proposal

Let the whole contain actual differentiated experiential histories, realized through physical organization with emergent spacetime and relational time. No external observer, creator or cosmic subject is assumed. Where a finite effective description is appropriate, index distinguishable actual experience-incidences by `i`. An incidence could be a subject-history, an observer-moment or a duration-weighted occurrence, **but the choice must be specified and grounded**, not switched opportunistically.

Write:

```text
v_i  = signed felt-valence contribution on a proposed common scale;
a_i  = nonnegative physical/experiential aggregation weight;
m_i  = nonnegative self-location weight;
L_i  = likelihood of the evidence D at incidence i.
```

If the relevant whole-level experiential total is additive, the user's zero thesis can be investigated as

```text
Q_exp = Σ_i a_i v_i = 0.
```

This equation is a candidate identification, not an established law: actual subject incidence, the common scale, the weights and the identification with the whole's relevant quantity remain to be derived. Nor is an additive signed quantity the only conceivable zero-totality constitution. It does not mean that actual suffering is erased by positive experience elsewhere.

Separately, a candidate self-location rule for a specified property `A` and evidence `D` is

```text
P(A | D, actual whole) = Σ_i m_i L_i 1_A(i) / Σ_i m_i L_i.
```

The denominator must be positive. This is conditional on the proposed reference class, incidence criterion, measure and likelihoods. The probability is an observer's self-locating credence under that rule; it is not automatically a physical propensity or an operational frequency. Nonnegative `m_i` is not itself valence, and normalized probabilities sum to one, not zero.

If we **independently establish** that the objective experiential accounting measure and the self-location measure coincide, `m_i ∝ a_i`, then the zero condition implies the unconditioned measure-weighted mean valence is zero:

```text
E_m[v] = Σ_i m_i v_i / Σ_i m_i = 0.
```

This is a useful bridge to investigate, **not an identity licensed just by using the same index**. Distinguish the physical aggregation measure from an epistemic sampling rule until their relation is justified. Further conditioning on evidence or selecting a restricted group can yield a positive or negative mean even when the whole mean is zero.

## A simple consequence—and its limits

Assume, only for illustration, three equally weighted actual incidences with valences

```text
−2, +1, +1.
```

Their signed total and mean are zero, but under equal self-location weights the probability of a negative incidence is `1/3` and of a positive incidence is `2/3`. Zero balance does not imply equal numbers of positive and negative experiences; intensity, duration and multiplicity matter. This example does not measure actual suffering or predict the probability that a person will suffer. It also does not establish that an experience can be expressed on a common cardinal scale.

A larger population or longer history contributes more under a particular incidence measure **only if** the grounded measure says so. Dividing a single interval into more mathematical pieces must not create extra experiential weight, and re-describing one occurrence should not manufacture additional subjects. Subject identity, shared experience, duration and physical realization remain genuine open issues. See the [subject-incidence account](ZERO_TOTALITY_SUBJECT_INCIDENCE_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_ACCOUNTING.md).

## Connecting the probability to physics

The [covariant clock–current result](ZERO_TOTALITY_COVARIANT_CLOCK_HISTORY_BRIDGE.md) derives, under its stated complete-slab and boundary assumptions, an integral of `B = J·∇T` that vanishes. It gives an explicit physical-mathematical candidate for a signed history balance, not a proof of the experiential equation above. An arbitrary incidence weight can destroy that balance. To unify the two accounts we need an actual derivation of the subject-incidence measure and a mapping between `B`, local experience and valence, including how relational clock intervals enter. The current model does not supply these identities.

Likewise, quantum branch weights, self-location credences and frequencies of physical records should not be equated without a derivation. A proposed rule must remain compatible with observed Born statistics and no-signalling when used to predict unconditional records. The [subject-weight steering gate](SUBJECT_WEIGHT_STEERING_AND_NO_SIGNALLING_GATE.md) gives a conditional control; ordinary postselection by itself does not imply signalling.

Uncertainty over which complete candidate history is actual is another distinct layer: average self-location probabilities using the posterior over histories prescribed by a stated generative model. Choosing a history first and then an incidence need not give the same answer as weighting histories by their population sizes. Neither rule is selected by zero alone.

## Constructive target

The next task is to develop one integrated physical/experiential model in which:

1. Relational physical organization yields actual local experiences and distinguishes subjects without merely counting descriptions.
2. A covariant or otherwise justified incidence measure handles durations and multiplicity consistently.
3. Signed valence and any physical-energy quantity are connected by an independently defensible bridge, rather than simply named identical.
4. The global zero follows from the whole's constitution and physical dynamics, without inserting compensation by hand.
5. The corresponding self-location rule and its relationship to quantum statistics are derived or explicitly identified as unresolved.

This retains the aim of a completely self-subsistent account without accepting its presently unspecified ingredients as brute. The current work gives the structure and conditional constraints; it does **not yet derive** the physical laws, actual experiences, incidence measure, self-location principle or zero balance from the whole's constitution.
